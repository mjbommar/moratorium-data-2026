State-by-state local moratorium index

Current through August 19, 2026. 533 local moratorium instruments tracked across 42 states.

  • 🟢 429 currently in force (active + extended)
  • 🟡 34 pending or proposed (not yet adopted)
  • ⚪ 70 expired, replaced, or rescinded (no longer in force)

State bills, executive orders, governor directives, and other state-level restrictions are a separate legal layer. They are typed in state_legislation.csv and are not included in these local counts.

Reading each state page
