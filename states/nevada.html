Nye County

  • Type: County
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Recorded date: 2026-06-02 (Nye County Board of County Commissioners unanimously approved a countywide moratorium on new data center applications at its Tuesday, June 2, 2026 meeting, per Pahrump Valley Times)
  • Date field: 2026-06-02 (exact)
  • Duration: No fixed term; Commissioner Strickland was quoted estimating a new data center ordinance could be ready 'in about 90 days,' but the moratorium's stated end condition is adoption of local data center regulations, not a calendar date.
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center, cryptocurrency mining
  • Record ID: nv-nye-county-2026
  • Authority and source: Board of County Commissioners resolution under NRS 244.110/244.119
  • What prompted it: Nye County Water District Governing Board's 2026-05-27 emergency request that the commission impose a moratorium, driven by concerns over data center water demand on a 'critically over-appropriated' aquifer (Nevada Hydrographic Basin 162) in the Pahrump Regional Planning District
  • Affected projects: Manse Technology Campus (formerly 'Project Blackjack'), a proposed ~900-acre, $18.4B Gigawatt Development Partners LLC data center campus in the Pahrump area, is now blocked by the August 18, 2026 Basin 162 ban; per KTNV (2026-09), 'given the ban, [it] appears to no longer be feasible, at least not where it was originally proposed.'
  • Status notes: June 5, 2026 reporting describes a countywide moratorium on new data-center applications adopted June 2 until local regulations. Nevada Current reported August 20 that the board voted 5–0 on August 18 to ban construction and operation in Basin 162 and planned a September 14 meeting on regulations outside that area. A county notice reports adoption of Ordinance 631 on August 18 with September 7 effectiveness conditional on publication; its linked PDF is labeled Bill 2026-11 and describes potentially broader unincorporated-area scope. On September 4 the Pahrump Valley Times reported cancellation of a special meeting on Bill 2026-14 and no replacement hearing scheduled at that time, but calls the meeting Monday, September 4, an internally inconsistent date; it does not establish the canceled meeting was September 14. These later captures do not restore custody of the original September source bodies. Final text, publication, adopted scope, relationship to the countywide moratorium and later force remain unverified. [VERIFY: Ordinance 631 scope and publication, subsequent countywide action.]
  • Outcome: June reporting describes the June 2, 2026 countywide application moratorium. August 20 reporting describes an August 18, 5–0 Basin 162 ban; the county notice reports Ordinance 631 adoption, conditional publication for effect, while a linked bill suggests potentially broader scope. September 4 reporting says a Bill 2026-14 special meeting was canceled and no replacement hearing was scheduled at that time; its meeting date is internally inconsistent. Final legal scope and later force remain unverified.

  • Sectors: data center
  • Record ID: nv-white-pine-county-2026
  • Authority and source: Resolution No. 2026-18 (White Pine County), imposing a temporary moratorium on acceptance, processing, and approval of land use applications for data center development, under NRS 244.137-244.146 and NRS 278.250/278.260
  • What prompted it: Preemptive pause after White Pine County was identified at an April 24, 2026 Nevada Legislature Joint Interim Committee on Public Lands meeting as a target for data center development (with BLM officials present), combined with concerns over the Steptoe Valley aquifer, limited rural electric infrastructure, and the lack of data-center-specific zoning standards in Title 17.

  • Status notes: Resolution 2026-18 was adopted and took effect July 22, 2026. It specifies 180 calendar days, subject to earlier termination upon Board adoption of regulations or one qualifying extension. The row previously reported active status as of September 23, 2026; later formal action and present legal force are not established. [VERIFY: later regulations, termination or extension and current legal force.]
  • Outcome: White Pine County Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution 2026-18, a 180-day moratorium on data center land use applications, directing staff to study water, power, and infrastructure impacts and draft data-center-specific zoning regulations.
