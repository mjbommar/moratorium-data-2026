Nye County

  • Type: County
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Date enacted: 2026-06-02 (Nye County Board of County Commissioners unanimously approved a countywide moratorium on new data center applications at its Tuesday, June 2, 2026 meeting, per Pahrump Valley Times)
  • Normalized date: 2026-06-02 (exact)
  • Duration: No fixed term; Commissioner Strickland was quoted estimating a new data center ordinance could be ready 'in about 90 days,' but the moratorium's stated end condition is adoption of local data center regulations, not a calendar date.
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center, cryptocurrency mining
  • Moratorium ID: nv-nye-county-2026
  • Legal basis: Board of County Commissioners resolution under NRS 244.110/244.119
  • What prompted it: Nye County Water District Governing Board's 2026-05-27 emergency request that the commission impose a moratorium, driven by concerns over data center water demand on a 'critically over-appropriated' aquifer (Nevada Hydrographic Basin 162) in the Pahrump Regional Planning District
  • Affected projects: Proposed 'Blackjack Data Center' and a large Pahrump-area data center proposal covered in local reporting through July 2026
  • Detailed status: Active as of 2026-07-31. A follow-on permanent ordinance (Nye County Bill No. 2026-11, adding Nye County Code Chapter 17.13 'Data Centers,' a prohibition in unincorporated areas without an elected town board) had a public hearing scheduled for 2026-08-04; that draft bill's recitals state the Board 'has recently approved a temporary moratorium on data centers while permanent regulations are developed,' corroborating that the June moratorium remained in force as of the bill's July 7, 2026 agenda item.
  • Outcome: Countywide moratorium on new data center applications adopted; permanent prohibition ordinance in process
