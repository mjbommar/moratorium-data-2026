SB212 / LC0292

  • Policy ID: mt-sb212-lc0292
  • Sponsor / party: Sen. Daniel Zolnikov; R
  • Recorded status: Signed by Governor 2025-04-16, Chapter 150 assigned 2025-04-17
  • Provisions: Right to Compute Act. Enrolled section 3 limits government restrictions infringing property/free-expression rights to those demonstrably necessary and narrowly tailored to a compelling government interest. Section 4 requires a reasonable risk-management policy after deploying a critical AI system controlling critical infrastructure; a plan prepared under federal requirements constitutes compliance. Section 7(2)(d) includes prevention or abatement of common-law nuisances created by physical datacenter infrastructure within compelling government interests. This is not a moratorium or blanket exemption from regulation. Later amendments, implementation and current force remain unverified.
  • Mechanism / scope: other; statewide
  • Legal effect: unknown
  • Effective date: 2025-04-16
  • End condition and limitations: Enrolled section 10 provides effect on passage and approval; April 16, 2025 is derived from that clause and the retained official governor-signature event. No sunset is specified in the reviewed enrolled act. Later amendments and present force were not checked.
  • Primary reference: Official source
