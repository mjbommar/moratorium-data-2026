Austin County

  • Type: County
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Recorded date: 2026-07-27, Austin County Commissioners Court; KWHI reports unanimous adoption following a July 13 public hearing. The signed order bears the handwritten adoption date July 27, 2026 and provides for immediate effect upon adoption.
  • Date field: 2026-07-27 (exact)
  • Duration: Until the comprehensive health and safety review is complete, under the signed order; no fixed term is specified. Subsequent completion or other later action has not been checked.
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center, battery storage
  • Record ID: tx-austin-county-2026
  • Authority and source: Order of Austin County, Texas, Regarding Battery Energy Storage Systems and Data Centers Health and Safety Review, adopted by the Commissioners Court on 2026-07-27 under Texas Health and Safety Code section 121.003 and Texas Local Government Code section 352.016, among other authorities. The signed, executed order was located directly on austincounty.com (distinct from the earlier unsigned draft). It suspends county permit, plan-review, and inspection approvals for BESS and data center construction in unincorporated areas until a 'Comprehensive Health and Safety Review' (covering fire/life-safety, cybersecurity/foreign-adversary equipment verification tied to the Lone Star Infrastructure Protection Act and the Governor's Prohibited Technologies List, and an Energy Development Zone siting study) is complete, with the Commissioners Court retaining sole discretion over when that review is finished. It requires County Fire Marshal plan review/approval and an independent third-party engineering evaluation before construction, and authorizes the County's legal counsel to seek injunctive relief against violators.
  • What prompted it: Comprehensive health and safety review of AI data centers and battery energy storage systems
  • Affected projects: —
  • Status notes: Active as of 2026-09-23. The Commissioners Court unanimously adopted the signed order on 2026-07-27 following the July 13, 2026 public hearing; KWHI (2026-07-28) independently confirms the unanimous vote and describes the order's scope (equipment-manufacturer audits, an Energy Development Zone siting study by the County Fire Marshal and County Engineer, and injunctive-relief enforcement authority). No fixed term applies; the order remains in effect, by its own terms, until the Commissioners Court (in consultation with the County Fire Marshal) determines the Comprehensive Health and Safety Review is complete. No repeal, rescission, or completion of the review was found in sources reviewed through 2026-09-23. KWHI notes Austin County is 'the second county in Texas to adopt a moratorium of this nature,' after Hill County (which later rescinded its moratorium following a developer lawsuit; see the existing tx-hill-county-2026 row).
  • Outcome: —
