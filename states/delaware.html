SB 312

  • Policy ID: de-sb-312
  • Sponsor / party: Sen. Poore; Rep. Burns; Rep. Heffernan; Rep. Minor-Brown; others; D
  • Recorded status: Signed and effective 2026-08-26 (85 Del. Laws ch. 447).
  • Provisions: Amends Title 9, Title 22, and Title 29 of the Delaware Code relating to data centers. Prohibits state agencies, counties, and municipalities from entering into nondisclosure agreements that would restrict public access to information about potential or actual large-scale data center development (defined by reference to facilities capable of using 100+ MW), aiming to preserve local transparency and public input into data-center siting decisions.
