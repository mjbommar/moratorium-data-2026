HB 259 (34th Leg., 2026)

  • Policy ID: ak-hb-259-34th-leg-2026
  • Sponsor / party: Rep. Donna Mears; Democrat
  • Status: Heard and held in House Energy Committee 2026-02-03; BASIS listed (H) ENE, then CRA, status date 2026-01-20 as observed 2026-11-07. Final disposition unresolved.
  • Provisions: Not a moratorium. The introduced bill would regulate "large energy use facilities" through RCA-approved utility contracts, cost-causation rules, long-term service commitments, direct assignment of facility-driven utility costs, and municipal community benefit agreements before contract approval. The introduced threshold is 20 MW peak electric demand or 20 percent of prior-year utility electricity sales, or 2 billion standard cubic feet of gas annually or 20 percent of prior-year gas sales, whichever is smaller for gas. The contract section excludes utility service to another public utility.

SB 250 / CSSB 250(CRA) (34th Leg., 2026)

  • Policy ID: ak-sb-250-cssb-250-cra-34th-leg-2026
  • Sponsor / party: Sen. Loki Tobin; Democrat
  • Status: Passed Senate 2026-05-16 (Y14 N5 E1 on reconsideration) as CSSB 250(CRA); transmitted to House 2026-05-16, read first time and referred to House Community & Regional Affairs 2026-05-17; heard and held 2026-05-18. BASIS listed (H) CRA with status date 2026-05-17 as observed 2026-11-07; final disposition unresolved.
  • Provisions: Not a moratorium. The committee substitute would directly regulate data centers and utility service. It would require data centers to pay facility-driven utility costs, provide financial security for utility capital investments above $1 million, use contracts that avoid fuel-supply and reliability risk shifting, include backup power and reliability planning, execute municipal CBAs, address waste heat, water use, broadband interconnection, digital equity, and file decommissioning and recycling plans. The proposed contract and decommissioning sections do not apply to data centers consuming both less than 15 MW peak electric demand from an electric utility and less than 1.5 billion standard cubic feet of gas annually; the separate utility cost-recovery provision does not state that exemption.
  • Mechanism / scope: utility_large_load_restriction; statewide
  • Legal effect: unknown

  • End condition and limitations: —
  • Primary reference: Official source
