City of Chesapeake

  • Type: City
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Date enacted: 2026-07-21 (City Council adopted Resolution 26-R-042 by a 9-0 vote)
  • Normalized date: 2026-07-21 (exact)
  • Duration: 8 months from adoption, or until City Council takes final action on comprehensive plan amendment PLN-COMP-2026-002, whichever occurs first
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center
  • Moratorium ID: va-city-of-chesapeake-2026
  • Legal basis: Resolution 26-R-042, adopted by Chesapeake City Council 9-0 on July 21, 2026
  • What prompted it: Council sought to pause discretionary review of data center land-use applications while comprehensive plan amendment PLN-COMP-2026-002 is pending
  • Affected projects: No single named project; applies citywide to discretionary data center land-use applications pending before the Planning Commission or City Council.
  • Detailed status: Active as of the July 31, 2026 research date. The resolution temporarily defers Planning Commission recommendations and City Council consideration of discretionary land-use applications (e.g., rezonings, conditional use permits) for proposed data centers, for 8 months from adoption (to approximately 2027-03-21) or until Council takes final action on PLN-COMP-2026-002, whichever occurs first. It does not stop administrative or by-right processing.
  • Outcome: Enacted deferral of discretionary data center land-use applications pending completion of a related comprehensive plan amendment.
