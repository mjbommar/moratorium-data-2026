HB0064 (2013)

  • Policy ID: wy-hb0064-2013
  • Sponsor / party: Joint Minerals, Business and Economic Development Interim Committee; Committee sponsor
  • Recorded status: Enacted as 2013 Wyoming Session Laws Chapter 29 (CH0029); adopted 2013-02-14, effective 2013-07-01, chapter number assigned 2013-02-15
  • Provisions: Historical co-location expansion of the data-processing-services-center exemption. Retrieved 39-15-105(a)(viii)(S)(IV) allows entities occupying a data-center facility to satisfy subdivision(III) requirements in aggregate regardless of multiple ownerships of buildings and equipment. The $2 million annual-purchase threshold and $5 million/$50 million capital tiers, applicable location/retention and record conditions, and Business Council job certification still matter. The Business Council handout describes certification and Department of Revenue qualification; this is not automatic eligibility for every co-located facility.
  • Mechanism / scope: incentive_support; fiscal
  • Legal effect: unknown
  • Effective date: 2013-07-01
  • End condition and limitations: The retrieved Title39 compilation retains the conditional data-center exemption at 39-15-105(a)(viii)(S). SF0079/2026 Chapter19 consolidates use-tax administration into Chapter15 and repeals Chapter16 generally effective July1,2026; its section2 alone is delayed to July1,2027. The old separate use-tax section is not current code, and its repeal is not itself abolition of the data-center exemption. No sunset is stated in the reviewed data-center clause; individual eligibility, comprehensive amendment lineage, rulings and litigation remain unverified.
  • Primary reference: Official source

HB0067 (2010)

  • Policy ID: wy-hb0067-2010
  • Sponsor / party: Rep. Peter Illoway; R (WY House Republican)
  • Recorded status: Enacted as 2010 Wyoming Session Laws Chapter 50 (CH0050); adopted and effective 2010-03-05, chapter number assigned 2010-03-08
  • Provisions: Created the historical data-processing-center sales/use-tax exemption framework. Retrieved 39-15-101(a)(xliv) defines the physical server/storage infrastructure class. Current 39-15-105(a)(viii)(S)(I),(III) covers qualifying software/computer equipment when annual qualifying purchases exceed $2 million, with at least $5 million capital investment or the specified five-year lookback alternative, physical-location/equipment-retention, records and Business Council job-certification conditions. This is statutory eligibility text, not a finding that any taxpayer qualifies. Later2026 administration was consolidated under Chapter15.
  • Mechanism / scope: incentive_support; fiscal
  • Legal effect: unknown
  • Effective date: 2010-03-05
  • End condition and limitations: The retrieved Title39 compilation retains the conditional data-center exemption at 39-15-105(a)(viii)(S). SF0079/2026 Chapter19 consolidates use-tax administration into Chapter15 and repeals Chapter16 generally effective July1,2026; its section2 alone is delayed to July1,2027. The old separate use-tax section is not current code, and its repeal is not itself abolition of the data-center exemption. No sunset is stated in the reviewed data-center clause; individual eligibility, comprehensive amendment lineage, rulings and litigation remain unverified.
