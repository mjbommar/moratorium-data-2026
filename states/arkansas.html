  • Status: Acts 173 and 174 approved and effective 2024-05-03; legislative notifications 2024-05-09
  • Provisions: In 2024 Act 173 added noise-reduction requirements for covered digital asset mining businesses and barred local ordinances prohibiting or requiring prior approval for home mining. Act 174 created state permitting for covered mining businesses with an initial-rules transition and preserved consistent local regulation in areas not otherwise addressed. Both addressed prohibited foreign ownership. Later amendments and current force remain unverified.
