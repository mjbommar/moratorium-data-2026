Butler County

  • Type: County
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Date enacted: 2026-03-16, Butler County Board of Supervisors, 4-1 roll-call vote (Griess, Vandenberg, Bauer, Steager aye; Krafka nay)
  • Normalized date: 2026-03-16 (exact)
  • Duration: 9 months from adoption (2026-03-16)
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: wind, solar, battery storage, data center, general
  • Moratorium ID: ne-butler-county-2026
  • Legal basis: Resolution 2026-07 (Butler County Board of Supervisors), mirroring the Planning Commission's Resolution 2026-04 but striking language that would have excluded projects already under construction
  • What prompted it: Butler County has no zoning; a countywide survey (~800 responses) found roughly 90% support for a moratorium on the top land uses of concern (wind, solar, data centers, battery storage, landfill), following years of unregulated development including the in-progress Sandhills Energy solar project.
  • Affected projects: Sandhills Energy solar project (in development since 2019, construction reportedly started February 2026, ~$15M invested/$100M in equipment orders per company representative Brian Boerner) was initially covered by the moratorium as adopted, then later exempted after the company threatened litigation.
  • Detailed status: Active as of 2026-09-23, though modified: adopted 2026-03-16 as a 9-month moratorium (through approximately 2026-12-15) on wind, solar, data center, battery storage, and landfill land uses while the county completes a comprehensive plan and zoning regulations (targeted for completion around September 2026). Per Flatwater Free Press, after the threat of a lawsuit the county later exempted the ongoing Sandhills Energy solar project (which had been under development since 2019) from the moratorium.
  • Outcome: 9-month countywide land-use moratorium adopted covering wind, solar, data centers, battery storage, and landfills, later amended to exempt an in-progress solar project.

  • Moratorium ID: ne-cass-county-2026

  • What prompted it: specific_project trigger (Tenaska/Google-linked proposal: Tenaska optioned 1,354 acres in Cass County along Highway 75 south of Murray and Beaver Lake for a data center/natural-gas power plant project) combined with a regulatory gap; a separate set of proposed zoning regulations was tabled to Sept. 22, 2026 for further discussion.

A companion set of proposed data-center zoning regulations was tabled the same day to the Board's Sept. 22, 2026 meeting.

Fillmore County

  • Type: County
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Date enacted: 2026-06-23, Fillmore County Board of Supervisors, 6-1 roll-call vote
  • Normalized date: 2026-06-23 (exact)
  • Duration: 6 months from the date of approval (2026-06-23)
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: solar
  • Moratorium ID: ne-fillmore-county-2026
  • Legal basis: Resolution No. 2026-08 (Fillmore County Board of Supervisors)
  • What prompted it: Resident concerns raised at public comment about health, safety, and property-value impacts of proposed solar energy farms.
  • Affected projects: None named; framed as a proactive regulatory review rather than a response to a specific pending application.
  • Detailed status: Active as of 2026-09-23. Resolution No. 2026-08, adopted 2026-06-23 on a 6-1 vote, imposes a 6-month moratorium on accepting new conditional-use-permit applications for solar energy farms (through approximately 2026-12-23) and directs the Planning Commission to review the comprehensive plan/zoning regulations (setbacks, infrasound, health concerns, property-value impacts, 'occupied dwelling' definition, etc.) and report recommendations within 4 months. Existing operations and already-issued pending permits are not affected.
  • Outcome: 6-month moratorium on new solar farm applications adopted while the Planning Commission reviews and recommends zoning changes.

  • Legal basis: Board of Supervisors resolution (specific resolution number not located in sources reviewed)

  • Detailed status: Active as of 2026-09-23. Supervisors said the moratorium could be extended if more time is needed.
  • Outcome: 12-month data center permit moratorium adopted to allow time to develop county-specific data center regulations; supersedes the county's prior 8-month moratorium from August 2026.

  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center
  • Moratorium ID: ne-otoe-county-2026
