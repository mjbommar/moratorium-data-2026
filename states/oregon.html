HB 4084 (2026)

  • Policy ID: or-hb-4084-2026
  • Sponsor / party: Introduced at the request of Gov. Tina Kotek for the Office of the Governor; Democratic administration bill
  • Status: Enacted; Chapter 50 (2026 Laws); Governor signed 2026-03-31; effective 2026-06-05
  • Provisions: Creates a Joint Permitting Council and fast-track permitting program for eligible economic development projects. As enacted, it also restricts enterprise-zone treatment for data centers: the extended exemption period may not apply to qualified property used in operating a data center, flexible hiring alternatives do not apply to data center property, and an eligible business firm may not be authorized for property consisting of a data center project before 90 days after the 2027 regular session adjourns. This is a tax-incentive authorization moratorium, not a construction moratorium.
  • Mechanism / scope: incentive_restriction; statewide
  • Legal effect: unknown
  • Effective date: 2026-06-05
  • End condition and limitations: Section 11b bars enterprise-zone authorization for data-center project property before 90 days after the 2027 regular legislative session adjourns sine die; section 11b is repealed January 2, 2029. These are provision-specific, not whole-act expiration dates.
  • Primary reference: Official source
