No Land Use Code amendments addressing data centers had been adopted as of that extension; a public survey that closed 2026-06-12 reportedly found 94% of respondents opposed to data center construction.
  • Outcome: County is drafting Land Use Code definitions and standards and opened a public survey through 2026-06-12.

Logan County

  • Type: County
  • Status: ⚪ Replaced
  • Date enacted: 2025-10-21
  • Normalized date: 2025-10-21 (exact)
  • Duration: 180 days as adopted; replaced by Resolution 2026-5 (effective 2026-02-17) before the full term elapsed
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center, battery storage, solar, wind
  • Moratorium ID: co-logan-county-2025
  • Legal basis: Resolution 2025-25
  • What prompted it: County needed time to draft regulations before accepting or processing applications for data center, battery, solar, and wind facilities; meeting records cited grid, infrastructure, agricultural land, and land use compatibility concerns.
  • Affected projects: County Commissioners' October 14, 2025 work-session minutes record a briefing by developer Blake Marcus (Granite Renewables) and consultant Ben Gager (SWCA Environmental Consultants) describing a proposed project east and west of County Road 9 (approximately 21 miles northwest of Sterling) at close to 2 GW nameplate capacity, including 1 GW of on-site natural gas generation, 630 MW of solar as ancillary power, and 1,800 MWh of battery storage. Commissioner Yahn noted the moratorium had already been planned before this specific proposal surfaced. As of a January 2026 hearing, commissioners stated no formal permit application had yet been received.
  • Detailed status: Replaced. All four facility types covered by Resolution 2025-25 were superseded by their own permanent regulation resolutions, each with an explicit rescission clause tied back to Resolution 2025-25: Resolution 2026-4 (Battery Energy Storage Systems, effective 2026-02-17), Resolution 2026-5 (Data Center Facilities, effective 2026-02-17), Resolution 2026-6 (Solar Energy / Community Solar Garden, effective 2026-02-17), and Resolution 2026-11 (Wind Energy Facilities, adopted 2026-04-07, shortly before the moratorium's own ~2026-04-19 lapse date). None of the four components lapsed unaddressed.
  • Outcome: Resolution 2026-5 adopted Data Center Facility regulations. The county planning page lists Resolution 2026-5 among amended regulations effective 2026-02-17 and 2026-04-07.
