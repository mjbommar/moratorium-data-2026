Box Elder County

  • Type: County
  • Status: 🟢 Active
  • Recorded date: County Commission unanimously adopted Ordinance 654 on 2026-06-10.
  • Date field: 2026-06-10 (exact)
  • Duration: 180-day temporary land-use regulation on data centers, data center power plants, and associated digital infrastructure in areas where the county retains land-use authority. Stops new land-use and business-license applications, building permits, administrative appeals, conditional use permits, subdivisions, zone changes, site plans, development agreements, and similar approvals. Applications formally determined complete before the effective date may proceed; incomplete covered applications are suspended.
  • Current end date: —
  • Sectors: data center
  • Record ID: ut-box-elder-county-2026
  • Authority and source: Ordinance 654, Box Elder County Commission
  • What prompted it: Adopted while the county develops updated data center regulations, in areas outside the Military Installation Development Authority's Stratos project area.
  • Affected projects: Explicitly does not apply to the Stratos project (MIDA-controlled project area)
  • Status notes: Active as of 2026-07-31; 180-day pause from 2026-06-10. County officials expressly stated it does not affect the Stratos project, because the Military Installation Development Authority (MIDA), not the county, controls land use within that project area.
