# Tennessee adjournment-source gap cleared for six records

[SJR1280](https://capitol.tn.gov/Bills/114/Bill/SJR1280.pdf) provides regular-session sine-die adjournment at close of business in both houses on April 23, 2026. Its [official history](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default.aspx?BillNumber=SJR1280&ga=114) records Senate adoption and House concurrence on April 23. April 30 enrollment and May 5/6 speaker signatures are distinct later events.

The [April 23 House journal](https://capitol.tn.gov/bills/114/House/Journals/04232026RD65.pdf) records the Senate adopted-resolution message, House concurrence on printed page 5083 (PDF105), and actual House sine-die declaration on printed page 5097 (PDF119). Those two pages and the resolution page were visually inspected.

The discovery index described an older unofficial draft. The actual November 9 capture has no UNOFFICIAL marker in its layout and includes later administrative appendices. No separate journal-adoption date is asserted; the resolution and official action history corroborate the date. May extraordinary-session adjournments and authorized post-adjournment committee investigation are separate from this regular-session action.

The retained independent reviews had already assessed the historical actions and proposed provisions for seven bill pairs. Exact sine-die documentation was the sole remaining gap for HB1461/SB1832, HB2054/SB1999, HB2061/SB1682, HB2392/SB2653, HB2456/SB2112 and HB2463/SB2681. [The adjudications](tennessee-session-adjudications.json) clear those six holds and preserve the original source observation clocks. HB2047/SB2584 remains open for SA0665 adoption and analytical-scope questions; the shared adjournment evidence does not answer those questions.
