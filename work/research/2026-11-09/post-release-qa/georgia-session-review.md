# Georgia session-end evidence and two scoped closures

The [2026 House journal](https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/document/docs/default-source/house-journal-document-library/2026-regular-session-house-journal.pdf), printed page 5009 (PDF page 5013), records the successful motion and actual sine-die adjournment on April 2. The page was rendered and visually inspected. The Senate-created [April 2 status sheet](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_ggpd_1019824201-2026-04-02), preserved by the state library, independently records Senate adjournment.

The [Georgia courts session summary](https://georgiacourts.gov/enacted-legislation/) confirms that 2026 was the second biennium year and unpassed legislation is not eligible for passage in 2027. This is an official explanatory source, not an assertion that each bill history literally says failed.

The House journal numerical tabulation lists HB559 with No Action in 2026 (PDF page 5326) and HB1012 with references only to printed pages 102 and 129 (PDF page 5345). Combined with their retained official bill histories and prior independent provision assessments, these sources clear the original terminal/session-end hold for those two named proposals. Failure is an inference from the combined sources; their existing failed categories do not change.
