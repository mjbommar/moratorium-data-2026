# South Dakota enacted-law follow-up

**Recommendation:** Make the guarded historical and source corrections for both records, including a qualified inferred effective date of July 1, 2026. Keep `legal_effect_status` as `unknown` and both whole records unresolved pending current-force checks. The accompanying private adjudications specify the exact old and proposed values and row guards; no dataset changes were applied.

The complete official session-law copies of [HB 1038, chapter 185](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/SessionLaws/306640.pdf), and [SB 135, chapter 190](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/SessionLaw/306613.pdf?Year=2026), each state that the Governor signed on **March 24, 2026**. The [final session index](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/286219.pdf) records each signature in an entry dated **March 30**. The [session-laws foreword](https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/Session_Laws/Foreword/71) authenticates the 2026 volume as true and correct copies subject to clerical errors, dated **May 1**. These official source copies were captured on **November 9**, a separate observation date.

Neither complete act specifies an effective date. [SDCL 2-14-16](https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/2-14-16) provides July 1 following passage for a regular-session act that prescribes no date, subject to constitutional and statutory provisions concerning vetoes and referendum. July 1, 2026 is therefore an **inferred, conditional default**, not a date printed in either act or proof of actual current effect. If entered in the date field, the accompanying status must retain that qualification and the lack of current-force verification.

- **HB 1038:** Chapter 185 authorizes assessment of actual costs of processing specified requests for approval involving a public-utility data-center customer with peak demand of at least 10 MW; the definition includes digital-currency mining. The index identifies the chair of House Commerce and Energy, at the Public Utilities Commission's request, as sponsor. The index confirms House passage 60–5, Senate passage as amended 32–2, and House concurrence 67–0 on March 10. The [rendered chapter](https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/Session_Laws/Chapter/33905) links directly to the reviewed PDF.
- **SB 135:** Chapter 190 covers separate electric-service terms and fairly attributed costs, local regulatory authority, water-provider notice and determinations, water allocation limits, and semiannual reporting. The index identifies Senator Chris Karr and Representative Jon Hansen as prime sponsors, with additional co-sponsors; its member listings establish their names, and another listing identifies Hansen as Speaker. It confirms Senate passage as amended 34–0, House passage as amended 60–7, and Senate concurrence 33–1 on March 10.

The remaining questions are whether a referendum or other applicable constitutional or statutory circumstance changed the inferred dates, and whether subsequent amendments, repeal, litigation, or other developments affect the provisions' current force. The 2026 index verifies the recorded session histories; it does not establish that later laws were checked. All six cited source captures' body and text hashes were verified against their receipts, and the index layout was regenerated byte-for-byte from the verified PDF.
