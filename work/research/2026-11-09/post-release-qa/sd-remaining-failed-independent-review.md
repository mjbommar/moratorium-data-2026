# South Dakota: four 2026 failed bills

The retained [final session index](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/286219.pdf), bill texts and committee minutes support closing all four named-bill records. Recommend two party-cell corrections and two unchanged records. These conclusions concern the named 2026 proposals and their recorded session histories; they do not certify current law or assess successor legislation.

| Bill | Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| HB 1198 | Index PDF page 148 records a 9–4 committee vote on February 11 and a 21–41 House floor failure on February 12, citing House Journal 321. John Sjaarda is the sole listed prime sponsor; the member listing identifies him as Republican. The [introduced text](https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/27162/299234) supports the permit and 20 MW summary. The accepted analytical scope review excluded this generally framed high-energy facility bill from the data-center-specific scope. | Confirm unchanged. |
| HB 1246 | Index PDF page 161 records a 9–3 committee vote on February 18 and a 30–37 House floor failure on February 20, citing House Journal 391. John Hughes is the Republican prime; Senator Jamie Smith, a listed cosponsor, is a Democrat. The [introduced text](https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/27243/299800) supports the nondisclosure summary and also specifies public-record treatment. | Replace the unresolved party annotation with **R lead; bipartisan cosponsors**. |
| SB 127 | Index PDF page 49 records adoption of amendment 127A and a 5–3 deferral to the 41st legislative day on February 18, with no later action listed. Taffy Howard is the Republican prime and Senator Red Dawn Foster is a Democratic cosponsor. The [introduced text](https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/26300/297147) and [committee minutes](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/302307.pdf) together support the 10 MW peak threshold, residence or residential-area setback, residential-area property-line noise measurement and nuisance summary. | Replace the unresolved party annotation with **R lead; bipartisan cosponsors**. |
| SB 128 | Index PDF page 49 records adoption of amendment 128B and a 5–3 deferral to the 41st legislative day on February 18, with no later action listed. Taffy Howard is the Republican prime. The [introduced text](https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/26547/297148) and [minutes](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/302307.pdf) support the water measures, corrected cross-reference, 10 MW anticipated peak definition and additional utility cost protections. The definition retains the introduced post-July-1-2026 establishment qualifier. | Confirm unchanged. |

The index says the session concluded March 30, 2026. Its member headings identify Sjaarda, Hughes, Howard, Foster and Smith and their parties; Howard's [2026 profile](https://sdlegislature.gov/Legislators/Profile/4763/Detail) independently confirms her identity and party. The index and rules were captured November 9, 2026 UTC; the committee meeting and legislative actions occurred in February. These dates describe different events.

Under [joint rules](https://mylrc.sdlegislature.gov/api/Documents/295722.pdf) 7-19 and 7-19.1, a committee could recall a deferred bill under the specified voting and timing conditions. The final index supplies the post-session history supporting the bounded failed dispositions; deferral alone would not establish that no recall was possible. An independently engrossed SB 128 text was not located, but the adopted amendment and introduced text directly support the claims above. The private adjudications retain exact row guards, proposed old and new cells, source hashes, capture times and locators. The index, original extracted text and separately derived layout were verified against the retained files.
