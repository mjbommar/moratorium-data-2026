# California independent evidence audit

**Recommendation: confirmed unchanged for all three records, with closure of the named historical-bill questions.** The reviewed bill versions support the provision and scope descriptions; dated official legislative analyses support party; official member pages corroborate the full author names; and the earlier bounded procedural findings are retained. No cell changes are proposed. This recommendation does not certify current law, successor legislation, or freshness beyond the earlier procedural observations.

- **AB 1095 (2025–2026):** The [April 21, 2025 amended bill](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB1095), section 1 and digest, supports the waste-heat financing summary and identifies Assembly Member Papan. The [official member page](https://papan.asmdc.org/) identifies Assemblymember Diane Papan. The dated AB 93 Senate floor analysis identifies Papan (D); both bills identify Assembly Member Papan as their author in the same 2025–2026 session and were introduced in 2025. The party evidence is direct for AB 93 and applied to AB 1095 through that shared legislative identity.
- **AB 93 (2025–2026):** The [September 15, 2025 enrolled bill](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB93), sections 2–3 and 5, supports the water-use and statewide-scope descriptions. The annual-use report is linked to renewal certification; the text does not itself establish a separate annual submission schedule. The Senate Rules Committee floor analysis, analysis 393031, page 1, directly identifies Papan (D) for the September 5, 2025 amendment; it is available through the [official analysis index](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billAnalysisClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB93). The member page supplies the full name, not historical party.
- **SB 1298 (2023–2024):** The [August 19, 2024 amended bill](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB1298), digest and sections 1 and 3, supports the proposed thermal-powerplant certification exemption and associated backup-generation and air district provisions. Senate Rules Committee floor analysis 374262, page 1, identifies Cortese (D) for the April 22, 2024 amendment; the [official analysis index](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billAnalysisClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB1298) links it. The earlier analysis is used for party only; the August bill version supplies the material-content evidence. The [official member page](https://sd15.senate.ca.gov/) identifies Senator Dave Cortese and is used for his full name, not historical party.
