# HB2047/SB2584 scope resolved; adoption remains open

The [official bill page](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default.aspx?BillNumber=HB2047&ga=114), re-captured November 9, directly describes qualified-data-center certification and links [SA0665](https://capitol.tn.gov/Bills/114/Amend/SA0665.pdf). The retained three-page amendment expressly identifies this bill pair and proposes facility, equipment and incentive-qualification definitions. Every page was rendered and inspected.

The proposed data-center definition includes buildings at one or multiple sites housing high-tech computer systems and related equipment. Its industrial-machinery clauses include mobile data centers and equipment/services used by qualified centers. Its qualified-facility text specifies investment over $100 million, at least 15 net new full-time jobs, wages at least 150% of the state average occupational wage, and a state best-interest determination linked to the exemption and credit. No comparison to the prior statutory baseline is asserted.
