# Lochbuie: independent identity and operative-term adjudication

The signed [Ordinance 2026-720](https://www.lochbuie.org/media/17186), pages 1–3, and the [July 21 minutes](https://www.lochbuie.org/media/17221), page 4, resolve the ordinance identity: **2026-720**. The town meeting action summary calls it 2027-720; the executed document and minutes are stronger evidence of the adopted number. The minutes record the five-year motion carried 6–1, with Moya identified; the signed ordinance records six in favor and one against.

Section 2 suspends submission, acceptance, processing, review and approval of covered applications and requests, directs refusal to accept filings, and stops further processing or review of pending applications. Section 3 provides three alternative data-center definitions: the 1 MW and water and wastewater criteria occur within one alternative and are not universal thresholds. Section 6 excludes continued operation of existing centers, but includes expansion. Section 4 starts the five-year term on the effective date, unless terminated earlier or extended by further ordinance; section 8 makes effectiveness 30 days after final passage and publication by title.

The handwritten adoption date is July 21, 2026. The clerk's certification states that the ordinance was adopted then and **ordered published** by title in *The Brighton Blade* on July 30, 2026. The retained text does not itself contain the newspaper issue or unequivocally establish actual publication. If publication occurred July 30, and 30 days are counted as ordinary elapsed days after that date, the resulting effective date would be August 29, 2026, with a five-year anniversary of August 29, 2031, absent earlier termination or extension. These are conditional calculations, not confirmed legal dates. Leave the endpoint blank and retain the ordinance's operative anchor and qualifications.

Its only title after 2026-720 concerns Freestone Street, with no visible later data-center item. Index titles cannot establish completeness or current force. The prior active category should not be flipped solely because subsequent actions have not been researched.
