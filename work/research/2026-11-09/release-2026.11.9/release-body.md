This release publishes the current reviewed dataset **with pending QA**. Further source checks and corrections continue. It does not claim universally verified current legal force or a passed experimental extractor.

## Current data

- **1,330 local records across 47 states; 447 state-policy observations.**
- **57 ballot measures**, with 64 retained result versions and 53 current observations across 52 measures; five current observations are explicitly certified.
- All **1,777 raw records** have primary and independent review artifacts. The full disposition ledger retains **1,501 bounded unresolved, 95 corrected-resolved, 180 confirmed-unchanged and one superseded duplicate**, with zero not-attempted.

## Since the September 23 snapshot

Local records increase from **1,291 to 1,330**: 40 additions, including historical backfills, and one reviewed Seattle duplicate retirement whose original identity/history remains available. State-policy records increase from **440 to 447**.

**95 existing local records change recorded status**; 63 state-policy records change legislative category. Recorded active/extended local rows rise from 1,063 to **1,089**, pending falls from 77 to **64**, and recorded past rises from 151 to **171**. Two rejected and four unknown stages are now represented separately. These changes mix reported events and corrections, not solely new post-September enactments.

Dates, terms, scope, instrument descriptions and source links were corrected. Current sector maps use recorded arrays and preserve multi-sector membership; historical legal maps carry a visible April-edition notice. Tables, summaries and living copy are updated. Historical manuscripts, book editions and project/disclosure outputs retain their own dates.

## Qualifications and ongoing QA

Unresolved questions concern source authority, identity, adopted wording, terms, later history, implementation and current force. Electoral approval, certification and operative restrictions remain separate. Held frozen candidate payloads are not additional disjoint contests. There are **979 verification-marked local rows**; unflagged does not guarantee legal force.

The event cutoff is **November 7, 2026 at 13:38:39.877065 UTC**. Later acquisition/review timestamps remain actual. Experimental extractor trials failed their gates; none of their outputs was automatically promoted. The user explicitly authorized publication with these qualifications while QA continues.
