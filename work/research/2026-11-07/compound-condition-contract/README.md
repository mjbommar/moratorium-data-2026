# Compound condition extraction contract revision

The frozen compound-chunk experiment remains failed. No candidates are promoted and no extraction-quality improvement is established by this revision.

Independent Codex CLI diagnosis with gpt-6.1-terra identified an instruction conflict: the extractor was told to separate prohibition, procedure and exemption clauses, while the frozen completeness rubric required the governing rule, including material exceptions and prerequisites, in one clause. Mandan prohibition and Harwood applicability outputs exhibited this split. Other failures were genuine omissions or unresolved interpretations, so the conflict does not explain every failure or establish a causal effect size.

The revised instructions require each governing clause to retain its material definitions, prerequisites, scope, exceptions and discretionary qualifications. Additional purpose clauses may coexist but cannot replace a complete governing clause. Each condition-clause fact contains one complete clause, permitting independent review. Supporting quotations cannot replace missing structured predicates. Missing context remains unresolved; fabricated quotes are prohibited. Recursive expressions and source-qualified text predicates suffice for the observed requirements; no schema change is justified by this diagnosis.

The frozen outputs, review judgments, scores, protocol and budgets are unchanged. Default and whole-text policies remain below the frozen support and coverage gates. Reviews are source-informed AI judgments with retained disagreements, not human gold. Any future provider evaluation needs a separately frozen protocol and budget, with development sources distinguished from an unseen holdout. Prior paid-call reservations are not renewed.
