# Louisiana policy source review

Applied **21 field corrections across all five existing Louisiana policy records**, after two primary and independent terra CLI reviews, 95 field decisions, all 19 exact live guards per row and 24 source/context representation checks. The inventory classification context is frozen separately from legal source bodies. No records were added or removed; census totals are unchanged.

The official final disposition table records HB 1206 and HB 922 as died in House committee. HB 1206 concerns consumptive water use and permits; HB 922 concerns utility cost allocation for qualifying new or expanded demand. Committee consideration dates and draft amendment listings are distinguished from adopted amendments or detailed meeting conclusions.

Act 730's historical rebate, July 1, 2024 effective date, agreement term and optional renewal are distinguished from later Act 11 codification as an exemption. Agreement duration is not a whole-act sunset; current effect of all provisions and the reported Meta/governor connection remain qualified.

HCR 61's House adoption and Senate calendar return are recorded, but final death/noncarryover is unverified; typed disposition and legal effect become unknown while the old claim is preserved in status/audit. Its June 4 reengrossed text is not silently substituted for the later House-amended version. Scope remains unresolved pending the known amendment/adopted documents.

SB 79 adds data-center activity to industrial purposes, rather than automatically designating every center an industrial area. Cooperative-service agreement authority, deleted fire-protection language, existing statutory services and resident safeguards are distinguished through PDF markup. Its historical August 1, 2025 effective date does not prove current force.
