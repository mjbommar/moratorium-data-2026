# Kansas policy source review

Applied **62 field corrections across all twelve existing Kansas policy records**, following three primary and three independent terra CLI reviews, 228 field decisions and a focused amendment/carrier identity supplement. All 19 exact live values were guarded per row and 45 source representations were rehashed. No records were added or removed.

The rejected Murphy Senate floor amendment to HB2433 remains a distinct record under its permanent ID. Its legal form is now `legislative_amendment` and its chamber is Senate; the House carrier's later approval does not enact this rejected text. The Senate journal records rejection without a tally. The newspaper-reported 12–22 vote and Blasi support remain qualified leads, not verified journal facts. The amendment and SB531 propose county drought conditions, a monthly maximum demand threshold and exceptions, rather than an unconditional statewide ban.

This form correction changes typed bill rows **428 → 427** while all-policy rows stay **443**. One amendment, ten resolution rows, three precursor rows and two other typed actions are separately counted. Enacted-category bill rows remain 94. Local moratoria and expanded ballots are unchanged. Schema, apply, validator, codebook and prose-number contracts admit the additional form, and regression tests exercise guarded correction, stale-conflict refusal, new rows, unknown-form rejection and ranking denominators. Final downstream consumer reconciliation remains open.

HB2450 and SB545 proposed incentive support with firm-specific exemption terms, rather than construction pauses. HB2483 and HB2664 proposed different utility/energy packages; sponsor, requester and referral committee are distinguished. HB2728's proposed pause limit is conditional on a hypothetical local moratorium, not a sunset of that bill. SB400's open-loop cooling conditions do not prohibit every cooling system.

The original and substituted versions of SB51 and SB98 are retained as versions of their own instruments; associations do not merge separate bill IDs. Enrolled SB98 excludes electricity from eligible costs, unlike the earlier SB545 proposal. SB92's amendment changes an existing subsection's expiration to 2027; surrounding service and peak-demand rules are existing text. Section or firm-specific terms do not establish whole-act sunsets. Governor approval, program application and publication-based effectiveness remain separate; statute-book publication and current force remain unresolved where not verified.

Source bodies were observed November 5 or November 7 as recorded. Some nominal `.pdf` URLs served HTML and were reviewed using their actual markup.
