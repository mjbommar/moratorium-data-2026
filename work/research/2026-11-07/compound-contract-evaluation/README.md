# Revised complete-condition contract: frozen development experiment

This new experiment tests the revised extraction instructions at `a76ea6a` on the same two reviewed documents and four fixed document/policy cases as the earlier comparison. The extractor remains `openai:gpt-6-sol`; the 7,000/400 and 42,000/400 chunk policies, order, per-case budgets, strict source support and complete-group coverage gates are unchanged. All prior outputs remain failed. This is development, not an unseen holdout, general accuracy estimate or P4 completion. The known slots and source-informed reviews have their original interpretive limitations; no adaptive tuning after freeze.

This is an independent allocation, not a renewal of the old cap: at most 16 HTTP attempts and $12 in estimated reservations, $0.75 per attempt. The locally pinned pricing overlay is an estimate, not an invoice or verified current provider price; unpriced failed attempts retain their reservation. The new private namespace has a 64 MiB guarded storage cap and must have no provider directory before execution. Storage admission and its host preflight are recorded here.

`protocol.json` binds the actual current extraction code, reviewer instructions, scripts, source bodies and text, immutable canonical snapshot inputs, model settings, routing fingerprint, pricing and runtime manifest. The slot criteria and supporting material are unchanged; only their snapshot identifier reflects the current canonical snapshot. The runner stops on hash, runtime, routing, pricing or other invariant drift. The protocol and its pinned files must be independently reviewed and the exact protocol bytes committed before any provider run; the runner checks the committed bytes. No real call is authorized by this preparation.

`offline-validation.json` records two runs through the real controlled transport with a mock HTTP endpoint. The failure fixture retains the failed attempt's reservation and both runs replay without extra calls. Synthetic empty candidates do not measure extraction quality. Review requires the coordinator and two independent reviewers to assess every candidate and all 24 case/slot combinations; summaries and base claims are outside the condition-field score. The source selection, fixed order, single sample, uneven chunk opportunities, AI judgment and nonminimal support windows limit interpretation.
