# Independent source review instructions

Read `protocol.json` and `required-slots.json`, every retained `domain-candidates.private.json`, and both exact retained source texts. Review all candidates, including invalid or off-target ones. Do not use agreement or schema validity as proof of semantic entailment. Do not edit candidates or infer current law, actual adoption, election result or certification from a proposal. Preserve every compound criterion, definition, threshold, unit, exception, scope and aggregation qualification.

Return a JSON object with `protocol_sha256`, a unique `reviewer`, `candidates`, and `slots`. Each candidate row requires `case_id`, `candidate_id`, `disposition` (`supported`, `unsupported`, `ambiguous`, `off_target`), Boolean `high_impact`, nonempty `rationale`, and `source_spans` as exact half-open character offsets in that case's frozen supplied text. Every non-title field is high impact. A supported row requires a supporting span; a located quote alone is not sufficient support. Review the entire candidate value and its assertion context, not only the quoted sentence.

Each of the 24 case/slot combinations requires `case_id`, `slot_id`, Boolean `covered`, `candidate_ids`, `clause_indexes`, nonempty `rationale`, and `source_spans`. Coverage requires exactly one supported condition candidate and exactly one complete zero-based clause index. Do not join partial trees across candidates or clauses. Uncovered slots use empty candidate/index arrays and explain omissions. Two clauses may share a candidate array without being interchangeable.

Mandan's defined Large-Scale predicate and its capacity-or-demand prohibition language must both remain visible, including their unresolved interaction; a bare demand-only ban is insufficient. Harwood major applicability requires the industrial/discretionary gates and any ONE of all five major criteria. Moderate applicability requires the same gates and AT LEAST TWO of all six criteria. Preserve the full water comparison without inventing available capacity. Proposal scope is distinct from adoption and operative effect.

Review native summaries and base claims separately if discussing them: they are not covered by the condition-field score, are not approved copy and must not enter publication through the scored gate. Explain genuine uncertainty and do not manufacture coverage to pass the 95% target.
