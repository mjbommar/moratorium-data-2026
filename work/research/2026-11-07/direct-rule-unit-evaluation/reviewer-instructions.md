# Independent source-informed review

The coordinator and two distinct reviewers work independently and bind their identities and the exact protocol SHA-256. Inspect both complete frozen source texts, the private plans and retained packet, including every planned unit and every returned clause. Keep source text, quotations, plan details and review files private. These are AI judgments, not human gold, legal authority or a source-freshness determination.

Write one JSON object with `protocol_sha256`, nonempty `reviewer`, `units`, and `slots`. For each of the 86 planned units, provide exactly one row with `case_id`, `unit_id`, `disposition` (`supported`, `unsupported`, `ambiguous`, `off_target`, or `unresolved`), `high_impact: true`, `source_extractable` (true, false, or null when unknown), nonempty `rationale`, and `source_spans`. Spans are `[start,end)` character offsets into the entire frozen UTF-8-decoded text, not a chunk; ensure they are in bounds and accurately support the reasoning. Supported requires a returned clause and at least one exact supporting span. Only a unit with no returned clause may be unresolved. A clause remains in the support denominator even when its packet outcome is unresolved because its expression contains unknown. Assess every clause for meaning, all conditions, exceptions, definitions, scope, and unsupported or ambiguous legal assertions; quote and schema validity alone do not establish support. Review source-extractability even for failed, unresolved and unattempted units, and explain omissions and uncertainty. Do not filter candidates after seeing results.

Review each of the 12 case/family entries in the frozen `required-slots.json` using its existing criteria, supporting contexts and interpretive limits. Provide exactly one slot row with `case_id`, `slot_id`, boolean `covered`, `unit_ids`, `clause_indexes`, nonempty `rationale`, and `source_spans`. A covered slot requires exactly one supported unit, `clause_indexes: [0]`, and a nonempty source span: its one complete clause must satisfy the entire criterion. Do not join partial clauses. An uncovered slot has empty `unit_ids` and `clause_indexes`. The criteria are for review only and must not enter extraction prompts.

The auditor uses the coordinator judgments for the strict development gates and reports disagreements between independent reviewers. All generated clauses count, including unknown-expression clauses; zero clauses cannot pass. Report uncertainty rather than assuming legal force, adoption, completeness or human authority. No canonical promotion or P4 closure follows from this review.

A source-reviewed unresolved response without a returned clause is not itself a factual error. It may remain unresolved only when the reviewer establishes `source_extractable: false` with a source rationale. Unknown extractability and omissions of extractable rules still prevent readiness; all twelve required slots and all other strict gates remain required. Every returned clause, including a clause accompanied by an unresolved packet outcome, stays in the support denominator. Abstaining on everything cannot pass the nonzero-clause and required-slot gates.
