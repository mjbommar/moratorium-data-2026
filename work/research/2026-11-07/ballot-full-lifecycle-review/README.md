# Full current ballot-measure lifecycle primary pass

All **53 current measure IDs** have completed retained-evidence primary review in four disjoint cohorts (13, 13, 13 and 14). The [accounting receipt](primary-pass-accounting.json) verifies exact coverage against the pinned canonical snapshot and hashes the private ledgers. Each ledger separately assesses qualification/wording, results/denominators, certification/recount and implementation/current force. Original excerpts, bodies and rendered pages remain private. This accounting verifies population coverage; source-grounded acceptance requires the independent/coordinator review still in progress.

Two reported original-body gaps were lookup failures: Peru's one-page and Nottingham's seven-page ordinance originals are present, hash-matched and re-read by root. The [source-recheck addendum](../ballot-ordinance-source-recheck/README.md) supersedes those availability findings without promoting passage or current force. Other unresolved requests remain field-specific: final wording/placement, official returns where missing, competent certification/recount records, operative text/effective provisions and later amendments/court orders. A signed moderator's result certification, clerk attestation, Planning Board recommendation and current law are distinct evidence categories.

The first 26 measures are in independent review; the remaining 27 still need that step and coordinator reconciliation. No canonical ballot value was changed by these four passes. Reading retained evidence does not advance the source observation date, prove a complete national election census or reconcile every frozen seed. The broader 50-state/all-sector refresh, extraction evaluation, consumer integration and final release remain incomplete.
