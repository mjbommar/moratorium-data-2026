# Nottingham September ballot promotion

This packet promotes the reviewed September 8, 2026 special town election
question, proposed provisions and town-posted counts. The
[source review](../nottingham-ballot-review/README.md) verifies source versions,
proposed clauses and Planning Board signature context.

Accepted scope: Nottingham identity, special-election date and question wording;
a proposed twelve-calendar-month permitting pause from adoption with earlier
legislative-body repeal; its definition, conditional exclusions, geography and
study requirement; and marked-ballot counts of 1,335 yes / 143 no. The derived
1,478 is only their sum, not turnout. This is an earlier election, not a November
3 measure. County identity is unreviewed and null.

The result PDF retains a SAMPLE BALLOT heading. No dated certification, passage
rule, reported/calculated outcome, bindingness, formal referral or operative
legal interval is accepted. Proposal signatures are Planning Board support,
not voter-adoption execution. No new legacy moratorium row is created.

Visual claim receipts use reviewed, reproducible `pdftoppm` 160-DPI renders of
the marked ballot page 1 and proposed ordinance page 2. Exact native-text spans
support duration and study/effectiveness clauses. The definition's extraction
reading-order defects are addressed through visual review, preserving original
native text. This does not claim full bc-evidence extraction coverage.

`identity-plan.json` pins the prior identity registry and allocates opaque IDs.
`publication-plan.json` pins the baseline/candidate snapshots, registry and
field reviews. `visual-reviews.json` includes prior-cohort receipts so the whole
candidate can be rechecked. Private staging, source/render artifacts and the
registry preview remain under `/data0/moratorium-refresh-2026/`.

Schema passes and dry runs alone do not establish canonical publication.
The retained canonical publication receipt is authoritative; GitHub release and
consumer refresh remain separate deliverables.
