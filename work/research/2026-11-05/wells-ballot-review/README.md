The warrant's Article 5, page 5, incorporates Appendix 1 by reference and reports
recommendations of 5-0 from the Board of Selectmen and 7-0 from the Planning
Board. Appendix 1 supplies the proposed submitted text. Its definition requires
the specified primary computing activity **and aggregate Data Center Area of
100,000 square feet or greater**. That defined area includes specified computing
floor space/infrastructure and associated impervious surfaces, including access
ways, parking, transmission corridors and accessory buildings/structures. It
covers qualifying principal and accessory uses, including AI and cryptocurrency
operations. The 100,000 figure is not simply server-room floor area.

Section 4 pauses specified siting, construction/operation, applications and
municipal approvals for qualifying new/expanded data centers. Section 5 anchors
the **180-day duration to June 16, 2026, the Date of Applicability**, unless
extended, repealed or modified. It does not supply a fresh 180-day term starting
at the November vote. Section 8 instead specifies immediate effectiveness upon
Town Meeting enactment. Retroactive coverage, duration anchor and effectiveness
must remain distinct. The conditional permit-nullification clause is retained
as source text, without accepting a legal conclusion about any particular permit.
No operative expiry or exercised extension is inferred.
