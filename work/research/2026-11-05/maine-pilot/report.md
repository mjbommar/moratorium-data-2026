## Peru

The [town's posting](https://townofperumaine.org/temporary-moratorium-of-the-development-and-permitting-of-data-centers-to-be-on-the-november-2026-ballot/) links its [proposed moratorium](https://townofperumaine.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Temporary-Moratorium-of-the-Development-and-Permitting-of-Data-Centers.pdf). The scanned text states that the pause takes effect immediately upon passage and expires 180 days from that effective date unless repealed, extended or replaced. It excludes routine upgrades to existing telecommunication facilities. The [local press report](https://www.sunjournal.com/2026/11/03/peru-backs-food-sovereignty-data-center-ballot-questions/) supplies the result above; it is not a certification. The existing pending inventory record `me-peru-undated` is a likely same-instrument candidate and requires identity review before adding an instrument. The Article 2 designation remains a lead because the official ballot was not recovered. Peru's PDF was OCRed; its September signature date is handwritten and was not relied on.

## Wells

The [town warrant](https://wellstown.org/DocumentCenter/View/8514/November-3-2026-Special-Town-Meeting-Warrant?bidId=) Article 5 asks voters to enact a retroactive data-center moratorium. Its Appendix 1 says it takes effect immediately upon enactment, but applies to covered proceedings and applications pending on or after **June 16, 2026**, and runs 180 days from that applicability date, subject to extension, repeal or modification. Ordinary date addition yields December 13, 2026 as a candidate boundary, not a verified legal expiration or inclusive end date. Do not use election day as the 180-day anchor. The proposed definition sets a threshold of at least 100,000 square feet of aggregate Data Center Area, a defined measure that also includes specified associated impervious surfaces; it is not simply building floor area. It includes specified cryptocurrency and AI activities.

The [town clerk's certificate](https://www.wellstown.org/DocumentCenter/View/8559/November-3-2026-Official-Election-Results?bidId=) is a scan: Article 5 numbers and the November 4 signature date were checked against the page image, not OCR alone. The yes/no count is 7,100, and adding 459 blanks gives 7,559. Its certificate supports the reported vote, while legal effect and any later changes remain subject to review. A separate [agenda attachment](https://www.wellstown.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/6565?fileID=16871) is a commented template with a blank threshold, and should not be mistaken for the filled warrant text or a proven predecessor. No Wells, Maine matching row was identified; Wells, Nevada is a different jurisdiction.

## Wiscasset

The [town's unofficial results](https://www.wiscasset.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/211) identify town Question 2 as the data-center moratorium. The 2,075 yes/no votes do not equal the 2,155 ballots cast; the difference is not characterized as undervotes without a source. The [town voter guide](https://www.wiscasset.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1735/Special-Town-Meeting-Voter-Guide---November-3-2026) describes a six-month moratorium after enactment, applicable to applications filed on or after November 3, with earlier lifting or extensions under stated conditions. It identifies exclusions for equipment incidental to another business, non-expanding maintenance/replacement, and governmental IT. The guide separately identifies a different school question as non-binding; classification of Question 2 as an ordinance rather than advisory remains an inference pending the ballot and full text. Six calendar months must not be silently converted into days. No Wiscasset, Maine matching inventory row was found in the bounded jurisdiction check.
