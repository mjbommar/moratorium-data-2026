# Brownfield and Freedom official-source review

These packets verify retained primary sources for the two remaining Maine census
candidates. They do not publish canonical records or establish current legal force.

## Brownfield

The signed/attested November 3 special-town-meeting warrant labels the moratorium
question **Article 1**. The town-posted tally calls it **Question 1**, reports
**671 yes / 191 no**, and explicitly declares **862 ballots counted**. The ballots
counted statement is a source observation, distinct from arithmetic agreement
with yes plus no. Blanks are not reported and are not filled with zero. The sheet
contains election officials' names/declaration but no separate dated certification.

[Official warrant](https://brownfieldme.civicpluswebopen.com/media/6711),
[official tally image](https://www.brownfieldmaine.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1000/Referendum-Results).

The October 13 draft defines covered primary computing uses with at least
**5,000 square feet aggregate Data Center Area**, including specified associated
impervious surfaces. It then literally says computing power must be **less than
250 kW** and total electrical service **less than 1 MW**. Preserve those operators;
do not reverse them into common minimum-load thresholds, silently repair the text,
or conclude which projects the clause covers before legal review. Qualifying
principal/accessory uses and the proposed area calculation also require careful
interpretation.

The draft pauses specified activity, applications and municipal approvals. Its
180-day duration starts from **September 16 applicability**, subject to extension,
repeal or modification; that is separate from Town Meeting adoption/effectiveness.
The draft's watermark and incomplete execution remain explicit. No actual expiry
or subsequent extension is inferred.

[Official proposed ordinance, especially pages 2–3](https://brownfieldme.civicpluswebopen.com/media/6701).

The tally download is a **native JPEG with EXIF orientation**, despite the document
endpoint. It must not be treated as PDF. Direct native-JPEG verification, including
orientation, remains needed for canonical visual evidence. `brownfield-review.json`
pins the source bytes, warrant render, reviewed draft renders and exact power/term
spans. Final enacted text, power-clause interpretation, dated certification,
passage outcome, operative history and publication gates remain open.

## Freedom

The four-page town-hosted text is labeled Draft and leaves its adoption date as
`<DATE>`. Its preamble says the town requires **at least 180 days** to develop and
implement regulation. Its operative sections define computing facilities and
prohibit development/application processing, but supply **no automatic expiration
clause**. They specify immediate passage-based effectiveness and leave a related
applicability/adoption date blank. The preamble's study need is not silently turned
into a 180-day operative term or an inferred expiry date.

September 17 hearing notes describe **approximately six months**, discuss a
question about the end of 180 days and possible extension, and distinguish the
proposal from an outright ban. These are attributed hearing descriptions, separate
from the draft's missing operative end date. They also discuss a recall ordinance;
that political-process measure is not folded into the data-center question.

[Official draft](https://www.freedomme.org/_files/ugd/8d8f5c_be3f789c00ae4e1eb75f14bae34aac9e.pdf),
[official hearing notes, page 2](https://www.freedomme.org/_files/ugd/8d8f5c_46de79206147493da9212585e618d912.pdf).

`freedom-review.json` verifies both source versions, the four visually reviewed
draft pages, the hearing passage and exact text spans. The original native text
has duplicated-glyph extraction defects, so visual comparison matters. This is
not completed bc-evidence extraction. Worker news-result assertions remain leads;
this packet does not accept official counts. Official ballot/results, enacted
term, certification, operative interval, later force and publication gates remain
open.
