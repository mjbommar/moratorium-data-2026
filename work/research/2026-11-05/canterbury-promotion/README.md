# Canterbury Article 4 promotion

This packet prepares the March 10, 2026 annual town election question, proposed
zoning provisions and town-posted result counts for expanded canonical data.
It is an earlier election, not a November 3 measure or a temporary moratorium.
The [source review](../canterbury-ballot-review/README.md) pins both official
PDFs and the original text/render hashes.

Accepted scope: Canterbury identity, annual election date, Article 4 wording,
proposed permanent all-zone prohibition and its definition/exemptions, and
322 yes / 63 no. The derived 385 is only yes plus no, not turnout or ballots cast.
Bindingness, formal placement, passage rule, reported/calculated outcome,
certification and operative legal force remain unresolved. County is null.
No legacy inventory row or active-moratorium count is added.

The four visual receipts use a fresh page-1 `pdftoppm` render at 160 DPI checked
against the retained PDF. The native-text provision claim has an exact located
span. Each accepted domain field has a scoped review decision; all previous
canonical records are preserved byte-for-byte at the record level.

`identity-plan.json` pins the previous identity-registry hash and explicitly
allocates new identities. `publication-plan.json` pins the prior snapshot,
candidate snapshot, registry and every reviewed record. `visual-reviews.json`
retains prior-cohort receipt references as well as this cohort's receipts.
The private candidate and registry preview are under
`/data0/moratorium-refresh-2026/canterbury-promotion/`.

Preparation and a schema pass alone are not proof of canonical publication.
The retained canonical snapshot/publication receipt is authoritative.
