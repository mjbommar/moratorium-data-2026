# Eliot Question 5: reviewed question and proposed ordinance

Reviewed November 5, 2026. This records a candidate review, not an addition to
the canonical moratorium inventory or a determination of current legal force.

The official sample ballot's Question 5 asks whether to enact the Town of Eliot
Data Center Moratorium Ordinance and direct preparation of permanent land-use
standards. The signed special-referendum warrant presents the same question as
Article 5. The ordinance's municipal-officer certificate identifies the official
proposed text for the November 3 election, with the Select Board's August 27 date.
These records support classification as a binding enactment question referred by
the governing body, with a yes vote favoring restrictions.

Paragraph 2 includes data centers of any size, including cryptocurrency mining,
and excludes accessory server equipment incidental to a lawful principal use on
the same lot. Paragraph 3 pauses action on applications for siting, permitting,
construction, installation, expansion and operation. Paragraph 4 specifies 180 days
from effectiveness, subject to earlier repeal, modification or superseding
permanent standards, and describes possible extensions.

Paragraph 5 mentions both enactment by voters at the November 3 election, as
certified by the clerk, and seven days after adoption. Paragraph 6 separately
addresses applications filed on or after enactment. We have not resolved those
operative-date provisions or established a current legal-force interval. No exact
expiry date or accepted measure-to-instrument link follows from this review.

The prior result review retains 2,965 yes, 909 no and 183 blank votes, with 4,057
ballots cast. Result certification and legal implementation remain separate facts.
