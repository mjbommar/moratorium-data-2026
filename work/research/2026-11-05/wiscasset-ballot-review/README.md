# Wiscasset Question 2 source review

The retained town results page is titled **Unofficial Election Results**. Its
Special Town Meeting – Town Referendum section identifies Question 2 as Data
Center Moratorium and lists **1,363 yes / 712 no**, with blanks left as an empty
line. The arithmetic sum is 2,075, not turnout or an established valid-vote total.
The page's separate **Total Ballots Cast: 2,155** follows results for several
state, school and town contests. This review does not accept that number as a
question-specific denominator, or derive 80 blanks from the difference. The
worker candidate's `ballots_cast=2155` requires correction before promotion.

[Official unofficial-results page](https://www.wiscasset.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/211).

The official November 3 voter guide describes a proposed stop on accepting,
processing, reviewing or approving applications for new, enlarged or expanded
data centers. It summarizes a term of **six months after enactment**, unless
lifted earlier or extended under stated conditions, applying to applications
filed on or after November 3. Six calendar months is not encoded as 180 days.
The guide separately describes possible extensions of **no more than 180 days
each**, requiring continued-need and reasonable-progress findings. These are
conditional extensions, not automatically exercised terms.

The guide summarizes exclusions for equipment incidental to another business,
routine maintenance or replacement that does not expand an existing center, and
governmental IT used for governmental purposes. Its study/report process concerns
possible additional zoning/building standards. The guide is an explanation of a
proposed temporary moratorium, not a permanent prohibition or executed operative
ordinance. Its statement that no proposals were under consideration is historical
guide context, not a fresh project census.

[Official voter guide](https://www.wiscasset.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1735/Special-Town-Meeting-Voter-Guide---November-3-2026).

`review.json` pins the retained results HTML, original pydoll client JSON,
extracted-text hash, guide PDF/native-text hashes and a visually checked page-2
render. Result quote offsets refer specifically to the original client JSON's
`content.text`; they do not relabel HTML as native plain text. Compatible evidence
registration remains necessary before canonical promotion. Raw artifacts stay
private, and the frozen census candidate is unchanged.

Official question wording, adopted ordinance, formal placement/bindingness,
certification, passage rule/outcome, question-specific ballot totals, operative
dates and later legal force remain unresolved. This packet neither publishes a
canonical expanded record nor changes a legacy moratorium count.
