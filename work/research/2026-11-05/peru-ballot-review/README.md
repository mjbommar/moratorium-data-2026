The town's proposed one-page Temporary Moratorium of the Development and
Permitting of Data Centers defines a data center as a building or series of
buildings designed/used for high-performance computing infrastructure, servers,
networking and storage, including related cooling, backup power and dedicated
electrical infrastructure. It pauses acceptance, processing and approval of
specified development applications/permits for construction or operation.
Routine upgrades to existing telecommunications facilities are exempt.

Sections 3 and 5 specify immediate effectiveness upon passage and expiration
**180 days from effectiveness**, unless repealed, extended or replaced by later
legislative action. Those possibilities are not automatically exercised terms.
The document bears Selectboard signatures dated **September 14, 2026** and a
clerk true-attested notation. The town posted it as a proposal for the November
ballot; September signatures alone do not establish November voter enactment,
result certification or the operative expiration date.
