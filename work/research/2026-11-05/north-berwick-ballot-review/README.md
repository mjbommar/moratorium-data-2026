# North Berwick Question 2 official-source review

The official sample ballot identifies the **November 3, 2026 local election** and
Question 2, asking enactment of the Proposed Zoning Ordinance Amendment to Define
and Prohibit Data Centers. The download URL's November-4 slug does not override
the date printed on the ballot. The town's signed result sheet shows **2,114 yes
and 582 no**, explicitly **Certified 11/3/2026**, with Town Clerk Christine M.
Dudley's signature. The arithmetic sum is 2,696; turnout, ballots cast and blanks
remain unknown. Certification concerns the result observations, not a finding
that the prohibition is currently enforceable.

[Official sample ballot](https://www.townofnorthberwick.org/DocumentCenter/View/1601/Town-Referdum-November-4-2026),
[official signed results](https://www.townofnorthberwick.org/DocumentCenter/View/1639/2026-Election--Town-Results).

The proposed amendment occupies pages 20–21 of the 21-page referendum packet.
Its Data Center definition has two cumulative requirements: the specified primary
computing/network/infrastructure use, **and aggregate Data Center Area of at
least 25,000 square feet**. Named activities include AI, blockchain,
cryptocurrency mining, cloud computing, colocation, enterprise, hyperscale and
edge computing. The separate defined area includes computing equipment and
supporting infrastructure, cooling/maintenance space, and associated impervious
surfaces such as access ways, parking lots, transmission corridors and accessory
buildings/structures. It is not simply a 25,000-square-foot server-room floor limit.

The definition excludes server rooms or IT infrastructure accessory to the site's
principal use **if the accessory use has aggregate area below 25,000 square feet**.
The strict less-than condition must remain distinct from the definition's
greater-than-or-equal threshold. Page 21 marks the Data Center row **NO in all
eleven listed district columns** and states the intent to prohibit establishment
in all zoning districts. This is a proposed permanent prohibition, not a temporary
moratorium; it should not add to temporary-moratorium counts.

[Official referendum packet, pages 20–21](https://www.townofnorthberwick.org/DocumentCenter/View/1615/November-2026-Referendum).

August 18 Select Board minutes, page 1, report that the Planning Board advanced
the amendment August 13. They then record a motion approving the November ballot
questions, seconded and passed **3-0**. This supports the placement-source account;
it does not establish the later operative amendment interval.
