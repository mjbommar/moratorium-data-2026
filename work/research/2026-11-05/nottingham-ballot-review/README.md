# Nottingham September 8 ballot source review

The town-hosted marked ballot identifies the September 8, 2026 special town
election and asks whether to adopt a Temporary Moratorium on Data Centers
Ordinance pursuant to RSA 674:23. The question describes twelve months to study
impacts and develop zoning regulations. The handwritten row shows **1,335 yes
and 143 no**. Their sum, 1,478, is only yes plus no; it does not establish turnout,
ballots cast, blank votes or invalid votes. The document still bears a prominent
“SAMPLE BALLOT” heading. A town-clerk signature in the ballot heading is not
accepted as a dated election-result certification.

[Official town-posted marked ballot](https://www.nottingham-nh.gov/sites/g/files/vyhlif3611/f/news/2026_nottingham_special_town_election_results.pdf).

The seven-page proposed ordinance was checked in native text and selected page
renders. Page 2 defines a data center by its primary data storage, processing,
management and transmission use. Its embedded/accessory-computing exclusion
requires **all three** conditions: no more than 25% of the specified on-site
floor area; service to the on-site owner's enterprise functions without primarily
providing third-party services; and no leasing of data services to third parties.
The percentage alone is not a general exemption. A separate exclusion covers
qualifying telecommunications and broadband-provider facilities.

The proposed permitting pause includes site plans, subdivisions, building permits,
conditional-use permits, special exceptions, applicable variances and waivers
for establishing, constructing, expanding or operating data centers. Page 3
applies the pause to all zoning districts and principal/accessory uses, subject
to the definition's exclusions. The proposed duration is twelve calendar months
from adoption unless the legislative body repeals it earlier; effectiveness is
also conditioned on legislative-body adoption. No operative adoption date or
expiry date is inferred from these proposed clauses.

Page 7 records a **July 1 Planning Board vote: five in favor, none opposed and
none abstained**, with signatures on board-member/alternate lines. These are
Planning Board proposal signatures, not proof of September voter enactment or
execution of a final ordinance. The ballot itself separately notes Planning
Board approval, 5-0-0. Formal placement and bindingness still require legal review.

The raw proposal's native extraction has reading-order defects on page 2.
The definition and prohibition were therefore checked against the rendered page;
the original extraction is preserved. The clean duration paragraph on page 3
has an exact codepoint span in `review.json`. Pages 2, 3 and 7 were rendered
at 140 DPI; this is scoped visual review, not full-document extraction coverage.

`review.json` verifies both retained body/text hashes against the acquisition
ledger and pins four reviewed image hashes. Raw sources/renders remain private.
This source-review packet itself does not promote an expanded record or change
an active-moratorium tally. The subsequent [promotion packet](../nottingham-promotion/README.md)
records scoped canonical publication without a new legacy inventory row. Certification, legal passage, operative dates,
later amendments/repeal and the legacy-instrument link remain unresolved.
