# Canterbury Article 4 source review

The March 10, 2026 annual town ballot asks whether to amend the zoning ordinance to ban data centers. Its explanation adds a Data Center definition and marks the use Not Allowed in all zones. This is a permanent-prohibition candidate, not a temporary moratorium or a November-election observation.

The town-hosted two-page results PDF was checked visually. Article 4 shows **322 yes and 63 no**. Their sum, 385, is not established turnout or ballots cast. Blank and invalid votes remain unknown. The first sheet bears the town clerk signature and “True Copy attest”; neither sheet supplies a separate certification date. No certification date or calculated passage outcome is accepted.

The separate official Article 4 explanation proposes a definition covering commercial/third-party computing facilities, including cryptocurrency mining, server farms, cloud computing and colocation with specified infrastructure. It excludes equipment solely supporting on-site lawful business/institutional activities and personal household servers accessory to residential use. These are proposed zoning provisions, not verification of current consolidated law.
