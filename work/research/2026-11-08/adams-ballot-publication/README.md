# Monroe and Sprigg ballot publication

Two independently reviewed Adams County, Ohio general-zoning questions are now canonical. Complete official county sample wording and qualified unofficial county result observations are retained. Monroe records **148 For / 100 Against**, source Total Votes **248**; Sprigg records **330 For / 297 Against**, source Total Votes **627**. Source Times Cast is separate and does not establish contest turnout, undervotes or registration. Neither a numerical majority nor the sample’s majority sentence establishes legal passage or effective law.

The new generation contains **56 measures, 63 retained results and 52 current observations over 51 measures**. Current certified observations remain five; five measures have no current result. These additions have unknown named-sector coverage, development-relative polarity, bindingness, operative terms, implementation and current legal effect. They are general-zoning/infrastructure collection records, not proved data-center bans or anti-development wins. All 54 prior measures and every other baseline row are preserved.

Separate primary and independent Terra reviews were followed by coordinator adjudication of identity, complete wording, source-specific publication rights, and corrected field provenance. Physical sample page 1 establishes the general-election label and county producer; page 2 supports the question and date. Named contest claims bind official return URLs; retained acquisition metadata binds observation time to November 5, rather than a later review or the PDF’s report clock. Explicit total claims support each source-total review. Complete official ballot legal text is accepted as `public_record` under the existing source-specific governmental-record policy; raw PDF bodies remain private.

[Application](application.json) pins the publication. [Identity application](identity-application.json) records the allocated objects and two reviewed frozen-source aliases. [Coordinator adjudication](coordinator-adjudication.json) records acceptance scope. [Frozen accounting](frozen-accounting.json) maps Monroe and Sprigg without inheriting their old unsupported attributes: 53 mapped frozen identities, two outside-freeze identities and one Augusta notice with unresolved correspondence. Three frozen payloads remain held and cannot simply be added as disjoint contests. Historical five-held receipts are preserved.

Remaining gaps include full adopted plans/maps/exceptions, filing/placement, actual issued-ballot inventory, precinct-split relationships, whole-jurisdiction reporting and final certified canvass. The full publisher dry-run and application passed against actual coordinator-reviewed receipts; final generated-file checks, pushed-source and hosted delivery are recorded separately. This is a working-data increment, not completion of all-record freshness, P4, consumer delivery or the final GitHub release.
