# Ballot release accounting checkpoint

This checkpoint reconciles all **53 canonical measures**, their **61 retained result versions**, and the **five reviewed-withheld frozen candidate groups**. It uses the existing primary, independent and coordinator reviews; it does not establish a complete national census, a fresh source observation, or current legal force.

There are **50 current result observations across 49 measures**: 31 unofficial, 14 with unknown certification and five certified. Four measures have no current result observation. Wrightstown has two county-component observations for one measure; those observations must not be counted as two measures or a whole-measure certification.

The original 56 frozen groups comprise 51 published identities and five withheld groups: Morgan, Augusta, Monroe/Adams (Ohio), Ottawa (Kansas) Question 2 and Sprigg/Adams (Ohio). Jay Article 3 and Wilmington Chapter 1161 are the two current measures outside that frozen projection. Withheld groups retain their [reviewed authority requirements](../../2026-11-07/ballot-frozen-seed-dispositions/README.md).

[Measure dispositions](measure-dispositions.json) give each measure's current row guard, result IDs, review artifact hashes and separate question, result, certification, implementation and exemption dispositions. The original evidence dates and explicit nulls remain. An authority hold is a substantive unresolved disposition, not approval to infer certification or operative law. A proposed self-executing clause does not itself establish implementation.

[Verification](verification.json) checks the current canonical rows, result-version arithmetic, unique identities, review artifact existence and all substantive input hashes from the retained private accounting packet. Two process documents changed after that packet was assembled; their old/new hashes are recorded separately, and no ballot evidence changed. Full source bodies and private review narratives remain private. This checkpoint has no new canonical changes and does not close independent national discovery, fresh later-action checks, the final release, or consumer synchronization.
