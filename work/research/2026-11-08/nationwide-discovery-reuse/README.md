The user authorized the priced ceilings on November 8 with an explicit instruction to avoid duplicating work. The initial 600-cell proposal must not be executed unchanged.

## Admission rules for the reduced follow-up

1. Compare intent, jurisdiction, sector and observation window, not merely exact query strings. Rewording a completed query does not establish a gap.
2. Reuse retained results and known ballot candidates before seeking new leads. Broad multi-sector election searches are partial evidence, not automatically five independent sector censuses.
3. For each proposed new request, retain the prior attempts considered and a concrete reason: failed acquisition, missing independent sector/neutral discovery, or a specific later-action/publication question not covered previously. Two-day-old collection alone does not justify a national repeat.
4. Match retained URLs and source versions before fetching. Reuse available body/text hashes; document why any newer version is needed.
5. Pin the reduced plan and updated execution contract before dispatch. Preserve all historical spend and the approved ceilings; do not transfer or reset prior allowances.

## Decision on the proposed repeat sweep

A Terra review of the 600 proposed cells recommends **zero new broad search queries**. The [cell-level reuse matrix](proposal-cell-reuse.json) links 250 reused sector-action cells and 350 broad-partial election/neutral cells to retained attempts. Broad-partial remains incomplete coverage; it does not justify automatically rerunning all 350 cells. The coordinator checked all 600 unique cell identities and each linked response hash against the deduplicated register. [Decision receipt](gap-decision.json) pins counts and the private review matrix.

The five held ballot groups still need specific primary authority: Morgan County IL, Augusta Township MI, Monroe and Sprigg Townships in Adams County OH, and Ottawa KS Question 2. Earlier searches already overlap those questions. Pursuing known authority records and retained leads is the next useful step; these are not new discoveries to count twice.

This review did not manually read every hit in all 4,073 responses. It establishes that the proposed broad repeat is unnecessary, not a complete national census, full state-policy/later-action coverage, or absence of further gaps. Future specific searches require documented review of pertinent retained queries and source captures. No new-phase paid provider calls have executed; the approved ceiling remains unused. Record-level freshness, source recovery, evaluation, consumer and release gates remain open.
