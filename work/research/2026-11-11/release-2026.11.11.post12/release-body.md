This update adds Fairfax, Iowa’s proposed 18-month pause on utility-scale battery storage. It also updates two text fields in the existing Fairfax data-center record. We have not confirmed final adoption or publication of either proposed moratorium.

The battery-storage proposal is separate from the data-center moratorium and zoning proposals. The November 10 agenda proposes setting a December 8 hearing on a data-center prohibition. That is a public hearing, not a ballot measure.

The local inventory now has 1,329 records, including 64 pending proposals. No existing record changes status. There are 1,085 records listed as active or extended, 173 replaced, expired, or rescinded, 3 rejected proposals, and 4 with unknown status. These classifications do not confirm that every restriction remains in effect.

The review queue has 1,347 open records. Questions include final adopted texts, effective dates, end dates, and later actions. Ballot tables are unchanged. The main November search ended November 7; later checks retain their own source dates.

The data files and dashboard are published and checked.
