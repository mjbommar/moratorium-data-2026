# Braintree and Methuen proposal review

Two official two-page documents support pending data-center moratorium proposals.
The coordinator reviewed all four PDF pages, original body/text hashes and cited
passages. Braintree Order 26-063 proposes one calendar year with possible earlier
termination. Methuen TO-26-20 proposes one year, possible earlier regulation, and a
single extension up to 180 days. Neither document establishes final adoption.

Both records retain blank enactment and operative-end dates, calendar terms rather
than fixed-day conversions, and explicit final-action/current-force/geography gaps.
All 1,295 prior records are unchanged. Validation includes Sun Prairie's separate
addition, source archive preservation, full build/check and zero-change replay.
