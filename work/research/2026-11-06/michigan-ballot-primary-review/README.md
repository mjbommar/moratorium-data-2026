# Augusta Township zoning referendum primary review

Status: three retained primary source versions and five scoped claims; no canonical publication.

Washtenaw County’s retained report explicitly labels its August 4, 2026 primary results Official Election Results. Its Augusta Twp Zoning Referendum row reports 269 yes and 2,314 no, with source percentages 10.41 and 89.59. Early, absentee and election-day columns sum to each reported total. The derived 2,583 yes-plus-no count is not ballots cast or a statutory valid-vote denominator. Countywide registration, turnout and precincts, the adjacent farmland/open-space proposal and the fire millages are excluded. The August 31 report-creation clock is not a certification date.

The township’s earlier post calls its returns unofficial; this source version remains distinct from the later county official report. Its 40.54 percent turnout is not a zoning-question denominator.

Both pages of draft Resolution 26-04 in the February 24 agenda packet (physical pages 16–17) were fully read and visually inspected. It proposes a referendum on Ordinance 2025-02 rezoning specified Augusta Development Corp parcels from Agricultural Residential to General Industrial. Petition and prior adoption dates are draft recitals, not independently verified legal events. The draft has blank roll-call and signature fields, and an inconsistent older footer referring to 2023-02, 7171 Willis Road and an August 24 ballot. It is not treated as the final adopted referral or final ballot. Other agenda materials are not claim-reviewed.

This is a site-specific zoning question, not a townshipwide prohibition. The actual ordinance, final ballot, bindingness, subsequent zoning status and independent data-center/project association remain open.
