# Frenchtown instrument follow-up

The targeted Exa search led to the official township zoning index. The index was successfully fetched with bc-web and its actual `/DocumentCenter/View/763` link was followed. The returned compiled zoning ordinance is labelled updated August 24, 2026. Both successful source versions and their hashes are pinned in `acquisition-review.json`; full bodies remain private.

Section 7.13 in the client extraction describes general moratorium authority, an initial maximum of six months and supported extensions. It does not establish the effective date of the separately adopted technology-use Ordinance 300-19 or the endpoint of the October extension. No canonical field or status has been changed from this acquisition. The exact executed instrument, publication notice and complete extension remain open. This is a scoped text review, not a full-document or visual legal review.

The official agenda index also links the April 28 agenda file, which is a 55-page scanned packet. Local Tesseract OCR located the ordinance on physical pages 31–32; both pages were visually inspected and carry a DRAFT watermark. The draft states a six-month period from effectiveness and effectiveness seven days after publication of passage. Selected visually checked passages were separately registered as a derivation, preserving the original empty client extraction. Render hashes and extraction lineage are pinned in the JSON review. The preprinted adoption date in a premeeting draft is not proof of execution, and no calendar endpoint is accepted. The exact-number Exa search is retained as a bounded search, not evidence of absence.
