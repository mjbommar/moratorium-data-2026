# Downe Township primary ballot review

Status: official sample ballot reviewed; candidate remains unpublished.

Recovered the county-hosted two-page Downe sample ballot through bc-web. Both rendered PDF pages were inspected. PDF page 1 contains the ballot faces and identifies the Township of Downe and November 3, 2026 election; page 2 contains voting instructions and the same election date. The public question asks whether voters support data centers. Its interpretive statement expressly describes gauging public sentiment and explains that no expresses opposition. This supports advisory sentiment classification; it does not enact a ban or authorize a project.

The exact English question ends with a period. The interpretive statement contains a printed “what” typo, retained in the review instead of silently corrected. This is a county-published sample version, not proof of a cast ballot, result, later canvass or operative law. Referral resolution and result-source review remain open. The original frozen candidate and canonical snapshots are unchanged by this review.
