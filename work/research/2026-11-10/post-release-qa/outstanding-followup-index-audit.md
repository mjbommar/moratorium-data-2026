# Outstanding follow-up index audit

The queue builder omitted the explicit `source_followup` and `source_followup_required` fields while retaining generic `unperformed_checks` limitations. This made some existing reviews look like requests to repeat broad research instead of showing their recorded next source.

The corrected extraction recovers **424 already recorded instructions across 421 unresolved records**: 406 local records and 15 state-policy records. The question index grows from 1,670 to 2,094 entries, but the unresolved population remains **1,347 records**. This is recovery of existing instructions, not a new research scope or a new set of records. [Exact comparison](outstanding-followup-index-audit.json).

For example, Independence, Missouri's queue previously showed only that the adjudication made no network calls. Its retained independent review also specified inspecting the adopted operative resolution for its effective-date anchor and later council actions. The latter instruction is now indexed alongside the original limitation. The independent review already resolved the previously missing news body; that recovery is not repeated or reversed by this change.

Verification compares every old and new queue record. All record IDs and canonical row hashes match, and every previous question remains present. Canonical CSVs, policy scope decisions, numerical summaries, the frozen review ledger and disposition counts are unchanged. Existing post-release overlays continue to supersede older questions, preserving their guarded review receipts.

The larger affected state groups are Ohio (91 records), New York and Washington (32 each), Tennessee (28), Kentucky (25), Minnesota and Missouri (21 each). These are record counts, not disjoint categories of research problems or measures of new coverage.

Use the recovered instructions for source-specific acquisition. Inspect retained bytes and later receipts before fetching; do not equate an independent review's lack of new network calls with a missing primary acquisition. Conversely, a method limitation does not prove that required later-action checks were completed. An instruction conditional on current force or project applicability remains qualified and is not automatically counted as a resolved record. No closure, legal-status change, source freshness renewal or release completion follows from this index repair.
