## Review procedure

Use an independent reviewer with the actual sources, as required by section 7 of the process reset plan. For Kentucky and Louisiana, have the reviewer extract the material facts and source locators before reading the proposed answer. Source agreement is insufficient without role, version and locator support. Do not delegate or launch jobs until session authorization permits it.

For every record, return the exact input row guard (or full-file guard for the new candidate), accepted/rejected/held proposed cells, exact suggested replacement text, source body hash and locator for each accepted assertion, conflicts, and precise remaining questions. Distinguish acceptance of a historical fact or section date from a current-force determination and from whole-record closure. A correction may be accepted while the record remains unresolved.

Inspect HTML markup when the original source is a legislative HTML rendering: flattened text can retain struck provisions. Inspect the original scanned Kentucky order pages and the relevant Louisiana journal pages rather than treating OCR/search absence as legal adjudication. Preserve the boundaries between the failed bill, a successor law, an existing statute and individual project eligibility.

Keep network admission at zero for the initial retained-evidence review. If a new source is necessary, identify the exact missing question and endpoint first; collection requires a separately bounded acquisition allowance. Do not repeat exhausted searches or refetch pinned bodies merely to reconstruct the packet.

The coordinator applies only supported, accepted cells with current guards, handles conflicts and cross-record consistency, appends scope/version provenance where required, and regenerates affected public surfaces once for the reviewed batch. New data or copy requires corresponding release/delivery verification; source-only review artifacts do not require redeploying the unchanged page.
