RESOLUTION IMPOSING A:' TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON APPLICATIONS FOR
TECHNOLOGY OVERLAY DISTRICT DESIGNATION

WHEREAS, pursuant to 55 ICLS 5/5-1001 ef seq., the Lee County Board has the authority
and obligation to preserve, defend, and protect the citizens and property of Lee County, the Lee County
Board is charged with maintaining laws and ordinances consistent with its goals as outlined in the Lee
County Comprehensive Plan.

WHEREAS, the Lee County Zoning Ordinance establishes a Technology Overlay District to
facilitate the appropriate development of technology-related uses in areas of Lee County where
existing or proposed infrastructure can adequately support such uses; and

WHEREAS, the Lee County Board desires to review and consider amendments to the
Technology Overlay District regulations to ensure that the County’s zoning standards adequately
address the potential land-use, infrastructure, public-safety, environmental, and community impacts
associated with the continuously evolving technology-related uses; and

WHEREAS, no complete or active petition or application for Technology Overlay District
designation is pending before Lee County as of the date of this Resolution; and

WHEREAS, temporarily suspending the acceptance of new petitions and applications for
Technology Overlay District designation will preserve the status quo and provide sufficient time for
the County to conduct this review and consider appropriate amendments; and

WHEREAS, the Lee County Board finds that a temporary moratorium is reasonable, limited
in duration, and in furtherance of the public health, safety, and welfare of Lee County and its residents.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEE COUNTY BOARD that,
effective immediately upon passage and approval of this Resolution, Lee County shall temporarily
cease accepting new petitions or applications requesting the establishment, designation, expansion, or
amendment of a Technology Overlay District under the Lee County Zoning Ordinance; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this moratorium shall take effect immediately upon its
passage and approval, and shall remain in effect through November 19, 2026, and shall expire
automatically at 11:59 p.m. on that date unless earlier terminated, extended, or otherwise modified by
action of the Lee County Board; and

PASSED BY THE LEE COUNTY BOARD
