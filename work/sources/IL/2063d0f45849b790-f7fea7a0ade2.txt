Community Insight Panel On Data Centers

BLOOMINGTON, IL -- The Bloomington-Normal Data Center Community Forum, hosted by Susan Saunders, brought together industry, environmental and energy experts to discuss data centers and their potential impacts on our community. The conversation covered water and energy use, economic development, jobs, environmental concerns, infrastructure and potential safeguards for future projects.

Thank you to everyone who attended, submitted questions and took the time to be part of the discussion.

Current Regulatory Status

Both Bloomington and Normal currently have moratoriums on hyperscale data centers while the communities evaluate potential regulations. Following the August 26 forum, each community will continue its respective public review process, including opportunities for residents to provide input before proposed regulations are considered by their respective governing bodies.

Purpose of the Forum

The forum is designed to facilitate transparent, fact‑based discussion and share insights from a broad range of experts. It will help inform both communities’ ongoing evaluation of data center impacts, including environmental considerations, land use, infrastructure needs, economic factors, and community input.

Both the City of Bloomington and Town of Normal remain firmly committed to transparency and creating a thoughtful, community‑centered process as they evaluate future data center regulations. They are dedicated to ensuring that residents’ questions are answered, feedback is actively solicited, and stakeholders are meaningfully engaged throughout each step of the regulatory process.
