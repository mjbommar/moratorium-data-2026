    2026 ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A SIX (6) MONTH MORATORIUM ON DATA
         CENTER APPLICATIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT IN MACON COUNTY


        WHEREAS Macon County, Illinois has been granted authority to regulate and restrict
location and use of structures pursuant to 55 ILCS 5/5-12001 et. seq. and to amend its’ zoning
regulations pursuant to 55 ILCS 5/5-12014, and
       WHEREAS, the Macon County Board is aware of the desire to develop Data Centers of
varying sizes and capacities throughout the State of Illinois and Macon County; and
         WHEREAS, for the purposes of this Ordinance Data Centers are considered to be those
facilities utilized for housing computer systems and related equipment for the storage,
management, distribution, and processing of digital data. Examples include, but are not limited to,
cloud services, artificial intelligence, crypto mining, data storage, and essential computing
infrastructure.
       WHEREAS, the Macon County Board is aware of the extensive concerns of the residents
and taxpayers of Macon County in regards to the development and operation of Data Centers, and
        WHEREAS, the Macon County Zoning Ordinance does not currently have a dedicated
section addressing the siting, regulations, and potential hazards of Data Centers; and
        WHEREAS, the extensive development of renewable energy in Macon County has created
a vastly increased interest in the development of Data Centers in Macon County; and
        WHEREAS, the Macon County Board has an interest in protecting the County’s
infrastructure, natural resources, wildlife and property values; and
       WHEREAS, the Macon County Board has an interest in protecting the health, safety,
morals and general welfare of the residents of Macon County; and
        WHEREAS, the Macon County Board, Macon County Zoning Board of Appeals, Director
of Planning & Zoning, Supervisor of Assessments & GIS, and States Attorney will require
substantial time to hold public hearings, gather information, and coordinate with multiple agencies
for the purpose of reviewing & creating a comprehensive Data Center Ordinance; and
       WHEREAS, the Macon County Board wishes to place a temporary, six (6) month
moratorium on special use permit and building permit applications for data centers; and
        NOW THEREFORE, IT IS ORDAINED AND ORDERED, by the Macon County
Board following review, consideration, and discussion, that a temporary moratorium on accepting
applications for Data Centers be utilized to allow County Officials time to further study the
concerns expressed by residents & taxpayers and develop comprehensive rules and regulations for
the siting of Data Centers. Said moratorium will be in full effect upon the passage of this

Ordinance, with an expiration date of January 31, 2027, at 11:59pm CST. During the tenure of the
moratorium no applications for a Data Center shall be accepted, processed, or considered by
Macon County. Further, no Data Center may proceed with construction or operation of such
facility, unless such facility was operational prior to the effective date of this Ordinance. This
moratorium does not apply to any data center that is an enterprise data center which solely serves
as IT infrastructure, data storage or processing of a previously existing Macon County business or
organization.
        BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED AND ORDERED, it is the Macon County Board’s
desire to instruct the Macon County Zoning Board of Appeals and applicable County Officials to
research, consult, review, develop and draft a comprehensive Macon County Data Center
Ordinance, hold any required public hearings, and present a final draft of the Ordinance by the
October 2026, Macon County Board Meeting.
       BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED AND ORDERED, by the Macon County Board that this
temporary, six (6) month moratorium on new Data Center applications will expire on January 31,
2027 at 11:59pm CST, unless ended earlier by a County Board Ordinance or extended prior to
January 31, 2027, by a County Board Ordinance.
