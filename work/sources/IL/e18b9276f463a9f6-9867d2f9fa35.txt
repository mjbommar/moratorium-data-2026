Citizen Participation

- Three city residents spoke about the recent oil and chip work done on streets in Ward 3. There were issues

with materials used and the excessive amount of dust, due to lack of adequate street sweeping afterwards.

Mayor Nonn informed the citizens that the supply of slag, which the city typically uses, is low. Supply was

depleted when the steel mills went down. The mills are back up, so there will be a new supply of slag. The

street sweeper also needed repair. Mr. Alldredge added that parts to repair the sweeper have been ordered.

- Kathy Scheller, Director of Ministries Unlimited, informed the council that they are preparing to construct

a new facility on Buckeye Drive. When complete, they will move there from the Collinsville Road location,

which has become too small.  She provided an organization pamphlet to council members.

Public Works Committee Report

Bid Tab – Bargraves Concrete Swale

Bid opening was on September 10thEngineer estimate for the project is $61K. KRB Excavating was the low

.

bid at $52,192.20. Mr. Keeven said that, in the original capital budget, the city was not planning for a concrete

swale. Rip-rap had been budgeted at $40K, but the city was also going to rip-rap the ditch on Cherry Lane.

$25K was also budgeted for tree removal. It was then decided that a concrete swale was a better solution for

the ditch on Bargraves.

Motion: To award the contract to KRB Excavating at the unit price of $139.50 per square foot with total

cost not to exceed $65K.

Moved by Turner. Second by Henderson. Vote: Motion passed. (Aye = 7; Nay = 0)

Aye: Flint, Hellrung, Henderson, Italiano, Knoll, Stirling, Turner. Nay: none.

Franklin Avenue/Street Reconstruction with Storm Sewer – He is applying for a $275K CDBG grant from

Madison County for a new closed storm sewer system. This will address flooding issues on Franklin Avenue

and Henderson Street. A resolution of support will be on the city council agenda at the October 5th meeting.
