                                 NOTICE
                          STOCKBRIDGE TOWNSHIP
             NOTICE OF ADOPTION OF RESOLUTION AND ORDINANCE

Stockbridge Township Board of Trustees of Stockbridge Township, Ingham County,
. Michigan, at a regular meeting held on the 16th day of March 2026, adopted Resolution No.
02, entitled:
“Resolution to Adopt an Ordinance to Impose a Moratorium on the Issuance of Permits,
Licenses, or Approvals for Data Centers.”
The Resolution approves and adopts Ordinance No.26-02, which establishes a temporary
eighteen (18) month moratorium—or until amendments to the Township’s Zoning
Ordinance are adopted, whichever occurs first—on the issuance of any permits, licenses,
or approvals related to the establishment or operation of Data Centers within Stockbridge
Township.
The purpose of the moratorium is to allow Stockbridge Township adequate time to review
and consider appropriate provisions, procedures, and standards within its zoning
regulations addressing Data Centers.
