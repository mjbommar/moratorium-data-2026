4.   NEW BUSINESS
     a. FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT APPEAL
     Supervisor Reeder explained the Freedom of Information Act Appeal Process. There were several options
     for the Board of Trustees to consider in order to complete business for this item.
     Deputy Supervisor Grabow noted specifics regarding this appeal.

     Motion: Marino     Support: Rosseel
     Motion to deny Arianna Welsh’s Freedom of Information Appeal in full.
     Roll Call Vote: Marino- Aye, Rosseel- Aye, Gurley- Aye, Reeder- Aye, Sutherland- Aye. All Ayes. Motion
     passes.

     b. 2026 JULY 1, 2026, TO DECEMBER 31, 2026, EMPLOYEE WAGE SCHEDULE
     Supervisor Reeder reported that wages were discussed at a recent Budget Workshop. Among several
     options, was to give the staff Cost of Living increases, except a few employees that it was believed should
     be given larger increases to balance their job responsibilities.

     Motion: Reeder      Support: Marino
     Motion to approve the proposed 2026, July 1, 2026 to December 21, 2026 Wage Schedule for
     employees.
     Roll Call Vote: Reeder- Aye, Marino- Aye, Sutherland- Aye, Gurley- Aye, Rosseel- Aye. All Ayes.
     Motion passes.
