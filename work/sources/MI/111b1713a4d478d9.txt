26-118 (E) Resolution Recognizing Juneteenth in the City of Pontiac. Moved by Councilperson
James and seconded by Councilperson Goodman.

WHEREAS, Juneteenth is celebrated in recognition of June 19, 1865 as the date on which news of the
end of slavery reached the slaves in the southwestern states of the United States of America; and,
WHEREAS, news of the end of slavery did not reach the frontier area of the United States, in particular
the State of Texas and other southwestern states, until months after the conclusion of the American Civil
War, more than two and a half years after President Abraham Lincoln issued the Emancipation
Proclamation on January 1, 1863; and,

WHEREAS, on June 19, 1865, Union soldiers, led by Major General Gordon Granger, arrived in
Galveston, Texas with news that the Civil War had ended and the enslaved were free; and,

WHEREAS, African Americans who had been slaves in the southwest celebrated June 19, commonly
known as Juneteenth Independence Day, as inspiration and encouragement for future generations, and
African Americans from the southwest continued the tradition observing Juneteenth through the
generations that followed; and,

WHEREAS, Juneteenth has continued to grow in widespread familiarity and celebration throughout the
entire country and is now a recognized federal holiday; now,

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the City Council, in partnership with Mayor McGuinness, hereby
recognizes June 19, 2026 as Juneteenth in the City of Pontiac; and further

RESOLVED, the City Council warmly welcomes the many programs and observances that are being
planned in the City of Pontiac to mark Juneteenth this year; and further

RESOLVED, the City of Pontiac supports the continued celebration of Juneteenth to provide an
opportunity for the people of Pontiac and the United States to learn more about the past and to better
understand this important, dark era in history.

Ayes: Carrington, Goodman, Jackson, James, Jones, Austin, and Campbell
No: None
Resolution Passed

Motors, which was based in our City of Pontiac, in January 1926 and was a roaring success, selling over
76,000 vehicles in their first year; and,

WHEREAS, the massive success of the Pontiac brand birthed the renamed Pontiac Motor Division of
General Motors, which was headquartered in our City of Pontiac for seventy years, and was a major
driver of our City’s growth; and,

WHEREAS, for generations the iconic Pontiac automobiles that became synonymous with our historic
City of Pontiac helped shape American culture, manufactured millions of vehicles, helped win World
War II for America with epic wartime production, generated great wealth for our citizens, our city, our
county, our region, our state, and our nation; and,

WHEREAS, the iconic Pontiac brand is turning 100 years old this year, the City of Pontiac has partnered
with Pontiac Transportation Museum for many celebratory activities, and the Pontiac-Oakland Club
International is holding their Annual Convention in the City of Pontiac in June 2026; now,
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Pontiac City Council and Pontiac Mayor Mike McGuinness
hereby celebrates the phenomenal milestone of Pontiac Motor Division’s 100th anniversary; and further
RESOLVED, the City hereby welcomes Pontiac-Oakland Club International and their guests to the City
of Pontiac for their Annual Convention; and further

RESOLVED, the City Council acknowledges the remarkable, rich, and enduring legacy of the products
and people that defined the Pontiac Motor Division and made an indelible, defining mark on our City’s
history.

26-118 (J) Resolution Authorizing the Initiation of the 30-Day Public Comment Period for the
Draft 2026 Annual Action Plan for Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Funds. Moved
by Councilperson James and seconded by Councilperson Goodman.

WHEREAS, the City of Pontiac is an entitlement community eligible to receive federal Community
Development Block Grant (CDBG) and HOME Investment Partnerships Program (HOME) funds from
the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD); and,

WHEREAS, HUD regulations require the City to prepare and submit an Annual Action Plan outlining
the proposed use of federal entitlement funds for Program Year 2026; and,

WHEREAS, the City has prepared a draft 2026 Annual Action Plan, which includes a proposed budget
and activities funded through the estimated allocation of $1,399,738.00 in CDBG funds and $428,550.91
in HOME funds; and,

WHEREAS, the draft 2026 Annual Action Plan will be made available for public review and comment
for a 30-day period beginning June 16, 2026, through July 16, 2026, in accordance with HUD
regulations and the City’s Citizen Participation Plan; and,

WHEREAS, a duly noticed public hearing was held on June 16, 2026, during a regular City Council
meeting to solicit public comments regarding the proposed activities and funding allocations contained
within the 2026 Annual Action Plan; and,

WHEREAS, the City has complied with the applicable requirements of the Housing and Community
Development Act of 1974, as amended, and all related federal regulations; now,

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the City Council of the City of Pontiac hereby authorizes the
release of the draft 2026 Annual Action Plan for the required 30-day public comment period; and further
RESOLVED, the City Council welcomes and encourages public participation during the public review
and comment period; and further

RESOLVED, that the City shall continue to comply with all applicable federal laws, regulations, and
program requirements in the administration and implementation of the activities identified in the 2026
Annual Action Plan.

Ayes: Carrington, Goodman, Jackson, James, Jones, Austin, and Campbell
No: None
Resolution Passed
