Supervisor Ron Milkey called the regular meeting of the Township of Manchester to order at 7:00 pm.

Seefeld, Trustee Lisa Moutinho, Trustee Mike Fusilier, Trustee Mike Walter.

FIRE DEPARTMENT:

    a)   Fire Department Activity Report: Responded to 51 calls for service in September 2025.

    b)   Fire Chief’s Report: Fire Chief Kouba reported that the officer’s meeting was held on September 16, 2025.
         Membership meeting and training was held on September 24, 2025 – sent minutes to the Board.

         Lt. Goodell conducted pump operations and truck driving training on October 8th, which was well attended.

         The state EMS inspection was on September 24th, and everything went well. We are good for another year.

         We have provided EMS coverage for all of the home football games without any incidents.

         We have placed the order for the replacement of U71. It is a 1-ton, single axle Chevy pickup. As stated last month we
         will install custom equipment boxes on the rails and will be using the old skid pump unit in the back. The purchase
         will be funded by the Dutchman Firefighters Association. There will be no cost to the township or the taxpayers.

         I would like to make the recommendation to hire Jeff Hannah. Jeff is a licensed paramedic and instructor who has been
         working for HVA for several years. Jeff lives in the city limits and will be a great asset to our department.

         Motion Milkey, second Fusilier to approve the Chief’s recommendation to hire Jeff Hannah as a firefighter/EMT.

         Ayes: All
         MOTION CARRIED
