Call to Order/Pledge of Allegiance: Bigford called the meeting to order at 6:01 p.m
Roll Call: Present — Aldrich, Bigford, Helmrich, Marcy, Osman. Also, present - DeFranco
Reports

1) Presentation(s): N/A

2) Treasurer’s Report — Helmrich delivered the treasurer's report and amended agenda
item 6 (1)(a) Total Bills and Payroll to $479,582.41.

3) Planning Commission Report — Osman delivered the report.

4) Manager Report — DeFranco delivered the Manager report, River Bluff Park work
continues, Quarterly report has been submitted to MISDA, Rural Readiness kickoff event
was held, New Community Co-Ordinator has been hired, Grant application for the Blue
Star Action Plan has been submitted by the Allegan Road Commission on behalf of

the Township.

Public Comment:

Trevor Sloughman requested an update on the trail near Captains Quarters.

Tom Bigford thanked the Commissioners and looks forward to collaborating with them.
Becky (6848 Dale Ct.) Addressed the trash build up at 6050 Dale Ct., there are
unlicensed vehicles, discarded tires, and other trash being collected and stored. It has
been 490 days since she reported it to the Township, 607 days since the first citation
was issued.

Brad Lubbers (County Commissioner) reported that all taxes have been turned over to
the County, taxable value has risen to 7.08%, Health Dept. reported that tic season will
be particularly bad this season and they are seeing tic species not normally found in the
area. Pictometry images have been updated. Michigan Geological Survey has been
ongoing on aquafers in the County. The County has until the end of this year to use up
ARPO funds residents with a well that is questionable can contact Allegan County to see
if they qualify for assistance to remedy their well.

Motion by Marcy, supported by Osman, to approve the agenda as amended, adding agenda
item 13.1 Deputy Supervisor appointment. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.

1) Commission Appointments
1. Planning Commission

Motion by Helmrich, supported by Marcy, to approve the appointment of Jackie Ground
and Ken Butler to the Planning Commission for 3-year terms set to expire on June 30,
2029. Motion carried by unanimous roll call vote (5-0).

2. ZBA

Motion by Osman, supported by Aldrich, to appoint Patrick Stewart as full member and
Rex Felker as alternate member to the Zoning Board of Appeals for a three-year term to
expire June 30, 2029. Motion carried by unanimous roll call vote (5-0).

2) Bond Authorizing Resolution and Lease Agreement for New Fire Station:

Motion by Aldrich, supported by Helmrich, to adopt Resolution No. 2026-12, Resolution
Authorizing Issuance of 2026 Capital Improvement Bonds (Limited Tax General
Obligation), authorizing the issuance of bonds in an amount not to exceed $5,000,000
for the acquisition and construction of a new fire station for the Saugatuck Fire District,
approving the Reimbursement and Lease Agreement between Saugatuck Township and
the Saugatuck Fire District as provided therein, and authorizing the Township Supervisor,
Treasurer, and other appropriate officials to take all actions necessary to complete the
sale, issuance, and delivery of the bonds. Motion carried by unanimous roll call vote (5-
0).

3) Part-Time Employee

Motion by Osman, supported by Helmrich, to approve the temporary part-time
employment of Thomas Bigford to provide administrative support to the Building,
Planning, and Assessing Departments at a rate of $25.00 per hour, for no more than 30
hours per week, as a non-exempt employee not eligible for Township benefits, and to
authorize the Township Manager to execute all necessary employment documents, with
compensation retroactive to June 1*, 2026. Motion carried by (4-0) vote, with Bigford
abstaining from vote.

4) Community Development Coordinator Employment Agreement

Motion by Marcy, supported by Aldrich, to approve the Community Development
Coordinator Employment Agreement as presented between Saugatuck Township and
Kate White. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
