     Commission's perspective, stating that if a moratorium is pursued, the Township should make full use
     of the 12-month period to carefully review the issue. He suggested that ordinance language could be
     drafted during that time, while delaying formal documentation until the Planning Commission has had
     an opportunity to fully evaluate the matter. Another resident questioned whether work on the
     moratorium is already underway and expressed concern that discussions may be occurring without
     being formally documented. A resident asked to clarify if the Board was going to talk about a planning
     commission board member resignation.

   17.    Old Business:
          A) Moratorium – Resolution 2026-6.1 – Board members discussed the proposed moratorium and the
                requirement to demonstrate progress after 12 months if an extension is sought, noting that it may be
                difficult to show sufficient progress within that timeframe. There was discussion about tabling the
                matter for a month to consult with the Planning Commission regarding how much time they would need
                to adequately prepare. While Board members generally supported implementing a moratorium, they
                debated when the timeline should begin. PC Chair addressed the Board, and our Planner indicated that
                he anticipated it would take approximately six months to develop a draft ordinance. PC Chair suggested
                that having the ordinance prepared would allow the Planning Commission time to catch up, enabling the
                Township to implement it if necessary. Ultimately, Don proposed adopting the moratorium effective
                June 1 for a one-year period, with the option to extend it if needed, and this proposal was approved by
                the Board. Motion by Vander Kuyl, seconded by Meyer, to approve Resolution 2026-06.1 to Enact a
                Temporary Moratorium on Data Centers.
                                                                                         Motion Carried 5-0
