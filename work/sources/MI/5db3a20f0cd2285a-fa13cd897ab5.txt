TOWNSHIP OF PINE GROVE COUNTY OF VAN BUREN, STATE OF MICHIGAN NOTICE OF ORDINANCE ADOPTION


To: The Residents and Property Owners of the Township of Pine Grove, Van Buren County,

Michigan, and Any Other Interested Persons:

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the following is a summary of Ordinance No. 06-03-2026,

which was adopted by the Pine Grove Township Board of Trustees on June 3, 2026. This

Ordinance is effective upon publication.

ORDINANCE NO. 06-03-2026 TEMPORARY DATA CENTER MORATORIUM ORDINANCE


SECTION I. FINDINGS. The Section sets out the findings of fact that establish the need for the

Township to institute a moratorium on the processing of any applications for data centers as the

Township studies the potential public health, safety, and welfare concerns of data centers on its

residents and existing land uses.

SECTION II. MORATORIUM/TERM OF MORATORIUM. The moratorium is for a period

of six (6) months from the effective date of this ordinance, or until the effective date of any

ordinance regulating data centers, whichever is first.

SECTION III. EXTENSION OF MORATORIUM. The moratorium may be extended by

resolution of the Township Board at any time before its expiration.

SECTION IV. SEVERABILITY. If any section of this Ordinance shall be declared invalid, the

rest of the Ordinance shall not be affected by such a determination.

SECTION V. REPEAL. This Section provides that any ordinances that are in conflict with this

Ordinance are repealed.

SECTION VI. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Section provides that the Ordinance shall take effect

30 days after publication following adoption.
