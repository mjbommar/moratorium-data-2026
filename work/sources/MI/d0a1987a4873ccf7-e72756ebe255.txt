Ordinances

About

Local ordinances are an important part of living in the Charter Township of Comstock. Ordinances are a statement of the Township’s values and a binding agreement within our community. For your convenience, local ordinances have been codified and are available online in a Municode Library, which you can reach from the links provided on this page.

Please use the document to your right to reference new ordinances that may have not yet been codified into our Code of Ordinances.
