                                               MEMORANDUM
            Date: September 4, 2026
            To:   Planning Commission
            From: Jeffrey M. Polkowski, Director of Development
            RE: Extending a Moratorium on the Approval of Data Centers

            On March 23, 2026, the Board of Trustees of the Charter Township of Waterford adopted
            a Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment enacting a temporary moratorium on the
            submission of site plan review applications for Data Centers under Section 4-004 of the
            Zoning Ordinance until October 1, 2026. Initially enacted for six months, the moratorium
            proposed to be extended an additional six months, now set to expire at the end of
            September 2026, unless rescinded earlier by the Board of Trustees. This pause is intended
            to allow Township officials sufficient time to review existing zoning regulations
            pertaining to Data Centers and consider the implementation of new or revised regulations
            as necessary to address community health, safety, and welfare concerns. Additional time
            is needed to evaluate possible regulations to recommend to the Board of Trustees.

            Over the past several months, communities throughout Michigan and the broader
            Midwest have experienced a rapid increase in interest from private developers seeking to
            construct large-scale data center facilities. These facilities often involve substantial land
            areas, significant electrical demand, intensive water usage for cooling systems, on-site
            backup generation, fuel storage, and enhanced security infrastructure. While such
            developments may provide certain economic benefits, they also present complex land
            use, infrastructure, and public policy considerations that require careful evaluation at the
            local level.

            In addition to state and federal regulations governing utilities, environmental compliance,
            and telecommunications infrastructure, the Township retains authority under the
            Michigan Zoning Enabling Act to regulate land use in furtherance of the public health,
            safety, and welfare. The Township’s current Zoning Ordinance does not specifically
            address large-scale data centers as a distinct land use category, nor does it establish
            standards related to their location, buffering, energy demand, noise from mechanical
            equipment and generators, water consumption, fuel storage, building massing, or
            compatibility with surrounding uses.

            Through internal planning and administrative review conducted by the Township’s
            Development Services Department, it has been identified that the substantial and
            potentially long-term impacts of data center facilities on electrical infrastructure capacity,
            public utilities, emergency services, traffic patterns, land consumption, and overall
            community character warrant further study. Accordingly, Development Services
            recommends evaluating whether additional zoning standards, performance criteria, and
review procedures are necessary to ensure that any future data center development is
appropriately sited and regulated.

This proposed moratorium would impose a temporary suspension on the acceptance of
applications for rezoning, site plan approval, special land use approval, building permits,
and business registrations for new data center facilities within Waterford Township. The
purpose of this moratorium is to allow Township Staff and the Township Attorney
sufficient time to study the impacts of such facilities, review best practices from other
Michigan communities, and draft potential amendments to the Township’s Zoning
Ordinance and Code of Ordinances addressing the regulation of data centers.

The definition of data center to be considered for this moratorium is as follows:

       Data Center. A facility or group of buildings whose primary function is the
       housing, operation, and maintenance of computer servers, data storage systems,
       telecommunications equipment, and associated infrastructure used for the
       processing, storage, or transmission of digital information. Such facilities may
       include backup power generation, cooling systems, fuel storage, and security
       infrastructure. This term does not include server rooms or data-processing
       facilities that are accessory and incidental to a principal permitted use.

By imposing this temporary moratorium, Waterford Township will ensure that any future
policy decisions regarding data center development are made deliberately, transparently,
and in a manner that protects the Township’s long-term infrastructure capacity, fiscal
stability, and land use planning objectives.
Planning Commission Recommendation and Findings
The Planning Commission reviewed this proposed zoning ordinance amendment and
resolution at the regularly scheduled meeting on August 25, 2026, and resolved
unanimously to forward a favorable recommendation to the Township Board.
Motions
Based upon the Planning Commission’s favorable recommendation at the August 25,
2026 regular meeting of the Planning Commission, should the Board want to consider
adopting the zoning ordinance amendment, the appropriate motion would be to introduce
the attached Ordinance and schedule it for possible adoption at the September 28, 2026
meeting. However, if the Board does not want to adopt the zoning ordinance amendment,
the appropriate motion would be to not introduce the ordinance and deny the zoning
amendment.

In addition to the proposed zoning ordinance amendment, it is recommended that the
Board of Trustees adopt a resolution extending the six-month moratorium on accepting
applications for site plan approval and business registration for new Data Centers within
the Township. Property or business owners affected by the moratorium may request a
hearing within 45 days to demonstrate that it prevents viable economic use of their
property or violates state or federal law. If the Board determines the request is valid,
relief from the moratorium may be granted.

                                     CHARTER TOWNSHIP

                    RESOLUTION EXTENDING A MORATORIUM ON
                   THE APPROVAL OF NEW DATA CENTERS WITHIN
                            WATERFORD TOWNSHIP

       WHEREAS, the Charter Township Act, Public Act 359 of 1947, MCL 42.15 authorizes a
township board to enact ordinances necessary to provide for the health and safety of people within
the Township.

      WHEREAS, the Township is aware of increased development activity throughout
Michigan and surrounding Midwestern communities involving large-scale data center facilities;
and

        WHEREAS, data center facilities typically involve substantial land areas, intensive
electrical demand, significant water usage for cooling systems, on-site backup power generation,
fuel storage, enhanced security infrastructure, and large building massing that may materially
impact surrounding properties and public infrastructure; and

       WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees finds that it is beneficial to the public health, safety,
and welfare of its residents to study the potential impacts of data center development within the
Township and to consider whether amendments to the Zoning Ordinance and Code of Ordinances
are necessary to ensure appropriate location, design, and operational standards; and

         WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees finds that it is in the public interest to extend for an
additional six (6) months a temporary moratorium on applications for rezoning, special land use
approval, site plan approval, building permits, and business registration for new data center
facilities in order to review and consider necessary regulations and preserve the status quo during
such review.

       BE IT RESOLVED, by the Waterford Township Board of Trustees that a moratorium be
imposed barring the acceptance of applications for site plan approval and business registration for
new data centers to locate within the Township shall be extended for an additional period of six
(6) months, or the date that the Board of Trustees repeals this Resolution, whichever occurs first.

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that during the 6-month moratorium, or any extension
thereof, the Board of Trustees, along with the Planning Commission and appropriate staff and
consultants, shall attend to reviewing and making recommendations on amendments to the Zoning
Ordinance and Code of Ordinances, considering the public health, safety, and welfare of its
residents, and if amendments are determined to be necessary, preparing and recommending such
amendments for consideration by the Board of Trustees.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that an aggrieved property owner or business owner may
request and be entitled to a hearing by the Board of Trustees for the purpose of attempting to
demonstrate that the moratorium will preclude all viable economic use of their property or
otherwise violate applicable provisions of state or federal law. Said petitioner shall, in writing and



                                                  1
directed to the Township Clerk, request a hearing that describes the grounds of the request. The
hearing shall be held during a Board of Trustee meeting within forty-five (45) days of receipt of
the request. Upon concluding the hearing, the Board of Trustees shall determine whether the
petitioner has made the required demonstration and if so, shall grant relief from the moratorium.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that notice of this Resolution shall be posted at the
Township Hall and on the Township website throughout the moratorium period, or any extension
granted herein.
