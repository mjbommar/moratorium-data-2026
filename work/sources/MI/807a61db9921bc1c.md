# Draft Data Center Ordinance and Limited Moratorium on the development of Data Centers – Ordinance 132 - Erie Township

source: https://erietownshipmi.gov/limited-moratorium-on-the-development-of-data-centers-ordinance-132/
fetched: 2026-09-23T11:49:52+00:00

The Erie Township Planning Commission is currently working with planners from the Spicer Group to create a Data Center Ordinance. A DRAFT copy is available here: [DRAFT Erie Township Data Center Ordinance\_September15PC\_Clean](https://erietownshipmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/DRAFT-Erie-Township-Data-Center-Ordinance_September15PC_Clean.pdf)

The Erie Township Board adopted a limited moratorium on the development of data centers ordinance on February 10, 2026.

The ordinance can be viewed here: [data center moratorium.](https://erietownshipmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/data-center-moratorium.ord-132.pdf)[ord 132](https://erietownshipmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/data-center-moratorium.ord-132.pdf)
