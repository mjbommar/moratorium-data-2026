ORDINANCE NO. 26--001


TOWNSHIP OF CHESANING SAGINAW COUNTY, MICHIGAN ADOPTED: May 7, 2026 PUBLICATION DATE: May 17, 2026 EFFECTIVE: May 18, 2026


An ordinance of the Township of C CHESANING, Saginaw County, Michigan, to enact a 12-

month moratorium on Data Centers/Facilities in the Township of CHESANING; to protect the

health, safety and general welfare of CHESANING Township’s residents and property owners

through t this Ordinance; t to provide a name for this Ordinance; t to provide definitions; to provide for a

purpose and findings; t to provide a moratorium; to provide direction to the C CHESANING Township

Planning Commission; to provide that pending proceedings are not affected; to provide for

severability, to repeal all Ordinances or parts of Ordinances in conflict herewith; to p provide for an

Effective Date and to direct the publication of this Ordinance or a legally-permissible summary.

THE TOWNSHIP OF CHESANING SAGINAW COUNTY, MICHIGAN ORDAINS:


SECTION I NAME


This Ordinance shall be known and may be cited as the “Chesaning Township Data

Centers/Facilities Moratorium Ordinance.”

SECTION II DEFINITIONS


A. Any undefined word(s) or term(s) used in this Ordinance shall be given its/their

plain and ordinary meaning.  A dictionary may be consulted in the interpretation of an undefined

word or term used in this Ordinance.

B. The following words/terms used in this Ordinance shall have the following

specific definitions:

1. “Data Centers/Facilities” means any building, dedicated space within a building,

or group of buildings a primary purpose of which is to house computer systems

and/or associated components such as telecommunications, digital data, or storage

systems.

2. “This Ordinance” means the Chesaning Township Data Centers/Facilities

Moratorium Ordinance.

3. “Person” means an individual(s), corporation, company, partnership, or entity.

4. “Planning Commission” means the Chesaning Township Planning Commission.

5. “Township” means the Township of Chesaning.

6. “Township Board” means the Chesaning Township Board of Trustees.

SECTION III PURPOSE AND FINDINGS


A. The purpose of this moratorium is to provide sufficient time for the Chesaning

Township Planning Commission to fully explore, analyze, research, and make recommendations

to the Chesaning Township Board regarding potential zoning regulations applicable to Data

Centers/Facilities and related appurtenances and infrastructure. In support of this Ordinance, the

Chesaning Township Board has determined the following:

1. The Township has become aware of an increase of Data Centers/Facilities in the

region, which is a more-recent and emerging land use. It is clear that interest in

these Data Centers/Facilities is increasing across the state, and that applications

for the construction and location of Data Centers/Facilities within the Township

may be imminent.

2. The integration of these Data Centers/Facilities within the Township's existing

land uses requires suitable regulations and controls for the protection of the

health, safety and welfare of all of the Township’s residents and property owners

and proprietors.

3. Pursuant to the Michigan Zoning Enabling Act (Act 110 of 2006, MCL 125.3101

et seq.,) the Township has adopted a Zoning Ordinance, which is a comprehensive

land-use plan.

4. The Township wishes to consider whether amendments to its Zoning Ordinance

to regulate the establishment and use of Data Centers/Facilities is necessary in

order to better protect the public health, safety, and welfare of Township’s

residents and property owners.

5. Imposing a moratorium on the siting, establishing, installation, construction,

and/or maintenance of a Data Center(s)/facilities, on a limited temporary basis, is

reasonable and necessary to allow the Township Planning Commission time to

fully explore, analyze, research and develop proposed zoning amendments and

make recommendations to the Chesaning Township Board regarding potential

amendments to the Township's Zoning Ordinance applicable to Data

Centers/Facilities.

6. A moratorium should and shall be imposed upon the issuance of any and all

siting, establishing, installing, constructing, or maintaining of a Data

Center(s)/facility/facilities, as well as any and all permits, licenses, and/or

approvals for any property subject to or under the jurisdiction of the Township's

Zoning Ordinance for the establishment and/or use of Data

Center(s)/facility/facilities for 12 months from the effective date of this Ordinance

or until this Ordinance is repealed, whichever occurs first.

SECTION IV MORATORIUM


A moratorium is hereby imposed on any and all siting, establishing, installing,

constructing, or maintaining of Data Center(s)/Facility/Facilities, as well as on the issuance of

any and all permits, licenses, and/or approvals for any property subject to or under the

jurisdiction of the Township's Zoning Ordinance for the establishment and/or use of Data

Center(s)/Facility/Facilities for 12 months from the effective date of this Ordinance or until this

Ordinance is repealed, whichever occurs first.  Before this moratorium expires, the Township

may by Ordinance extend the moratorium for up to six (6) additional months to allow sufficient

time to complete amendments to its Zoning Ordinance, if necessary.

SECTION V DIRECTION TO TOWNSHIP PLANNING COMMISSION


The Chesaning Township Planning Commission is hereby directed to research and

consider regulations relating to Data Center(s)/Facility/Facilities and, if appropriate, develop

proposed amendments to the Chesaning Township Zoning Ordinance which address, among

other things, the design, location, setbacks, land degradation, seasonal sun aperture, and other

matters deemed appropriate to regulate the siting, installation, construction, maintenance,

operation, decommissioning and dismantlement of Data Center(s)/Facility/Facilities in

Chesaning Township.

SECTION VI PENDING PROCEEDINGS NOT AFFECTED.


Nothing in this ordinance shall be construed to affect any suit or proceeding impending in

any court, or any rights acquired, or liability incurred, or any cause or causes of action acquired or

existing, under any act or ordinance hereby repealed as cited in t this ordinance; nor shall any just or

legal right or remedy of any character be lost, impaired, or affected by this ordinance.

SECTION VII SEVERABILITY


Should any section, clause, or provision of this Ordinance b be declared t to be invalid by a court

of competent jurisdiction, the same shall not affect the v validity of t the Ordinance as a whole or any

other part t thereof other than the parts so declared to be invalid.  The C Chesaning Township Board of

Trustees hereby declares that it would have enacted this Ordinance even without whatever provision

may be declared invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction.

SECTION VIII REPEAL OF CONFLICTING ORDINANCES


All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

SECTION IX EFFECTIVE DATE


This Ordinance and the rules, regulations, provisions, requirements, orders, and matters

established and adopted hereby shall take effect the day following publication as required by law

following adoption by the Township Board.

SECTION X TOWNSHIP PUBLICATION


The Township Clerk is hereby ordered and directed to cause this Ordinance to be

published, either in full or in the form of a legally-permitted summary, in a newspaper of general

circulation within Chesaning Township.

This Ordinance is hereby declared to have been adopted by the Chesaning Township

Board of Trustees, County of Saginaw, State of Michigan, at a regular meeting held on the 7th

day of May, 2026 and ordered to be given publication in the manner prescribed by law.
