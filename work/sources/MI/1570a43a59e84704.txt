                                   WILLIAMSTOWN TOWNSHIP
                            Board Meeting Synopsis – September 9, 2026
The Williamstown Township Board held a regular Board Meeting on Wednesday, September 9, 2026 with
Supervisor Poth, Clerk Cleveland, Treasurer VanErp, Trustee Brinker, Trustee Duffy, and Trustee Steinberg
present. The Board approved the following:
   • Consent Agenda
   • MDOT Resolution for Lothamer 5K Charity Run.
   • Contracting with C2AE for engineering & design services for the Township Park.
   • Amendment of the Neighborhood Road Policy to address recent changes in funding.
The next regular Board meeting will be held September 30, 2026 at 6:30pm in the Community Room of the
Township Hall, 4990 Zimmer Rd., Williamston. Visit williamstownmi.gov for the latest agenda, documents, and
updates. Phone (517) 655-3193.
