CHARTER TOWNSHIP OF YORK BOARD OF TRUSTEES REGULAR MEETING MINUTES

Tuesday, February 10, 2026, 7:00 P.M.

The Charter Township of York Board of Trustees held a Regular Board Meeting called to order at 7:00 p.m. by Supervisor

Ludwig. He then led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Roll Call of Board Members: Sally Louis, Tom Gary, Kayleigh Zupi, Helen Neill, Jason McGovern, and John Hargrove.

Moved by Louis to approve the February 10, 2026, Regular Agenda as amended, adding New Business Item 8 “Appoint

Representatives to Attend a Facilitation Conference with Township Attorney.” Seconded by Neill.

Discussion: None. Voice Vote: Aye: 6; Nay: 0; Absent: 1. Motion carried.

VERBAL REPORTS

Corporal Carter, r, WCSO – January 2026 statistics reported, new report format. Update on speed sign given.

Mike Siefker, r, Zoning/Building Depts. – Building and Zoning report given for January 2026. 1010 Mooreville Rd update

given. Fire hydrants off Platt Rd have been used without authorization after Highland Treatment winterized them; they

will need to be winterized again.

BOARD REPORTS

Trustee Gary – Planning Commission met last night, light agenda. Resolution to formally deny Ricky Steele’s site plan for

gate construction adopted.

Clerk Zupi – Clerk’s office is continuing to reconcile the General Ledger in preparation for the 25-26 FY audit. Gearing up

for elections beginning in August, no May election is scheduled. The Environmental Committee is hosting a Conservation

Field Day at Mary McCann Park in partnership with NWTF to clear out invasive species. Public is welcome and

encouraged to attend. The Non-Motorized Transportation Committee held am open house on February 4, 2026. Less

attended than the prior two years, but still heard a lot of good feedback for the committee to consider as the project

continues. Reminder that going forward the committee meetings will be on the third Wednesday of the month at 7 pm,

with Supervisor Ludwig at the Board Representative. The Personnel Committee is meeting February 24th to continue

Page 1 of 3

discussing employee raises and upcoming contracts for the 26-27 FY. Lastly, finally received the METRO Act Permit

application from Prime-One, and both parties' attorneys are finalizing the actual permit for the Board to consider.

Treasurer Louis – A week from today is the last day to pay winter taxes; the due date is extended until February 17th due

to the holiday. Met with Supervisor Ludwig, Clerk Zupi, and Lt. Thompson from WCSO for an update on the Sheriff’s

Department. After last month’s Board meeting, emailed a spreadsheet outlining different scenarios for what direction

the Board chose to go regarding Saline Area Fire Department. Working with Mike Siefker to source film for exterior

public doors to reduce glare inside the lobby and front counter area.

Trustee Neill – No meetings to report. Reviewing the 26-27 FY budget and providing feedback.

Trustee Hargrove – MAFD Fire Board met end of January, discussed purchase/lease of a new tanker. Brief discussion

regarding cost recovery with the Fire Chief. At the SAFD Fire Board meeting, one of the partner municipalities’ attorneys

provided additional feedback regarding fire authority. SAFD election of officers took place, budget for 26-27 FY was

approved.

Supervisor Ludwig – SAFD steering committee meeting tomorrow morning to review fire authority feedback. Met with

Trevor Feazel from WCRC last week to discuss road 2026 projects and pricing. Update on County matching portion and

options provided. Lawn care contract for Township Hall, parks, cemeteries, etc., to be bid for the 2026 season.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS:

1. Updated Proposed Budget for 2026-2027 FY

Update provided by Supervisor Ludwig, thank you to Trustees Gary and Neill for their questions and help. May need a

higher transfer from the General Fund to the Public Safety Fund to support the two fire departments and the Sheriff’s

Office.

NEW BUSINESS:

1. Resolution #2026-03: To Amend Resolution #2025-09 to Adopt the General Appropriations Act, 2025-2026 FY

Moved by Neill, seconded by Hargrove, to adopt Resolution #2026-03, to Amend Resolution #2025-09, that established

a General Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 2025-2026. Discussion: There will be another budget amendment at the

March regular meeting. The fiscal year budget is adopted in March using the best estimates by the Board of Trustees,

but there are unanticipated expenses, or lack thereof, that require budget amendments throughout the fiscal year.

Moved by Gary, seconded by Zupi, to appoint Brad Neuhart to the Planning Commission to fill the vacancy created by Dave

expressed interest in joining the Planning Commission once more after he retired.

Voice Vote: Aye: 6; Nay: 0; Absent: 1. Motion carried.

4. Saline Area Fire Department Capital Improvement and Operating Budgets for 25-26 FY

Moved by Hargrove, seconded by Gary, to approve the Saline Area Fire Department Capital Improvement Budget of

$46,980.00 and Operating Budget of $768,807.00 for 2026-2027, totalling $815,787.00. Discussion: Roughly an

$88,000.00 increase. Wages, based on the contract, are about a 16% increase within the budget. Other increases are at

about 3%. The wage increase will get SAFD personnel to a median wage for the County.

This resolution shall be known as the Charter Township of York General Appropriations Act.

general circulation and a public hearing on the proposed budget was held on March 11, 2025.

allocated millage of one (1) mill and various miscellaneous revenues. Estimated township public safety fund

revenues for fiscal year 2025/2026 totaling $893,500.00 includes voter-authorized Public Safety millage of

one-and-one-half (1-1/2) mills, plus a transfer of $500,000.00 from the General Fund and various

miscellaneous Public Safety Fund revenues for a total of $1,393,000.00.

Resolution  #2026-04

Resolution of Gratitude to Chuck Tellas

Whereas, Chuck Tellas  has served the Charter Township of York as a Planning

Commissioner, Trustee, and Supervisor, and

over 27 years, and

express their most sincere appreciation to Chuck Tellas for his many years of

dedicated service, and be it further

records of the Charter Township of York.
