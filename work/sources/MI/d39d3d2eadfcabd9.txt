PRESENTATION & DISCUSSION
Pat Hanniford from Pfeffer, Hanniford & Palka, CPAs (PHP), presented York Township’s audit report for the fiscal year
beginning April 1, 2025, through March 31, 2026. PHP has issued an unqualified opinion, as they have in the past,
meaning that the financial statements, disclosures, and footnotes are fully in accordance with generally accepted
accounting principles. It is the highest level of assurance that an outside auditor can give York Township. The audit went
very smoothly.
Compared to similar townships, York Township is in an excellent position. There is no general fund debt, and no
underfunded retirement benefits. Graphs showing the progression of the fund balances over the last 15 years were
discussed. 25-26 FY was a successful financial year, and the township is in a good financial position.

                                 Resolution #2026-19
                     Resolution to Extend Data Center Moratorium

       At a Regular Meeting of the Township Board of the Charter Township of York,
Washtenaw County, Michigan held at the Township Hall in said Township, on the 11th
day of August, 2026, at 7:00 p.m.


PRESENT:      Dave Ludwig, Kayleigh Zupi, John Hargrove, Helen Neill, Jason McGovern,
              Tom Gary
ABSENT:       Sally Louis

        The following preamble and resolution were offered by Zupi and supported by
Gary.
WHEREAS, the York Charter Township Zoning Ordinance has no regulatory provisions
for the construction, development and use of commercial data centers; and

WHEREAS, the York Charter Township Board of Trustees adopted Ordinance No. 167
at its March 10, 2026, meeting, with an effective date of March 18, 2026, to place a
temporary moratorium of 180 days on development of commercial data centers in the
Township; and

WHEREAS, the moratorium expires on September 14, 2026; and

WHEREAS, the general purpose of Ordinance No. 167 is to provide an adequate
opportunity for the Planning Commission and professional consultants of the Township
to develop appropriate and adequate regulations and safeguards to protect the local
community from potential adverse effects caused by the development and operation of
data centers; and

WHEREAS, the Township through its Township Planner, Township Attorney and
Township Planning Commission has worked to research, review and prepare
amendments to the York Charter Township Zoning Ordinance to provide regulations and
safeguards to protect the local community from potential adverse effects caused by the
development and operation of data centers, however the process is not complete and the
Zoning Ordinance has not yet been amended; and

WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 167 provides in Section 4 that the “moratorium may be
extended by resolution of the Township Board for additional 180 day periods or until such
time as the Township Board is satisfied with the adoption of policies and ordinance
regulations to adequately address public health, safety, and welfare concerns related to
permitting data center uses in the Township;” and
WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has proceeded in good faith to develop the
appropriate standards for the regulation of proposed data centers, but it requires
additional time to complete its process and submit a recommended Zoning Ordinance
amendment to the Township Board for approval; and

WHEREAS, an extension of the moratorium is necessary to allow adequate time for the
adoption of policies and ordinance regulations to adequately address public health,
safety, and welfare concerns related to permitting data center uses in the Township.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the moratorium established in Ordinance
No. 167, the Data Center Moratorium Ordinance, is hereby extended for an additional
180 days or until such time as the Township Board has adopted policies and ordinance
regulations to adequately address public health, safety, and welfare concerns related to
permitting data center uses in the Township, whichever occurs first.

ROLL CALL VOTE:
     AYES: McGovern, Gary, Zupi, Ludwig, Neill, Hargrove
       NAYES: None
       ABSENT: Louis
       ABSTAIN: None
RESOLUTION DECLARED ADOPTED.
