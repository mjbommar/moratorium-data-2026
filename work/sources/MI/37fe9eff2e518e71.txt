  5.I. Fall Hydrant Flushing Began September 8th

         Waterford Township DPW will perform Fall hydrant flushing September 8th through
         mid-October. Flushing is done between 4-8 pm, Monday through Thursday.
         Customers may see brown water coming from their faucets and/or in their toilets. If
         that happens, please flush the lines by running cold water in the bathtub or laundry
         tub until it runs clear. Please contact the Waterford Township Department of Public
         Works at 248-674-2278 with any questions or concerns.

         The Township will be hosting a moderated Town Hall Meeting on Wednesday,

         Questions may also be emailed in advance to communications@waterfordmi.gov by
         5:00 p.m. on September 15th. The meeting will be broadcast on Municipal Channel

5.IV. 35th Annual Walk And Roll For Waterford Meals On Wheels

       Bring the family out Saturday, September 19th for the annual fundraising walk (and
       roll) in support of Waterford's Meals on Wheels program! Just $30 for adults, kids
       participate free with a registered adult. Free t-shirt with pre-registration, and lunch is
       included. Check-in begins at 10:15am, and the walk & roll starts at 11am. This event
       helps feed Waterford's homebound seniors more than 1,200 meals each week! Once
       you participate, you'll want to make this an annual family tradition.

 5.X. Seeking New Member For Drayton Plains Nature Center Advisory Committee

        Waterford Township is seeking a new member to serve on the Drayton Plains Nature
        Center Advisory Committee. Waterford’s boards, committees and commissions
        provide opportunities for citizens with a wide variety of talents and experience to work
        together and make our community a stronger, more vibrant place to live, work and
        play.

        We invite you to bring your ideas and insight and to actively participate in the mission
        to keep Waterford moving in the right direction for the future of our residents, business
        owners and visitors.
