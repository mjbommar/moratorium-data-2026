13) Other New Business

   1) Ordinance 2026-04 – Tracer Wire Requirement for Non-Metallic Water Service Lines.

   Ordinance No. 2026-04, an ordinance to amend Section 36-152 of the Township Code of Ordinances
   to require the installation of tracer wire when non-metallic material is used for a water service line
   connected to the Township's water system. The proposed ordinance establishes the tracer wire
   requirement as a condition of connecting to and receiving service from the Township's water
   system. As the owner and operator of the water system, the Township has independent authority to
   establish reasonable requirements governing service connections.

   Motion by Helmrich, supported by Osman, to adopt Ordinance No. 2026-04, an ordinance to amend
   Section 36-152 of the Saugatuck Township Code of Ordinances to require the installation of tracer
   wire when non-metallic water service pipe is used in connection with the Township water system.
   Attached: 1. Ordinance 2026-04 to Amend Section 36-152 of the Saugatuck Township Code of
   Ordinances. Motion carried by unanimous roll call vote (5-0).

   2) Saugatuck City Consent Agreement for STFD Sewer Connection

   Consent Agreement between Saugatuck Township, the City of Saugatuck, and the Saugatuck
   Township Fire District that would allow the Fire District to connect its Fire Station property to the
   City of Saugatuck's sanitary sewer main on Maple Street. Fire Station property does not have
   frontage on Maple Street and is not a residential lot, the Township's Sewer System Operations
   Contract with the City requires a consent agreement before the connection may be approved. The
   term of the proposed agreement is for a period of thirty (30) years.

   Motion by Aldrich, supported by Marcy, to approve the Consent Agreement for Sanitary Sewer
   Service between the City of Saugatuck, Saugatuck Township, and the Saugatuck Township Fire
   District. Motion carried by unanimous roll call vote (5-0).
