# Data Center FAQ - City of Gibraltar

source: https://cityofgibraltarmi.gov/data-center-faq/
fetched: 2026-09-23T11:50:04+00:00

● [SURVEY RESULTS: Community Input on Proposed Data Center Development](https://cityofgibraltarmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Community-Input-on-Proposed-Data-Center-Development.pdf) (Added 5/19/2026)
From 4/23/2026 – 5/7/2026, the City issued a public survey to its residents seeking input regarding the proposed Raeden data center project in the community. This survey aimed to assess public awareness, attitudes, and priorities related to a proposed data center project. Results will be used to inform planning, policy discussions, and community engagement efforts. Responses are reported in a summary form and will remain anonymous. Participation was voluntary. This survey was intended to gather community input and does not represent a final decision by the City.

● [FAQ: Project Wall-I Data Center Proposal by Raeden](https://cityofgibraltarmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026.03.31-Updated-Frequently-Asked-Questions.pdf) (Updated 3/31/2026)
Utilizing information from Raeden, independent resources, and other local municipalities, this FAQ will be updated to reflect unbiased facts on data centers generally and the specific Raeden project proposed in Gibraltar. If you have a question that is not addressed in this FAQ, please email it to City Administrator Witherspoon, at rwitherspoon@cityofgibraltarmi.gov.

● [Economic Impact Study](https://cityofgibraltarmi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Gibraltar-Economic-Impact-04.22.2026.pdf) (Added 4/23/2026)
Raeden has provided the City with the attached economic impact study. Please note that this study includes the economic impact of both the City of Gibraltar and the Gibraltar School District.
