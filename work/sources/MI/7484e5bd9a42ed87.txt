MEMO
     To:       Fenton Township Planning Commission

  From:        Michael Deem, AICP, Zoning Administrator

  Date:        February 18, 2026

Subject:       ZO26-001 Data Center Zoning Amendment


At the February 17, 2026 meeting, The Township Board adopted an ordinance placing a
moratorium on the review of data centers for 12 months to allow the Planning
Commission an opportunity to research and develop reasonable regulations for data
centers.

The moratorium defines a data center as:

 “Data Center”. A data center is a specialized physical facility or building that centrally
houses IT operations, computing machines, and data storage systems. It may act as the
backbone of digital infrastructure, providing secure, controlled environments for servers,
networking equipment, and data management, often supporting cloud computing and
high-speed transmission.

Attached with this memo are resources provided by Michigan Association of Planners and
Michigan Township Association on data centers. Information includes:
     What Michigan Local Governments Should Know about Data Centers prepared by
       the Center of Empowering Communities, University of Michigan
     Data Center Development in Michigan: Economic and Fiscal Impact Analysis
       prepared by Anderson Economic Group, LLC
     Catching Heat: Using Waste Heat Generated from Data Centers, prepared y
       reimagine Appalachia.
     Enterprise Data Center sales and Use Tax Emption Guideline, Michigan Strategic
       Fund

Staff recommends discussion to focus on defining data centers and identifying additional
information needed to make an informed decision. Consideration for the definition may
include language relating to the intensity of use, amount of power draw, and amount of
water usage.
