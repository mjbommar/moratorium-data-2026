A. Attorney’s Report: Attorney Goldenbogen reported that the blight situation at
2713 Division received a judgement for abatement. The owner has 15 days to
remove the blight to the satisfaction of the township and if that doesn’t happen
the township will remove it with those costs assessed to the property. He also
stated that the Resolution to Correct Scriveners Error that is on the agenda is
entirely proper and was done at his recommendation.

PERFORMANCE RESOLUTION FOR MUNICIPALITIES

This Performance Resolution (Resolution)is required by the Michigan Department of
Transportation for purposes of issuing to a Municipality an "Individual Permit for Use of State
Highway Right of Way", and/or an “Annual Application and Permit for Miscellaneous
Operations within State Highway Right of Way".

"MUNICIPALITY," periodically applies to the Michigan Department of Transportation,

vendors and contractors of the MUNICIPALITY, or their subcontractors or any other
person not a party to the PERMIT without the DEPARTMENT’S specific prior written
consent and notwithstanding the issuance of the PERMIT. Any claims by any contractor
or subcontractor will be the sole responsibility of the MUNICIPALITY.

The MUNICIPALITY shall take no unlawful action or conduct, which arises either directly

results in claims being asserted against or judgment being imposed against the State of
Michigan, the Michigan Transportation Commission, the DEPARTMENT, and all officers,
agents and employees thereof and those contracting governmental bodies performing
permit activities for the DEPARTMENT and all officers, agents, and employees thereof,

of the PERMIT, it will be considered as a breach of the PERMIT thereby giving the State
of Michigan, the DEPARTMENT, and/or the Michigan Transportation Commission a right
to seek and obtain any necessary relief or remedy, including, but not by way of limitation,
a judgment for money damages.
