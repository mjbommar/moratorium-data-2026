# Data Center Ordinance | Pittsfield Charter Township, MI

fetched: 2026-09-23T11:51:02+00:00

**View the draft language for the** [**Data Center Ordinance**](https://www.pittsfield-mi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/13073/Data-Center-Draft-Ordinance-5-22-26)

**View the** [**Public Survey Summary**](https://www.pittsfield-mi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14021/Data-Centers-Survey-Response-Summary)

In 2025, the Township Board of Trustees adopted a temporary moratorium on new data center development. The temporary moratorium is set to expire in November 2026.

Over the past six months, a draft ordinance has been prepared to address topics such as energy use, water conservation, noise, traffic, environmental protection, mitigation, and long-term site management. The Township also collected public input during May and June 2026 through a Public Survey, receiving over 300 responses.

On June 18, 2026, at a regular Planning Commission meeting, discussion was held regarding the draft language. No public comment occurred at the meeting.

On August 6, 2026, a public hearing was held at a regular Planning Commission meeting. Public comments were received by the Planning Commission, and appropriate revisions were made to the draft ordinance. The Planning Commission voted 6-0, with one member absent, to recommend the Zoning Ordinance Amendment to the Board of Trustees for approval.

On September 9, 2026, a public hearing was held at a regular Board of Trustees meeting. Public comments were received by the Board of Trustees and discussion was held on the draft ordinance. The Board of Trustees voted 7-0 to approve the First Reading of the Zoning Ordinance Amendment

The Board of Trustees will hold a second [public hearing](https://www.pittsfield-mi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14522/ZOA-26-236-Data-Center-Ordinance---Second-BOT-Public-Hearing) and consider approval of the Second Reading for the Data Center Zoning Ordinance Amendment at its regular meeting on Wednesday, October 14th at 6:30 pm in Morris Hall, 6201 W. Michigan Ave.
