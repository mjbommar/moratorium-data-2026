Ed English closed the regular meeting at 7:07 p.m.

**a. Public Hearing – Special Assessment Roll for Fire Protection Operation Expenditures**

Mr. English opened the public hearing regarding Special Assessment Rolls for Fire Protection Operation Expenditures at 7:08 p.m.

Mr. English noted to run Fire Station costs between $275,000 – $280,000. The assessment will be for 1.3 mils as noted in the Newspaper. This will begin with the December property tax bills. He pointed out that Fabius Township is responsible for half of the Fire Protection Operation costs.

**Public Comments –**

One individual noted that the Fire Department never asked for anything that they did not need.

A motion approve the Special Assessment Rolls for Fire Protection Operation Expenditures was made by Lari Roberts with support from Bill Brislen. The motion carried.

Mr. English closed the Public Hearing regarding the Special Assessment at 7:11 p.m.

**b. Public Hearing – Truth in Taxation – Road Millage**

Mr. English opened the Public Hearing regarding Truth in Taxation – Road Millage at 7:12 p.m.

Mr. English stated that the law requires any non-budgeted items must have a public hearing in order to approve a levy of additional millage rates in accordance with the General Property Tax Act. The proposed .4 mils resolution for the roads did not receive approval until August, 2026 requiring the truth-in-taxation hearing today.

**Public Comments –**

* Sandy Brown – questioned the requirement to inform the public of a change in the budget. Mr. English pointed out that the funds remain in Park Township for the next 3 years and are only for repairing the roads.
* Cindy Newell – asked how the roads to be repaired are chosen.
* Mr. English noted that the County keeps track and makes those decisions in conjunction with the Townships. Further discussion involved striping of the roads.

Ed English closed the public hearing at 7:22 p.m.

Review by the Board –

A motion was made by Tom Springer with support from Lari Roberts to approve the additional .4 mill for Road Maintenance and Improvements.

**c. Data Center Information**

Mr. English reported Planning and Board Members went to a Seminar regarding Data Centers in Dorr. He pointed out that no one has called regarding building a Data Center in Park township and this was just trying to get ahead and get educated. The seminar was purely informational. The members brought home relevant information. He noted the importance of learning what can and cannot be done to manage them. The Township should try to manage by ordinance and changes to the Master Plan which would include adding an attachment regarding the Data Centers vs redoing the Master Plan.

Mr. English asked all present to review a sample ordinance he had available and to make comments and notes for the Planning Commission to review. He noted it was important to get the township’s ideas and comments. It was noted from the meeting that these Data Centers depreciate quickly so any property tax value also goes down. At the present time everyone is waiting for the State’s rules on data centers.

Dale Hutson pointed out that many times the owners sign a lease agreement and if they get enough property they put in their own substation and utility lines without seizing land.

**Three Rivers Hospital (Beacon Health) – Maria Behr**

* The Emergency Department has been busier since the close of Sturgis Hospital. Their average was between 48 and 55 patients daily. But now the average is about 75 patients daily. Their highest day was 102 patients.
* They have hired more mid-level providers and 2 traveling nurses. They have also hired 15 people from Sturgis Hospital.
* The have completed the Lab renovations and added more space.
* In September they are doing a cookout for all associates.
* The Nursing Team did a carnival for the associates and their families with food, games, etc.
* Demolition for the Medical Building will begin around September 3. The new building is being constructed in Oshkosh, WI and planning to be delivered and completed in late Spring/early Summer 2027. The new building will have a Wound Clinic, Oncology Clinic and Infusion Clinic with 18 private infusion areas that will be large enough for a family member to be with the patient during treatment.
* Beacon has 2 locations in Sturgis – Family Health Internal Medicine Clinic and a cardiac clinic.
* HealthTrac will be closing on September 30 with the pool and track also closing. Physical Therapy and Occupational Therapy will still be providing services.
