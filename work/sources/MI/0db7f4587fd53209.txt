ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING DATA CENTER AND BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE
              SYSTEM MORATORIUM (POLICE POWER)

                                     Ordinance No. 26-02
                                      Notice of Adoption

Please take notice that on April 21, 2026 the Township Board of Webster Township adopted
Ordinance No. 26-02, which imposes a one (1) year moratorium on the permitting, consideration,
approval, location, erection, construction, and/or installation of Data Centers and Battery Energy
Storage Systems (BESS) in the Township in order to allow appropriate time to study and
potentially adopt revised regulations governing Data Centers and BESS. Copies of the Ordinance
may be obtained from Tonie Brovont, Webster Township Clerk, at 5665 Webster Church Rd
Dexter, MI 48130 during regular business hours.
