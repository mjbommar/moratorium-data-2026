Moved by Director Barbeau, supported by Director McRorie, to approve Resolution 2026-
107, Establishing a Moratorium Prohibiting the Development, Approval, or Operation of
Artificial Intelligence (AI) Data Centers on Tribal Lands.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Sault Ste. Marie Tribe of Chippewa Indians
hereby establishes a moratorium prohibiting the approval, permitting, development, construction,
leasing, or operation of artificial intelligence (AI) data centers on all Tribal lands and trust lands
under its jurisdiction.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this moratorium shall apply to all Tribal governmental
departments and any external entities seeking to conduct such activities within Tribal jurisdiction.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that no Tribal official, department, or entity shall enter into
agreements, negotiations, or commitments that would allow or facilitate the development of AI
data centers during the duration of this moratorium.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this moratorium is enacted to protect Tribal treaty reserved
resources, environmental integrity, water resources, energy sustainability, cultural preservation,

and community well-being.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this moratorium shall remain in effect indefinitely unless
and until explicitly lifted by a subsequent resolution of the Board of Directors following:

e Acomprehensive Tribal impact assessment

e Environmental and cultural resource evaluations

e Infrastructure and energy capacity analysis

e Robust community consultation and input process
BEIT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors affirms that any future consideration
of such developments must be grounded in Tribal values, long-term sustainability, and the
protection of future generations.

Moved by Director Barbeau, supported by Director LaPlaunt, to approve Resolution 2026-
109, Clarifying the Intent of the Creation of the Tribe's Separate and Distinct Legal Entities.
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby clarifies that the
intent of Sault Tribal Code Chapters 93 and 94 was to create legally separate and distinct economic
arms of the tribe that enjoy the Tribe's Sovereign immunity but carry out economic purposes of
the tribe, not governmental functions and that these entities are governed separately from the Board
of Directors and are not subject to the obligations of the tribal government unless explicitly and
expressly stated in tribal resolution or code.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby clarifies that Sault Tribal Code
Chapter 15: Tribal Freedom of Information Ordinance, in its current form, applies to governmental
entities only and does not apply to economic entities of the Tribe and further clarifies that the
KGA, the EA, Sault Tribe Inc, and Indian Energy are economic entities not governmental entities
not subject to Chapter 15 in its current form.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby authorizes the Legal
Department to update Tribal Code Chapters 15, 93, and 94 to reflect the intent of this Resolution.
