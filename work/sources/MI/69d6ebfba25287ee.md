Purpose of Meeting:
**A. To Conduct the Public Accuracy Test for the November 3, 2026 General Election**To the qualified electors of the Charter Township of Pere Marquette, Mason County, State of Michigan: Notice is hereby given that a Public Accuracy Test for the electronic equipment that will be used in Precincts 1 for the November 3, 2026 General Election is scheduled for September 23, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. at Pere Marquette Charter Township Hall, 1699 S. Pere Marquette Hwy., Ludington, MI 49431. The Public Accuracy Test is conducted to demonstrate that the computer programming used to tabulate the votes cast at the election meets the requirements of Michigan election law.

**B. To approve the previous minutes, to appoint election inspectors for the November 3, 2026 General Election, and any other business that may come before the Commission.**

This notice is posted in compliance with the Open Meetings Act, Public Act 267 of 1976, as amended, (MCL 41.72a (2)(3)) and the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990.

It is possible that there could be a quorum of the Township Board present during a portion of this meeting.

PERE MARQUETTE CHARTER TOWNSHIP
MASON COUNTY, MICHIGAN
Notice of Adoption
[Ordinance No. 162](<https://www.pmtwp.org/Ord. 162 for website.pdf?t=202608140829180>)

Please take notice that on August 11, 2026 the Township Board of Pere Marquette Charter Township adopted Ordinance No. 162, which imposes a one (1) year moratorium on the permitting, consideration, approval, location, erection, construction, and/or installation of Data Centers in the Township in order to allow appropriate time to study and potentially adopt revised regulations governing Data Centers.

The complete text of the Ordinance may be obtained from Rachelle Enbody, Pere Marquette Charter Township Clerk, at 1699 S. Pere Marquette Hwy. Ludington, MI 49431 during regular business hours and is published on the Township’s website at [www.pmtwp.org](http://www.pmtwp.org).

This Ordinance shall be effective the date following publication of the notice of adoption.

Published by Order of the Township Board

ZONING ORDINANCE AMENDMENT

Notice Adoption
[Ordinance No. 163](<https://www.pmtwp.org/Ord. 163 for website.pdf?t=202608261317130>)

Please take notice that on August 25, 2026 the Township Board of Pere Marquette Charter Township adopted Ordinance No. 163, which amends the Pere Marquette Charter Township Zoning Ordinance, to add Article 33.07 to enact a one (1) year moratorium on the permitting, consideration, approval, location, erection, construction, and/or installation of Data Centers in the Township in order to allow appropriate time to study and potentially adopt revised regulations governing Data Centers. Copies of the Ordinance may be obtained from Rachelle Enbody, Pere Marquette Charter Township Clerk, at 1699 S. Pere Marquette Hwy., Ludington, MI 49431 during regular business hours.

The Zoning Ordinance Amendment has the following sections, which are summarized below: Section 1: Findings; Section 2: Amendment to Zoning Ordinance; Section 3: Title; Section 4: Definition; Section 5: Moratorium; Section 6; Waiver; Section 7: Severability; Section 8: Repeal; Section 9: Publication; Section 10: Effective Date.

This Ordinance shall take effect September 6, 2026.
