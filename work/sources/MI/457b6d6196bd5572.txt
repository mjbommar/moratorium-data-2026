Res. #26-072 Motion by Boismier and seconded by Dill to adopt the Data Center Moratorium
resolution as follows:
        Resolution Imposing a Temporary Moratorium for a period of twelve (12) months on the
        establishing, permitting, consideration, approval, location, construction and/or
        installation of any data centers in the City of Gibraltar.

       WHEREAS, the City currently has (research and development facilities, technical centers
       and laboratories listed as a use permitted by right in Article XXI - M-1 Light
       Manufacturing Districts or (M-2) of its Zoning Ordinances, but has yet to establish,
       approve, and implement additional standards specific for data centers; and

       WHEREAS, it has recently come to the attention of the City Council that the City should
       study and review whether data centers require additional local regulation within the City
       of Gibraltar, and whether absent such additional local regulation, data centers may
       interfere with other land uses, and may have substantial impacts on the environment,
       public health, safety and welfare; and

       WHEREAS, the City has a legitimate public purpose in assessing the regulation of the
       establishment and use of data centers within the City; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council is desirous of assessing its regulations to implement an
       appropriate regulatory approach for addressing potential impacts of data centers while
       ensuring the productive and healthy development of data centers within City, which may
       include designated areas where data centers may be located within the City; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council wishes to consider and assess amendments to its current
       regulations regarding the use of data centers that are consistent with all applicable state
       and federal laws, including any controlling precedent from the United Stated Supreme
       Court; and

       WHEREAS, the time to consider and assess regulation of data centers will take several
       months; and

       WHEREAS, the city is legitimately invested in ensuring that data centers do not interfere
       with other land uses, or have substantial negative impacts on the environment, public
       health, safety, and welfare; and
CP 3/9/2026                                                                                Page 3


       WHEREAS, it is a routine and an appropriate method to enact a reasonable and
       temporary moratorium to consider enactment of any amendment; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is necessary and reasonable to establish a
       temporary moratorium on the establishment and use of data centers within the City for
       twelve (12) months or until the City has enacted a regulatory ordinance setting forth the
       regulations for the establishment and use of data centers within the City

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council as follows:

       Section 1: Moratorium. The City hereby temporarily enacts a blanket prohibition on the
       establishment and use of data centers, including without limitation, approval of such use
       within the City, pending further study and enactment of City regulation addressing the
       same.

       Section 2: Term; Renewal. This Moratorium shall terminate and be of no further effect
       twelve (12) months from the effective date of this Moratorium unless the City Council
       adopts a resolution extending the moratorium or terminating the moratorium.

       Section 3: Waiver. A waiver of the moratorium may be granted upon the request of an
       applicant, if the City Council finds that waiver would not impact the health, safety and
       welfare of the City and is deemed reasonable by the City Council in exercising its
       legislative authority over land use regulation to remain consistent with the City Zoning
       Ordinance.

       Section 4: Effective Date. This Moratorium shall take effect immediately.
                                                                    Carried unanimously.
