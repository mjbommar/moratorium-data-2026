SUBJECT: Resolution of Concurrence for the Housing Tax Increment Financing (TIF) Brownfield Plan


DESCRIPTION:


For the Board's consideration is Resolution No. 2026-16, a Resolution of Concurrence for the Housing Tax

Increment Financing (TIF) Brownfield Plan for the proposed Enclave Project. This is the first Housing

TIF/Brownfield project proposed in Saugatuck Township. Because the Township has not established a

Brownfield Redevelopment Authority (BRA), the Housing TIF Brownfield Plan will be considered and

administered by the Allegan County Brownfield Redevelopment Authority. As part of the County's

approval process, the Township is requested to concur that the Brownfield Plan constitutes a public purpose

and consent to the Housing TIF Brownfield Plan.

The proposed Brownfield Plan has been prepared pursuant to Act 381 and would allow eligible housing

development activities to be reimbursed through tax increment financing. The Plan requests reimbursement

of up to $3,365,000 in eligible housing development activities for the project and includes reimbursement

of up to $600,000 to the Township for construction of a non-motorized path along the south side of Old

Allegan Road between Blue Star Highway and the I-196 overpass. Reimbursement would occur over an

anticipated 16-year period, with 85 percent of the annual tax increment reimbursed to the developer and 15

percent reimbursed to the Township for the pathway project. The Township will continue to receive taxes

based on the property's current taxable value throughout the reimbursement period.

The proposed project advances several long-term Township priorities. The development would add 36 new

for-sale housing units, helping address a housing segment identified by the Allegan County Housing Needs

Assessment as having the greatest unmet demand. The Township's Housing Survey likewise identified a

strong demand for additional housing, with respondents citing limited housing stock as one of the primary

barriers to purchasing a home. In addition, the project includes construction of sanitary sewer infrastructure,

including a new lift station and connection to the regional wastewater treatment system. This investment

will make it possible to extend public sanitary sewer along the Blue Star Highway Corridor from

approximately Maple Street to 134th Avenue, supporting existing and future development in an area

identified by the Township for additional residential growth.

Adoption of the resolution does not approve the proposed development. Rather, it signifies the Township's

concurrence that the Housing TIF Brownfield Plan serves a public purpose and its consent for the Allegan

County Brownfield Redevelopment Authority to consider and administer the Plan. Roll Call

SUBJECT: MERS Healthcare Savings Program Amendment


DESCRIPTION:


For the Board’s consideration is an amendment to the Township’s participation in the MERS Healthcare

Savings Program (HCSP). Last year, the Township established the HCSP for employees with a required

0.5% employer contribution and 0.5% employee contribution. Employees have responded positively to the

benefit, and there is interest in increasing both contributions to 1% each.

Under the HCSP, contributions are deposited into individual employee accounts that are invested and grow

tax-free. Upon separation from employment, employees may access the funds for eligible medical expenses

on a tax-free basis. Increasing the contributions will provide employees with additional resources for future

healthcare expenses and represents a modest improvement to the Township’s employee benefits. Periodic

improvements to employee benefits are an important tool for retaining employees and remaining

competitive as an employer.

The proposed amendment would increase the Township’s required contribution from 0.5% to 1% and the

mandatory employee contribution from 0.5% to 1%, effective September 1, 2026. The estimated additional

cost to the Township is approximately $3,500 annually.

The Board should consider modifying the terms of the Township’s MERS HCSP participation and

authorizing execution of the amended Participation Agreement. Voice Vote.

SUBJECT: Heavy Equipment Noise


DESCRIPTION:


For the Board’s consideration is Ordinance No. 2026-05, an ordinance to amend Section 14-117(d) of the

Township Code of Ordinances to prohibit heavy equipment noise between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and 6:00

a.m. when occurring within 600 feet of a residential area.

The Township’s current ordinance prohibits heavy equipment noise between 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. At a

special meeting in late June, the Board discussed amending these hours after the Township was contacted

by multiple contractors seeking to begin work before 7:00 a.m. due to extreme summer heat and

temperature requirements associated with certain construction materials, including concrete. Following that

discussion, the Board directed staff to prepare an ordinance amendment changing the prohibited hours from

10:00 p.m.–7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.–6:00 a.m.

Ordinance No. 2026-05 is now before the Board for consideration and would allow heavy equipment

operations to begin at 6:00 a.m. while continuing to prohibit heavy equipment noise during overnight hours.

Roll Call.
