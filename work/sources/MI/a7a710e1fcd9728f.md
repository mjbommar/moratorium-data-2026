5.I.

## Fall Hydrant Flushing Began September 8th

Waterford Township DPW will perform Fall hydrant flushing September 8th through mid-October. Flushing is done between 4-8 pm, Monday through Thursday. Customers may see brown water coming from their faucets and/or in their toilets. If that happens, please flush the lines by running cold water in the bathtub or laundry tub until it runs clear. Please contact the Waterford Township Department of Public Works at 248-674-2278 with any questions or concerns.

5.II.

## Town Hall Meeting on the Police & Fire Millage Renewals

The Township will be hosting a moderated Town Hall Meeting on Wednesday, September 16th, at 6:00 p.m. in the Town Hall Auditorium to discuss the Police and Fire Millage Renewals that will appear on the November 3rd ballot. Residents will hear a brief presentation from the panelists and have an opportunity to submit questions. Questions may also be emailed in advance to communications@waterfordmi.gov by 5:00 p.m. on September 15th. The meeting will be broadcast on Municipal Channel 20, and streamed live on the Township website.

5.III.

## Bat Hike at Drayton Plains Nature Center

Friday, September 18th join Six Rivers Land Conservancy at the Drayton Plains Nature Center for a FREE guided twilight hike to discover the incredible world of bats. Participants will have the chance to see echo meter detectors used in real time and learn about the important role that bats play in our ecosystem. Hike starts at 7:30 p.m. Meet at the pavilion. No registration necessary.

5.IV.

## 35th Annual Walk and Roll for Waterford Meals on Wheels

Bring the family out Saturday, September 19th for the annual fundraising walk (and roll) in support of Waterford's Meals on Wheels program! Just $30 for adults, kids participate free with a registered adult. Free t-shirt with pre-registration, and lunch is included. Check-in begins at 10:15am, and the walk & roll starts at 11am. This event helps feed Waterford's homebound seniors more than 1,200 meals each week! Once you participate, you'll want to make this an annual family tradition.
