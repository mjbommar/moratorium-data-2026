**September 22, 2026 - Township Board Public Hearings**

The Garfield Township Board will conduct public hearings during its meeting on September 22nd, at 6:00pm, at the Garfield Township Hall, 3848 Veterans Drive, Traverse City. The items to be considered at the public hearings include:

* An application submitted by James C. Jones to rezone a portion of a parcel located at 1361 N. West Silver Lake Road to R-1 One-Family Residential zoning.
* Zoning Ordinance amendments for Child Care Centers and Signs for Mixed Use Suburban (MU-S) District.

The[Public Hearing Notice](https://www.garfieldmi.gov/download/96984/09-22-26-ph-jones-rezoning-zo-amd-child-care-centers.pdf) provides further information.

**August 25, 2026 - Adoption of Resolution for Temporary Moratorium on Data Centers and Cryptocurrency Mining Facilities**

The Garfield Township Board adopted a Resolution at the August 25, 2026, Township Board meeting that imposes a temporary moratorium on the review, acceptance, processing, or issuance of any applications, permits, rezonings, licenses, or approvals for Data Centers and Cryptocurrency Mining Facilities, including but not limited to zoning approvals, site plan approvals, special land use permits, zoning compliance permits and building permits.

The purpose of this moratorium is to allow the Township sufficient time to review, study, and recommend amendments to the Township’s Master Plan and/or Zoning Ordinance to appropriately regulate Data Centers and Cryptocurrency Mining Facilities in a manner that protects the public health, safety, and welfare.

The moratorium imposed by this Resolution shall remain in effect for twelve (12) months from the effective date of August 25, 2026, unless terminated earlier by action of the Township Board, and may be extended by Resolution for up to an additional six (6) months.

The Resolution enacting the moratorium may be inspected at the Township Clerk’s Office, 3848 Veterans Drive, Traverse City, Michigan, 49684, during the Township’s regular hours of 7:30am to 6:00pm Monday through Thursday, or on the Township website www.garfieldmi.gov.
