Public Comment Policy
PUBLIC COMMENT

As required by PA 276 of 1976, The Open Meetings Act, there shall be a minimum of one (1) Public
Comment period at any Open Meeting of Norvell Township government. Any member of the public
wishing to address the public body will limit their comments to one (1) three (3) minute period during each
period of Public Comment so that all members of the public wishing to address that body may have an
opportunity to be heard. In cases where more than 30 members of the public are present at a meeting and
wish to provide public comment, the Township Board limits the length of public comment made by an
individual to two (2) minutes in duration during each period of Public Comment. The Chair, at their
discretion, may extend that period. A member of the public not using the full three (3) minutes allotted,
may not cede any unused time to another speaker. In the event that a group of more than three (3)
persons supporting or opposing the same position desires to be heard, in the interest of time, a
spokesperson may be designated to express the group’s concern and the spokesperson may be allotted up
to ten (10) minutes to speak.

MANNER OF ADDRESSING THE BOARD/COMMISSION

Upon recognition by the meeting Chair, each person addressing the Board/Commission is asked (but not
required) to stand and give his/her name and address in an audible tone of voice for the record. All
comments are to be addressed to the meeting Chair. No person other than members of the
Board/Commission and the person recognized shall be permitted to enter into any discussion, either
directly or through the members of the Board/Commission. No question shall be asked of the
Board/Commission members except through the meeting Chair.

BREACHING THE PEACE

Any person who breaches the peace may be requested to leave. Breaching the peace includes repeatedly
speaking without being recognized, threatening harm to any person or property, or otherwise disrupting
the orderly proceeding of the meeting. If an individual who is breaching the peace refuses to leave, law
enforcement will be called to remove the subject.

RECORDING OF TOWNSHIP MEETINGS

Any person shall have the right to record (either audio or video), broadcast or take pictures of the
proceedings of an Open Meeting but shall not utilize the electric outlets of the Township without prior
permission of the Township Clerk. Any recording device and picture taking device shall be kept a minimum
of ten (10) feet from any Board member and may not be placed behind them. Said devices shall not be
placed so as to impede the vision of other members of the public. All cords related to those activities are
to be kept in a secure manner so as not to cause a trip/fall hazard.

OLD BUSINESS

Pension Plan Change

Supervisor Sutherland read his memo concerning the possible pension plan change. A short
discussion followed. He also stated that legal council is recommending updating the enabling
ordinance governing the pension plan, whether the board decides to change providers or not. A
motion was made by Supervisor Sutherland, seconded by Clerk Pero to change providers from
The Principal to the Municipal Employees Retirement System (MERS). Motion carried on a roll
call vote as follows: Ayes-Sutherland, Sauers, Pero, Francis, & Haystead. Nays-None.

A/V Equipment for the Township Board Room

Supervisor Sutherland read his memo regarding the need for audio and possibly video equipment
to be used for board meetings, stating he has received comments regarding residents being unable
to hear board members’ comments during meetings. He received quotes from IVSCOMM (the
townships IT provider) and presented 3 options for the board to consider, No. 1 being Audio only,
which includes 4 ceiling mounted speakers, 8 wireless microphones, a wall mounted lockable
cabinet to house the equipment, with installation at a cost of $4,939.00. Option No 2 would add
35 inch Video monitors, a HDMI splitter to distribute the input signal to all monitors, & a laptop
or other device to connect to the wall port with output displayed on all 3 screens and installation
for an additional cost of $6,071.00, and No. 3, full integration for virtual meeting capabilities at an
additional cost of $14,543.00. Discussion followed. A motion was made by Trustee Haystead,
seconded by Trustee Francis to approve option 1 (one) from IVSCOMM for Audio only, at a cost
not to exceed $5,000.00. Motion carried on a roll call vote as follows: Ayes-Sutherland, Sauers,
Pero, Francis, & Haystead. Nays-None.

Supervisor Sutherland, seconded by Treasurer Sauers to approve Resolution 2026-03, as
presented. Motion carried on a roll call vote as follows: Ayes-Sutherland, Sauers, Pero, Francis,
& Haystead. Nays-None.

Zoning Text Amendment 2026-02 Temporary Moratoriums

2026-02 amending the Township’s Zoning Ordinance to add a process that allows temporary
moratoriums under Section 16.11 Temporary Moratoriums as recommended by the Planning
Commission at the March 18, 2026 Planning Commission meeting, in the planning commission
public hearing; because the amendment will meet the required standards for zoning text
amendments under section 3.3 Zoning Text and Map Amendment of the Norvell Township Zoning
Ordinance as discussed at the April 20" board meeting, the March 18, 2026 Planning Commission
meeting, in the planning commission staff report and because the proposed amendment is
compatible with the Norvell Township Master Plan, will protect the health, safety and welfare of
the citizens of the Township and is consistent with Michigan Law. Motion carried on a roll call
vote as follows: Ayes-Sutherland, Sauers, Pero, Francis, & Haystead. Nays-None.
