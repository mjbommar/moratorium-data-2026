ORDINANCE NO. 203


AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND ARTICLE 5- SCHEDULE OF REGULATIONS, CHAPTER 3.-TEMPORARY

MORATORIUM, SECTION 138-5.301 TEMPORARY MORATORIUM FOR DATA CENTERS, TO EXTEND THE

MORATORIUM SUSPENDING THE CITY’S PROCESSING AND ACCEPTANCE OF APPLICATIONS AND PLANS

FOR DEVELOPMENT, AND IMPROVEMENT OF DATA CENTERS, TO CHAPTER 138- ZONING, OF THE CODE

OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF ROCHESTER HILLS, OAKLAND COUNTY, MICHIGAN, TO PRESCRIBE A

PENALTY FOR VIOLATIONS, AND TO REPEAL INCONSISTENT OR CONFLICTING ORDINANCES. THE CITY OF ROCHESTER HILLS ORDAINS:


Section 1. Chapter 3.-Temporary Moratorium, Section 138-5.301, Temporary Moratorium for Data

Centers, to Chapter 138-Zoning, of the Code of Ordinances, Article 5-Schedule of Regulations, shall be

amended as follows:

Chapter 3, TEMPORARY MORATORIUM

Sec. 138-5.301. Temporary Moratorium for Data Centers.

(a) Purpose.e.

1. The City has identified concerns relating to the potential location and regulation of

Data Centers  due to the s speed of and significantly evolving nature of Data Centers

and their potential impacts, including, but not limited to, building height, size, density

of development, noise, vibration, light pollution, water requirements, electricity

demands, and t their c compatibility with residential and other zoning districts and what

regulations should be required to protect other existing uses within the City and

appropriately regulate size, location, and conditions of operation for Data Centers.

2. In order to maintain the status quo while the City studies and reevaluates Data

Centers and evaluates appropriate zoning locations and regulations for such uses,

the City Council determines it is in the City’s best interest to adopt a moratorium

temporarily suspending the City’s processing or acceptance of applications, plans,

or permits for the development, improvement, or alteration of land for Data Centers.

(b) Moratorium. A temporary moratorium is established suspending the processing and

acceptance of any applications, plans, or permits for the development, improvement, or alteration of

any land parcel, lot, or premises for Data Centers within the City. This moratorium does not apply to any

Data Center development or building that received, or has submitted complete plans to the City for, site

plan approval on or before February 23, 2026.

This moratorium shall remain in effect until repealed by the City Council or until the expiration of

180 calendar days, whichever occurs sooner.  The City Council also reserves the right, at the end of the

180 day period to extend the moratorium upon a showing that work and review of existing and proposed

ordinances has been ongoing and that additional time is necessary to complete such work. The time of

such extension shall be determined at that time and shall be the least amount of time necessary to

appropriately complete such work. The City has been actively researching best practices related to

locating and operations, and impacts of Data Center development. The City is reviewing ordinances

proposed and adopted in neighboring communities and throughout the state and country along with

potential state and federal regulations, consulted with sound experts, and has been actively working on

development of potential regulations regarding Data Center development. Due to the need for

continued study and additional evaluation of community impacts associated with Data Center

development, City Council is extending the existing temporary moratorium for an additional 180-days

extension to the existing temporary moratorium, currently scheduled to expire on September 5, 2026.

Section 2. Severability. This ordinance and each article, section, subsection, paragraph, subparagraph,

part, provision, sentence, word, and portion thereof are hereby declared to be severable, and if they or

any of them are declared to be invalid or unenforceable for any reason by a court of competent

jurisdiction, it is hereby provided that the remainder of this ordinance shall not be affected thereby.

Section 3. Penalty. All violations of this ordinance shall be municipal civil infractions and upon a

determination of responsibility therefor shall be punishable by a civil fine of not more than $500.

Section 4. Repeal, Effective Date, Adoption.

(1) Repeal. All regulatory provisions contained in other City ordinances, which are

inconsistent with the provisions of this ordinance, are hereby repealed.

(2) Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective on Monday, August 31, 2026,

following its publication in the Oakland Press on Sunday, August 30, 2026.
