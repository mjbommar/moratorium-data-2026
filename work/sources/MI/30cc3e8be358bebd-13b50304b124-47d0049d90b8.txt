RE: Drenth Grove Playground

Executive Summary

The documents in your packet are the most recent playground options from Sinclair Recreation. Option 6, for

a total of $219,807 includes an area of additional poured-in-place surfacing. Option 7 removes that section,

for a total cost of 208,929. Prien and Newhoff is estimating approx. $3000 of additional work to prepare the

playground area for the poured in place surface for both options for a combined grand total of $222,807 for the

higher cost option. Prien and Newhoff will be on hand for any questions.

A RESOLUTION ENACTING TEMPORARY MORATORIA ON ENERGY

FACILITIES AND DATA CENTERS

RECITALS

WHEREAS,

A. Section 3.36 of the Zoning Ordinance for Zeeland Charter Township authorizes the

Township Board to impose moratoria on uses, the acceptance of applications for zoning

approvals, permissions, or permits, and the issuance of any  zoning approvals, permissions,

or permits.

B. The Township is in the process of reviewing and updating its master p plan.

C. The Township Board finds that comprehensive planning and the proper development of

zoning regulations regarding energy facilities and data centers require extensive study and

review.

D. The Township Board further finds that the maintenance of the status quo best facilitates

comprehensive planning and the proper development of zoning regulations  regarding

energy facilities and data centers.

E. The Township Board further finds that until the master plan review is complete and zoning

ordinances can be enacted based on the updated master plan, it is appropriate to maintain

the status quo regarding energy facilities and data centers within the Township.

RESOLVED

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT,

1. A moratorium is hereby imposed on commercial energy facilities and data centers.

2. A moratorium is hereby imposed on  the acceptance of applications for approvals,

permissions, or permits issued for commercial energy facilities and data centers  pursuant

to the Zoning Ordinance for Zeeland Charter Township.

4910-8338-6058, v. 2

3. A moratorium is hereby imposed on the issuance of any approvals, permissions, or p permits

for commercial energy facilities and data centers pursuant to the Zoning Ordinance for

Zeeland Charter Township.

4. The term “energy facility” means energy facility as defined in Part 8 of the Clean and

Renewable Energy and Waste Reduction Act, being PA 295 of 2008, as amended.

5. The term “commercial energy facility” m means an energy facility that i) absorbs, stores, and

discharges electricity, ii) captures and converts solar energy into electricity, or iii) captures

and converts wind energy into electricity for the primary purpose of sale or for use in

locations other than the property upon which the energy facility is located.

6. The term “data center means” any facility whose primary purpose is t to h house, operate, and

maintain computer servers, data storage systems, routers, switches, and associated

information or telecommunications infrastructure dedicated to the centralized processing,

storage, management, and dissemination of digital data. This term includes but is not

limited to server farms, cloud computing hubs, colocation centers, artificial intelligence

computing facilities, crypto currency mining facilities, and telecommunication switching

hubs. This term excludes server rooms or data processing facilities that are accessory and

incidental to a permitted d principal use.

7. A violation of this Resolution is a violation of the Zeeland Charter Township Zoning

Ordinance.

8. The moratoria imposed under this Resolution shall remain in effect for an initial term of

365 days following the effective date of the moratoria.

9. The moratorium imposed pursuant to Resolution No. 1027 is hereby repealed.

10. The above recitals are incorporated as if restated herein.

11. This Resolution is effective upon adoption. The moratoria imposed pursuant to this

Resolution shall take effect upon publication of a notice of adoption in a newspaper of

general circulation within the Township.

RE: FOIA Policy Amendment

Executive Summary

The Board of Trustees previously adopted Resolution 821 which adopted the Michigan Township Association’s

(MTA) 2015 Model FOIA Procedures and Guidelines.  The MTA model procedures and guidelines have since been

updated (last update was 2024). Legal counselrecommends a comprehensive review of FOIA policies at a future

Board meeting. The current policy designates the Supervisor as the FOIA Coordinator. The FOIA coordinator is

responsible for processing FOIA requests and ensuring that appropriate records are provided to the public,

proper legal procedures are followed, and that record identification is delegated to the proper staff member.

The resolution included in your packet updates the current FOIA policy in two specific ways: 1) it designates the

Superintendent as the FOIA Coordinator and 2) clarifies that it is the general policy of the Township to charge

fees to the extent permitted by law.

Budget Impact: FOIA limits the amount the Township can charge in fees. In most cases, labor costs will be

charged at the hourly wage of the lowest-paid Township employee capable of doing the work, regardless of who

actually performs work. The FOIA policy is designed to ensure the Township recoups the costs of processing a

FOIA request to the extent permitted by law. Any FOIA revenue will not be material to the budget.

Legal Analysis: Legal counsel drafted the attached resolution.

Manager Recommendation: Recommend that the proposed resolution be adopted to 1) designate the

Superintendent as the FOIA Coordinator and 2) clarify that it is the general policy of the Township to charge fees

to the extent permitted by law.
