                         TOWNSHIP OF ERIE
                    COUNTY OF MONROE, MICHIGAN
       LIMITED MORATORIUM ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF DATA CENTERS
                             ORDINANCE
                         ORDINANCE NO. 132


       An Ordinance enacted pursuant to Act 246 of the Public Acts of 1945, as amended, to
provide for the public health, safety, and general welfare of the persons and property of the
Township of Erie by the imposition of a limited moratorium on the development of data centers
within Erie Township, Monroe County, Michigan.

     THE TOWNSHIP BOARD OF THE TOWNSHIP OF ERIE, COUNTY OF MONROE,
STATE OF MICHIGAN, ORDAINS:

SHORT TITLE.

      Sec. 1. This Ordinance shall be known and referred to as the “Erie Township Limited
Moratorium on the Development of Data Centers Ordinance”.

FINDINGS.

       Sec. 2.

               A.     The Erie Township Board has recognized that the Erie Township Zoning
Ordinance lacks provisions dealing with data centers and related land uses and lacks specific
standards regarding said land uses.

                B.    The Erie Township Board has recognized that data centers being proposed
in neighboring communities typically involve substantial energy consumption, water usage,
infrastructure demand, continuous operation, and limited long-term employment relative to the
proposed data center’s physical and environmental footprint.

                C.     The Erie Township Board finds that it is in the best interest of Erie
Township that the Erie Township Planning Commission and the Erie Township Board have
sufficient time to study zoning and other regulations regarding and applicable to data centers, and
to review and adopt Zoning Ordinance amendments, general code ordinances, and/or regulations
to adequately protect the public health, safety, and general welfare of the persons and property of
Erie Township.

                 D.     The Erie Township Board has concluded that during the pendency of its
study and consideration of Zoning Ordinance amendments regarding data centers and related land
uses that it is desirable and in the public interest for the reasons set forth herein that there be an
immediate moratorium on the acceptance and/or processing of any applications (including existing
applications) for data centers, and that such moratorium run (1) a period one year from the effective
date of this Ordinance, or (2) until the effective date of a Erie Township Zoning Ordinance
amendment addressing data centers, whichever date occurs first.

MORATORIUM.

        Sec. 3. There is hereby imposed a moratorium upon Erie Township’s acceptance and/or
processing of any applications, special land use or conditional land use applications (including
existing applications, if any) for approval of and/or development of data centers, including, but not
limited to building permits, land use permits, special land use permits, conditional land use
permits, site plan approval, certificates of occupancy, and zoning permits for data centers. More
specifically, there shall be no consideration or action taken by the Township Board, Planning
Commission, or other Township entity, official, or agent, on applications for data centers under
the Zoning Ordinance or any other applicable section of Erie Township general code ordinances.

       No new data centers shall be accepted for review, considered, approved, or otherwise
allowed during the moratorium period.

        The Township Planning Commission, along with the Township planners, Township
engineers, Township officials, and Township attorney, shall proceed with efforts to study the
relevant state of the law, and shall present to the Township Board and Planning Commission a
proposed plan for amending the Zoning Ordinance, or general ordinances, or any combination
thereof, with respect to applicants for data centers, as deemed appropriate.

TERM OF MORATORIUM AND PROHIBITION.

        Sec. 4. This moratorium imposed hereunder shall expire upon (1) one year from the
effective date of this Ordinance, or (2) the effective date of an Erie Township Zoning Ordinance
amendment addressing and regulating the land use known as data centers, whichever date occurs
first.

SEVERABILITY.

Sec. 5. This Ordinance and the various parts, sentences, paragraphs, sections, subsections, phrases,
and clauses hereof are hereby declared to be severable and if any clause, sentence, word, section,
or provision is declared void or unenforceable for any reason, by any court of competent
jurisdiction, it shall not affect any portion of the Ordinance other than said part or portion thereof.
EFFECTIVE DATE.

publication of the Ordinance in a newspaper of general circulation in Erie Township, pursuant to
