AN ORDINANCE TO ENACT A TEMPORARY RY MORATATORIUM ON DATATA TA CENTERS

An Ordinance to protect the public health, safety, and welfare by establishing regulations relating to a temporary

moratorium on considering applications for Data Centers to protect the public health, safety and welfare of the

Mason ToTownship while the ToTownship completes a study of the Ordinances and regulations pertaining thereto

and considers possible subsequent revisions to current Ordinances pertaining thereto; to allow for an extension

of the temporary moratorium; to provide for severability; to repeal all Ordinances or parts of Ordinances in

conflict therewith; and to provide an effffective date.

MASON TOWNSHIP

CASS COUNTY, Y, MICHIGAN

ORDAINS:

SECTION 1

FINDINGS

In accordance with Public Act 1945 PA PA 246, MCL 41.181 et seq., as amended, the Mason Township Board of

TrTrustees has determined the following:

1. That the approval of Data Centers may result in or produce negative impacts on permitted land uses and

development and may harm the public health, safety and general welfare of property owners and residents of

Mason ToTownship.

2. The ToTownship Board has received comments and information from the public raising public health, safety,

and welfare concerns about the impacts of Data Centers on the residents and property owners of the Township;

the ToTownship has a fiduciary duty to promote the health, safety and general welfare of property owners and

residents within its territorial boundaries.

3. The ToTownship has a legitimate public purpose in assessing the regulation of the establishment and use of

Data Centers within the ToTownship to ensure that Data Centers do not interfere with other land uses, or have

substantial negative impacts on the environment, public, health and safety.

4. The ToTownship Board finds that adopting a temporary moratorium is reasonable and necessary for, among

other reasons, the following:

A. Michigan courts have recognized that a moratorium is a common and legitimate tool to preserve the

status quo while formulating a development strategy.

B. The ToTownship Board desires to review and study the public health, safety, and welfare concerns

regarding Data Centers and any ToTownship regulations that may impact and/or regulate such projects.

C. The adoption of a temporary moratorium will allow the Township Board and Planning Commission

adequate time to study and possibly implement revisions to the Township’s Ordinances and regulations,

including attention to and consideration of citizen input and involvement, public debate, and full

consideration of all issues and points of view.

D. The Michigan Legislature is furthermore presently undertaking a study of Data Centers as a land use

and may be considering peremptory legislation on the topic.

5. The ToTownship Board accordingly determines that it is desirable and in the public interest for the reasons set

forth above that the ToTownship Board adopt a temporary moratorium on the acceptance and/or processing of any

applications for Data Centers.

SECTION 2

MORATATORIUM/TERM OF MORATATORIUM

The ToTownship Board adopts a temporary moratorium on the acceptance and/or processing of any applications

for a Data Center. The temporary moratorium is in effffect twelve (12) months from the effffective date of this

Ordinance, or until the effffective date of any amended or new Township Ordinances or State regulations

addressing Data Centers are effffective, whichever date occurs first.

SECTION 3

EXTENSION OF MORATATORIUM

Before this moratorium expires, the Township Board may extend the temporary moratorium by resolution as

appropriate to allow sufffficient time to complete amendments or additions to its Ordinances and regulations.

SECTION 4

SEVERABILITY

The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared to be severable. If any clause, sentence, word, section or

provision is hereafter declared void or unenforceable for any reason by a court of competent jurisdiction, it shall

not affffect the remainder of such Ordinance which shall continue in full force and effffect.

SECTION 5

REPEAL

All Ordinance or parts of Ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed.

SECTION 6

EFFECTIVE DATATE

This Ordinance shall take immediate effffect upon publication, after adoption.
