PUBLIC NOTICE — GUN PLAIN CHARTER TOWNSHIP

The Gun Plain Charter Township Board adopted Ordinance No. 196 on Thursday, August 6,
2026, at 6:00 PM at the regular Township Board meeting. The proposed ordinance adopted a
Temporary Moratorium on considering future applications for Battery Energy Storage Systems.

Ordinance No. 196 Moratorium, would put a temporary moratorium in effect until December 21,
2026 or until the effective date of any amended or new Township Ordinance or regulations
addressing Battery Energy Storage Systems are effective, whichever date occurs first.

A complete copy of the proposed ordinance is available for review at the Township Hall during
regular business hours or online at www.gunplain.org.

                                         Effective date

This ordinance takes effect upon publication after adoption pursuant to Michigan Public Act 110
of 2006. All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict herewith are repealed.
