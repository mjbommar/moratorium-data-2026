In 2025, the Township Board of Trustees adopted a temporary moratorium on new data center development. The temporary moratorium expired in November 2026.

Since adoption of the moratorium, a draft ordinance has been prepared to address topics such as energy use, water conservation, noise, traffic, environmental protection, mitigation, and long-term site management. The Township also collected public input during May and June 2026 through a Public Survey, receiving over 300 responses.

On June 18, 2026, at a regular Planning Commission meeting, discussion was held regarding the draft language. No public comment occurred at the meeting.

On August 6, 2026, a public hearing was held at a regular Planning Commission meeting. Public comments were received by the Planning Commission, and appropriate revisions were made to the draft ordinance. The Planning Commission voted 6-0, with one member absent, to recommend the Zoning Ordinance Amendment to the Board of Trustees for approval.

On September 9, 2026, a public hearing was held at a regular Board of Trustees meeting. Public comments were received by the Board of Trustees and discussion was held on the draft ordinance. The Board of Trustees voted 7-0 to approve First Reading ZOA #26-236 to add Section 40-11.51 to the Zoning Ordinance.

On October 28, 2026, a public hearing was held at a regular Board of Trustees meeting. Public comments were received by the Board of Trustees and discussion was held on the draft ordinance. The Board of Trustees voted 7-0 to adopt at Second Reading ZOA #26-236, an Ordinance to amend the Pittsfield Charter Township Zoning Ordinance to add Section 40-11-.51, High Resource Demand Dacilities.

The Data Center Ordinance will be published on Sunday, November 1, 2026, and will take effect on Monday, November 9, 2026.
