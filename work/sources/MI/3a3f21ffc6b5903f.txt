2) Data Center Temporary Moratorium Extension:

DeFranco introduced resolution to extend temporary moratorium on Data Centers.
Explained that in September, the Board would receive an ordinance from the Planning
Commission to review for approval. Extension would provide sufficient time to adopt
the new resolution.

Motion by Osman, supported by Marcy, to adopt Resolution No. 2026-17, a resolution to
extend the existing moratorium on the acceptance of building and zoning applications
for data center development, pending the approval of the new ordinance. Motion
carried by unanimous roll call vote.

Yes: Aldrich, Helmrich, Bigford, Osman, Marcy
No: None

3) Michigan Natural Resource Trust Fund Grant Resolutions and Agreements

DeFranco presented two grant agreements from the Michigan Natural Resources Trust
Fund Program and accompanying resolutions to accept (1) a grant award of $2,464,300

extension of the Blue Star Trail from 129" Ave to 124" Ave. In approving the project
agreements, the Board would be committing itself to meeting the required local
matches and terms of the grant program.
