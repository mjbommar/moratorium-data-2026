                            CHARTER TOWNSHIP OF MONITOR
                           REGULAR TOWNSHIP BOARD MEETING
                                     March 9, 2026

The Supervisor called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.

Members present: Spencer, Stewart, Kloha, Arnold, Bellor, Meier, and Meylan
Absent: None
Also present: R. Sheppard, Attorney

Pledge of Allegiance to the flag of the United States was recited by all.

Public input was held.

Motion by Bellor seconded by Meier to adopt agenda and consent agenda.
Motion carried.

Motion by Stewart seconded by Meylan to approve the payment of the bills $ 73,183.24.
Roll call vote:
Yes:      Spencer, Stewart, Kloha, Arnold, Bellor, Meier, and Meylan
No:       None
Motion carried.

Motion by Bellor seconded by Kloha to adopt Resolution R-2026-002 Intent to Create a Special
Assessment District and Notice of Public Hearing for Mill Pond Meadows Condominium
Association and associated lots outside the Condominium Association.
Roll call vote:
Yes:      Stewart, Kloha, Arnold, Bellor, Meier, Meylan, and Spencer
No:       None
Motion carried.

Motion by Kloha seconded by Stewart to increase the Deputy Treasurer pay to $25 per hour
effective 2.9.2026.
Roll call vote:
Yes:      Kloha, Meylan, Spencer, Stewart
No:       Arnold, Bellor, Meier
Motion carried.

Motion by Arnold seconded by Meylan to deny the request from DWS on behalf of 3205 S.
Huron Road for penalty removal on the January bill.
Roll call vote:
Yes:      Arnold, Bellor, Meier, Meylan, Spencer, Stewart, Kloha
No:        None
Motion carried.

Motion by Bellor seconded by Stewart to approve budget adjustments as presented.
Roll call vote:
Yes:      Bellor, Meier, Meylan, Spencer, Stewart, Kloha
No:        Arnold
Motion carried.
