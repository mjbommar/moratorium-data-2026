                           CITY OF WIXOM
                      DATA CENTER MORATORIUM
                    AUTHORIZING RESOLUTION 2026-47

At a regular meeting of the Wixom City Council held on August 25, 2026 at the Wixom City Hall,
49045 Pontiac Trail, Wixom, Michigan, the following resolution was offered by Councilmember
Kennedy and seconded by Councilmember Gronlund-Fox:

       A Resolution to repeal Resolution 2026-06 Imposing a Temporary Moratorium for a
       period of six (6) months on the establishing, permitting, consideration, approval,
       location, construction, and/or installation of any data centers in the City of Wixom
       to set the date of termination of this moratorium effective on September 6, 2026
       to coincide with the enactment date of City of Wixom Ordinance No. 2026-01
       that establishes a Zoning Ordinance definition and regulations for Data Center
       uses in the City of Wixom effective September 6, 2026.

WHEREAS, the City currently has the data processing listed as a use permitted by right in certain
        zoning districts defined in its code of ordinance, Title 18 Zoning, within Chapter
        18.06 Commercial Districts, Chapter 18.08 Office and Research Districts, and
        Chapter 18.09 Industrial Districts, but has yet to establish, approve, and implement
        an updated definition for data centers, or additional standards specific for that
        new definition; and

WHEREAS, it has recently come to the attention of the City Council that the City should study
        and review whether data centers require additional local regulation within the City
        of Wixom, and whether absent such additional local regulation, data centers may
        interfere with other land uses, and may have substantial impacts on the
        environment, public health, safety, and welfare; and

WHEREAS, the City has a legitimate public purpose in assessing the regulation of the
        establishment and use of data centers within the City; and

WHEREAS, the City Council is desirous of assessing its regulations to implement an appropriate
          regulatory approach for addressing potential impacts of data centers while
          ensuring the productive and healthy development of data centers within the City,
          which may include designated areas where data centers may be located within
          the City; and

WHEREAS, the City Council wishes to consider and assess amendments to its current regulations
          regarding the use of data centers that are consistent with all applicable state and
          federal laws, including any controlling precedent from the United States Supreme
          Court; and

WHEREAS, the time to consider and assess regulation of data centers will take several months;
          and

WHEREAS, at its November 18, 2025 meeting, the Wixom City Council moved “to direct
         Planning Commission to review the City’s zoning ordinance regulations as it relates
         to large scale data centers and provide recommendations back to City Council”
         for the purpose in assessing the regulation of the establishment and use of data
         centers within the City; and

WHEREAS, the City is legitimately invested in ensuring that data centers do not interfere with
          other land uses, or have substantial negative impacts on the environment, public
          health, safety, and welfare; and

WHEREAS, it is a routine and an appropriate method to enact a reasonable and temporary
        moratorium to consider enactment of any amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council found that it was necessary and reasonable to establish a
         temporary moratorium on accepting and processing applications for the
         establishment and use of data centers within the City for six (6) months, or until
         the City enacted a regulatory ordinance setting forth the regulations for the
         establishment and use of data centers within the City.

WHEREAS, on March 24, 2026 the City Council adopted Resolution No. 2026-06 that
         Imposed a Temporary Moratorium for a period of six (6) months on the
         establishing, permitting, consideration, approval, location, construction, and/or
         installation of any data centers in the City of Wixom; and
WHEREAS, on June 29th, 2026 the Wixom Planning Commission held a formally noticed public
         hearing to take comment on proposed ordinance amendments to define and
         regulate data center uses and approved a recommended set of amendments to
         City Council; and

WHEREAS, on August 11th, 2026 the City Council approved the introduction and first reading
         of an Ordinance to Amend the Wixom Code of Ordinances, Title 18, Zoning,
         Amend Section 18.09 – Industrial Districts, Section 18.09.020 – Schedule of Uses
         to Address the Permissibility of Data Centers in the M-1 and M-2 Zoning Districts;
         Amend Section 18.09.040 – Standards Applicable to Specific Uses to Provide
         Specific Standards for Data Center Uses in the M-1 and M-2 Zoning Districts; and
         Amendment Section 18.24.030 to Provide Definitions for Data Centers as, as
         amended and

WHEREAS, on August 25th, 2026 the City Council approved the second reading and adoption
         of Ordinance No. 2026-01, to Amend the Wixom Code of Ordinances, Title 18,
         Zoning, Amend Section 18.09 – Industrial Districts, Section 18.09.020 – Schedule
         of Uses to Address the Permissibility of Data Centers in the M-1 and M-2 Zoning
         Districts; Amend Section 18.09.040 – Standards Applicable to Specific Uses to
         Provide Specific Standards for Data Center Uses in the M-1 and M-2 Zoning
         Districts; and Amendment Section 18.24.030 to Provide Definitions for Data
         Centers; and

WHEREAS, this ordinance will be enacted and go into effect as of September 6, 2026

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council as follows:

Section 1:        Moratorium. The City hereby repeals Resolution 2026-06 that temporarily
                  enacted a blanket prohibition on processing site plans proposing the
                  establishment and use of data centers, including without limitation, approval of
                  such use within the City, pending further study and enactment of City regulation
                  addressing the same.

Section 2:        Effective Date. This Moratorium shall be terminated effective immediately
                  following the enactment and enforceable effect of Ordinance No. 2026-01 on
                  September 6, 2026.




VOTE:
          YEAS: (7) Mayor Beagle, Deputy Mayor Gottschall, Councilmember Behrmann,
                    Gronlund-Fox, Kennedy, O’Dea, and Simmons

          NAYS: (0)

          ABSENT: (0)


Resolution adopted this 25th day of August, 2026.
