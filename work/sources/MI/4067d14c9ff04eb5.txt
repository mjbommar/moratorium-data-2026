MORATORIUM, SECTION 138-5.301 TEMPORARY MORATORIUM FOR DATA CENTERS, TO
ARTICLE 5-SCHEDULE OF REGULATIONS, TO ESTABLISH A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM
SUSPENDING THE CITY’S PROCESSING AND ACCEPTANCE OF APPLICATIONS AND PLANS FOR
CODIFIED ORDINANCE NO. 201


DEVELOPMENT, AND IMPROVEMENT OF DATA CENTERS, TO CHAPTER 138, ZONING, OF THE
CODE OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF ROCHESTER HILLS, OAKLAND COUNTY, MICHIGAN, TO
PRESCRIBE A PENALTY FOR VIOLATIONS, AND TO REPEAL INCONSISTENT OR CONFLICTING
ORDINANCES.

THE CITY OF ROCHESTER HILLS ORDAINS:

Section 1. Section 138-13.101 to Chapter 138 of the Code of Ordinances, shall be amended to
add a definition for Data Center, as follows:

       Data Center: A facility used primarily for the storage, management, processing, and
       transmission of digital data, which houses computer and/or network equipment, servers,
       data storage devices, and other associated components. This use includes the mechanical
       and electrical infrastructure necessary to support the facility's operation, and may also
       include, but is not limited to cooling systems, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), backup
       generators, electrical substations, and fuel storage. A Data Center is distinct from a
       professional office or warehouse use.

Section 2. Chapter 3, Section 138-5.301 to Chapter 138 of the Code of Ordinances, Article 5-
Schedule of Regulations, shall be added as follows:

Chapter 3, TEMPORARY MORATORIUM

Sec. 138-5.301. Temporary Moratorium for Data Centers.

     (a)   Purpose.

           1.   The City has identified concerns relating to the potential location and regulation
                of Data Centers due to the speed of and significantly evolving nature of Data
                Centers and their potential impacts, including, but not limited to, building height,
                size, density of development, noise, vibration, light pollution, water requirements,
                electricity demands, and their compatibility with residential and other zoning
                districts and what regulations should be required to protect other existing uses
                within the City and appropriately regulate size, location, and conditions of
                operation for Data Centers.

           2.   In order to maintain the status quo while the City studies and reevaluates Data
                Centers and evaluates appropriate zoning locations and regulations for such
                uses, the City Council determines it is in the City’s best interest to adopt a
                moratorium temporarily suspending the City’s processing or acceptance of
                applications, plans, or permits for the development, improvement, or alteration
                of land for Data Centers.

      (b) Moratorium. A temporary moratorium is established suspending the processing and
acceptance of any applications, plans, or permits for the development, improvement, or alteration
of any land parcel, lot, or premises for Data Centers within the City. This moratorium does not
apply to any Data Center development or building that received, or has submitted complete plans
to the City for, site plan approval on or before February 23, 2026.

      This moratorium shall remain in effect until repealed by the City Council or until the
expiration of 180 calendar days, whichever occurs sooner. The City Council also reserves the right,
at the end of the 180 day period to extend the moratorium upon a showing that work and review
of existing and proposed ordinances has been ongoing and that additional time is necessary to
complete such work. The time of such extension shall be determined at that time and shall be the
least amount of time necessary to appropriately complete such work.

Section 3. Severability. This ordinance and each article, section, subsection, paragraph,
subparagraph, part, provision, sentence, word, and portion thereof are hereby declared to be
severable, and if they or any of them are declared to be invalid or unenforceable for any reason
by a court of competent jurisdiction, it is hereby provided that the remainder of this ordinance
shall not be affected thereby.

Section 4. Penalty. All violations of this ordinance shall be municipal civil infractions and upon a
determination of responsibility therefor shall be punishable by a civil fine of not more than $500.

Section 5. Repeal, Effective Date, Adoption.

       (1)     Repeal. All regulatory provisions contained in other City ordinances, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this ordinance, are hereby repealed.

        (2)     Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective on Monday, March 16, 2026
following its publication in the Oakland Press on Sunday, March 15, 2026.

         (3)    Adoption. This ordinance was adopted by the City Council of the City of Rochester
Hills at a meeting thereof held on Monday, March 9, 2026.
