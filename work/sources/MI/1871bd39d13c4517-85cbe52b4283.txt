ORDINANCE NO. ZA 2026-02

AN ORDINANCE TO IMPOSE A MORATORIUM ON THE ISSUANCE OF

LAND USE PERMITS FOR DATA CENTERS OR SIMILAR USES

WITHIN HURON COUNTY, MICHIGAN

Text Amendment to Huron County Zoning Ordinance Article XV:

[New Section] Section 15.02.13 Data Center Moratorium

The County of Huron ordains:

WHEREAS, the County desires to ascertain the best and safest path to regulate the use of

land under the Huron County Zoning Ordinance for data centers and similar uses, e.g., data

mining, server farms, etc.; and

WHEREAS, the County Board of Commissioners has determined that it is desirable to avoid

the issuance of land use permits for data centers and similar uses until an amendment to the

Zoning Ordinance becomes effective, and specific standards and regulations are established;

and

WHEREAS, the County Planning Commission recommended to the County Board that it set a

moratorium for the approval of land use permits for data centers and similar uses for six (6)

months, to allow time for the development of standards for the regulation of proposed data

centers and similar uses; and

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the following ordinance – to impose a moratorium on

the issuance of land use permits for data centers and similar uses for a period of six (6) months

from the effective date of this ordinance, or until the effective date of adoption of proper text

amendments to the Zoning Ordinance, whichever occurs first – is hereby adopted.

Section 1. Findings.

1. It is within the right of the County under the County Zoning Ordinance t to establish

reasonable regulations to control land uses for data centers and similar uses.

2. A moratorium should be imposed upon the issuance of any and all land use

permits for data centers and similar uses for a period of six (6) months, or until

an amendment of the Zoning Ordinance becomes effective, whichever occurs

first.

Section 2. Moratorium. There is imposed for a period of six (6) months from the

effective date of this ordinance a moratorium upon the issuance of any and all land use permits

for data centers and similar uses within the jurisdiction of the Huron County Zoning Ordinance.

Section 3. Expiration. The moratorium imposed by this ordinance shall expire the

earlier of six (6) months from its effective date, or on the effective date of an amendment to the

Zoning Ordinance, whichever occurs first.

Section 4. Savings Clause. This ordinance shall in no manner affect pending

litigation, either civil or criminal, founded or growing out of any ordinance, resolution, order or

parts thereof, hereby repealed; and this ordinance shall in no manner affect any rights, claims,

privileges, immunities or causes of action of the County or other person, either criminal or civil,

that may have already occurred, accrued or grown out of any ordinance, resolution, order or

policy, or any part thereof, hereby repealed.

Section 5.  Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect upon the expiration of

seven (7) days after notice of ordinance adoption is published in a newspaper of general

circulation in the County.
