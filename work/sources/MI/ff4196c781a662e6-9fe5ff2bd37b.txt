Planning Commission

The Planning Commission is made up of 7 members of the community, appointed by the Township Board. This commission is responsible for guiding and overseeing the development and growth of a community or municipality. The duties of a Planning Commission typically include:

Comprehensive Planning: Developing and maintaining the Master Plan which is a comprehensive plan for the community’s growth and development. This plan includes land use policies, transportation plans, housing strategies, and environmental considerations.
Zoning Regulations: Recommending and reviewing zoning ordinances that regulate how land and buildings can be used within the community. This includes determining appropriate zones for residential, commercial, industrial, and other purposes.
Land Use Reviews: Evaluating and making recommendations on proposed developments, subdivisions, and site plans. This involves considering factors such as environmental impact, infrastructure needs, and adherence to zoning regulations.
Public Input: Engaging with the public to gather input on planning matters. This may involve holding public hearings, workshops, or forums to ensure that community members have a voice in the planning process.
Capital Improvement Planning: Collaborating on the identification and prioritization of infrastructure projects and capital improvements needed to support the community’s growth.
Policy Recommendations: Providing recommendations to the local legislative body (township board) on matters related to land use, development, and community growth.
Regional Coordination: Coordinating with neighboring communities and regional entities to address issues that may cross municipal boundaries, such as transportation and environmental concerns.
Reviewing Amendments: Reviewing and recommending changes to existing zoning ordinances and comprehensive plans as needed to adapt to evolving community needs and priorities.
