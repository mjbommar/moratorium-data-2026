                                      WILLIAMS CHARTER TOWNSHIP
                                    BOARD OF TRUSTEES- Regular Meeting
                                              May 12, 2026

The Board of Trustees of Williams Charter Township held a Regular Meeting at Williams Township Hall on
May 12, 2026.

I.    Call to Order and Roll Call
The meeting was called to order by Jerry Putt (Supervisor) at 3:00 p.m.

Present:       Jerry Putt (Supervisor), Terri Charbonneau (Treasurer), Kellie Andrus (Clerk), Doug
               Behmlander (Trustee), Tom Paige (Trustee), Jim Plant (Trustee), Jason Gower (Trustee)
Excused:       None
Pledge of Allegiance to the flag was said in unison.
II.    Public Input none.
III.   Changes to the Agenda- Jerry Putt moved, supported by Tom Paige, to add agenda items: 1652 W.
Midland Road, 1580 Roberts Road, Wolverine Fireworks Display July 9, BR 800X Magnum Blower, and
Supervisor Credit Card. AYES: 7 NAYS: 0 Motion unanimously carried.

IV.      Consent Agenda- Jerry Putt moved, supported by Tom Paige, to accept and approve the Consent
Agenda as presented:
         1. Approval of Regular Meeting Minutes for April 14, 2026
         2. Receive Quarterly Investment and Monthly Financial Statements for April 2026
         3. Receive Monthly Payroll and Revenue & Expenditure Reports for April 2026
         4. Approve List of Available Workshops in Michigan
         5. Receive Committee Reports
         6. Receive Miscellaneous Communications and Correspondence
Roll call vote taken. Motion unanimously carried.

V.       Elected Officials Reports- was held.
VI.      Regular Agenda

         necessary, has been authorized at the April 14, 2026 Board of Trustees Meeting.

      3. 5035 S Garfield Road- Land Use Violation- will submit amended site plan. Legal action, if necessary,
         has been authorized at the April 14, 2026 Board of Trustees Meeting.

      4. 1997 W. Midland Road- Land Use Violation- will submit amended site plan. Legal action, if

     6. 1580 Roberts Road- Land Use Violation

    7. Movies in the Park
Jery Putt moved, supported by Jim Plant, to authorize the Treasurer to collaborate with the City of Auburn to
use the township facilities to show a movie and host trunk or treat in the hall in October at no charge. AYES: 7
NAYS: 0. Motion carried.

    8. Bay Area Storm Water Authority (BASWA) Appointments
Jason Gower moved, supported by Jim Plant, to appoint Jerry Putt to serve as trustee and Tom Paige to be
alternate trustee on BASWA as term is determined by BASWA Board. AYES: 7 NAYS: 0. Motion carried.

    9. Ordinance No. 33-202 Declaring a Moratorium
Tom Paige moved, supported by Jim Plant, to accept the recommendation to waive 2nd reading of Ordinance No.
33-202 Declaring a Moratorium on Data Center Review, adopt and direct the Clerk to publish the ordinance
summary in the Pinconning Journal on Wednesday, May 20, 2026. AYES: 7 NAYS: 0. Motion carried.

    10. Ordinance No. 33-203 Fences, Dumpsters, and Site Plan Review
Doug Behmlander moved, supported by Tom Paige, to accept the recommendation to waive 2nd reading of
Ordinance No. 33-203 Fences, Dumpsters, and Site Plan Review, adopt and direct the Clerk to publish the
ordinance summary in the Pinconning Journal on Wednesday, May 20, 2026. AYES: 7 NAYS: 0. Motion carried.

    11. Ordinance No. 33-204 Zoning Map Amendment
Doug Behmlander moved, supported by Kellie Andrus, to accept the recommendation to waive 2nd reading of
Ordinance No. 33-204 Zoning Map Amendment, adopt and direct the Clerk to publish the ordinance summary in
the Pinconning Journal. AYES: 7 NAYS: 0. Motion carried.

    12. Ordinance No. 56-J Sewer Rate
Tom Paige moved, supported by Jim Plant, to accept the recommendation to waive 2nd reading of Ordinance
No. 56-J Sewer Rate, adopt and direct the Clerk to publish the ordinance summary in the Pinconning Journal.
AYES: 7 NAYS: 0. Motion carried.

    13. Township Property- 1136 W. Midland Road- Withdrawn by Supervisor.

    14. Metro Act Right of Way Permit Extension
Tom Paige moved, supported by Kellie Andrus, to approve the METRO Act Permit Extension and authorize
the Supervisor or Clerk to sign the extension as requested by Windstream Norlight, LLC for a term to end
April 23, 2028 contingent upon Windstream Norlight providing Williams Township with the information

     15. Wolverine Fireworks Display
Jason Gower moved, supported by Jerry Putt, to authorize the Supervisor to sign and issue a Permit for
fireworks to Wolverine Fireworks Display, Inc. for July 9, 2026 (rain date July 10), at the end of 8 Mile Road off of

VII.    Approve Unpaid Bills
Jason Gower moved, supported by Jerry Putt, to pay all bills on the Unpaid Bills List as presented with the
addition of VTG monthly invoice. Roll call vote taken. Motion unanimously carried.
