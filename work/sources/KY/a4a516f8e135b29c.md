# Councilwoman Chappell reintroduces temporary moratorium on data centers

source: https://louisvilleky.gov/news/councilwoman-chappell-reintroduces-temporary-moratorium-data-centers
fetched: 2026-09-23T12:02:49+00:00

Louisville (May 11, 2026) – In response to growing community concerns regarding future data center proposals in Louisville, Councilwoman Jennifer Chappell (D-15) has reintroduced an ordinance imposing a temporary moratorium on rezoning, conditional use permits and plan applications relating to telecommunication hotels and data centers pending recommendations from the Planning Commission.

Metro Council approved a resolution on September 25, 2025, requesting the Planning Commission to review the Land Development Code regarding data centers. At the time, the review was expected to take approximately six months to complete, with initial recommendations coming from the Planning commission 120 days from the resolutions passage (January 23, 2026). A previously filed temporary mortarium ordinance expired in committee on March 11, 2026, before final action was taken.

Since the Metro Council has not received recommendations from the Planning Commission, Councilwoman Chappell has refiled the ordinance to ensure future data center proposals are reviewed under any updated standards recommended by the Planning Commission, vetted by the community, and adopted by Metro Council.

This legislation would apply only to applications filed after the ordinance’s effective date. If approved, the temporary moratorium would remain in effect for 180 calendar days.

“We are well past the expected timeline for recommendations from the Planning Commission,” said Councilwoman Chappell. “The failure to produce these recommendations has eroded the community’s trust in that Louisville Metro is working to protect residents from harmful and exploitive data centers, I reintroduced this temporary moratorium to ensure our community remains protected while we await the Commission’s recommendations, which are hopefully forthcoming.”

The legislation was filed on Monday, May 11, and will be discussed during the Planning & Zoning Committee meeting on Tuesday, June 2, at 1:00 p.m.
