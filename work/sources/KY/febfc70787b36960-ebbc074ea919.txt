About Nelson County, KY

Located in the heart of the Bluegrass State, Nelson County is a wonderful place to live, work and raise a family. With a thriving business community and a strong family atmosphere, Bardstown and Nelson County have become a destination location. Our history dates back to 1784, and Bardstown, our county seat is Kentucky’s second-oldest city.
