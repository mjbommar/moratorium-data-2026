WHEREAS, the Legislative Council of the Louisville/Jefferson County Metro Government (the "Council") passed Resolution No. 082, Series 2025 requesting the Planning Commission review the Land Development Code regarding Data Centers on September 25, 2025; and
WHEREAS, the expectation was that the review would take approximately six (6) months to complete (by March 25, 2026), with the resolution requesting Planning Commission recommendations within one hundred twenty (120) days of passage (by January 23, 2026); and;
WHEREAS, there was a contemporaneously introduced proposed moratorium on data centers pending the review and revisions which was never passed and instead expired on March 11, 2026; and
WHEREAS, the Council still has not received any recommendations from the Planning Commission, nor has a public hearing been held for the public to comment on and suggest potential changes and public hearings are currently being held by the Planning Commission on the Office of Planning's recommended changes; and
WHEREAS, in light of the significance of the subject matter and the substantial delay, during the pendency of the ongoing review, the Council desires to preserve the status quo by preventing changes that might render the Planning Commission's review meaningless.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL OF THE LOUISVILLE/JEFFERSON COUNTY METRO GOVERNMENT AS FOLLOWS:
