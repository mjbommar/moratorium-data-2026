ACTION TAKEN:

The Nelson County Fiscal Court approved the purchase of 0.2 of acres on Timber Trails
in Boston for the construction of a new Communication Tower that will enhance
communications for First Responders.

On motion of M.T. Harned, second of Jon Snow, by unanimous vote of the court, IT IS
HEREBY ORDERED to approve the purchase of 0.2 acres for a new Communication
Tower and authorize Judge Hutchins to sign all Documents related to this project.

Nelson County Fiscal Court approved a land swap that will add 10.25 acres to Dean
Watts Park.

The County owns 16.437 acres on the edge of the Industrial Park appraised for
$250,000.00. That property will be traded for the 10.25-acre tract appraised at
$255,000.00 and is owned by Revelation Properties.

On motion of Jeff Lear, second of Adam Wheatley, by unanimous vote of the court, IT
IS HEREBY ORDERED to approve the trade of land as presented and authorize Judge
Hutchins to sign all Documents related to this project. See page 846.
