During the discussion on the Commissioners’ request to seek sealed bids for

hydrogeologic assessment, Ahmic reported that there have been ongoing conversations

regarding a potential ““water study”, or assessment, between the county and the Natural

Resources Districts. T The collaboration will require an interlocal agreement between all

of the entities, which has already been drafted. After meeting with both the Upper Big

Blue and the Lower Platte South Natural Resources Districts, both have agreed to come

together and fund half of the cost of the assessment. Because the total cost of the

project would exceed the requirements of the sealed bidding process under the County

Purchasing Act, the board needs to seek bids in order to proceed. The contract will be

between the county and the vendor chosen when the bid is awarded. Seward County

will pay the cost of the contract to the vendor, and Natural Resources Districts will

reimburse the county for half of the cost. The sealed bids will be solicited through a

public notice in the local newspaper once a week for two consecutive weeks, and will be

opened at the October 27, 2026 Board of Commissioners meeting.

Moved by Pekarek and seconded by Curtis to seek sealed bids for a hydrogeologic

Affirmative Vote: Pekarek, Curtis, Zabrocki, Ahmic Motion Carried
