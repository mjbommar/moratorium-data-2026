OFFICIAL RESOLUTION OF THE HITCHCOCK COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS RESOLUTION NO. 26-21


WHEREAS, pursuant to Public Law 118-185, the Hitchcock County Board of


Commissioners (COUNTY) is eligible to pursue transfer of legal title of certain lands and

facilities within the District; and

WHEREAS, the Bureau of Reclamation (“RECLAMATION”) and the COUNTY have


taken initial steps to proceed with title transfer; and

WHEREAS, RECLAMATION has established a categorical exclusion under the


National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) to facilitate certain transfers of projects and facilities

from the United States to qualifying non-federal entities; and

WHEREAS, RECLAMATION is requesting that the COUNTY verify and affirm the


COUNTY’s eligibility as a qualifying non-federal entity with various Categorical Exclusion

Qualification Factor Statements; and

WHEREAS, RECLAMATION has provided the COUNTY with a copy of the


completed Low Intensity Real Property Questionnaire completed during the property inspection,

most recent concession reviews, and all other relevant inspections and reports related to the

transferred lands; and

WHEREAS, Public Law 118-185 requires the COUNTY to confirm its ability to meet


all terms, conditions, and obligations necessary to receive conveyance of Federal lands,

including demonstrating technical capability, financial capacity, and long-term management

responsibility for the requested Federal land, as well as complying with State fire, safety, and

health codes within the required timeframes.

WHEREAS, RECLAMATION is requesting the COUNTY confirm that the duly


authorized signatory for all legal instruments required to effectuate the transfer is the Chairman

of the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners as of the date of signing.

WHEREAS, appraisals establishing the fair market value of the requested Federal lands


have been completed in accordance with Public Law 118-185 and the appraisal results have been

provided to the COUNTY.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Hitchcock County Board of


Commissioners agrees and affirms the following:

1. The COUNTY has the technical and financial capability to operate and maintain the

proposed transferred land consistent with current and historical use and plans to continue

to do so through its own contracts and assessments.

2. The COUNTY ensures that there are no competing demands for use of the proposed

transferred lands, with the exception of those demands accommodated by existing

contractual arrangements. Areas that do have competing interests not accommodated by

an existing arrangement will not be transferred and will continue to support the project

lands and facilities, as well as meet the demands for public use. These areas will be

identified and included in the title transfer agreement. Generally, these would be areas

that contain high value wildlife habitat or provide for a high level of public use.

3. The COUNTY ensures that the facilities proposed for transfer are not hydrologically

integrated with other facilities, thereby impacting other contractors, stakeholders, or

activities, with the exception of those impacts accommodated by existing contractual

arrangements.

4. The COUNTY ensures that any issues involving existing contracts and agreements, and

interstate compacts and agreements, will be resolved, and treaty and international

agreement obligations will be fulfilled prior to transferring the proposed lands.

5. The COUNTY agrees to assume responsibility for all commitments and agreements into

the future related to the proposed transferred facilities identified in the title transfer

agreement.

6. The COUNTY confirms it has reviewed all materials provided by RECLAMATION,

including the Low Intensity Real Property Questionnaire, concession reviews, and

relevant inspection reports, and affirms that it understands the proposed transferred lands

and facilities will be conveyed in their current ‘as-is’ condition.

7. The COUNTY confirms its understanding that, in accordance with Public Law 118-185,

it assumes sole responsibility for ensuring that all applicable portions of the conveyed

lands, facilities, and associated improvements are brought into compliance with

applicable State fire, safety, and health codes and standards no later than two years after

the date of conveyance, as required under Section 3(a)(1)(C)(ii) of Public Law 118-185.

8. The COUNTY confirms it has reviewed the requirements of Public Law 118-185 and

possesses the technical, managerial, and financial capability to operate, maintain, and

manage the proposed transferred lands consistent with Public Law 118-185 and agrees to

fulfill all obligations outlined in the title transfer agreement, including long-term

operation, public access, recreation, and resource management responsibilities, subject to

and conditioned upon final approval and execution of the land transfer agreement.

9. The COUNTY approves and designates the Chairman of the Hitchcock County Board of

Commissioners as the duly authorized signatory for all legal instruments necessary to

effectuate the transfer under Public Law 118-185.

10. Subject to and conditioned upon final approval and execution of the land transfer

agreement, the COUNTY agrees that, as consideration for the conveyance, it shall pay to

the Secretary of the Interior the full appraised fair market value for the lands conveyed as

required by Public Law 118-185, Section 3(a)(4)(A)(i).
