RESOLUTION 26-20


BE IT RESOLVED by the County Board of Commissioners, Hitchcock County, Nebraska,


that for the purpose of transferring money from the General Fund to the County Road Fund per

the supporting schedule of transfers budgeted found in the County Budget, that the Board directs

the County Treasurer to transfer as needed and available a portion of the monies collected

pursuant to the General levy for 2026 to the Road Fund, and that said transfer(s) be made by the

County Treasurer after collections have been calculated.

The total portion from the 2026 County General levy to be transferred to the Road Fund is

.071895 not to exceed $173,796 for the budget year ended June 30, 2027.

The County Board believes that this is the most accurate way to keep track of Road Fund and

General Fund revenues.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 5th day of October, 2026.
