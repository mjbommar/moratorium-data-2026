The regular meeting of the Dundy County Commissioners was held on May 18, 2026, in the
Commissioner Room. The notice of this meeting was published on May 14, 2026, in the
issue of the Scoop Media News.
Present: Chairman Sye Tecker, Commissioner Tom Richard, Deputy Clerk – Christina
Collier, Assessor-Tish Burrell, Treasurer-Penny Denny, Sheriff Bang, County Attorney-Arlan
Wine, Karen Baker, Deputy Lucci, Steve Melnick (Colonial Life), Joann Decker, Bob Willis,
Russell Stamm, Wendi Vanderford, and Brian Hill (Lake Mac Assessments).

Chairman Sye Tecker called the meeting to order at 8:32 AM and announced the location of
the Open Meetings Act on the board on the south wall.

Motion was made by Commissioner Richard to approve the agenda, seconded by
Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Richard, Tecker. Absent – Keiser.

Commissioner Richard moved to approve the minutes of May 4, 2026, seconded by
Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Richard, Tecker. Absent – Keiser.

Public Comment: Steve Melnick addressed the commissioners to share there are no
changes with the Colonial Life premium fees and thanked the county for their business.

Brian Hill with Lake Mac Assessments addressed the commissioners to share findings for
the previous year’s property assessments. Approximately 700, rural properties were
evaluated with no major changes due to assessment tables being similar to previous years.
There was approximately $25 million in new construction, primarily from Blackshirt
Feeders. Notices of Valuation Changes will be mailed by June 2, 2026, with Lake Mac
Assessments to be here to assist with any valuation protests. The coming year’s
assessments will be for commercial properties.

The Benkelman Cemetery Board spoke with the commissioners about their concerns for
the future of the cemetery. The most important concern is the upkeep of the cemetery
grounds. Currently, there is no cemetery sexton to take over when Bob Willis ceases his
maintenance duties at the end of May (totaling 32 years). The tax income for the cemetery
each year is approximately $14,000 with insurance costs last year of $4,104.44. This does
not leave enough funds to properly pay sextons even though they recently raised the rate
for plot purchases. The cemetery board asked if the county would be willing to take on the
grounds keeping as they may have an easier time hiring a sexton. Chairman Tecker would
like to be able to look over budgeting information and revisit at the next meeting.

Commissioners reviewed claims for the pay period of April 26 – May 9, 2026. Questions
and/or comments were addressed and discussed as presented. Motion made by
Commissioner Richard to approve claims as presented, seconded by Chairman Tecker.
Ayes: Richard, Tecker. Absent – Keiser.

No monies were transferred from the Inheritance Tax account.

Penny Denny addressed the commissioners on behalf of the Methodist Church. The church
is hosting Vacation Bible School next week and would like to use the grass area to the north
of the courthouse. The commissioners agreed that would be an acceptable use of the area.

Christina Collier shared information pertaining to the recent primary election. She
reported there were no major issues on election day and appreciated the extra help from
Dave Shively, sent to help by Secretary of State Evnen’s office. The deputy clerk thanked
Sheriff Bang and Chief Deputy Lucci for their assistance transferring voting machines to
and from Haigler. She wanted to especially thank the poll workers and businesses/groups
that allow their buildings to be used as polling places.

Karen Baker spoke on changes that were approved for the employee handbook in
December 2025 but had not been followed through on. In the meeting minutes from
December 15, 2025, there were three changes to take effect. Firstly, changing
“Probationary Period” to “Introductory Period”. Second, changing billing hours from 35 to
32 for employees to earn PTO hours. Lastly, there was a change regarding the use of PTO
hours, from a minimum of two-week notice to as much notice as practicable. Chairman
Tecker instructed the deputy clerk to insert a copy into each employee paystub for
employee knowledge.

Ms. Baker also addressed the ongoing issue of the emergency phone in the courthouse
elevator, which has been out of compliance for over a year. Approximately a month to a
month and a half ago, she had given information to the County Clerk to contact Kone
Service Center and was informed the company doesn’t come here. Ms. Baker felt that it
was possible the information wasn’t relayed correctly so she contacted the company
herself. She was informed that the company provides services for several counties in our
region and they are willing to come here to do repairs. Sheriff Bang asked about the phone
control being ajar from its control panel and the door open as he feels it could be a safety
concern. Ms. Baker informed the group that there had been an attempt to correct the issue
by the County Clerk and phone had never been put back into its proper position. The issue
has been continued until the next meeting so that the board can review forms presented to
allow services to be completed by KONE.


Mike Boden and Jessica Witting with NACO presented material and addressed the
commissioners with the changes coming as it is nearly time for open enrollment for county
employees. It looks as though the rate for the plans we have will go up 4.68% from the
current year. Commissioner Richard mentioned the difficulty of finding applicants for open
positions with no paid family insurance coverage. Mr. Boden stated that there are several
counties that are going or have gone to partial pay for family insurance. The commissioners
asked for information on some plans with family options for the next meeting.

Elaine Menzel, also with NACO, discussed different pieces of legislation that have been
passed or failed to pass in the state of Nebraska and how they pertain to Dundy County.
Some of those are LB 663, LB 468, LB 1219, LB 596, and LB 803.

Approving the bid(s) for contract hauling gravel will be added to the 6/1/26 agenda.

Next on the agenda was to approve the gravel agreement with Windmill Ranch, LLC. The
agreement is for $7/ton and will not renew automatically on an annual basis.
Commissioner Richard made a motion to approve the agreement as written, seconded by
Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Richard, Tecker. Absent – Keiser.

With the absence of Commissioner Keiser, the topics of truck leasing bids and road
maintenance routes will be moved to the 6/1/26 agenda.

County Attorney Arlan Wine addressed the commissioners on the Region 51 Emergency
Management Agreement that the commissioners approved at the 5/4/26 meeting. The
agreement has been sent to the City of Benkelman for their meeting this evening. After the
city has approved the details, the agreement will be sent to the Village of Haigler.
The next topic for Mr. Wine was the Max tornado siren. Brophy Electric inspected the
current siren and deemed it unusable. The estimated cost to replace it is $10,000-$25,000.
Brandon Meyers with Region 51 Emergency Management is working on a grant for the
expense of replacing the siren. The siren must be functioning or taken down; therefore, it
will be completely removed until a replacement has been found.

Sheriff Bang presented his April report including 38 calls for the month. There are two
inmates in long-term lodging, one in Red Willow County and one in Lincoln County.

Deputy Clerk Collier asked for a discussion in executive session to protect the information
and reputation of a county employee. A motion was made by Commissioner Richard to go
into executive session at 10:15 am, seconded by Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Richard, Tecker.
Absent – Keiser.

At 11:02 am, Chairman Tecker made a motion to exit executive session, seconded by
Commissioner Richard. Ayes: Richard, Tecker. Absent – Keiser.

Regarding the executive session, Mr. Wine will draft a letter to the employee discussed. The
commissioners also approved the hire of a full-time employee for the County Clerk’s office.

With no further business, there was a motion made by Commissioner Richard to adjourn
the meeting at 11:04 am, seconded by Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Richard, Tecker. Absent –
Keiser. The next meeting is scheduled for June 1, 2026, at 8:30 A.M. in the Commissioners
Room.
