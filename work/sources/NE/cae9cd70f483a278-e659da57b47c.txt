RESOLUTION #2025-6


(Temporary Moratorium on

Wind Energy Installations & Solar Power Generation Systems)

WHEREAS, the County of Hitchcock County, Nebraska, is a duly organized county, existing

pursuant to the laws of the State of Nebraska; and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners seeks to place a temporary

moratorium on development of commercial wind energy conversion systems and the issuance of

conditional use permits for the same pursuant to Section 612 of the Hitchcock County Zoning

Regulations for the lesser of one year or until such time as the Hitchcock County Board of

Commissioners has the opportunity to study and adopt updated zoning regulations with respect

to wind energy conversion systems covered in Section 612 of the Hitchcock County Zoning

Regulations; and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners also seeks to place a

temporary moratorium on development of commercial grade solar energy generation/conversion

systems, which for purposes of this moratorium shall be any solar conversion system with a rated

capacity in excess of one hundred (100) kW in name plate generating capacity, as accessory

uses in all zoning districts as well as the issuance of any conditional use permits in this regard

pursuant to the Hitchcock County Zoning Regulations for the lesser of one year or until such

time as the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners has the opportunity to study and adopt

appropriate zoning regulations with respect to commercial and/or utility grade solar energy

generation/conversion systems; and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners believe in the rights of

individual landowners to make the best and most efficient use of their property and hereby state

that the intent of this moratorium is not to discourage the adoption and use of “small wind energy

systems” as defined in Section 612.02 of the Hitchcock County Zoning Regulations nor is it the

intent to discourage individual land owners from the adoption and use of small scale solar energy

conversion systems (ie, less than one hundred (100) kWs in total name plate generating

capacity), specifically designed and used for private, non-commercial applications, including

without limitation, solar powered livestock watering facilities and other such farm or single

family residential applications. The Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners specifically

acknowledges the utility of such systems, especially within the ranching and agriculture

industries, as well as their utility to individual homeowners and fully support their continued use

and proliferation in that regard; and

WHEREAS, the Board, states that it is the express intent of the Hitchcock County Board

of Commissioners to enact this moratorium to provide time for a comprehensive regulatory

scheme to be developed to manage and regulate the location, implementation and

decommissioning of all commercial and/or utility grade wind energy and solar energy conversion

systems which would otherwise provide direct financial benefit to a relative few Hitchcock

County landowners while allowing the negative impacts to be borne by all Hitchcock County

residents.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Hitchcock County Board of

Commissioners, that for a period of one year from the date of this resolution, or until such time

as the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners can study and adopt appropriate updated

zoning regulations with respect to commercial and/or utility grade wind and solar energy

conversion systems, as described hereinabove, whichever is lesser, a moratorium shall hereby be

enacted and no commercial and/or utility grade wind energy conversion systems or solar energy

conversion systems with a capacity of one hundred (100) kWs or more, in total name plate

generating capacity, shall be constructed or allowed as accessory uses in any zoning district nor

shall any conditional use permits be issued for the same.

A motion was made by County Board member Baker, seconded by County Board

member Nichols to approve and adopt this herein Resolution.

Voting Aye: Nichols, Baker

Voting Nay: none

Abstaining: none

Not Present: Wertz

ADOPTED this 3rd day of February, 2025

RESOLUTION #2025-7


(Temporary Moratorium on D Data Centers & Crypto Mining Facilities)

WHEREAS, the County of Hitchcock County, Nebraska, is a duly organized county, existing

pursuant to the laws of the State of Nebraska; and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners seeks to place a temporary

moratorium on location, construction, development and operation of commercial “Crypto Mining

Facilities”being a facilitwith specialized setups designed to mine cryptocurrencies, such as

, y

Bitcoin and Ethereum and in which the mining involves solving complex mathematical problems

to validate transactions on a blockchain network, which is essential for maintaining the

network’s integrity and generating new units of the cryptocurrency, and the issuance of

conditional use permits for the same in the “A“AG-G, General Agricultural District” pursuant to

Section 501.06 and in the “RCI, Rural Commercial/Industrial District” pursuant to Section

502.05 of the Hitchcock County Zoning Regulations for the lesser of one year or until such time

as the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners has the opportunity to study and adopt

appropriate zoning regulations with respect to such crypto mining facilities as the same are not

adequately addressed in the Hitchcock County Zoning Regulations at this time; and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners also seeks to place a

temporary moratorium on location, construction, development and operation of commercial

“Data Centers”, being a specialized facilities designed to house computer systems and associated

components, such as telecommunications and storage systems that provide the infrastructure

necessary for various IT operations, including data storage, management, and dissemination,

and the issuance of conditional use permits for the same in the “A“AG-G, General Agricultural

District” pursuant to Section 501.06 and in the “RCI, Rural Commercial/Industrial District”

pursuant to Section 502.05 of the Hitchcock County Zoning Regulations for the lesser of one

year or until such time as the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners has the opportunity to

study and adopt appropriate zoning regulations with respect to such crypto mining facilities as

the same are not adequately addressed in the Hitchcock County Zoning Regulations at this time;

and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners believe in the rights of

individual landowners to make the best and most efficient use of their property. However

because (a) crypto mining fafacilities are known for high energy consumption due to the intensive

computational requirements of mining, this has led to concerns about their environmental

impact; (b) crypto mining fafacilities also require significant cooling solutions but may not have

the same level of advanced systems as true “data centers”, may lead to higher environmental

footprints in some cases; and (c) the energy consumption of data centers varies widely depending

on the size and type of operations, appropriate regulation of the same is necessary to ensure the

operation is consistent with, and in the best interests of the residents of Hitchcock County; and

WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners hereby state that the intent of

this moratorium is not to discourage the location, operation and use of data centers and crypto

mining facilities as a conditional use in the “I, I, InIndustrial District” as defined in the Hitchcock

County Zoning Regulations, upon such appropriate and necessary conditions as may be

determined to ensure that such use is consistent with the intent statement of said District; and

WHEREAS, the Board, states that it is the express intent of the Hitchcock County Board

of Commissioners to enact this moratorium to provide time for a comprehensive regulatory

scheme to be developed to manage and regulate the location, implementation and

decommissioning of all such data centers and crypto mining centers which would otherwise

provide direct financial benefit to a relative few Hitchcock County landowners while allowing

the negative impacts to be borne by all Hitchcock County residents.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Hitchcock County Board of

Commissioners, that for a period of one year from the date of this resolution, or until such time

as the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners can study and adopt appropriate updated

zoning regulations with respect to commercial Data Centers and also Crypto Mining Facilities, as

described hereinabove, whichever is lesser, a moratorium shall hereby be enacted and no

commercial Data Centers or Crypto Mining Facilities, shall be located, constructed, developed,

operated or allowed as accessory uses in the “A“AG-G” or “RCI” I” zoning districts nor shall any

conditional use permits be issued for the same.

A motion was made by County Board member Nichols, seconded by County Board

member Baker to approve and adopt this herein Resolution.

Voting Aye: Nichols, Baker

Voting Nay: none

Abstaining: none

Not Present: Wertz

ADOPTED this 3rd day of February, 2025
