at the Hitchcock County Courthouse Commissioner’s Room, 229 East D Street, Trenton,
Nebraska on Monday the 20th day of October 2025 commencing at 9:00 o’clock a.m. Present
were Chair Paul Nichols, Commissioner Ron Wertz and County Clerk Margaret Pollmann.
Commissioner Christopher Baker was absent. Notice of the meeting was given in advance
thereof by publication on October 16th, 2025 in Scoop Media News, the designated method for
giving notice. Board agendas are posted on the County’s website www.hitchcockcounty.ne.gov.
Notice of the meeting was given to the Chair and all members of the Board. All proceedings
hereafter shown were taken while the convened meeting was open to the attendance of the
public. The Open Meetings Act was available for review and Chair indicated the location of
such copy in the room where the meeting was being held.

the office of the County Clerk and on the County’s website www.hitchcockcounty.ne.gov.

                                    RESOLUTION #25-20

        WHEREAS, the County of Hitchcock County, Nebraska, hereinafter referred to as
“COUNTY”, is a duly organized political subdivision, existing pursuant to the laws of the State of
Nebraska and acting by and through the Hitchcock County Board of Commissioners (hereinafter
referred to as the “BOARD”); and

       WHEREAS, COUNTY previously adopted and implemented the Hitchcock County Zoning
Permit Application and the Hitchcock County Application for Conditional Use forms; and

         WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Planning Commission did on October 8th, 2025 having
first held the requisite public hearing(s) and public meeting(s) did recommend to COUNTY,
approval of amendment(s) to the Hitchcock County Zoning Permit Application and the Hitchcock
County Application for Conditional Use, as set forth in the Resolution/Recommendation of the
Hitchcock County Planning Commission which recommendation was presented to the COUNTY
on October 20th, 2025; and

       WHEREAS, the COUNTY held a public hearing October 20th, 2025 to hear comments and
to receive evidence regarding the proposed amendment(s) to the Hitchcock County Zoning
Regulations; and

        WHEREAS, notice of said hearing was first duly provided in accordance with law by
publishing notice thereof in Scoop Media News, a newspaper of general circulation in the County,
on October 9th, 2025; and

        WHEREAS, a true and complete copy of the proposed amendment(s) to the Hitchcock
County Zoning Regulations were available for inspection during the regular business hours at the
office of the County Clerk and on the County’s website at least ten days prior to said hearing; and

         WHEREAS, a true and complete copy of the proposed amendment(s) to the Hitchcock
County Zoning Permit Application and the Hitchcock County Application For Conditional Use,
are attached hereto as Exhibit “A” and Exhibit “B” and are incorporated herein by this reference
as if set forth at length at this point; and

        WHEREAS, the COUNTY, having first duly considered said recommendation and the
public comments offered at said public hearing by the parties in interest and the citizens of the
COUNTY in attendance thereat, and being fully advised in the premises, finds that the
amendment(s) to the Hitchcock County Zoning Permit Application and the Hitchcock County
Application For Conditional Use as recommended by the Hitchcock County Planning
Commission are appropriate, and are in the best interests of the COUNTY; and

       WHEREAS, the BOARD finds that the Hitchcock County Planning Commission
recommends approval of the amendment(s) to the Hitchcock County Zoning Permit Application
and the Hitchcock County Application For Conditional Use forms; and

         WHEREAS, the BOARD being first duly advised and satisfied in the premises, finds that it
is in the best interests of the COUNTY to adopt and implement the proposed amendment(s) to the
Hitchcock County Zoning Permit Application and the Hitchcock County Application For
Conditional Use.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the BOARD does hereby amend the
Hitchcock County Zoning Permit Application and the Hitchcock County Application For
Conditional Use, accordingly and the same are hereby adopted to become effective October 20th,
2025.
