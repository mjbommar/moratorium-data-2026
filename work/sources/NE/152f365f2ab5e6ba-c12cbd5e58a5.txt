County Clerk’s Office during normal business hours. The agenda is held open until one

Commissioner Reports:


Commissioner Curtis reported he attended a Seward City Council meeting, rode along

with a deputy from the Sheriff’s Department and toured the task force buildings.

Commissioner Pekarek reported he had a meeting with Commissioner Ahmic regarding

a potential energy study, met with the Veteran Service Officer, and received phone calls

regarding road conditions.

Commissioner Ahmic reported she had a meeting with Commissioner Pekarek regarding

a potential energy study, created an application for Board of Adjustment and had the

Human Resources Director put it, along with the Planning Commission member

application on the county’s website, attended a Milford City Council meeting, received

calls regarding road conditions, had discussions regarding the potential replacement of

a county Fleet/Public T Transit vehicle, attended a meeting with other Seward/Saline

County Solid Waste Management representatives, and was contacted by Upper Big Blue

Natural Resources District regarding the water study interlocal between the county and

the Natural Resources Districts.

Commissioner Zabrocki reported he received a complaint regarding road conditions and

relayed the concerns to the Road Department, and worked with the Public T Transit

Director regarding potentially replacing a county Fleet/Public Transit vehicle.

Commissioner Hain reported she received an email from an organization requesting

information regarding Opioid Funds, and encouraged them to contact the Detention

Center Director, sent road condition information to the Road Department regarding

roads in her district, spoke with the Emergency Manager regarding damage from the

recent flooding, contacted the Beaver Crossing Board Chair about damage in the village

from the recent flooding, reported on the Juvenile Comprehensive Plan Team’s social

media posts and encouraged the others to share them to get information out to the

public, received calls and messages about road conditions and forwarded those to the

Road Department, and continues to encourage people to contact the Road Department

with their reports. Hain also noted that she has received messages from constituents

related to their appreciation for the work done by the Road Department.

Ann Dobesh was present from the Extension Office to discuss the reverse osmosis

system in their office. She noted that the tank for the reverse osmosis system was

replaced last year, and now the system has sprung a significant leak and the filtration

unit needs to be replaced. The total cost for the current repair is beyond what they

have in their budget, so she was inquiring whether building repairs should come from

individual office budgets or a different budget, such as the Building & Grounds budget.

After a brief discussion, the Commissioners were in agreement that repairs such as this

should be funded by the Building & Grounds budget, but questions arose as to whether

the reverse osmosis system was needed, as none of the other buildings are equipped

with it. Some individual departments do subscribe to Culligan water services but were

asked to end those services due to budgeting restrictions. Dobesh presented estimates

obtained for the repair, with the lowest being $540.00 and the highest being $845.00

but reported that she needed to clarify the difference in the estimates. Ahmic asked her

to seek clarification as to what the estimates include, and to seek estimates to see how

much it would cost to instead connect to city water and come back for further

discussion.

Megan Forbes of Seward County Chamber & Development Partnership (SCCDP)

presented a Public Membership Agreement for the Commissioners’ consideration.

Jalissa Gruhn, Public Transit and Fleet Director, was present to request direction from

the Commissioners on potentially replacing one of the county fleet vehicles. The county

had a fleet vehicle that was primarily used by the District Judge Bailiff for travel

between Seward and York Counties, but that car was recently totaled in a traffic

accident. Since that time, the Bailiff has been using another fleet vehicle, which is

causing issues with a shortage of vehicles. An insurance check was issued for the

vehicle that was totaled. There was discussion regarding whether a new fleet vehicle

should be purchased and if so, whether it should be a new vehicle or a comparable

used vehicle to the one that was totaled. Zabrocki has found a similar vehicle to the one

that was totaled. Ahmic noted that the newly acquired vehicle would be a Fleet/t/Public

Transit vehicle, so Public Transit will be able to use it when it is not in use by the Bailiff.

The Commissioners agreed to proceed with replacing the totaled vehicle with a

comparable vehicle priced under $15,000. Armstead noted that three informal bids are

needed for a purchase up to $15,000. Gruhn will work with Zabrocki to find a potential

vehicle, and have legal counsel review a preliminary purchase agreement.

Marissa Pfile, Zoning Administrator, was present to discuss a Conditional Use Permit for

a proposed new wireless tower in the S ½ NW ¼ of Section 27, Township 12 North,

Range 2 East of the 6th P.M. A Public Hearing for this Conditional Use Permit was held

on September 29, 2026. After the Public Hearing, the Commissioners decided that the

applicant on the Conditional Use Permit should be changed, and additional information

on another tower mentioned during the Public Hearing be sought before taking action

on the permit. A  A new application has since been received with the tower company as

the applicant, and Pfile reported she contacted the Federal Communications

Commission (FCC) and discovered that the tower in question during the public hearing

is listed in their database but was never constructed. The Commissioners asked Pfile to

contact the landowner to have the tower removed from the FCC database. Doug Barker

and Darren Hunter, representing The Towers LLC, were present to give information on

the Conditional Use Permit. There was discussion about language in the current Zoning

Regulations, with Hain noting that the current regulations state that a Conditional Use

Permit should be issued to a landowner or their designated agent, with the agent

designation made in writing. Hunter and Barker confirmed that there is a Memorandum

of Lease on record making said designation.
