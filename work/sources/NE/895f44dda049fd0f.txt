          FURNAS COUNTY BOARDS OF COMMISSIONERS, EQUALIZATION and WEED DISTRICT
                                             PROCEEDINGS
                                        August 26, 2025, 9:00 a.m.
    The Furnas County Board of Commissioners meet on Tuesday, August 26, 2025, for a regular board
meeting in the Commissioners’ Meeting Room, 3rd Floor in the County Courthouse. Notice of the meetings
was published in the Valley Voice newspaper and posted on the County’s website. The combined agenda
was e-mailed to each commissioner, posted on County’s website and kept current in the County Clerk’s
Office.
    Chairman Sexton opened the Board of Commissioners meeting at 9:00 a.m. and informed those present
of the Open Meetings Act poster displayed on the wall, stating the provisions of the Act will be followed at
this meeting. Board members present: Mike Sexton, Dennis Tegtman, Allan McCoy. Also, present Lois
Bishop, County Clerk, Patrick Calkins, County Attorney.
    The agenda was approved on a motion by Tegtman, second by McCoy. RCV: Ayes: Sexton, Tegtman,
McCoy. Motion carried.
     The minutes for the August 12, 2025, Board of Commissioner & Equalization meetings and the August
20, 2025 Budget Workshop, were reviewed and approved on a motion by McCoy, second by Tegtman.
RCV: Ayes: Sexton, Tegtman, McCoy. Motion carried.

     Reports received: Treasurers’ Revenue List; Sheriff report on amounts of Collected & Uncollected
Distress Warrants. Correspondence: None.
     Public Comment: No one from the public in attendance.
     Gregg O’Brien, NMC Caterpillar, discussed two NASPO state bid quotes for 2025 Caterpillar 140-15
motor graders with 7-year, 7500-hour Premier warranties. Motion by McCoy, second by Tegtman to
purchase one 2025 Caterpillar 140-15B for $ 291,100 (after $52,500 trade-in) to be paid out of Inheritance
Fund.
     Roger Powell, Emergency Manager, discussed WCNND membership, Trailblazer RC&D and Building &
Grounds updates.
     Tegtman discussed the Region 3 committee meeting he attended on August 22, 2025.
     McCoy discussed his conversation with 316 Asphalt Paving Company working in Edison on Friday and
asked for an estimate to seal Road 430 going south out of Edison. The estimate was received today for
$20,725.00. If work can be done while the equipment is still in Edison this week or next, it would be a
substantial savings. Motion by Tegtman, second by McCoy to approve the Estimate #210 from 316 Asphalt
Paving for work to be done with a deposit of $5,181.25.
     The Board reviewed Resolution 2025-8-26- Temporary Moratorium on Wind Energy Installations & Solar
Power Generation Systems. After review, Resolution 2025-8-26 was approved on a motion by Tegtman,
second by McCoy. RCV: Ayes: Sexton, Tegtman, McCoy. Motion carried.
     County Inventory Statements for the Offices and Shops were reviewed and approved on a motion by
McCoy, second by Tegtman. RCV: Ayes: Sexton, Tegtman, McCoy. Motion carried.
