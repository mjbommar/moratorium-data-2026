Commissioner Hain requested to place an emergency item on the agenda. The Sheriff

gave notice to the Commissioners on October 26th that he plans to retire November 5th

.

Hain contacted the County Attorney’s office regarding the process of filling the vacancy.

Due to the timing of his retirement, there will not be time for the Commissioners to

discuss the process of filling the vacancy in a public meeting prior to the Sheriff’s

retirement, as the November 3rd meeting is limited because of it being Election Day.

Hain noted the nature of the emergency is that once the position is vacated, there will

not be an acting Sheriff, and if there is not a plan in place for what will occur, the

county will not have an acting Sheriff. Ahmic questioned the nature of the emergency,

stating that the discussion needs to take place, but it needs to be done correctly. Hain

added the emergency item to the agenda.

Emergency Item Added – – Discussion Only – – Appointment Process for Seward County

Sheriff, Added at 10:02 a.m.

The Commissioners discussed the process of appointment for Sheriff. Armstead noted

that the board does not have to wait for the position to be vacant to appoint someone.

They can appoint an individual, with the appointment to begin upon the vacancy of the

Sheriff. A After a lengthy discussion about the various logistics of the situation, it was

decided to ask Roxanne Knutson, the Human Resources Director, to join the meeting to

offer input.

The Commissioners recessed at 10:18 a.m.

The Commissioners reconvened at 10:31 a.m.

Roxanne Knutson, Human Resources Director, was present for the discussion. Armstead

noted that, although the Sheriff stated his intention of making a member of his

Command Staff the acting Sheriff until the next Sheriff begins their term, it does not

apply because the Sheriff’s Office does not have a Chief Deputy. Under these particular

circumstances, the appointment can happen before the position is vacant. It was

decided to hold a special session on November 4th at 11:30 a.m. The preliminary results

of the election should be determined by that time, and the individual who wins the

election can be appointed to fill the vacancy beginning November 6thZabrocki will

.

contact Sheriff Vance, as well as the candidates running for Sheriff, to make sure all are

aware of the decision. The County Attorney’s Office will draft a contract for the

individual to fill the remainder of the Sheriff’s term.
