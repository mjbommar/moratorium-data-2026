A regular meeting of the Seward County Board of Commissioners was convened in open

and public session at 9:00 a.m. on November 3, 2026 in the Commissioner’s room at

the Seward County Courthouse. Notice of the meeting was posted on the Courthouse

bulletin board, on the Commissioner’s Room door, on the Seward County website:

sewardcountyne.gov and published in the Seward County Independent. The agenda for

all meetings is kept continually current and is available for public inspection at the

County Clerk’s Office during normal business hours. The agenda is held open until one

business day prior to the meeting for appearance before the Board. The Board of

Commissioners has the right to modify the agenda to include items of an emergency

nature only at such public meeting.

The Commissioners recessed at 9:02 a.m.

The Commissioners reconvened at 9:05 a.m.

Moved by Curtis and seconded by Pekarek to accept the retirement of Sheriff Mike

Vance.

Affirmative Vote: Curtis, Pekarek, Zabrocki, Hain, Ahmic Motion Carried

The Commissioners presented Sheriff Vance with a plaque in honor of his years of

service and his retirement.

Ahmic led discussion on identifying the appointee to fill the vacancy of the Sheriff.

There was discussion during the October 27th meeting regarding the appointment

process for the upcoming vacancy of the Sheriff’s position. The Commissioners

discussed appointing the winner of the November 3rd election to fill the vacancy for the

remainder of the term at that time, but no formal action was taken. Barb Armstead,

Deputy County Attorney, noted that there should be a contingency plan in case the

winner cannot be determined right away.

Moved by Curtis and seconded by Hain to appoint the winner of the November 3rd, 2026

Sheriff’s election, and if there is a contested action, Captain Lisa Borges, to be

appointed to fill the remainder of the term from November 6, 2026 to January 6, 2027.

Affirmative Vote: Curtis, Hain, Pekarek, Zabrocki, Ahmic Motion Carried
