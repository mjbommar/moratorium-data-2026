April 7, 2026 – 9:30 A.M.

A Regular Meeting of the Board of Commissioners of Kearney County, NE was held April 7, 2026 at 9:30

a.m. in the Board Room of the Courthouse in Minden, N NE. Present were Chairman Brent Stewart and Board

Members Heath HofstetterJames MillerRobert Swanson, Jason Sinsel, Jeff England and N Nathan AveryAlso

,

The Board of Commissioners took the following action:

-Approved minutes of the Board of Commissioners meeting of March 17, 2026

.

-Approved Bills and ClaimsMarch payroll and Hospital Taxes(See below)

, .

-Held a Public comment from 9:30 to 9:35 a.m.

-Approved the County Clerk and Clerk of the District Court Fee Reports for March 2026.

-Reviewed the Co. Sheriff quarterly report ending 3-31-2026.

-Approved Resolution for interfund transfer from wireless service fund to wireless holding fund

-Approved Moratorium on accepting or approving any data center, crypto mining, other large energy permits or

battery energy storage systems applications

-Approved Re-employment of former County Employee

-Approved mowing contract with Tracy Donley

-Met with Gavin Blum & Luke Poore – KC Health Service to discuss Hospital Facility master planning

-Met with Joe Anderson, Hwy Supt. to discuss amending the County’s driveway policy, the possible purchase of

additional equipment

th

Asphalt Overlay

-Met with Dave Arnold – – Royal Engineered Composites regarding No-Build Easement

-Approved Extension of Resolution for Solar Farm, Wind Turbine and Nuclear Energy Moratorium

-Met with Dustin Will and Corey Carpenter for a presentation on County Health Insurance

-Discussed tax foreclosure properties in the County, will be addressed at a future meeting

-Discussed a County Appointment Policy Revision, will be addressed at a future meeting

-Held a Closed Session from 11:17 a.m. to 12:58 p.m. for legal advice regarding a personnel matter to protect

the public’s interest.

-Adjourned the Board of Commissioners meeting at 12:59 p.m.
