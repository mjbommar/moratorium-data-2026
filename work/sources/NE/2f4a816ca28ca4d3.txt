                                     RESOLUTION 26-15

   WHEREAS, the County of Hitchcock County, Nebraska, hereinafter referred to as
“COUNTY”, is a duly organized county, existing pursuant to the law of the State of Nebraska;
and

   WHEREAS, Hitchcock County receives funds from the Nebraska Public Service
Commission (PSC) referred to as 911 PSC Funds which are maintained by COUNTY as a
separate fund identified as “911 PSC Fund #2913”; and

  WHEREAS, the Public Service Commission (PSC) has developed criteria for use and
expenditure of said PSC funds, one of which is dispatcher wages not to include benefits.

   WHEREAS, the Hitchcock County Sheriff has budgeted to expend $160,000.00 of PSC (non
legacy) funds for dispatcher wages during the fiscal year 2026/2027; and

   WHEREAS, Hitchcock County has paid dispatcher wages for the period of July 1, 2026
through August 12, 2026 from the General Fund (Line Item #0100-671-1-0400 “Regular Time
Salaries – Dispatchers”) in the total amount of $34,129.68 as follows:
    • Roma Sensel             $9,124.74
    • Shauna Shutz            $8,101.04
    • Mary Forbes             $7,467.04
    • Kayla Hodges            $1,123.43
    • Alyson Lunsford          $2,177.67
    • Carrie Sandmann         $6,135.76

   WHEREAS, it is necessary to transfer PSC (non legacy) funds from the 911 PSC Fund
#2913 to the County General Fund as reimbursement for the dispatcher wages paid during said
period; and

   WHEREAS, it is further necessary to make future transfers of PSC (non legacy) funds from
the 911 PSC Fund #2913 to the County General Fund for payment of dispatcher wages to paid
going forward for fiscal year 2026/2027 not to exceed a total of $160,000.00, the amount
budgeted for the 2026/2027 fiscal year.

   NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Hitchcock County Board of
Commissioners, Hitchcock County, Nebraska, that the County Treasurer is hereby directed to
transfer at this time, the sum of $34,129.68 from the 911 PSC Fund #2913 to the County General
Fund per the supporting schedule of transfers budgeted, found in the 2026/2027 COUNTY
budget for Sheriff Department dispatcher wages; and
   BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the County Treasurer is hereby authorized and directed
to make future transfers, not to exceed a total of $125,870.32, during the 2026/2026 fiscal year
on a pay period basis from the 911 PSC Fund #2913 to the County General Fund per the
supporting schedule of transfers budgeted, found in the 2026/2027 COUNTY budget for Sheriff
Department dispatcher wages; and

  BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the County Treasurer shall make said transfers on a bi-
monthly basis upon receipt of documentation from the Hitchcock County Clerk certifying payroll
amounts for dispatcher gross wages for said pay period


  PASSED AND APPROVED this 8th day of September, 2026.
