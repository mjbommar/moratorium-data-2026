MCPHERSON COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND BOARD OF EQUALIZATION PROCEEDINGS OCTOBER 14, 2026


The McPherson County Board of Commissioners met as per adjournment of September 16,

2026, for their regular monthly meeting. Notice of meeting having been published in the

Stapleton Enterprise, issue dated October 8, 2026. The meeting was called to order at 9:00

AM by Chairman Bryant, who noted the Open Meetings Law, which is posted on the North

wall of the meeting room. Members John Bryant and Brett Nason were present. Also present

were Highway Superintendent Tom Werblow, Road Department employee Ed Black,

Treasurer Kerri Kramer, County Attorney Whitney Schroeder, Sheriff Lawrence Stump and

County Clerk Kathy Hoberg. Bryant made a motion to excuse Jeff Arensdorf who was absent

due to illness. Nason seconded. Voting Aye: Bryant and Nason. Voting Nay: None. Nason

moved to approve the minutes of the September 16, 2026, Special Hearing to Set the Final

Tax Request, the Budget Hearing and Special Commissioner’s Meeting as presented, Bryant

seconded. Voting Aye: Nason and Bryant. Voting Nay: None.

The commissioners reviewed the Fund Balance Reports provided by the Treasurer.

Nason made a motion to approve the following claims as presented. Bryant seconded. Voting

Aye: Nason and Bryant, Voting Nay: None.

The commissioners had a discussion with the Sheriff about a new Law Enforcement Vehicle.

Nason made a motion to allow the Sheriff to open the bidding process. Letters will be sent out

by the Sheriff requesting informal bids to be sent to the Clerk’s office. Bids will be reviewed at

the November 10th commissioner’s meeting. Bryant seconded. Voting Aye: Nason and Bryant.

Voting Nay: None.

The board reviewed and signed the 3rd Quarter Fee Report from the Sheriff.

The Sheriff requested a letter to be sent by the Board and County Attorney to the owners of

abandoned property on 6th Street. The property needs waste items and dead trees removed.

Nason made a motion to approve and adopt RESOLUTION #2026-18 Setting 2026-2027

Subdivision Levies in McPherson County. Bryant seconded. Voting Aye: Nason and Bryant

Voting Nay: None.

County Roads were discussed with Ed Black. The department continues to haul rock, patch

asphalt, repair, and maintain roads.

Ed requested an additional camera to be placed at the Fair Building dumpster site. The current

camera does not cover the complete area.

Bryant made a motion to approve the request. Nason seconded. Voting Aye: Bryant and Nason.

Voting Nay: None.

Nason made a motion to approve and sign an agreement between McPherson County and Level

LLC for pulverizing and laying down asphalt and rock on Cemetery Road. Bryant seconded. Voting

Aye: Nason and Bryant. Voting Nay: None.

An Easement and repairs needed on Tupper Drive were discussed with the County Attorney.

Kenny Rundback and Ryan Schultis were present to listen and address the board concerning the

road. There will be correspondence sent from the County Attorney and the matter will be re

visited at the next board meeting.

Bryant made a motion to accept the October Road Plan as presented by Nason. Nason seconded.

Voting Aye: Bryant and Nason. V Voting Nay: None.

A Safety Committee Meeting was held. Those present were Brett Nason, John Bryant, Ed Black,

Whitney Schroeder and Kathy Hoberg. Tom Werblow gave flyers and a presentation about Driving

on Gravel Roads.

The board received and reviewed the Delinquent Tax Listing from the Treasurer.

The board reviewed and signed the 3rd Quarter Fee Report from the Clerk.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 10:18 AM. The Board will meet

again on November 10, 2026, at 1:00 PM. This meeting will be held in the courtroom of the

McPherson County Courthouse and will be open to the public.

The Courthouse will be closed on November 11, 2026, due to the Veteran’s Day Holiday.
