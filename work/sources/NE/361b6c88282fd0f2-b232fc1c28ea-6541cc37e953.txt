Assessor Elise Taylor spoke with the board about not legally being able to properly assess

properties due to the Attorney General opinion that the county assessor is not exempt from being

charged with trespassing. Taylor will work with Central Plains Valuation on a contract enabling

them to complete some valuations. The cost will be covered by funds already available in the

assessors office with the extra to be included in the 2026-2027 budget.

Taylor also told they board she had been working with County Attorney Steve Bowers on a

“consent to access” form. Return has been a struggle to get them back. The board directed

Taylor to present the contract at the next board meeting.

Motion by Govier 2nd by Markham to open Public Hearing at 10:00 a.m. approval of Poultry

Regulations.

Tibbs presented Resolution 76Z-26 which adopts amendments to the Custer County Zoning

Regulations Pertaining to Poultry, Game Birds, and Ratites updated Section 7.08. the updates

included new and revised definitions to better distinguish between types of birds and operational

practices, including dry and wet litter systems. Establish thresholds for small, medium and large

operations. Additional provisions including proper facility maintenance.

Overall, the changes are intended to provide clearer standards, protect public health and the

environment, and ensure responsible operation of poultry-related facilities within the County.
