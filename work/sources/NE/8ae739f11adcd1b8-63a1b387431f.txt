At 9:58 A.M. the public hearing for Special Use Permit for Concrete Batch Plant #1CB was opened by Chairman

Borrenpohl. Russ George with Ready Mix Concrete was present to discuss the proposed Plant and to answer

questions.

Chairman Borrenpohl went over the following requirements and that all were followed. Listed below are the

requirements.

1. Be compatible with and similar to the use permitted in the district. That is covered

2. Not be a matter which should require re-zoning of the property. No problem

3. Not be detrimental to adjacent property. No problem

4. Not tend to depreciate the value of the surrounding structures or property. No problems

5. Be compatible with the stated intended use of the district. No problems

6. Not change the character of the district. Falls in the character of the district.

7. Be in accordance with the Comprehensive Plan. Does not change

The special use permit meets all 7 requirements.

A motion was made by Weber and was seconded by Agena to approve Special Use Permit #1CB for a concrete

Batch Plant located E ½ NW ¼ less Highway Section 25 Township 5 Range 11. Yeas: Agena, Weber, Borrenpohl.

Nays; none motion carried.

Chairman Borrenpohl closed the public hearing at 10:14 A.M.
