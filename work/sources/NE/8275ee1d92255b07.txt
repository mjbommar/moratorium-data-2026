BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS MEETING                                                          August 24, 2026
        The Red Willow County Board of Commissioners met in the Commissioners’ Room in the
courthouse on August 24, 2026. A notice of the meetings was published in the McCook Gazette and posted
on the County’s website. The combined agenda was e-mailed to each commissioner and the news media,
uploaded on the County’s website, and kept current and available to the public at the County Clerk’s
Office.
        At 8:30 a.m. Chairman Ted Gans called the Board of Commissioners meeting to order with the
following members present: Commissioners Ted Gans, Randy Dean and Charles Fritsche. Gans informed
the public about the location of the posted Open Meetings Act.
        A motion was made by Fritsche, seconded by Dean, to approve the agenda as presented. Roll call:
Ayes: Gans, Dean, Fritsche.
        A motion was made by Dean, seconded by Fritsche, to amend the agenda to add “discuss the West
Central Nebraska Development District Contract”. Roll Call: Ayes: Gans, Dean, Fritsche.
        A motion was made by Dean, seconded by Fritsche, to add an “appointment to the Planning and
Zoning Commission” to the consent agenda. Roll Call: Ayes: Gans, Dean, Fritsche.
        Chairman Gans offered the floor for Citizens’ Comments. Leon Kolbet was present to advocate
for CO2 pipelines. Brent Niese from Carbon Solutions were present to answer questions about the proposed
CO2 pipeline.
        There were no issues for the Board of Equalization.
        A motion was made by Fritsche, seconded by Dean, to approve the following items on the consent
agenda: 1) minutes from the previous Board of Commissioners and Board of Equalization meetings, 2)
miscellaneous accounts payable and payroll claims in the amount of $689,272.10, 3) Inventories for All
Departments, 4) Resolutions No. 1603 through 1610, Book 24, Pages 109-116 Setting Levys for Local
Subdivisions, 5) Resolution No. 1611, Book 25, Page 1 – Adopt 12 Month Zoning Moratorium on Pipelines,
6) Resolution No. 1612, Book 25, Page 2 – Extend for Additional 12 Month Zoning Moratorium on Data
Centers, 7) Resolution No. 1613, Book 25, Page 3 – Recommit to NIRMA, 8) Appoint Rhea Hernandez as
Director of Red Willow Health Department, 9) Appoint Dean McCoy to the Planning and Zoning
Commission to replace Mike Daffer, Term to Expire April 30, 2027. Roll call: Ayes: Gans, Dean, Fritsche.
        Crystal Dack, Treasurer, joined the Board representing the Personnel Committee. There are
concerns regarding vacation and comp time that have been revealed by the new payroll system. A motion
was made by Dean, seconded by Fritsche, to table this issue indefinitely while they look into the matter.
Roll Call: Ayes: Gans, Dean, Fritsche.
        Dan Miller joined the Board to review the budget.
        The Board discussed the contract with West Central Nebraska Development District for the
County’s comprehensive plan. It was decided to continue with WCNDD.
        The following correspondence was reviewed and placed on file: 1) County Attorney Paul Wood –
Memorandum Regarding Public Defender Policy Board, 2) NDOT – Bridge Load Postings, 3) Nebraska
Department of Water – Modified Construction and Operating Permit for Kingdom Concentrated Animal
Operation.
        The Sheriff’s Monthly Revenue Report was reviewed and placed on file.
