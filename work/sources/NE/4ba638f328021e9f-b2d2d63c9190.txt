Sornson opened a public hearing regarding zoning regulations relating to data centers and /or the need to

issue a moratorium on applications for conditional use and/or building permits relating to data centers.

Speaking from the public were: Wynee Benedict, Mayrene Thummel, Jane Dose, Victoria Watkins, Joann

Martens, Jim Nemec, Ted Vasko, Jeff Parker, Ann Boellstorff, and Dale Wurtele. Concerns brought up were,

noise, pollution, water/power usage, saving ag land, etc. Following discussion, the public hearing was closed.

Sornson stated that the county zoning regulations were needing to be reviewed and updated. Crownover made

a motion, seconded by Sornson, to create a zoning review committee to review and update current regulations

and to prepare a report for the Commissioners. The committee would include: County representatives Jerad

Sornson, Chuck Cole, Dan Thomson, and Jenniffer Panko-Rahe and Planning Board representatives Dan

Hodges, Howard Bebout, and Cheri Wirthele. Voting aye were Crownover, Cole, Sornson, and Parsons. Nay –

none.

Cole made a motion, seconded by Crownover, to enter a moratorium on building and conditional use permits

regarding data centers for up to one yearThe zoning review committee will update the zoning regulations to

.

establish regulations, and the moratorium may be lifted earlier if regulations are updated and approved. Voting

aye were Cole, Sornson, Parsons, and Crownover. Nay – none. Motion carried.

Cole made a motion, seconded by Parsons, to adopt Resolution 5-19-26-4 to decrease the Sheriff’s petty cash

amount. The resolution authorizes the petty cash amount to be decreased from $1000.00 to $500.00. Voting

aye were Sornson, Parsons, Crownover, and Cole. Nay – none. Motion carried.

There was discussion of the Nemaha NRD Hazard Mitigation Plan. Parsons made a motion, seconded by

Crownover, to adopt Resolution 5-19-26-5 approving the Nemaha NRD Hazard Mitigation Plan. Voting aye

were Crownover, Cole, Sornson, and Parsons. Nay – none. Motion carried.

Parsons made a motion, seconded by Cole, to authorize the County Clerk to sign and submit the Blue Cross

and Blue Shield Subgroup application. The application is for the 2026-2027 plan year. Voting aye were

Parsons, Crownover, Cole, and Sornson. Nay – none. Motion carried.

Cole made a motion, seconded by Parsons, to authorize the use of County Keno funds to be used to hold a

Household Hazardous Waste Cleanup event. One was provided by Keep Nebraska City Beautiful on May 16,

2026. The event was to last 3 hours and the allotted truck space was filled within an hour. The board

recognizes the need for another event and has asked Gregg Goebel, EMA Director, to find a vendor and set up

another event. Voting aye were Cole, Sornson, Parsons, and Crownover. Nay – none. Motion carried.

Brian Briley, Highway Supervisor, presented a road report.

There was no public comment.

There being no further business to come before the Board at this time, and since all items on the agenda have

been disposed of Chairman Sornson declared this meeting adjourned at 9:52 a.m. The next regular meeting
