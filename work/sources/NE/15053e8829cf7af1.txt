No monies were transferred from the Inheritance Tax account.

Caitlyn Whitehead of Prairie Plains CASA presented an annual update and requested
continued funding for the program. This program enlists volunteers to support children in
abuse/neglect cases and impacts multiple counties in its coverage region.

Kaye T Bieck with the Family Resource Center presented the center’s annual report and
requested continuing funding for the program. The Family Resource Center empowers
children/families and connects communities with support and access to essential
resources.
Julie Jones and Peggy Parker addressed the commissioners regarding replacing the heat
pump at the Dundy County Library. They received a bid from Schorzman Heating and Air
Inc. in the amount of $13,499.00. There were many issues last summer including have to
close the library due to the heat. The current system is requiring several more repairs to
keep the library and its technology going this summer. It was suggested to try grants and
fundraisers as well. Ms. Jones and Ms. Parker also shared a calendar of events the Dundy
County Library has been hosting over the past year.

Joann Decker was in to update the commissioners on the status of the Benkelman
Cemetery maintenance. There has been an individual that has stepped up to finish out the
mowing season. This leaves preparing graves for burials as an unknown. If this has to be
contracted out the cemetery will lose money on burials. Ms. Decker was asked if local
youth groups could assist with the upkeep of the cemetery. She stated that workman’s
compensation and insurance present issues with that option. The cemetery board will keep
the commissioners up to date with any changes.

Continued from the 6/1/26 meeting, the commissioners considered extending the
Conditional Use Moratorium for another 12 months. Commissioner Keiser made a motion
to adopt Resolution 76-26-9 extending the Conditional Use Moratorium, seconded by
Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Unanimous.

The next item on the agenda was to consider the engagement letter from Gabriel Burger
Else CPA, PC, as the current auditor for Dundy County. Treasurer Penny Denny stated that
the company has been good to work with. Christina Collier shared that the company has
been helpful with the delinquent reconciling of the Imprest account from December 2022
forward. Commissioner Keiser made a motion to approve the signing of the engagement
letter from GBE, seconded by Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Unanimous.

Another engagement letter was up for consideration for the budget preparer, Daniel D.
Miller, CPA, PC. Clerk Mary Seybold sent an email requesting to wait to approve the letter
for a bid from another individual. With time constraints as it is budget prep time, a motion
was made by Commissioner Richard to sign the engagement letter from Mr. Miller,
seconded by Commissioner Keiser. Ayes: Unanimous.

Attorney Arlan Wine addressed the commissioners regarding coroner procedures in
unattended deaths. Mr. Wine wanted the commissioners to know that the County may have
to cover costs for transport and/or autopsies. However, every effort would be made to make
sure the County is repaid for such.

Mr. Wine also presented an interlocal agreement pertaining to the compensation for the
court bailiff that is shared by nine counties. He presented an email from Judge Patrick Heng
requesting an increase in wages and a schedule of such going forward. Commissioner
Keiser made a motion to accept the request as presented, seconded by Commissioner
Richard. Ayes: Unanimous.
The commissioners continued the ongoing discussion of making a change to road
maintenance routes. Commissioner Keiser made a motion to approve the changes as
suggested by then Roads Supervisor Kurt Sellers, seconded by Chairman Tecker. Ayes:
Unanimous.

The discussion of a supervisor in the Haigler roads shop was shortly reviewed. It was stated
that there simply is not a supervisor for that shop at this time.

On Wednesday, June 10, 2026, the water heater in the annex building had a major
malfunction which resulted in extensive water damage. There was an update and brief
discussion of the situation. Ag Extension Wendi Vanderford will be working remotely until
further notice while the County Attorney’s office will continue to work in the annex building.

Commissioner touched on the employee PTO policy to make sure all department heads
were on the same page. All hourly employees are to use PTO to fill in time gaps for their
required full-time hours. Department heads were also asked to keep a running tally of their
employees’ PTO hours to be compared with the hours calculated by the clerk’s office.

The roads department needs additional stock of road grader blades. Commissioner Keiser
presented bids from B’s Enterprises Inc., CAT, and John Deere. He stated that Chase
County Roads is willing to share a load of blades to keep costs down for both Dundy and
Chase counties. Ultimately, Commissioner Keiser made a motion to accept the bid from
John Deere due to cost and time of receipt of goods, seconded by Chairman Tecker. Ayes:
Unanimous.

Attorney Arlan Wine presented an item from his office that had not previously been covered
in this meeting. The Region 51 Emergency Management agreement between the County,
City of Benkelman, and Village of Haigler has been signed by each entity and is in full
effect.

Sheriff Bang shared there are currently two inmates in custody, one in Red Willow County
and one in Lincoln County. He stated there will be an open house for the public to see the
newly updated communication center and jail on Saturday, June 20, 2026, from 9:00 am to
12:00 pm. The sheriff stated there will be a “Phase 2” that will cost approximately $14,000
to $15,000 max as was previously discussed. He said it looks like the summer will be a
busy one for him and his deputies. Chairman Tecker asked about the traffic on West
Highway 34. The sheriff said that his office had a Selective Traffic Operation which resulted
in over 20 violations. He also stated that his department will probably begin doing a couple
Selective Traffic Operations each year going forward.

Commissioner Keiser went over the departments and funding lines that are over budget for
the fiscal year. There will be a special meeting on June 22, 2026, at 8:00 am to discuss
budget authority transfers and create resolutions for corrections.
Executive Session: None

With no further business, there was a motion made by Commissioner Keiser to adjourn the
meeting at 10:35 am, seconded by Chairman Tecker. Ayes: Unanimous. The next meeting
is scheduled for July 6, 2026, at 8:30 A.M. in the Commissioners Room.
