Sheriff Vance was present to request the reallocation of Sheriff Treasury Fund

Expenditures for July 2025 Payroll to General Fund. In July 2025, payroll for some

Sheriff’s Office employees was paid using Treasury Funds. After that payroll period, all

of the Sheriff’s Office payroll has been paid out of the General Fund. After a brief

discussion, A Armstead advised against reallocating the funds to come out of the General

Fund rather than the Treasury Fund at this time, due to there being an open inquiry

from the Treasury Department in order for them to complete their compliance review.

She noted that, while the county will likely have to pay the funds back, from a legal

standpoint it is best to let the inquiry play out and wait for the conclusions to come

back and address this at that time, rather than addressing it retroactively.

Moved by Pekarek and seconded by Zabrocki to deny the reallocation of Sheriff

Treasury Fund Expenditures for July 2025 Payroll to the General Fund.

Affirmative Vote: Pekarek, Zabrocki, Ahmic Motion Carried

Ahmic led discussion on the Moratorium on Data Centers in Seward County. Because

the current Zoning regulations in the county do not contain regulations governing data

centers, the Board asked the Deputy County Attorney to draft a temporary moratorium

to allow them time to research and amend the regulations. Ahmic read the resolution

and then invited members from the public who were in attendance to speak either for

or against the moratorium. No one from the public spoke in favor or opposition of the

moratorium.

Moved by Zabrocki and seconded by Pekarek to adopt Resolution No. 3937 which

creates a moratorium on Data Centers in Seward County for a period of 12 months, or

as such time as Zoning Regulations are adopted.

Resolution # 3937 of the Seward County Board of Commissioners


WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners of Seward County, Nebraska

(“Board”), recognizes recent increased interest in and growth of major

data centers across the United States;

WHEREAS, the Board recognizes the advantage of responsibly

encouraging business development to meet the changing needs of

consumers;

WHEREAS, data centers may have significant impacts on the natural

resources, infrastructure, utilities, and the health and well-being of

residents in areas in which they exist;

WHEREAS, the current zoning regulations in Seward County do not

contain regulations governing data centers of any size or scope;

WHEREAS, the authority to make, adopt, amend, extend, and implement

county zoning regulations is conferred upon the County Board of

Commissioners in each county pursuant to Neb. Rev. Stat. §23-114;

WHEREAS, as used herein, “data center” shall refer to any facility that

receives the majority of its income from the compiling, storage, and

maintenance of documents, records, and other information in digital form

utilizing mainframe computers, servers, storage devices, cloud storage, or

any similar medium. This term does not include general business offices,

computer-related sales establishments, or business and personal services;

WHEREAS, it is imperative to ensure responsible and sustainable

development of data centers to align with the existing community,

infrastructure, and comprehensive plan of Seward County;

WHEREAS, regulation of data center development must be designed to

minimize any negative impact on county resources and residents, while

staying consistent with the Seward County Comprehensive Plan;

WHEREAS, a temporary moratorium is deemed necessary to ensure that

regulations can be enacted to regulate the establishment of data centers

before development occurs without regulations in place that address

potential impacts on resources, infrastructure, and the community;

WHEREAS, during the life of the moratorium, the Board will research how

best to avoid potential harms associated with data center development,

including inviting public input, reviewing approaches adopted by similarly

situated jurisdictions, researching applicable industry performance

standards, and ultimately amending the Seward County Zoning

Regulations as appropriate;

WHEREAS, failure to enact a temporary moratorium on data center

development until such time as research can be conducted and

regulations drafted that will mitigate potential harm, while still

encouraging business development in Seward County, could result in

irreparable harm to Seward County and its resources;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED a moratorium on data center

development as defined herein shall be imposed, effective immediately,

upon the following in Seward County:

X_ Commencement of construction of data centers

_

_X_ Issuance of building permits to construct data centers

_X_ Issuance of conditional use permits for data center development

_X_ Any and all authorization of projects intended to include construction

of data centers

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the temporary moratorium shall remain in

effect until the sooner of 12 months or upon new zoning regulations

regarding data centers being formally adopted, published, and becoming

legally binding within Seward County, at which time this moratorium

automatically expires unless extended by separate resolution.

Dated this 30th day of June 2026.
