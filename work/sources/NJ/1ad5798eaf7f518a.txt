ORDINANCE NO. 10-2026

ORDINANCE OF THE TOWNSHIP OF MULLICA,
COUNTY OF ATLANTIC, STATE OF NEW JERSEY,
AMENDING AND SUPPLEMENTING CHAPTER 63
SECTION 10 ENTITLED SPEED LIMITS
WHEREAS, the Township Committee of the Township of Mullica obtained a speed
study for Railroad Avenue, attached hereto as Schedule A; and
WHEREAS, in consultation with the Chief of Police, Township Engineer, and in
consideration of the report, the Township Committee finds it necessary and proper to establish a
speed limit on Railroad Avenue;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Township Committee of the
Township of Mullica, County of Atlantic, State of New Jersey as follows:
SECTION 1. Section 63-10 entitled, entitled, “Transfer Station Regulations” of the
Township of Mullica is hereby supplemented and amended to state as follows:
A. Railroad Avenue; Speed Limit 35 MPH; Location: Entire Length.
SECTION 2. All remaining provisions of Section 63-10 shall remain the same.
SECTION 3. The Township Clerk is directed to give notice at least ten (10) days prior
to the hearing on the adoption of the Ordinance to the Atlantic County Planning Board and to all
other persons or entities entitled thereto pursuant to N.J.S.A. 40:55D-15 including the Clerk of
adjoining municipalities.
SECTION 4. All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances inconsistent herewith are hereby

repealed to the extent of such inconsistencies.

SECTION 5. Severability.

RESOLUTION NO. 176-2026

APPOINTING A NEW GOVERNOR'S COUNCIL ON SUBSTANCE USE DISORDER
(GCSUD) MUNICIPAL ALLIANCE COORDINATOR AND CHAIRPERSON

WHEREAS, vacancies currently exist for both the positions of Municipal Alliance
Coordinator and Municipal Alliance Chairperson for the Township of Mullica due to the recent
resignations of the previous individuals holding said positions; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes the importance of maintaining continuity in
the operations, programs, and community outreach efforts of the Municipal Alliance; and

WHEREAS, the Township Committee of the Township of Mullica recommends the
appointment of qualified individuals to fill these respective vacancies for terms expiring June 30,
2028.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Township Committee of the
Township of Mullica, County of Atlantic, State of New Jersey, as follows:

1. Appointment of Coordinator: Tiffany Stanley is hereby appointed as the Municipal
Alliance Coordinator for the Township of Mullica effective immediately to fill the term
expiring on June 30, 2028

2. Appointment of Chairperson: Monica Newton is hereby appointed as the Chairperson of
the Municipal Alliance for the Township of Mullica, effective immediately to fill the
term expiring on June 30, 2028.

3. Notification: A certified copy of this Resolution shall be forwarded to the Atlantic
County Municipal Alliance Coordinator, the Chief Financial Officer, and the newly
appointed Coordinator and Chairperson.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this Resolution shall take effect immediately.
