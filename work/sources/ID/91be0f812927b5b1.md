# Data Center Ordinance | Bannock County

source: https://www.bannockcounty.gov/datacenter/
fetched: 2026-09-23T12:43:23+00:00

As data center companies look to Idaho as a potential home for data center projects, Bannock County wants to ensure the interests of our community are protected. In order to allow time to create an ordinance that would protect natural resources, energy infrastructure, utility ratepayers, and community character, the Board of Commissioners [enacted](https://www.bannockcounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Data-Center-Moratorium_Final.pdf) a 180-day moratorium on any permits for data center projects on July 30, 2026.

Until the moratorium ends on January 26, 2027, applications for any data center projects within county jurisdiction will not be accepted by Bannock County. During this time, county planners will draft an ordinance that will be available for public comment during the public hearing process.

**Jurisdictional Boundaries**

This moratorium legally applies to **Unincorporated Bannock County**, which is land within unincorporated county limits. It DOES NOT apply to municipalities within Banncok County, (such as the City of Pocatello) and does not impact city proceedings for any proposed data centers within city limits.

**Regulatory Timeline & Public Participation**

County planners are currently finalizing a separate, years-long rewrite of the Land Use and Development Ordinance (LUDO). Because data centers were not included in the broader LUDO scope, county staff chose to address data center regulations separately during this 180-day pause rather than delay LUDO’s upcoming public hearings.

Once LUDO public hearings conclude, staff will focus fully on crafting the dedicated data center ordinance. Public input is a vital part of this process, and residents will have formal opportunities to provide feedback before any new regulations are adopted.

To keep residents directly informed, Bannock County has launched this dedicated webpage, **bannockcounty.gov/datacenter**. Residents can visit this site to track timeline updates, review draft materials, and subscribe to receive direct email notifications whenever public input opportunities or new project details are announced.
