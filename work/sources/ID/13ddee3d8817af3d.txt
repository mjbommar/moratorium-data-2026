COMMISSIONERS MINUTES                                                                  JULY 2, 2026

The Elmore County Commissioners met in regular session on the above date in the Commissioners
Room, basement of the Elmore County Courthouse, 150 South 4th East, Mountain Home, Idaho.

Present at the meeting were Chairman Al Hofer, Commissioners Bud Corbus and Crystal Rodgers,
Prosecutor Shondi Lott, Shelley Essl, Clerk Julie Kennedy and Deputy Clerk Francene Payne.


Julie Kennedy Clerk Elect and write in candidate Sonya Yelton appeared in front of the Board to present
their desire to become the next clerk. The Board asked both candidates questions before deciding on who
the best fit for the new Elmore County Clerk would be.

Motion by Hofer, second by Corbus, to approve Julie Kennedy as the new Elmore County Clerk and was
sworn in by the Board of Elmore County Commissioners.

PUBLIC HEARING continued: Abbey Germaine outside council, Mitra Mehta-Cooper principal
planner Dan Pauly Planner and James Roddin Land Use presented Development Agreement DA-2026-03
Idaho Power Company for Mayfield Substation and Transmission Lines. The Public Hearing was opened
at 1:31pm. The public was able to speak to the Board as their name was called from the sign-in list. No
members of the public were in favor; no members were neutral and no members of opposition were in
attendance. Jeff Maffucio, Paris Dickerson and Josh Leonard council representing Idaho Power Company
were given the opportunity to present its appeal and provide any information it wishes the Board to
consider. Public Hearing was closed at 2:28pm.

Motion by Corbus, second by Hofer, to approve the Development Agreement DA-2026-03 Idaho Power
