Meeting Schedule

The Plain Township Board of Trustees meets on the second and fourth Tuesday of each month at 6:00 p.m. Work sessions may be scheduled prior to each meeting at 5:20 p.m. The Plain Township Board of Trustees are permitted to call Special Meetings at any time for purposes they are permitted by law to act upon. The press must be notified of a Special Meeting 24 hours in advance of the scheduled meeting. The public notice must state the date, time, locations, and purpose of the meeting.

All meetings are public and held at the Plain Township Hall located at 2600 Easton Street NE.
