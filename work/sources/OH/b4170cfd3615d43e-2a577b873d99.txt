AW ORDINANCE BY THE VILAGE OF WILLIAMSBURG
ORDINANCE:1249 -26
AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A MORATORIUM ON THE AUTHORIZATION OR

ESTABLISHMENT OF COMPUTER DATA CENTERS WITHIN THE VILLAGE OF WILLIAMSBURG, OHIO
AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

WHEREAS, the Village of Williamsburg desires to impose a moratorium on the construction of data centers
within the Village of Williamsburg for the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the Village of
Williamsburg; and

WHEREAS, the definition of computer data center for the purpose of this Ordinance shall be defined as a
facility, or portion of a facility, primarily designed and used to house computer systems, servers, data
storage devices, and related telecommunications and networking equipment for the purpose of storing,
processing, managing, or transmitting electronic data. A computer data center typically includes
supporting infrastructure such as electrical power systems, backup generators, cooling and ventilation
equipment, fire suppression systems, security measures, and network connectivity, and is intended for
continuous operation; and

WHEREAS, the Council for the Village of Williamsburg desires time to study the economic and
environmental impacts on the Village and its residents, or, alternatively, to develop and implement
regulations regarding the possible location and operation of data center facilities within the Village of
Williamsburg.

NOW, THERFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the Village of Williamsburg, Clermont County, Ohio,
at least a majority of its members concurring:

SECTION 1. The Council for the Village of Williamsburg, Clermont County, Ohio, hereby establishes a
moratorium with respect to the authorization or establishment of any computer data center, as that term
is defined above, within the Village of Williamsburg for one (1) year from the date this Ordinance takes
effect.

SECTION 2. This moratorium applies to all pending applications for which a final, non-appealable permit
has not been issued as of the date of introduction of this Ordinance.

SECTION 3. This Ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health, safety, and welfare of the Village of Williamsburg and its
residents.

SECTION 4. That it is hereby found and determined that all formal actions of this Village Council
concerning and relating to the passage of this Ordinance were taken in an open meeting, and that all
deliberations of this Village Council and any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were
taken in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including §121.22 of the
Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 5. That this Ordinance shall be effective at the earliest date allowed by law.
