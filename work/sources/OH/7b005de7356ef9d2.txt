                        Village of Middlefield Council Minutes


                                     June 11, 2026


Council met in regular session Mayor Garlich called the meeting to order by roll call at
7:00 p.m. with the following members present: Mayor Garlich, Mr. Scott Klein, Mr. Carl
Hornung, Ms. Kaitlyn Arganti, Mr. Sam Morrow, Mr. Bill Blue, and Mr. Kyle Kaplan.

Also present: Ms. Cindy Naftzger- Administrative Assistant, Chief Tucholski, Mr. Nick
Giardina- Fiscal Officer, Mr. Luke McConville- Village Solicitor, Mr. Alex Duncan, Ms.
Kim Breyley, Mr. John Boksansky- Zoning Inspector, Ms. Ann Wishart-Maple Leaf, River
Kale, District Outreach Representative Congressman David P. Joyce Office, and Mr.
Ben Reed- Fire Department. Ms. Leslie McCoy- Village Administrator and Lt. Graley are
excused.

1. Opening Ceremony: Pledge of Allegiance was recited.

2. Agenda: No additions/deletions.

3. Approval of Minutes: May 14, 2026. Mr. Hornung made a motion to approve May
14, 2026 minutes, motion seconded by Ms. Arganti. Roll Call. All in favor: Yeas (6).

4. Payment of Bills: Mr. Blue moved to authorize payments totaling $988,892.22,
motion seconded by Mr. Klein. Roll Call. All in favor: Yeas (6).

5. FISCAL OFFICER’S REPORT: Income Tax and Financials – The report is included
in the packet. Mr. Giardina stated income tax, sewer, and water are up about 14%
compared to the previous year. Mr. Giardina stated income tax withholdings are strong.
Expenses are up due to large projects happening in the Village but there are some loan
disbursements coming in that will offset those expenses. Mr. Giardina stated individual
deposits are strong which shows that employment in the Village is strong. Income tax
seems to be up 20% year after year. Mr. Giardina stated Village finances are doing very
well.

6. AMBULANCE REPORT – Report is included in the packet.

7. FIRE DEPARTMENT REPORT – Report is included in the packet. Mr. Reed stated
there was mutual aid training in May that included live burn training with Windsor Fire.
Mr. Reed stated the Fire Department participated in the Cardinal Elementary Field Day
and will be at the Cheese Festival with the Fire Safety Trailer.

8. ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT: Ms. McCoy is excused but her report is included in the
Council packet.

9. ZONING REPORT: Report is included in packet. Mr. Boksansky stated he was able to
issue 9 zoning permits for the month of May .

10. STREETS, SIDEWALKS & UTILITIES REPORT – Report is included in the packet.
Chief Tucholski stated there has been a lot of grass mowing, park maintenance,

PUBLIC PARTICIPATION

   1. River Kale, District Outreach Representative Congressman David P. Joyce
      (OH-14) Office: River Kale is present to speak about what he does. Mr. Kale
      stated he serves as the District Outreach Representative for David P. Joyce, who
      represents Ohio's 14th Congressional District in the U.S. House of
      Representatives and joined Congressman Joyce's office in 2025. Mr. Kale serves
      as the Congressman's liaison to local governments, businesses, nonprofit
      organizations, schools, and community organizations throughout the district. Mr.
      Kale would be the one to contact about federal grant opportunities, requests for
      meetings with Congressman Joyce, community project support, assistance
      navigating federal agencies, and invitations for the Congressman to attend local
      events.
