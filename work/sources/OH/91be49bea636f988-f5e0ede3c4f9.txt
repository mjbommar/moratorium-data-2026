AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A MORATORIUM, NOT TO
EXCEED ONE YEAR, ON THE ACCEPTANCE AND
PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR ZONING, BUILDING,
OR OCCUPANCY PERMITS FOR DATA CENTERS WITHIN THE
CORPORATE LIMITS OF THE CITY OF CORTLAND AND
DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

WHEREAS, the City of Cortland is a charter municipality and authorized to enact zoning regulations
to protect the public health, safety, and welfare of its residents; and

WHEREAS, industrial-scale data centers may significantly impact utility infrastructure and conflict
with land-use goals, necessitating a review of current zoning regulations; and

WHEREAS, Council finds it necessary to temporarily halt any such data center developments in order
to study appropriate zoning regulations and determine whether the City has the necessary processes,
resources, regulations, and infrastructure to address the growth and demands of data centers.

NOW, THEREFORE:
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF CORTLAND, OHIO:

Section 1, That a twelve (12) month moratorium is hereby established within the City of Cortland
tegarding the acceptance, processing, or approval of applications for the establishment of Data Centers
within the City.

Section 2. "Data Center" is defined as a facility used for storage, management, and dissemination of
digital data, which houses computer, telecommunications, and/or network equipment, systems, servers,
appliances, and other associated components related to digital data operations, and/or a facility designed
or modified to house networked computers and data and transaction processing equipment and related
infrastructure support equipment, including but not limited to power and cooling equipment to provide
data and transaction processing services, outsource information technology services, and computer
equipment colocation services, as weil as server farms, cloud computing hubs, colocation centers, and
facilities used for cryptocurrency mining, blockchain processing, and high-density computing.

Section 3. That the Council of the City of Cortland intends this moratorium fo be temporary and during
the next twelve months Council recommends to the Planning, Zoning, & Building Commission that it
study the impact of data centers on Cortland’s infrastructure and review Part 11 (‘Planning and Zoning
Code”) of the Cortland Codified Ordinances regarding utility and land use impacts and propose updates
to the zoning regulations consistent with the best interests of the residents of the City of Cortland.

Section 4, This Ordinance having the procedural rules suspended shall take effect immediately as being
in the best interest of the public health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the City of Cortland, Ohio.

PASSED IN COUNCIL THIS 1 DAY OF JUNE, 202f.
