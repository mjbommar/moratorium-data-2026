# Township Info

source: https://www.jacksontwpclermontoh.gov/township-info.html
fetched: 2026-09-23T12:01:04+00:00

Jackson Township, located in Clermont County, Ohio, was formed in June of 1834 from parts of Stonelick, Wayne and Williamsburg townships and named in honor of then current president of the United States Andrew Jackson. Today, Jackson Township is a predominately agricultural and rural community with a population of around 3100 residents, with a land area of 31.2 square miles.

Jackson Township, Clermont County is one of 37 Jackson Townships in the State of Ohio

## **Government**

![Picture](https://www.jacksontwpclermontoh.gov/uploads/1/0/6/6/10667116/published/pxl-20251202-001506612.jpg?1776295837)

The Jackson Township Board of Trustees Regular Business Meetings are held each month on the First Monday at 7:00 P.M.
\* If the scheduled meeting date is on a Holiday the meeting will be moved to the next day (Tuesday) at 7:00 P.M.
The Jackson Township Board of Trustees will hold a Work Session Meeting on the Third Monday of each month at 7:00 P.M.
\*All meetings of the Board of Trustees are held at: The Jackson Township Community Center located at 3263 U.S. Route 50, Williamsburg, OH 45176 ~ unless otherwise posted.

Any **cancellations,** ***special*** or ***emergency meetings*** will be posted on the [Township Calendar](https://www.jacksontwpclermontoh.gov/township-calendar.html)
​and on the bulletin board located adjacent to door #3 of the Community Center.

All meetings are open to the public in accordance with the Ohio Open Meetings Act.
