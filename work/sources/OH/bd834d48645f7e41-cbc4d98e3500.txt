o Timeline: If approved, contract negotiations occur in November, pre-

THE NECESSARY APPURTENANCES THERETO; AND PROVIDING FOR
IMMEDIATE EFFECT.

o The ordinance provides for the issuance and sale of bonds in a
maximum principal amount of $2,000,000 to pay the local shar of
costs for improvements to Howe Road and other streets. The work
involves construction, reconstruction, improving, grading, draining,
paving, curbing, sidewalks, bike lanes, turn lanes, traffic control
devices/signals, and acquiring necessary real estate and marked
improvements.

o The city received $1,250,000 in Ohio Public Works Commission
(OPWC) funding, which includes an interest-free OPWC loan.
Initially, the city considered paying the local share in cash or using a
$1,000,000 community project funding award from Representative
Sykes’s office secured through HUD. However, after discussions
with HUD and Representative Sykes’s office, it was determined that
the federal funding timeline will not align with the mandatory OPWC
project timeline because feral funds cannot be spent in advance of
appropriations.

o Ms. Gilbride recommended rolling the city’s cash balance by
$2,000,000 portion into this bond issue rather than depleting the
city’s cash balances by $2,000,000, noting that it is a more financially
prudent approach given recent wage increases and project timelines.
She confirmed that this bond issue will not affect the city’s bond
rating or debt limit because the city maintains a very low debt
threshold for its size.

o Council members noted that the Howe Road project was delayed in
part due to county delays regarding force main replacements.
Moving forward with the bond use allows the city to keep the project
on track.
