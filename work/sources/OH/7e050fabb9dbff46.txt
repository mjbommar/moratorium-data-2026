ORDINANCE NO, 26-118 INTRODUCED BY: Green, Coleman, Gorjanc
Mayor Marnecheck

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE ACCEPTANCE AND
PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR ZONING, OCCUPANCY, CONDITIONAL USE AND/OR
BUILDING PERMIT APPROVALS FOR NEW DATA CENTERS IN ALL ZONING DISTRICTS IN THE
CITY OF NORTH ROYALTON FOR A PERIOD OF ONE (1) YEAR TO ALLOW FOR STUDY AND
DEVELOPMENT OF APPROPRIATE REGULATIONS, AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

WHEREAS: In accordance with the Constitution of the State of Ohio and the pertinent provisions of the
Ohio Revised Code, the City of North Royalton has the power to enact planning and zoning
laws that are intended to protect the health, welfare and safety of the citizens of the City; and

WHEREAS: The City is authorized by Article 18, Section 3 of the Ohio Constitution to regulate land use in
furtherance of the public health, safety, and welfare, including by establishing polices that
control certain land uses in furtherance of sound development strategies; and

WHEREAS: The City of North Royalton has recently been made aware of data centers and similar high-
intensity computing facilities and recognizes that such uses are increasing in prevalence
regionally and nationally; and

WHEREAS: _ City Council finds the need for a comprehensive and deliberate evaluation of data centers and
similar uses before permitting such development within the City; and

WHEREAS: City Council further finds that the City’s current zoning code does not specifically address
data centers or establish performance standards tailored to their unique characteristics,
including but not limited to high electrical demand, continuous operations, and specialized
cooling and mechanical systems; and

WHEREAS: City Council recognizes that potential uses within the City, including but not limited to high-
intensity computing operations such as cryptocurrency mining, may share some operational
characteristics with data centers, and that it is necessary to evaluate how such uses should be
defined, classified, and regulated in a consistent and rational manner; and

WHEREAS: City Council finds that immediate and unregulated establishment of such uses prior to
completion of a review could result in development patterns or infrastructure demand that are
inconsistent with the City’s long-term planning objectives; and

WHEREAS: City Council desires to enact a moratorium on accepting for filing and consideration and/or
issuing zoning and/or occupancy permits for any building, structure, use or change of use that
involves Data Centers as a principal use in the City for a period of one (1) year; and

WHEREAS: City Council further finds that establishing a defined period for study and review will allow
the City to adopt clear, consistent, and legally defensible regulations governing data centers
and similar uses.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF NORTH ROYALTON,
COUNTY OF CUYAHOGA AND STATE OF OHIO, THAT:

Section 1. The Council of the City of North Royalton hereby imposes a moratorium for a period of one (1)
year from the effective date of this Ordinance, the city shall not accept or process any applications for zoning,
occupancy, conditional use and/or building permit approvals for Data Centers in any Zoning District in the
city, and further shall not issue any such approvals during said period.

Section 2. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the
adoption of this Ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council and that all deliberations of this
Council and any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public in
compliance with all legal requirements.

Section 3. This Ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure immediately necessary for the
preservation of the public peace, health, safety and welfare of the city, and for the further reason that it is
immediately necessary to permit the City Administration, Council and Planning Commission necessary time to
undertake a review of the North Royalton Zoning Code and possible amendment thereof relative to the
location and approval process for Data Centers.
