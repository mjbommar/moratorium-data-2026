   •   Property Tax Elimination Proposal (State of Ohio)
          • Fiscal Officer attended a county meeting regarding a state initiative
             to eliminate property taxes in Ohio.

          Key points:
                • A poll suggests the measure could pass with around 67% voter
                    support.
                • Townships derive about two-thirds of their funding from property
                    tax; elimination would be devastating.

          •   Proposals to offset lost revenue:
                    • Raising sales tax (potentially up to a cap of around 14%).
                    • Raising state income tax from ~2.5% to possibly 12–14%.

          •   Key Concerns Regarding Township Services:
                 • Safety Services: Fire, EMS which rely heavily on local levies,
                    could face funding depletion and operational shut down.
                 • Infrastructure: Road and bridge maintenance, along with cemetery
                    upkeep, would likely be neglected due to lack of funds.
                 • Operating Budget: General funds for daily township operations and
                    personnel would vanish.
                 • Proponents have reportedly collected more than enough signatures
                    to place it on the ballot. Signatures are currently being validated,
                    and signatures can be carried forward to future elections if needed.
                 • Earliest expected effective date, if passed: January 1, 2027
                  •   It is expected that rural townships may vote against the measure,
                      but 65% of Ohio’s population lives in cities and villages and may
                      vote in favor, as they rely less directly on property tax for local
                      services.

NEW BUSINESS:

Purse Bingo Fundraiser

   •   The recent Purse Bingo fundraiser held at the fire station was very successful,
       with all tickets sold out. Volunteers from Richfield and Amboy Township assisted
       with the event. Alcohol was served and no issues were reported. Overall, the
       event went very well, and suggestions were noted for improving future events.

It was also noted that an insurance rider with a multi-million-dollar coverage limit was
obtained for the event.

budget, and no action was taken at this time.
