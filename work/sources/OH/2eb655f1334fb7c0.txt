MOTION #2026-177

Sue Fields moved to accept the following resolution:

        WHEREAS, Data Centers have become a controversial topic in Ohio and the effects on
local political subdivisions remain to be seen. Studies must be done on the impact of the health,
safety, utilities, and infrastructure of our community; and

       WHEREAS, the studies being conducted to provide more comprehensive information on
this matter require sufficient time to be completed; and
        NOW THEREFORE BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED by Brimfield Township Board of
Trustees, Portage County, Ohio to place a six (6) month moratorium on the operation/location of
any new Data Center in Brimfield Township to allow time to assess Data Center impacts on
health, safety, utilities and infrastructure.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED by the Brimfield Township Board of Trustees to consider
adding a dedicated section on Data Centers to the Brimfield Township Zoning Code during the
moratorium to fully and completely address the issue.

Kevin Scott second.

        Nicholas Coia - yea                             Sue Fields - yea
        Kevin Scott - yea                               Motion Passed
