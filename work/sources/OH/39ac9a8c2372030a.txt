ORDINANCE NO. 26-60                                    INTRODUCED BY: Mr. Reynolds


      AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A MORATORIUM ON FUTURE DEVELOPMENT OF DATA
      CENTERS IN THE CITY OF AVON LAKE AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY.


      WHEREAS, pursuant to the power vested in Council through the Avon Lake City
Charter and the Ohio Constitution, Council has the inherent power to enact planning,
zoning, and business regulation laws that further the health, safety, and welfare of
the public, including restricting or prohibiting certain business and land uses; and

      WHEREAS, over the past few years, interest has increased in developing and
constructing data centers in municipalities; and

        WHEREAS, the City’s Administration, Planning Commission, and Council have all
expressed concerns about data centers and will need time to undertake a thorough
review of all applicable state codes, regulations, and local ordinances in order to
formulate a proper local response to future development or construction of these
facilities in the City of Avon Lake; and

       WHEREAS, a data center that is incidental and/or subordinate to a permitted
principal use and primarily supports on-site operations is not included in this
moratorium. Accessory data center(s) shall not include any separate exterior
mechanical or electrical equipment beyond what is typical for principal use.

       WHEREAS, Council finds that a one-year moratorium on data centers will
provide City officials with a sufficient amount of time to study, plan for, formulate,
and draft reasonable and comprehensive regulations with respect to data centers and
that these regulations will further the health, safety, and welfare of the public.

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF
                     THE CITY OF AVON LAKE, STATE OF OHIO:

       Section No. 1: That for the reasons stated in the preamble above, Council
imposes a moratorium beginning with the effective date of this Ordinance and running
through June 22, 2027, on the consideration, approval, or authorization of any
Permitted Use, Conditional Use, or Accessory Use, i.e., any development of data
centers. This would include the granting of zoning permits, building permits, or
certificates of occupancy for any building, structure, use or change of use that would
enable said development within the City. Said moratorium extends to the submission,
consideration, or approval of all applications for all review procedures.

       Section No. 2: That this moratorium shall become effective on the date that
this Ordinance, following its passage by Council, is approved by the Mayor, and shall
remain in effect through June 22, 2027, following the date upon which it became

effective, unless extended by Council for a period of no more than an additional six
months, if Council finds such continuance is required to allow sufficient time for the
Administration to complete its study and recommend what action, if any, the City of
Avon Lake should take to safeguard the health, safety, and welfare of the public
through the provision of adequate planning, zoning, land use, or other regulatory
controls applicable to activities covered under Avon Lake Code of Ordinances.

       Section No. 3: That this moratorium suspends and tolls any time periods,
prescribed by law, within which Planning Commission or any other official or official
body of the City, is required to take action upon an application for any review
procedure for the development of a data center. No such application shall be deemed
to be approved by the failure of Planning Commission, or any other public official or
official body, to approve or deny said application during the time when this
moratorium shall be in effect.

        Section No. 4: No data-center zoning framework or other action authorizing
data-center uses shall be advanced to Council for final action unless and until the City
has completed an economic-development strategy for the City of Avon Lake, associated
data-center zoning, safety, infrastructure, socioeconomic, community-impact, and
fiscal-impact analyses, at least two publicly noticed public hearings or stakeholder
engagement sessions, review by the Economic Development Committee and Public
Service Committee, and subsequent referral to Council. If these steps are not
completed before the expiration of the moratorium, Council shall affirmatively consider
extending or modifying the moratorium to maintain protections until the required
review process is complete.

       Section No. 5: If any section, phrase, sentence, or portion of this Ordinance is
for any reason held invalid or unconstitutional by any Court of competent jurisdiction,
such portion shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision, and
such holding shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions thereof.

      Section No. 6: That any data center that is incidental and/or subordinate to a
permitted principal use and primarily supports on-site operations is not included in
this moratorium.

       Section No. 7: That it is found and determined that all formal actions of
Council relating to the passage of this Ordinance were taken in an open meeting, and
that all deliberations of Council and its committees resulting in such actions were
conducted in meetings open to the public in compliance with all applicable legal
requirements, including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

       Section No. 8: That this Ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency
measure, the emergency being the necessity to impose a moratorium at the earliest
possible date so as to enable the City to responsibly begin to study, analyze, and
review regulations related to future development of data centers in the city and for
the reasons set forth in the recitals of this Ordinance, thus for the health, safety, and
