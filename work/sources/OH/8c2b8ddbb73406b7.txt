WHEREAS, pursuant to the Ohio Constitution and local ordinances. City Council possesses the

WHEREAS, data processing centers (also referred to as "data centers") are not explicitly defined
or addressed within the Clayton Codified Ordinances or the City's zoning code; and
WHEREAS, due to this absence of a specific definition or use classification for data processing
centers, such facilities could theoretically be interpreted to fall withinthe City's existing definition
of warehousing or warehouse-type uses, potentially allowing applications to be submitted and
processed under that classification without adequate standards or review criteria specific to data
centeroperations; and

WHEREAS, for the purpose ofthis moratorium, "DataProcessing Center" or "Data Center" means
a building, stmcture, room, facility, campus, or portion thereof, the principal use ofwhich is to

WHEREAS, the term includes any materially similar use as detennined by the Zoning
Administrator, regardless of whether the applicant describes the use as warehousing,
telecommunications, utility infrastoucture, office, research, industrial, logistics, or another use
classification; and

WHEREAS, Council finds it necessary and in the public interest to seek time to study, develop,
and implement zoning regulations and standards regarding the possible use, location, and
operations of data processing centers within the City ofClayton; and
WHEREAS, Council desires to adopt the present 365-day temporary moratorium in order to
review and analyze data center impacts andto develop appropriate regulatory standards that ensure
the public peace, health, safety, and welfare ofthe citizens ofClayton, Ohio; and
WHEREAS, this moratorium shall not affect permits prior to the effective date of the adoption of
the present moratorium or have a current application pending; and
