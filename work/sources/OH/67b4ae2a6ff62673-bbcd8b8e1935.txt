WHEREAS, pursuant to Ordinance No. 556-2026, passed July 15, 2026, effective

July 20, 2026, this Council declared a moratorium on the review and issuance of all use,

building and zoning permits, certificates of occupancy, and other license or permit

applications, including licenses or permits issued by the Department of Public Utilities,

for the expansion of an existing standalone data center or establishment of a data center

as a principal use in a standalone facility or building; and

WHEREAS, the moratorium commenced on the effective date of Ordinance No.

556-2026, which was July 20, 2026, and ends on the earlier of: (i) the effective date of

an ordinance regulating or prohibiting data centers; or (ii) three (3) months after the

effective date of Ordinance No. 556-2026, which date is October 20, 2026, unless

extended by Council if Council determines that additional time is needed to further

review and determine what action is appropriate for the City; and

WHEREAS, a Data Center Working Group was formed that includes data center

experts, public utility representatives, Council policy staff, business and nonprofit

leaders, area residents, community members, and City leaders and staff from the

Departments of Public Utilities, Law, City Planning, Sustainability and Health to

compile information in order to make informed decisions about the best way to manage

the impact of data centers in the City; and

WHEREAS, this Council and its staff, as well as representatives from the

Administration, have worked diligently to research laws in other areas of Ohio and the

country and to begin the collaborative process of drafting data center regulations; and

WHEREAS, this Council determines that, while everyone involved has worked

relentlessly these last three months to come up with appropriate policies to regulate

data centers, still more work must be done to ensure that the adopted policies and

regulations are suitable for the City and citizens of northeast Ohio, and can be

straightforwardly implemented; and

WHEREAS, this ordinance constitutes an emergency measure for the immediate

preservation of the public peace, property, health, safety, or welfare in that an extension

of the moratorium on the review and issuance of all use, building and zoning permits,

certificates of occupancy, and other license or permit applications, including licenses or

permits issued by the Department of Public Utilities, for data centers is necessary so

that this Council and the Administration can to continue to consider reasonable

regulations or prohibitions of the operation of data centers in the City, and to continue

to review the Codified Ordinances to consider the impact of data centers on utility

infrastructure; now therefore,

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF CLEVELAND:

Section 1. That, for the reasons stated in the preamble, this Council hereby

extends the moratorium on the review and issuance of all use, building and zoning

permits, certificates of occupancy, and other license or permit applications, including

licenses or permits issued by the Department of Public Utilities, for the expansion of an

existing standalone data center or establishment of a data center as a principal use in a

standalone facility or building for a period beginning October 20, 2026 and ending on

the earlier of (i) the effective date of any newly enacted or amended codified ordinance

sections regulating or prohibiting data centers or (ii) January 29, 2027.
