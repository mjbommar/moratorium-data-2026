Toby Miller – Explained time table and approval process. This change to Zoning Regulations

began when the township decided to update its Comprehensive Land Use Plan. Work began

with a third party in conjunction with public input. Cost covered by a grant. Work began June of

last year. We passed a one-year moratorium on data centers in December. Gave us time to

review zoning resolutions. The third party working on the land use plan recommended we

enhance our regulations with a M-3 Industrial regulations and classifying different tier levels.

A Data Center Advisory Committee was formed in January of 2026, consisting of one trustee,

one zoning board member, one Board of Zoning Appeals member, Zoning Inspector and 10

residents. The Power Point presentation was the result of their efforts.

Any text amendments made to our zoning regulations are first sent to the Lucas County Plan

Commission. They have a public hearing and make recommendations to the zoning board.

The zoning board then reviews the plan commission recommendations and they have a public

hearing as well. The township trustees also have to have a public hearing upon

recommendation to approve/disapprove the zoning board recommendation. This process takes

several weeks/months to go thru as each organization involved has to give prior public notice.
