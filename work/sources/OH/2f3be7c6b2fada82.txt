ORDINANCES (SECOND OF TWO READINGS AND ENACTMENT AS AN EMERGENCY)

                              ORDINANCE NO. O – 06 – 26 – 09

        AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AMENDMENTS TO DESIGNATION
              OF JOB CATEGORIES AND PAY SCHEDULES AND
                    ENACTMENT AS AN EMERGENCY

WHEREAS, Section 3.01(D) of the Clayton Charter provides that Council may authorize and set
personnel’s rate of compensation; and

WHEREAS, a revised pay table have been prepared by the City Manager (appended hereto as
Exhibit A), reviewed by the Finance Director, and recommended for approval by the City Manager
to revise the pay table(s) for part-time fire and EMS personnel.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
CLAYTON, STATE OF OHIO:

1. That the revised Pay Table(s) appended hereto as Exhibit A be and hereby are authorized and
approved.

2. That this Ordinance shall be effective as an exception to any ordinance, resolution or other
legislation of the City of Clayton, Ohio, inconsistent with this Ordinance or which imposes
additional requirements for effectiveness or validity.

3. That in accordance with Clayton Charter Section 4.031(B) the requirement that this enactment
be read on two (2) separate days is dispensed with by affirmative vote of at least five (5) of the
members of Council.

4. That competitive recruitment and retention efforts for part-time Fire and EMS personnel are
necessary to ensure the continued provision of emergency response services to the residents of the
City of Clayton. Unless prompt implementation of the revised pay rates can be achieved, the City’s
ability to recruit and retain qualified part-time Fire and EMS personnel may be adversely affected.
Accordingly, in accordance with Charter Section 4.033(A) this ordinance is hereby declared to be
an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety
and welfare of the City and shall therefore be in full force and effect from and immediately upon
affirmative vote of at least five (5) of the members of Council.

ADOPTED BY COUNCIL ON JULY 16, 2026
ORDINANCE (FIRST OF TWO READINGS)

                               ORDINANCE NO. O – 07 – 26 - 10

   APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE SIDE LOT PROGRAM FOR THE CITY OF
     CLAYTON, OHIO; AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER OR THE CITY
    MANAGER’S DESIGNEE TO ADMINISTER THE PROGRAM AND TO TAKE
   NECESSARY ACTIONS RELATING TO THE TRANSFER OF ELIGIBLE CITY-
        OWNED SIDE LOTS; AND DECLARING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

WHEREAS, the City of Clayton, Ohio owns or may acquire certain vacant, underutilized, or
remnant parcels of real property that are not presently needed for municipal facilities, operations,
utilities, rights-of-way, parks, or other public purposes; and

WHEREAS, certain City-owned parcels may be suitable for transfer to owners of adjoining
residential or other eligible properties for use as side lots, yard extensions, maintenance areas, or
other lawful accessory uses consistent with the City’s zoning and property maintenance
requirements; and

WHEREAS, the City has reviewed a proposed Side Lot Program, as described in the
correspondence and materials submitted to City Council and incorporated into this Ordinance as
Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, the purposes of the Side Lot Program include reducing City maintenance obligations,
returning underutilized property to productive private ownership, improving neighborhood
appearance, supporting code compliance, and establishing a uniform administrative process for
evaluating and transferring eligible side lots; and

WHEREAS, City Council desires to approve the Side Lot Program and authorize the City
Manager, or the City Manager’s designee, to administer the program in accordance with this
Ordinance, applicable City ordinances, the City Charter, and Ohio law; and

WHEREAS, City Council finds that adoption of the Side Lot Program is in the public interest and
will promote the public health, safety, convenience, and general welfare of the City and its
residents.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
CLAYTON, OHIO:

SECTION 1. Approval of Side Lot Program

City Council hereby approves and adopts the Side Lot Program, substantially in the form attached
to this Ordinance as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference.

The Side Lot Program shall establish the City’s policy and administrative procedure for
identifying, reviewing, approving, and transferring eligible City-owned side lots to qualified
applicants, subject to the requirements of this Ordinance and any additional conditions approved
by the City Manager or City Council.
SECTION 2. Definitions

For purposes of this Ordinance and the Side Lot Program, the following terms shall have the
meanings set forth below unless the context clearly requires otherwise:

   1. “Applicant” means the owner of real property adjoining an eligible side lot who applies
      to acquire the side lot under the Side Lot Program.
   2. “City” means the City of Clayton, Ohio.
   3. “City Manager” means the City Manager of the City of Clayton, Ohio, or the City
      Manager’s authorized designee.
   4. “Eligible Side Lot” means a vacant, underutilized, remnant, or otherwise eligible parcel
      of real property owned by the City that:
          o adjoins real property owned by an applicant;
          o is not needed for municipal purposes, utilities, rights-of-way, drainage, public
              access, parks, infrastructure, or other public use;
          o is suitable for transfer under the Side Lot Program; and
          o satisfies any additional criteria set forth in Exhibit A.
   5. “Side Lot Program” means the policy, guidelines, application process, eligibility criteria,
      and transfer requirements approved by this Ordinance and attached as Exhibit A.
   6. “Side Lot” means a parcel transferred under the Side Lot Program for use in conjunction
      with adjoining real property, subject to all applicable zoning, subdivision, property
      maintenance, building, nuisance, and land-use requirements.

SECTION 3. Administrative Authority

The City Manager is hereby authorized to administer the Side Lot Program and to take all actions
reasonably necessary to implement this Ordinance, including, but not limited to:

   1. preparing and updating application forms, program guidelines, and administrative
       procedures consistent with Exhibit A;
   2. identifying City-owned parcels that may qualify as eligible side lots;
   3. reviewing applications and supporting documentation;
   4. obtaining comments from relevant City departments, including zoning, planning,
       engineering, public service, utilities, finance, law, police, fire, and code enforcement, as
       applicable;
   5. determining whether a parcel is needed for any municipal purpose;
   6. determining whether an applicant satisfies the eligibility requirements of the Side Lot
       Program;
   7. imposing reasonable conditions on any approved transfer;
   8. preparing or approving deeds, closing documents, affidavits, certifications, and related
       instruments;
   9. coordinating recording of deeds and instruments with the Montgomery County Recorder
       or other appropriate public office; and
   10. taking such other administrative actions as are necessary to carry out the intent of this
       Ordinance.
SECTION 4. Eligibility Criteria

Unless otherwise provided in Exhibit A or approved by City Council, an applicant must satisfy the
following minimum eligibility criteria:

   1. the applicant must own real property adjoining the requested side lot;
   2. the applicant must be current on real property taxes and assessments for property owned
      within the City;
   3. the applicant must not have unresolved City code enforcement violations, nuisance
      violations, zoning violations, or delinquent municipal charges, unless waived by the City
      Manager for good cause;
   4. the requested side lot must not be required for present or reasonably anticipated public use;
   5. the proposed use of the side lot must comply with the City’s zoning regulations, property
      maintenance requirements, subdivision regulations, and all other applicable laws;
   6. the applicant must agree to maintain the side lot in a safe, sanitary, and lawful condition;
      and
   7. the applicant must agree to execute all documents required by the City to complete the
      transfer.

SECTION 5. Review of City-Owned Parcels

Before approving any transfer under the Side Lot Program, the City Manager shall cause the parcel
to be reviewed to determine whether the parcel is needed or should be retained for any public
purpose, including:

   1.   utilities, drainage, stormwater management, or infrastructure;
   2.   public right-of-way, access, sidewalks, pathways, or transportation improvements;
   3.   public safety, fire access, or emergency management needs;
   4.   parks, recreation, open space, or environmental purposes;
   5.   economic development or redevelopment purposes;
   6.   existing easements, restrictions, encumbrances, or title concerns; or
   7.   any other municipal purpose determined by the City Manager.

No parcel shall be transferred under the Side Lot Program unless the City Manager determines that
the parcel is not needed for municipal purposes and is appropriate for disposition under the
program.

SECTION 6. Transfer Price, Costs, and Fees

The transfer price for an eligible side lot shall be established in accordance with Exhibit A.

Unless otherwise approved by City Council or the City Manager, the applicant shall be responsible
for all costs associated with the transfer, including, as applicable:

   1.   application fees;
   2.   title examination or title evidence;
   3.   deed preparation;
   4.   recording fees;
   5.   conveyance fees;
   6.   survey or legal description costs;
   7.   closing costs;
   8.   real property taxes and assessments arising after transfer; and
   9.   any other cost required to complete the conveyance.

The City Manager may waive or reduce administrative charges where permitted by law and where
the waiver or reduction is consistent with the purposes of the Side Lot Program.

SECTION 7. Conveyance Documents and Conditions

Any transfer approved under the Side Lot Program shall be made by deed or other appropriate
conveyance instrument approved by the City’s legal counsel.

The City may include in the deed, purchase agreement, closing documents, or other transfer
instruments any lawful condition deemed necessary or appropriate, including, but not limited to:

   1. a requirement that the side lot be maintained as part of, or in conjunction with, the
      applicant’s adjoining property;
   2. a requirement that the side lot not be separately sold, transferred, leased, divided, or
      developed except as permitted by the City’s zoning and subdivision regulations and any
      deed restrictions;
   3. a requirement that the owner maintain the side lot free from weeds, debris, junk vehicles,
      nuisance conditions, and code violations;
   4. reservation of easements for utilities, drainage, access, public improvements, or other
      municipal purposes;
   5. a statement that the transfer is made subject to all existing easements, restrictions, rights-
      of-way, covenants, zoning regulations, and matters of record;
   6. a statement that the City makes no warranty regarding soil conditions, environmental
      conditions, buildability, access, marketability, or suitability for any particular use;
   7. a requirement that the applicant accept the property in its existing “as is, where is”
      condition;
   8. a reversion, repurchase, or enforcement provision if the applicant fails to comply with
      material program conditions, if such provision is included in Exhibit A or otherwise
      approved by City Council; and
   9. any other condition reasonably related to the purposes of the Side Lot Program.

SECTION 8. Zoning, Subdivision, and Lot Consolidation

Approval of a transfer under the Side Lot Program shall not constitute approval of any zoning
variance, conditional use, subdivision, lot split, lot consolidation, building permit, occupancy
permit, or development approval.

The recipient of a side lot shall comply with all applicable requirements of the Clayton Codified
Ordinances, including zoning, subdivision, building, property maintenance, nuisance, and land-
use regulations.
Where required by the City Manager, the applicant shall complete a lot consolidation, parcel
combination, covenant, deed restriction, or other instrument to ensure that the side lot is used in a
manner consistent with the Side Lot Program and applicable law.

SECTION 9. Authority to Execute Documents

Upon approval of an application under the Side Lot Program, the City Manager is authorized to
execute, on behalf of the City, purchase agreements, deeds, settlement statements, affidavits,
certificates, closing documents, and all other instruments necessary to complete the transfer of an
eligible side lot, provided that the transfer is consistent with this Ordinance, Exhibit A, and
applicable law.

If City Council approval is required for a particular transfer under the City Charter, Ohio law, the
Clayton Codified Ordinances, or the terms of Exhibit A, the transfer shall be presented to City
Council for separate approval before conveyance.

SECTION 10. No Obligation to Transfer Property

Nothing in this Ordinance or the Side Lot Program shall require the City to sell, transfer, or
otherwise dispose of any City-owned property.

The City reserves the right to reject any application, retain any parcel, impose reasonable
conditions on any transfer, or discontinue processing of any proposed transfer if the City
determines that such action is in the public interest.

SECTION 11. Equal Application and Non-Discrimination

The Side Lot Program shall be administered in a uniform and non-discriminatory manner.

No application shall be approved or denied on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin,
disability, familial status, ancestry, military status, age, or any other classification protected by
federal, state, or local law.

SECTION 12. Records and Reporting

The City Manager shall maintain records of applications, approvals, denials, parcel reviews,
transfer documents, and recorded instruments relating to the Side Lot Program.

The City Manager may provide periodic updates to City Council regarding the administration of
the program, including the number of applications received, parcels transferred, parcels retained,
and any recommended program amendments.

SECTION 13. Compliance With Law

All actions taken under this Ordinance shall be subject to the City Charter, the Clayton Codified
Ordinances, the Ohio Revised Code, and other applicable federal, state, and local laws.
To the extent any provision of Exhibit A conflicts with this Ordinance or applicable law, this
Ordinance and applicable law shall control.

SECTION 14. Severability

If any section, subsection, paragraph, sentence, clause, phrase, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect
the validity or enforceability of the remaining portions of this Ordinance. City Council declares
that it would have adopted this Ordinance and each section and provision herein irrespective of
the fact that any one or more sections or provisions may be declared invalid or unenforceable.

                             RESOLUTION NO. R – 07 – 26 – 46

      A RESOLUTION ENACTING AN IMMEDIATE TEMPORARY 365 DAY
  MORATORIUM ON THE ACCEPTANCE AND PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS
  FOR ZONING, CONDITIONAL USE, AND/OR BUILDING PERMIT APPROVALS
   FOR DATA PROCESSING CENTERS WITHIN THE CITY OF CLAYTON, OHIO

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Ohio Constitution and local ordinances, City Council possesses the
inherent power to enact appropriate planning, zoning, and business regulation laws that further the
health, safety, welfare, comfort and peace of its citizens; and

WHEREAS, data processing centers (also referred to as “data centers”) are not explicitly defined
or addressed within the Clayton Codified Ordinances or the City’s zoning code; and

WHEREAS, due to this absence of a specific definition or use classification for data processing
centers, such facilities could theoretically be interpreted to fall within the City's existing definition
of warehousing or warehouse-type uses, potentially allowing applications to be submitted and
processed under that classification without adequate standards or review criteria specific to data
center operations; and

WHEREAS, for the purpose of this moratorium, “Data Processing Center” or “Data Center” means
a building, structure, room, facility, campus, or portion thereof, the principal use of which is to
house computer systems, servers, network equipment, telecommunications equipment, data
storage systems, transaction-processing equipment, artificial-intelligence or high-performance
computing equipment, cryptocurrency mining or blockchain transaction-processing equipment,
cloud computing infrastructure, colocation equipment, server farms, or related mechanical,
electrical, power-generation, battery-storage, backup-power, cooling, security, or utility
infrastructure used primarily for the storage, management, processing, transmission, mining,
validation, dissemination, or distribution of digital data; and

WHEREAS, the term includes any materially similar use as determined by the Zoning
Administrator, regardless of whether the applicant describes the use as warehousing,
telecommunications, utility infrastructure, office, research, industrial, logistics, or another use
classification; and

WHEREAS, Council finds it necessary and in the public interest to seek time to study, develop,
and implement zoning regulations and standards regarding the possible use, location, and
operations of data processing centers within the City of Clayton; and

WHEREAS, Council desires to adopt the present 365-day temporary moratorium in order to
review and analyze data center impacts and to develop appropriate regulatory standards that ensure
the public peace, health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Clayton, Ohio; and

WHEREAS, this moratorium shall not affect permits prior to the effective date of the adoption of
the present moratorium or have a current application pending; and

WHEREAS, upon declaration of a moratorium, the City Manager, Law Director, Planning and
Zoning Department, Planning Commission, and other appropriate City staff are directed to study
the potential impacts of Data Processing Centers; review applicable federal, state, county, regional,
and local laws and utility constraints; evaluate the adequacy of the Clayton Codified Ordinances
and zoning code; and prepare recommended zoning text amendments, use classifications,
development standards, and review procedures for Council’s consideration.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
CLAYTON, STATE OF OHIO:

Section 1: That an immediate and temporary moratorium of 365 days be implemented on the
acceptance and processing of applications for zoning, conditional use, and/or building permit
approvals, and associated pre-occupancy inspections for data processing centers within the City of
Clayton, Ohio. Said moratorium shall expire on July 16, 2027 unless earlier revoked by Council
before that date.

Section 2. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating
to the passage of this Resolution were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all such
deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were
in meetings open to the public in compliance with all legal
