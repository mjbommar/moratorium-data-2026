    AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A MORATORIUM ON ACCEPTING FOR FILING
    AND CONSIDERATION, AND/OR ISSUING ZONING AND/OR OCCUPANCY
    PERMITS FOR ANY BUILDING, STRUCTURE, USE OR CHANGE OF USE
    INVOLVING DATA CENTERS AS A PERMITTED USE FOR A PERIOD NOT TO
    EXCEED TWELVE (12) MONTHS AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY TO ALLOW
    THE CITY TIME TO STUDY, ANALYZE, REVIEW AND POSSIBLY ESTABLISH
    REGULATIONS FOR DATA CENTERS AS A PERMITTED USE IN THE CITY

             WHEREAS, pursuant to the Ohio Constitution, Charter and local ordinances, City
    Council possesses the inherent power to enact appropriate planning, zoning, building,
    and business regulation laws that further the health, safety, welfare, comfort and peace
    of its citizens, including restricting, prohibiting and/or regulating certain business uses;
    and

           WHEREAS, the city's Planning and Zoning Codes to not currently permit Data
    Centers as a permitted use within the city; and

            WHEREAS, Data Centers, for the purposes of this moratorium, shall be defined
    as a "physical facility housing computers and/or networking equipment that is principally
    use for data processing and storage"; and

            WHEREAS, notwithstanding the fact that the city's zoning code does not
    currently permit Data Centers as a permitted use, out of an abundance of caution, the
    city administration is recommending that the moratorium herein be adopted to allow the
    city time to review its codified ordinances and any applicable state law to determine if it
    is necessary to adopt any regulations outside of and in addition to the city's current ban
    on Data Centers as a permitted use; and

            WHEREAS, City Council desires to enact a moratorium on accepting for filing
    and consideration and/or issuing zoning and/or occupancy permits for any building,
    structure use or change of use that involves Data Centers as a permitted use in the city
    for a period of twelve (12) months;

           NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council for the City of Aurora,
    County of Portage, State of Ohio, that:
 City of Aurora, Ohio                                Page 2                         Ordinance 2026-117


        Section 1. City Council hereby establishes a moratorium on accepting for filing
and consideration and/or issuing zoning and/or occupancy permits for any building,
structure, use or change of use that involves Data Centers as a permitted use int eh city
for a period of twelve (12) months.

       Section 2. The moratorium shall be in effect for a period of twelve (12) months
from the effective date of this Ordinance or until changes are enacted to amend the
Codified Ordinances of the City of Aurora to address the issues described herein or until
this Council approves legislation explicitly revoking this moratorium, whichever occurs
first.

       Section 3. No existing business in the city may expand in any way that would
involve Data Centers as a permitted use in the city for the duration of the moratorium
period hereby established.

        Section 4. The purpose of this moratorium is to allow the city time to study,
analyze, review and possibly establish regulations for Data Centers as a permitted use
in the city.

       Section 5. It is hereby found and determined that all formal actions of this
Council concerning and relating to the passage of this Ordinance were adopted in an
open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and any of its
Committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public in
compliance with the legal requirements, including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised
Code.

        Section 6. This Ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure
necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety of this municipality.
Wherefore, provided it receives an affirmative vote of six or more of the members
elected to this Council, this Ordinance shall take effect and be in force immediately upon
its passage by Council and approval of the Mayor; otherwise, it shall take effect and be
in force after the earliest time provided by law.
