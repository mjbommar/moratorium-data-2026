ORDINANCES

166-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to receive and administer funds from the Mental Health Recovery Services Board (MHRSB) for Specialty Docket Funds for the Lima Municipal Court.

167-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to receive and administer funds from the Mental Health Recovery Services Board (MHRSB) for Addiction Treatment Program (ATP) funds and Administrative funds for the Lima Municipal Court.

168-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Elite Builders General Contracting LLC for the Schoonover Observatory Site Improvements.

169-26 An ordinance amending Section 456.01 of the Codified Ordinances of the City of Lima, Ohio, concerning establishment of municipal parking lots.

170-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to apply for, receive, and administer funds from the Ohio BWC 2026 Firefighter Exposure to Environmental Elements Grant for the Fire Department.

171-26 An ordinance amending Section 244.255 of the Codified Ordinances of the City of Lima, regarding the basic pay plan and benefits for the Fire Chief and Deputy Fire Chiefs.

172-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to apply for, receive, and administer funds from the U.S. Department of Justice’s Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS Office) FY26 Community Policing Development (CPD) Microgrants Program.

173-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to apply for, receive, and administer funds from the U.S. Department of Justice’s Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS Office) FY26 Community Policing Development (CPD) Microgrants Program.

174-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to apply for, receive, and administer funds from the U.S. Department of Justice’s COPS Hiring Program (CHP).

175-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to apply for, receive, and administer funds from the U.S. Department of Justice’s Office of Community Oriented Policing Services FY26 Safer Outcomes: Enhancing Crisis Response Training for Law Enforcement, for the Police Department.

176-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with PropertyRoom.com, Inc. for the Police Department.

177-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to terminate the Water Service Agreement with Golf at Sugar Creek, LLC, and any other agreements regarding the Golf at Sugar Creek, LLC, and to enter into any associated agreements or to execute any associated document, all as determined by the Law Director to be necessary to the termination authorized herein.

178-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with govdeals.com and to otherwise dispose of obsolete hard drives for the Data Systems Group.

179-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with New Lima-Housing for the Future, Inc. for the First Home Lima – Admin Support/DPA Services.

180-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to administer funds for the Home Update Project.

181-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with New Lima-Housing for the Future, Inc. for Housing Property Maintenance Training.

182-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with New Lima-Housing for the Future, Inc. for Community Housing Development Organization Operating Funding.

183-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with New Lima-Housing for the Future, Inc. for the Community Housing Development Organization (CHDO) Set-Aside Project.

184-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to administer funds for Home Administrative Support.

185-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Soldiers of Honor for administration of the Boxing Bootcamp Program.

186-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with the Bradfield Community Center for administration of the Hoops and Wellness program.

187-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Trinity United Methodist Church, Lima, Ohio, for administration of the Freedom School Program.

188-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Youth Connect for administration of the Youth Mental Health program.

189-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Prevention Awareness Support Services for administration of Store It Safe/Lima.

190-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Children’s Hunger Alliance for administration of the Weekend Meal Boxes Program.

191-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with Oheneba Soccer Academy for administration of the Soccer Academy Program.

192-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with West Ohio Community Action Partnership for administration of Housing Counseling Services.

193-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to enter into contract with West Ohio Community Action Partnership for administration of Fair Housing Services.

194-26 An ordinance authorizing the Mayor to apply for, receive, and administer funds from the US Conference of Mayors Healthy & Sustainable Communities Grant for the Department of Housing & Neighborhoods.

195-26 An ordinance amending the City Zoning Map to Class II Residential for multiple parcels in the City of Lima, as herein described.

196-26 An ordinance enacting an additional six-month moratorium on the processing of applications, approvals, licenses, or permit, of any kind involving any type of shared living facility.

197-26 An ordinance amending the 2026 annual budget.

198-26 An ordinance enacting an eighteen-month moratorium on the establishment, development, processing, review and approval of stand-alone data center development within the City of Lima and on new requests for City supplied water for date center developments outside the corporate limits.
