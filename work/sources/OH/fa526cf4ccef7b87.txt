                             RESOLUTION NO. 50-2026

                TITLE: AMENDING RESOLUTION NO. 164–2023
                VARIOUS TYPES OF BUSINESSES MORATORIUM

      AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RESOLUTION 164-2023 BY REMOVING
 RETAIL DISPENSING OF MARIJUANA TO THE LIST OF USES PROHIBITED
 BY THE MORATORIUM AND ADDING VAPE STORES AND DATA CENTERS
 TO THE LIST OF USES PROHIBITED BY THE MORATORIUM, AND
 DECLARING AN EMERGENCY.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of
 Barberton, State of Ohio:

        SECTION 1. That Council herby amends Resolution No. 164-2023, “Various
 Types of Businesses Moratorium,” by showing said amendment with a strikethrough for
 any deletions and bold for any additions to read as follows:

2-2023 VARIOUS TYPES OF BUSINESSES MORATORIUM

        A Resolution imposing a Moratorium on the issuing of Zoning Permits until the
adoption of the new City of Barberton Zoning Code for Used Car Lots, Mini Warehouse,
Limited Indoor Storage, new build Dollar Stores, Tobacco Shops, Data Centers and
Vape Stores, and the Cultivation, Processing, and/or Retail Dispensing of Marijuana
within the City of Barberton, with the exception of permitting Mini Warehouses within
Industrial Use zoning districts.

          SECTION 2. Council hereby imposes a moratorium until the adoption of the new
 City of Barberton Zoning Code for Used Car Lots, Mini Warehouse, Limited Indoor
 Storage, new build Dollar Stores, Tobacco Shops, Data Centers and Vape Stores, and
 the Cultivation, Processing, and/or Retail Dispensing of Marijuana within the City of
 Barberton, with the exception of permitting Mini Warehouses within Industrial Use zoning
 districts.

        SECTION 3. That it is hereby found and determined that all formal actions of
 this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were taken in an
 open meeting of this Council and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its
 committees that resulted in such formal action were meetings open to the public in
 compliance with the law.

         SECTION 4. That this ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure
 necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety, convenience
 and welfare of the City of Barberton and the inhabitants thereof, in order to facilitate
 timeliness, and provided it receives the necessary votes required by the City Charter,
 shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage and approval; otherwise to be in
 full force and effect from and after the earliest period allowed by law.
