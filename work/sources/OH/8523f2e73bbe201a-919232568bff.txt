1846-1848 Lieutenant Grant's bravery resulted in a promotion to Captain. Grant married Julia

31, 1861, after the start of the Civil War, Grant re-entered the army and received a promotion to

Brigadier General. He was a great military leader and strategist and after great victories in

wanting the war over as quickly as possible. Slightly more than one year later, the war ended in
