**Important Information**

* The Jackson Township Board of Trustees Regular Business Meeting is held each month on the First Monday at 7:00 P.M. Work Sessions will be held each month on the Third Monday (beginning in April 2025) at 7:00 P.M. All meetings are held at the Jackson Township Community Center located at 3263 U.S. Route 50, Williamsburg, OH 45176. In the event the meeting date is a holiday, the meeting will be moved to the following day (Tuesday) at 7:00 P.M.

* The Zoning Commission regular meetings are typically held the second Wednesday of each month at 7:00 p.m. These meetings may be cancelled if there is no business to be discussed. All meetings will be posted on the [Jackson Township Calendar](https://www.jacksontwpclermontoh.gov/township-calendar.html) as scheduled. All meetings are held at the Jackson Township Community Center located at 3263 U.S. Route 50, Williamsburg, OH 45176.

​​

* Notices for all meeting changes, special meetings, and emergency meetings will be posted on the township calendar found below[ ](https://www.jacksontwpclermontoh.gov/township-calendar.html)and on the exterior bulletin board adjacent to Door #3 of the Community Center in accordance with the Ohio Open Meetings Act. ​
