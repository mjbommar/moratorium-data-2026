Dan Karant, Summit County Combined General Health District, Board of Health —
Norton Representative — Mr. Karant noted that he was providing this update in public
health as it may impact the Norton community. He noted that his comments are
based on nearly 50 years in healthcare as a community pharmacist. He is not citing
the policies of the Summit County Board of Health. Mr. Karant provided health
updates on Respiratory Activity/Influenza, Measles, and Cyclosporiasis (a copy of
Mr. Karant’s report and PowerPoint presentations are attached herewith).

He acknowledged several public health concerns throughout the country and stated he
would focus on Ohio and Summit County. The Cyclosporiasis outbreak was first
identified in Summit County around June 26. After the source of the food supply
borne infectious disease was identified and pulled from grocery shelves, the cases
began to subside. Mr. Karant noted that Ohio’s flu season typically runs from
October to May and peaks between December and February. He suggested that all
receive the influenza vaccine after October 1, 2026 to achieve the ultimate
effectiveness, Mr. Karant reported the County’s Syringe Exchange Program accepts
unwanted syringes from community members and they can be dropped off at City
