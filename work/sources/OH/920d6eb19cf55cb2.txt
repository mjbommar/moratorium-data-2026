Report of Village Officials
Police: Call report submitted. Busy season right now - patrols, investigations, and events. Another
successful BLAS ST celebration with no incidents or accidents. A lot of cars and traffic but no incidents
to report of. Hats off to the BLASST committee, a lot of work they do behind the scenes. Chief's sixth
year working BLASST. BLAS ST pays the officers for their Special Duty time on that day. Utilized
police golf cart on that night, passing out popsicles to the kids. They will continue to do this - if you see
a cooler on the back of the cart. Noise ordinance - still getting some complaints past 11 pm. Still seeing
no tags on some golf carts. Will advertise again for free golf cart inspections. Thanked Jae's Towing for
allowing the Village to utilize their large mobile street light tower to create a safe makeshift crosswalk
during BLASST. The transfer from Mayor's Court to Municipal Court is going smoothly. All charges,
including traffic violations, are now being sent through municipal court.

Fire: Run report submitted. Busy time of year for their department as well. State of Ohio EMS grant
received last month for $3,055.70 and a donation from BLASST for $1,500. Drug license has been
updated and renewed. 4 runs during BLASST, only 1 of them was related to the festivities

Public Works: Absent, report was covered in Public Service Committee meeting earlier tonight.

Development: Read her submitted written report and showed new maps for short-term rentals and
zoning areas within the village that are available on the village website.

Parks and Recreation Commission: Resident would like to see a water feature for the kids to play in.
It was asked- if we cannot have the park restrooms open currently, could we have a portable toilet at the
park during the Summer?
