ORDINANCE NO. 2026.007

AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A MORATORIUM ON THE
AUTHORIZATION OR ESTABLISHMENT OF COMPUTER DATA
CENTERS WITHIN THE VILLAGE OF BLANCHESTER, OHIO

WHEREAS, the Village of Blanchester (“Village”) desires to impose a moratorium on the construction
of data centers within the Village of Blanchester for the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the
Village of Blanchester; and

WHEREAS, the definition of computer data center for the purpose of this Ordinance shall be defined as
a facility, or portion of a facility, primarily designed and used to house computer systems, servers, data
storage devices, and related telecommunications and networking equipment for the purpose of storing,
processing, managing, or transmitting electronic data. A computer data center typically includes
supporting infrastructure such as electrical power systems, backup generators, cooling and ventilation
equipment, fire suppression systems, security measures, and network connectivity, and is intended for
continuous operation; and

WHEREAS, the Council for the Village of Blanchester desires time to study the economic and
environmental impacts on the Village and its residents, or, alternatively, to develop and implement
regulations regarding the possible location and operation of data center facilities within the Village of
Blanchester; and

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Council for the Village Council of Blanchester,
Clinton County, Ohio that:

SECTION 1. The Council for the Village of Blanchester hereby establishes a moratorium with respect
to the authorization or establishment of any computer data center within Village of Blanchester for two
years from the date this Ordinance takes effect.

SECTION 2. That this Council hereby finds and determines that all formal actions relative to the passage
of this Ordinance were taken in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council
and of its committees, if any, which resulted in formal action, were taken in meetings open to the public,
in full compliance with applicable legal requirements, including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 3. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately, or upon the earliest date as otherwise allowed
by law.
