MINUTES FOR THE REGULAR CITY COUNCIL MEETING
June 8, 2026

Bellevue City Council met on Monday, June 8, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. Following the Pledge of
Allegiance, a prayer was offered by Council President, Ronald Smith, where he offered a prayer to the
Wallingford Family and the citizens of Bellevue for their recent loss of Law Director, David A. Wallingford.

Council Members present: MR. BAKER, MR. DANIEL, MR. HILL, MR. MORRISON, MS. MUNIZ, MR.
SHEPHERD, and MR. WYNBISSINGER.

The minutes of the regular City Council meeting were presented for approval. A motion was made

by Mr. Baker to approve the minutes as written. lt was seconded by Ms. Muniz. The minutes were approved
as written.

REPORTS AND COMMUNICATIONS FROM CITY OFFICIALS: Starting with Mayor Strecker, who has
worked with Mr. Wallingford as far back as 1991, each City Official gave a touching tribute to Law Director
David A. Wallingford, who had recently passed. They spoke of his kindness, dedication, and willingness
to help others. He was loyal to the City, the Community, and his family. When asked a question, he had

a way to explain the answer so all could understand. He will be a big loss to the City and to all who knew
him.

Safety-Service Director, Mr. Brugnone, gave updates on roads and thermal stripes. The Route
18 Project started today and will be headed this way. ODOT projects moving a couple of medians and
fixing catch basins. Mr. Brugnone said he is hoping to redo the bike trail from York Township Hall to Burger
King. He plans to have the in-town streets list for the next meeting.

REPORTS FROM CITY COUNCIL MEMBERS:

Treasurer Dave Freitag reported on RITA and the allocation of 2025 costs for income tax
collection. The costs of collection are 1.03% The total 2025 retainer is $215,786.00 and our total collections
were $7,293,987.00. The refund amount for the City is $140,383.00.

Mr. Shepherd asked if we can do an inspection of sidewalks that run parallel when doing the
streets.

Mr. Wynbissinger reported that we need to look at E. Bikes and Scooters.
Ms. Muniz had a citizen inquire how close they can park to the driveway.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

Council as a Whole — There was a committee meeting prior to Council tonight to discuss adding
Ordinances 14-26, 15-26, and 16-26.

Mr. Wynbissinger moved to add Ordinance No. 14-26 and 15-26 to the agenda, seconded by Mr.
Hill. The vote was aye.

Water Superintendent, Brandon Good ~ reported that they are making some upgrades with
meters. A utility Tech. is checking all the meters in town, looking for dead meters. The list is minimal at
this time. They are finalizing the water rate study compared to other cities.


Some citations have been issued on mowing and permit applications for driveway approaches.
CITIZEN COMMENTS:

Patti Gray, 252 Gunther St., reported that 3523 bags have been distributed at $3.60 per bag.

Mrs. Gray also reported on behalf of the Tourism Bureau. They received a letter from the State
Auditor and they found no issues.

Mrs. Gray also commented that some citizens enjoy the lighted flags in the City, she really likes to
see real flags flying.

John Laviola, 858 Monroe Street, said he was happy to see the Union Pacific Big Boy stop in the

City of Bellevue. There were about a 1,000 people in attendance. He thanked the City for their part in
getting them to stop here.

PRESIDENT’S REPORT:

President Smith stated that Ordinance no. 16-26 should be added to the next council meeting's
agenda.

Mr, Shepherd moved to add Ordinance No. 16-26, seconded by Mr. Morrison. The vote was aye.

Safety-Service Director Mr. Brugnone reported that Chief Linder will be at the next meeting
concerning a couple of officers.

LEGISLATION:

Ordinance No. 14-26 was given its first reading: AN ORDINANCE APPROVING A JOB
CREATION INCOME TAX CREDIT AGREEMENT WITH AUTOKINITON, AND DECLARING AN
EMERGENCY.

A motion was made by Mr. Hill to suspend the rules and give Ordinance No. 14-26 its second and third
reading by title only, seconded by Mr. Baker. A vote was taken and passed by a vote of 7-0.

Ordinance No. 14-26 was given its second and third reading by title only. AN ORDINANCE

APPROVING A JOB CREATION INCOME TAX CREDIT AGREEMENT WITH AUTOKINITON, AND DECLARING AN
EMERGENCY.

Mr. Baker moved to adopt Ordinance No. 14-26, seconded by Mr. Wynbissinger. The vote was 6-1 with Mr.
Shepherd being the no vote.

ORDINANCE NO. 15-26 was given its first reading: AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A TEMPORARY
MORATORIUM ON THE ESTABLISHMENT, CONVERSION OF OTHER STRUCTURES OR BUILDING INTO A
DATA CENTER, INCLUDING THE DEVELOPMENT AND CONSTRUCTION OF ANY BUILDING, STRUCTURE,
USE OR CHANGE OF USE THAT WOULD ALLOW DATA CENTERS FOR A PERIOD OF TWELVE (12) MONTHS
FROM THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS ORDINANCE IN ORDER TO ALLOW THE CITY ADMINISTRATION AND
CITY COUNCIL TIME TO REVIEW APPLICABLE FEDERAL CODES, OHIO STATUTES, CODES AND

REGULATIONS ALONG WITH THE CITY’S COFICID ORDINANCES RELATIVE TO SUCH ACTIVITY, AND
DECLARING AN EMERGENCY.

Mr. Morrison moved to suspend the rules and give Ordinance No. 15-26 its second and third reading by title
only. A vote was taken and passed by a vote of 7-0.
