VILLAGE OF CARROLLTON, OHIO
ORDINANCE NO, 2026-10

An Ordinance Authorizing a Moratorium on Data Centers in the Village of Carrollton

Whereas, data centers are industrial—scale facilities that require significant energy, water and
infrastructure capacity, that would cause water supply issues that the Village could not support,
utility demand that will likely cause utility rate increases, as well as potential environmental and
other impacts on Village of Carrollton residents, that will not significantly contribute to economic
development or employment, and that will potentially conflict with the long-term planning goals of
the Village;

Whereas, Council finds it to be in the best interests of the public peace, health, safety and welfare of
the residents of the Village of Carrollton to impose a moratorium on data centers in the Village.

Now, Therefore, Be It Ordained by the Council of the Village of Carrollton, Ohio that:
1. Council hereby imposes a moratorium on data centers within the corporate limits of the

Village of Carrollton.
2. This ordinance shall become effective at the earliest date allowed by law.

Passed the 27th day of May , 2026.
