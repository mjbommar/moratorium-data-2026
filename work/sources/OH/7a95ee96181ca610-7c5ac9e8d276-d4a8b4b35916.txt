A RESOLUTION OF THE T TOWNSHIP OF PARIS, UNION COUNTY, OHIO

ENACTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH A COMPREHENSIVE PLAN AND THE PROVISION


OF CHAPTER 519, OHIO REVISED CODE, DIVIDING THE TOWNSHIP INTO ZONES

AND DISTRICTS, ENCOURAGING, REGULATING AND RESTRICTING THEREIN THE LOCATION, CONSTRUCTION, RECONSTRUCTION, ALTERATION AND USE OF STRUCTURES AND LAND: PROMOTING THE ORDERLY DEVELOPMENT OF RESIDENTIAL, BUSINESS, INDUSTRIAL, RECREATION, AND PUBLIC AREAS: PROVIDING FOR ADEQUATE LIGHT, AIR, AND CONVENIENCE OF ACCESS TO


PROPERTY BY REGULATING THE USE OF LAND AND BUILDINGS AND THE BULK

OF STRUCTURES IN PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAYS: PROVIDING T THE COMPATIBILITY OF


DIFFERENT LAND USES AND THE MOST APPROPRIATE USE OF LAND: PROVIDING

FOR T THE ADMINISTRATION OF THIS RESOLUTION AS PROVIDED HEREAFTER,


AND PRESCRIBING PENALTIES FOR THE VIOLATION OF THE PROVISIONS IN THIS

RESOLUTION OR ANY AMENDMENT THERETO. ALL FOR THE PURPOSE OF


PROTECTING THE PUBLIC HEAL T TH, SAFETY AND GENERAL WELFARE: AND FOR

THE AMENDMENT THEREOF.


THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF TOWNSHIP TRUSTEES OF PARIS

TOWNSHIP, UNION COUNTY, OHIO.

Section 100 Title:


This Resolution shall be known and may be cited to as the "Zoning Resolution of t the

Township of P Paris, Union County, Ohio" .

Section 101 Use of L Land or Buildings for Agricultural Purposes Not Affected:


It is the intent of t this Resolution to be and remain in compliance with ORC 519.21 Powers not

conferred on township zoning commission by chapter. ORC 519.21 is a statute, created and

maintained by the State, which limits the authority of t townships and establishes what is

commonly referred to as the "agriculture exemption". How ORC 519.21 impacts this Resolution

is described herein.

This Resolution does not affect the use of a any land for agricultural purposes, or the construction

or use of b buildings or structures incident to the use for agricultural purposes of t the land on wh ich

such buildings or structures are located and no zoning certificate shall be required for any such

building or structure. (Residential dwellings do require a permit however.)

There are two conditions where the agriculture exemption does not apply. 1) In any platted


subdivision; and, 2) In any area consisting of f fifteen or more lots approved under ORC 711.131

that arc contiguous to one another, or some of w which are contiguous to one another and adjacent

to one side of a  a dedicated public road, and the balance of w which are contiguous to one another

and adjacent to the opposite side of t the same dedicated public road. When either of t these two

conditions exist, the requirements of t this Resolution apply to:

2

1. Agriculture on lots of o one acre or less; and,

2. Buildings or structures incident to the use of l land for agricultural purposes on lots greater

than one acre but not greater than five acres by: setback building lines, height, and size;

and,

3. Dairying and animal and poultry husbandry on lots greater than one acre but no greater

than five acres when at least thirty-five percent of t the lots in the subdivision are

developed with at least one building, structure, or improvement that is subject to real

property taxation or t that is subject to the tax on manufactured and mobile homes. After

thirty-five percent of t the lots are so developed, dairying and animal and poultry

husbandry shall be considered nonconforming.

Section 102 - Medical Marijuana:


The cultivation, processing or dispensing of m medical marijuana is prohibited in the

unincorporated territory of P Paris Township. The terms "cultivation" "processing" "dispensing"

and "medical marijuana" shall be interpreted in a manner t that is consistent with the definitions

found in the Ohio Administrative Code Chapter 3796:1-1-01.

Section 109 Compliance with Regulations:


The regulations for each district set fotth by this Resolution shall be minimum regulations

and shall apply uniformly to each class or kind of s structure or land, except as hereinafter

provided; or as otherwise granted by the Board of Z Zoning Appeals.

1. No buildings, structure or land shall be used or o occupied and no building or structure

or p patt t thereof s shall be erected, constructed, reconstructed, moved or structurally

altered except in conformity with all of t the regulations herein specified for the district

in which it is located.

2. No yard or lot existing at the time of p passage of t this Resolution shall be reduced in

dimension or area below the minimum requirements set forth herein. Yards or lots

created after the effective date of t this Resolution shall meet at least the minimum

requirements set forth herein.

Section 110 Provisions of R Resolution Declared to be the Minimum Requirements:


In their interpretation and application, the provisions of t this Resolution shall be held to be

minimum requirements, adopted for the promotion of t the public health, safety, and general

welfare. Whenever the requirements of t this Resolution conflict with the requirements of a any

other lawfully adopted rules, regulations, or resolutions, the most restrictive, or t that imposing

the higher standards shall govern. Where private building restrictions in recorded deeds or

on a platted subdivision are greater than those imposed by this Resolution, they are not

replaced by the provisions of t this Resolution.

Section 120 Separability Clause:


Should any section or p provision of t this Resolution be declared by the courts to be

unconstitutional or invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of t the Resolution as a

whole or any part thereof o other t than the part so declared to be unconstitutional or invalid.

No building, or other structure, shall be erected, moved, added to, structurally altered, nor

The application for zoning permit shall be signed by the owner or applicant attesting to the

truth and exactness of a all information supplied on the application. Each application shall

clearly state that the permit shall expire and may be revoked if w work has not begun within six

months or substantially completed within eighteen months of i issuance. At a minimum, the

application shall contain the following information:

1. Name, address, and telephone number of t the appl icant;

2. Legal description of p property;

3. Existing use;

4. Proposed use;

5. Zoning district;

6. Plans in duplicate showing t the actual dimensions and the shape of t the lot to be built

upon; the exact size and location of e existing buildings on the lot, if if any, and the

location and dimensions of t the proposed buildings or alteration;

7. Building h heights;

8. Number of o off-street parking spaces or loading berths;

9. Number of d dwelling units;

10. Such other matters as may be necessary to determine conformance with, and provide

for the enforcement of t this Resolution.

11. Documentation of a any reports and recommendations from the Soil and Water

Conservation District, as required by other sections of t the Resolution.

12. Fees - The application for a Zoning permit must be accompanied by the appropriate

fee required.

Section 302 - Approval of Z Zoning Permit:


Within ten (10) days after the receipt of a an application, the Zoning Inspector shall either

approve or disapprove the application in conformance with the provisions of t this Resolution.

All zoning permits shall, however, be conditional upon the commencement of w work within

six months. One copy of t the plans shall be returned to the applicant by the Zoning Inspector,

after the Zoning Inspector has marked such copy either as approved or disapproved and

attested to same by his signature on such copy. One copy of p plans, similarly marked, shall be

retained by the Zoning Inspector. The Zoning Inspector shall issue a placard, to be posted in

a conspicuous place on the property in question, attesting to the fact that the proposed use or

alteration is in conformance with the provisions of t this Resolution.

Section 304 - Expiration of Z Zoning Permit:


If t the work described in any zoning permit has not begun within six months from issuance

thereof, f, said permit shall expire and be deemed terminated. The zoning permit holder shall

provide the Township written notice of w work commencement. The zoning permit shall be

deemed void if t the zoning permit holder fails to provide written notice of w work

commencement within six months of t the permit's issuance, unless this provision is waived

by written acknowledgment of t the Zoning Inspector, executed upon the original permit.

property tax duplicate.

If t the work described in any zoning permit is substantially completed, then prior t to the

expiration of o one year from the permit issuance, the zoning permit holder may apply for one

ARTICLE IV - NON-CONFORMITIES


Section 400 - Intent:


Within the districts established by this Resolution or future amendments that may later be

adopted there exist lots, uses of l land, structures, and uses of s structures and land in combination

which were lawful before this Resolution was passed or amended, but which would be

prohibited, regulated, or restricted under the terms of t this Resolution or future amendments. It is

the intent of t this Resolution to permit these non-conformities to continue until they are removed

or discontinued. It is further the intent of t this Resolution that non- conformities shall not be

enlarged upon, expanded or e extended, nor be used as grounds for adding other structures or uses

prohibited elsewhere in the same district.

Section 410 - Incompatibility of N Non-Conformities:


Non-conformities are declared by this Resolution to be incompatible with permitted uses in the

districts in which such use is located. A non-conforming use of l land, or a non- conforming use

of a  a structure and land in combination shall not be extended or enlarged after passage of t this

Resolution. The addition of o other uses of a  a nature which would be generally prohibited in the

district in which such use is located are likewise prohibited.

Section 420 - Avoidance of U Undue Hardship:


To avoid undue hardship, nothing in this Resolution shall be deemed to require a change in the

plans, construction, or designated use of a any building on which actual construction was lawfully

begun and diligently carried on prior to the effective date of a adoption or amendment of t this

Resolution. Actual construction is hereby defined to include the placing of c construction

materials in permanent position and fastened in a permanent manner. Where demolition or

removal of a an existing building has been substantially begun and diligently carried out

preparatory to rebuilding, such demolition or removal shall be deemed to be actual construction.

Section 430- Single Non-Conforming Lots of R Record_;_


In any district in which single family dwellings are permitted, a single-family dwelling and

customary accessory buildings may be erected on any single lot ofrecord at the effective date of

adoption or amendment of t this Resolution. Such lot must be in separate ownership and may

not be of c continuous frontage with other lots in the same ownership. This provision shall apply

even though such lot fails to meet the requirements for area or width, or both, that are generally

applicable in the district, provided that yard dimensions and requirements other than these

applying to area or width, or both, of t the lot shall conform to the regulations for the district in

which such lot is located. Variance of y yard requirements shall be obtained only through action of

the Board of Z Zoning Appeals.

Section 431 - Non-Conforming Lots of R Record in Combination:

Section 1201 Governmental Signs Excluded:


For the purpose of t this Resolution "sign" does not include signs erected and maintained pursuant

The regulations contained in this section shall apply to all signs and all use districts.

1. In no circumstance shall an illuminated sign or lighting device be placed or directed so as

to permit the beams and illumination therefrom to be directed or beamed upon a public

nuisance;

2. No sign shall be placed on the roof o of a any building;

3. No sign of a any classification shall be installed, erected or attached in any form, shape or

4. All billboard signs shall be plainly marked with the name of t the person, firm or

corporation responsible for maintaining the sign;

5. Should any sign be or become unsafe or be in danger of f falling, the owner t thereof o or t the

person maintaining the same, shall upon receipt of w written notice from the Zoning

Inspector proceed at once to put such sign in a safe and secure condition or remove the

sign;

6. No sign shall be placed in any public right-of-way except publicly owned signs, such as

traffic control signs and directional signs. Signs directing and guiding traffic and parking

are permitted on any property.

7. No sign shall be placed to obstruct any portion of a  a window, doorway, transom, or o other

architectural detail.

8. No sign shall project above the edge of t the roof o of a  a structure.

9. No sign shall project more than 12 inches from the surface upon which it is attached.

The surface area of a  a sign shall be computed as including the entire area within a regular

geometric form or combinations of r regular, geometric forms comprising all of t the display area of

members, not being advertising matter, shall not be included in computation of s surface area.
