A. ORDINANCE 04-26 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING PART THREE OF

THE CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF WATERVILLE –

SCHEDULE II OF THE TRAFFIC SCHEDULES: SPEED LIMITS BY

ADDING SHARDED USE PATHS


Third Reading and Legislative Action

Due to the increasing concern in the community regarding the speed of motorized,

electric "e-bikes" on shared use paths, it is the recommendation of the administration to

establish a speed limit of 15 miles per hour on all of the shared use paths located

throughout the City. E-bikes are classified in three different categories: Class 1, Class 2

and Class 3. Class 1 and 2 e-bikes can reach speeds of 20 miles per hour and are

2

permitted on shared use paths under the Ohio Revised Code (ORC). Under the ORC,

Class 3 e-bikes are prohibited on shared use paths, require the operator to be at least 16

years of age and wear a protective helmet. Due to the weight and the potential speed of

an e-bike, a collision with a pedestrian has the potential to cause significant injury.

Setting a standard speed limit will ensure the safety of all residents using the shared use

path system which was originally designed for use by walkers, joggers and bicyclists,

not motorized vehicles. The speed limit would apply to all shared use path users

including conventional bicycles and scooters. All shared use paths would be marked

with speed limit signs and the City would implement a public information campaign to

raise awareness of the new requirements to protect overall public safety which would

include outreach to the schools and informational brochures.

Mr. Borowski made a motion to move for the passage of Ordinance 04-26. Mr. Jahns

seconded the motion.

Roll Call: Mr. Wagner - yes, Mr. Sargent - yes, Mr. Borowski - yes, Mr. Jahns - yes,

Mrs. Duncan - yes, Mr. Frey – yes and Mayor Pedro - yes. The motion passed

unanimously

A. ORDINANCE 06-26 A ZONING CHANGE FROM PLANNED BUSINESS

PARK (PBP) TO INDUSTRIAL DISTRICT (M-1) FOR THE PROPERTY

LOCATED AT 7551 DUTCH RD


First Reading Scheduled for J June 8, 2026; Second Reading Scheduled for

June 22, 2026; Third Reading and Legislative Action scheduled for J July ly 27, 7, 2026

Thomas Duvall, property owner, has submitted a request to rezone 3.9 acres of land

from Planned Business Park District (PBP) to (M-1) Light Industrial District for the

property located at 7551 Dutch Road. The Duvall parcel was recently split off from the

re-plat of the former Fallen Timbers Business Park which is now known as the

"Riverbend Flex-Industrial Park". Since the Duvall property is no longer located in the

Riverbend Industrial Park Plat, is less than the required 15 acres in size and is no

longer under the development standards of the business park, the parcel is now a "non

conforming use of land".  In order to allow for the future development of this property

in compliance with the City's zoning ordinance, Mr. Duvall has requested a zoning

change to (M-1) Light Industrial District. The requested (M-1) zoning is compatible

with the parcel's current use, the future (M-1) uses permitted in the adjacent business

park and conforms with the goals of the recently completed 2025 Comprehensive Plan.

At their June 1, 2026, public meeting, it was the recommendation of the Planning

Commission by a vote of 4-0, with one member absent, to approve the proposed zoning

change request from Planned Business Park District (PBP) to (M-1) Light Industrial

District for the property located at 7551 Dutch Road.

B. ORDINANCE 07-26 AN ORDINANCE AUHORIZING THE PURCHASE OF

CERTAIN REAL PROPERTY OWNED BY THE STATE OF OHIO TO THE

CITY OF WATERVILLE AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

This Ordinance would authorize the City of Waterville to enter into an agreement

with the State of Ohio for the purchase of property which would include the Roche

de Boeuf Interurban Bridge. The emergency measure is requested due to the

scheduled demolition of the bridge by the Ohio Department of Transportation.

Rex Childers, 450 Cedar Ln., provided a power point presentation on the benefits to the

Waterville community of saving and restoring the Roche de Boeuf Bridge for public

use. Former Mayor Dave Myerholtz also provided comments regarding the proposed

restoration project. John Pautz and William Vermes, P.E. of “This Old Bridge, LLC”

also answered questions from Council regarding the “partial demolition” process of the

bridge proposed by ODOT.

Mayor Pedro made a motion to suspend the rules to allow for the passage of Ordinance

07-26 as an emergency measure. Mr. Sargent seconded the motion.

Roll Call: Mayor Pedro - yes, Mrs. Duncan – yes, Mr. Borowski – yes, Mr. Frey– yes,

Mr. Wagner – yes, Mr. Sargent – yes and Mr. Jahns – yes. The motion passed

unanimously.

Mr. Pedro made a motion to move for the passage of Ordinance 07-26 as an emergency

measure subject to the recommended revisions to the Memorandum of Understanding

(MOU) and the Purchase Agreement by the Law Director. Mr. Wagner seconded the

motion.

Roll Call: Mr. Wagner – – yes, Mr. Frey – – yes, Mr. Borowski – – yes, Mr. Jahns – – yes,

Mrs. Duncan – – yes, Mr. Sargent – – yes and Mayor Pedro – – yes.  The motion passed
