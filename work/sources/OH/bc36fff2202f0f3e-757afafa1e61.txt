Brimfield Township Board of Trustees Regular Meeting Monday October 19, 2026


The Brimfield Township Board of Trustees met in a regular meeting at the

Brimfield Townhall on the above date. Present were Trustees, Kevin Scott, Sue Fields

and Fiscal Officer Jasmine Golden. Absent was Trustee Nicholas Coia.

The meeting was called to order at 7:00 PM by Trustee Kevin Scott.

Meeting Guest: Shirley Mars – presented a proposal to replace the deteriorated


Nativity display in Town Center Park with a new 13-piece fiberglass set, with figures

approximately 53–54 inches tall. The project has received support from local churches

and community organizations, and the display has been ordered for installation before

the annual tree lighting. A township resident advanced the purchase cost of $5,523, and

another resident volunteered to construct a stable. She requested the Township’s

assistance with the project by granting permission to place the display in Town Center

Park, allowing Road Department employees to assist with set-up, considering storage of

the display during the off-f-season, and permitting a dedication ceremony following

installation. Community donations are being solicited from individuals, businesses, and

organizations to reimburse the purchase cost. Donations will be processed through

Brimfield Methodist Church and will be tax-deductible. She invited trustees and other

community members to contribute to the project.
