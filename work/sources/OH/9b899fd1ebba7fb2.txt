OPEN FORUM 1:

Joe Balanda addressed the Council and thanked them for their service. He continued stating the
community spoke clearly in support of Sasha at the recent election. He made points to S.
Chetyrkina-Miller's contributions to the Council. He added that what is important to him is that


the Council can work together, respecting each other's viewpoints. Mr. Balanda talked about
live-streaming meetings and how many in the community would like this option.

Tom Bevin thanked the Council members for their service. He asked the members to put the last
Council meeting in the past and work together for the betterment of the community. Mr. Bevin
wanted to address the issue of mattresses that are in a yard on Hines Hill Rd., and what can be
done to clean them up. He stated the Village residents should be embarrassed by this mess. Mr.
Maccarone indicated the Village is trying to get the owner to clean it up. Fines have been levied,
and he has instructed the Public Works Department to take care of it.

Mark Miller thanked the Council and wanted to say he feels the new Council people will doa
great job. He stated he thought the Council got stuck in the “maze”, and that blaming, negativity,
bickering, and false rumors get everybody nowhere. He would also like to see live streaming as
well. He went on to say people need to take personal responsibility to heal these relationships.

NEW BUSINESS:

S. Chetyrkina-Miller asked if there is a littering sign or a way to enforce less littering?
Marshal Pitchford, Solicitor, stated there are rules on the books with respect to littering and also
State laws. Marshal also included that there is a catch-all in the traffic code to issue citations if
needed. He continued to explain that there should be a level of degree; anything that doesn’t have
a specific level attached is automatically a minor misdemeanor. S. Chetyrkina-Miller stated she
was not asking for action but wanted to bring it up to consider if there’s any additional action, like
a survey, they would want to do to see if putting up signs would be an effective way to discourage
this activity. Mr. Maccarone discussed that the road crew usually takes care of trash weekly, either
by phone call or driving around and observing trash to be collected.

SECOND OPEN FORUM:

Neil Kasmarcik wanted to say he has two (2) five-gallon buckets full of trash next to two(2) no
parking signs by his residence, and they have been there for three(3) weeks. He stated they were
dropped off during the night, and if the road crew is out checking, why are they still there? Mr.
Maccarone asked him if he had contacted anyone, they might feel it’s someone’s personal property
and don’t want to remove it. It will be taken care of now that he brought it to someone's attention.
