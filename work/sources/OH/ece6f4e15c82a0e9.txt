The Council Meeting was called to order at 7:02 p.m. by Mayor Richard Patrick with Council President Tom
Hardesty, Rich Beatty, John E. Brachna, Sheri Johnson, Fred Ahrens, Jeff Kaiser, William Mason, Donna Love,
Keith Whan, and Brian Cain, in attendance. Also in attendance were two representatives from Equitable
Advisors, Cameron Shank and Sydney Woolard.

This meeting is being recorded to facilitate the written minutes. Once the minutes are approved, the recording
will be erased,

Moved by Beatty, seconded by Kaiser to approve the meeting minutes from July 8, 2026.
VOTE AYES — Unanimous

Cameron Shank and Sydney Woolard of Equitable Advisors talked to Council about offering the employees
another option for retirement. They would like the opportunity to meet with employees and to set up payroll
deduction for employees. Equitable Advisors has had James A Garfield School District as a customer for over
twenty years. They will offer retirement strategy for employees getting close to retirement. Council approved
the meeting with employees and Mason will look at the law for setting up payroll deduction.

Moved by Beatty, seconded by Johnson to accept the financial reports and bills paid for July 2026.
VOTE AYES — Unanimous

PUBLIC PARTICIPATION:

At the July 8, 2026, Council meeting, Lt. Keith Whan supplied Council with a handout that had ORC traffic laws
about the operation of low-speed, under speed, utility vehicle or a mini-truck and copies of other communities
laws regarding traffic laws for off-street vehicles. He and Mason are still working on the new ordinance and
Whan hopes to present it to Council at the September Council meeting.

ROUNDTABLE:


Brachna — The flashing pedestrian light for the Headwaters Trail and State Route 88 is scheduled to be installed

in September.

Brachna had supplied to the Council packets quotes to have a traffic study done at the intersection of Main,
Water and Center Streets. Woolpert’s quote was $14,950.00, EMH&T quote was $13,000.00. Brachna stated
that due to the increased foot traffic from the amphitheater, the study needs to be completed. Ahrens stated
that the intersection of Maple and Center should be included in the study. Beatty said that the study is solving a

problem that does not exist.

Moved by Brachna, seconded by Hardesty to move forward with the study and accept the bid from EMH&T for
$13,000.00.

VOTE AYES — Hardesty, Brachna, Kaiser

VOTE NAYES—Johnson, Ahrens, Beatty

Mayor Patrick broke the tie with AYE

Hardesty — Stated he applied for a $5000.00 grant for Park Cemetery. It would be used at the Veteran’s
Memorial. He is waiting to hear if the Village will receive it.

Hardesty would like approval to bid on curbing on the streets that are set to be paved in 2027, Harris, Crestwood
and French. The curbing will help to preserve the paving on the streets. The estimated cost is $300,000.00.

Moved by Hardesty, seconded by Brachna to take bids for curbing on Crestwood, Harris and French streets and
allocating $300,000.00 for the project.
VOTE AYES — Unanimous
