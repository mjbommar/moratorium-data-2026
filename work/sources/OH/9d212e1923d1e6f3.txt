ORDINANCE NO. 2026-39

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A MORATORIUM ON ACCEPTING FOR FILING AND CONSIDERATION,
AND/OR ISSUING ZONING AND/OR OCCUPANCY PERMITS FOR ANY BUILDING, STRUCTURE, USE, OR
CHANGE OF USE INVOLVING DATA CENTERS AS A PRINCIPAL USE FOR A PERIOD NOT TO EXCEED
TWELVE (12) MONTHS IN THE CITY OF WICKLIFFE, OHIO; AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Ohio Constitution, Charter, and local ordinances, Wickliffe City Council possesses the inherent
power to enact appropriate planning, zoning, building, and business regulation laws that further the health, safety, welfare,
comfort and peace of its citizens, including restricting, prohibiting and/or regulating certain business uses; and

WHEREAS, “Data Centers,” for purposes of this moratorium, shall be defined as a “physical facility housing computers and/or
networking equipment that is principally used for data processing and storage”; and

WHEREAS, the City Administration is recommending that a moratorium herein be adopted to allow the City to review its
Codified Ordinances (and any applicable state law) to determine if it is necessary to adopt any regulations including, but not
limited to, restricting, prohibiting, and/or regulating “Data Centers” within the City of Wickliffe, Ohio; and

WHEREAS, this Council desires to enact a moratorium on accepting for filing and consideration and/or issuing zoning and/or
occupancy permits for any building, structure, use, or change of use that involves Data Centers as a use in the City for a period
of twelve (12) months.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WICKLIFFE,
COUNTY OF LAKE, STATE OF OHIO:

SECTION 1. Wickliffe City Council declares the above findings to be true and correct.

SECTION 2. Wickliffe City Council hereby establishes a temporary moratorium on accepting for filing and consideration
and/or issuing zoning and/or occupancy permits for any building, structure, use, or change of use that involves Data Centers as
a principal use in the City for a period of twelve (12) months.

SECTION 3. The moratorium shall be in effect for a period of twelve (12) months from the effective date of this Ordinance or
until changes are enacted to amend the Codified Ordinances of the City of Wickliffe to address the issues described herein or
until Wickliffe City Council approves legislation explicitly revoking this moratorium, whichever occurs first.

SECTION 4. No existing business in the City may expand in any way that would involve Data Centers as a use in the City for
the duration of the moratorium period established hereby.

SECTION 5._The purpose of this moratorium is to allow the City Administration and Council to consider whether it is prudent
to introduce amendments to pertinent Codified Ordinances and to prepare regulations which may be necessary, and to study
where and whether to allow, limit or to prohibit Data Centers as a principal use in the City.

SECTION 6. That it is hereby found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the
passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any
of its committees that resulted in such formal actions were in meetings open to the public in compliance with all legal
requirements, including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 7. That this ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of
the public peace, health and safety of said city and for the additional reason of allowing the City of Wickliffe the time to quickly
and responsibly begin to study, analyze, review, and, possibly, establish regulations for Data Centers as a principal use in the
City; wherefore, this ordinance, provided it receives an affirmative vote of two thirds (2/3) of the members elected to Council,
shall take effect immediately upon its passage and approval by the Mayor; otherwise, it shall take effect and be in force from and
after the earliest period allowed by law.
