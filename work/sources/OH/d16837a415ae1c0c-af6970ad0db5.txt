AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM NOT TO EXCEED

ONE YEAR ON THE GRANTING/ISSUING OF ANY PERMITS FOR ARTIFICIAL

INTELLIGENCE DATA CENTERS WITHIN THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF THE CITY

OF ATHENS; AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY.

WHEREAS, the City of Athens is a municipal corporation organized and

existing under the Constitution and laws of the State of Ohio, possessing all powers of

local self-government granted by Article XVIII, Section 3, of the Ohio Constitution; and

WHEREAS, the Council of the City of Athens has the authority to enact zoning

regulations and land use controls pursuant to Ohio Revised Code Sections 713.06

through 713.14, including the power to regulate the location, construction, and use of

buildings and structures in the interest of promoting the public health, safety,

convenience, comfort, prosperity, and general welfare; and

WHEREAS, artificial intelligence (AI) data centers are large-scale facilities that

house computing infrastructure designed to support machine learning, neural network

training, large language model operations, and other artificial intelligence processing

activities, and such facilities typically consume extraordinary amounts of electrical

power, generate significant heat requiring extensive cooling systems, impose

substantial demands on water resources, and create unique land use impacts; and

WHEREAS, the rapid development and deployment of AI data center facilities

nationwide has created urgent land use planning challenges for municipalities,

including but not limited to impacts on electrical grid capacity and reliability, water

supply and wastewater infrastructure, traffic and transportation systems, noise and

vibration, cybersecurity and public safety considerations, and compatibility with

surrounding residential, commercial, and institutional uses; and

WHEREAS, the City of Athens currently lacks comprehensive zoning

regulations, performance standards, and site plan review criteria specifically

addressing the unique characteristics and impacts of AI data centers, and the existing

zoning code provisions applicable to general industrial or commercial uses may not

adequately protect the public health, safety, and welfare in relation to this emerging

land use category; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds that immediate action is necessary to prevent the

establishment of AI data centers while the City conducts a comprehensive study of the

land use, infrastructure, environmental, economic, and community impacts of such

facilities and develops appropriate regulations to address them; and

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE SERVICE-SAFETY DIRECTOR TO SUBMIT

AN APPLICATION AND ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT FOR A STATE

INFRASTRUCTURE BANK (SIB) LOAN FOR THE STATE ROUTE 682 MAJOR

REHABILITATION PROJECT; AND DESIGNATING A DEDICATED REPAYMENT

SOURCE FOR THE LOAN.

WHEREAS, small city State Infrastructure Bank (SIB) loans offer a zero percent

interest rate, for up to $500,000, for a loan term of five (5) years or less, fully

amortized; and

WHEREAS, Ordinance 59-26 authorized an LPA/Federal Local-Let Project

agreement with ODOT for major rehabilitation of the State Route 682 Project;

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF ATHENS, OHIO:

SECTION I: The Service-Safety Director is hereby authorized to submit an

application and enter into a small city State Infrastructure Bank (SIB) loan agreement

for the State Route 682 Major Rehabilitation Project in the amount of up to Four

Hundred Ninety-Nine Thousand Dollars ($499,000.00).

SECTION II: The 2026 Appropriation Ordinance 120-25 is hereby amended by

appropriating from the unappropriated balance the following sums:

Two Thousand Dollars ($2,000.00) to Street Rehabilitation Fund, 572, T.C. 500, for the

loan origination fee; and

Four Hundred Ninety-Nine Thousand Dollars ($499,000.00) to Small Cities ODOT

Fund, 589, T.C. 500, and increasing the total appropriations by said amounts.

SECTION III:  The dedicated source of the loan repayment will be derived from

Small Cities ODOT Fund, 589.

SECTION IV:  This Ordinance shall be in effect and full force upon passage and

approval by the Mayor.
