SERVICE COMMITTEE ~ Mr. Peters discussed the previously approved Dora Ordinance. He said the ordinance
limits the city to.six events per year. He said there had been some complaints from local establishments. He
believes it was not their intention to limit the events to just six per year. The days and hours were to be Thursday
through Sunday throughout the year with the exception of events such as the Apple Fest, 4" of July...etc. The
wording was not clear on what an event is. If the event needed street closures, they would need to go through a
permit process in which the PD could decline if they wished to. However, he didn’t feel they had meant to limit the
events to six. He would bring that for discussion at the next meeting.

NEW BUSINESS~ There was a BOZA meeting this evening. The committee granted two variances. Another
meeting is scheduled for Monday, July 27, 2026 at 6:45PM.

Mrs. Hinsch expressed her concern for all of the low hanging limbs through out town. They are hindering walkers.
Many require the pedestrian to duck under or walk out in the street to avoid them, and some are causing site line
issues. It is a safety concern. Mayor Evans said he will have his tree trimmers look into it.

Mr. Bopp said he is upset with the number of stray cats around town. We have money placed in the budget to take
care of this problem, but it isn’t helping. Mayor Evans said there has been an improvement since they began the
program of catching them, having them spayed or neutered, and then releasing them. Mr. Bopp can contact the
group who has been working on this, to let them know to focus in his neighborhood.

Mrs. Hinsch made a motion to adjourn, with a second from Mrs. Colby. In a voice vote, all members
agreed. Motion Carried.
