RESOLUTION NO. 2026.12


A RESOLUTION IMPOSING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A DATA CENTER, INCLUDING THE DEVELOPMENT AND CONSTRUCTION OF ANY BUILDING OR STRUCTURE, APPROVAL OF ZONING CERTIFICATE OF USE, CHANGE OF USE OR REZONING THAT WOULD ALLOW DATA CENTERS THROUGH JANUARY  31, 2027 IN ORDER TO ALLOW THE CITY ADMINISTRARATION AND CITY COUNCIL TIME TO REVIEW APPPPLPLICABLE FEDERARAL CODES, OHIO STATUTES, CODES AND ND REREGULATIONS ALONG WITH THE CITY'S CODIFIED ORDINANCES AND ZONING CODE REGULATIONS RELATIVE TO SUCH ACTIVIVITY


WHEREAS, pursuant to the Constitution of the State of Ohio, the Ohio Revised Code and

the City of SuSununbnbuburury ry Charter provide that municipalities have the power to enact laws that are for

the health, safety, and welfare of their citizens which may include restricting areas used for

certain businesses and trades; and

WHEREAS, this Council finds it to be in the best interest of the health, safety, and

general welfare of the citizens of SuSununbnbuburury ry to adopt a moratorium through January 31, 2027 on

the establishment, development, approval of a certificate of sue or change of use or rezonings

that would facilitate or development or construction of data centers within the City, while it

undertakes a review and/or study of the ordinances of the City of SuSununbnbubururyry, including zoning,

building, and businessregulation sections ofthe SuSununbnbubururyryCodified Ordinancesto assure thatsuch

facilities are properly zoned, safe for the community, properly constructed to mitigate the

potential harmful impact such facilities can have on residential and commercial areas; and

WHEREAS, City Council wishes to study the impact of data centers on the public health and

safety of surrounding neighborhoods and consider and study the benefits that a data center may

bring to the community. Groups have raised concerns about property values, noise, health,

safety, the power grid, the environment and water usage in areas surrounding these fafacilities.

WHEREAS, this Council will be able to enact reasonable regulations to protect the health,

safety, welfare, peace, and comfort of the citizens of the City of SuSununbnbuburury ry more effectively if a

moratorium is in place.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED  by the Council for the City of Sunbury, State of Ohio, that:

SECTION I - That the Council of the City of SuSununbnbuburury ry hereby imposes an moratorium

t h r o u g h J a n u a r y 3 1 , 2 0 2 7on the consideration, approval or authorization of any

licenses, permits, permitted uses, special uses, accessory uses or variances that would enable

the of the granting of any permits, allow establishment, construction, conversion of other

structures into data centers, or commencement of construction of data centers within the City of

SuSununbnbubururyry. Said moratorium extends to the submission, consideration or approval of all applications for

licenses, permits, general development plans, final development plans, special or accessory use permits,

zoning certificates, occupancy permits, building permits or rezoning to Limited Industrial District (L-I).

Data centers are defined for this moratorium as AI data centers, enterprise data centers, managed services

data centers colocation data centers, cloud data centers or edge data centers as well as physical facilities

that organizations use to store their computing machines and related hardware that exceed 5,000 square

feet in size. This moratorium includes construction of structures that house information technology (IT)

systems infrastructure like servers, data storage drives, and network equipment and construction of

accompanying accessory uses such as utilities, external mechanical equipment, water towers, and security

facilities that may be located on the same site of a data center. This moratorium does not apply to repair or

replacement of existing data centers or the data centers contained in existing businesses within the City

of Sununbnbubururyry,if if ananyny.

SECTION II - That this moratorium shall become effective on the date that this resolution,

following its passage by the Council, shall remain in effect following the date upon which it became

effective until January 31, 2027 unless extended upon a majority vote of the Council, if the Council

finds such continuance is required to allow sufficient time for the administration to complete its study

and recommend what action, if any, the City of SuSununbnbuburury ry should take to safeguard the public health,

safety and welfare through the provision of adequate planning, zoning, land use, or other regulatory

controls that are specifically applicable to activities covered under this resolution. City Council may

also lift the Moratorium prior to its expiration.

SECTION III- That this moratorium suspends and tollsthe SuSununbnbuburury ry Codified Ordinances, any

time periods prescribed by law, within which any official or official body of the City of SuSununbnbubururyry, is

required to take action upon an application for a permit, license, special use permit, building permit, site

plan approval or the like, applications for rezoning to Limited Industrial District (L-I) for data centers and

no such application shall be deemed to be approved by the failure of any other public official or official

body to approve or deny said application during the time when this moratorium shall be in effect.

SECTION IV - That the City Manager is hereby directed to begin a study to recommend what

action, if any, the City of SuSununbnbuburury ry should take to preserve the public health, safety and welfare through

the provision of adequate planning, zoning, land-use, or other regulatory controls that are specifically

applicable to data centers.

SECTION V - If any section, phrase, sentence, or portion of this resolution isfor any reason

held invalid or unconstitutional by any Court of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed a

separate, distinct, and independent provision, and such holding shall not affect the validity of the

remaining portions thereof.

SECTION VI -That it is found and determined that all formal actions of the Council of the City

of SuSununbnbuburury ry concerning and relating to the adoption of this resolution were taken in an open meeting of the

Council of the City of SuSununbnbuburury ry and that all deliberations of this City's Council and any ofits committees

that resulted in those formal actions were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal

requirements.

WHEREFORE, pursuant to Section 4.07 of the Charter of the City of Sunbury, this Resolution

shall be in effect immediately after its passage.
