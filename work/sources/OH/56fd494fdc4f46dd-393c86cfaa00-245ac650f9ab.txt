COMMITTEE REPORTS:

Mr. Semick reports on the Port Authority meeting. Most of the meeting pertained to their marketing plan.
They’d like to draw more people to the City. They discussed possibly bringing the kayak launch rental in house
so they are exploring running that themselves.

Mr. Kasunick asks if the previous vendor for rentals is backing out?
Mr. Semick says no, it’s not settled yet. They are just exploring what all it would take for them to take it over.

Ms. Schmidt says they had cages with kayaks at a place she visited. You texted your payment and they gave you
a code to unlock it and it was a neat self service station.

Mr. Semick says he will bring that up to them. He also says there was a lot of discussion over a grant that was
applied for. There was concern over the boat launch ramp and the use of it for motorized boats. The river in that
area cannot support that. Will that grant be modified?

Ms. Schindel says the grant was required to be submitted 24 hours after his question. She believes it is limited
by horsepower. It is not for all boats. She does not remember exactly what the horsepower number was. On this
grant, should we be awarded the whole thing, we can choose what we want to accept and what we do not. We
had to get the application in due to timing. If that line item is not acceptable, we can just refuse it and take the
rest.

Mr. Semick says hopefully the City will work with the Port Authority to make the best of that money should we
receive it.

Ms. Schmidt reports on ECDC. They finished up the year. They are doing their lantern for the business. She
thanks Mayor Overstreet and Ms. Schindel for allowing the purchase and navigating the processes required for
that. She thanks Ms. Schindel for all of her help. They will have an Organizational meeting end of January. Last
day to submit a business is December 17. They will deliver the lantern on December 20 to the winner. An email
will go out.

RECOGNITION OF THE PUBLIC: 1/2 hour, 3-minutes per person

Mr. Marr, a Timberlake resident comes to speak. He is the Mayor of Timberlake. He expresses full support for
the moratorium that is being considered tonight. Data center risks are real. He shares an example of why other
cities are hitting pause on these data centers. It pertains to noise and frequency. There is also environmental
harm. The water has to be treated with chemicals. These chemicals get discharged back into the environment.
This poses threats to the Lake. There is also the sheer amount of power that they require. Tonight’s moratorium
is the right choice and it buys the time to measure the risks. It is not anti-business. He supports this 100% and
thanks Eastlake for taking this step.

Heather Freeman, 1252 E 357 St, thanks Council for doing a moratorium pertaining to the data centers. She
thinks that the City can zone them out and prohibit certain uses in the City. She asks about the Ordinance
creating 521.11 pertaining to the Nuisance Ordinance. She reviewed it. She is wondering if the Community
knows about what the purpose of it was. She is also perplexed why the Safety Director is the one who
determines that it is a nuisance. It has to go back to the same person. That doesn’t seem right. She challenges
Council to take a look and see if there’s a different way. What about poorly maintained structures? There are a
lot of things in Willowick’s proposed Ordinance that we should look at. She asks that this item goes through 3
readings. Let the community know what the purpose is. It sounds like you're trying to get money back for going
out on these calls. If someone owns a property where there was a felony, how do you abate that nuisance and
charge them? She does not understand. How do you abate a person who made a bad decision that lives in your
City? You want that money back? Maybe someone from Ordinance Committee can shed light on that. She
addresses Mr. Gulley as the Ordinance Chair. She thinks this needs more work. Where is this coming from?
