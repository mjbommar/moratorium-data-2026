The Washington Township Board of Trustees regularly scheduled meeting held at Washington Township

Administration Building, 6200 Eiterman Road, Dublin, Ohio, on September 8, 2026, at 6:00 p.m., with

Mr. Charles Kranstuber, Chairman, calling the meeting to order, and it opened with the Pledge of

Allegiance.

Mr. Stu Harris, Vice Chair – Present

Mr. Jan Rozanski, Trustee – Present

Franklin County Deputy Sheriff’s Report:


Deputy Burton Haren with the Franklin County Sheriff’s Office (FCSO) submitted a written and verbal

report to the Trustees for the period of August 11 through September 82026, including the following

,

updates:

• Deputy Haren expressed his appreciation for being able to return to his routine of attending the

Trustee meetings following his special assignment.

• During this period, there were twenty-three (23) Total Calls for Service / Self-Initiated Activity,

four (4) Total Reports Taken, and six (6) Total Arrests / Citations:

o The FCSO Detective Bureau is investigating fraudulent charges made using a resident’s

credit card information in Springboro.

o Deputy Haren has been monitoring speeds on Cosgray Road. He noted that since

Hayden Run Road has reopened, he has stopped only one individual for a speed

violation on Cosgray Road.

o Deputy Haren noted that he stopped a vehicle that failed to stop at a bus stop and has

increased his presence on local bus routes now that school has resumed.

o Deputy Haren reported that the FCSO coordinated a traffic enforcement operation with

the Ohio State Highway Patrol, Hilliard Police Department and Columbus Police

Department last month on the north and west sides of I-270. The operation resulted in

more than three hundred (300) citations being issued, many of which were for speeds

exceeding 100 miles per hour.

• Mr. Harris relayed to Deputy Haren a resident’s appreciation for his enforcement efforts in the

area of Cosgray Road.  Append #001

Opportunity for Residents to Speak:


Mr. Gene Bostic provided a brief update on local cemetery projects. The Monterey Square development

proposal has been approved by the Dublin Planning and Zoning Commission. He noted that the Shell

service station will be removed as part of the project. Mr. Bostic will address with City Council a previous

memo from the City Manager that depicted a scattering garden rather than the columbaria currently

proposed for the Dublin Cemetery expansion area.

The Memorial Preserve at St. John Lutheran Church has also completed review by the Planning and

Zoning Commission and is scheduled for review by Dublin City Council on September 14. Finally, Mr.

Bostic may request a City Council agenda item to discuss Dublin-owned land as a potential location for a

new cemetery.

Mr. Kranstuber requested that Mr. Bostic inform the Trustees when he plans to appear before City

Council so that the Trustees may provide supporteither in the form of another letter to Council or by

Resolution to Extend Data Center Development Moratorium within the Unincorporated Territory of the Township: Discussion:


Responding to Mr. Kranstuber, Mr. Richter confirmed the extension will allow additional time to

coordinate updates to the Zoning Resolution and monitor the progress of any potential legislative action

that could supersede local authority.

Motion:


Mr. Rozanski introduced and moved the adoption of the following Resolution:

RESOLUTION EXTENDING DATA CENTER DEVELOPMENT MORATORIUM WITHIN THE UNINCORPORATED TERRITORY OF THE TOWNSHIP


(Ohio Revised Code Section 519.02)

WHEREAS, the Board approved a comprehensive land use plan for the Township in November 2005 (the

“Land Use Plan”);

WHEREAS, the Board has determined that large scale data centers are inconsistent with residential land

uses as outlined in the Land Use Plan by requiring heavy water and electric service, causing air and noise

pollution and posing significant fire and emergency medical issues;

WHEREAS, the Board on December 9, 2025 voted to approve a 90-day moratorium on the approval or

construction of large scale data centers within the Township, expiring March 9, 2026, and on February

10, 2026 the Board voted to further extend a six month moratorium to September 5, 2026;

WHEREAS, House Bill 646 is pending as emergency legislation in the Ohio General Assembly to create a

state-level data center study commission to examine the impacts of data centers on the environment,

electrical grid, water usage, noise and light pollution, local economy and farmland use, among other

issues;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Township Trustees of the Township of Washington,

Franklin, Delaware and Union Counties, Ohio, that:

Section 1: The Board hereby extends its large scale data center (Data Center Tiers 2-4)

development moratorium six months, from September 8, 2026 to March 9, 2027.

Section 2: It is hereby found and determined that all formal actions of this Board concerning and

relating to the passage of this Resolution were taken in an open meeting of this Board,

and that all deliberations of this Board and of any of its committees that resulted in

such formal action were in meetings open to the public in compliance with all legal

requirements, including Ohio Revised Code Section 121.22.

Mr. Kranstuber seconded the motion, and the roll was called, and all members of the Washington

Township Trustees who were present voting Aye. Resolution adopted September 8, 2026.

A Resolution Accepting the Human Resource Manager’s Recommendation and Issuing Discipline


Against Firefighter Anthony J. Harding: Discussion:


Ms. Grossman recommended that the Township issue discipline against Washington Township Fire

Department Firefighter A.J. Harding, including unpaid leave, due to his expired Fire and Paramedic

certifications.

Motion:


A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING THE HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGER’S RECOMMENDATION AND ISSUING


DISCIPLINE AGAINST FIREFIGHTER ANTHONY J. HARDING


WHEREAS, on September 1, 2026, Fire Administration became aware that Anthony J. Harding’s fire and

paramedic certifications lapsed on July 22, 2026, and;

WHEREAS, State of Ohio certified firefighters and paramedics are required to complete the continuing

education requirements within each certification cycle to maintain active certification, and;

WHERES, the Township’s Employee Handbook, approved on December 10, 2024, and updated in

September 2025, states:

As required by the State of Ohio, and the Township, all Fire Department personnel certified as

Paramedics or Emergency Medical Technicians (for part-time personnel), shall perform only those

procedures for which they are currently certified. Knowledge of certain procedures without current

certification does not constitute sufficient reason for engaging in actions. Engaging in actions without

certification may be grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

WHEREAS, possessing active Fire and Paramedic certifications is a requirement of Anthony J. Harding’s

position as a Washington Township Firefighter / Paramedic, and;

WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of Washington Township reviewed Human Resource Manager

Catherine Grossman’s findings and deliberated this issue in open session and now accepts the Human

Resource Manager’s recommendation and issue discipline against Firefighter Anthony J. Harding that

includes unpaid leave effective September 3, 2026, until his certifications are reinstated by the State of

Ohio Division of EMS.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Trustees of Washington Township, Franklin,

Delaware, and Union Counties, State of Ohio:

Section 1: Pursuant to the authority granted by the Washington Township Employee Handbook,

the Washington Township Trustees decrees Anthony J. Harding beginning leave

without pay on September 3, 2026, starting at 8:00 a.m. EST until his required State of

Ohio Firefighter and Paramedic certifications are reinstated and he is eligible to

resume the duties of his position. Additionally, Anthony J. Harding may not

supplement accrued paid time off for any unpaid leave and will not accrue sick and/or

vacation leave.

Section 2: It is hereby found and determined that all formal actions of this Board concerning and

relating to the passage of this Resolution were adopted in an open meeting of the

Board and that all deliberations of the Board and any of the decision making bodies of

the Board of Trustees which resulted in such formal actions were in meetings open to

the public in compliance with all legal requirements of the State of Ohio.

Mr. Harris seconded the motion, and the roll was called, and all members of the Washington Township

Trustees who were present voting Aye. Resolution adopted September 8, 2026. #2026.09.08.023
