COMMUNICATIONS FROM THE PUBLIC — Mr. Aaron Burnette, 4009 Greenwich Rd,
Norton OH 44203. Mr. Burnette complimented Chief McShane and Sergeant Sams on their
professionalism in dealing with an investigation. He noted that the outstanding issue of his
private property is also being resolved. Mr. Burnette said that he was recently reported for
stalking while watching his son playing football at Manchester. He researched the Crime
Victim Services reports and learned that individuals reporting stalking instances can obtain
money for working with the police and said that Summit County paid out $343,000 last year
in claims. Mr. Burnette reported he went to Victim’s Assistance in Akron and was informed
that the most common type of fraud is Victim’s Assistance, Catholic Charities, and the Ohio
Domestic Violence Network. He questioned if the City of Norton has a policy or procedure
for addressing the filing of false reports for claims.

COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE

Personnel & Rules Mr. Lukens explained the legislation pertaining to data centers and all of
the areas that have been addressed. He remarked on the noise amendment and cross
referenced those in the City Charter. Mr. Harbert noted that the purpose of this amendment
is to amend the Section 1298 (Conditional Uses) of the Codified Ordinances related to large-
scale data centers is to include the phrase “and data mining operations.” Mr. DeHarpart said
that the data centers are now referring to data mining operations as an additional safeguard.
Mr. Tousley said that data mining is also known as crypto mining and this information was
brought forward by Norton resident, Mr. Yasco. Mr. DeHarpart said he agrees that the
names of the operations will continue to change over time and the City of Norton needs to
change along with them. Mr. Lukens moved to add Amended Ordinance No. 61-2026 to
tonight’s agenda with the intent to waive readings; seconded by Ms. Lee.

TOPICS FOR NEXT MEETING — Monday, September 21, 2026 — Mr. DeHarpart
reported the public dumping areas on Wadsworth Road are filling up and he questioned if
there should be legislation identifying where this type of activity can be located. Mr.
DeHarpart requested Administration to investigate the zoning code and offer suggestions.

NON-AGENDA ITEMS

Mr. Lukens noted that e-bikes are becoming problematic with near accidents and he believes
these occurrences are getting worse. Mr. Lukens has spoken with Chief McShane and it was
suggested that there could be a public meeting with e-bike providers to offer training. If
there is an opportunity to help the riders follow basic traffic laws, it could be useful. Mr.
DeHarpart suggested the Police Chief be allowed to determine what is necessary for
managing this concern and working within his network of safety professionals.

Mr. Harbert announced an informational meeting on the proposed Charter amendments on
Wednesday, September 30, 2026, 6:00 — 7:30 p.m. at the Norton Library Community Room.
