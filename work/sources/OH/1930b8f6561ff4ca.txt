B) OR54-2026 — Second Reading

Staff recommends motion to approve: A second reading of prohibiting public urination within
the Village of Ada.

SUMMARY: The ordinance prohibits urination, or defecating, on public property, in places open
to the public, or in areas exposed to public view, except when using designated restroom
facilities. The ordinance, first brought before Council in May 2026, would establish violations to
be classified as a minor misdemeanor for a first offense, with increased penalties for repeat
violations, potentially up to a third-degree misdemeanor. The ordinance was introduced by
Councilwoman Coressel.

MOTION to move to the floor. Motion by Councilwoman Coressel, seconded by
Councilman Beck.

Council discussed whether Ordinance 54-2026 should be declared an emergency. It was noted
that the ordinance would decrease the severity of the penalty from the applicable Ohio state law
provision to a misdemeanor under Village law. Councilman Wells expressed that an emergency
declaration was not necessary at the second reading because the conduct is already prohibited
by law. Solicitor Napier explained that if an emergency declaration becomes necessary, it could
be considered at the third reading. Under the standard procedure, legislation requires three (3)
readings and does not take effect until thirty (30) days after passage. Ms. Napier went on to
explain the circumstances under which emergency legislation is appropriate, including matters
involving safety, health, and welfare of citizens or situations where an earlier effective date is
necessary.

It was agreed upon by Council to proceed with the second reading without declaring an
emergency.

MOTION to adopt the second reading of Ordinance 54-2026. Motion by Councilwoman
Coressel, seconded by Councilman Beck, and carried 6-0.

C) OR55-2026 — First Reading

Staff recommends motion to approve: An Ordinance authorizing the Mayor to assess
streetlights within the Village of Ada for year 2027.

SUMMARY: This ordinance would authorize the Mayor to assess the cost of street lighting
within the Village of Ada for 2027 at a rate of 0.75 mil of the taxable value of each benefited
property, excluding Village-owned property. The ordinance provides for collection of the
assessments through the County Auditor and establishes the procedures for payment and
collection of delinquent assessments.

7. OLD BUSINESS
A) Eric Wolber Ave. Plan Update

Ordinance 53-2026 was discussed concerning the vacation of Railroad Drive in connection with
the proposed expansion project. Jeff Oestreich of Configure, LLC, provided an update, noting
that the rail design has changed and may accommodate additional railcars, potentially reducing
the need for cars to be staged on the main line. The engineering plans are not yet complete,
and the company is working with the railroad and engineers regarding the proposed rail spur,
utilities, and site development.

Mr. Oestreich went on to say that the expansion is expected to result in higher paying, more
technical positions, although the exact number of additional jobs is not yet known. He also noted
that the current material deliveries average approximately ten (10) truckloads per week and are
expected to increase significantly with the expansion.

Council discussed postponing further action on the ordinance until the engineering work is

substantially completed. Mr. Oestreich stated that in approximately sixty (60) days, they will be
able to provide an update on whether the proposed vacation of Eric Wolber Avenue will still be
necessary. Council agreed to postpone consideration pending further engineering information.

MOTION to table OR53-2026 indefinitely. Motion by Councilman Beck, seconded by
Councilman Rowe, and carried 6-0.
