I call the following to your attention this evening:

City Calendar:
    • The Splash Pad and the Sunny Lake Park Boathouse are open. Splash Pad hours are 10
       a.m. to 8 p.m. and the Boathouse hours are weekdays noon to 8 p.m. and weekends 10
       a.m. to 8 p.m.
    • Food Truck Wednesdays began June 10 and continue on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays
       through August 26 from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Kiwanis-Moore Park.
    • Summer Concerts began June 11 and continue through August 27 on the 2nd and 4th
       Thursdays, 6:30 p.m. at Veterans Memorial Park.
    • City offices closed Friday, July 3 in observance of Independence Day.

Aurora Park: The Aurora Park IT building is going up quickly and will be followed by the
restroom and office for the Gatehouse. We are awaiting the decking for the pier. The drawings
for 1B are about 50% completed. The Law Director is working on obtaining a liquor license for
the park, which we will have to buy from another entity that has been identified by our
consultant. Perhaps most exciting is that on June 10, 2026 the Ohio legislature passed the
state's $3.7 billion capital budget (Senate Bill 450), which included $1 million allocated to the
City of Aurora for the development of the beach area as part of Phase 2A of Aurora Park.

Fourth of July: The July 4th Fun Run will start at Bicentennial Park by the library with check-in
and registration at 7 a.m., the 1 Mile at 8 a.m., and the SK at 8:30 a.m. Early packet pickup will
be the evening before, 4-6 p.m. at the Walker Building. The parade will be stepping off at 11
a.m. at the Heinen's parking lot. Our Festival will run noon to 3 p.m. at Kiwanis-Moore Park
with food trucks, carnival food, entertainment, inflatables, balloon art, character meet and
greets, and more. You can sign up for these events at Auroraoh.com. A special addition this
year, as part of the celebration of America's 250th birthday, is a concert featuring Moonshine
Groove that will take place from 7-9 p.m. at Kiwanis-Moore Park, followed by Fireworks at
dusk. West Pioneer ballfields and the road will be closed at about 9:30 p.m. from 43 to
Birch bark.

Parade Grand Marshal: I am pleased to announce that this year's Fourth of July parade Grand
Marshal is Barb Kudley. Barb recently retired after more than four decades of service in the
Aurora City Schools. Barb is a lifelong resident, former elementary school librarian, active in
numerous community organizations, and helped bring Relay for Life to Aurora. Congratulations,
Barb!
82 paving update: After several delays from ODOT, paving is now expected to begin July 6.

Purple Heart City: Portage County Auditor Matt Kelly and Steven Farhat presented the City of
Aurora and me with a plaque designating Aurora as a Purple Heart City on June 15.

New Rescue Squad: The Fire Department recently took possession of a new rescue squad that
was ordered in March 2023. The Department is preparing the squad for service with storage
bins, supplies, radios, and equipment. It should be ready to respond to calls in a few weeks.

Party in the Park 250: "Birthday Bash Tour" is the final installment of the Party in the Park
series celebrating America's 250th birthday on August 1, with the opportunity to explore
participating businesses and enjoy special giveaways or activities, collecting stamps along the
