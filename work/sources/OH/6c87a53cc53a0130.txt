                        Village of Middlefield Council Minutes


                                      July 9, 2026


Council met in regular session Mayor Garlich called the meeting to order by roll call at
7:00 p.m. with the following members present: Mayor Garlich, Mr. Scott Klein, Mr. Carl
Hornung, Ms. Kaitlyn Arganti, Mr. Sam Morrow, Mr. Bill Blue, and Mr. Kyle Kaplan.

Also present: Ms. Leslie McCoy- Village Administrator, Ms. Jessica Giardina- Fiscal
Office, Chief Tucholski, Mr. Nick Giardina- Fiscal Officer, Mr. Luke McConville- Village
Solicitor, Lt. Graley, Mr. Alex Duncan, Ms. Kim Breyley, Mr. John Boksansky- Zoning
Inspector, Ms. Sabine Weizer, Mr. Joe Svete, Ms. Jackie Janke, Lou Champney- Youth
Running Program Coordinator, 14U Softball Team & Parents, and Mr. Ben Reed- Fire
Department.

1. Opening Ceremony: Pledge of Allegiance was recited.

2. Agenda: No additions/deletions.

3. Approval of Minutes: June 11, 2026. Mr. Hornung made a motion to approve
June 11, 2026 minutes, motion seconded by Ms. Arganti. Roll Call. All in favor:
Yeas (6).

4. Payment of Bills: Mr. Klein moved to authorize payments totaling $1,062,787.71,
motion seconded by Mr. Hornung. Roll Call. All in favor: Yeas (6).

5. FISCAL OFFICER’S REPORT: Income Tax and Financials – The report is included
in the packet. Mayor Garlich stated the Village finances continue to improve and
everything is growing.

6. AMBULANCE REPORT – Report is included in the packet.

7. FIRE DEPARTMENT REPORT – Report is included in the packet. Mr. Reed stated
June was a busy month. There is an Amish Safety meeting coming up and the
department has been participating in live fire trainings and education training with
University Hospitals.

8. ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT: Ms. McCoy stated she has been communicating with
local businesses on current needs and opportunities as well as monitoring available
properties. Ms. McCoy stated she has a request for a sign for the storefront renovation
program and it exceeds the balance that the program currently has. Ms. Arganti made a
motion to add funds to the Storefront Renovation Program Fund, motion
seconded by Mr. Kaplan. Roll Call. All in favor: Yeas (6).

9. ZONING REPORT: Report is included in packet. Mr. Boksansky stated he was able to
issue 11 zoning permits for the month of June.

10. STREETS, SIDEWALKS & UTILITIES REPORT – Report is included in the packet.
Chief Tucholski stated there has been a lot of grass mowing, park maintenance, Eagle
Park work, cemetery maintenance, street sweeping, equipment repair, and more.

11. POLICE REPORT – Report is included in the packet. Chief Tucholski stated there
was 655 calls for service in the month of June. Chief Tucholski stated CPT training is
almost done and there are more trainings scheduled for Officers. Fishing with Cops is
this Saturday 9am-12pm at Mineral Lake Pond.

12. PARKS & RECREATION REPORT – Report is included in the packet. Ms. McCoy
stated Fall Sports registration is currently open and the last day to register to save is July
13, 2026. Mayor Garlich stated they are working on getting quotes to get lights installed
at the pickleball court.
