REPORTS OF STANDING COMMITTEES


Finance, Mr. Moreton- Thank you, Mr. President. Thank you to those who attended the

Community Connect last week. We had a great discussion on strategic land use for our Village

and some exciting upcoming projects. I also want to share my experience from this morning’s

Greater Cincinnati Firefighters Memorial Service. I had the privilege of attending this event for

my second year now, with this year not seeing any firefighter from St. Bernard on the last call

list. It was again a beautiful reflection on the sacrifice our fire department endures every day,

even beyond the times where they are running into danger while the rest are running away from

it, like the consequences of occupational hazards due to carcinogens and more that many

firefighters face after their service. I retell all of this to encourage others to share my appreciation

of our Fire Department and their commitment to our community. Lastly, I want to give a

reminder of an important deadline regarding the Homestead Exemption for property taxes. The

State has authorized a one-time relief in the estimated amount of $400-500 for those already

receiving the Homestead Exemption. What this means is that if you already receive the

Homestead Exemption, no action is needed from you to receive this additional relief. If you do

not currently receive this exemption but believe you may qualify, please be sure to submit your

completed application by October 16th to the County Auditor’s Office to ensure they have

enough time to fully review your application before the deadline. Thank you, Mr. President. That

concludes my report.

Service, Mr. Stuchell- Absent. Report read by Ms. Rytov. I would like to inform residents that

the Service Department will begin their annual leaf collection on Monday, October 26th.

Residents are reminded to place their leaf piles along the curb, grass verge, or edge of their front

yards, ensuring that piles do not extend into the street or obstruct sidewalks whenever possible.

For residents who have accumulated leaves prior to the start of leaf collection, please place them

in a trash can at the curb for collection during the Village’s regular yard waste pickup. We also

ask that residents refrain from raking leaves onto sidewalks until leaf collection begins operating

on October 26th.
