## Request Meeting Time

If you would like to request time on one of the Trustee meeting agendas, please contact the Fiscal Officer at [(614) 652-3920](tel:\(614\)%20652-3920) with the date and nature of your request so they can allocate time on the agenda for you to address the Trustees.
