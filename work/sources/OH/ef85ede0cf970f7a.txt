                                              RESOLUTION No. 18-26

                            RESOLUTION TO ENACT A SIX-MONTH MORATORIUM ON
                          THE RECEIPT, PROCESSING, ISSUANCE, OR APPROVAL OF ANY
                           APPLICATION FOR A ZONING PERMIT FOR A DATA CENTER


                                           RECITALS

        WHEREAS, the Board of Township Trustees of Prairie Township, Franklin County, Ohio
(the “Board”), has adopted the Prairie Township Zoning Resolution (the “Zoning Resolution”),
which contains numerous zoning districts with respect to residential, business, commercial, and
other land uses; and

        WHEREAS, the State of Ohio, including Franklin County, has experienced a recent surge
in land being used for data centers and related services; and

        WHEREAS, a recent study has shown that Ohio ranks fourth in the nation in the volume
of data centers; and

       WHEREAS, data centers and related services consume vast amounts of land, electricity,
and often water, depending upon their cooling technology, thereby creating a tremendous strain
on public resources and infrastructure; and

       WHEREAS, data centers may also have an adverse impact as a non-compatible
surrounding land use, through conditions such as noise and other related development issues;
and

       WHEREAS, House Bill 646 is currently pending in the General Assembly, which will create
a Data Center Commission to study the impacts of data centers in Ohio; and

        WHEREAS, this Board also recognizes that due to such potential demand for land in and
around Prairie Township for data centers and related services, the current Zoning Resolution
needs to be reviewed and updated to ensure proper development can occur within Prairie
Township and that Prairie Township has the necessary processes, resources, and infrastructure
in place to address such growth and demand for data centers; and

        WHEREAS, the Board, staff, and legal counsel need time to review, evaluate, revise, and
implement certain text revisions to the Zoning Resolution and to determine related planning
decisions, processes, and resources in order to address the recent growth of data center demand
in and around Prairie Township; and

        WHEREAS, the Board hereby finds that significant legitimate public interests exist and
that a temporary preservation of the status quo by suspending the receipt, processing, issuance,
or approval of any application for a Zoning Permit for a data center is necessary to achieve the
legitimate public interests and will further promote the health, safety, and general welfare of
Prairie Township and its residents.


                                         RESOLUTION

      NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Board of Trustees of Prairie Township, Franklin
County, Ohio, that:

        Section 1. Moratorium. The Board believes it is in the best interest of the Township to
institute a temporary, six (6) month moratorium until 11:59 P.M. on October 15, 2026, on the
receipt, processing, issuance, and approval of any application for a Zoning Permit for a Data
Center as that term may be interpreted in this Resolution. This moratorium is necessary to
ensure such future developments align with the intent of the Board and to ensure proper
development can occur within Prairie Township and that Prairie Township has the necessary
processes, resources, and infrastructure in place to address such growth and demand for data
centers and that such interests are a legitimate public interest necessary to protect the public
health, safety, and general welfare. The term “Data Center” as used in this Resolution, means a
large, centralized physical facility or group of facilities dedicated to housing an information
technology infrastructure, including vast networks of computer servers, storage systems, and
networking equipment, primarily designed for the storage, processing, and distribution of large
amounts of data generally by systems and/or users located elsewhere, and/or the facilities used
to maintain and/or support such infrastructure, including environmental control systems. The
Board may rescind this moratorium at any time prior to the expiration of the six (6) months.

        Section 2: Limitations for Existing Construction. For Data Centers currently under
construction, previously permitted, or demonstrating investment-backed expectations prior
to the date of this Resolution, construction may proceed in accordance with the site plans, zoning
permits, and/or conditional use permits approved by the Township.

       Section 3. Study Period. During this moratorium, the Township Zoning Commission,
planning consultants, and legal counsel shall study the impacts of data centers and recommend
any text amendments to the Zoning Resolution.

       Section 4. Open Meetings Act. The Board finds and determines that all formal actions of
this Board concerning and relating to the passage of this Resolution were taken in an open
meeting of this Board, and that all deliberations of this Board that resulted in such formal action
were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements.

      BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall be in full force and effect
immediately upon its adoption.
