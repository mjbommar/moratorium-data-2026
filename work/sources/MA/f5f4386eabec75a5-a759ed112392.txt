12. ZONING ORDINANCES (NOT YET REFERRED)

Process note: Per M.G.L. Chapter 40A, Section 5, the city council shall submit proposed zoning

ordinances to the planning board for review within fourteen days of receipt. No zoning ordinance or

amendment thereto shall be adopted until after the planning board in a city or town, and the city council

or a committee designated or appointed for the purpose by said council has each held a public hearing

thereon, together or separately.

Rule 5.2.3 provides that no ordinance shall be voted on by the City Council until it has been considered

by the Committee on Legislative Matters.
