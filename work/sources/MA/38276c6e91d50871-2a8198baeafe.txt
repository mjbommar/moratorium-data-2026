3. TR-2026-50 – A Resolution confirming the appointment of Peter Smus, 149 Elmar Drive, Feeding Hills,

MA to the Agawam License Commission to a term expiring January 1, 2029 (Sponsored by Mayor

Johnson)

Motion to approve made by Councilor Sandlin and seconded by Councilor Bitzas. Councilors Sandlin, Borgatti,

McNair, Russo, and Rossi all spoke in support to Peter Smus.

The vote was 11 YES, 0 NO approving the item.

4. TR-2026-51 - A Resolution urging the Commonwealth to eliminate the income tax on qualified

overtime pay and qualified tips (Sponsored by Councilor Bitzas) (Referred to Community Relations

Committee)

Motion to approve made by Councilor Bitzas and seconded by Council Vice President Suffriti. As Chair of the

Community Relations Committee, Councilor Bitzas provided the report stating that the committee voted 2 Yes, 3 No

to send a negative recommendation to the Full Council. Councilor Bitzas made a motion to amend the Resolution in

the Now Therefore by adding “consistent with federal law” which was seconded by Councilor Mercadante. The vote

was 9 Yes, 2 No (Councilors Rossi and Sandlin).

The vote on the main Motion as amended was 8 YES (Councilors Bitzas, Borgatti, Calabrese, McNair, Mercadante,

Rickmon, Russo, and Suffriti), 3  NO (Councilors Hendrickson, Rossi, Sandlin) approving the item as amended.

5. TR-2026-52 - A Resolution amending the Rules and Regulations of the Agawam City Council

(Sponsored by Councilors Borgatti and Calabrese) (Referred to Legislative Committee)

Motion to approve made by Councilor Mercadante and seconded by Councilors Sandlin and Suffriti. As Chair of the

Legislative Committee, Councilor Hendrickson provided the report stating that the committee sent a positive

recommendation to the Full Council. Councilors Borgatti and Calabrese spoke in favor of the Resolution.

The vote was 11 YES, 0 NO approving the item.
