August 26, 2026 Article 10 – Adoption of G.L. Ch 39, §23D, Mullins Rule – Ms. Lind, Assistant Town Manager, is


requesting the Town adopt the Mullins Rule. Many municipalities have adopted the Mullins Rule which

allows for member of an Adjudicatory/Regulatory Board of Commission to miss one meeting from a

session and submit certification that they have watched or listened to a recording of the missed session

and thereby be eligible to continue their participation in the public hearing and not be exempted from

the process due to an absence. This will cover all adjudicatory boards.

Ms. Richards asked about the certification process. Ms. Lind said a form is completed and submitted

confirming the member has watched or listened to a recording.

Mr. Malaguti asked about alternate members. Ms. Lind said the alternate member must have been a

sworn member or alternate present at the opening or initial session of that specific public hearing for

the Mullins Rule to be applicable.

Article 17 – Title V Repair Fund: Mr. Michael Silveira, Deputy Director of Planning, is requesting


to borrow $400,000 to finance the Septic System Repair Program.

Town Meeting has approved seventeen prior rounds for this greatly needed and successful

septic system improvement program. To date, this program has enabled 422 homeowners to

repair/replace their failed systems and is administered by the Office of Community

Development on behalf of the Town. $546,914 has been funded/applied for since Fall Town

Meeting 2025 – some of which was funded with leftover money allocated in 2024. Due to the

popularity of the program, the funds are often expended quickly, and the $251,347 that

remains may be expended by Spring Town Meeting 2027. Similar to previous funding rounds,

the Department of Planning and Development recommends that the Town Treasurer borrow

$400,000 from the Massachusetts Water Pollution Abatement Trust. The State provides these

funds to the Town at a 2% interest rate. The funds are made available to local homeowners for

septic system repairs, upgrades, or replacements (systems must be failed to qualify). In cases

where Town sewer is available, these funds can also be used to cover the costs associated with

sewer connections. Loans up to $35,000 are available per household at 5% interest (interest

charge covers the Town's cost of administering the program). Loans are recorded as liens on

the property and are billed by the Assessors' Office as a tax bill attachment. Terms of the loan

range from five to fifteen years.

Mr. Malaguti asked if this request was sufficient to carry till next fall. Mr. Silveira said yes.

Ms. Richards asked if the Town is advertising this towards pond front properties. Mr. Silveira

said the advertising didn’t target a specific property.

Mr. Malaguti moved to recommend Article 17 to Town Meeting. Ms. O’Connor second.

Mr. Snyder thinks this is a great program and hopes everyone supports it. Mr. Lalley and Ms.
