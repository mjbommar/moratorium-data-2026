### Ordinance No. 28,135

AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING AND PLANNING CODE, AMENDING CHAPTER 59 OF THE OKLAHOMA CITY MUNICIPAL CODE, 2020, RELATED TO SOLAR ENERGY, AMENDING ARTICLE II – DEFINITIONS, BY AMENDING SECTION 59-2150 – DEFINITIONS, TO ADD DEFINITIONS FOR EQUIPMENT, GROUND- OR BUILDING-MOUNTED AND TYPES OF SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS; AMENDING ARTICLE VI – ZONING BASE DISTRICTS, BY AMENDING SECTION 59-6100 – AGRICULTURAL AND RESIDENTIAL DISTRICTS, TABLE 6100.2, TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS; SECTION 59-6200 – OFFICE AND COMMERCIAL DISTRICTS, TABLE 6200.2 TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS; SECTION 59-6250 – INDUSTRIAL DISTRICTS, TABLE 6250.2, TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS; AMENDING ARTICLE VII – SPECIAL PURPOSE DISTRICTS, BY AMENDING SECTION 59-7150 - BRICKTOWN CORE DEVELOPMENT DISTRICT, SECTION 59-7200 - DOWNTOWN DESIGN DISTRICTS, SECTION 59-7300 - NEIGHBORHOOD CONSERVATION DISTRICT AND SECTION 59-7350 - STOCKYARDS CITY DEVELOPMENT DISTRICT TO ADD CERTIFICATE OF APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS FOR ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS AND SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS, BUILDING INTEGRATED; AND TABLES 7150.1, 7200.2, AND 7300.2 TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS; AMENDING ARTICLE XII – SITE DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS, BY AMENDING SECTION 59-12200 – STANDARDS FOR ACCESSORY BUILDINGS, STRUCTURES AND USES, TO ESTABLISH USE STANDARDS FOR SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS; AND TABLE 12200.1, TO ADD SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS AS PERMITTED ACCESSORY STRUCTURES IN ALL ZONING DISTRICTS; SECTION 59-12250 – PERMITTED ENCROACHMENTS TO ADD SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS, GROUND-MOUNTED TO THE LIST OF EXCEPTIONS FOR OPEN YARD REQUIREMENTS; AMENDING ARTICLE XIII – ZONING OVERLAY DISTRICTS, BY AMENDING SECTION 59-13500 – SCENIC RIVER OVERLAY DESIGN DISTRICTS (SRODD), TO PROVIDE FOR STAFF APPROVAL OF ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS AND SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS, BUILDING INTEGRATED; TO EXEMPT ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF PARKING STRUCTURES; TO AMEND SERVICE AREA, OUTDOOR STORAGE, AND EQUIPMENT SCREENING REQUIREMENTS AND TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS IN THE MERIDIAN GATEWAY DISTRICT, STOCKYARDS RIVER DISTRICT, FARMERS MARKET DISTRICT, WESTERN GATEWAY DISTRICT, AND REGATTA DISTRICT; TABLE 13550.1 TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS IN THE STOCKYARDS TRANSITIONAL DISTRICT; AND TO CORRECT TABLE NUMBERING; AMENDING SECTION 59-13550 – STOCKYARDS CITY TRANSITIONAL DEVELOPMENT OVERLAY DISTRICT TO EXCLUDE ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF PARKING STRUCTURES; TO AMEND SERVICE AREA, OUTDOOR STORAGE, AND EQUIPMENT SCREENING REQUIREMENTS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR STAFF APPROVAL OF ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS AND SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS, BUILDING INTEGRATED; SECTION 59-13700 – URBAN DESIGN OVERLAY DISTRICT, TO PROVIDE FOR STAFF APPROVAL OF ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS AND SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS, BUILDING INTEGRATED; TO ADD ACCESSORY-USE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS TO THE LIST OF EXCEPTIONS TO FRONT BUILDING LINE REQUIREMENTS; AND TO AMEND SERVICE AREA, OUTDOOR STORAGE, AND EQUIPMENT SCREENING REQUIREMENTS; AND TABLE 13700.2 TO EXCLUDE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FROM THE MEASURED HEIGHT OF BUILDINGS IN THE LINCOLN BOULEVARD CORRIDOR SUBDISTRICT.
