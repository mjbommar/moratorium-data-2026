ORDINANCE #105-26

A TEMPORARY ZONING ORDINANCE EXTENDING THE MORATORIUM REGULATING UTILITY-SCALE ALTERNATIVE ENERGY SYSTEMS IN ALL TURNER COUNTY ZONING DISTRICTS

Motion by Benson, seconded by Hagemann, to approve and adopt the following ordinance. Roll call was taken Benson aye, Ciampa aye, Hagemann aye Kaufman aye with Hybertson abstaining. Motion carried.

PURPOSE: The Turner County Board of Commissioners has determined that additional time is necessary to continue studying the impacts of utility-scale alternative energy systems in Turner County and to evaluate whether current zoning regulations reasonably address concerns for the public health, safety, and welfare.

WHEREAS, SDCL § 11-2-10 authorizes the Board of County Commissioners, in the interest of public health, safety, and general welfare, to adopt temporary zoning controls for a reasonable period of time in order to study, identify, and consider necessary land use regulations; and

WHEREAS, The Turner County Board of Commissioners adopted Ordinance #100-25 on August 26, 2025, establishing a temporary one-year moratorium on the issuance of permits and approval of land uses for utility-scale alternative energy systems; and

WHEREAS, The Board finds that the review and evaluation of the impacts, regulations, and zoning standards applicable to utility-scale alternative energy systems remains ongoing and that additional time is needed to complete such review and consider potential amendments to the Turner County Zoning Ordinance; and

WHEREAS, The Board finds that extending the temporary moratorium is necessary to protect the public health, safety, and general welfare while such review is completed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED that the Turner County Board of Commissioners hereby extends the existing moratorium on the issuance of any new permits and/or approval of land uses for proposed utility-scale alternative energy systems, including both solar and wind energy systems, regulated under Ordinance 67-19 (Amending the 2008 Revised Zoning Ordinance for Turner County by amending Section 3.00, A-1 Agricultural District; Section 9.00, I-2 General Industrial District; Section 13.13, Additional Use Regulations, Wind Energy Conversion Systems (WECS); Section 20.08, Conditional Use Permits, Expiration; Section 22.09, Zoning Board of Adjustment, Limitations; and Section 27.02, Definitions) of the Turner County Zoning Ordinance, in all applicable (A-1 Agricultural, RR Rural Residential, C Commercial, I-1 Light Industrial, and I-2 General Industrial) Turner County Zoning Districts.
This extension of the moratorium shall remain in effect for one (1) year and shall expire on August 25, 2027, unless sooner repealed, amended, or otherwise extended as authorized by law.

BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED by the Board of County Commissioners of Turner County that the provisions and adoption of this ordinance shall take effect upon completed publication pursuant to SDCL §§ 11-2-10 and 7-18A-8.


Approved this 25th day of August 2026.
