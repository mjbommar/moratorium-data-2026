         The Chairman declared that it was time for public comments. Several residents of the
 Somerville community came forward to express their concems regarding the purchase of a I 5-acre
 tract of land by a company proposing to construct a data center,/bitcoin mining facitity near their
 homes.
         Several residents discussed the potential impact ofthe facility's utility consumption. citing
 the possibility of increased utility rates and harmful electromagnetic field (EMF) exposure from
the high-voltage transmission lines that would be required to supply electricity to the site.
 Residents also expressed concem that the continuous operation of cooling fans could create
 sustained noise levels with potential impacts on mental well-being and the surrounding ecology.
Another concem expressed was whether their local Volunteer Fire Department was adequately
equipped to respond to the specialized challenges that could arise in the event ofan emergency at
such a facility.
         One resident noted the presence of endangered species ofbats, turtles, and salamanders in
the area and requested that environmental studies be conducted to evaluate the potential effects of
the proposed facility on local wildlife and the Tennessee River ecosystem. A cancer survivor and
a grandparent also spoke, expressing concems about possible health impacts associated with the
operation of the facility in their community.
         Residents emphasized that the community was united in its efforts to ensure that the
Commission understood the importance of preserving the community's values. A petition had been
circulated seeking signatures to oppose the proposed construction, as many residents felt that the
company did not share the same commitment to the area or value it in the same way as those who
Iived there.
         The Chairman thanked the residents for their feedback and closed the comment portion of

ADOPT RESOLUTION PLACING A TWELVE (I2) MONTH MORATORIUM ON THE
  CONSTRUCTION, DEVELOPMENT, OR OPERATION OF ANY DATA CENTER,
   BITCOIN MINING FACILITY, OR OTHER SIMILAR TYPE FACILITY USED
     PRIMARILY FOR THE STORAGE, MANAGEMENT, PROCESSING, AND
 TRANSMISSION OFDIGITAL DATA, IN ANY UNINCORPORATED RESIDENTIAL
  AREA OR Df,VELOPMENT WITHIN MORGAN COUNTY IN ORDER TO STUDY
AND EVALUATE THE ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS AND CONCERNS THAT SUCH
      FACILITIES MAY HAVE ON THE CITIZENS OF MORGAN COUNTY.

          Mr. Greg Abercrombie, member of the Commission, offered the following resolution and moved
for its adoption. Upon the same being duly seconded by Mr. JeffClark, it was put to a vote and unanimously
adopted to wit:

                                          RESOLUTION 26.302

                RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING'I'EMPORARY ONE.YEAR
          MORATORITJM ON ALL PROPERTY DEVELOPMENT, CONSTRUCTION,
                 AND OPERATION OF DATA CENTER CAMPUSES AND
                    SIMILAR HICH.IMPACT I.'ACILITIES IN THE
            UNINCORPORATED R-ESIDENTIAL AREAS OF MORGAN COUNTY

         WIIEREAS, Morgan County is responsible fbr and has a vested interest in promoting and
protecting the health, safety, and general welfare of its citizens.

        WHEREAS, Morgan County has no resolutions or regulations that specifically define data centers;
however, the term "data center" commonly refers to a facility used primarily for the storage, management,
processing, and transmission ofdigital data which houses computer or network equipment, systems, servers,
appliances and other associated components related to digital data operations, and may also include air
handlers, power generators, water cooling and storage facilities, bitcoin mining facilities, utility substations,
and other associated utility infrastructure to support sustained operations;

        WHEREAS, the typical physical characteristics ofdata centers include, but are nol limited to, the
following: (l) power systems; (2) cooling systems; (3) battery energy storage energy systems; (4) use of
equipment to cool the hardware and operating space; and/or (5) power generators;

         WHEREAS, these types ofindustrial facilities present unique challenges and potential impacts that
may not be adequately addressed under any current laws or regulations, including. but not limited to, the
following concems: (l ) exlremely high energy consumption and strain on existing utility infrastructure; (2)
significant noise generation from cooling systems and backup generators, al'fecting adjacent residential
arias and general quality of life; (3) substantial water usage lbr cooling purposes; (4) potential
                                                                                              June 23. 2026

environmental impacts, including hazardous material storage and electronic waste disposal; and (5) land
use compatibility concerns, particularly in rural or agricultural areas:

        WHEREAS, Morgan County currently has no regulations governing these specific types of high-
impact uses in mind, and the Commission ne€ds time to develop appropriate, comprehensive standards to
ensure that any future development ofsuch facilities is consistent with Morgan County's long-term land
use plan and protects the public interest;


        WHEREAS, Morgan County has a legitimate public interest in regulating the design, size, location
and operation of data centers in its unincorporated residential areas;

         WHEREAS, a temporary moratorium on the development of data centers is advisable and
appropriate to preserve the status quo while the County Commission undertakes to study appropriate
regulations or resolutions as may be required incident thereto;

        NOW THEREFORE. BE IT RESOLVED by the Morgan County Commission while in regular
meeting on the 23'd day ofJune, 2026, as follows:

        l.      This Resolution shall be known and cited as the "Morgan County Data Center
Moratorium".

        2.       A moratorium is hereby established on the acceptance, processing or approval of any
required permits, site development plans, subdivisions, and site development plan amendments, if any,
beginning on the effective date ofthis Resolution, and a material purpose of which the is the development
ofa new data center, bitcoin mining facility, or the expansion (or other increase the gross-floor area) ofany
existing data center use.

        3.       The moratorium established hereby shall expire on the date that is one year from the
effective date ofthis Resolution, unless otherwise extended or repealed prior to such date.

         4. If any section, sentence, clause or phrase of this Resolution is for any reason held to be
invalid or unconstitutional by declaration ofany court of competent jurisdiction, such declaration shall not
affect the validity ofthe remaining portions ofthis Resolution.

        5.       The provisions of this Resolution are severable. All Resolutions or parts of Resolutions
inconsistent with this Resolution are hereby suspended to the extent ofsuch inconsistency, ifany.

        6.      This Resolution is adopted on this 23rd day of June 2026, and shall become effective
immediately.
