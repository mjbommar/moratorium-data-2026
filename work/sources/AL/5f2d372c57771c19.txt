                              MINUTES     OF PRE -COUNCIL          MEETING
                                 CITY OF HUEYTOWN,            ALABAMA
                                    TUESDAY,       JULY     14, 2026


The City Council of the City of Hueytown, Alabama, met at 5: 30 P. M. in the
Council Chamber meeting room at the City of Hueytown, City Hall, on Tuesday,
July 14, 2026.
President Hurliman called the meeting to order with Council Members Zinnerman,
Randall, Wright and Jacks present. The Mayor was present with the City Attorney,
Charlie Waldrep of Waldrep Stewart 8v Kendrick, LLP present. There were five
individuals   in the audience.


The purpose of the meeting was to discuss general municipal business and review
the City Council Meeting Agenda.

   8.   MAYORS     REPORT:


Mayor Stephen M. Ware welcomed those in attendance and thanked everyone for
attending.


The Mayor reported that the Red, White and Boom event was a great success and
thanked everyone who helped make it possible.

The Mayor provided an update on the Forest Road resurfacing project in front of
Hueytown Intermediate School and reported that the City is applying for a grant to
continue sidewalk installations.


The Mayor introduced Reverend Barbara Mosley of Bethlehem United Methodist
Church, who announced that the Birmingham Chapter of the Sons of the American
Revolution will sponsor a Patriotic Grave Marking Ceremony honoring Reverend
Captain James L. Tarrant, Sr., on July 25, 2026. Reverend Captain Tarrant served
under General George Washington at the Battle of Yorktown and received his
commission as Captain from President Thomas Jefferson.
