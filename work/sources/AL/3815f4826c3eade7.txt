ORDINANCE 2026-08-03-03

AN ORDINANCE DECLARING A SIX (6) MONTH MORATORIUM ON DATA
CENTERS

WHEREAS, the Town Council of the Town of Harpersville, Alabama, is authorized to
adopt regulations necessary to protect the public health, safety, and welfare and to promote the
orderly development of tne Town; and

WHEREAS, the Town Council seeks to encourage responsible economic development
while ensuring that new commercial and industrial uses are compatible with existing land uses,
infrastructure capacity, and the long-term interests of the community; and

WHEREAS, the Town Council recognizes the increasing development of data centers,
including facilities that house computers, servers, telecommunications equipment, blockchain
processing operations, cryptocurrency mining operations, cloud computing infrastructure,
artificial intelligence computing facilities, server farms, and other facilities where digital
information is processed, transmitted, or stored; and

WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that such facilities may significantly affect the
Town’s infrastructure «nd municipal services through increased electrical demand, water’
consumption, emergency service requirements, noise generation, and other operational
characteristics; and

WHEREAS, the Town Council has determined that additional time is necessary to study
the impacts of data centers, evaluate infrastructure capacity, review best practices adopted by other
municipalities, and consider whether amendments to the Town 's zoning ordinance, business
licensing requirements, and other municipal ordinances are necessary to protect the public health,
safety, and welfare; and

WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that it is necessary to adopt a temporary moratorium
while the Town researches, evaluates, and develops appropriate zoning regulations, business
licensing requirements, and other municipal regulations applicable to data centers in order to
protect the public health, safety, and welfare; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Town Council of the Town of
Harpersville, Alabama as follows:

Section 1. Moratorium

A temporary six (6) month moratorium from the effective date of this ordinance is hereby imposed
on zoning, rezoning, the issuance of new business licenses, the issuance of new building permits,
and/or the issuance of any other municipal approval required for the establishment or expansion
of a data center within the corporate limits or police jurisdiction of the Town, to the extent
permitted by law.

For purposes of this Ordinance, "data center" includes facilities primarily used for housing
computer systems, telecommunications equipment, servers, cloud computing infrastructure,


artificial intelligence computing, digital storage, cryptocurrency mining, blockchain transaction
processing, or similar high-density computing operations.

Section 2. Study and Review During Moratorium

During the six-month mcratorium, the Town Council may conduct monthly informational public
sessions to study and review issues related to data centers. The Town Council anticipates
addressing the following subjects during the moratorium period

1: What data centers are, what they do, and why there is an increase in data center
development.

2: How data centers choose property and what factors are considered when selecting a
location.

3: Zoning and how data centers could be addressed through the Town's zoning ordinance
and development regulations.

4: Review the information gathered and hear about how other cities and towns are
addressing data centers.

5: Present a proposed ordinance to regulate data centers within the Town of Harpersville.
The Town will hold a 30-day public comment period to allow Harpersville residents and
businesses an opportunity to provide comments and input on the proposed ordinance.

6: Review the proposed regulations and vote on the adoption of an ordinance regulating
data centers.

The schedule may be adjusted as needed to allow the Town Council the proper time to review
information, receive public input, and develop appropriate regulations.

Section 3. Severability.

If any provision of this C rdinance is held invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent
jurisdiction, such determination shall not affect the validity of the remaining provisions, which
shall remain in full force and effect.

Section 4. Expiration

Unless extended by subsequent action of the Town Council or terminated earlier, this
moratorium shall automatically expire six (6) months from its effective date

Section 5. Effective Date.
This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon its adoption.
