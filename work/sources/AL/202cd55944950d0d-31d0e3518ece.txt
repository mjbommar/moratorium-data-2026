Public Safety

Governor Kay Ivey has declared September 2026 as National Preparedness Month in Alabama.

A new notification system state-wide and to sign up go to: www.ema.alabama.gov

Tips: Check batteries in smoke alarms monthly. Standard 9 Volt change every six to twelve months and

safety groups suggest changing when Daylight savings time begins and ends.

Know risks, learn what common disaster affects our area. Create an emergency plan and how your

family will communicate and create a supply kit. Stay informed and sign up: ema.alabama.gov and links

to the Shelby County alert system. Practice your emergency plan.
