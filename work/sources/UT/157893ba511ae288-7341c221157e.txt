Notice Title Summary of an Ordinance - Moratorium on Data Centers Notice Type(s) Notice Event Start Date & Time July 1, 2026 02:30 PM Description/Agenda The following ordinance was adopted and approved by the Logan Municipal Council, Logan, Utah on June 30, 2026. ORD. 26-12 - MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS AND DATA CENTER POWER PLANTS - Adopted a Temporary Land Use Regulation Prohibiting the Development and Construction of Data Centers and Data Center Power Plants. This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon publication. Full text of the ordinance may be reviewed at the Office of the Logan City Recorder, City Hall, 290 North 100 West, Logan, Utah during regular business hours, on the Logan City website at www.loganutah.gov or the Utah Public Notice Website at https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html Teresa Harris, City Recorder 435-716-9002 teresa.harris@loganutah.gov DATE OF PUBLICATION: July 1, 2026 1. Definitions

Government Type: A categorization of government entities, including state, county, municipality, special districts, and schools, among others. The designation of special district includes all local districts and special service districts. There are many different designations, and the term special district is only used as a common term.
Entity: All state agencies, political subdivisions (cities, counties, special districts), the legislature, judiciary, and public education or state-funded higher education institutions.
Public Body: A board, commission, or committee that posts notices or holds meetings, according to the Open and Public Meetings Act, Utah Code 52-4-103(7)
