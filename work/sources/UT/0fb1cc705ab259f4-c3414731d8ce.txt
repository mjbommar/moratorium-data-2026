Action Item Link to video recording of meeting at time stamp PUBLIC HEARING – – Consideration of a proposed ordinance adopting a Temporary Land Use Regulation Prohibiting the Development and Construction of Data Centers and Data Center Power Plants – – Ordinance 26-12 – Craig Carlston, City Attorney


City Attorney Craig Carlston discussed the proposed 180-day moratorium on data centers and clarified

the statutory limitations and process for adopting new regulations.

Councilmember Simmonds asked whether, although state code limits a moratorium to 180 days, the

ordinance development process could continue beyond that timeframe if the proposed land

development regulations had reached the Planning Commission before the moratorium expired.

City Attorney Craig advised that, under the pending ordinance doctrine, if the code amendment

process has begun, applications submitted after that point may be subject to the proposed regulations.

He confirmed that while the moratorium itself is limited to 180 days, the ordinance adoption process

may extend beyond that period if the ordinance is actively moving through the required review and

approval process.

Chair Johnson invited questions from councilmembers. Hearing none, the Council opened the public

hearing.

Prior to taking public comments, Chair Johnson reviewed the public hearing procedures and requested

that comments remain focused on the proposed 180-day data center moratorium only and not future

data center regulations.

Chair Johnson opened the meeting to a public hearing.

Gail Hanson, a Logan resident, expressed appreciation for the Council's transparency. She stated her


support for the proposed moratorium and for the development of thoughtful regulations governing data

centers within Logan City. She also expressed concerns regarding recent actions of the Municipal

Infrastructure Agency (MIA) Board, stating that those concerns reinforced the need for the

moratorium.

Josh Molitor, a Logan resident, spoke in favor of the moratorium, describing it as an important first


step toward protecting Cache Valley. He expressed concerns regarding future growth, water use, and

the potential impacts of data centers.

Chair Johnson also noted that Cache County had recently adopted a moratorium applicable only to

unincorporated areas of the county and that it did not apply within Logan City limits.

There were no further comments, and Chair Johnson closed the public hearing.

ACTION. Motion by Councilmember Simmonds seconded by Vice Chair López to adopt Ordinance 26-12 as presented. Motion carried by roll call vote (5-0). Dahle: Aye Johnson: Aye Lee-Koven: Aye López: Aye Simmonds: Aye


Vice Chair López thanked the City's intern for providing research materials that helped inform the

Council's discussion and stated that additional questions should be addressed during the moratorium

period before permanent regulations are adopted.

Councilmember Dahle asked about the process for developing the proposed regulations.

Chair Johnson responded that Community Development, Light & Power, Water, and Legal staff had

already begun work on land development code amendments and utility-related proposals.

Councilmember Simmonds added the process would include public engagement, and draft regulations

would ultimately be reviewed by the Planning Commission before returning to the Municipal Council
