Resolutions
2026-34 FAA – Automated Weather Observation System Lease
Motion by Kalms, seconded by Gauger to approve Resolution 2026-34 – Extension of the lease of real property at the
Door County Cherryland Airport with the Federal Aviation Administration for the purpose of maintaining an Automated
Weather Observing System and an access road for a term of 20 years.
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-35 Approval of County Highway Improvement Grant to the Door County Highway Department
Motion by Kalms, seconded by Fisher to approve Resolution 2026-35 – Approval of the three grant awards to the
Door County Highway Department to be used for highway construction and improvements as follows: $147,482.61
County Highway Improvement Grant for County C, 4 lane to County DK (2026); $582,468 County Highway
                                                                                                        Page 3 of 10
Improvement Supplemental Grant for County B, County G to Birch Lane (2027); and, $361,977.79 County Highway
Improvement Discretionary Grant for County W, Detroit Harbor Road to McDonald Road (2028).
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-36 Approval of National Fish & Wildlife Foundation Sustain Our Great Lakes Grant for Invasive Species
Control
Motion by Thayse, seconded by Smith to approve Resolution 2026-36 – Acceptance of The National Fish and Wildlife
Foundation award to the Soil & Water Department in the amount of $200,000 with a County match of $194,376.89, for
the purpose of hiring staff and contractors to conduct treatments of Phragmites australis, knotweed, purple loosestrife
and other priority invasive species.
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-37 Appointments to County Board Subunits: Zoning Board of Adjustment and Library Board
Motion by Thayse, seconded by Norton to approve Resolution 2026-37 – Appointment of Brian Forest and Cheryl
Mick as regular members of the Board of Adjustment and Bob Ryan as an alternate member of the Board of
Adjustment, terms expire June 2029; and Matthew Huston and Nicole Matson as City appointees to the Library Board,
terms expire May 2027.
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-38 Highway Department – Position Change
Motion by Thayse, seconded by Robillard to approve Resolution 2026-38 – Approval to reclassify a Mechanic Position
to Highway Worker.
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-39 Partnership with Door County Economic Development for Broadband Implementation
Motion by Thayse, seconded by Miller to approve Resolution 2026-39 – Approval of a Memorandum of Understanding
with Door County Economic Development to ensure that the County can complete the final broadband objectives and
still be within targeted project expenditures.

County Administrator Ken Pabich noted the partnership with DCEDC is critical after our current Broadband
Coordinator Jessica Hatch recently accepted a position with the State. The county tasks yet to be completed would
not warrant the County hiring a new Broadband Coordinator; this partnership will assist the County in completing the
remaining tasks.
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-40 Authorization to Obtain a Feasibility Study for the Emergency Services Middle Station Project –
Transfer of Non-Budgeted Funds
Motion by Miller, seconded by Shaw to approve Resolution 2026-40 – Approval of the transfer of an amount not to
exceed $30,000 from the Capital Outlay Emergency Services Expense Account to fund the retention of professional
services to conduct a feasibility study evaluating the purchase of vacant land with new construction versus the
purchase and remodel of an existing building for the Middle Emergency Services Station.

County Administrator Ken Pabich reviewed the progress to-date following the approval of the Board to search to
identify potential sites. Three options would be included in the study – purchasing and remodel of an existing building,
purchasing vacant land and building a facility, and constructing an addition to the Jacksonport Fire Station.

Motion carried by roll call vote with 19 Members voting Yes; 1 Member voting No – Pluff; 1 Member excused.

2026-41 Data Center and Battery Energy Storage System Development Moratorium
Motion by Thayse, seconded by Shaw to approve Resolution 2026-41 – Imposing a moratorium on the development
of Data Centers and Battery Energy Storage Systems within the unincorporated areas of Door County to provide time
to investigate and address, through regulation, planning, and otherwise, the potential impact on such land uses. The
moratorium is necessary to maintain public health, safety, and general welfare, in addition to other reasons. The
moratorium shall be in effect within the unincorporated areas of Door County for a period of up to twelve (12) months
from the date of this resolution; and the Door County Board of Supervisors reserves the right to extend the
moratorium by separate action if deemed necessary.
                                                                                                       Page 4 of 10
Supervisor Todd Thayse noted at this time there is no known projects related to a Data Center in our county. CC
Donohue noted this isn’t a forever moratorium rather it allows the Land Use Services Department time to review and
address the concerns from our county. The initial moratorium period is for 12 months with the ability to extend should
we need that. This allows the County time to take a pause to determine what zoning implications these types of
facilities have and to allow time to update zoning ordinances and the Comprehensive Plan if needed. Land Use
Services Director Karyn Behling explained the Resource Planning Committee discussed this over several different
committee meetings. This resolution allows time to research and craft an appropriate plan for the future.
Motion carried by voice vote.

2026-42 Approval of Town of Brussels Zoning Ordinance Map Amendment
Motion by Fisher, seconded by Miller to approve Resolution 2026-42 – Approval of the Town of Brussels zoning map
amendment rezoning the eastern 1.848 acres of parcel 0040003262433A from Light Industrial (LI) to Rural
Residential (RR).
Motion carried by voice vote.

Special Reports
No special reports were presented.

New Business
Addressing Project – Update / Status
Emergency Management Director JEB Saelens presented a PowerPoint reviewing the project which included: the
project is 42% complete; Phase I (crews replace green sign with blue sign that have a metal post in good working
condition within 20’ of the driveway. If a new post is needed or post needs to be relocated crews will circle back
around once Diggers has been to the address); municipalities worked through; projected total costs - $755,000;
estimated completion date is Fall 2027; currently working on Phase II in the Town of Sturgeon Bay and Town of
Clay Banks and prepping the Town of Union for Phase II.

Museum Project – Timeline / Next Steps
Facilities & Parks Director Wayne Spritka and County Administrator Pabich provided an update on the project
which included: proposed interior floorplan which includes two tenant spaces, shared restrooms, an office for the
Museum, and shared conference rooms; building elevations; preliminary schedule – out to bid in June with County
Board review and approval in July; estimated costs - Museum archives & storage space - $1,882,233, commercial
space renovations for tenants - $809,219, and ADA upgrades to existing Museum - $178,018.

Broadband Update
Broadband Coordinator Jessica Hatch presented a PowerPoint which included: Municipal broadband Grant Status;
Municipal broadband next steps – Broadband adoption and digital-skill building; BEAD award summary; recent
State grant opened; and BEAD next steps.

New Employee & Administrative Policy Manual (for review)
Corporation Counsel Sean Donohue provided a background on how we arrived at this draft; this document
incorporates and updates previous versions of the Employee Handbook and Administrative Manual originally
adopted in 2014. The new Manual has been thoroughly reviewed internally, legally, and has been provided to all
employees for further input. The Board will have time over the next month to review the document and provide
final comment prior to the June Board meeting. The new Manual will be a working, living document and changes
can be made at any time as needed.
