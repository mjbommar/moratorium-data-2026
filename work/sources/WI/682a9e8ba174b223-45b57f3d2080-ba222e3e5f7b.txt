Public Meeting

Call to Order - Chairman Tuschen called the meeting to order at Call to Order at 5:30pm.

Roll call - Town board members present - Chairman Tuschen, Supervisor Taylor Dahl, Plan Commission

member Tony Trapp, and Clerk Nanette Hensen. Supervisor Bill Hoffman and Treasurer Lori Toutant were

absent. In addition, Citizen Ann Plata was present.

Pledge of Allegiance - Led by Chairman Tuschen.

Data Center Moratorium Ordinance and Discussion - The articles of the ordinance, GEC engineering

report, time requirement and county request were discussed.

Dahl made a motion to adjourn the public meeting. Seconded by Tuschen. Motion passes.

Special Board Meeting  -

Call to order r – Chairman Tuschen called the meeting to order at 5:40pm.

Roll call - Town board members present - Chairman Tuschen, Supervisor Taylor Dahl, Plan Commission

member Tony Trapp, and Clerk Nanette Hensen. Supervisor Bill Hoffman and Treasurer Lori Toutant were

absent. In addition, Citizen Ann Plata was present.

Dahl made motion to enact Ordinance No. 18 Town of Hampden Data Center Temporary Moratorium

as presented. Seconded by Tuschen. Motion passes.

Dahl made a motion to adjourn. Seconded by Tuschen. Meeting adjourned at 5:42pm.
