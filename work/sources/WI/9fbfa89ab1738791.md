## [***18 Month Moratorium on Data Center applications as amended and adopted by the Manitowoc County Board of Supervisors 4-28-2026***](https://townoftworivers.wi.gov/CMS/files/2026/Moratorium%20as%20adopted%20by%20the%20Manitowc%20County%20Board%204.28.2026.pdf)

### The Town of Two Rivers, Mishicot and Two Creeks have sent resolutions to the Manitowoc County Planning and Zoning to pass on to the County Board requesting that a one year moratorium on Data Centers applications be put in place to allow time to make sure that requirements protect the heath and safety of our residents and our agriculture land, as we are part of the Farmland Preservation area.
