STATE OF WISCONSIN
Columbia County
TOWN OF HAMPDEN

Notice is hereby given that the Town of Hampden, Columbia County,
Wisconsin, shall hold a public meeting on May 26th, 2026 at 5:30pm, at the
Hampden Town Hall, N1291 County N, Columbus to discuss a proposed
ordinance for a temporary moratorium on the data centers within the township.

This ordinance includes a temporary 12-month moratorium on Data Centers
due to the potential strain these facilities may place on local capacity, public
services, and infrastructure. An additional six-month extension may be
considered if needed. This pause will allow the township sufficient time to
research and draft an ordinance providing comprehensive and appropriate
regulations of Data Centers.

The full text of the proposed ordinance may be obtained from the Town of
Hampden Clerk, Nanette Hensen, phone: (608) 333-9110; email:
clerk@townofhampden.wi.gov or may be viewed at the Town Hall at N2191
County Road N, Columbus, Wisconsin by appointment.

Notice is hereby given this 15th day of April, 2026
Town of Hampden by Nanette Hensen, Clerk
