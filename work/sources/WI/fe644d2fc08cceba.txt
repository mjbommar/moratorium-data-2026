Public Hearing Description

The hearing will be on a proposed ordinance to implement a twelve (12) month moratorium on
the acceptance, consideration, or approval of any application or permit to develop, construct,
enlarge or operate a solar energy system (SES) exceeding one acre in size, any battery energy
storage system (BESS), or any data center. The proposed moratorium is to allow Vilas County
adequate time to assess potential impacts and secondary effects of these types of land use and to
develop and implement appropriate and necessary regulations.

A copy of the proposed ordinance is on file in the Vilas County Zoning Office and is available at
www. vilascountyzoning.com
