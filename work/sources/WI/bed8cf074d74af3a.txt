PROCLAMATION JUNE 2026 PRIDE MONTH
WHEREAS, the events at the Stonewall Inn in June 1969 marked a historic turning point in the national
movement for dignity, equal rights, and authentic self-expression for the Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual,
Transgender and Queer (LGBTQ+) community; and WHEREAS, La Crosse County recognizes the work
of generations of local and national LGBTQ+ advocates who have advanced understanding, visibility, and

inclusion, strengthening the fabric of our shared community; and WHEREAS, landmark decisions such
as Obergefell v. Hodges, which struck down state laws banning same-sex marriage, have affirmed
important legal protections for the LGBTQ+ community, even as recent years have brought renewed
challenges and rhetoric that have affected many residents and families; and WHEREAS, La Crosse
County is committed to ensuring that all people are treated with respect, supported in living safely and
openly, and welcomed as full participants in community life; and WHEREAS, local organizations,
neighbors, families, and allies continue to foster connection, education, and support that make La Crosse
County a more inclusive and compassionate place for everyone. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED,
that the La Crosse County Board hereby recognizes June as a month to honor this history and celebrate
the contributions of the LGBTQ+ community here and across Wisconsin, and reaffirm our dedication to
dignity, understanding, and belonging for all residents.

RESOLUTION NO. 5-6/26 RE: APPROVAL OF BLUFFLANDS FUNDING FOR THE PURCHASE AND
OPERATION FOR BICYCLE & PEDESTRIAN TRAIL COUNTER PROGRAM
WHEREAS, the County approved the “Blufflands Plan” in 2016 to guide a regional approach to the
conservation and recreational use of area blufflands; and, WHEREAS, the County signed a Memorandum
of Understanding in September of 2021 to join the Blufflands Coalition to facilitate the implementation of
the “Blufflands Plan”; and, WHEREAS, County staff has developed “Guidelines for Requesting Blufflands
Funding” to provide a consistent way for community organizations and municipalities within La Crosse
County to request funding assistance for blufflands projects from the County; and, WHEREAS, the
County has received a request from the City of La Crosse for $6,252.50 to help purchase four (4) portable
trail counters and cover a five-year operation of the counters for bicycle & pedestrian trails, and that the
request follows the guidelines for requesting funding and meets all the requirements that the County has
set for providing funding. NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the La Crosse County Board hereby
approves the contribution of $6,252.50 to assist the City of La Crosse with the purchase four (4) portable
trail counters and operations up to a five-years; and, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the County
Board Chair, after approval by Corporation Counsel is hereby authorized to execute a grant agreement
with the City of La Crosse to ensure that funds will be used to purchase four portable trail counters and
cover a five-year operation of the counters for bicycle and pedestrian trails. FISCAL NOTE: This
$6,252.50 contribution will be paid from Account 100.130.1190-63100.00 Economic Development
Bluffland Protection. That account currently has $86,100.00 which has been budgeted in previous years
and carried forward into 2026.

RESOLUTION         NO.     6-6/26      RE:     TEMPORARY        MORATORIUM            ON    DATA    CENTERS
WHEREAS, “Data centers, data hosting and similar data storage or processing” are currently an
authorized use in the “Light Industrial District” and “Industrial District” of the La Crosse County Zoning
Code [s. 17.14(2)(j)]; and WHEREAS, the La Crosse County Zoning Code does not currently further
define types of “Data Centers” and does not sufficiently address the possible environmental, economic,
health, safety, land use, infrastructural, and general welfare concerns that may result from the location,
construction, and operation of Data Centers; and WHEREAS, Data Centers typically feature high-
performance servers, storage arrays, networking equipment, cooling systems, and power backup
solutions to ensure uninterrupted operation; and WHEREAS, concerns have been raised about the
potential environmental, economic, health, safety, land use, infrastructural, and general welfare issues
related to the location, construction, and operation of Data Centers, including electrical connections,
energy demand, electromagnetic fields, noise pollution, impacts on air quality and water resources, water
use, and fire hazards; and WHEREAS, in May of 2026, the County Board of Supervisors authorized the
creation of an ad hoc committee to study Data Centers and make recommendations to the Executive
Committee and full Board of Supervisors [Resolution #2-5/26]; and WHEREAS, pursuant to Wis. Stat.
§59.69(4), the County may enact a moratorium that is not a "development moratorium" as that term is
defined in Wis. Stat. §66.1002(1)(b); and WHEREAS, a moratorium would allow the county time to fully
explore, analyze, and research the environmental, economic, health, safety, land use, infrastructural,
and general welfare impacts of Data Centers and develop consistent zoning and regulatory standards to
be applied to Data Centers in La Crosse County; NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED; the La Crosse
County Board of Supervisors, pursuant to Wis. Stat. §59.69(4), hereby enacts the following moratorium:
       1. Notwithstanding any other provision in La Crosse County Ordinance Chapter 17, there shall
           be a moratorium on the receipt of applications and the granting of all permits or
           approvals for the siting or construction of Data Centers. The purpose of this moratorium
           is to allow La Crosse County time to research the possible environmental, economic,
           health, safety, land use, infrastructural, and general welfare effects of Data Centers and establish
           consistent zoning and regulatory standards to be applied to Data Centers in La Crosse County.
                                                      (23-26/27)

          It will also allow the ad hoc committee to complete their study and make recommendations to
          t h e E x e c u t i v e C o m m i t t e e a n d f u l l Board of Supervisors regarding any permanent
          amendments to County Ordinances. The moratorium shall be in effect for a period of 18 months
          from the date this resolution is published or until the County Board adopts any amendments
          to the Zoning ordinance recommended by the ad hoc committee or rescinds this
          moratorium, or both. The moratorium enacted is not intended to be, and does not, constitute
          a “development moratorium” as that term is defined in Wis. Stat. § 66.1002(1)(b).
      2. For purposes of this moratorium, “data center” and “data centers, data hosting and similar
          data storage or processing” means one or more facilities housing computing, networking, or
          data-management equipment that can store, process, retrieve, or distribute more than 1
          petabyte (1,000 terabytes) of data or that consume more than 2MW of electricity.
FISCAL NOTE: No material fiscal impact to the County. The County would not collect zoning permit
revenue for new data centers during the moratorium.

RESOLUTION NO. 7-6/26 RE: ACCEPTING ENERGY ASSISTANCE FUNDING AND AUTHORIZING
ONE (1.0 FTE) ENERGY ASSISTANCE SPECIALIST POSITION
WHEREAS, La Crosse County Human Services administers the Wisconsin Home Energy Assistance
Program (WHEAP) for La Crosse County residents, which provides financial help for heating and electric
bills, crisis support for energy emergencies, and additional services like HVAC and weatherization
assistance; and, WHEREAS, the workload for administering WHEAP will be significantly increasing due
to state-level changes where La Crosse County staff will be responsible for processing all applications
within the service area, including online applications previously processed through a state vendor; and,
WHEREAS, the Wisconsin Department of Administration has increased its allocation for La Crosse County
to support the hiring of an additional (1.0 FTE) Energy Specialist Position to handle the additional
workload (this includes an additional $12,000 for the last three months of the current Federal Fiscal Year,
with a commitment for continued increased funding for the next fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026).
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the La Crosse County Board of Supervisors hereby accepts
$12,000 in WHEAP funds and authorizes the addition of one (1.0 FTE) Energy Assistance Specialist
position within the Human Services Department, effective July 2026; and, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED
that the position shall remain contingent upon continued availability of increased WHEAP funding. FISCAL
NOTE: The position and associated onboarding and training costs are fully funded through WHEAP funding
provided by the Wisconsin Department of Administration. Costs will be tracked under the 210.480.4960
org within the County financial statement under the Economic Support section.

RESOLUTION NO. 8-6/26 RE: CIVILIAN REVIEW BOARD PILOT PROJECT EXTENSION
WHEREAS, the La Crosse County Board of Supervisors created a pilot Civilian Review Board (CRB) in
February 2024 (Resolution 43-2/24) for a period of two years; and WHEREAS, six members of the
Civilian Review Board were appointed on September 19, 2024; and WHEREAS, the Civilian Review Board
conducted its first meeting in November 2024; and WHEREAS, prior to beginning the work of reviewing
public reports, the Civilian Review Board developed policies and procedures, a website, and marketed
the process to assist in effectively conducting the work as directed by the County Board of Supervisors;

extends the pilot Civilian Review Board until November 2028; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the
Civilian Review Board follow the conditions of Resolution 43-2/24 entitled: Creation of Pilot Civilian Review
