MEETING OF THE COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MANITOWOC COUNTY, WISCONSIN


Tuesday, April 28th, 2026  6:00 P.M.

Pursuant to Wis. Stats. 59.04, the County Board of Supervisors of Manitowoc County,

Wisconsin convened in open session at the Heritage Center, in the City of Manitowoc, being the

28th day of April 2026, for the purpose of transacting business as a Board of Supervisors.

Chairman Phipps called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

Supervisor Schiesl gave the invocation, followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag by the

entire assemblage.

Elaine Olsezewski-Steckler, Town of Two Rivers, opposed to a data center in Manitowoc

County.

Susan Dirkmann, Town of Mishicot, opposed to a data center in Manitowoc County.

Natalie Ford, City of Manitowoc, opposed to a data center in Manitowoc County.

Katherine Leone, City of Manitowoc, opposed to a data center in Manitowoc County.

Tom Sprang, Town of Two Creeks, opposed to a data center in Manitowoc County.

Kay Lutze, Town of Mishicot, was in favor of the moratorium.

Cameron Ross, Town of Two Rivers, suggested more research be done and was opposed to a

data center in Manitowoc County.

Pat Klein, City of Manitowoc, opposed to a data center in Manitowoc County.

Dean Anhalt, Town of Mishicot, was in favor of the moratorium. He spoke regarding the need

for the County re-review the five-year manure lease that was lifted last month. He believes there

needs to be some regulations in place. He also suggested the committee appointments be

reviewed to make sure proper appointments are designated.

No one else present wished to speak, subsequently Chairman Phipps closed public input at 7:02

p.m.

COMMITITTEE REPORTS, RESOLUTIONS, ORDININANCES


Aging and Disability Board: Supervisor Shimulunas gave a report.

Board of Health: Supervisor Metzger gave a report.

Supervisor Metzger moved, seconded by Supervisor Hacker to adopt Resolution 2026/2028-8

Accepting 2026 ARPA COVID Discal Recovery Mini-Grant. Upon vote, the motion passed

unanimously.

Expo-Ice Center Board: Supervisor Behnke gave a report.

Highway Committee: Supervisor Behnke gave a report.

Supervisor Behnke moved, seconded by Supervisor Bonde to adopt Resolution 2026/2028-9

Authorizing Transfer of Property to the Town of Gibson (Zander Road Bridge). Upon voice vote,

the motion passed unanimously. Voting System failed, thus a voice vote was taken.

Human Services Board Committee: Supervisor Klein gave a report.

Land Conservation Committee: Supervisor Bonde gave a report.

Planning and Park Commission: Supervisor Zimmer gave a report.

Supervisor Zimmer moved, seconded by Supervisor Schwerma to enact Ordinance 2026/2028-10

Amending Manitowoc County Code Ch.8 Enacting a Moratorium on Data Centers. Discussion

followed.

Supervisor Sortwell moved, seconded by Supervisor Neils to amend the ordinance by adding

lines 57 and 58 “with the capability of managing more than 1 petabyte of data. Upon discussion

and vote, the motion carried with 17 ayes and 6 no. Supervisors Lillibridge, Heilman, Pope,

Dallas, Hammel, and Schwerma voted no.

Supervisor Behnke moved, seconded by Supervisor Hammel to amend the ordinance on line 71,

by striking 12 and replace it with 18. Upon discussion and vote, the motion carried with 22 ayes

and 1 no. Supervisor Shimulunas voted no.

Supervisor Zimmer moved, seconded by Supervisor Schwerma to enact Ordinance 2026/2028-10

as Amended Amending Manitowoc County Code Ch.8 Enacting a Moratorium on Data Centers.

Upon vote, the motion carried unanimously.

Public Works Committee: Supervisor Sitkiewitz gave a report.

ADJOURNMENT


Supervisor Hammel moved to adjourn, seconded by Supervisor Lillibridge and the motion was

adopted by acclamation. The meeting adjourned at 7:52 p.m.
