Data Center Information

Chippewa County’s 12-month moratorium on data centers expires on August 11, 2027. The Planning & Zoning Committee is tasked with reviewing the potential impacts of data centers on the environment, natural resources, public health and safety, emergency response, infrastructure, and the community’s overall welfare.

The proposed review process will include public participation and consideration of impacts on residents, wildlife, property, businesses, visitors, and the county’s natural resources.
