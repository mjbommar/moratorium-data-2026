This ordinance is enacted pursuant to the authority granted by the Wisconsin Statutes, including, but not limited to, Wis. Stat. § 59.69(4). The purpose of the Sauk County Zoning Ordinance is to protect the public health, safety, convenience, and general welfare of the residents of Sauk County. In the interpretation and application, the provisions of this ordinance will be held to be the minimum requirements for the promotion of the public health, safety, convenience, and general welfare, and will be liberally construed in favor of the County and will not be construed to be a limitation or repeal of any other power granted by Wis. Stats. and possessed by the County.

This ordinance is further intended to control or lessen congestion in the rights-of-way; to secure safely from fire, panic, and other dangers; to promote adequate light and air; to manage growth and the impact of land development; to encourage protection of ground and surface water and other natural resources; to preserve productive farmland; to preserve the County’s rural character; to prevent overcrowding of land; to preserve, protect, and promote property values; to promote high quality and lasting community design; to clearly present land development opportunities and the review process; and to facilitate adequate provision of transportation, water, sewerage, educational institutions, parks, and other public facilities. The ordinance is also purposed with following the Sauk County Land and Water Resource Management Plan and Farmland Preservation Plan. The ordinance is adopted to implement the Sauk County Comprehensive Plan; and incorporate any comprehensive plans adopted by townships subject to County Zoning authority.

Notwithstanding any other provision in the Sauk County Chapter 7 Zoning Ordinance, there shall be a moratorium on the receipt of applications and the granting of all zoning permits or approvals for the siting, placement, or construction of data centers. For the purposes of this ordinance, a data center is any facility housed in one or more structures with the primary use of storage, management, processing, and/or transmission of digital data, and housing computer and/or network equipment, systems, servers, appliances and other air handlers, power generators, water cooling and storage facilities, utility substations, and other associated utility infrastructure to support sustained operations. It may also contain accessory office uses necessary to monitor and manage sustained operations. This moratorium is to allow Sauk County time to fully explore, analyze, and research the possible environmental, economic, public health, safety, and general welfare effects of data centers, establish consistent zoning and regulatory standards to be applied, and allow data centers to be addressed as part of the Sauk County Comprehensive Plan. The moratorium shall be in effect for a period of eighteen (18) months from the date this ordinance is published (i.e. its effective date) or until the County Board adopts amendments to this ordinance or rescinds this moratorium. The moratorium enacted is not intended to be, and does not, constitute a “development moratorium” as that term is defined in Wis. Stat. § 66.1002(1)(b).

Due to the comprehensive rewrite and reenactment of the Sauk County Zoning Code, there are two versions of the Zoning Code in effect. The Sauk County Zoning Code existing prior to the reenactment will remain in effect for one year in any town that was subject to the prior ordinance, or the new ordinance is adopted by that town via a resolution, whichever is sooner. This moratorium is to be enacted in both the Sauk County Chapter 7 Zoning Ordinance amended on March 21, 2017, pursuant to Ord. No. 3-17 and the reenacted Sauk County Chapter 7 Zoning Ordinance amended on September 15, 2026, pursuant to Ord. No. ____-26.
