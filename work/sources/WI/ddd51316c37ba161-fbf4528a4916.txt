The Vilas County Zoning & Planning Department regulates all development, land use, and land disturbance in unincorporated areas of Vilas County. This includes, but is not limited to, the placement and use of structures; the use of property; filling, grading, and vegetative removal on shorelands; floodplain development; gravel pits and quarries; and wireless communications towers. In addition, the Department also regulates private sewage disposal (septic systems, privies, etc.) countywide. The Department also administers the Vilas County Recycling programs. Click on the tabs above for more detailed information on specific programs.

Please note, some towns may have local ordinances that are more restrictive than Vilas County codes and ordinances. Contact your local town authorities to determine if your project is permitted under local town Ordinances. Projects within the boundary of the Lac du Flambeau Tribal Reservation may be subject to Tribal regulations and permits. Contact the Lac du Flambeau Tribe to determine if your project within the reservation boundary is permitted under Tribal regulations.
