NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that pursuant to Chapter 35A.63.220 of the Revised Code of Washington, the Covington City Council will hold a public hearing for consideration of a six-month moratorium on the acceptance, processing and/or approval of applications for building and land development permits and related land use decisions for battery energy storage systems located, or proposed to be located, within the City of Covington. The public hearing will take place at the city council’s regular meeting on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. In compliance with state law, city council special and regular meetings will be held in a hybrid format with in-person, telephonic, and virtual options for public viewing and participation.

The purpose of this public hearing is for the city council to receive comments from the public, both written and oral, regarding the proposed six-month moratorium on the acceptance, processing and/or approval of applications for building and land development permits and related land use decisions for battery energy storage.

All persons desiring to comment may do so in writing to Krista Bates, City Clerk, at 16720 SE 271st Street, Suite 100, Covington, Washington, 98042 (please be sure to include your name and mailing address), or by email to kbates@covingtonwa.gov, or by testifying at the public hearing on October 27, 2026.

NOTICE IS FURTHER GIVEN that agenda information will be posted by the Friday prior to the above meeting at Covington City Hall, and on the city’s website: https://www.covingtonwa.gov/city_departments/executive/cityclerk/councilminutes.php

For further information, please contact Salina Lyons Community Development Director at slyons@covingtonwa.gov or by phone at 253-480-2441.
