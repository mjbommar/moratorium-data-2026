ORDINANCE NO. 3949

AN INTERIM ORDINANCE DECLARING ESTABLISHMENT OF AN

IMMEDIATE MORATORIUM ON DEVELOPMENT APPLICATIONS AND

PERMITS RELATING TO THE REDEVELOPMENT OF EXISTING MOBILE

HOME OR MANUFACTURED HOME PARKS TO ALLOW THE  CITY TO

REVIEW OPTIONS, COMPREHENSIVE PLAN POLICIES, AND DRAFT

REGULATIONS FOR THE PRESERVATION OF EXISTING MOBILE HOME

AND MANUFACTURED HOME PARKS IN THE CITY OF MOUNT VERNON,

WASHINGTON.

WHEREAS, land values, home prices, and interest rates continue  to rise. Such factors in

combination with inflationary pressures has resulted in housing becoming less and d less affordable

in  the City of Mount Vernon, Washington, and across  the United States; and

WHEREAS, severe housing cost  burdens disproportionately impact t low-income and fixed

income households; and

WHEREAS, m mobile home and manufactured home parks provide some of the most affordable

ownership and housing opportunities for elderly and low-income residents available today; and

WHEREAS, w within the City of Mount Vernon, s staff have initially identified 808 mobile and

manufactured homes, depicted on the map attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, t the City Council has determined that there is  a need to evaluate methods to

preserve existing mobile home and manufactured home parksto ensure their continued

provision of affordable housing; and

WHEREAS, the mobile home and d manufactured home parks remaining within the City m might

be subject to redevelopment  pressure and the residents of those parks bear risk of being displaced,

and many of the units in those parks might not be suitable or able to relocate within the City;

and

WHEREAS, a moratorium  on applications to redevelop, establish a new use or accessory use, or

change a use or accessory use for any site currently used as a mobile home or manufactured home

park would prevent the vesting  of new development rights leading to development t or

redevelopment that t displaces existing mobile home  or manufactured home park tenants; and

Section 1. The Recitals above are hereby adopted asfindings in support of this interim ordinance.

Section 2. The City of Mount Vernon hereby imposes an immediate  moratorium on the filing, acceptance,

processing and/or review  of any application to  redevelop, establish  a new  use or accessory use, or

change a use or accessory use for any site currently used as a mobile  home or manufactured home park k in

the City of Mount Vernon, as described in the Mount Vernon Municipal Code including but not limited to

all development regulations set forth in MVMC Titles 14, 15, 16, and 17. This ordinance shall not apply to

any application vested before the effective date of this ordinance. An application shall be vested pursuant to

the Mount Vernon Municipal Code.

Section 3 3. The City of Mouoununt Vernon r restricts redevelopment activities on any site currently used d as a  a

Mobile H Home Park Redevelopment Moratorium

A. Purpose

The purpose of this moratorium is to allow  the City adequate time to explore  options and prepare

zoning and land use  regulations that preserve existing mobile  home and d manufactured home parks.

B. Moratorium Imposed

The City Council imposes an immediate moratorium  on the filing, acceptance, p processing and/d/or

review of any application to redevelop, establish a new  use or accessory use,  or change a use  for any

site currently used as a  mobile home or manufactured home  park in the City of Mount Vernon. See

Exhibit A attached hereto. Any  such application submitted will be rejected d and returned d to the

applicant.

Applications to replace in-kind, repair, m maintain, otherwise improve an existing mobile  home or

manufactured home, or add d a new mobile home  or manufactured unit to an empty y pad within the City,

or any other applications necessary y for public health and safety as determined d by the Director of

Development Services designated under MVMC 2.35.020 A are not impacted d by this moratorium and will

be allowed d to proceed through the standard d application and review process.

Applications to locate individual new designated manufactured homes as provided on lands not in

use as mobile home or manufactured home parks are not subject to this moratorium but remain

subject to applicable development regulations of the City in effect at the time such applications vest

by operation of law.

Section 4. Effective Period

This moratorium will be in effect  for one year r unless one of the following has occurred: (1) the City

Council has not timely  conducted a public hearing within 60 calendar days of the passage of this

ordinance as required; or (2) fifinal regulations and d codes governing the preservation of existing mobile

homes or manufactured home parks have been adopted by the City Council before the expiration

period; or (3) this moratorium has been repealed by further ordinance of the City Council; or (4) this

moratorium has been extended d as provided for by state statute.

Section 5. Public H Hearing Required

As required by RCW 36.70A.390 and RCW 35A.63.220, within 60 calendar days of passage of this

ordinance the City Council will hold a public hearing on this moratorium.

Section 6. Work Plan

During the moratorium  period of one year, City staff will employ reasonable efforts to study

the issues  concerning the loss of affordable housing options through redevelopment of existing mobile

home and manufactured home parks as set forth in the Work Plan attached hereto as Exhibit B. Staff will

prepare appropriate revisions to the Mount Vernon Municipal Code and conduct  the public review

process required d for amendments. In order to extend the moratorium beyond one year a revised

work plan will  be presented at a public hearing of the City Council for consideration  and renewal  of

the moratorium and work plan for an additional six  months. Pursuant to RCW 36.70A.390 and RCW

35A.63.220, the Council may renew the moratorium for additional six-month periods.

Section 7. Declaration of Emergency

The City Council hereby declares that an emergency exists necessitating that this Ordinance take

effect immediately upon passage by a majority plus one of the whole membership of the Council, and

that the same is not subject to a referendum. The adoption of the interim regulations within this

Ordinance need to be immediately implemented to address the on-going housing crisis. This

Ordinance will not affect any existing rights, or any vested applications previously submitted to the

City.  Without an immediate moratorium on the City’s a acceptance of development applications for

property on which mobile and manufactured home parks are located, such applications could become

vested, leading to development, redevelopment, or change in use of existing mobile home and

manufactured home parks, resulting in the loss of mobile and manufactured home stock and

displacement of the individuals living in this stock. Therefore, the moratorium must be imposed as an

emergency interim measure to prevent the submission of development nt a apapplicatations t to the City i in an

atattempt to vest development rights f for an indefinite p period, and thereby immediately p preserve and protect t the

public health, s safety, and d welfare of the residents o of Mount Vernon.

Section 8. E Effective Date

This ordinance takes effect immmmedidiatately ly upon passage.
