"What is “BESS”?"

BESS facilities are defined as one or more devices assembled together that are capable of storing energy in order to supply electrical energy at a future time, including associated control, power conversion, and safety systems. BESS does not include residential-scale battery systems accessory to single-family or duplex residences that are intended primarily for onsite consumptions/emergency backup power, or consumer products utilizing batteries for routine portable/accessory purposes.

"Why is there a moratorium on BESS facilities?"

Certain BESS Facilities, particularly those utilizing lithium-ion battery technology, may pose fire, explosion, thermal runaway, and hazardous material risks that require specialized emergency response capabilities and regulatory standards. Additionally, incidents in other jurisdictions have demonstrated a need for the careful evaluation of fire suppression requirements, hazardous material containment, evacuation procedures, and land use compatibility.

The current development regulations in the City of Puyallup do not adequately address the unique risks, siting considerations, emergency response protocols, environmental impacts, and operational characteristics associated with BESS facilities. The City Council finds that the adoption of an emergency moratorium is necessary for the immediate preservation of public health, safety, and welfare while the City conducts further study and develops permanent regulations governing BESS facilities.

"What is included in the BESS moratorium?"

The moratorium imposes the acceptance, processing, and approval of applications for the siting, establishment, expansion, or modification of BESS facilities within the City of Puyallup. The moratorium shall include, but is not limited to:

Land use applications, such as conditional use permits, variances, preliminary site plan review, shoreline permits, SEPA, subdivisions, and binding site plans
Any construction permits, including buildings and civil permits
Electrical permits associated with new BESS facilities or expansions of existing BESS facilities
Applications for business licenses associated with newly proposed BESS facilities
Any other development permit or land use approval associated with the establishment of a BESS facility

Please note that no application deemed complete after the effective date of Ordinance No. 3344 shall vest under the city’s existing development regulations with respect to BESS facilities subject to this moratorium

"How long is the BESS moratorium?"

The BESS moratorium will remain in effect for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of Ordinance No. 3344 (June 15, 2026) unless extended, modified, or terminated by City Council following applicable public hearing requirements.

Pursuant to RCW 35A.63.220 and RCW 36.70A.390, the City Council shall hold a public hearing within sixty (60) days of the adoption of Ordinance No. 3344 to receive public testimony regarding the moratorium and the city’s work plan for development on permanent regulations.

During the established moratorium period, the city shall undertake a work plan to evaluate and develop permanent regulations that govern BESS facilities.
