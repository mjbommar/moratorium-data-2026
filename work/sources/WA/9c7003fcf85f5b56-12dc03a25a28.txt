8. COMMITTEE REPORTS

a) Mayor Sarah Moore

Mayor Moore reported that the City of Burien's petition for a public hearing with the

Port of Seattle had been accepted. The public hearing is scheduled for July 9, 2026, from

4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. at the Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA) Conference

Center and will also be available virtually.

b) Deputy Mayor Hugo Garcia

Deputy Mayor Garcia reported on the recent King County Board of Health meeting,

including updates on HIV/STI trends and maternal health, and noted participation on a

Board of Health subcommittee addressing maternal mortality. He also reported on the

Sound Cities Association (SCA) Equity and Inclusion Cabinet meeting and expressed

appreciation to Discover Burien and City staff for organizing the FIFA World Cup watch

parties.

c) Councilmember Rocco DeVito

Councilmember DeVito reported on the recent Municipal Solid Waste Advisory

Committee meeting, including an update on upcoming King County solid waste rate

adjustments.

9. COUNCIL REPORTS

a) Mayor Sarah Moore

Mayor Moore reported attending the Green Burien Park Stewards event and the

Father's Day Car Show, and recognized Park and Public Works staff for their efforts to

promote the City's fireworks ban.

b) Councilmember Rocco DeVito

Councilmember DeVito expressed appreciation to Discover Burien and the City's

Economic Development team for their work in organizing the FIFA World Cup watch

parties.

c) Councilmember Alex Andrade

Councilmember Andrade reported participating in the selection process for a new

president of Highline College, attending the Father's Day Car Show, the Association of

Washington Cities (AWC) Annual Conference, including a WA-CELI (Washington

Collaborative Elected Leaders Institute) gathering, and reminded the community of the

upcoming 4th of July Parade.

12. BUSINESS AGENDA

a) Introduction to Resolution No. 517, Southwest Sewer District Utility Easement

PaRCS Director Casey Stanley presented this item to request authorization to execute all

documents related to a utility easement.

A motion was made by Councilmember Alex Andrade, seconded by

Councilmember Kevin Schilling, to  approve Resolution No. 517

authorizing the PaRCS Director to grant the Easement and execute all

documents with Southwest Sewer District.  The motion passed

unanimously.

b) Burien Transportation Impact Fee Update Briefing

Development Manager Brian Tornow, Senior  Planner Chaney Skadsen presented this

item. They were joined by Sydney Segal, Senior Transportation Planner with Fehr and

Peers. They provided information on transportation impact fees, current fee

comparison, methodology, a staff recommendation and adoption timeline.

c) Introduction to Ordinance No. 905, Data Center Moratorium

Interim City Attorney Ann Marie Soto presented the item and provided information

regarding the drafting of the ordinance and voting requirements for adoption of the

emergency moratorium.

A motion was made by Councilmember Kevin Schilling, seconded by

Councilmember Rocco DeVito, to adopt Ordinance No. 905, adopting a

one-year emergency moratorium on the acceptance, processing, and/or

approval of applications to establish or expand data centers to be

effective immediately.  The motion passed unanimously.
