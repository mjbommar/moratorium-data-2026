## STREET, HIGHWAY, BRIDGE and OTHER PROJECTS WITH MAJOR TRAFFIC IMPACTS

### District 1

* **Commerce and St. Francis, Waterman to Kellogg:** This project will reconstruct both Commerce and St. Francis Streets as two-way roadways with sidewalk, parking, and new pedestrian and vehicular connections between both streets. The project also includes water and drainage improvements. Aesthetic enhancements such as trees, benches, trash receptacles, bike racks, and public art components will also be constructed.
* Traffic Impact: Commerce is CLOSED south of Waterman and St. Francis will resume to one lane southbound only from Lewis to Kellogg.
* Contractor: Prado Construction
* City Contact: Kyle Kerns (268-4044 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e58e8e80978b96a5928c868d8c9184cb828a93))
* Estimated completion: 4th Qtr. 2025

### District 2

* **21st Street - Interchange of 21st and K-96:** This project will perform concrete repairs on the street between the on & off ramps of 21st to K-96.
* Traffic Impact: 21st Street is reduced to one lane in each direction between the on & off ramps of K-96.
* Contractor: Kansas Paving Construction
* City Contact: Bryan Hamel (268-4047 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection))
* Estimated completion: May 2025

### District 3

* **I-135 Sanitary Sewer Relocation:** This project will reconstruct the sanitary sewer pipe that is currently located under the south bound lanes of I-135 with the new line being constructed along Southeast Boulevard and within the adjacent neighborhood south of Pawnee.
* Traffic Impact:
* Southeast Boulevard is reduced to one lane in each direction from Pawnee to south of Wassall. Left turns are restricted from Southeast Boulevard onto Pawnee. Left turns at the intersection of Wassall and Southeast Boulevard are restricted for eastbound Wassall onto Southeast Boulevard and northbound Southeast Boulevard onto Wassall. Southbound

I-135 off ramp onto Pawnee will be CLOSED starting April 2nd, 2025. Traffic will be detoured to the Harry Street exit, utilizing Southeast Boulevard and Hydraulic

* Contractor: S.J. Louis Construction
* City Contact: Kyle Kerns (268-4044 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b0dbdbd5c2dec3f0c7d9d3d8d9c4d19ed7dfc6))
* Estimated completion: 4th Qtr. 2025
* **George Washington Boulevard, Hillside to Menlo Street:** This project will perform concrete street repairs on George Washington Blvd. from Hillside to Menlo.
* Traffic Impact: Traffic will be reduced to one lane in each direction in the area of construction
* Contractor: Kansas Paving Construction
* City Contact: Bryan Hamel (268-4047 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#16747e777b737a56617f757e7f627738717960))

### District 4

* **West Street, Pawnee to Harry, including Pawnee and Southwest Boulevard Intersections:** This project will convert West Street from a four-lane roadway to five-lane. The project performs drainage system, waterline, and pedestrian improvements. The intersections of Pawnee and Southwest Boulevard will be reconfigured and reconstructed.
* Traffic Impact: Traffic is reduced to one lane in each direction on West Street from south of Pawnee to Harry. Left turns are restricted from West Street onto Southwest Boulevard.
* Contractor: Wildcat Construction
* City Contact: Kyle Kerns (268-4044 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#046f6f61766a7744736d676c6d70652a636b72))
* Estimated completion: Early 2026
* **West Street, 47th to MacArthur:** This project will convert West Street from a two-lane roadway to three-lane including a left center turn lane, curb, and drainage improvements. The project will also include a 10-feet wide multi-use path and six feet sidewalk along West Street for the length of the project. The intersection of West and 47th Streets will be reconstructed and will remain a four way stop with dedicated left turn lanes for all directions
* Traffic Impact: West Street is northbound traffic from 47th Street South to MacArthur. Traffic is detoured utilizing MacArthur, Meridian, and 47th Street South.
* Contractor: Pearson Construction LLC
* City Contact: Kyle Kerns (268-4044 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#345f5f51465a4774435d575c5d40551a535b42))
* Estimated completion: 2nd Qtr. 2026
* **MacArthur Road, Hoover Road to West Street:** This project will perform bridge repairs on MacArthur Road between Hoover Road and West Street.
* Traffic Impact: MacArthur Road is CLOSED east of Hoover Road and West of West Street. Traffic will be detoured south to 55th Street at Hoover Road and West Street. Access to residences and businesses will remain open to local traffic.
* Contractor: Barkley Construction
* City Contact: Bryan Hamel (268-4047 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection))
* Estimated completion: June 2025

* **North Junction to I-135/K-96 Interchange – The Gold Project, Phase 2B:** This phase includes a direct link from EB K-96 (from NB I-235) to EB K-96 at the junction on I-135, and new ramps for WB K-96 onto I-135. Additional project information can be found at [www.135Gold.com](https://www.135Gold.com).
* Traffic Impact: The intersection of 29th Street North and Hydraulic Avenue is CLOSED. This closure also includes the 29th Street North ramp to southbound I-135. The closure is necessary for girder erection operations and is anticipated to take approximately 60 days, weather permitting. An 11-foot width restriction on the two lanes of southbound I-135, south of the I-235/K-254 overpass, is in effect (along with a 50-mph work zone speed limit). Southbound I-135 exit to eastbound K-96 has an 11-foot width restriction.
* Contractor: Bergkamp – King Venture
* KDOT Contact: J.B. Wilson (337-7331 / [\[email protected\]](https://www.wichita.gov/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f7bdb5d9a09e9b849899b79c84d9909881) )
