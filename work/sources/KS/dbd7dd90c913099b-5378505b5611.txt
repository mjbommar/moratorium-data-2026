Public Notice

Starting August 7, 2025, Riley County will begin using a new software to manage meeting agendas, packets, and minutes for the Board of County Commissioners. The link to view Commission documents will be https://rileycoks.portal.civicclerk.com/

You can still use our previous on-demand web portal to view previous meetings as well as do searches for historical documents until the contract expires on September 30, 2025. If you are unable to find a historical document, please contact the County Clerk’s office for assistance at 785-565-6202.

Historical documents will be moved into the new system before September 30, 2025.
