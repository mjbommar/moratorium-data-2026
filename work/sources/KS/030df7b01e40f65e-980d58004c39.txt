Data Centers & Battery Energy Storage Systems

Riley County has enacted a Data Center and Battery Energy Storage System moratorium through May 14, 2027. This means we are unable to accept or review any development proposals for either use. The moratorium provides the time necessary to educate ourselves about these uses and make a decision on how to proceed.
