1.2 Why is this initiative a strong return on investment for the State of Kansas?

New households are enormously valuable to state and local economies. They pay taxes,

purchase homes, and generate new consumer spending in the local economy. With the

support of the Indiana University Public Policy Institute, MakeMyMove developed a

methodology to estimate the direct and induced economic impact of new households

recruited by a community. The method applies IMPLAN, the nation's leading input output

model, to calculate the tax revenue and economic output generated by recrui ted

households' consumer activity. Direct state and local income taxes tied to recruited

households' actual incomes are then added. Finally, a conservative attrition assumption,

consistent with the longest running programs, is applied to ensure p rojections remain

realistic.

With a state investment of $15 4,722, these 15 new households are projected to generate

$1,166,234 in new annual economic impact for Riley County and the State of Kansas.

In addition to the $$1,239,917in new annual economic impact produced by Riley County's

round 1 households, this round's 15 new households are projected to generate an additional

$1,166,234 in new annual economic impact, bringing Riley County's combined pro gram

impact to $2,250,157 each year.

"Community Scholar Designation," which provides access to lecture series, garden parti es,

art gatherings, and Kansas State libraries and Wi - Fi, with the goal of creating multiple

touchpoints that help new residents feel connected and invested in the community over the

long term.

4.2 How does this recruitment effort align with your broader local or regional economic

development strategies?

Local and regional economic development strategies collectively reflect the importance of

increasing Riley County’s population. The county was awarded a Home in the Heartland

grant in 2025 with the goal of recruiting 20 new households to Riley County. We’v e nearly

achieved that in just over a year of starting the program. This new recruitment effort aims

to bring an additional 15 households to Riley County.

Our strategies again highlight the many ways increased population adds to the county’s

economy:

Expanding the workforce : More people in a community contribute to the available labor

force. Several of the movers we recruited with this past effort brought partners and

children with them. Those additional family members may need jobs as well and the

children will attend local schools. New people bring new skills and ability, expanding the

available labor pool.

Increases consumer spending : The more people in a community, the more spending on

ancillary items such as restaurants, entertainment, gas, and more. More people will spend

money will directly impact the local economy.

Grows the tax base : People who move here will either buy or rent a home. The more

public services. Increased tax base expands Riley County’s economy.

Attracts new businesses, investment, and adds opportunities for entrepreneurship : This is

a part of expanding the workforce and adding business opportunities. There will be

increased potential for new and expanding businesses because there are more people to

fill these positions. Additional people may also encourage new ideas and star tups. New and

expanded businesses have a positive impact on the regional economy.
