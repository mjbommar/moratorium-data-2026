SECTION 8: PROJECT DESCRIPTION


The Special Traffic Enforcement Program (STEP) is designed for local law enforcement agencies to

participate in designated traffic safety mobilizations to improve driver behavior and decrease crashes,

fatalities and serious injuries. High visibility enforcement is used by agencies to address their

communities’ most significant traffic safety issues. Enforcement is supported by community education

and publicity to deliver effective safety countermeasures in Kansas communities. The mobilizations

selected as fofocus areas by the agency are detailed in Section 11 and correspond to the local problem

identification provided in Section 6.

STEP grant funding will directly address resource gaps by providing the dedicated overtime hours

necessary to execute targeted enforcement initiatives. To address impaired driving and underage

drinking near Kansas State University and Fort Riley, officers will deploy to entertainment districts and

student residential areas for dedicated DUI p patrols and roving  minor in possession details.

Non-enforcement overtime hours will be used to support dispatcher overtime for coordinated saturation

patrols with multiple officers on duty, including during peak traffic hours and weekend bar closing

times.  The agency may purchase PBTs and foldable cones to support impaired driving enforcement and

saturation patrols.
