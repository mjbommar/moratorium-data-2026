                               RESOLUTION NO. 2026-06-561

               A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
               CITY OF LYNN HAVEN, FLORIDA, DIRECTING CITY
               STAFF NOT TO ACCEPT, PROCESS, OR ACT UPON ANY
               APPLICATION    FOR   DEVELOPMENT      PERMITS,
               DEVELOPMENT ORDERS, SITE PLANS, REZONINGS,
               SPECIAL EXCEPTIONS, VARIANCES, OR BUILDING
               PERMITS FOR DATA CENTERS OR AI DATA CENTERS
               PENDING THE ADOPTION OF AN ORDINANCE; AND
               PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

        WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Lynn Haven, Florida (the "City"), plans
to introduce an Ordinance (the "Moratorium Ordinance"), which would impose a temporary one-
year moratorium on the acceptance, processing, and approval of applications for Data Centers and
AI Data Centers within the corporate limits of the City; and

       WHEREAS, the Moratorium Ordinance is proceeding through the regular adoption
process required by Section 166.041(3)(a), Florida Statutes, including two readings and a duly
noticed public hearing; and

        WHEREAS, Section 163.326, Florida Statutes, as created by Chapter 2026-65, Laws of
Florida, expressly provides that local governments shall maintain the authority to exercise the
powers and responsibilities for comprehensive planning and land development regulation with
respect to large load customers, including data centers; and

        WHEREAS, the City's current Comprehensive Plan and Unified Land Development Code
do not contain specific provisions, standards, or criteria addressing the unique planning,
infrastructure, environmental, and compatibility considerations presented by data center and AI
data center development; and

        WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the acceptance and processing of applications
for data center or AI data center development during the pendency of the Moratorium Ordinance
could undermine the purpose of the moratorium by creating vested rights or entitlements before
the City has had an opportunity to study the impacts of such development and adopt appropriate
regulations; and

        WHEREAS, the City Commission finds it necessary and appropriate to direct City staff,
on an interim basis, not to accept, process, or act upon any such applications until the Moratorium
Ordinance is considered and acted upon by the City Commission;

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Commission of the City of Lynn
Haven, Florida, that the City Manager, Director of Planning, Building Official, and all other City
staff are hereby directed not to accept, process, review, or act upon any application for a
development permit, development order, building permit, site plan approval, rezoning, special
exception, variance, comprehensive plan amendment, or any other official action of the City
having the effect of permitting the construction, establishment, or operation of a Data Center or
AI Data Center, as those terms are defined in the Moratorium Ordinance or used in common
language pending final action by the City Commission on the Moratorium Ordinance;

         BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this Resolution shall remain in effect until the earlier
of: (a) the date on which the City Commission adopts the Moratorium Ordinance; (b) the date on
which the City Commission formally declines to adopt the Moratorium Ordinance; or (c) sixty
(60) days from the date of adoption of this Resolution, whichever occurs first.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this Resolution is effective immediately upon
passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED at the regular meeting of the City of Lynn Haven, in Bay
County, Florida on the 23rd of June, 2026.



                                            CITY OF LYNN HAVEN, FLORIDA
