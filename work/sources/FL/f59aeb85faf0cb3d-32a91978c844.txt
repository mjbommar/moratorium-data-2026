CODE, ORDINANCE 2003-067, AS AMENDED, AS FOLLOWS: REVISION FOR

ENACTING A ONE-YEAR MORATORIUM ON THE ACCEPTANCE, REVIEW,

PROCESSING, AND APPROVAL OF ZONING APPLICATIONS AND RELATED

REQUESTS FOR ZONING APPROVALS FOR LARGE-SCALE DATA

CENTERS THAT ARE LARGE LOAD CUSTOMERS AS DEFINED IN SECTION

366.043, FLORIDA STATUTES, AMENDING ARTICLE 4- USE REGULATIONS;

PROVIDING FOR: INTERPRETATION OF CAPTIONS; REPEAL OF LAWS IN

CONFLICT; SEVERABILITY; A SAVINGS CLAUSE; INCLUSION IN THE

UNIFIED LAND DEVELOPMENT CODE; AND, AN EFFECTIVE DATE.

WHEREAS, Section 163.3202, Florida Statutes, mandates the County compile Land

Development Regulations consistent with its Comprehensive Plan into a single Land Development

Code; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to this statute the Palm Beach County Board of C County Commissioners

(BCC) adopted the Unified Land Development Code (ULDC), Ordinance 2003-067, as amended from

time to time; and

WHEREAS, on May 7, 2026, the Governor approved Chapter 2026-65, Laws of Florida

(Senate Bill 484); and

WHEREAS, Chapter 2026-65, Laws of Florida created Section 163.326, Florida Statutes, the

intent of w which is to allow local governments, including Palm Beach County, to regulate data centers

and large load customers through local comprehensive planning and land development regulations;

and

WHEREAS, Section 163.326(2), Florida Statutes states that local governments "shall

maintain the authority to exercise the powers and responsibilities for comprehensive planning and

land development regulation granted by law with respect to large load customers;" and

WHEREAS, these statutes w were enacted after Chapter 2 2025-190, S Section 28, Laws of F Florida,

was enacted, which prohibits counties listed in the Federal Disaster Declarations for Hurricanes

Debby, Helene, or Milton from proposing or adopting more restrictive or burdensome amendments to

their comprehensive plans or land development regulations or more restrictive or burdensome

procedures concerning review, approval, or issuance of a site plan, development permit, or

development order until October 1, 2027; and

WHEREAS, Palm Beach County was listed in the Federal Disaster Declaration for Hurricane

Milton; and

WHEREAS, because Senate Bill 484 was enacted after Chapter 2025-190, Section 28, Laws

of Florida, and expressly authorizes local governments and counties to regulate large load customers

through comprehensive planning and land development regulations, it supersedes t the restrictions set

forth in Chapter 2025-190, Section 28, Laws of Florida; and

WHEREAS, in order t to promote effective development regulation of large-scale data centers,

which, as defined in this Ordinance, are large load customers pursuant to Chapter 2026-65, Laws of

Florida, the BCC wishes to preserve the status quo while researching, studying, and analyzing the

potential impact of large-scale data centers upon adjacent uses, the surrounding area, the

environment, surrounding property values, demand for county services, and other aspects of the

general welfare; and

WHEREAS, the BCC has determined that the proposed temporary moratorium on the

development of large-scale data centers will allow Palm Beach County a sufficient period of t time to

best formulate land development regulations and comprehensive plan policies that appropriately

govern the use of real property for large-scale data centers; and

WHEREAS, the Land Development Regulation Commission has found this amendment t to the

ULDC to be consistent with the Palm Beach County Comprehensive Plan; and

WHEREAS, the BCC hereby elects to conduct its public hearings on this Ordinance at 9:30

a.m.; and

WHEREAS, the BCC has conducted public hearings t to consider t this amendment t to the ULDC

in a manner consistent with the requirements set forth in Section 125.66, Florida Statutes.

PALM BEACH COUNTY, FLORIDA, as follows:

Section 1. Adoption

The revisions set forth in Exhibit 1, attached hereto and made a part hereof, are hereby

adopted.

Section 2. Interpretation of C Captions

All headings of a articles, sections, paragraphs, and sub-paragraphs used in this Ordinance are

intended for the convenience of usage only and have no effect on interpretation.

Section 3. Repeal of Laws in Conflict

All local laws and ordinances in conflict with any provisions of this Ordinance are hereby

repealed to the extent of such conflict.

Section 4. Severability

If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, phrase, word, map, diagram, or any other item

contained in this Ordinance is for any reason held by the Court to be unconstitutional, inoperative,

void, or otherwise invalid, such holding shall not affect the remainder of t this Ordinance.
