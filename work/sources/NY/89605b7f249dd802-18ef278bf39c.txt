NEW BUSINESS: Approval- Local Law #2 of 2024- Moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Systems:


A motion was made by Councilman Burke, seconded by Councilman Dugan approving Local Law

#2 of 2024 that will impose a six-month moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Systems in the

Town of Newstead to allow the Town Board to consider amendments, revisions and additions to

Chapter 180 of the Town Code.

(Resolution) Izydorczak-Aye, Dugan-Aye, Burke-Aye, Jendrowski-Aye, Mutter- Aye

Carried Unanimously

Privilege of the Floor/Question Period: The following residents were in attendance to voice their


concerns with the noise at Niagara Specialty Metal:

Martin & Lori Schuman, 74 Jackson Street Michael & Kathryn Neelon, 3 Brodie’s Way Adrian Guadalupe, 5 Brodie’s Way


The residents in attendance were looking for the Board’s support with the noise concerns they have

with Niagara Specialty Metals. The banging noises typically start around 6am most mornings and has

gone as late as midnight some nights. They have filed multiple complaints with the CEO with no

resolution. They would like to know what the next step would be and wish to have some

communication from the CEO.

Councilman Jendrowski explained to the residents what Niagara Specialty Metals does, they do run 2

shifts, and what the noise is that they are hearing. Also explained that the property is zoned industrial

and what they are doing is completely permitted.

Supervisor Izydorczak stated that she will speak with the CEO and reach out to Bob Shabala from

Niagara Specialty.

The residents are also very concerned about the speed on Hake Road and are requesting a traffic study

be done. The Supervisor will have a resolution drawn up to be approved at our next meeting to initiate

that process.
