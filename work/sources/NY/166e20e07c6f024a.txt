Section 202-7          East Side - TWO HOUR PARKING EXCEPT
                       SATURDAY, SUNDAY & HOLIDAYS - starting

                       curbline of Delmar Avenue, then south

Hempstead as constituted by local law number one of nineteen
hundred and sixty-nine, said Section last amended by local law
number six of two thousand twenty five is hereby amended by
repealing therein "REGULATIONS AND RESTRICTIONS" to limit parking
at the following location:


FRANKLIN SQUARE        ROOSEVELT AVENUE (TH 535/24) -
Section 202-7          East Side - TWO HOUR PARKING EXCEPT
