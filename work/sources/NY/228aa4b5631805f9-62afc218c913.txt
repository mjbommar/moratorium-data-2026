NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING FOR LOCAL LAW NO.3-2024 TO REVISE TOWN OF WALWORTH ZONING LEGISLATION TO ADDRESS SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS AND BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS


PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that a proposed Local Law is under consideration by Town

Board of the Town of Walworth, New York, designated as Local Law No. 3-2024 “to

Revise Town of Walworth Zoning Legislation to Address Solar Energy Systems and

Battery Energy Storage Systems,” and its purpose is to implement updated regulations to

more comprehensively address Solar Energy Systems and Battery Energy Storage

Systems in Town.

PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that said proposed Local Law is on file at the

Walworth Town Clerk’s Office located at 3600 Lorraine Dr., Walworth, New York,

where it is available for public inspection during regular business hours.

PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that a public hearing upon said proposed Local

Law has been scheduled for the 5th day of September, 2024, at 6:30 PM, to be held at the

Walworth Town Hall, 3600 Lorraine Drive, Walworth, NY. An opportunity to be heard

in regard thereto will then and there be given to members of the public. Written

comments may also be directed to the Walworth Town Clerk, Walworth Town Hall, 3600

Lorraine Dr., Walworth, NY.

Dated: August 15, 2024

BY THE ORDER OF THE WALWORTH TOWN BOARD


Jenna Camacho

Town Clerk
