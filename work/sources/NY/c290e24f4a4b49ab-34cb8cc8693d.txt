6. NEW MEMBERSHIP – – MIKE ELMENDORF – – TOWN OF JOHNSTOWN:


Supervisor VanValkenburgh proposed Mike Elmendorf to fill a vacancy on the Fulton County Planning

Board. Currently, no Town representative serves on the Board. A county resident for 38 years, he served as

Chief Investigator and Deputy Director of Licensing for the New York State Department of State’s Division

of Licensing Services.

Mr. Elmendorf served on the NYS Mortgage Fraud Task Force, the DCJS Security Guard Advisory Council,

the Governor’s HALT Task Force on aggressive lending tactics, and as an advisor to the NYS Joint

Commission for E-Licensing and several related boards or committees. Concurrently, he served as a Fulton

County Deputy Fire Coordinator and Fire Investigator, retiring in 2014, and represented the office and my

fire company on the Fulton County Fire Advisory Board.

Since my retirement, Mr. Elmendorf has served on the committee reconstructing and redeveloping the Town

of Johnstown Solar Energy Facilities Law 2025, and currently serves in a similar capacity on regulations

governing Battery Energy Storage Systems. Recently, he has served on the Town of Johnstown Zoning

Board of Appeals.

MOTION: To recommend that Mike Elmendorf be added to the Fulton County Planning

Board and to forward to the Fulton County Public Works Committee.

MADE BY: Jimmy Anderson

SECONDED: Jay Pierz

VOTE: Unanimous

7. NYS GENERAL MUNICIPAL LAW SECTION 239-M:


In accordance with New York State General Municipal Law §239-m, the following applications were

forwarded to the County Planning Board for review:

A. Town of Perth Town Board – Moratorium on Renewable Energy


The Town of Perth Town Board is looking to enact a 12-month moratorium, referred to as Local Law

Imposing a Moratorium on Renewable Energy, which includes battery storage, solar, and wind energy.

The reasons the Town Board is enacting this moratorium are as follows:

1. Work on the Comprehensive Plan to align Local Law on Solar has been delayed due to pressing,

time-sensitive matters before the Perth Town Planning Board.

2. Results of the Town-wide survey of residents to gather input and desires on updating the Town

Comprehensive Plan, specifically regarding solar project development in the Town, are not yet

available.

MOTION: Recognizing that there are no regional impacts that could occur on the Town of

Perth’s moratoria, the Fulton County Planning Board offers no recommendation.

MADE BY: Jerry Moore

SECONDED: John Kessler

VOTE: Unanimous
