### 20261 document

* [A Local Law Amending Chapter 51 of The Town Code Entitled “Taxation"](<https://www.northhempsteadny.gov/Documents/Government/Proposed Local Laws/2026/Chapter 51 Taxation 4-17-26.pdf?t=202604201514390>)

document date 02-27-2026

The purpose of the local law is to amend Chapter 51 of the Town Code entitled “Taxation” in order to (1) establish a real property tax exemption for eligible senior citizens to 65%; (2) establish a real property exemption of no more than 50% of the assessed value of the primary residence for surviving spouses of police officers killed in the line of duty; (3) establish a partial exemption for active duty service members whose military station places residence in the State of New York and has served in a combat zone within the last year; and (4) establish a partial exemption for surviving spouses of volunteer firefighters and ambulance workers.
