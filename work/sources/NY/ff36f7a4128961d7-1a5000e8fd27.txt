NOW HIRING

Zoning Clerk

Town of North Harmony – Code Enforcement Department

The Town of North Harmony Code Enforcement Department is seeking a dependable, organized, and detail-oriented individual to fill a part-time Zoning Clerk / Officer position up to 12 hours per week.

The successful candidate will assist the Code Enforcement Department with monitoring and maintaining compliance with Town and zoning ordinances.

Job Responsibilities

Duties include, but are not limited to:

Receiving and reviewing complaints and zoning violations.
Tracking and maintaining records of all open complaints and violations.
Preparing letters, notices, and orders to property owners.
Preparing documentation and records for court appearances.
Processing and preparing Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) requests received by the Zoning Department.
Assist with other duties as needed

Qualifications and Skills

Proficiency in Microsoft Word and Excel.
Ability to learn and utilize the Town's zoning software system.
Knowledge of Town ordinances and zoning regulations is beneficial.
Strong organizational and recordkeeping skills.
Good written and verbal communication skills.
Ability to communicate professionally by telephone and in person.
Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks.
