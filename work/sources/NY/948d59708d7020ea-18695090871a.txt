4:00 p.m.

A specially scheduled meeting of the Town of Montgomery Town Board was held in the Town

Government Center, 110 Bracken Road, Montgomery, New York, on the above date and time.

Present:

Supervisor:  L. Stephen Brescia

Council Member:  Cindy Voss

Council Member: Mark Hoyt

Council Member:  Sherry Melick

Council Member:  Michael Setteducato

Town Clerk:  Tara Stickles

Also Present:  William Frank, Attorney for the Town

Audience

Supervisor Brescia called the meeting to order at 4:35 p.m., asking all to rise for the Pledge of

Allegiance.

Town Clerk Stickles called the roll.

Attorney Frank explained the Board had a Public Hearing on Introductory Local Law No. 1 of 2025,

which is to remove battery energy storage systems as a permitted use in any zone within the Town.

Prior to the Public Hearing, the Board declared itself as lead agency for SEQRA purposes and

preliminarily classified the action as a Type 1. The Town sent out a Notice of Intent to serve as lead

agency to all interested agencies in the County and to the New York State DEC. More than 30 days

elapsed since that notice was sent out and no other agency expressed interest and deferred to the

Town as lead agency. The matter was referred to Orange County Planning, pursuant to General

Municipal Law 239-M, and we received back written response from the County stating this was a

local determination. The matter was also referred to the Town of Montgomery Planning Board by

correspondence dated February 11th. The Board received non-substantive comments back from the

Town Planning Board which will be incorporated in the file version to give to the Clerk in the event

that the Board decides to adopt this law today. He further explained that the public hearing was

closed on February 19thother than keeping the hearing open for the submission of written

,

comments. The Board received one correspondence today from Joseph Keenan who is in support of

the adoption of the Local Law. We’ve prepared a long-form full environmental assessment form.

__________Town Board Minutes __________pg. 2_______ February 25, 2025 ________

The findings set forth that by adopting this law, there are no adverse environmental impacts. There

is a resolution before the Board to adopt a negative declaration under the SEQRA regulations. If the

Law is adopted this afternoon, it will take battery energy storage systems out of the Code, and it

will cancel out the moratorium once the law is filed with the New York State Department of State.

Resolve

Motion by Councilwoman Melick, seconded by Councilwoman Voss that the Town Board of the

Town of Montgomery adopts the Resolution adopting Local Law No. 2 of 2025, entitled “A Local

Law of The Town of Montgomery, County Of Orange, Amending Chapter 235 of The Town Code

Zoning and The Accompanying Tables In The Zoning Code To Remove Battery Energy Storage

Systems As A Specially Permitted Use Within The Town”.

Roll Call: 5 Ayes/0 Nays/s/0 Absent Carried: 5-0-0

**********************************************************************

Councilwoman Melick explained that she felt there were just too many unknowns concerning

Battery Energy Storage Systems, the Fire Departments are concerned as well about these systems

and that they could create a hazardous situation for Town residents. She stated that the matter can

be addressed in the future as well.

Supervisor Brescia concurred with Councilwoman Melick and stated that there are just too many

unknowns about these systems.

Attorney Frank advised the Board that he will incorporate into the law, the minor comments that

the Town Planning Board had given the Town Board.
