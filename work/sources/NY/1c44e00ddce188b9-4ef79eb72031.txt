Section 1. Title.

This Local Law shall be referred to as “A LOCAL LAW AW “ Amending Local Law No. 1 of

2024 “a Local Law, w, which extended for a further 12 months, Local Law No. 4 of 2023 a

six-month moratorium prohibiting the review and approval of clean renewable energy

technology businesses devoted to research, education, distribution or application of technological

innovation in alternative energy uses in the ToTown of WaWarwick for an additional six (6) months to

expire June 30, 2025.

Section 2. Definitions.

“Code” means the "Code of the ToTown of WaWarwick".

“ToTown” means the ToTown of WaWarwick.

“ToTown Board” means the ToTown Board of the ToTown of WaWarwick.

“ToTown Clerk” means the ToTown Clerk of the ToTown of WaWarwick.

“Clean Energy Business” means Business Use No. 53 set forth in 164 Attachment 1, §

164-40M, TaTable of Use Requirements of the Zoning Law of the ToTown of WaWarwick.

Section 3. Legislative Findings, Purpose and Intent.

The ToTown Board has determined that the Clean Energy Business regulations as written in

the Zoning Law lack the specificity sufffficient to oversee the approval and operation of such uses,

including but not limited to Battery Energy Storage, so as to protect the public health, safety and

welfare, and as certain revisions to the Zoning Law may be required to afffford such protections.

Pursuant to the statutory powers vested in the ToTown to protect the health, safety and

welfare of its residents, the ToTown Board of the ToTown of WaWarwick hereby declares a six-month

moratorium on the submission, processing and/or approval of any applications of Clean Energy

Business use, including but not limited to the placement of any Battery Energy Storage,

pursuant to applicable provisions of the Zoning Law pending the ToTown Board’s completion and

adoption of certain amendments to the Zoning Law that through their review are found to be

necessary to better implement the purpose and intent of said law.

Section 4. Scope of Controls

During the effffective period of this Local Law, w, no applications or approvals for the

establishment of Clean Energy Businesses shall be accepted or processed by any Departments of

the ToTown, and no approvals for such use shall be issued by any department or Board of the

ToTown as provided for in the Zoning Law.

Section 5. TeTerm.

The moratorium imposed by this Local Law shall be in effffect for six (6) months from the

effffective date of this Local Law.

Section 7. Penalties.

Any person, firm or entity that operates a Clean Energy Business without required

approvals shall otherwise violate any of the provisions of this Local Law shall be subject to:

A. Such penalties as may otherwise be provided by applicable local laws, ordinances,

rules, regulations of the ToTown for violations; and

B. A fine not to exceed $500.00 per day from the date that any action is taken in

contravention of this local law, w, together with any other civil remedies available at

law; and

C. Injunctive relief in favor of the ToTown to cease any and all such actions which

conflict with this Local Law and, if necessary, y, to remove any such uses that may

have taken place in violation of this Local Law.

Any application accepted or approval granted in violation of this Local Law shall be null

and void.
