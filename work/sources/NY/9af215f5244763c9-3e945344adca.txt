STATE OF NEW YORK COUNTY OF ALBANY TOWN OF BERNE PUBLIC HEARING – 6:30 PM 6:30 PM PUBLIC HEARING – PROPOSED LOCAL LAW NO. 3-2026 Proposed Solar Moratorium Extension ROLL CALL


Supervisor Giebelhaus called the Public Hearing to order at 6:31 PM and took roll call

attendance. Present were Supervisor Giebelhaus, Councilmember Bunzey,

Councilmember Duncan, Councilmember la Cour, and Councilmember Miller,

constituting a quorum. Also present were Town Clerk De Oliveira, Deputy Town Clerk

Local Law No. 3-2026, extending the Town's solar moratorium, Supervisor Giebelhaus

asked the public to keep comments to three minutes and if there was a group with
