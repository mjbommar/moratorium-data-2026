3. Resident Concerns


Supervisor Drozd opened the floor for Public Comment; and Stan Grabowski of

4 Kissing Bridge Lane asked the Board if there was any update on when the water project

is going to start, since it was supposed to have begun in May. Supervisor Drozd told Mr.

Grabowski that there was an issue at the KB Water plant that was being addressed.

Today he was told the water tests all came back great from the health department. He

believes that the new date to start is 6/24/26. Town Attorney Attea spoke in regards to

the easements that have been received from residents. Some of those easements were

submitted to the engineer on two-sided paper, which the Erie County Clerk’s office will

not accept. Mr. Rogers from MDA will be going to those residents to get those signed

again, but it should not delay anything.  Mr. Grabowski asked if all of the parts and

pieced are available-Supervisor Drozd said he believes everything is all set with that.

The contractor has been great, and has held his prices since last year.  He should be

finished within a week of beginning.

Kathy Lux addressed the Board with a slight concern. She used to be able to post

the menu for the Senior Center on their Facebook page, and now the comments have been

shut off and no one outside of the Senior Center is able to make posts on the page.

Supervisor Drozd deferred this question to Town Clerk Safford, since she attended the

NYS Town Clerk’s seminar in regards to social media posting. Town Clerk Safford

explained that the changes to the Facebook posts are because of the records that need to

be kept on all social media posts for the Town. The Town has implemented a new Social

Media Policy that states that the public can no longer comment on posts, and if they want

something placed on a Town Facebook page, they need to contact the administrator of

that page to request that it is posted. Ms. Lux wants the menu to still be posted on the

Facebook page, and Town Clerk Safford let her know that she needs to contact Senior

Center Director or her assistant and they would be the ones to put that on the page. Ms.

Lux asked if the Town Historian’s Facebook page has the same rules for posting

comments-all three of the Town’s Facebook pages now have the same rules (dog control,

senior center and the Bensley center).

Village Trustee Barbara Lipka also had some questions on the rules about the new

Town Social Media Policy, and Board members and Town Clerk Safford discussed those

with her.

c) Approval to purchase new Cab and Chassis 10-wheeler- Supt. Heim was at a Highway

Supt. Meeting a few months ago where the truck manufacturers came around and let

them know that there are going to be new levels of safety and emissions coming out on

all trucks. The price jump for these new levels as of August 1st will be a minimum of

$15,000 for just the emissions changes. The oldest truck at the Highway garage is 2012,

and the six by six is a 2015 which needs some work. Supt. Heim would like to sit down

at some point and discuss a plan for the four trucks they have. It would be good to get

them on an eight-year rotation-buy a new one every other year. If the new truck is

ordered now, but not picked up until next year, then the price jump will not take effect,

and it can get paid for out of next year’s budget.

Motion by Supervisor Drozd, seconded by Council Member Krzemien, to

approve buying the truck with the Sourcewell bid price of $205,391.98, with delivery for

2027. Council Member Drake asked if this can be cancelled before delivery, and Supt.

Heim let him know that it can be cancelled before it is built. Council Member Drake said

at this time the balance of the B fund is unknown so purchasing the truck is going out on

a limb. Kaufman Utz had difficulty balancing the B fund at the end of last year. 5 voting

aye, 0 nays.  Carried.

k) Approve Clinton Brown Co. Architecture to evaluate Town Hall condition for grants

Supervisor Drozd has been speaking to Deputy Mayor Baker about grants for historic

buildings in the Village in regards to work that needs to be done at Town Hall, which is

on both the State and National Historic register. The most noticeable work that needs to

be done is on windows and masonry. Before the Town can apply for any grants to have

work done a report needs to be completed documenting the conditions of the exterior of

the building. The anticipated cost for the report is $20,000, and it would show repair cost

versus replacement cost. There is no grant funding for this type of study, but there is

$25,000 in the budget for a grant writer in the Supervisor line, so Supervisor Drozd is

asking to take up to $20,000 out of that budget line to do this before winter.  Council

Member Zittel stated that is feels good to work with the Village.
