D.1. PROPOSED LOCAL LAW – CONTINUING A TEMORARY MORATORIUM ON LARGE-SCALE BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS (BESS)


Respond to a referral from the Town Board for a recommendation on a proposed local law “Continuing a

Temporary Town-Wide Moratorium on Development of Large Scale Battery Energy Storage System Installations

Within the Town of Poughkeepsie.”

__________________________________________________________________________________________

On January 22, 2025 the Town Board adopted Resolution 1:22 - #14 of 2025 which, among other things, referred a

proposed local law extending a temporary moratorium on large-scale battery energy storage systems (BESS) to the

Planning Board for an advisory recommendation pursuant to §210-154. The resolution and the proposed local law are

attached. The resolution also set a Town th

Board Public Hearing for February 19, 2025. It is expected that on February 19

the Town Board will receive comments and adjourn the public  hearing to March 5, 2025 to allow time for Dutchess

County and for the Planning Board to provide their respective recommendations.

As noted in the local law, currently the Town of Poughkeepsie Zoning Code does not contain a definition of a Large Scale

Battery Energy Storage System Installation, or specific use,  area or bulk regulations for such use. The Town Board

recognizes that the Town needs to study and analyze many considerations that affect the preparation of local legislation to

regulate the future creation and siting of Large Scale Battery Energy Storage System Installations in a manner consistent

with public health, safety and community character. A moratorium has been in place since March of last year, and the

resolution describes the many steps that the Town has taken to gather information and evaluate options for regulating

these facilities since that time. The Town Board believes it is necessary to continunue the temporary moratorium for an

additional period of six months in order to permit the Town  adequate time in which to consider and draft suitable

legislation for this purpose.

Staff recommends that the Planning Board convey a positive recommendation to the Town Board regarding adoption of

the proposed local law.
