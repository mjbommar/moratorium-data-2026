NOTICE OF ADOPTION TOWN OF WARWICK
LOCAL LAW No.4 of 2023

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that after a public hearing introduced on July 13, 2023 and adopted on September 14, 2023 The Town Board duly enacted Local Law No. 4 of 2023 entitled “Adopt Local Law No. 4 of 2023 –a Local Law establishing a six-month moratorium prohibiting the review and approval of clean renewable energy technology businesses devoted to research, education, distribution or application of technological innovation in alternative energy uses in the town of Warwick.” A complete copy of Local Law No. 4 of 2023 is available for inspection at the Town Clerks office or at townofwarwick.org.

Dated: September 20, 2023

By order of the Town
Board of Warwick Town of Warwick
Eileen Astorino
Town Clerk
