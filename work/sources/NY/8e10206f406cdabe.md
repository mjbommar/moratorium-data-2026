**TOWN OF TYRE**

**NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING**

**PLEASE TAKE NOTICE**, that a public hearing will be held by the Town Board of the Town of Tyre on the 22nd day of July, 2026, at 6:30 p.m., at the Tyre Town Hall, 1082 Gravel Road, Seneca Falls, New York, regarding the adoption of a Local Law of the Town of Tyre for the year 2026 entitled “A Local Law Establishing a Moratorium on Data Centers and Artificial Intelligence Computing Facilities within the Town of Tyre”. This local law would establish within the Town of Tyre a one (1) year moratorium which would prohibit the review, approval, creation, establishment, placement, relocation, construction, reconstruction, enlargement, modification or erection anywhere within the Town of any Artificial Intelligence Computing Facility or Data Center, as well as the processing, granting or issuing of any preliminary or final approvals for site plans or subdivisions, building permits, variances, special use permits or other approvals for or including any Artificial Intelligence Computing Facility or Data Center anywhere within the Town during the pendency of the local law.

Any person shall be entitled to be heard upon said proposed Local Law at such public hearing. Copies of said proposed Local Law are available for review at the Tyre Town Hall, 1082 Gravel Road, Seneca Falls, New York.

This by Resolution of the Town Board of the Town of Tyre.

Carolyn Sosnowski, Town Clerk
