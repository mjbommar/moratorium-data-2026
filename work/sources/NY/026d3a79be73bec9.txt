 * The town clerk was contacted regarding available space for public use. A local Cato
mom who helps run the local Girl Scout group, was interested in finding a place to hold
their groups meetings. She stated that the previous place no longer had availability to
accommodate the troop. Other avenues resulted in places asking for money the troop
simply does not have. The question asked was if the town hall could be made available
to them every Thursday from 5:30 – 7 pm. There would be eight girls and three adults.
They would do typical Girl Scout meetings. Things in the meeting might be cookie
sales/strategies, seasonal arts and crafts, and things of that nature. They offered to buy
office supplies such as toilet paper, hand soap or paper towels, in lieu of use. The troop
carries their own insurance and would be in the building during the same time the town
clerk holds hours. The only time there may be a scheduling conflict would be in the
evenings when the Planning Board meets. On those evenings they would close their
meetings at 6:30. The concern expressed by the board was once the town allowed use
to one group other groups may want to do the same. Mr. Jim Mignogna, Victory Fire
Department President, offered to contact the Girl Scout troop leader and see if the fire
house could accommodate the troops needs.
