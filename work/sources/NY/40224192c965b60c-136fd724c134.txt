Welcome to the Town of Bethlehem’s Meeting Management Web Portal
Here you will be able to find IDA, Planning Board, Town Board and Zoning Board of Appeals meeting agendas, agenda attachments, minutes, live streaming videos and on-demand archived videos.

To view a meeting video, click the Video link listed to the right on the meeting calendar below. The media viewer will appear with the meeting agenda listed below it. You can click the agenda items to automatically advance the video to that agenda item. Each agenda item will also provide you access to any associated agenda attachments. For on-demand archived videos, you can also view the voting record for the item.

For live streaming meeting videos, a green LIVE link will appear above this Welcome message when the broadcast begins.

The linked Web Portal User Guide will give you more detailed information on the features of the meeting management system. We hope you enjoy your viewing experience!

Tip: If you wish to view one agenda item’s attachment(s), instead of opening the consolidated agenda pdf links on this page, click on the meeting date and it will open the agenda web outline. On the agenda web outline you can choose individual agenda attachments.

Meeting schedule:
All meetings take place at Town Hall in the auditorium.

Bethlehem IDA - last Friday of the month
Planning Board - first and third Tuesdays of the month
Town Board - second and fourth Wednesdays of the month
Zoning Board of Appeals - first and third Wednesdays of the month
