               Data Center Moratorium Updates:

    May 2026 – Reach out by phone or email (preferred) to be
    considered for Data Storage / Industrial Code advisory committee

    5/7/2026 – Public Hearing on Local Law #4 – 6-month
    moratorium on data storage centers. 6:30PM at Lysander Town
    Hall. Comments at the Public Hearing will be limited to the favor
    or opposition of the 6-month moratorium itself, not the pros and
    cons of data centers. Moratorium passed. Expires 11/7/26

    4/27/2026 – Town of Lysander Planning Board passes
    resolution 5-0 to recommend Town Board approval of Local Law
    #4 of 2026, 6-month Moratorium on Data Storage Centers.

    4/24/2026 – Lysander Town Board passes resolution 4-0 to
    refer Local Law #4 of 2026, 6-month Moratorium on Data Storage
    Centers, to both the Onondaga County Planning Board and Town
    of Lysander Planning Board for recommendations.

    Lysander Town Board also passes resolution 4-0 to advertise and
    hold Public Hearing on Thursday, May 7th for Local Law #4 of
    2026 at 6:30PM ay Lysander Town Hall.

    4/16/2026 – Lysander Town Board passes resolution 4-0 to
    allow Town Attorney to draft a Moratorium on Data Centers.
