valued cultural resource to the comm~ty and currently comprise more than ten pei=eent-(1 Q~~f
the population of Freedom, which population is rapidly growing and it is the sense of the Town
Board that it desires to make certain that this vulnerable population is protected and that this
vulnerable population is permitted to ob.serve their religion and engage in their religious practices
without interference and in peace, and ·;

WHEREAS, the Town of.Freedom has been apprised that in the Case 17-F-0282-Application

provided by Professor Steven Nolt regarding the Swartentruber Amish and such testimony
established that the Swartzentruber Amish worship on a rotating basis in their family homes
(''Amish Homes") and other buildings on their properties such as barns that are of sufficient size
and quality that the church district can conduct religious· services and proceedings therein ("Amish
Buildings") and that Amish Homes and Amish Buildings are churches; now therefore be it
