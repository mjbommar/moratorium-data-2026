NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Town Board of the Town of Pound Ridge will hold a public hearing on March 24, 2026, commencing at 7:30 pm, or as soon thereafter as time allows, to provide an opportunity for public comment on an extension of the moratorium with respect to Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

The public may attend the meeting remotely or in person at the Town House. Those wishing to view the meeting remotely may access the video livestream at https://vimeo.com/event/2423847.

Those wishing to join the meeting via videoconference must register by 12:00 noon on Tuesday, March 24, 2026. Email townclerk@townofpoundridge.gov to receive Zoom information.

Erin D. Trostle, Town Clerk
Dated at Pound Ridge, New York
March 4, 2026
