Section 1.     STATEMENT OF LEGISLATIVE INTENT:

         The Town Board finds that recent advances in cloud computing, artificial intelligence,
digital storage, and related technologies have resulted in proposals for increasingly large-scale data
centers and artificial intelligence computing facilities throughout New York State and the nation.

        The Town Board further finds that such facilities may present unique land use,
infrastructure, utility, environmental, public safety, and community character impacts that are not
adequately addressed by the Town's existing comprehensive plan, zoning regulations and other
local laws.

       Potential impacts include, but are not limited to:

   •   Significant electrical demand and associated transmission infrastructure;
   •   Construction of electrical substations and related utility facilities;
   •   Water consumption and wastewater impacts;
   •   Noise generated by cooling systems, generators, transformers, and mechanical equipment;
   •   Traffic impacts during construction and operation;
   •   Visual impacts and lighting;
   •   Emergency response considerations;
   •   Impacts on agricultural resources and rural character;
   •   Compatibility with existing land uses; and
   •   Other impacts not currently addressed by the Town's zoning regulations and other local
       laws.

         The Town Board finds that it is in the best interests of the Town to temporarily suspend
the review, approval or creation of all new data centers and artificial intelligence computing
facilities on lands within the Town of Tyre. This will afford the Town sufficient time to review
the Town of Tyre Comprehensive Plan, the Zoning Law of the Town of Tyre, the Town of Tyre
Site Plan Review Regulations, and other land use controls, local laws and ordinances and, if
determined to be necessary, amend pertinent sections of the Town of Tyre Comprehensive Plan or
of existing local laws or to draft and adopt new regulations and/or laws.

         Accordingly, the Town Board hereby determines that a temporary moratorium is necessary
to preserve the status quo by ensuring that any data centers and artificial intelligence computing
facilities are reviewed under the new regulations and not under the existing laws and that doing so
will protect the public health, safety, and welfare while such actions are undertaken.

Section 2.     DEFINITIONS

A.      ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE COMPUTING FACILITY: A Data Center whose primary
purpose is to house or operate computing equipment used for the development, training, fine-
tuning, testing, deployment, execution, or inference of artificial intelligence or machine-learning
models or systems, including associated servers, graphics processing units, specialized computing
accelerators, networking equipment, cooling systems, backup power generation, electrical
equipment, and other supporting infrastructure.
B.     ZONING ENFORCEMENT OFFICER: Town of Tyre Zoning Enforcement Officer

C.      DATA CENTER: Any building, structure, facility, campus, or portion thereof whose
primary purpose is to house and operate centralized computing equipment and associated
infrastructure for the storage, processing, management, hosting, analysis, transmission, or
distribution of digital data or information, including cloud computing, colocation, artificial
intelligence computing, high-performance computing, or cryptocurrency or blockchain
processing.

The term includes server buildings, colocation facilities, cloud computing facilities, artificial
intelligence computing facilities, hyperscale data centers, digital storage facilities, and all
accessory structures, equipment, uses, and infrastructure principally serving such facility,
including backup power generation facilities, battery energy storage systems, cooling systems,
water supply facilities, electrical substations, and transformers.

The term applies regardless of the terminology used by an owner, operator, developer, or applicant,
but does not include computers, servers, telecommunications equipment, or other information-
technology equipment that are incidental and accessory to a permitted principal use whose primary
purpose is not data processing, data storage, artificial intelligence computing, cloud computing,
high-performance computing, cryptocurrency processing, or another similar computational
service.

D.     PLANNING BOARD:                Town of Tyre Planning Board

E.     TOWN:           Town of Tyre, Seneca County, New York

F.     TOWN BOARD:             Town of Tyre Town Board

G.     ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS:               Town of Tyre Zoning Board of Appeals

Section 3.     MORATORIUM.

A.      The Town Board hereby enacts a moratorium, which shall prohibit the review, approval,
creation, establishment, placement, relocation, construction, reconstruction, enlargement,
modification or erection anywhere within the Town of any Artificial Intelligence Computing
Facility or Data Center.

B.       This moratorium shall be in effect for a period of one (1) year from the effective date of
this Local Law and shall expire on the earlier of (i) the date one (1) year from said effective date,
unless renewed; or (ii) the enactment by the Town Board of a resolution indicating the Town Board
is satisfied that the need for the moratorium no longer exists.

C.     This moratorium shall apply to all zoning districts and all real property within the Town.
D.       Pursuant to this moratorium, the Planning Board shall not review or process any
applications for any Artificial Intelligence Computing Facility or Data Center nor shall it grant any
preliminary or final approval to any site plan or subdivision application or grant or approve any
special use permit that includes an Artificial Intelligence Computing Facility or Data Center as
part of the application; except that reviews of existing special use permits that are required to come
regularly before the Town for re-approval, may be reviewed and approved by the Town, provided
such applications do not request any modifications to an existing Artificial Intelligence Computing
Facility or Data Center.

E.      Pursuant to this moratorium, no Building Permits nor Certificates of Occupancy shall be
issued for any Artificial Intelligence Computing Facility or Data Center anywhere within the Town
unless it has received special use permit approval and/or final site plan approval, as applicable,
prior to the effective date of this local law.

F.      Pursuant to this moratorium, the Zoning Board of Appeals shall not accept, review, process,
hear, or determine any appeal or application involving the establishment, placement, relocation,
construction, reconstruction, enlargement, modification, or erection of an Artificial Intelligence
Computing Facility or Data Center, including any application for an area variance, use variance,
interpretation, or other relief under the Zoning Law of the Town of Tyre.

Section 4.     EXEMPTIONS

The provisions of this Local Law shall not apply to:

A.     Routine maintenance, repair, or replacement of existing telecommunications or
       information technology equipment that does not materially increase capacity, electrical
       demand, building area, or operational intensity.

B.     Data processing equipment used solely for the operation of a permitted principal use and
       that is incidental and accessory thereto.

C.     Existing facilities that have received all required discretionary land use approvals prior to
       the effective date of this Local Law.

Section 5.     PENALTIES.

       Any person, firm, entity or corporation which shall violate the provisions of this Local
Law, shall be subject to:

       1.      A penalty in the amount of $250.00 for each day that such violation shall exist; and

       2.      Injunctive relief in favor of the Town to cease any and all such actions which
               conflict with this Local Law and, if necessary, to remove any construction or
               improvements which may have been built in violation of this Local Law.
       It shall be the duty of the Zoning Enforcement Officer to enforce the provisions of this
Local Law.

Section 6.     RELIEF FROM APPLICATION OF MORATORIUM

A.     The Town Board reserves the authority to grant limited relief from the strict application of
       this Local Law where a property owner demonstrates that application of the moratorium
       would impose an extraordinary and unnecessary hardship that deprives the owner of all
       reasonable use of the affected property.
B.     Mere delay in applying for or receiving a zoning amendment, site plan approval, special
       use permit, subdivision approval, variance, building permit, certificate of occupancy, or
       other municipal approval during the term of this moratorium shall not constitute
       extraordinary or unnecessary hardship.
C.     An application for relief shall be submitted in writing to the Town Clerk and shall identify
       the affected property, the relief requested, the circumstances supporting the request, and
       the evidence upon which the applicant relies.
D.     The Town Board shall conduct a public hearing upon the application after providing notice
       as required by law. In determining the application, the Town Board shall consider whether:
       1. The applicant would be deprived of all reasonable use of the affected property without
           relief;
       2. The alleged hardship is unique to the property and was not self-created;
       3. The requested relief would adversely affect public health, safety, or welfare; natural
          resources; public utilities or infrastructure; neighboring properties; agricultural
          resources; or the rural character of the Town;
       4. The requested relief would conflict with the purposes of this Local Law or with any
          regulations under consideration by the Town; and
       5. Any relief can be limited or conditioned to minimize potential adverse effects.
E.     The Town Board may grant, grant with conditions, or deny the application. Any relief
       granted shall be limited to the minimum extent necessary to alleviate the demonstrated
       hardship.
F.     Relief granted under this section shall constitute relief only from the temporary
       restrictions of this Local Law. It shall not constitute or substitute for any zoning
       amendment, use variance, area variance, special use permit, site plan approval,
       subdivision approval, building permit, certificate of occupancy, or other approval
       otherwise required by law.

Section 7.     VALIDITY.

        If any clause, sentence, paragraph, section or part of this local law shall be adjudged by
any court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, such judgment shall not affect, impair or
invalidate the remainder thereof, but shall be confined in its operation to the clause, sentence,
paragraph, section or part thereof directly involved in the controversy in which such judgment
shall have been ordered.
Section 8.    SUPERSEDING EFFECT.

        Pursuant to New York Municipal Home Rule Law, Section 22, the provisions of this law
are to supersede any inconsistent provision of state or local law.

Section 9.    EFFECTIVE DATE.

This Local Law shall take effect immediately upon filing with the New York Department of State
and shall remain in force and effect for a period of one (1) year from the date of such filing.
