Updated Position Inventory for Civil Service: Michael noted he and the Town Clerk met with two representatives from

Steuben County Personnel and reviewed the current positions listed on their inventory. Several of those positions were

not reported correctly by past management and it’s time to clean this up. Therefore, Resolution #21 of 2026 was

presented to the Board for review:

Whereas, the Steuben County Personnel Department, which oversees civil service positions for the Town of Thurston,

is requesting our Position Inventory be updated by removing civil service positions no longer in use by the Town and;

Whereas the positions of part-time Account Clerk for the Highway Superintendent, Cleaner, part-time Custodian, and

Highway Laborer are all positions that, if the Town utilized them, would be paid by voucher and not added to payroll;

Therefore, be it resolved that, the civil service positions of Account Clerk, Cleaner, Custodian, and Highway Laborer

be abolished effective immediately.

Updating Town Real Property Inventory  Diana Dixon and Gerald Gebhard gave a presentation on why the Town should

update their real property inventory and get it on the County website. Diana said the majority of this is done using

footage from County drones with some done by the assessors driving past. She said an updated inventory helps ensure

accurate property information with each parcel needing to be updated. The information is then placed on the County

website for the public to see. A full inventory has not been done for a long time. This information is helpful when a

property is sold or being considered for sale as it helps with comparisons. If this is done by an outside hire, it can cost

up to $50,000.

Holly asked if we are telling the property owners when changes in assessments are made. Diana said it’s best to collect

the information first and then communicate it to the owner. Diana said it would mean extra money to be paid to the

Assessors as this is labor and time intensive. She would like a commitment from the Board that they would be paid so

they can get started. She is requesting an additional annual $7000 for the next two years. A lot of municipalities hire a

consultant to do this and that would cost well over the $14,000 she is requesting. We received our 2026 County sales

tax payment which was more than originally budgeted. Gerald Gebhard said assessments wouldn’t always increase

but could potentially decrease for some property owners.

Dan Gee said he feels this is an invasion of privacy. Diana said they don’t go on anyone’s land or into their home.

Jennifer Hargrave asked what we do if the public is opposed to this update. Diana said if we get this data and compare

it to other towns it may help with our equalization rate. The Board felt that a public meeting should be held to inform

Thurston residents of exactly what the purpose of this inventory update is for.

Therefore, motion was made by Holly Chase and seconded by Jennifer Hargrave that a public meeting be held on June

th

17at 6:30 to present to the public what a real property inventory is and how it is conducted. Put to vote, all those

present, aye. Carried.
