Assessor:


Seneca County assessors will decide on October 7, 2026, whether to

continue with the current error-prone tax program or revert to the old one due

to significant issues with recent school tax bills.

• School tax bills mailed in the first week of September 2026 contained

multiple errors.

o Incorrect equalization rates (E rates) were used, affecting

exemptions for agricultural land, senior citizens, and STAR.

o Acreage figures were also wrong; for example, one bill listed

1,030 acres for a 10.3-acre property due to a misplaced

decimal.

• In response to the errors, social media reports initially advised

residents not to pay their bills.

• The state contacted all school districts in Seneca County (e.g., Junius

has four, Tyre has three) and provided options:

o Option 1: Send out no new bills and place an omitted tax on the

January 2027 bill.

o Option 2: Recorrect the bills for September 2027.

• All school districts in the county reportedly chose Option 2, meaning

residents may face an adjusted bill in September 2027, as the current

incorrect bills are likely lower than they should be.

Residents are experiencing significant issues with the state-run STAR

program, including an inability to get assistance via the designated phone line.

• The Town Board has not received many calls about the bill errors, but

the Assessor has received many questions about missing veterans’

exemptions (which only apply to county and town taxes, not school

taxes).

• The most significant issue reported by residents involves the STAR

program, which is administered by the state, not the town.

• Residents are directed to call a state phone number (518-457-2036) for

STAR-related issues.

• Callers report receiving an automated message stating the line is too

busy or that they will receive a call back in a long timeframe, such as

96 minutes.

• One resident reported missing the callback and having to wait another

96 minutes.

IT support for the court system will be transitioned from the town’s


vendor (CST) to a dedicated New York Courts system by November 1,


2026.

• Currently, the town uses CST for IT security, cybersecurity, and

network monitoring for all departments.

• New York Courts is now requiring that all court computers be

supported exclusively by their own IT system.

• The town will transition the court’s IT support from CST to the New

York Courts system by the deadline of November 1, 2026. The court

will be given a dedicated network switch to isolate its computers from

the town’s network, preventing cross-contamination from cyber-attacks.

After the transition, the court will no longer be able to use CST for IT

support. This change will result in a cost savings for the town.

• Several months ago, the Town Board approved purchasing two

ChatGPT business licenses. Implementation of this purchase has been

delayed. Supervisor Partee has learned of a new program, “OpenAI for

Government,” which will become available on October 1, 2026. It

appears that this program offers user licenses with ChatGPT for

government use at zero cost through December 31, 2028. Supervisor

Partee submitted an application for the “Open AI for Government”

program and is waiting to receive more information.
