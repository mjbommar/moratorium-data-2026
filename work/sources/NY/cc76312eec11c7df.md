TOWN OF CLIFTON PARK
COUNTY OF SARATOGA
STATE OF NEW YORK

NOTICE OF ADOPTION
LOCAL LAW NO. 3 of 2026

PROHIBITING BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS FACILITIES WITHIN THE
TOWN OF CLIFTON PARK

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the Town Board of the Town of Clifton Park, at a meeting thereof held on Tuesday, January 20, 2026, duly enacted and adopted Local Law No.3 of 2026, a Local Law prohibiting Battery Energy Storage Systems facilities within the Town of Clifton Park.
