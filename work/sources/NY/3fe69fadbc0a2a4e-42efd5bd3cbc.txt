Welcome to the Town of LeRay’s Local Laws page. Here you’ll find a comprehensive listing of all local laws adopted by the Town Board, including zoning regulations, land use codes, and municipal ordinances that govern our community. These laws are designed to protect public health, safety, and welfare while supporting responsible growth and development across the Town.

​

Use this page to review, reference, or download official legislation that may affect residents, property owners, and businesses. Each local law is listed by title and date of adoption, with links to full text where available.

​

For questions about interpretation, enforcement, or pending legislation, please contact the Town Clerk’s Office.
