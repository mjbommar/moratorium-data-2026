LEGAL NOTICE
The Town Board of Benson will hold a Public Hearing at the Town Hall, located at 2213 Co.
Hwy. 6, Northville, N.Y. on Thursday, May 28, 2026 at 6:30 p.m. The subject matter will be
Local Law No. 1 of 2026 titled Establishing a Moratorium on Battery Energy Storage
Systems. The Local Law No. 1 of 2026 is available for the public to peruse at the Town Hall
in the display window outside the Town Hall and on the Town of Benson Website.
