                             Town of Norfolk
                   Public Hearing – Local Law #5-2025
       Moratorium on all Energy Storage System Development
                              June 12, 2025


The Norfolk Town Board held a Public Hearing for proposed Local Law #5 -
2025 Moratorium on all Energy Storage System Development on Thursday,
June 12, 2025 at 5:45 PM at the Norfolk Town Hall.

Present: Supervisor Charles Pernice, Deputy Supervisor Eric Molnar,
Councilman Martin Blair Sr., Councilwoman Jean Gang and Councilman
Travis Villnave

Supervisor Pernice opened the Public Hearing at 5:45pm.

Proposed Moratorium on all Energy Storage System Development Operating
in the Town.

Public Comments:
none

Public Hearing was closed at 6:00pm with a motion by Councilwoman Gang
and Councilman Villnave. All in Favor - Carried

                                  Supervisor – Charles Pernice
                                  Deputy Supervisor – Eric Molnar
                                  Councilman – Travis Villnave
                                  Councilwoman – Jean Gang
