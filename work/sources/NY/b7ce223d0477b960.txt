       Public Hearing for Local Law 3 of 2023: MORATORIUM AMENDMENT TO THE TOWN OF LONG LAKE
                                                        BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM Page 1 of 1

A Public Hearing for Local Law 3 of 2023: Moratorium Amendment to the
Town of Long Lake Battery Energy Storage System Law of the Town Board of
Long Lake, Hamilton County, New York was held August 30, 2023, at the
Long Lake Town Hall. Sixty-two people were in attendance.
          Members present:         Clay J. Arsenault        Supervisor
                                   Mark A. Bird             Councilman
                                   Thomas L. Donnelly       Councilman
                                   Richard B. Dechene       Councilman
                                   Clark J. Seaman          Councilman

Also in attendance: Confidential Secretary to the Supervisor, Matthew Newby, the First Deputy
Town Clerk, Amber Wamback, and the Town Attorney, Carl Ferrentino.

Supervisor Arsenault stated that written comments will not be read at the public hearing.
Supervisor Arsenault informed the public that the public hearing is not a question-and-answer
session for the specific battery storage project in Raquette Lake.
Supervisor Arsenault opened the public hearing for Local Law 3 of 2023 titled: MORATORIUM
AMENDMENT TO THE TOWN OF LONG LAKE BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM LAW
at 7:01PM.
From the public, Lou Burke, Nancy Weeyl, Richard Lopez, Jack Carney, Monique Masse, and
Catherine Grace were all in favor of Local Law #3 of 2023 presented by the Long Lake Town
Board.
Councilman Clark Seaman and Councilman Mark Bird also spoke in favor of Local Law #3 of
2023.
Motion by Supervisor Arsenault, seconded by Richard Dechene to close the public hearing for
Local Law #3 of 2023 titled: MORATORIUM AMENDMENT TO THE TOWN OF LONG LAKE
BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEM LAW at 7:12 PM.
               Adopted:     Clay J. Arsenault               Aye
                            Mark A. Bird                    Aye
                            Thomas L. Donnelly              Aye
                            Richard B. Dechene              Aye
                            Clark J. Seaman                 Aye


The Town Board took a brief recess to receive council from the Town Attorney before
proceeding with the regular board meeting.
