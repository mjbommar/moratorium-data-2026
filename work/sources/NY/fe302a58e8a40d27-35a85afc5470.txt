TOWN OF CONCORD LOCAL LAW NO. 5 OF THE YEAR 2026


A Local Law amending Local Law No. 3 of 2026, which is extending the

temporary moratorium on the issuance of applications and the granting of permits,

licenses or approvals for all Solar Energy System Facilities in the Town of Concord

pursuant to Chapter 150 of the Town of Concord Code in order to allow for the

consideration and possible adoption of appropriate amendments to existing regulations

pertaining to such land uses,

Be it enacted by the Town Board of the Town of Concord as follows:

SECTION I. SHORT TITLE


This Local Law amends a prior Local Law known as “A Local Law extending the

temporary moratorium on the issuance of applications and the granting of permits,

licenses or approvals for all Solar Energy System Facilities in the Town of Concord”

adopted by the Town of Concord on June 11, 2026.

SECTION II. LEGISLATIVE FINDINGS


WHEREAS, the Town Board of the Town of Concord adopted Local Law No. 1

of the Year 2021 amending the Town of Concord Zoning Ordinance providing for the

regulation of Solar Energy System facilities within the Town of Concord. It is

acknowledged that Local Law No. 3 of the Year 2026 previously established a six month

moratorium on the issuance of applications, permits or approvals for all Solar Energy

System Facilities in the Town of Concord in order for existing local laws and regulations

to be updated, and

WHEREAS, the updates and revisions to the existing laws and regulations are

currently under review and discussion, but additional time is required to complete the

process of updating the same to address such concerns and issues and enact new laws,

ordinances or regulations, and/or appropriate amendments to existing laws, ordinances or

regulations to properly address new and emerging technologies, community impacts,

concerns or issues in a manner which is meaningful and consistent with the Town’s

Comprehensive Plan.

IT IS THEREFORE FOUND BY THE TOWN OF CONCORD TOWN BOARD

that

the Town Board of the Town of Concord hereby declares that no new applications shall

be issued and no permits or approvals shall be granted by any officer, department, board

or agency of the Town of Concord with respect to Solar Energy System facilities in the

Town of Concord pursuant to the Chapter 150 of the Town of Concord Code for an

additional period of six months from the effective date of Local Law 3 N No. of 2026,

unless earlier terminated by other local law, duly adopted. Tier 1 (roof mounted) and tier

2 (ground mounted) systems for residential purposes are allowed while this moratorium is

in effect.

SECTION III. AUTHORITY


This Local Law is enacted pursuant by the Town Board of the Town of Concord

pursuant

to its authority to adopt local laws under Article IX of the New York State Constitution

and the Municipal Home Rule Law Section 10.

SECTION IV. MORATORIUM AND DURATION


The Town Board of the Town of Concord hereby declares that no new applications shall

be issued and no permits or approvals shall be granted by any officer, department, board

or agency of the Town of Concord with respect to Solar Energy System facilities in the

Town of Concord pursuant to the Chapter 150 of the Town of Concord Code for an

additional period of six months from the effective date of Local Law N No.3 of 2026, unless

earlier terminated by other local law, duly adopted. Tier 1 (roof mounted) and tier 2

(ground mounted) systems for residential purposes are allowed while this moratorium is

in effect.

SECTION V. SEVERABILITY


The invalidity of any word, section, clause, paragraph, sentence, or part or

provision of this Local Law shall not affect the validity of any other part of this Local

Law which shall be given effect.

SECTION VI. EFFECTIVE DATE


This Local Law shall become effective immediately, as provided by law, upon

filing with the New York Secretary of State.
