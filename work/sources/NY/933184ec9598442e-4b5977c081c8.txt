PROPOSAL DETAILS

PROPOSAL DETAILS OVERVIEW

The Town of Southampton Town Board is proposing legislation that will enact six (6) new local

laws for Battery Energy Storage Systems or BESS facilities as an amendment to the Town Zoning

Code. These code amendments are putting forth a comprehensive approach and a tiering system

to best regulate energy storage facilities and infrastructure throughout the Town of Southampton.

Currently the Town of Southampton has a local law proposed to end a temporary moratorium that

was initially adopted by the Town Board on August 22, 2023 by L.L. No. 28-2023 and has been

granted several 3 3-month extensions by both the Southampton Town Board and the Suffolk County

Planning Commission.

The moratorium extension was pending so that the Town can finalize their BESS zoning code

after they have had the time necessary to review, consider, and adopt findings of the Town of

Southampton Battery Energy Storage Steering Committee’s Interim Report dated November 21,

2024. The link to that report is as follows:

https://ny-southampton.civicplus.com/DocumentCenter/View/33363/BESS-Interim-Report

122024

The final findings report provides for a revised BESS zoning code, parts of which are contained

herein.

File Number: SH-25-05

#2025-612 Local law to repeal Town Code Chapter 330-162.21 entitled “Battery Energy

Storage Systems in Excess of 600 kWh” in its entirety.

Legislative Intent

The Town Board seeks to create legislation that provides clear guidance for the permitting

of battery energy storage systems (BESS) using a tiering system within a newly enacted

Article XXXIII based on storage capacity, with maximum thresholds of storage provided

within each tier. The tier system will allow for the consideration of appropriately sized

BESS facilities to be designed, located, operated, and maintained to minimize risks

associated with facility equipment and fire potential by first establishing that systems over

600 kWh to a maximum of 5 MW may only be requested within Light Industrial zones as

part of a Special Exception Review by the Planning Board. All systems greater than 600

kWh must be placed at a minimum distance of 300 ft away from property lines of parcels

containing existing residential use and occupied community buildings. The standards

require a significant amount of information and review than was previously required,

especially related to compliance with National Codes standards and Testing methods, the

requirement for Peer review, enhanced fire safety plans/procedures and grid

interconnection studies and agreements. This legislation corresponds to the findings of a

select Town based BSS Steering Committee that reviewed public concerns expressed at

many Board meetings, Town Board input, the New York State Iter-Agency Fire Safety

Working Group, recent updates to relevant codes, standards, testing methods and

procedures regulating the BESS industry as well as input from NYSERDA and the Long

Island Power Authority.

STAFF ANALYSIS

The Staff acknowledges the significant effort and commitment of the Town of Southampton to

address the need and concerns regarding the storage of renewable energy in their community. It

is the intent of this local law to add a new Article X XXXIII, entitled “ “Battery Energy Storage Systems”

to the Southampton Town Code Section 330 entitled Zoning to provide appropriate zoning and

locational standards for reviewing applications in connection with Battery Energy Storage

Systems and to create a tiering system for facilities in excess of 600kwh to a maximum of 5 MW.

Furthermore, the accompanying local law intends to repeal the existing §330-162.21 in its entirety

and enacts new local law for the review of Battery Energy Storage Systems, with Special

Exception Standards, within this section limiting their placement to Light Industrial Zones and

limiting storage potential to a maximum of 5 MW.

Battery Energy Storage Systems in excess of 5 MW are prohibited in the Town of Southampton.

In addition, the Town of Southampton has identified ‘Avoidance Areas’ where Type 2 BESS

applications are not allowed.

GENERAL MUNICIPAL LAW AW CONSIDERATIONS: New York State General Municipal Law,

Section 239-l provides for the Suffolk County Planning Commission to consider inter-community

issues. Included in such issues are compatibility of land uses, community character, public

convenience and maintaining of a satisfactory community environment.

The proposed changes to the Town Code are intended to best plan for and scale Battery Energy

Storage Systems (BESS) into locations that are logical to ensure public safety, public benefit, and

environmental health without causing any detriment to nearby properties or inhabitants.

LOCAL COMPREHENSIVE PLAN RECOMMENDATIONS: The Town of Southampton previously

enacted its BESS zoning regulations in January 2021; however, following concerns of the

community over their safety, the Town of Southampton adopted a moratorium (August 2023). Also,

the Town’s Comprehensive Plan documents (“Sustainable Southampton 400+” and

“Southampton Climate Action Plan”) require the Town to take action on developing a long-term

and comprehensive energy plan that supports improvements to the electrical grid infrastructure.

It is the belief of the staff that the proposed legislation amending the zoning code and zoning map

are consistent with local plan recommendations, that zoning and related development regulations

be revised to maintain consistency with the Comprehensive Plan. The proposed Battery Energy

Storage System zoning code amendment and related development regulations are in accordance

with Town goals for public safety and welfare, as well as supporting sustainable green energy

initiatives.

BACKGROUND

On August 2, 2023, the Suffolk County Planning Commission approved the initial six (6) month

moratorium on BESS facilities requested by the Town of Southampton. In addition, the Town

adopted a six (6) month extension that was heard by the Suffolk County Planning Commission on

February 7, 2024 (to August 13, 2024). On July 10, 2024, the SCPC approved a requested six

(6) month extension to the moratorium (to February 13, 2025). The final moratorium extension for

three (3) months ends May 27, 2025.

The proposed code changes are intended to establish rules for siting Battery Energy Storage

Systems (BESS) in the Town of Southampton. A After an extended moratorium and many meetings,

better protect the community.

A summary of the key elements of the proposed code changes includes the following:

• It would allow residential homes and businesses to install batteries capable of storing up

to 600 kilowatts of energy (Tier 1 system) with similar compliance to building safety codes.

• Tier 2 commercial scale systems of between 600 kilowatts and 5 megawatts of storage

capacity will only be permitted via Special Exception, via Planning Board review, in Light

Industrial zoning districts and cannot be within 300 feet of a residential property line,

schools, or public buildings.

• The proposed Code would prohibit BESS in excess of 5 megawatts in the Town.

• Tier Two BESS facilities shall be considered Type I Actions pursuant to SEQRA and

therefore require a Full Environmental Assessment review.
