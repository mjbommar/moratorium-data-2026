Data Center Moratorium

Local Law No. 1 of 2026 — Town of Highland, Sullivan County, New York

The Town of Highland has enacted a temporary moratorium on the approval, permitting, and construction of large-scale data centers. The moratorium lasts 12 months and may be extended up to 24 months. During this time, the Town Board will review and update its zoning laws and study the potential impacts of data centers on our rural community.
