Resolution No. 37 of 2026 “A Resolution Establishing the Town of Berne

Budget Office and Adopting the Budget Office Charter”


Supervisor Giebelhaus offered the following resolution, which was seconded by

Councilmember Miller:

WHEREAS, the Town Board of the Town of Berne is responsible for the oversight of

the Town’s financial management and fiscal operations; and

WHEREAS, the Town Supervisor serves as the Town’s Chief Fiscal Officer

pursuant to New York State Town Law §29; and


WHEREAS, the Town Board finds that the establishment of a structured financial

administration framework will strengthen internal financial controls, improve financial

reporting, and ensure compliance with applicable municipal accounting standards; and

WHEREAS, the New York State Office of the State Comptroller recommends that

municipalities implement financial systems that provide appropriate segregation of


duties, budget oversight, and independent financial review; and


WHEREAS, the Town Board desires to establish a centralized Budget Office

responsible for financial administration, budget development, fiscal monitoring, and

financial compliance; and

WHEREAS, the Town Board has reviewed the proposed Town of Berne Budget

9

Office Charter, which defines the organization, responsibilities, and operating


procedures of the Budget Office.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED Section 1 — Establishment


The Town Board hereby establishes the Town of Berne Budget Office to administer

financial operations, budget management, and fiscal reporting for the Town of Berne

and its special districts.

Section 2 — Organizational Structure


The Budget Office shall consist of the following functional roles:

• Bookkeeper / Senior Account Clerk responsible for daily financial transaction

processing including accounts payable, payroll administration, accounts receivable, and

purchase order administration.

• Budget Manager responsible for budget development, budget monitoring, financial


reporting, fiscal analysis, and financial planning.

• Comptroller (Contracted Certified Public Accountant) responsible for


financial compliance, preparation of the Town’s Annual Financial Report (AFR),

technical accounting guidance, and coordination with the New York State Office of the

State Comptroller.

Section 3 — Administrative Oversight


The Budget Office shall operate under the administrative oversight of the Town

Supervisor, who shall provide fiscal leadership and present financial reports to the

Town Board.

Section 4 — Financial Reporting


The Budget Office shall prepare and provide monthly financial reports to the Town

Board, including:

• Budget vs. Actual Expenditure Reports

• Revenue Status Reports

• Fund Balance Summaries

• Cash Flow Status Reports

These reports shall support the Town Board in exercising fiscal oversight and

monitoring the financial condition of the Town.

Section 6 — Adoption of Charter


The Town Board hereby adopts the Town of Berne Budget Office Charter,

Section 7 — Implementation

adopted Charter.
