a) Hwy Supt Heim asked the Board to receive and file his

Highway Report.

Supt. Heim read a letter that was sent to the towns from the Highway Association.

The Town of Marilla is going to be sending a similar notice out. It speaks about the high

cost and availability of road salt, and states that alternative methods may be used to take

care of roads this winter. Supt. Heim thinks that when he put the winter ad in the paper

he should include something similar to the letter he read. The Board agrees.  He also

spoke about changes that were made in plowing the snow in Crane Ridge last year, since

the trees block the sun from melting the snow on the roads. Those changes seemed to be

helpful. He is going to put together a letter to send to the residents explaining how his

department intends to do winter maintenance in Crane Ridge this year.

Motion by Council Member Krzemien, seconded by Council Member Zittel to

receive and file the Highway Department Report. All members voting aye. Carried.
