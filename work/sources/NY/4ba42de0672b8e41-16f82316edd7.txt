RESOLUTION

A MORATORIUM ON BATTERY

f Cortlandt does hereby adopt Local

Local Law No. 1 of 2026

(A Local Law Extending a Moratorium for New Battery Energy Storage Systems)

Section 1. Legislative Intent

The Town has received interest from Battery y Energy Storage developers about potentially

installing Battery Energy Storage infrastructure in n Cortlandt. Currently, the Town Code is devoid

of provisions directly related to Battery Energy Storage. The Town originally enacted a

moratorium pursuant to Local Law Number 2-2024. Thereafter, the Town convened a committee

to work on a Local Law. The Town’s committee made great progress on a Local Law related to

Battery Energy Storage Systems, and the Town  Board adopted Local Law Number 10-2024, Local

Law Number 2-2025, and Local Law 7-2025 extending the Moratorium to allow the progress to

continue.

Other communities in Westchester have either passed moratoria or are considering passing

moratoria. The Town Board believes it to be prudent t to allow the State to issue additional guidance.

The Town Board notes that the State has taken significant interest in battery energy storage systems

as well as data centers, evidenced by the State Legislature recently passing a data center

moratorium.

Section 2. Moratorium

No application shall be considered, nor approvals granted, by the Planning Board, Zoning Board

of Appeals, or the Code Enforcement officials of f the Town with respect to any applications for

new commercial Battery Energy Storage Systems.

Section 3. Duration

The moratorium herein shall be extended through December 31, 2026 unless terminated earlier.

Section 4. Hardship

The Town Board retains unto itself the right to consider variances to this Local Law and to make

all determinations with respect to its applicability and interpret and determine the same. Any

issues with respect to the interpretation of and variances from this Law shall be determined by the

Town Board upon written request of a property owner. This supersedes § 267 of the New York

State Town Law as to variance process.

Section 5. Severability

If any section, subdivision, paragraph, clause, or phrase of this law shall be adjudged invalid or

held unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction, any judgment made thereby shall not

affect the validity of this law as a whole or any part thereof other than the part or provision so

adjudged to be invalid or unconstitutional.
