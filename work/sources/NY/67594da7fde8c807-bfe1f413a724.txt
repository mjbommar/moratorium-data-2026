Dog Control: Mike Wiggins- 06/30/2024

Public Comment:


* Mr. Wiggins presented a letter from Cayuga County Sherriff Brian Schenck. In short, the

letter shared the recent issues that the department has been dealing with. Some of the issues

are “the ability to continue providing public safety services in a manner that meets your

expectations and those of everyone in our local community.” In the recent there was an

eviction that turned into a dangerous encounter in a neighboring county. Some of the other

challenges are regarding the funding that may be cut from the budget that should be utilized to

meet the challenges and needs of the community. The agency is currently being stretched

very thin with their current budget. The letter asked for the support of all who received the

letter. The offer to contact Sheriff Schenck was made and he included his number as well as

his email address: 315-253-4337 & Sheriff@cayugacounty.us As of yet, no cuts have been

made to the current budget.

Any other business:


* Jim Mignogna- Offered a fire department report.

* Doreena Xedis- stated the possible influx of traffic on county Rt. 38 may be due to the

construction on the Rt. 34 and a bridge being reduced to one lane. She also informed the

board that there will be a motorcycle ride coming through in the coming weekend. Mr. Wiggins

did share that they tend to close the road off and direct traffic for the event. She also noticed a

huge number of tanker trucks moving down Rt. 38 and wanted to know if anyone was aware of

why.

* Melissa Zavaski- wanted to know if there was a possible correlation between the construction

and the influx of accidents happening at the intersection of Rt. 370 & Rt. 38. She shared her

concern with the board regarding the blind area on Rt. 370. She wanted to know who was

responsible for the roadways. She expressed that the owners of the properties along the

roadway should take responsibility for keeping the brush, trees and even grass maintained.

* Laurie Reichert- The signage is not adequate for the miles per hour and time available to

break with enough time to be safe. She shared all the observations and suggestions that she

will be offering to the state when they do their site studies. She has photos of all the areas

where improvement can be made. Some of the grievances she felt should be addressed

immediately due to the seriousness of danger. She concluded with an expression of gratitude

to the board for acting as quickly as they did with passing a resolution for the intersection.

* Kathy Hill- she did a bit of research herself regarding the intersection. She found that there

are criteria offered by the state that must be met as well as a battle when it comes to finances.

The criteria were quite a harsh reality. There needs to be several accidents but accidents that

included fatalities. Those fatalities are a set number, and the end number needs to exceed the

states guidelines. She stated that she still needs to find out the current number needed and if

we have reached that amount yet. She encouraged the town members to write letters and

include pictures and stories when addressing them to the state. She has created a template,

and it will be available at the clerk’s desk. She will collect the letters and mail them out to the

state. She also started a petition that can also be found at the clerk’s desk. She stressed to

share your stories and support of the board in this matter. She thanked the board and anyone

else who acted thus far.

* Mark Southwick- reminded the community to not forget about the politicians in Albany. They

need to be on the mailing list for all correspondence.

* Mike Wiggins- stated he was planning on sending the information from the meeting to the

county legislature who agreed to give a letter of support in the matter.

* Len Hand made a motion to adjourn the meeting, Mark Southwick seconded the motion. 4

ayes, 0 nays. The motion carried. The meeting adjourned at 7:50 PM.
