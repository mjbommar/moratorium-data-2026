PUBLIC HEARING A LOCAL LAW TO ESTABLISH STANDARDS AND PERMITTING REQUIREMENTS FOR BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS IN THE TOWN OF PENDLETON


Supervisor Maerten opened the Public Hearing at 6:57 p.m.

The notice for the Public Hearing was published in the Lockport Union Sun & Journal on December 17,

2025 and on the Town’s website.

The purpose of the hearing was t to consider the adoption of a proposed Local Law entitled “A Local Law

to Establish Standards and Permitting Requirements for Battery Energy Storage Systems in the Town of

Pendleton” which will amend the Town Code to add new provisions regulating the siting, design,

construction, operation, and decommissioning of battery energy storage systems within the Town.

A copy of the proposed Local Law was made available for public review at the Pendleton Town

Hall during normal business hours and on the Town of Pendleton website at www.pendletonny.gov. All

interested persons were given an opportunity to be heard at the time and place of the hearing.

Supervisor Maerten opened the floor to public comments.

Sara Siracuse of Monroe Street commented that the draft of the proposed local law used the terms

“local law” and “ordinance” interchangeably throughout the document and requested clarification on

the difference between the two. Supervisor Maerten asked Town Attorney Charles Malcomb, who

prepared the draft, to explain the distinction. Following his explanation, Attorney Malcomb stated that

he would review the document and revise the wording if necessary.

Mrs. Siracuse also noted that the document referenced both the 2020 and 2023 editions of NFPA 855,

which differ, and indicated that this may require correction. Additionally, she commented on the

references to Special Use Permits in Section H, the absence of a reference to Town Code Section 247-

16, and the inclusion of a reference to a “24 permit,” which does not currently exist in the Town Code.

Next, Alex Riled of Nexamp, a community solar provider operating in the area, spoke in support of the

proposed amendment to the Town Code.

Supervisor Maerten explained that a resolution would be considered later in the meeting to extend the

moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Facilities established by Local Law No. 2 of 2025 for an

additional three (3) months, pending further comments and feedback from Attorney Malcomb.

There were no further comments from the public.

Motion made by Councilman Buechler, seconded by Councilman Hickman, to close the Public Hearing

at 7:08 p.m. Motion carried.

RESOLUTION 334-25

EXTENSION OF MORATORIUM ON BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE FACILITIES


Motion by Councilman Buechler, seconded by Councilman Hickman, the following resolution was

5

WHEREAS, on June 9, 2025, the Town Board of the Town of Pendleton (the “Town Board”) duly

adopted Local Law No. 2 of 2025, entitled A Local Law Establishing a Six-Month Moratorium on

Battery Energy Storage Facilities (the “Moratorium”), which imposed a moratorium for a period of one

hundred eighty (180) days on the filing, acceptance, and processing of applications for the construction

or operation of battery energy storage facilities within the Town; and

WHEREAS, the Moratorium was adopted pursuant to Municipal Home Rule Law § 10 for the purpose

of preserving the status quo while the Town Board evaluates and considers amendments to the Town

Code governing the siting, design, safety, and regulation of battery energy storage facilities; and

WHEREAS, the one hundred eighty (180) day moratorium period commenced on June 9, 2025, and

expired on December 6, 2025; and

WHEREAS, Section 7 of Local Law No. 2 of 2025 expressly authorizes the Town Board, in its

discretion, to extend the Moratorium for one (1) additional three (3) month period by resolution; and

WHEREAS, the Town Board finds that additional time is necessary to continue its review of regulatory

approaches, safety standards, and best practices applicable to battery energy storage facilities, including

consideration of potential amendments to the Town Code;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN BOARD OF THE TOWN OF

PENDLETON THAT: The Moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Facilities established by Local Law

No. 2 of 2025 is hereby extended for an additional three (3) month period, commencing upon adoption

of this Resolution and expiring three (3) months thereafter unless sooner terminated by further action of

the Town Board. All terms, conditions, and provisions of Local Law No. 2 of 2025 shall remain in full

force and effect during the extension period. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
