Welcome to the Town of Sherburne, NY

Home of the Pageant of Bands, Rogers Environmental Center, Gilligan's Island and more.

Thank you for visiting our website, where you’ll find resources, news and other information about our town and surrounding area. Our Town of 4,000 residents is home to the Rogers Environmental Center, Gaines Memorial Park with its bandstand, Rexford Falls, Paddleford Park and the Town Public Library which is housed in a historic building in the Village and is part of the 4-County Library System. Our Town provides many services and products. We are situated at the crossroads of Rt 80 East and West and Route 12 North and South making for easy access for travel and commerce.

Charles Mastro
Town Supervisor of Sherburne, NY
