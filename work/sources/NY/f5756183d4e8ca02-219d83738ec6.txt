Newstead Town Board Meeting – December 8, 2025


A public hearing was called to order by the Newstead Town Board on December 8, 2025, at

7:14pm at the Newstead Town Hall.

Present: Dawn Izydorczak - Supervisor

Joe Dugan – – Councilman

John Jendrowski - Councilman

Roll Call was taken with all board members present.

The Supervisor called the first public hearing to order at 7:14pm for a site plan/special use permit for

Zachary Harner at 13001 Main Rd.

The Clerk read proof of publication, and the Supervisor opened the floor for public comment.

The Harners, 6279 Utley Road, appeared and spoke about how they already have a few dogs that she

provides day care service for in their home. They also spoke about future plans for the business.

There being no further comments, a motion was made by Councilman Dugan, seconded by

Councilman Mutter to close the public hearing at 7:19pm. Carried Unanimously

The Supervisor called the second public hearing to order at 7:19pm for Local Law #4, placing a 6-

month moratorium on the application for major subdivision(s) within the town.

The Clerk read proof of publication, and the Supervisor opened the floor for public comment.

There being no comments, a motion was made by Councilman Dugan, seconded by

Councilman Mutter to close the public hearing at 7:21pm. Carried Unanimously

The Supervisor called the third public hearing to order at 7:21pm for Local Law #5, placing a 6-

month moratorium on battery energy storage systems with the town.

The Clerk read proof of publication, and the Supervisor opened the floor for public comment.

There being no comments, a motion was made by Councilman Burke, seconded by

Councilman Mutter to close the public hearing at 7:24pm. Carried Unanimously

The Supervisor called the fourth public hearing to order at 7:24pm for Local Law #6, placing a 6-

month moratorium on the application and construction and installation of wind energy projects

within the town.

The Clerk read proof of publication, and the Supervisor opened the floor for public comment.

There being no comments, a motion was made by Councilman Burke, seconded by

Councilman Mutter to close the public hearing at 7:26pm. Carried Unanimously
