GENERAL ORDINANCE EXTENDING THE TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON

BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS WITHIN THE CITY OF YONKERS

PENDING APPROPRIATE REVIEW

The City Council of the City of Yonkers does hereby ordain and enact as follows:

Section 1. Background, Purpose, Intent, and Findings


General Ordinance 08-2024, which was adopted by the City Council of the City of

Yonkers (the “City Council”) on July 16, 2024, established a temporary moratorium

on battery energy storage systems (“BESS”) within the City of Yonkers (the “City”)

pending appropriate review. As described more fully in General Ordinance

08-2024, the temporary moratorium was established for the purpose of providing

the City sufficient time to adequately study BESS, which have myriad associated

impacts including fire, chemical release, standard energy hazards, and noise, and

prepare appropriate regulations concerning BESS within the City. General

Ordinance 08-2024 established the temporary moratorium for a period of 180 days

and authorized the extension of the temporary moratorium for an additional 180

days if deemed by the City Council to be necessary to carry out the stated purpose

of the ordinance.

General Ordinance 01-2025, which was adopted by the City Council on January 14,

2025, extended the temporary moratorium on BESS established by General

Ordinance 08-2024 for an additional 180 days for the purpose of carrying out the

objectives and purpose set forth in General Ordinance 08-2024.

During the pendency of the temporary moratorium, in furtherance of the stated

objectives and purpose of General Ordinance 08-2024, the City issued a request for

proposals for a consultant to assist the City with studying and preparing appropriate

regulations concerning BESS (the “Work”) and selected and retained a consultant

to perform this Work. The City is actively working with the consultant to carry out

the Work, however the Work is ongoing and requires additional time to be

completed. BESS are a relatively new technology, pose potentially significant

impacts their surroundings, and the City is composed of a diverse array of land

uses. For these, and many additional reasons, the Work requires significant time

and effort.

Section 2. Moratorium Extension


The City Council hereby extends the temporary moratorium established by General

Ordinance 08-2024, as extended by General Ordinance 01-2025, through October

31, 2025 for the purpose of carrying out the objectives and purpose set forth in

General Ordinance 08-2024.

Except as expressly modified or amended herein, all terms and conditions set forth

in General Ordinance 08-2024 and General Ordinance 01-2025 shall remain in full

force and effect. Should any of the terms or conditions of General Ordinance

08-2024, General Ordinance 01-2025, or this General Ordinance potentially conflict,

conflict, or be inconsistent, the most recent General Ordinance shall govern over all

prior.

Section 3. New York State Environmental Quality Review Act


The extension of the temporary moratorium established by General Ordinance

08-2024 is classified as a Type II action under the New York State Environmental

Quality Review Act and its implementing regulations. Type II actions are

determined to not have a significant adverse effect on the environment and

therefore do not require further environment review.

Section 4. Severability


If any section, subsection, clause, phrase or other portion of this ordinance is, for

any reason, declared invalid, in whole or in part, by any court, agency, commission,

legislative body or other authority of competent jurisdiction, the portion of the

ordinance declared to be invalid will be deemed a separate, distinct, and

independent portion and the declaration will not affect the validity of the remaining

portions hereof, which will continue in full force and effect.

Section 5. Effective Date and Term


This general ordinance shall take effect immediately and shall continue through

October 31, 2025.
