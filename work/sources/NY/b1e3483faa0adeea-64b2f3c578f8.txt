CALL TO ORDER/APPROVAL OF MINUTES: Supervisor Peets called the Public Hearing to order at 4:00 PM. M. Peets read the newspaper legal notice aloud. Published 9/26/25.

A motion was made by J. White, seconded by S. Hourihan to approve the minutes of the September 10th, 2025 Public Hearing/Regular Town Board Meeting.  All in favor, motion carried.

Budget preliminary discussion- M. Peets states that he set a copy of the preliminary budget to the board members for review. Will look a bit different from last meeting. We had Casino funds on their own cover sheet but now it has to be put into the general fund per the comptroller’s office.  So, all the money we have or that we get will now be moved over into this account.  We also made a section where the land claim funds will be put into as well.  The land claim money, once signed by the president, will be $750,000 per year and the gaming compact will vary as it has in the past. M. Peets explains that the office from the state comptroller had a new auditor who would like to have the funds moved, Mark & Angie spoke with our lawyer as well because we had been doing things the same way for years but the auditor and her bosses explained what needs to happen as we move forward.  So, we will lay out the new version in the computer for 2026. We will close the casino line and only have the general account.  In the bookkeeping software we will still be able to track the different spending areas and also the revenue areas.  M. Peets notes that we don’t have a guarantee like Franklin County so we will need to be very smart regarding land claim.  M. Peets explains again that he and Angie have been meeting and will continue to meet with our auditor, lawyers, Bill Dashnaw (our old bookkeeper) and Williamson accounting to all get on the same page.  M. Peets explains we are not in any trouble just moving forward need to have different way of filing.  Casino compact money is still owed to us from as far back as 2022.  We are in good shape financially.  We hope to see those other funds come in soon. Brasher taxes are still on the low end.  Mark commends Larry on keeping his highway budget in check as well on his end. The budget is the best guess estimate. 

Sue Anne asked about medical insurance money increase?  Mark notes the medical insurance does go up every year but we have stabled out a bit with the retirees now moving to Medicare supplemental program and with the teamster’s contract. She asked if we have the same company.  Mark said yes, he has looked into United Health Care and MVP and they are more expensive compared to Teamsters who go with Foy company. It for sure is one of the most expensive things in our budget.  Sue asked if the employees pay any of it?  Mr. Peets notes they do pay 15 percent and when they retire, they will pay 10%.  

Mark states that the next meeting will be to finalize the budget, please call, text or email with any questions. 

A motion was made to close public hearing and move into regular board meeting at 4:15 PM by G. St. Hilaire, seconded by J. White, all in favor, motion carried. 

Privilege of the Floor- Carl Patterson 600 Smith Road asked when the next business meeting is?  Mark notes that as he explained before we don’t have such a thing but we do have Planning meetings and the next one will be October 15th.  Carl asked the time and Bethany noted usually 6PM.

REPORTS:

Highway Superintendent- L. Hewlett has submitted reported dated October 2024, a copy is attached to original of these minutes. We put 450 hours on the excavator this summer because the dry weather didn’t allow any down days.  It was a good, busy summer. 

Usually, all our water complaints are on the left side of the river with the smell, but this summer the complaints are on the right.  Larry was worried about the well drying up to the tank but our water guy says no all good.  He did get a complaint from a home owner that she is getting grass in her toilet and we are trying to find out why, the filters are so fine it shouldn’t be coming from tank.  Larry also explains they have upped the chlorine levels and the filter changes are more often which seems to have helped the sulfur smell. We test water every day and it is all good to drink.    

Larry Hewlett explains we are buttoned up for the year; we will do some odds and ends till we plow.  The guys have worked so hard this summer because the weather allowed each day to be a good work day.  We are so glad we got a ton done and the guys did a great job.  

M. Peets notes we have micro filters, sand filters and carboy filters and if we were to change them one time a month and have the materials on hand for the year the cost would be $6,000 to see if it will help with smell and taste of sulfur.  Sue Anne asked if it is guaranteed to work?  We can’t guarantee it will work but worth it to see if it does the job.  M. Peets states he feels the seismic activity and the low water table are the root of our issues this year. 

As far as sewer meter info, we did get a quote out of Baldwinsville of $856.58 to help with the flow out readings.  We did get a good rating for our inspection but this will help with our compliance. 

Assessor- Leon Shorette has submitted reported dated September 2025, a copy is attached to original of these minutes. M. Peets read report aloud as Leon has been excused from meeting as he is at conference for training. He did find out at conference that we need to be on a 6- year term so that is why I am asking to extend his appointment until 2031 as we had only approved him until 2028.

A motion was made by S. Hourihan, seconded by C. Rose, to approve Leon Shorette as assessor until 2031. All in favor, motion carried. 

Code Enforcement- Wes Lincoln has submitted his written report dated 10/07/2025; a copy is attached to original of these minutes. M. Peets read the report aloud as Wes is excused for work. 

The iworkQ’s system is moving along nicely. He has been training, supplied them the zoning book and next will be the trial. They should have enough information to be able to do violations next.  M. Peets explains that he has requested us to purchase him an iPad to help him use when he goes on the road.  Hopefully at the November meeting he will be able to report more in depth.  

A motion was made by S. Hourihan, seconded by G. St. Hilaire to purchase new iPad for him.  All in favor, motion carried.  

C. Rose asked how we are doing with the junk yard?  Mark states we are making some movement, once the trial is ready, we will sit down with the judges and move along with these complaints. 

Town Clerk- Bethany St. Hilaire has submitted her written report for September 2025; a copy is attached to original of these minutes. No questions for Bethany at this time. 

Economic Development- M. Peets notes that Boralex has been accepted by the state.  They are moving along. Their public hearing is November 20th in Massena and they would like to have one in Brasher as well, he just doesn’t have the date.  M. Peets notes that we have gotten one email from Jason Olsen, he lives over on CR 53, he writes he opposes the solar farm.  This has been passed on to others and to the company. The farmers that own the land want to stop farming and have solar on their property and I don’t think the one complaint will stop this project.  

Larry just got news the cell tower has gotten approved from railroad and they are laying the line currently so that will be very helpful.

NEW BUSINESS- 

Furnace Bids- M. Peets states that with us trying to change over to natural gas at Town Hall we are trying to get new furnaces that are compatible.  Express heating came in at $18,912.71 and Goodrich came in at $17,079.84 for their quotes.   M. Peets notes he called Cornerstone and they never sent a guy out to look into bidding. The easement has been approved and before the parking lot is paved we can get these lines to the building.  S. Hourihan asked do we know what brand furnace Goodrich wanted to go with?  M. Peets doesn’t have that info and it is not on the quote, I will email them tomorrow and get it out to you all.  

A motion was made by C. Rose seconded by J. White to accept the quote from Goodrich to install new natural gas furnaces. All in favor, motion carried. 

2025 Donation Request from Chamber- M. Peets states they have requested funding to help them with the tree lighting and Santa gifts. Last year we gave $1,500.00 and he asked if we wanted to go with same amount?  C. Rose asked if next year they could tell us in the letter how many kids and families attend.  S. Hourihan said with the cost increase of everything can we increase this amount?  Board agreed with cost increases. 

A motion was made by S. Hourihan seconded by G. St. Hilaire to donate $2,000.00 for tree lighting to Chamber of Commerce. All in favor, motion carried. 

2026 Potsdam Humane Society Contract- M. Peets explains that we continue to do business with them as we have for years.  This is where our dog warden takes the dogs he takes in.  They are not asking for an increase in funds this year with amount of $5,889.00.  

A motion was made by J. White seconded by C. Rose to accept the contact with Potsdam Humane Society for 2026. All in favor, motion carried. 

Volunteer Transport Center Inc. funding request- M. Peets again notes that we do this yearly.  They provide service of a bus that comes through town and takes people around places within the county.  They are still only asking for $400.00 which hasn’t changed in many years.  

A motion was made by G. St. Hilaire seconded by C. Rose to accept the contact with Volunteer Transport Center for 2026. All in favor, motion carried. 

Mark notes for your information the bridge came down fast and was a good job done.  Larry and Mark have been working very hard on FEMA stuff and they note they are down to the last two projects on the Hopson Road.  Larry notes we will have some money into it but the grants will pay a lot of it.  The 2 bridges they want to do are about $800,000 each and the grant will pay majority.

Mark thanked her for that report and states that is wonderful news.  Hopefully this meeting will fix a lot of problems and help the Town of Lawrence.
