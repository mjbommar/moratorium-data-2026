Edward Tietje asked if the town intends to adopt a zoning law as he feels the last sentence of the 2paragraph in

Section 2 tends to allude to that. The sentence is as follows: “Further, it is the purpose of this Local Law to facilitate

the adoption of land use regulations and laws to protect and enhance the Town’s natural, historic, cultural and

electrical resources.” The Town Supervisor said no, that is not our intent and this sentence will be removed before

the Local Law is filed.

Wayne Wells from Cameron said the Board should consider the impact of any business coming to a specific area and

should consider each situation as it comes along. Extensive research is needed and a comprehensive generic study

should be done to discover the wider scope of any impact studies.

Michael said it has been found that rural communities need regulations for these data centers. New York State

government has made it clear they are proposing data centers and nuclear facilities for Upstate communities.

There being no further comments, the public hear was closed at 6:56 p.m. upon motion made by Holly Chase and

seconded by Noel Sylvester. Put to vote, all those present voted aye. Carried.
