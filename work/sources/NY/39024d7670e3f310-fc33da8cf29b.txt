TOWN OF CLIFTON PARK
COUNTY OF SARATOGA
STATE OF NEW YORK

NOTICE OF ADOPTION
LOCAL LAW NO. 9 of 2026

ESTABLISHING A 12-MONTH MORATORIUM ON THE SITING, ACCEPTANCE, REVIEW
AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATIONS FOR DATA STORAGE CENTER WITHIN THE
TOWN OF CLIFTON PARK

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the Town Board of the Town of Clifton Park, at a meeting thereof held on Tuesday, July 7, 2026, duly enacted and adopted Local Law No.9 of 2026, a Local Law establishing a establishing a 12- month moratorium on the siting, acceptance, review and approval of applications for Data Storage Center within the Town of Clifton Park.
