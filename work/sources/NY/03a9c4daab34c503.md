# Town Board | Town of LeRay

source: https://www.townofleray.gov/town-board
fetched: 2026-09-23T14:40:39+00:00

The Town Board of LeRay is the primary governing body responsible for shaping local policies, passing legislation, adopting budgets, and guiding the overall direction of the Town. Composed of the Town Supervisor and four Council Members, the Board works to represent the interests of residents while balancing growth, stewardship, and community well-being. The Board meets regularly to discuss town operations, land use, public services, and other matters that impact daily life in LeRay.

​

All decisions are made in public meetings, guided by transparency, legal responsibility, and a commitment to serving the Town's best interests. Residents are encouraged to attend meetings, participate in public hearings, and engage with their elected representatives to help shape the future of our community.
