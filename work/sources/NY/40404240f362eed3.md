### Notice to Residents

**Notice: Public Meeting**

August 12, 2026
The Town of Cameron will hold a Public Hearing on Wednesday, August 12, 2026, at 6:00 PM on Local Law #2 of 2026, establishing a two-year moratorium on cryptocurrency mining operations and data processing centers. The meeting will be held at the Justice Court, 4075 Railroad Street, Cameron, NY. At such time, any person may be heard for or against any item therein contained. The regular August Town Board Meeting will follow the Public hearing on August 12th.
