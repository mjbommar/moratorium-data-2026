PUBLIC HEARING Local Law #4: New Chapter 18 Transferring Powers from the Planning Board to the Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) and Dissolving the Planning Board:  Mayor Lawson requested a motion. Trustee Gary Darnell made a


motion to approve local law #4, seconded by Trustee Joanne Seefeldt.

Mayor Lawson opened the floor for public comments. Planning Board Chair Jill Rohring stated that the Village

Board is taking a board of five planning board members to a board of three with the Zoning Board. She stated

that the planning board should be involved with the possible grants the Village may receive. They need to be

involved with plans for the Village not just handling variances and such.

Resident Wade Wagner stated that he has not been to the last few meetings and doesn’t understand what the

Board wants to accomplish with this.

Mayor Lawson asked if we have any additional public comments. No comments. Mayor Lawson then asked

the Board if they have any comments.

Trustee Greg Martin stated that it is true that we have difficulty maintaining a board of five with the Planning

Board. It has come to a time to where the village needs to grow and it is not a radical idea, NYCOM

recommends it and many other Villages have done it.

Trustee Gary Darnell stated that he agrees with Trustee Martin and that when speaking with NYCOM that we

can look into increasing the board.

Trustee Ellen Hibbard and Trustee Joanne Seefeldt that we should look at increasing the board as well.

Mayor Lawson stated that he is not in agreement with the new law. He believes that we have had multiple

people wanting to be on the board. He believes the Planning Board has a huge contribution and urges the

board to have more discussion and to table it before deciding.

Mayor Lawson asked if anyone had any additional comments. No comments.

Mayor Lawson asked the Village Board if he could table this. Trustee Hibbard stated that we need to vote on

the first and second motion. Discussion ensued. Mayor Lawson asked for all in favor, Trustee Hibbard, Trustee

Seefeldt, Trustee Martin and Trustee Darnell all stated aye, Mayor Lawson asked for all opposing. he stated aye.

Clerk Carey O'Connor took roll call. Mayor Arthur Lawson, no; Trustee Greg Martin, yes; Trustee Ellen Hibbard,

yes; Trustee Gary Darnell, yes; Trustee Joanne Seefeldt, yes. Majority voted yes. Local Law #4 has passed.

Local Law #5: New Chapter 178 Battery Moratorium:  Mayor Lawson asked for a motion, Trustee Greg Martin


made the motion to approve Local Law #5, seconded by Trustee Gary Darnell. Trustee Greg Martin stated that

this is a moratorium to allow our codes to catch up to the technology that's being moving quickly. We took a

training class and the engineer that all municipalities should put one of these in place. There's a lot of large

companies out there buying vacant properties and they are putting storage batteries in them and they will

collect energy and then sell it back to the grid at a higher cost.

Mayor Lawson opened it up for public comment. Carol Sattelberg asked if the Village has made an assessment

within the Village to know if anyone is currently doing this. Resident Wade Wagner spoke about his concerns

with the size of these batteries and the types of batteries that should be considered in this new law. Resident

Bob Morgan states that we need to promote new business within the village that will bring in sales tax revenue.

We need to find another way to bring revenue streams in other than taxes.

Trustee Martin stated that this is a bigger issue with safety issues. Trustee Darnell also stated that this is a

moratorium not a permanent law.

Legislator Godfrey also stated that he hasn’t read the law to know what it states, but that the wording should

be focused on what we are targeting, and shy away from windmills and small storage.

Trustee Greg Martin stated that we can table this for a month to look at it again to put the resident’s concerns

at ease. Trustee Ellen Hibbard is concerned about the safety issue and we should still go ahead with it.

Trustee Gary Darnell stated that the specific batteries should be listed and he would agree to take this back for

a month to refine it and produce it next month.

Mayor Lawson asked if all are in favor of local law #5. All board members are not in favor of passing local law #5

and would like to address this further and bring it to a public hearing for next month. Trustee Gary Darnell

made a motion to table it until next month, seconded by Trustee Ellen Hibbard. Local Law #5, Chapter 178

Battery Moratorium has been tabled until next month.
