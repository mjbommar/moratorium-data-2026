Resolution #25-2026- Justice Grant Application (JCAP)- Anthony Arquiett states they have done this for several years. There is a deadline in October that Marcie must send all info in. Tony notes that they aren’t in need of much but wanted to have the option to apply if they choose to do so.  

WHEREAS the Town of Brasher Justice Court has been invited to apply for a 2026-2027 Justice Court Assistance Program (JCAP) Grant which may be used for a variety of purposes, including office and security equipment, furniture, courtroom and court facility improvement and renovations, and

WHEREAS the applications and accompanying paperwork must be submitted no later than October 13, 2026.  Now therefore,

BE IT RESOLVED:  that the Brasher Town Board does hereby authorize the local justices to request up to the maximum amount of $30,000 per court for a grant from the State of New York for needed items in the local Town of Brasher Justice Court system.

A motion was made by S. Hourihan, seconded by C. Rose to approve This JCAP application process. All in favor, motion carried.
