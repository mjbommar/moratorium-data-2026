LOCAL LAW No. 4- 2026


TO CONTINUE A MORATORIUM ON BATTERY ENERGY


STORAGE SYSTEM FACILITIES


LEGISLATIVE FINDINGS AND INTENT


The Town Board finds that it is in the best interest of the Town of Halfmoon to extend a

moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Systems Facilities, which consists of rows of

rechargeable batteries housed in self-contained, interconnected storage units. The Town Board

recognizes the potential impact of Battery Energy Storage Systems Facilities on the Town' s

infrastructure, environment, and community character. The Town of Haifrnoon is committed to

ensuring the health, safety, and welfare of its residents, and to the proper regulation of land

uses within the Town of Halfmoon,

The Town Board has determined that it would be prudent to continue to temporarily

pause consideration of Battery Energy Storage System Facilities within the Town of Halfmoon.

Such an extension of the will provide sufficient time to study and address

pause any concerns,


including but not limited to zoning, environmental impacts, public safety, and economic

implications resulting from Battery Energy Storage System Facilities.

SCOPE OF CONTROL


a) Fora period of 180 days from the effective date of this local law, no application for

Battery Energy Storage System Facilities shall be accepted to any board with

appropriate authority in the Town, including but not limited to the Town Board,

Planning Board or Zoning Board of Appeals.

b) Effective Date: The local law applies to all applications for Energy Storage System

Facilities filed with the. T Town of Halfmoon that do not have a SEQRA determination

at the time the moratorium is enacted.

c) Each and every time period regulation and all default provisions otherwise

applicable to development, permits, and/ or approvals is hereby suspended and

temporarily superseded by this local law including but not limited to Section 276 of

the New York State Town Law, during its period of effectiveness.

d) The jurisdiction of the Zoning Board of Appeals to act pursuant to 267- b of the New

York State Town Law relative to relief from this local law is hereby suspended and

temporarily superseded by this local law for the of application for

purposes any

relief from this enactment during the moratorium period.
