October 7, 2026

The Special Tyre Town Board Meeting -Budget Workshop was held Wednesday, October 7, 2026

at 6:30 p.m. in the Tyre Municipal Building, 1082 Gravel Road, Seneca Falls, NY 13148, in the

Town of Tyre.

Supervisor Partee called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.

Pledge of Allegiance Roll Call: Councilman Jeffrey Bennett – here; Councilman Henry Bickel – here; Councilwoman


Nicole Bishop- here; Councilman Kenneth Sutterby – here; Supervisor Beth Partee – here

In Attendance: Mike Gross, Eric Bush


Open Privilege of the Floor: None


Review/Discussion of matters relating to the 2027 Town of Tyre Budgets


• Justice Department Budget Adjustments:


o Justice 1 full-time clerk: increase by $460 to $53,500 total. Wage schedule: current

rate for the first six months; 4% increase effective July 1.

o Justice 2 part-time clerk: increase by $5,691 to $28,400 total to reflect added hours.

• Tax Collector:


o Office hours will be available at Town Hall during collection periods.

o A lockbox will be installed in the lobby for payments.

o The position will be combined with Town Clerk in 2028.

• Town Clerk Budget Adjustment:


o Increase the contractual line by $1,000 (from $2,000 to $3,000). Purpose: cover the

$900 cost for a new software login for the deputy clerk.

• Website Upgrade:


o Significant increase planned to develop a new ADA-compliant website by April 2028.

o New requirements mandate all documents from the last two years be ADA

compliant.

• Central Data Processing (CST):


o Decrease by $8,000 to $12,000. Reason: New York courts now provide their own IT

help desk and security, saving approximately $570 per month.

• Dog Control:


o Recommendation to continue the contract with Finger Lakes SPCA for at least

another year.

• Highway Superintendent Salary:


o Discussed the proposed 4.39% increase versus a standard 4% and compared

wages with neighboring towns (e.g., Romulus). Board agreed to retain the proposed

salary of $70,000.

• Zoning Officer Salary:


o Decrease by $780 to $11,700 total.

• Refuse & Garbage Contract


o The three-year Casella refuse and garbage contract is in its final year (2027).

o An RFP must be issued next year for the 2028 contract.

o A resident asked about bulk item drop-offs; the town does not offer this service.

• Employee Benefits


o Retirement contributions (A9010.8) must increase by $8,000 to a new total of

$43,000. This increase follows a state law change signed by Governor Hochul

raising the employer contribution.

o Health insurance costs are rising by 17.5%; the budget line will increase by $2,000 to

a new total of $30,000. This health insurance line covers two people: the highway

superintendent and the bookkeeper.

• Revenue: Federal Aid, Other


o Question raised about increasing federal aid in light of a new federal headquarters

being built on Route 5 and 20. The new building will be a visitor center and house

offices.

DA Fund and Highway Department Budget • Salaries: A 4% increase is planned for budgeting; final salaries will be set at the


organizational meeting.

• Road Shoulders: Significant discussion on raising shoulders on town roads. Highway Machinery and Equipment


• Truck Replacement: Desire to retire the rusting 2017 red pickup due to costly repairs. A


one-ton dump truck was suggested as a more useful and safer replacement.

• Viking Truck: Payment still due for a Viking truck not delivered after 15 months;


outstanding amount ~$114,000. Contract and final price remain uncertain.

Budget Adjustments and Balancing


• Adjustments made to balance the budget:

o Reduced machinery contractual (DA5130.4) by $5,000.

o Reduced personnel services lines (DA5110.1 and 5142.1) by $3,000 each.

o Reduced capital outlay (DA5130.2) by $500.

• The budget is now balanced; changes can still be made before final adoption.

Fire Protection and Water Districts


• Fire Protection: Clyde Fire Department contract remains $88,000; response from Tyre


pending. Revenue from Del Lago Resort and Casino community mitigation plan will cover

the fire tax.
