RESOLUTION 59-2025

A RESOLUTION EXTENDING THE TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON
WASHINGTON COUNTY'S PROCESSING OF APPLICATIONS FOR
BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS IN ALL UNINCORPORATED
PORTIONS OF WASHINGTON COUNTY PENDING CONSIDERATION
OF AMENDMENTS TO THE WASHINGTON COUNTY ZONING
REGULATIONS

WHEREAS, on December 27, 2023, through Resolution 88-2023 the Board of County
Commissioners imposed a temporary moratorium on the processing of applications for battery
energy storage systems;

WHEREAS, the County has extended that temporary moratorium through several
resolutions to June 30, 2026;

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners desires to extend the temporary
moratorium to complete the process to adopt regulations;

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners incorporates the recitals of its legal
authority to impose temporary moratoriums as described in Resolution 88-2023 and all subsequent
resolutions extending the temporary moratorium into this Resolution; and

WHEREAS, the Board estimates that the time needed to complete its process concerning
the battery energy storage system regulations is an additional seven (7) months from November
25, 2025,

NOW BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF
WASHINGTON COUNTY, COLORADO:

1. This temporary moratorium shall take effect immediately. The County shall not
accept, process, or approve any applications for uses of land that involve the development of
battery energy storage systems, including without limitation special use permit applications and
applications for right-of-way permits.

2. This temporary moratorium shall remain in place until June 30, 2026, unless
terminated or extended.

3. County staff is hereby directed to work with the Washington County Planning
Commission and Board of County Commissioners to complete the regulation adoption process for
battery energy storage systems sufficient to protect the public health, safety and welfare.


DATED this 25th day of November, 2025.

BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
WASHINGTON COUNTY, COLORADO
