Official Regulations approved March 31, 2026

WASHINGTON COUNTY, COLORADO
BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS (BESS) REGULATIONS

1. INTENT AND PURPOSE

The purpose and intent of these Regulations are to establish standards and review procedures for
applications for Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in unincorporated Washington County to protect
the health, welfare, safety and quality of life of the general public, to allow for development while
protecting existing agricultural resources and rural character, the preservation of the environment and
wildlife, and to ensure compatibility with land uses in the vicinity of these facilities.

These regulations apply to all BESS facilities with a Nameplate Capacity equal to or greater than
IMW. These requirements shall apply to all BESS Facilities that are constructed in Washington County
after the effective date of approval by resolution.

2. APPLICABILITY

A These Regulations shall apply to all BESS that are proposed, operated, modified, or
constructed in the County.

B. No modification or alteration, excluding regular maintenance and repair, to an existing
BESS shall be allowed without full compliance with these Regulations.

C, BESS which are grid-scale and connected to a generating source, such as a wind or solar
energy conversion system, or placed on the transmission and distribution system at
substations to help balance local electric supply and demand are subject to these
Regulations and require a building permit.

3. PERMIT REQUIRED

All BESS subject to these Regulations require a use by special review permit.
4, DEFINITIONS
BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS (BESS)

An electrochemical device that collects and stores energy and then subsequently discharges that
energy to provide electricity back onto the power grid. Battery chemistry includes (but is not limited to)
lithium-ion, lead-acid, redox flow, and molten salt (including sodium-based chemistries) with the current
global market dominated by lithium-ion. BESS is grid-scale and paired with a generating resource, such
as a wind or solar energy conversion system, or placed on the transmission and distribution system at
substations to help balance local electric supply and demand. BESS includes the entire system, including
the battery energy storage device along with any ancillary motors/pumps, power electronics, control
electronics, and packaging. Since all electrochemical batteries produce DC current, a BESS typically
consists of the following components:

A The DC battery system (batteries, racks, etc.).
B. Enclosure(s) with thermal management.
