# Ordinances | Hudson, CO

source: https://hudsonco.gov/209/Ordinances
fetched: 2026-09-23T12:10:14+00:00

Municipal Ordinances are local laws passed by the Town Council that apply to the boundaries within Hudson. Common ordinances include, but are not limited to:

-Annexing new properties into Town limits

-Zoning and Rezoning properties

-Revising the Municipal Code to keep up with new state and federal laws as well as trends on good governance

-Monetary matters such as taxes, bonds, and fees

-Local issues such as weeds, curfew, nuisance complaints, and more
