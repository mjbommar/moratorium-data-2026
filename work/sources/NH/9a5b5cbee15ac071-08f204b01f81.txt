II. PUBLIC COMMENT

Chair McDaniel welcomed the public, he acknowledged by the number of people present they were here

to speak to the  recent article in the Concord Monitor about a potential data center in the former

powerplant building, in the Town of Bow.  Chair McDaniel noted that the data center was not on the

agenda, however,  anyone wanting to speak about t the matter could do during public comment.

Before comments were heard, Chair McDaniel went over in detail the process that developers would

need to follow in order to bring forth a proposed data center to the planning and zoning boards  and that

along the way in the process the public would be notified of public hearings, meetings etc. Chair

McDaniel confirmed that the final decision would come from the planning board, not the Board of

Selectmen.

Public Comments were heard from  Bow residents Robert Johnson  75 Bow Center Road, Kris Hancarik

1250 Route 3A, Susan Moore  11 Crockett Drive, Barbara Bennett t 3 Tower Hill Drive, Milo  Cocola  5

Nesbitt Drive, Lyn Lull  26B Grandview Road, Scott Coulombe 18 Merrill Crossing, and Linda Millman

80 Robinson Road. All but one resident was  against having a data center in Bow.

Public comments were also heard from non-residents Claire Edmonds, Nashua, NH, Beth Hamilton

Karas, Pembroke, NH,  Al Benbow, New Market, NH, Kendra Ford, Portsmouth, NH in addition to her

comments  she  provided Chair McDaniel with a signed petition  for 350.org. Executive Councilor Karen

Liot Hill, Lebanon, NH, State  Rep. David Luneau, Hopkinton,  NH, Lucas Cariaty, Hooksett, NH  and

Dr. Robert Feder, Hollis, NH  who were also not in favor of a data center in Bow.

Chair McDaniel noting he may have missed some people made one last request for anyone wishing to

speak. Final comments were heard from Charlie Giamette 3 Ferry Road, Bow, NH, Susan Belair 8

Edgewood Drive, Bow, NH and Katie Vozella,  a former resident of Bow, NH who also were not in

favor of having a data center in Bow.
