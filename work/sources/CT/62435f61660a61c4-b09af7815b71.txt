II. PUBLIC HEARINGS


II.1. REGA22-01, Data Center Moratorium Text Amendment- Town Wide

Staff stated that more public comments received since the close of business regarding data

centers have been provided to the Commission.

Commissioner Zod read the legal ad for REGA22-01

.

Staff stated that in March 2021, Governor Lamont signed a bill to offer tax incentives to

certain data center developments. The state will waive sales and property tax obligations for

a certain amount of time provided a minimum investment is made. The bill requires owners

and developers of qualified data centers to enter into a host municipality fee agreement with

a municipality before beginning the project.

Staff stated that Groton entered into a host municipality fee agreement with Gotspace Data

Partners, LLC for a project to be located off of Route 117, north of I-95. The town chose to

end host municipality fee agreement negotiations with NE Edge LLC for property located

Town of Groton, Connecticut Page 1 Printed on 07/08/2022

between Hazelnut Hill Road and Flanders Road, south of I-95.

Staff stated the Zoning Official determined that Data Centers are considered

Telecommunication Facilities and are allowed in the IM zone.

Staff shared that the proposed amendment, if adopted, would not allow any application for a

Data Center to be accepted or processed by the Planning & Zoning Commission or the

Office of Planning & Development Services. It would terminate 12 months from the effective

date or when replaced by a further text amendment.

Staff stated that the Southeastern Connecticut Council of Governments has reviewed the

application and determined that it will not have a negative inter-municipal impact. The

Connecticut Department of Energy and Environmental Protection has found the application

to be consistent with the Connecticut Coastal Management Act. Staff stated the Zoning

Official has recommended that the definition of Data Centers be modified to limit impact to

businesses and government entities that use computer servers during the moratorium.

Chairman Pritchard asked for any public comments:

III. CONSIDERATION OF PUBLIC HEARINGS


III.1. REGA22-01, Data Center Moratorium Text Amendment- Town Wide

The Commission discussed how the moratorium would affect new data centers versus

improvements to data centers that already exist in the town. They agreed that the

moratorium should apply only to data centers that are over 5,000 square feet.

MOTION: To approve Zoning Regulation text amendment application REGA22-01

to:

1.) Amend Section 2.1 (Definitions) to add a definition of Data Center and modify the

definition of Telecommunication Facility

2.) Add Section 11 Moratoria

3.) Add Section 11.1 Temporary Moratorium on Data Centers

4.) Revise Section 11.1-2 “Uses to read Moratorium is applicable to any Data Center as

defined by these regulations that is over 5,000 square feet”

The Town of Groton Planning and Zoning Commission modifies and adopts zoning

regulation text amendment REGA 22-01 in order to allow adequate time to develop and

adopt zoning regulations to address data centers pursuant to the following findings and

reasons for approval:

1. This amendment is consistent with the Town’s 2016 Plan of Conservation and

Development (POCD) with particular regard to goals, objectives, and recommendations for

the promotion of sustainable development patterns and the evaluation of current zones and

development standards to determine if uses, setbacks, and other requirements are

appropriate.

2. This amendment is consistent with the Town’s Municipal Coastal Program and the CGS

Section 22a-90 through 22a-111 (Coastal Management Act) with particular regard for

protecting coastal resources, protecting water quality, and carefully managing coastal

development

3. This zoning text amendment is made in accordance with the comprehensive plan for the

Town of Groton and provides for reasonable consideration of the environment and Long

Island Sound in accordance with the provisions of CGS 8-2.
