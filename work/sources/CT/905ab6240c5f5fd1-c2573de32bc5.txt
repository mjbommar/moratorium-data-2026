as identifies the zones in which they are allowed. The text amendment will eliminate the moratorium

on data centers currently in the regulations. Data centers w will be allowed in the industrial zone by

Special Permit provided the new use standards are used. Accessory data centers will be allowed for a

VIII. SITE PLANS


VIII.1. SIT23-03, Flanders Development, 0 Flanders Road

Town of Groton, Connecticut Page 4 Printed on 07/14/2023

Gregg Fedus, Professional Engineer, presented for SIT23-03, Flanders Development. Fedus stated the

property has extensive wetlands and the Inland Wetland Agency has granted approval for the

proposed plan. Fedus stated the development is designed as a flex space to accommodate indoor

equipment storage as well as office space. There will be a 60’ X 120’ building with two entrances and

eleven parking spaces. ADA and EVC spaces will be provided. A sidewalk will be constructed along

the frontage of the property as well as along the building and parking area. There will be a bike rack

and trash enclosure. There is a private well and septic on the property. Fedus reviewed the

stormwater plan. Fedus stated lighting will be around the building with additional lighting directly

above both entrances. All lights will be full cut off. There will be a landscape buffer around the

building.

Staff stated the property is located in the mixed-use industrial zone. Staff reiterated the lot is

constrained by wetlands and the plan has been approved by the Inland Wetland Agency.

The Commission discussed the number of employees allowed in the flex space and the lighting plan.

Commissioner Zod suggested a photocell sensor and a timeclock be added to the outdoor lighting.

He stated that the current plan for may provide inadequate light.

Staff stated that a change in use is reviewed when a Change of Tenant application is completed. Staff

will add the lighting as a technical item.

MOTION: To approve S Site Plan #23-03 for a proposed flex space warehouse industrial development

at 0 Flanders Rd. with the following modifications:

1. The design engineer shall certify the stormwater system has been constructed per the approved

plan prior to the issuance of a  a Certificate of Site Plan Compliance.

2. The lighting must be a full cutoff fixture configured with a photocell sensor and “timeclock on-time

clock off” operation and be turned off after business hours.

3. Any modifications to the site plan based on any Federal, State, or local agency requirements shall

require additional review and approval by the Town of Groton land use agencies, as appropriate.

4. Technical items as raised by staff shall be addressed.
