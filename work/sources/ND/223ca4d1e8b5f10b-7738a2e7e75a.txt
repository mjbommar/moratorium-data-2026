April 30th, 2026

OLIVER COUNTY COMMISSION PROCEEDINGS were called to order at 8:47 pm with all members present.

The Pledge of Allegiance was recited.

Discussion was held on the data center moratorium, ordinances and industrial zoning for data centers.

Lee Husfloen moved and Bradley Dahl seconded the motion to lift the Data Center and Cryptocurrency Mining Facilities moratorium. A roll call vote was held; Lee Husfloen-Aye, Bradley Dahl-Aye, Robert Schmidt-Aye. The moratorium was lifted.

Bradley Dahl asked if there were any ordinances to present to the Commission. No ordinances for data centers have been completed for review. Two plans/ordinances were formally presented at the Planning & Zoning meeting earlier in the evening, but the board did not have time to review. They will be on the agenda for the next meeting.

Discussion was held on the using industrial zoning for data centers.

Discussion was held on Home Rule Charter.

Lee Husfloen moved and Bradley Dahl seconded the motion to move into Executive Session for reasonably predictable litigation at 9:03 pm.

The Commission meeting was called back into regular session at 9:14 pm.

No final action was taken following the Executive Session.

Bradley Dahl moved and Lee Husfloen seconded the motion to adjourn the meeting. All in favor, none opposed; motion carried.

The meeting adjourned at 9:15 pm.

The next meeting of the Oliver County Commission will be at 1:00 PM, May 12th, 2026 at the Oliver County Courthouse meeting room.
