(A) For the period beginning June 16, 2025 and ending June 15, 2026 (“moratorium period”), no person shall site, construct, install, permit, or deploy large scale solar energy systems in Tippecanoe County except to the extent a jurisdiction has exempted itself under § 164.02; nor shall departments of Tippecanoe County accept applications for permits from parties seeking leave to site, construct, install, permit, or deploy such systems.

(B) As used in this section, LARGE-SCALE SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEM means a ground-mounted solar energy system, on a tract(s) equal to or more than ten acres, for the purpose of generating photovoltaic power with the primary purpose of selling wholesale or retail generated electricity.

(C) As used in this section, GROUND-MOUNTED SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEM means a solar-energy system that is directly installed into the ground and is not attached or affixed to an existing building.

(D) As used in this section, SOLAR-ENERGY SYSTEM means a system (including solar collector surface and ancillary solar equipment) that collects, stores, and distributes solar energy for uses including but not limited to heating or cooling, generating electricity, or heating water.

(E) During the moratorium period, the county shall study and recommend to the Area Plan Commission potential improvements to the county’s solar regulatory rules.
