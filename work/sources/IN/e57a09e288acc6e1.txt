                                  ORDINANCE NO. 16-2026

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON NEW DATA
                     CENTER DEVELOPMENT
       WHEREAS, the Common Council finds that the City’s Unified Development Ordinance
does not presently contain regulations specifically addressing data center development; and

        WHEREAS, the Plan Commission is actively engaged in reviewing potential




                                                    t
amendments to the Unified Development Ordinance governing data center development with the
assistance of a qualified subject matter expert; and




                                                  f
        WHEREAS, the Common Council finds that allowing new data center development to
proceed before completion of that review may be inconsistent with the City’s future land use




                                                 a
regulations and planning objectives; and




                          r
       WHEREAS, the Common Council finds that a temporary moratorium is necessary to
preserve the integrity of the planning process while permanent regulations are developed;




                        D
      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Common Council of the City of
Valparaiso, Indiana:

Section 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Ordinance is to temporarily suspend the approval of new data center
development while the Plan Commission completes its review and recommends amendments to
the Unified Development Ordinance.

Section 2. Moratorium
During the effective period of this Ordinance, no application for the establishment of a new data
center shall be approved, and no land use approval or permit authorizing the establishment of a
new data center shall be issued by the City.

Section 3. Exceptions.
This Ordinance shall not apply to:
   a. Existing lawful uses;
   b. Routine maintenance, repair, or renovation of existing facilities;
   c. Development for which vested rights have been established under applicable Indiana law.
Section 4. Duration
This Ordinance shall take effect upon passage as provided by law and shall automatically expire
upon the effective date of amendments to the Unified Development Ordinance establishing
regulations governing data center development, unless repealed or extended by the Common
Council; or by July 31, 2028.

Section 5. Severability
If any provision of this Ordinance is held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, such




                                                      t
invalidity shall not affect the remaining provisions, which shall remain in full force and effect.




                                                    f
Section 6. Effective Date
This Ordinance shall be effective upon its passage by the Common Council and approval by the
Mayor, or otherwise as provided by Indiana law.
