NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARINGS FOR A MORATORIUM ON THE APPROVAL OF

DATA CENTER PROJECTS

Notice is hereby given that on Thursday, April 23, 2026 at 6:00 P.M in the City Council

Chambers, located at 500 Central Avenue, Connersville, IN The Fayette County Indiana

Area Plan Commission, Indiana will hold a public hearing for a 12 month moratorium on

the approval and development of data center projects for the purposes of ensuring public

health, safety and general welfare.

Upon adoption of said moratorium by the, Board of Commissioners of Fayette County,

Indiana, and the Common Council, of the City of Connersville, Indiana a twelve month

moratorium on the approval and development of new data center projects, including AI

data centers, to allow research into issues associated with public health, safety, and

general welfare and the development of Area Zoning Code regulations regarding the

development and approval of Data Centers.

Persons desiring to present their views, either written or verbal, may be heard at said time

and place; written objections to the proposal that are filed with the Area Plan

Commission office before the hearing will be considered; oral arguments concerning the

proposal will be heard; and that the hearing may be continued from time to time.

Hard copies and electronic copies are available from the Fayette County, Indiana Area Plan

Commission office located on the lower level of the Fayette County, Indiana Government

Center.
