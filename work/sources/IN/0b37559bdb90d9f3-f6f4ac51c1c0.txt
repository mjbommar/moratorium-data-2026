RESOLUTION NO. 2026-_02

A RESOLUTION ADOPTING A STRATEGIC MORATORIUM ON APPLICATIONS
FOR DATA CENTER DEVELOPMENTS

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners of Shelby County, Indiana, as the executive
and legislative body of Shelby County, have the power to adopt resolutions relating to the
administration of Shelby County government,

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners seeks to foster desirable growth in an orderly
and well considered manner within Shelby County,

WHEREAS, the scale of projects, energy market, federal policy, and growth of data
center development has accelerated significantly and is projected to continue to grow,

WHEREAS, Shelby County, like many other communities, seeks to enact prudent and
common sense ordinances related to the siting, zoning, construction, operation, and maintenance
of Qualified Data Centers, as defined by Indiana Code 6-2.5-15-10 (2024),

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners seek to ensure Shelby County utilizes best
practices and expertise that has developed as a result of recent data center developments

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners believe that a strategic moratorium on the
processing of applications related to the development of Qualified Data Centers in Shelby
County is necessary to ensure forthcoming ordinances align with applicable Indiana law and
incorporate the most recent best practices associated with data centers, their zoning, siting,
construction, operation, and maintenance.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF
COMMISSIONERS OF SHELBY COUNTY, INDIANA,

A moratorium shall be enacted within Shelby County commencing February 23, 2026 for
the filing, processing, review, or acceptance of all new applications related to qualified data
center developments. The moratorium shall continue in full force and effect until the earlier of:
(i) the effective date of a local ordinance governing Qualified Data Center developments; or (ii)
August 23, 2026.

Duly adopted by the Board of Commissioners of Shelby County at a regularly scheduled
public meeting held on the as day of Fee, 2026.
