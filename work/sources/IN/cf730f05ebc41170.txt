                   THE WABASH COUNTY PLAN COMMISSION

      PROPOSAL TO IMPOSE A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM
  ON THE ISSUANCE OF IMPROVEMENT LOCATION PERMITS FOR
             THE DEVELOPMENT OF DATA CENTERS


                                               Background

       On December 5, 2022, the Board of Commissioners of Wabash County adopted Ordinance No.
2022-85-14, a Zoning Ordinance for the Unincorporated Areas of Wabash County, Indiana, and the Towns
of LaFontaine, Lagro, and Roann, Indiana (“Zoning Ordinance”).

        The Zoning Ordinance currently fails to provide any regulations related to data center projects
within Wabash County, which omission constitutes a defect in the Zoning Ordinance that should be
corrected before any improvement location permits for data center projects in any zoning district are issued.

         The Wabash County Plan Commission now proposes an ordinance to impose a temporary
moratorium on the acceptance, processing, and approval of all applications and permits, including the
issuance of improvement locations permits, in all zoning districts for any structure related to a data center
project, including any construction or operation of data centers and all appurtenant facilities, in order to
amend the Zoning Ordinance to address issues related to data center projects, as follows:
   BEFORE THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WABASH COUNTY, INDIANA

                        ORDINANCE NO. ___________________________

            AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON
            THE ISSUANCE OF IMPROVEMENT LOCATION PERMITS FOR
                     THE DEVELOPMENT OF DATA CENTERS

        WHEREAS, on December 5, 2022, the Board of Commissioners of Wabash County adopted
Ordinance No. 2022-85-14, a Zoning Ordinance for the Unincorporated Areas of Wabash County, Indiana,
and the Towns of LaFontaine, Lagro, and Roann, Indiana (“Zoning Ordinance”)

        WHEREAS, the Zoning Ordinance currently fails to provide any regulations related to data center
projects within Wabash County, which omission constitutes a defect in the Zoning Ordinance that should
be corrected before any improvement location permits for structures related to data center projects in any
zoning district are issued.

         WHEREAS, the Wabash County Plan Commission initiated a proposal to adopt an ordinance to
impose a temporary moratorium on the acceptance, processing, and approval of all applications and permits,
including the issuance of improvement locations permits, in all zoning districts for any structure related to
a data center project, including any construction or operation of data centers and all appurtenant facilities,
in order to amend the Zoning Ordinance to address issues related to data center projects.

        WHEREAS, the Plan Commission timely gave notice to interested parties and held a public hearing
in accordance with I.C. 36-7-4-604.

       WHEREAS, the Plan Commission timely certified its recommendation on the proposal in
accordance with I.C. 36-7-4-605, thereby recommending its adoption.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS ORDAINED AS FOLLOWS:

            1. “Data center” shall mean a structure used primarily for the storage, management,
               processing, and transmission of digital data and that houses computer or network
               equipment, systems, servers, appliances, and other associated components related to digital
               data storage, processing, and related operations. Data center uses include data storage
               facilities, server farms, artificial intelligence training or processing, image processing,
               cloud computing, email servicing, and other similar uses.

            2. There is now imposed a temporary moratorium on the acceptance, processing, and/or
               approval of all applications and permits, including the issuance of improvement locations
               permits, for any structure related to a data center project, including any construction or
               operation of data centers and all appurtenant facilities, in all zoning districts in the
               unincorporated areas of Wabash County.
   3.    This Ordinance shall be effective in the unincorporated areas of Wabash County subject to
        the jurisdiction of the Wabash County Zoning Ordinance upon its adoption. This Ordinance
        shall be effective in the incorporated areas of the Towns of LaFontaine, Lagro, and Roann,
        Indiana upon its adoption by the appropriate legislative body.

   4. This Ordinance shall remain in effect until the Zoning Ordinance is amended to provide
      regulations related to data center projects within Wabash County, or for a period of eighteen
      (18) months from the date of adoption, whichever occurs sooner.

   5. If any portion of this Ordinance is determined to be unconstitutional, void, ineffective, or
      otherwise in violation of any state or federal law by a court of competent jurisdiction, the
      remaining portions of this Ordinance shall continue to be in full force and effect.
