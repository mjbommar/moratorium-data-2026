The Putnam County Board of Commissioners met on November 17, 2025, at 9:00 AM in regular

session. The meeting was held at the Putnam County Courthouse. Woodall opened the meeting

and the pledge to the flag was given.

VISITORS BUREAU

Jerrod Jernigan, Director of the Putnam County Visitors Bureau, presented an award to the county

for Putnam County Best of the Best for Edna Collins Bridge.

Mr. Jernigan also brought to the Commissioners attention that two appointments would be up in

January 2026 for the Putnam County Tourism Commission and Putnam County Convention and

Visitors Bureau Board. He asked that Wes Kendall and Emily Knuth be reappointed.

PLANNING DEPARTMENT

Cheyenne Maddox with Planning & Building Department presented a rezone of 1.7 acres for Darnell

Barnett going from C2 to residential so they may build a residence. Cheyenne stated they have not

received anything against it. Beck made a motion approve ordinance 2025-11-17-1. Helmer

seconded. The motion carried unanimously.

COUNTY ROAD 125 NORTH

Bonnie Sparks stated she would like the gravel road that starts at CR 150 N and goes to 125 N to be

chipped and sealed. She stated handicapped and elderly residents live along it. Clint Maddox,

Highway Director stated he’s familiar with the road. He stated when they chip and seal on gravel

roads the residents usually are not happy with the end product. He stated gravels are gravel for a

reason due to the lack of traffic and the cost. Sparks stated she is concerned when it rains and

washes out over a third of the road. She asked if something could be done. Discussion was made.

Woodall stated they would investigate.

911 REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL EMPLOYEES

David Clodfelter, 911 Director was asked at the last meeting to find a way to help offset costs of

hiring 2 additional employees. Clodfelter stated he has 2 supervisory positions that are open with a

total budget of $116,000. He stated if he could have 4 senior dispatchers instead of his current 3

,

not fill the two supervisory positions he would only need to find money for one of the requested

positions. Discussion was made regarding where the remaining money would come from. Woodall

made a motion to start the next step asking the Council for approval and then figure out the

funding. Beck seconded. The motion carried unanimously.

PLANNING

October th C

Woodall stated at the 20meeting the ommissioners voted 2-1 to put a one-year

moratorium for UDO’s. The Planning Commission met and voted to recommend a moratorium on

solar energy. Beck stated after what they have dealt with this year he believes they have a lot of

research to do before they can decide to allow solar power. The moratorium would give them that

time. Woodall asked if a moratorium is approved today would that be for the future? Ensley stated

it would be effective today. y. It would not include the previous projects. The moratorium would not

apply to towns unless they vote to adopt it. The Commissioners heard from members of the

audience. Beck made a motion to enforce the moratorium. Helmer seconded. Woodall voted no.

The moratorium passed 2 to 1.

MINUTES

November 3, 2025 – Helmer made the motion to approve. Beck seconded. The motion passed

unanimously.

VETERANS OFFICE

Woodall stated the Veterans Officer resigned due to health issues. They have received one

application, Brian Hopkins. He works in Indianapolis for the Veterans Office. Helmer stated he

would be a good fit. Helmer made a motion to hire Brian Hopkins as the new Veterans Officer. Beck

seconded. The motion carried unanimously.

COUNTY PAINTINGS

Woodall stated they were made aware that the Commissioners needed to approve a person using a

picture of a painting owned by them for the cover of their book they wrote. Woodall made a motion

to allow Larry Tippin to use a picture of one of the county’s paintings for the front of his book. Beck

seconded. The motion carried unanimously.

KEIGHTLY ROAD PROPERTY

Woodall made a motion to pay a claim today for the purchase of the Keightly Road property.

Helmer seconded. The motion carried unanimously.

LAWNMOWER

Woodall stated that there is a lawnmower that is not in good condition at the Cloverdale EMS

station that an employee wants to purchase. Woodall made the motion to allow the employee to

purchase it for $300. Helmer seconded. The motion carried unanimously.

ANNEX UPDATE

Woodall stated Jason Simler with Baker Tilly will be at the council meeting tomorrow night to

discuss financing for the annex. The annex committee met in Indianapolis last week to look at

materials that will go in the annex. He stated part of the process to start the loan is to have

collateral and they will have to decide what that will be.

HEALTH DEPARTMENT

Woodall stated the property the county is purchasing for the health department is moving along.

The hospital will be moved out by March 1, 2026. Meantime, a construction company is working on

a quote to renovate the building.
