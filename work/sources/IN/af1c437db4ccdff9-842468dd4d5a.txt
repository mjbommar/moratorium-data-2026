NOTICE OF ADOPTION OF ORDINANCE

STARKE COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Notice is hereby given  that on the 17th day of February, 2026, the Board of Commissioners of

Starke County, Indiana, adopted Ordinance No. 02 of 2026.

Title of Ordinance:  AN ORDINANCE EXTENDING THE MORATORIUM ON

COMMERCIAL SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS (CSES).

Summary of Provisions:  The Ordinance extends the previously adopted moratorium (Ordinance

No. 07-2025) on the issuance of improvement location permits for Commercial Solar Energy

Systems (CSES).

The Board of Commissioners determined that the current Zoning Ordinance fails to provide

regulations related to CSES projects, constituting a defect that must be corrected. To allow time

to correct this defect, the moratorium is hereby extended through July 25, 2026.

Effective Date:  This Ordinance shall be effective in the unincorporated areas of Starke County

upon its adoption.

Availability of Text:  A full copy of the Ordinance is available for inspection in the Starke

County Planning and Building Department  and the Starke County Auditor's Office.

Dated this 17th day of February, 2026.
