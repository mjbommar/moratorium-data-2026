The City of Cedartown is governed by a five-member board of elected commissioners. Commissioners serve terms of four years, until their respective successors are elected and qualified.

The City Commission meets two times a month. Work sessions are conducted the first Monday of each month at 6 p.m. Regular sessions are held on the second Monday of each month at 6 p.m. Both work sessions and regular sessions take place in the Council Room at City Hall, 201 East Avenue.

In order to be eligible to qualify as a candidate for election to the city commission, that person must be 21 years of age and must currently reside and have lived in Cedartown for at least one year.

The terms of the commissioners’ offices begin on the first day of January following their election. Commissioners may succeed themselves as many times as they are elected.

**COMMISSION CHAIR SAM BRANCH**

![](https://i0.wp.com/cedartowngeorgia.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/SBranch-2022-web.jpg?resize=720%2C900&ssl=1)

Sam Branch was first elected in November of 2019 in a special election and has since been elected to serve his first full four-year term. Sam is a native and lifelong resident of Polk County. After graduating from Cedartown High School in 2009, Sam attended Jacksonville State University where he studied Political Science and was a member of the Kappa Sigma fraternity.

Sam has spent the last several years working in the banking industry and is currently employed by First National Community Bank in Rome as Treasury Management Officer for the Broad Street branch. Sam also serves as an assistant funeral director for Gammage Funeral Home and the host of “Sports Talk” on WGAA Radio. Sam also works as a play-by-play and color commentator for CHS basketball and baseball on WGAA, and the public address announcer for CHS Bulldogs football.

Sam is active in the community, volunteering in the Exchange Club of Cedartown where he is a past club president, and a current board member for the Polk County Fair and the Cedartown Civic Arts Commission. He also serves as a board member of the Our House Domestic Violence shelter and is a board member of the Cedartown Farmers Market. Sam currently serves as the city’s representative on the Northwest Georgia
Regional Commission Council. His email address is [sbranch@cedartowngeorgia.gov](mailto:sbranch@cedartowngeorgia.gov)

**COMMISSION PRO TEM MATT FOSTER**

![](https://i0.wp.com/cedartowngeorgia.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/MFoster-2022-web.jpg?resize=720%2C900&ssl=1)

Matt Foster was first elected to the City Commission in 2014.

A Cedartown native, Matt is employed as a teacher at Cedartown Middle School, where he also serves as the chair of the social studies department. Prior to moving to CMS, he taught at Northside Elementary School, where he was recognized as Teacher of the Year.

He also serves as a board member of the Cedartown Wheelchair Athlete Training Camp & 5K Race, the Cedartown Development Authority, and the Cedartown Performing Arts Commission, as well as being the head coach of the Cedartown High School tennis team and the assistant coach of the CHS cross country team. He enjoys volunteering in various community projects and charities, such as Keep Polk Beautiful and the CJSL Follies.

An Eagle Scout, Matt earned a Bachelor’s Degree in Political Science from the University of Georgia, and a Certificate in History and International Affairs from Oxford University in the United Kingdom. His email address is [mcfoster@cedartowngeorgia.gov](mailto:mcfoster@cedartowngeorgia.gov)

**COMMISSIONER JESSICA PAYTON**

![commissioner Jessica Payton](https://i0.wp.com/cedartowngeorgia.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Commissioner-Payton-2022-webjpg.jpg?resize=720%2C900&ssl=1)

Jessica Brewster Payton was elected to the commission in November of 2017. She was officially sworn into office on January 8, 2018. This is Jessica’s second term as a Cedartown City Commissioner. She is a longtime resident of Cedartown. Jessica is 1998 graduate of Cedartown High School, a 2004 graduate of Methodist University in Fayetteville, N.C. and a 2016 graduate of GNTC’s Neuromuscular Therapy program.

Jessica is very involved in Georgia Municipal Association where she serves on the Federal Policy Council. In addition to running her own business, Jessica is active in the community and serves on the boards of Mosaic Place and Polk Against Drugs. She is also a member of the Cedartown Junior Service League and the First United Methodist Church Choir. Her email address is jpayton@cedartowngeorgia.gov

**COMMISSIONER DALE TUCK**

![](https://i0.wp.com/cedartowngeorgia.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/DTuck-2022-webjpg.jpg?resize=720%2C900&ssl=1)

Dale Tuck is completing her fourth term in office. She served as Commission Chair for four years in her second term and has also served as Pro-tem. Tuck currently serves as Chairman of the Downtown Development Authority, a member of the Cedartown DCA, and a board member for both the Sara Hightower Regional Library and the Polk County Library.

Tuck retired from Polk School District in 2012 after 25 years, but returned to Cedartown and Rockmart High Schools as a dual-enrollment instructor for Georgia Northwestern Technical
College and Georgia Highlands College. After retiring for a second time in 2020, Tuck now serves as Chairman of the Board of the Polk School District Foundation.

Tuck earned a Bachelor’s Degree in Secondary Education from Berry College, a Master’s Degree from Shorter College, and an Educational Specialist Degree from the University of Alabama. Tuck was also a finalist for Georgia Teacher of the year in 2004.

Currently she dedicates her time to her family, the City of Cedartown and First Baptist Church, where she teaches the Gantt-Wheeler Sunday School Class. Her email address is [dtuck@cedartowngeorgia.gov](mailto:dtuck@cedartowngeorgia.gov)

**CITY MANAGER EDWARD GUZMAN**

![city manager Edward Guzman](https://i0.wp.com/cedartowngeorgia.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2F5A9021-copy.jpg?resize=720%2C900&ssl=1)

Edward Guzman is a 2006 graduate of Cedartown High School and a 2010 graduate of the University of Georgia, receiving undergraduate degrees in both political science and criminal justice. During his undergrad career, he studied abroad for a semester at the University of Oxford in England. He is bi-lingual, speaking both English and fluent Spanish. Edward earned his Master’s Degree in Public Administration from Kennesaw State University in May 2019. He was also selected as the 2019 Outstanding Scholar, School of Government and International Affairs while earning his MPA. He is also a 2019 graduate of the prestigious Leadership Georgia program. He has worked at the City of Cedartown for 11 years, serving in various capacities, including clerk, assistant city clerk and assistant city manager. In 2013, he was awarded the Emily Shaw Administrative Award. He may be contacted at 770-748-3220, extension 3039 or via email at [eguzman@cedartowngeorgia.gov](mailto:eguzman@cedartowngeorgia.gov)
