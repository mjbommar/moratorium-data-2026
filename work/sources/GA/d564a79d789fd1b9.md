## **Brooks County is Hiring!**

#### **Executive Assistant to the Administration and Staff**

#### **Job Description & Performance Requirements**

**Purpose**

This position provides executive-level administrative, purchasing, financial, payroll, project management, and event coordination support to the County Administrator and their respective Directors and staff. The position serves as a central administrative resource for Brooks County government and assists constitutional offices and County administrative departments while ensuring professionalism, confidentiality, efficiency, and compliance with applicable laws and county policies.

**Primary Responsibilities**

* Provide executive administrative support to the County Administrator and Admin Staff.
* Assist the County Clerk with calendars, meetings, agendas, minutes, correspondence, and special projects.
* Assist Human Resources and Finance in the maintenance of confidential personnel, legal, financial, and executive records.
* Coordinate communications with elected officials, department heads, constitutional officers, vendors, citizens, and outside agencies.
* Prepare reports, resolutions, ordinances, contracts, presentations, and other official documents, as assigned.
* Assist the County Clerk in coordinating Commission workshops, meetings, and follow-up actions.

**Purchasing Agent Responsibilities**

* Serve as the centralized purchasing agent for Brooks County administrative departments and constitutional offices as assigned.
* Ensure compliance with county purchasing policies and Georgia procurement requirements.
* Prepare purchase orders, solicit quotations, manage bids/RFPs, maintain procurement files, administer vendor relationships, monitor contracts and renewals, and assist departments with procurement planning.

**Payroll & Finance Support**

* Process payroll accurately and confidentially.
* Assist Human Resources in the maintenance of payroll records and assist in the reconciliation of payroll reports.
* Coordinate with Human Resources and the Finance Director regarding personnel actions affecting payroll.
* Assist with audits, budgeting, financial reports, and departmental expenditure tracking.
* Provide finance office backup during peak workloads, audits, and budget development.

**Budget Support**

* Assist in annual operating and capital budget preparation.
* Compile departmental requests, prepare spreadsheets, analyze expenditures, monitor amendments, and support grant and capital planning activities.

**Events Coordinator**

* Coordinate county-sponsored events, ceremonies, workshops, ribbon cuttings, employee recognition events, public meetings, and special initiatives.
* Coordinate venues, vendors, invitations, media notifications, logistics, audiovisual needs, volunteers, and post-event evaluations.

**Knowledge, Skills & Abilities**

* Excellent written and verbal communication.
* Advanced Microsoft Office proficiency.
* Ability to maintain confidentiality.
* Strong organizational, analytical, financial, and customer service skills.
* Ability to manage multiple deadlines with minimal supervision.

**Minimum Qualifications**

* Associate degree/substantial credits from an accredited business institution; bachelor's degree in public administration, business, accounting, finance, or related field preferred but not required for the position offer.
* 5 years of progressively responsible administrative experience preferred; At least 1 year experience in a municipal/county or state government position is required.
* Experience with governmental purchasing, payroll, budgeting, or municipal/county operations preferred.

**Performance Standards**

* Commission agenda packets completed by established deadlines.
* Payroll accuracy target of99% or greater.
* Purchase orders processed within established timelines.
* Bid and procurement files maintained in compliance with policy.
* Professional communications with elected officials, employees, vendors, and the public.
* Projects completed accurately and on schedule.
* Maintains strict confidentiality.
* Demonstrates initiative, sound judgment, and continuous process improvement.

***Other Duties***

* Perform additional duties assigned by the County Administrator consistent with county operations.

***This is a full-time position and will be eligible for all benefits offered to Brooks County employees via the employee hiring packet and handbook at the assigned start date.***

## **NOTICE OF PROPERTY TAX INCREASE**

The Brooks County Board of Commissioner has tentatively adopted a 2026 millage rate which will require an increase in property tax by 1.59 percent for the unincorporated area and by 13.75 percent for the incorporated area.

All concerned citizens are invited to the public hearing on this tax increase to be held at the Brooks County Admin Building, Board of Commissioners meeting room, 610 S Highland Rd, Quitman, GA on **August 20, 2026 at 11:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m.** Times and places of additional public hearings on this tax increase are at the Brooks County Admin Building, Board of Commissioners meeting room, 610 S Highland Rd, Quitman,GA on August 27, 2026 at 6:00 p.m.

This tentative increase will result in a millage rate of 14.079 mills, an increase of .220 mills for the
**unincorporated area**. Without this tentative increase, the millage rate will be no more than 13.859 mills. The proposed tax increase for a home with a fair market value of 200,000 is approximately $17.16 and the proposed tax increase for non-homestead property with a fair market value of 225,000 is approximately $19.80.

This tentative increase will result in a millage rate of 14.079 mills, an increase of 1.702 mills for the
**incorporated area**. Without this tentative increase, the millage rate will be no more than 12.377 mills. The
proposed tax increase for a home with a fair market value of 200,000 is approximately $132.75 and the
proposed tax increase for non-homestead property with a fair market value of 225,000 is approximately
$153.18.
