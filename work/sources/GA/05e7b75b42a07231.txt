 GEORGIA TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL                     CRITERIA       FOR    REQUIRED        FOR THE

        WHEREAS, the governing authority of the City of Social Circle, Georgia is the Mayor
and Council thereof; and


        WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Council of Social Circle, Georgia adopted its Uniform
Development Code on June 20, 2023, in the form               as   Ordinance 2023-3, and


        WHEREAS, the demand for consideration of additional data centers has accelerated and
the Mayor and City Council have determined that more precise regulations of this
                                                                                      type of use are
necessary under the Uniform Development Code of the City of Social Circle; and,


        WHEREAS, the Flaming Department Staff and Flaming Commission of the City of
Social Circle have met and considered recommended changes to the Uniform Development Code
for the purpose of more precisely regulating data center uses with the city limits which
recommendations are included in the amendment to the Uniform Development Code; and


        WHEREAS, the governing authority of the City of Social Circle, Georgia desires to amend
Section 3.4.6 of the    Uniform
                              Development Code as              herein consistent           with the
recommendations of the Flaming Staff and Flaming Commission of the City; and


        WHEREAS, the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Social Circle, Georgia, will
be positively impacted by the adoption of this Ordinance.


        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Mayor and Council of the City of
Social Circle, Georgia, and it is hereby ordained by authority of same, as follows:


        Section 1.     The current Section 3.4.6 of the Uniform Development Code is hereby
deleted in its entirety, and the following amended form of Section 3.4.6 is approved to read as

following:

5        Noise Standards.
    a.    Noise generated by any equipment, operations, or activities associated with a
          data center, including but not limited to cooling systems and emergency

          backup generators, shall not exceed 60 dB(A) during daytime hours (7:00 am.
            11:00 pm.) or 50 dB(A) during nighttime hours (1 1 :00 pm.      7:00 a.m.), as
          measured at the property line where the site adjoins residential or agricultural
          zoned property.
    b. Primary data center buildings shall be constructed of materials with
       demonstrated sound attenuation properties, such as tilt-up concrete panels,
       concrete masonry units (CMU), or equivalent assemblies. Metal panel

       construction shall only be permitted where the applicant demonstrates,
       through acoustic analysis, that the building envelope will achieve
       compliance with the           noise performance standards at the property line.
    c. Facilities shall be subject to inspection by the City, or its authorized agents,
       once per calendar year, at minimum, for compliance with the noise standards

       once facility is in operation.

    d. Emergency standby generators serving any lawful use may operate at any time
       during loss of utility power or other bona       emergency conditions and may
       also operate for routine maintenance and testing reasonably necessary to
       ensure emergency readiness; such maintenance and testing is permitted

       between the hours 7:00 am. and 6:00 pm, Monday through Friday, and
       between the hours of 9:00 am. and 6:00 pm. on Saturday, and shall not, by
               itself, constitute a violation of this chapter, provided the generators are
               maintained with manufacturer recommended                  sound attenuation and
               the operator retains maintenance/testing records for twenty-four (24) months
               and provides them to the city upon reasonable request; operation outside the
               stated hours shall not be a violation when reasonably necessary for emergency
               readiness due to manufacturer requirements, commissioning, repairs, post-
               event               or                  testing and when reasonable measures
               are taken to minimize    impacts.
