                                                                Resolution R-040726b

    A RESOLUTION OF THE NEWTON COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS
        ENACTING A LIMITED MORATORIUM ON THE ACCEPTANCE OF
       APPLICATIONS, REQUESTS, OR SUBMISSIONS THAT PROPOSE THE
                    DEVELOPMENT OF DATA CENTERS

        WHEREAS, the Newton County Board of Commissioners ("Board") is the governing
body of Newton County, Georgia ("County"), and therefore is charged with regulating
development and providing appropriate public infrastructure and services to best serve the public
interest, including but not limited to such infrastructure and services associated with current and
future developments within the County; and

       WHEREAS, the Board has recently observed increased interest in the siting and
development of data centers within the County; and

       WHEREAS, the Board has determined that the potential development of data centers in
the County threatens the County's ability to provide adequate infrastructure, water, utilities, and
services; and

       WHEREAS, the Board has determined that the County's ex1stmg ordinances and
development regulations do not address, except in limited part, or anticipate the unique
characteristics and potential impacts of data center developments; and

       WHEREAS, the Board intends to study, develop, and consider the adoption of an
ordinance to address data center development in a manner consistent with the public interest; and

       WHEREAS, the Board desires to delay submission and consideration of applications,
requests, or submissions that propose, in whole or in part, the develop~nt of a data center within
the County, including but not limited to applications for rezoning, special use permits, conditional
use permits, development permits, site plans, building permits, or other land-disturbing or
development approvals related to data center facilities, until such an ordinance has been adequately
studied, developed, and considered; and

        WHEREAS, a moratorium is a necessary tool enabling local governments to temporarily
discontinue applications, requests and submissions in order to update its current ordinances, rules,
and regulations; and

       WHEREAS, on February 17, 2026, the Board enacted an emergency moratorium barring
until March 18, 2026, the acceptance of applications, requests, or submissions that propose, in
whole or in part, the development of a data center within the County; and

        WHEREAS, on March 3, 2026, the Board revised the emergency moratorium, barring
until April 8, 2026, the acceptance of applications, requests, or submissions that propose, in whole
or in part, the development of a data center within the County; and

       WHEREAS, the Board desires to extend the moratorium on the acceptance of applications,
requests, or submissions that propose, in whole or in part, the development of a data center within
the County as it continue to study, develop, and consider the adoption of an ordinance to address
data center development in a manner consistent with the public interest; and

        WHEREAS, the Board finds that a moratorium barring until October 7, 2026, the
acceptance of applications, requests, or submissions that propose, in whole or in part, the
development of a data center within the County, including but not limited to applications for
rezoning, special use permits, conditional use permits, development permits, site plans, building
permits, or other land-disturbing or development approvals related to data center facilities to be
reasonably necessary, the least restrictive means available, a reasonable exercise of the County's
police power, and in the best interest of the public health, safety, and welfare;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS
OF NEWTON COUNTY, GEORGIA, AS FOLLOWS:

    1. The statements set forth in the preamble are incorporated herein by reference and constitute
        a material part of this Resolution.
   2.   The Board does hereby enact a moratorium barring until October 7, 2026, the acceptance
        of applications, requests, or submissions that propose, in whole or in part, the development
        of a data center within the County, including but not limited to applications for rezoning,
        special use permits, conditional use permits, development permits, site plans, building
        permits, or other land-disturbing or development approvals related to data center facilities.
   3.   The purpose of this emergency moratorium is to allow the County adequate time to study,
        develop, and consider the adoption of an ordinance to address data center development in
        a manner consistent with the public interest and the unique characteristics and potential
        impacts associated with the development of data centers.
   4.   The moratorium imposed by this Resolution shall terminate on the earliest date of (1)
        October 7, 2026; (2) approval by the Board of an additional moratorium after a public
        hearing; or (3) Board action terminating the moratorium; and
   5.   This Resolution shall be effective upon a majority vote by the Board.

      SO RESOLVED this 7th day of April, 2026, the public's health, safety, and welfare
demanding it.
