The Lamar County Board of Commissioners met in a regular meeting on August 18, 2026. Board
members present for the meeting were: Chairman Ryran Traylor, Vice Chair Jason Lovett,
District 1 Commissioner Jarrod Fletcher, District 3 Commissioner Ashley Gilles, and District 4
Commissioner Truman Boyle. Staff present were County Administrator Sean Townsend, County
Attorney Scott Mayfield, and County Clerk Anita Buice.

Chairman Traylor called the meeting to order at 7:07 pm and led everyone in the Pledge of
Allegiance. Invocation was delivered by Pastor Rex Kessler of Lilla Memorial Baptist Church.

Approval of Agenda

Commissioner Gilles motioned to approve the agenda as presented, seconded by Commissioner
Fletcher. Motioned carried 4-0.

Proclamation: 12U Lady Trojans State Championship

Commissioner Boyle invited the 12U Lady Trojans recreation team and coaches to the front of
the meeting to accept a proclamation (attached) honoring the team for the state championship
win,

Department Highlight: Animal Services

Director Tyler White was present to provide an update on operations. He provided figures
covering April 2025 through August 17, 2026 including 1,148 total animal intakes. Of those
intakes, 1,108 were able to have a live release, either through reclamation by the owner, adoption
through the shelter, or intake by a rescue organization. As these figures provide a 91% live
outcome, Lamar County Animal Services has been named a No Kill Shelter. Mr. White thanked
the board and the community for the continued success.

Board Appointments

Department of Family and Children Services: Re-appointment of Cyndy Roberts for a 4
year term

Chairman Traylor thanked Ms. Roberts, who was present in the audience, for her service.

Commissioner Gilles motioned to re-appoint Ms. Roberts for another 4 year term, seconded by
Commissioner Lovett. Motioned carried 4-0.

Commissioner Lovett motioned to approve Ordinance 2026-09 (attached) that includes the
following conditions: In the event applicants’ corporation is dissolved, the business ceases
operation for 12 months or ceases to be used for the purpose identified in the application, or in
the event ownership of the property is transferred and the transferee ceases to use the property
for the purpose identified in the application, Lamar County may, at its discretion, file an
application to rezone the property back to Agricultural Residential )A-R) and the owner will not
oppose same, The terms and conditions of the covenant shall be binding upon applicant and his
successors in title and shall run with the title to the property. Notice of this covenant may be filed
of record in the office of the Clerk of the Superior Court of Lamar County. Motion was seconded

Resolution 2026-10 To set the 2026 Millage Rate

Commissioner Lovett motioned to approve Resolution 2026-10 (attached), setting the 2026
millage rate at 9.406, seconded by Commissioner Fletcher for discussion. Commissioner Gilles
stated the county needs to reduce spending, and to generate revenue by selling property.
Commissioner Fletcher interjected that selling property only helps for one year, that building a
savings is the way to be sustainable. He added that he doesn’t want people to think the county
has a surplus. Yes, belts can be tightened but the county is not in a position to stop services.

PROCLAMATION HONORING THE LAMAR COUNTY RECREATION 12U LADY
TROJANS TRAINING LEGENDS STATE CHAMPIONS

WHEREAS, the Lamar County Recreation 12U Lady Trojans, under the leadership of Head Coach

Brandon Rivers, proudly represented Lamar County in the 2026 Training Legends All-Star Tournament;
and

WHEREAS, the 2026 season marked Lamar County Recreation's first participation in the Training

Legends All-Star program, making the Lady Trojans the first Lamar County team to capture the Training
Legends 12U Girls State Championship; and

WHEREAS, the Lady Trojans competed against outstanding teams from Central Georgia during the
Training Legends Substate Tournament held June 13-14, 2026, earning their advancement to the State
Tournament through exceptional teamwork, determination, and skill; and

WHEREAS, on June 27, 2026, the team competed in the Training Legends State Tournament, finishing

undefeated in pool play and advancing to the championship game held on June 28, 2026, in Hampton,
Georgia; and

WHEREAS, the Lady Trojans faced a talented Spalding County All-Star team in the championship game
and, through outstanding pitching, exceptional defensive play, timely hitting, anda complete team effort,
secured the 2026 Training Legends 12U Girls State Championship; and

WHEREAS, throughout the tournament, these young athletes displayed remarkable sportsmanship,
dedication, perseverance, and character while representing Lamar County with pride and distinction; and

WHEREAS, the Lamar County Recreation Department proudly recognizes the accomplishments of these
student-athletes and their coaches for their commitment, hard work, and the positive example they have
set for the youth of our community.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT PROCLAIMED, that the Lamar County Board of Commissioners hereby
congratulates the Lamar County Recreation 12U Lady Trojans on winning the 2026 Training Legends
12U Girls State Championship and commends the players and coaches for this historic achievement.
The Board extends its sincere appreciation for the pride, dedication, and sportsmanship they demonstrated
while representing Lamar County and wishes them continued success in all future endeavors,

ORDINANCE NO. 2026-10

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE PURPOSE OF REZONING PROPERTY IN LAMAR
COUNTY, GEORGIA, AND TO AMEND THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF LAMAR
COUNTY, GEORGIA

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners of Lamar County, Georgia (hereinafter
“County”) under the authority of its County Code and the Constitution and Laws of the State of
Georgia, is empowered by virtue of its police power to regulate the health, safety and welfare of
the citizens of the County, and to provide for and enact zoning and developmental regulations;
and

WHEREAS, the County has enacted the current Zoning Ordinance of the County and
thereby adopted the Official Zoning Map of the County; and

WHEREAS, in 2017, the County initiated the rezoning of over 2600 parcels of land,
including the property located at 8 Liberty Hill Street (028 010); this particular property being
rezoned from Agricultural-Residential to Estate Residential; and

WHEREAS, Lamar County Planning and Community Development, initiated an

application to change the zoning classification of 8 Liberty Hill Street (028 010) from Estate
Residential to Agricultural Residential; and

WHEREAS, notice of this application, public hearings, and action was properly
advertised in the legal organ, a sign was placed on the subject property, and letters describing the

application and hearing dates were sent to property owners within 500’ of the subject property;
and

WHEREAS, a public hearing was held on August 6, 2026 and the application was
reviewed by the Lamar County Joint Planning Commission, and a second public hearing on the
application was conducted by the County on August 18, 2026 pursuant to O.C.G.A. §33-66-1, et
seg. and local ordinances at the Lamar County Courthouse; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners considered the proposed amendment, and all
alternate proposals or amendments, the report of the Planning Commission, and all data and
evidence taken at both public hearings; and

WHEREAS, it is deemed by the County that rezoning the within described property and
amending the Official Zoning Map of the County is in conformance with the sound
comprehensive planning principles, of substantial benefit to the public, and in promotion of the
