# Now Accepting Public Comments about Data Centers, BESS, and Solar Energy Systems

source: https://www.putnamcountyga.us/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=22
fetched: 2026-09-23T13:49:16+00:00

Home Posted on August 20, 2026

Putnam County is seeking public comments regarding Data Centers, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), and Solar Energy Systems.

Copies of the proposed ordinances addressing these uses are available for review. The public is encouraged to review the proposed ordinances and submit comments for consideration by the Board of Commissioners. Submit your public comments here: [Public Comments about Data Centers, Battery Energy Storage Systems, and Solar Energy Systems](https://www.putnamcountyga.us/FormCenter/General-Forms-4/Public-Comments-about-Data-Centers-Batte-49)

[Resolution Enacting Moratorium](https://www.putnamcountyga.us/DocumentCenter/View/1226/2026-08-07-Resolution-for-Moratorium-on-Data-Centers-BESS-Solar-Farms-signed?bidId=)

[Proposed Ordinance: Chapter 53-Energy Creation, Energy Storage, and Digital Infrastructure](https://www.putnamcountyga.us/DocumentCenter/View/1230/260702-Sec-53---Technology-Uses-?bidId=)

The Putnam County Board of Commissioners enacted a moratorium on these uses on August 7, 2026. The moratorium will remain in effect through November 6, 2026.

A Town Hall Meeting regarding Data Centers, Battery Energy Storage Systems, and Solar Energy Systems has also been scheduled:

Thursday, August 27, 2026
6:00 p.m.
Room 203 – Putnam County Administration Building

Residents and interested parties are encouraged to attend the Town Hall Meeting and provide their comments and feedback regarding these proposed regulations.
