A RESOLUTION OF ROCKDALE COUNTY, AMENDING RESOLUTION NO. 2026-01
ON THE ACCEPTANCE, PROCESSING, AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATIONS
RELATING TO THE ESTABLISHMENT OR EXPANSION OF DATA CENTERS AND
BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS THROUGH OCTOBER 1, 2026;
PROVIDING FINDINGS OF FACT; STATING PURPOSE AND INTENT; PROVIDING
AN EFFECTIVE PERIOD; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners of Rockdale County have been vested with substantial
powers, rights, and functions to generally regulate the practice, conduct, or use of property for the
purposes of maintaining health, morals, safety, security, peace, and the general welfare of Rockdale
County; and

WHEREAS, data centers are energy- and water-intensive facilities that may impose significant
demands on developable tand, electricity generation, transmission. infrastructure, water supply,
wastewater capacity, and emergency services; and

WHEREAS, battery energy storage systems, including large-scale lithium-ion and similar
technologies, may present potential risks and impacts including fire and explosion hazards, thermal
runaway events, release of hazardous substances, noise, visual impacts, and increased demands on
emergency response capabilities; and

WHEREAS, the cumulative impacts of data centers have not been fully evaluated under Rockdale
County’s existing land-use regulations, comprehensive plan, and infrastructure planning
assumptions; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners of Rockdale County finds that additional time is
necessary to study and evaluate the cumulative impacts of data centers including but not limited
to energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, water usage, noise pollution, visual impacts,
heat discharge, public safety, resiliency, and compatibility with surrounding land uses; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners of Rockdale County further finds that a temporary
moratorium is a reasonable and necessary measure to prevent potentially incompatible or harmful
development while such studies and regulatory updates are undertaken; and

WHEREAS, this moratorium is intended to be temporary, narrowly tailored, and enacted in good
faith to allow for the development of clear, objective, and predictable development standards for
data center and battery energy storage system development;

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners adopted a moratorium on January 13, 2026, temporarily
suspending certain activities to ensure adequate time for review and revision of applicable codes,
regulations, and procedures; and.

WHEREAS, the Board of Commissioners, on May 12, 2026, extended this moratorium to
September 8, 2026 for further consideration of appropriate regulations for the Unified

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, and authorized by the Board of Commissioners of
Rockdale County, Georgia that Resolution 2026-01 be amended with the following language:

SECTION 1. AMENDMENT TO DURATION OF MORATORIUM

Resolution No. 2026-01, which currently provides that the moratorium shall remain in effect
until September 8, 2026, is hereby amended by deleting “September 8, 2026” and replacing it
with “October 1, 2026.”

SECTION 2, TEMPORARY MORATORIUM

1. A temporary moratorium is hereby imposed on the acceptance, processing, approval, or
issuance of any application, permit, entitlement, rezoning, special exception, conditional
use, site plan, building permit, or other discretionary or ministerial approval for the siting,
construction, or expansion of new data centers and battery energy storage systems, whether
proposed as standalone facilities or as accessory or ancillary uses, within Rockdale County,
Georgia.

2. The moratorium shall not apply to:

a. Maintenance, repair, or interior improvements to existing, lawfully established data
centers and battery energy storage systems that do not increase building area,
operational capacity, energy demand, or water use; or

b. Applications that received final, non-appealable rezoning or special use permit
approval prior to the effective date of this Resolution to include Treaty Oak
approved case #FLUM 2025-07 with Ordinance #0-2026-05 and case #REZ-2025-
13 with Ordinance #O-2026-04 as adopted by the Rockdale County Board of
Commissioners on April 14, 2026.

3. Except as expressly amended herein, all terms, provisions, findings, scope, exemptions,
and procedures of Resolution No. 2026-01 shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVE DATE
This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.

SECTION 4, REPEAL OF CONFLICTING RESOLUTIONS

SECTION 5, INCORPORATION OF RECITALS

forth herein.

SECTION 1. FINDINGS OF FACT
The Board of Commissioners of Rockdale County hereby finds and determines as follows:

1, The County is authorized under the Home Rule provisions of the Georgia Constitution
and 0.C.G.A. §§ 36-1-20 et seq, and 36-66-1 et seq. to regulate land use, zoning, and
development in order to protect the public health, safety, and welfare.

2, Data centers are energy- and water-intensive land uses that may impose substantial
demands on developable land, electricity generation and transmission infrastructure,
water supply systeras, wastewater capacity, stormwater facilities, and emergency
services.

3. The cumulative environmental impacts of data centers may include increased water
consumption, wastewater discharges, stormwater runoff, noise, air emissions from
backup power generation, heat discharge, visual impacts, and effects on nearby
residential, agricultural, and environmontally sensitive areas. Analysis is necessary to
review these various impacts to the County’s existing facilities and infrastructure.

4, Battery onergy storage systems constitute a distinct and evolving land use with potential
public safety, environmental, and infrastructure impacts that are not expressly regulated
under the County’s current zoning ordinance,

5. Large-scale battery energy storage systems may increase fire risk, require specialized
emergency response planning and equipment, and pose uncertainties related to long-term
operation, decommissioning, and disposal of battery materials.

6. Rockdale County has experienced increased interest in the siting and development of data
centers and battery energy storage systems that were not anticipated when the County’s
current zoning regulations and future land use recommendations were adopted.

7. Additional time is necessary for the County te study appropriate siting, design, safety,
operational, and decommissioning standards for data centers and battery energy storage
systems, including systems proposed as standalone facilities or as accessory uses.

8 Absent interim action, applications for data centers and battery storage systems could be
approved under existing regulations that do not adequately address these impacts.

%, A temporary moratorium is a reasonable, necessary, and a good faith planning measure to
allow the County to complete these evaluations and consider regulatory updates.

SECTION 2. PURPOSE AND INTENT
The purpose and intent of this Resolution is to:

1. Temporarily suspend the intake, review, approval, permitting, and development of new
data centers and battery energy storage systems in order to protect public health, safety,
welfare, natural resources, and infrastructure;

2, Allow Rockdale County to prepare and adopt clear zoning and development standards
specific to data centers and. battery energy storage systems;

3. Facilitate a comprehensive evaluation of the environmental, infrastructure, and
community impacts of data centers and battery energy storage systems, including
cumulative impacts; and

4, Ensure that any future data center and battery energy storage system development is
orderly, compatible with surrounding land uses, and consistent with the County’s long-
term planning goals.

SECTION 3. DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Resolution, the following terms shall have the meaning set forth below:

Data Center: Any facility, or portion thereof, primarily used to house computer systems,
servers, networking equipment, or related infrastructure for the storage, processing, or
transmission of digital data, including associated cooling systems and backup power generation,
whether operated for private, commercial, or institutional purposes.

Battery Energy Storage System (BESS): One or more devices, systems, or facilities that store
electrical energy for later use through electrochemical batteries or similar technologies, including
but not Limited to battery modules, racks, enclosures, containers, buildings, inverters,
transformers, cooling systems, fire suppression systems, and associated control and monitoring
equipment, whether installed as a principal use or as an accessory or ancillary use.

SECTION 4, ‘TEMPORARY MORATORIUM

1. A temporary moratorium is hereby imposed on the acceptance, processing, approval, or
issuance of any application, permit, entitlement, rezoning, special exception, conditional
use, site plan, building permit, or other discretionary or ministerial approval for the siting,
construction, or expansion of new data centers and battery energy storage systems,
whether proposed as standalone facilities or as accessory or ancillary uses, within
Rockdale County, Georgia.

2, The moratorium shall not apply to:

STATE OF GEORGIA

a) Maintenance, repair, or interior improvements to existing, lawfully established
data centers and battery energy storage systems that do not increase building area,
operational capacity, energy demand, or water use; or

b) Applications that received final, non-appealable approval prior to the effective
date of this Resolution.

3. This moratorium shall take effect immediately upon adoption and shall remain in effect
for a period of one-hundred-eighty (180) days, unless earlier repealed or extended by
action of the Board of Commissioners of Rockdale County in accordance with applicable
law.

SECTION 5. ADMINISTRATION DURING MORATORIUM

During the moratorium period, Rockdale County shall undertake studies, analyses, and public
engagement necessary to evaluate the environmental and infrastructure impacts of data centers
and battery energy storage systems and to develop proposed amendments to the County’s zoning
ordinance, comprehensive plan, and related development regulations.

SECTION 6, SEVERABILITY

If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of
