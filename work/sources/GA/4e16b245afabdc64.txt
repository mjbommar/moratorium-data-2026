The Lamar County Board of Commissioners met on Thursday, August 13, 2026 at 4:00 pm for a
workshop. Board members present were: Chairman Ryran Traylor, Vice Chair Jason Lovett,
District 1 Commissioner Jarrod Fletcher, District 3 Commissioner Ashley Gilles, and District 4
Commissioner Truman Boyle. Staff present were Anita Buice, County Clerk. County
Administrator Sean Townsend joined the meeting through the Teams application.

Chairman Traylor called the meeting to order at 4:05 pm.

Commissioner Lovett motioned to approve the agenda, seconded by Commissioner Gilles.
Motion carried 4-0.

Fire Department Equipment

Mr. Townsend explained the additional equipment first considered and approved for the new
passenger trucks were not sized correctly. A new quote has been gathered with an increase of
$3459.40 over the formally approved amount. The Board agreed by general consensus to move
forward with the purchase.

AmeriPro

Mt. Townsend informed the board that the current contract with AmeriPro to provide emergency
ambulance services to Lamar County is set to expire at the end of the year. Initial discussions
with the company included a proposed 16% increase of funding in the first year, then a 3%
increase in the following 4 years. Mr. Townsend proposed to AmeriPro a 7.25% increase yearly
and they countered with a year 1 increase of 9%, then a yearly increase of 6.5%. No action was
taken at this time.

Draft Agenda

The Commissioner then reviewed the draft agenda for the regular meeting of August 18, 2026.
Included in the review were the following:

. Proclamation for the 12U girls softball state champions

. Animal Services will be the department highlight

. Department of Family and Children Services board appointment

. Six sets of minutes from July

. Three rezoning hearings and one Character Area Map amendment hearing

. Resolutions to adopt the 2026 millage rates

Public Comment

No one present wished to participate in public comments.

Roundtable

Commissioner Gilles announced the state has approved a 2026 Tax Relief Grant for all
homestead properties. Property owners will not need to take any action, it will be applied
automatically.

Mr. Townsend updated the board on the paving project for Chappell Mill, High Falls, and Rock
Springs Road, noting six bids have been received already.

Commissioner Lovett questioned if there is any way to upgrade the technology used, specifically
the sound quality, when someone remotes into meetings.

Adjournment

As there being no further business, Commissioner Gilles motioned to adjourn the meeting.
Motion was seconded by Commissioner Lovett and carried 4-0. Meeting adjourned at 4:34 pm.
