                      RESOLUTION NO. 2026-___
 A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF KINGSLAND,
      GEORGIA, TEMPORARILY SUSPENDING THE EFFECTIVENESS AND
   ENFORCEMENT OF ORDINANCE NO. 2026-03 RELATING TO DATA CENTER
DEVELOPMENT; DIRECTING STAFF TO TEMPORARILY SUSPEND ACCEPTANCE
OF DATA CENTER DEVELOPMENT APPLICATIONS; PROVIDING FOR A PERIOD
OF PUBLIC REVIEW AND INPUT; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; REPEALING
     CONFLICTING RESOLUTIONS; AND FOR OTHER LAWFUL PURPOSES.


WHEREAS, the City of Kingsland, Georgia (“City”), is a municipal corporation duly organized
and existing under the laws of the State of Georgia and vested with authority to adopt ordinances
and resolutions for the protection of the public health, safety, and welfare pursuant to Article IX,
Section II, Paragraph I of the Constitution of the State of Georgia and Title 36 of the Official
Code of Georgia Annotated; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor and Council of the City of Kingsland previously adopted Ordinance No.
2026-03 (the “Data Center Ordinance”) to establish zoning, land use, infrastructure, and
operational standards applicable to data center development within the City; and
WHEREAS, the Mayor and Council find that Ordinance No. 2026-03 addresses matters of
substantial public interest, including but not limited to land use compatibility, utility demand,
environmental stewardship, infrastructure capacity, and community character; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor and Council further find that additional public engagement and
stakeholder input regarding the provisions, impacts, and implementation of Ordinance No. 2026-
03 will assist the City in ensuring that the ordinance reflects the informed interests of the public
and the long-term policy objectives of the City; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor and Council desire to temporarily suspend the effectiveness and
enforcement of Ordinance No. 2026-03 for a limited period in order to allow for additional
public review, comment, and consideration of potential amendments; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor and Council further find it necessary and appropriate to temporarily
prohibit the acceptance and processing of applications directly related to data center development
during the suspension period in order to preserve the status quo pending further public review
and legislative consideration; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor and Council find that a temporary suspension of Ordinance No. 2026-03
for the purposes stated herein is in the best interests of the public health, safety, and welfare and
constitutes a lawful exercise of the City’s legislative and police powers under Georgia law.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Mayor and Council of the City of Kingsland,
Georgia, and it is hereby resolved by authority of the same, as follows:
SECTION 1. TEMPORARY SUSPENSION.

The effectiveness and enforcement of Ordinance No. 2026-03, known as the Kingsland Data
Center Ordinance, are hereby temporarily suspended for a period of ninety (90) days from the
effective date of this Resolution, unless sooner reinstated, amended, or extended by subsequent
action of the Mayor and Council.

During the suspension period, Ordinance No. 2026-03 shall have no force or effect for purposes
of application, enforcement, permitting, or administrative review.
SECTION 2. TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF DATA CENTER DEVELOPMENT
APPLICATIONS.
During the suspension period established by this Resolution, City staff are hereby directed not to
accept, process, review, schedule, or otherwise administratively act upon any site plan,
development application, permit application, special use request, variance request, infrastructure
request, or other land use or development submittal that is directly and solely associated with,
intended for, or related to the development or operation of a data center as contemplated under
Ordinance No. 2026-03.

This temporary administrative suspension shall apply only to applications and submittals directly
related to data center development and shall not apply to general rezoning requests, text
amendments, or other legislative land use applications not expressly submitted for the purpose of
data center development.

SECTION 3. PURPOSE OF SUSPENSION.

The purpose of this temporary suspension is to allow the City adequate time to:
   1. Conduct additional public outreach and receive public comment regarding Ordinance No.
      2026-03;

   2. Solicit input from residents, property owners, utilities, environmental stakeholders, and
      other interested parties;

   3. Evaluate whether revisions or amendments to Ordinance No. 2026-03 are necessary or
      appropriate; and

   4. Consider any recommended amendments through the City’s regular legislative and
      zoning procedures in accordance with applicable Georgia law.

SECTION 4. PUBLIC INPUT PROCESS.

During the suspension period, City staff are directed to coordinate and facilitate an appropriate
public input process, which may include public workshops, stakeholder meetings, advertised
public hearings, and solicitation of written comments, for the purpose of gathering input and
recommendations concerning Ordinance No. 2026-03.
City staff shall compile public comments and recommendations received during the suspension
period and present the same to the Mayor and Council for review and consideration prior to any
reinstatement, amendment, or replacement of Ordinance No. 2026-03.

SECTION 5. NO VESTED RIGHTS CREATED.

Nothing in this Resolution shall be construed to create any vested right, development
entitlement, permit approval, or expectation of approval under Ordinance No. 2026-03 during the
suspension period.
SECTION 6. SEVERABILITY.

Should any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or provision of this Resolution be
declared invalid or unconstitutional by a court of competent jurisdiction, such declaration shall
not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Resolution, which shall remain in full
force and effect.

SECTION 7. REPEAL OF CONFLICTING RESOLUTIONS.

All resolutions or parts of resolutions in conflict herewith are hereby repealed to the extent of
such conflict.

SECTION 8. EFFECTIVE DATE.
This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its adoption.

SO RESOLVED AND ADOPTED this ___ day of ____________, 2026.
