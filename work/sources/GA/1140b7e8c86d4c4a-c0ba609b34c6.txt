Putnam County Board of Commissioners is pleased to announce the appointment of Melissia Rusk as Human Resources Director.

Bringing over 20 years of progressive public administration, financial management, and human resources experience, Rusk will lead HR functions, personnel operations, policy administration, and employee programs.

Rusk has dedicated her career to local government service across Georgia. Prior to her appointment, she served with the Walton County Board of Commissioners in key personnel leadership roles, including Human Resources Director and Assistant Human Resources Director. During her tenure in Walton County, she directed comprehensive HR operations for an expanding workforce, managed personnel and departmental budgets, updated Civil Service rules and regulations, administered benefit and wellness programs, and maintained pay and classification systems. According to Walton County Chairman David Thompson, "It is my pleasure to recommend Melissia Rusk, who served as the Human Resources Director for the Walton County Board of Commissioners after I appointed her to the position in 2021. Melissia accepted this leadership role during a challenging transition following the abrupt resignation of the previous HR Director. She quickly established stability within the department and carried out her responsibilities with professionalism, integrity, and sound judgment. Throughout her tenure, Melissia demonstrated a strong commitment to serving both employees and the organization. She managed sensitive personnel matters with discretion, maintained high professional standards, and proved to be a dependable member of our leadership team. I recommend Melissia without reservation for any Human Resources leadership position. I am confident she will be a valuable asset to any organization and wish her continued success in her career." Walton County Sheriff Brooks stated, "I hold Ms. Rusk in the highest regard as an executive leader and public servant. She possesses the vision, resilience, and character to thrive in any senior leadership or executive Human Resources position. I recommend her with the utmost confidence."

A highly credentialed professional, Rusk earned several certifications through the University of Georgia’s Carl Vinson Institute of Government, including Certified County Official, Advanced Certification in Human Resource Management, Certified Human Resource Manager, Certificate of Local Government Management, and Level I Certified Local Government Finance Officer.

She is a graduate of Leadership Walton and Leadership Putnam. Her leadership and contributions to public personnel administration have been recognized state-wide, receiving the Georgia Local Government Personnel Association Professional Achievement Award, where she previously served on the Board of Directors.

Rusk has been an active member of the Society for Human Resource Management, Georgia Public Risk Management Association, Georgia Local Government Personnel Association, Georgia Records Association, Walton Chamber of Commerce and United Way.

A lifelong resident of Putnam County, Melissia brings valuable experience in Human Resources and local government, along with a strong commitment to serving her community and supporting the employees who serve Putnam County citizens.

Please join us in congratulating Melissia Rusk on her appointment as Putnam County Human Resources Director.
