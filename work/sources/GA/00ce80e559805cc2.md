# Meeting Agendas & Minutes

source: https://www.leecountyga.gov/government/meeting-agendas-minutes
fetched: 2026-09-23T15:26:09+00:00

The meeting agendas and minutes are available online for the following boards and committees. Select a year to view the agendas and minutes for the specific entity. All files can be viewed with Adobe Reader unless specified. [To download Adobe Reader click here.](http://get.adobe.com/reader/)

Meeting agendas and minutes from boards and committees not listed above can be requested by contacting the [County Clerk's office](https://www.leecountyga.gov/government/county-clerk).
