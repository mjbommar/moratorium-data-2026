UPDATE: The Putnam County Board of Commissioners, at their October 21, 2026 meeting, extended the moratorium until November 17, 2026.

By resolution of the Putnam County Board of Commissioners dated August 7, 2026, a moratorium has been enacted on the consideration of and action on any land development permit to allow the installation, construction, or establishment of data centers, battery energy storage systems, and solar energy systems within Putnam County, Georgia. The moratorium is for a period of 3 months, ending November 6, 2026.

Resolution Enacting Moratorium

Proposed Ordinance: Chapter 53-Energy Creation, Energy Storage, and Digital Infrastructure

Putnam County is seeking public comments regarding Data Centers, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), and Solar Energy Systems. The public is encouraged to review the proposed ordinances and submit comments for consideration by the Board of Commissioners using the form linked here: Public Comments about Data Centers, Battery Energy Storage Systems, and Solar Energy Systems
