VI.   GRANTING AUDIENCE TO THE PUBLIC

           1. Terri Keller, 156 Woodhaven Drive, Kingsland, stated she appreciated all the
              work of the elected officials. She then read a letter in opposition of data
              centers, citing discharge of water, excessive heat from the center and drainage
              issues.
           2. Tory Adams with the St. Marys Riverkeeper read a letter in support of the data
              center moratorium and urged council to adopt a six-month moratorium similar
              to Camden County.
           3. Ken Kessler, 174 Austin Ryan Drive, Kingsland, spoke in favor of data
              centers.
           4. Victoria Funston, 462 Thompson Rd, Walthourville, GA spoke in opposition
              of data centers. She cited protecting the 100 miles of Georgia coast and the
              marshlands.
           5. Emmanuel (no last name given), 1426 Middle School Road, Kingsland, spoke
              in opposition of data centers.
           6. Kat Montgomery with the One Hundred Miles organization, spoke in
              opposition of data centers, citing a strain on local resources and impacts along
              the coastline.
           7. Tracy Wilson, Brighton Lakes, Kingsland, asked if there was somewhere
              online that citizens could find the mayor and council members stand on data
              centers. Mayor Day responded that it was not ethical for mayor or council to
              share views on an item going before the Planning Board.
           8. Jack Bowen, Browntown Community, Kingsland, asked with the current
              drought conditions, where would the water come from for the data centers. He
              also asked if they would be charged the same water rates as current citizens
              and why are they not considering Florida.
