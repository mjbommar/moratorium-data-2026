As demand for digital infrastructure continues to grow, the new ordinance establishes predictable standards that encourage responsible investment while ensuring that new development aligns with the municipality's long-term planning goals.

The ordinance includes standards addressing:

Appropriate zoning districts for data center development.
Minimum setbacks and buffering from residential neighborhoods and sensitive land uses.
Site design, landscaping, and architectural compatibility.
Noise, lighting, and visual impact mitigation.
Utility coordination and infrastructure capacity.
Emergency access and public safety requirements.
Environmental performance and stormwater management.
Ongoing operational standards and compliance monitoring.

The draft data center ordinance is being developed through a collaborative planning process that includes technical research and industry best practices. The resulting regulations provide certainty for property owners, developers, residents, and decision-makers while ensuring future projects are reviewed through a transparent and consistent process.

The ordinance ensures that facilities are located and designed to minimize impacts on surrounding properties and public services.
