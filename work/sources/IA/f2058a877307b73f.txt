May 5th, 2026
The meeting of the Board of Supervisors was called to order at 9:12 am by Chairman Kent Grabill. Present at the
meeting were Kent Grabill, Don Mosinski, Heath Hansen, Chassity Musfeldt, Jim Ricahrdson, David Weiderstein,
Gareth Stouffer, Miranda Bills, Shawn Deist, Steve Randeris, Mike Blum, Tyler Thygesen, Becky Marten, and Mitch
Rydl.
         Judge Jim Richardson and Attorney David Weiderstein presented a 250th Anniversary Flag from the
Audubon Bar Association to be flown at the Courthouse. Supervisors accepted the flag and took a picture with
Richardson and Weiderstein. Motion- Hansen Second- Mosinski to approve the agenda. Vote-all in favor. Public
Comment was opened at 9:13. Gareth Stouffer with APEX/JCG Land gave an update on the Wind Turbine project.
Chairman Grabill discussed the Ragbrai vendors that are wanting to set up outside of the Exira City limits. Meg
Andersen, Exira City Clerk, called in to discuss the process Exira is using to approve vendors. Andersen said they
would be willing to add a page to their Google sheets for the Ragbrai planning to include the rural portion for the
County. Motion- Mosinski Second- Hansen to approve using the same rates as Exira’s vendor fees and having Exira
City receive the vendor applications. Vote-all in favor. Chairman Grabill stressed the importance of having a map
laying out the vendors for EMA, Sheriff’s Department, and Secondary Roads.
         The meeting was moved into closed session pursuant to Iowa Code 21.5(1)(I) at 9:31 am. The closed
session ended at 10:03 am. Motion- Hansen Second- Mosinski to offer the Custodian/Maintenance position to
Shawn Deist. Vote-all in favor. The Public Hearing was opened at 10:03 am for the extension on the temporary
moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Systems and a temporary moratorium on Data Centers located within
Audubon County. Steve Randeris, Mike Blum, and EMA Director Thygesen discussed the issues/concerns of having
a Data Storage Center or Battery Energy Storage System within the County. Mike Blum discussed a data center
located within Manning, Iowa that is air cooled and is doing well within the community. Blum provided a contact
for Supervisors to reach out to regarding the center. The Public Hearings were closed at 10:35 am.
         Engineer Rydl gave Secondary Roads construction and maintenance updates. Motion- Mosinski Second-
Hansen to approve payment #1 for the Mockingbird HMA Paving Project. Vote-all in favor. Motion- Hansen
Second- Mosinski to approve Resolution 2026-24 to hire Chase St. Peter as a Grade 2 Classification, Equipment
Operator B as follows. Vote-all in favor.

                                                   Resolution 2026-25
    A resolution authorizing an extension to the temporary moratorium on utility-scale Battery Energy Storage
                                                     Systems (BESS)
WHEREAS, Iowa Code Section 331.302 requires a county to compile ordinances every 5 years, and the County
currently does not have a speciﬁc ordinance pertaining to utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS); and
Page 43
WHEREAS, the County has an interest in protecting residents’ life, health, and safety as well as safeguarding the
County’s infrastructure, natural resources and property rights through adequate zoning standards;
WHEREAS, the Audubon Board of Supervisors and the Planning & Zoning Commission will require additional time
to gather information and coordinate with diﬀerent agencies for the purpose of reviewing, updating or creating
ordinances, policies, and procedures relative to utility-scale BESS development;
WHEREAS, a public hearing on and reading of a resolution imposing a temporary extension moratorium on utility-
scale BESS was held at 10:00 a.m., on May 5th, 2026, in the Audubon County Board of Supervisors Meeting Room;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED; by the Board of Supervisors of Audubon County, Iowa, that Audubon County
now imposes a temporary moratorium on accepting or processing any applications for the construction of utility-
scale BESS, eﬀective immediately and until May 1, 2027; or until passage of a permanent Utility Scale BESS
Development ordinance.
ADOPTED in Audubon County, Iowa on Tuesday the 5th day of May, 2026.

                                                   Resolution 2026-27
                   A resolution authorizing a temporary moratorium on commercial Data Centers
WHEREAS, Iowa Code Section 331.302 requires a county to compile ordinances every 5 years, and the County
currently does not have a speciﬁc ordinance pertaining to commercial Data Centers; and
WHEREAS, the County has an interest in protecting residents’ life, health, and safety as well as safeguarding the
County’s infrastructure, natural resources and property rights through adequate zoning standards;
WHEREAS, commercial Data Centers is deﬁned as a facility consisting of one or more buildings (whether
constructed simultaneously or in separate phases) used primarily for the storage, management, processing, and
transmission of digital data, which houses computer or network equipment, systems, servers, appliances, and
other associated components related to digital data storage and operations. Data Storage Center included
commercial cryptocurrency mining operations and processing.
 WHEREAS, the Audubon Board of Supervisors and the Planning & Zoning Commission will require additional time
to gather information and coordinate with diﬀerent agencies for the purpose of reviewing, updating or creating
ordinances, policies, and procedures relative to commercial Data Center development;
WHEREAS, a public hearing on and reading of a resolution imposing a temporary moratorium on commercial Data
Centers was held at 10:15 a.m., on May 5th, 2026, in the Audubon County Board of Supervisors Meeting Room;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED; by the Board of Supervisors of Audubon County, Iowa, that Audubon County
now imposes a temporary moratorium on accepting or processing any applications for the construction of
commercial Data Centers eﬀective immediately and until May 1, 2027; or until passage of a permanent Data Center
Development ordinance.
ADOPTED in Audubon County, Iowa on Tuesday the 5th day of May, 2026.
