                                                  RESOLUTION NO. 7-8-2026
             A RESOLUTION OF THE PALO ALTO COUNTY SUPERVISORS APPROVING A
              MORATORIUM ON THE ACCEPTANCE, PROCESSING, AND APPROVAL OF
                  APPLICATIONS FOR ALL CLASSES OF DATA CENTERS WITHIN THE
                           UNINCORPORATED AREAS OF PALO ALTO COUNTY

         WHEREAS, Iowa Code Chapters 331 and 335 vests the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors (“Board”) with the
authority to protect the public health, safety and general welfare of county residents, and to regulate land uses within the
unincorporated areas of Palo Alto County; and,

         WHEREAS, data centers have begun to emerge nationwide as large-scale industrial facilities that vary significantly
in size operational intensity, environmental impact, water and energy demand, noise generation, and compatibility with
surrounding land uses; and,

          WHEREAS, such facilities may involve substantial levels of water use, electrical consumption, continuous
mechanical noise generation, large-scale cooling infrastructure, accessory substations, emergency backup power systems, and
other related components that may pose unique and significant land-use impacts on surrounding properties, natural resources,
public services and rural quality of life; and,

         WHEREAS, the Board finds that Palo Alto County’s current zoning ordinances do not specifically address the
general scope of modern data centers; and,

         WHEREAS, the Board finds that meaningful evaluation of these issues requires coordination among affected
municipalities, utility providers, regional planning organizations, state agencies, emergency management agencies, and other
public and private stakeholders before data center applications are accepted for review; and,

        WHEREAS, the Board believes that before any such facilities are constructed in Palo Alto County, ordinances
should be put in place to ensure that the Board is aware of these projects in Palo Alto County; and,

         WHEREAS, the Board wishes to engage in a thorough review of its zoning ordinance, including but not limited to
definitions, siting requirements, use classifications, performance standards, environmental protections, and public
infrastructure and environmental considerations related to data centers; and,

         WHEREAS, under the direction of the Board, the Planning and Zoning Commission will require time to gather
information and coordinate with multiple agencies for the purpose of reviewing, updating, or creating ordinances, policies,
procedures relative to data centers; and,

         WHEREAS, the Board finds that the acceptance or approval of new applications for data centers, until due diligence
regarding these zoning considerations can take place, would undermine the purpose of such review, could expose Palo Alto
County to incompatible land uses, and could jeopardize the County’s ability to develop consistent and protective zoning
standards; and,

         WHEREAS, a temporary moratoria of reasonable duration are a recognized and lawful planning tool used by
counties throughout Iowa to prevent development from occurring under outdated regulations while comprehensive studies
and ordinance updates are being completed; and,

          WHEREAS, the Board finds that establishing a temporary moratorium on data center applications is necessary and
reasonable to protect public health, safety, and welfare while the county studies the issue and develops appropriate
regulations. The Board intends that this moratorium be temporary in nature, limited in duration, and used to allow sufficient
time to evaluate existing regulations, obtain additional technical information, engage with stakeholders, prepare technical
studies, develop or update planning documents as appropriate, and determine whether amendments to the Palo Alto County
Comprehensive Plan or other county policies are appropriate; and,

         NOW THEREFORE, the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors, following review, consideration, and discussion,
RESOLVES to establish and impose a temporary moratorium on the acceptance, processing, consideration and approval of
any zoning, subdivision, site plan, conditional use, building permit, rezoning, or other development applications for any type
or class of data center within the unincorporated areas of Palo Alto County, effective immediately and until to July 8, 2027,
or upon the passage of an ordinance for the regulation of data centers, whichever is sooner.

         BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium may be extended as permitted by law by the Board for further
action if the Board determines that, upon the conclusion of the moratorium period, substantial deficiencies exist in the
comprehensive review and additional time is necessary to complete the required work for public protections such as:
technical studies; collecting and evaluating relevant data; resolving ordinances issues; coordinating with municipalities,
neighboring jurisdictions, utility providers, regional planning organizations, emergency management agencies, state and
federal agencies, and other affected stakeholders; updating the Palo Alto County Comprehensive Plan and related planning
documents; and obtaining public input due to lack of public review.

          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that no data center facilities, or appurtenance’s thereto, shall be constructed or
installed in the unincorporated areas of Palo Alto County. This shall include the renovation of any existing structures to be
used as said facility.

          BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that, for the purposes of this Resolution, the term “data center” shall include any
facility used for the centralized storage, management, processing, or transmission of digital data; and any facility whose
principal use involves high density servers, information technology equipment, specialized mechanical cooling systems, or
related digital infrastructure, such as energy storage systems. This definition is intended to be interpreted broadly and
includes enterprise data centers, colocation data centers, hyperscale data centers, server farms, cloud computing facilities, and
similar operations.

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the following direction to Palo Alto County Staff, including the Zoning and
Planning Commission:

         a) Conduct a comprehensive review of zoning provisions related to data centers utilized by other jurisdictions.
         b) Conduct a comprehensive review of issues related to data centers currently constructed and in use.
         c) Propose updated definitions, standards and regulatory frameworks designed to address modern data center land-
            use impacts.
         d) Present draft recommendations to the Board for further review and consideration.
         e) Consult with the County Attorney’s Office regarding the legal and regulatory implications of the proposed
            recommendations.

If any section, provision, or part of this resolution shall be adjudged invalid or unconstitutional, such adjudication shall not
affect the validity of the resolution as a whole or any section, provision, or part thereof not adjudge invalid or
unconstitutional.
