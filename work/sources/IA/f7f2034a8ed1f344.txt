                           RESOLUTION NO. 5-13-2025
        A RESOLUTION APPROVING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON
        UTILITY SCALE BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS

        WHEREAS, Iowa Code Chapter 335 granted counties the ability to adopt a Zoning Ordinance to
regulate the land use within its jurisdictional boundaries; and,

       WHEREAS, from time to time, it is necessary and prudent to review and study said ordinances
for necessary updates to ensure proper regulation; and,

       WHEREAS, the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors is aware of persons and/or companies
may desire to construct Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems in the unincorporated areas of
Palo Alto County, Iowa; and,

         WHEREAS, the Palo Alto Board of Supervisors believes that before any such facilities are
constructed in Palo Alto County, ordinances should be put in place to ensure that the Board and
Assessor are aware of these projects in Palo Alto County and that the construction, operation, and
deconstruction of these facilities would be subject to reasonable regulations that would ensure that said
facilities are constructed and operated in a safe manner, the right of persons or property within
reasonable distance of said facilities area appropriately protected, and the conditions of County roads are
protected; and,
       WHEREAS, Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems means a large-scale electrochemical
device that can store and discharge electricity from the grid or a power plant. Different battery
technologies, such as lithium-ion, sodium sulfur and lead acid, can be used for this purpose; and,

       WHEREAS, the County has an interest in protecting the County’s infrastructure, natural
resources and property rights through adequate setback provisions. The Planning and Zoning
Commission will require time to gather information and coordinate with multiple agencies for the
purpose of reviewing, updating, or creating ordinances, policies, procedures relative to Utility Scale
Battery Energy Storage Systems development; and,

        NOW THEREFORE, the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors, following review,
consideration, and discussion, RESOLVES to establish and impose a temporary moratorium on the
development and construction of Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems, and on the application
for permits relating to such development and construction of Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage
Systems effective immediately and until to April 1, 2026; or upon passing of an ordinance in relation to
Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems development, whichever comes first, for the purpose of
drafting and adopting a necessary and proper Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems Ordinance.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that no Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems facilities
or appurtenances thereto shall be constructed or installed in the unincorporated areas of Palo Alto
County. This shall include the renovation of any existing structures to be used as said facility.

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium approved by this Resolution shall become
effective immediately upon adoption of this Resolution and that upon April 1, 2026, if a Utility Scale
Battery Energy Storage Systems Ordinance has not been passed the moratorium could be extended by
resolution of the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors if deemed necessary.

If any section, provision, or part of this resolution shall be adjudged invalid or unconstitutional, such
adjudication shall not affect the validity of the resolution as a whole or any section, provision, or part
thereof not adjudge invalid or unconstitutional.

Ron Graettinger moved to introduce and approve Resolution No. 5-13-2025, and seconded by Linus
Solberg a Resolution approving a temporary moratorium on utility scale battery energy storage systems
on May 13,2025.
Ayes: Merrill, Graettinger, Wirtz, Solberg, Faulstick.
Nays: None.
Resolution Passed.
