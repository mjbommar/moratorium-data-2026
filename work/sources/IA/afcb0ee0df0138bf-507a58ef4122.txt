RESOLUTION # 2026 - 77 INTERFUND TRANSFER FROM GENERAL BASIC/RURAL BASIC TO SECONDARY ROADS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2025/2026


Whereas, it is desired to authorize the County Auditor to transfer sums from the General


Basic/ Rural Basic Fund to the Secondary Roads Fund during the 2025/2026 budget year,

and

Whereas, said transfers must be in accordance with Chapter 331.432 of the Code of Iowa. Now, Therefore, Be It Resolved by the Lee County Board of Supervisors as follows:


Section 1. The total amount to be transferred for fiscal year ending June 30, 2026 is

$3,010,993.

Section 2. Within seven working days of being notified of the current amount in said Fund,

the County Auditor shall order a transfer from said funds.

Section 3. Notwithstanding the provision of Section 2, of this Resolution, total transfers

shall not exceed the amount in Section 1.

Section 4. Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 2, the amount of any transfer shall

not exceed available fund balances in the transferring of funds.

Section 5. At the discretion of the County Auditor, fund transfers can be allocated

periodically during the fiscal year depending on fund balances.

The County Auditor has determined the amount to transfer on June 16, 2026 is as follows:

General Basic Fund to Secondary Roads Fund $ 73,544.75 Rural Basic Fund to Secondary Roads Fund $679,203.50


Section 6. The County Auditor is directed to correct her books when said operating

transfers are made and to notify the Treasurer of the amounts transferred.

RESOLUTION #2026-76 MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS


NOW on this 16th day of June 2026, the matter of Moratorium for Data Center Construction

comes to the attention of the Lee County Board of Supervisors.

WHEREAS the Lee County Board of Supervisors intends to begin research and

consideration of the need for a clear, comprehensive, and predictable regulatory

framework for the siting and operation of Data Centers, (both “Large” and “Small” Scale)

and other High-Density Facilities within Lee County and

WHEREAS, such facilities are characterized by substantial long-term demands on public

infrastructure, including electrical power, water supply, transportation systems, and

emergency services, as well as potential impacts on surrounding land uses and

environmental resources and

WHEREAS, the Lee County Board of Supervisors intends to ensure that such facilities are

evaluated, located, and developed in a manner that their infrastructure and operational

impacts are appropriately reviewed and mitigated; and that the County can fulfill its long

term fiscal, infrastructure, and community responsibilities associated with hosting

generational infrastructure facilities,

BE AND IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that for the above reasons, we have decided to adopt this

moratorium on the construction of Data Centers, (both “Large and “Small” Scale) and other

High Density Facilities within Lee County, for a period of up to one Year while we research

and consider the potential need for a clear, comprehensive, and predictable regulatory

framework for the siting and operation of such facilities.
