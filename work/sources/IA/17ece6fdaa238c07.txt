                                    RESOLUTION #1063-09-10-2024


   Resolution Approving a Moratorium on Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems


WHEREAS, Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems means a large scale electrochemical device
that can store and discharge electricity from the grid or a power plant. Different battery technologies, such
as lithium-ion, sodium sulfur and lead acid, can be used for this purpose; and

WHEREAS, concerns have been raised by public officials regardig the County’s level of preparedness
when it comes to the development of Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems within the County due
                                                                                           ‘
to not having a current Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems ordinance; and     .




WHEREAS, the County has an interest in protecting the County’s infrastructure, natural resources, and
property rights through adequate setback provisions. The Planning and Zoning Commission will require
time to gather information and coordinate with multiple agencies for the purpose of reviewing, updating,
or creating ordinances,         and procedure relative to Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems
                        policies,
development.

THEREFORE, the Jackson County Board of Supervisors wishes to extend the moratorium on the
development and construction of Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems and on issuing application
for permits relating to such development and construction of Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage
Systems, that was enacted in Resolution #1004-01-02-2024 allowing the Jackson County Zoning
Commission additional time to develop and reviewa suitable Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage
Systems Ordinance to present to the Jackson County Board of Supervisors for adoption.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Supervisors of Jackson County, Iowa, that a
moratorium on the development and construction of Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems, and
the application for permits relating to such development and construction of Utility Scale Battery Energy
Storage Systems is extended until June 30, 2025 at which time the moratorium could be extended by
resolution if deemed necessary by the Jackson County Board of Supervisors; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Jackson County Board of Supervisors reserves the right to end
the moratorium prior to the approved term of the moratorium upon completion of a satisfactorily
developed Utility Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems Ordinance; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium approved by this Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon adoption of this Resolution.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED BY THE JACKSON COUNTY BOARD OF             ON
                                                    SUPERVISORS
THIS 10 DAY OF SEPTEMBER, 2024.
