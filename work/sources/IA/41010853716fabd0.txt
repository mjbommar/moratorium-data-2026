                                  RESOLUTION NO. 2026 –          –

                 A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM
             ON THE ACCEPTANCE OF NEW APPLICATIONS TO REZONE PROPERTY
                     TO THE EU-3 LARGE-SCALE DATA CENTER DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, the Linn County Board of Supervisors ("Board") has adopted zoning regulations
governing Large-Scale Data Centers within Linn County, including the EU-3 Large-Scale Data
Center District; and

        WHEREAS, since adoption of those regulations, additional information has become
available regarding the potential cumulative impacts of Large-Scale Data Center development on
public infrastructure, water resources, electrical infrastructure, emergency response capabilities,
transportation systems, and long-range land use planning; and

        WHEREAS, the Board finds that Large-Scale Data Centers constitute regional-scale
infrastructure projects whose long-term impacts on electrical generation and transmission, water
resources, transportation systems, emergency services, economic development, and land use
frequently extend beyond the boundaries of a single jurisdiction; and

        WHEREAS, the Board further finds that meaningful evaluation of these issues requires
coordination among affected municipalities, utility providers, regional planning organizations,
state agencies, emergency management agencies, and other public and private stakeholders
before additional Large-Scale Data Center applications are accepted for review; and

       WHEREAS, the Board finds that because the EU-3 Large-Scale Data Center District was
adopted before substantial additional technical information and regional planning discussions
became available, it is appropriate to evaluate whether the County's recently adopted regulations
adequately address emerging issues associated with Large-Scale Data Center development and
whether amendments to the Unified Development Ordinance or related planning documents are
warranted before accepting additional applications for Large-Scale Data Center development; and

        WHEREAS, the Board further finds that preserving the status quo while this planning effort
is completed will promote orderly growth, protect the public health, safety, and welfare, and
ensure that future development occurs under regulations that fully reflect the County's planning
objectives; and

        WHEREAS, the Board recognizes the important role that advanced energy infrastructure,
including existing and future nuclear energy generating facilities, may play in supporting
economic development and the attraction of energy-intensive industries. The Board further finds
that nothing in this Resolution is intended to preclude the Board from considering future
amendments to the Linn County Unified Development Ordinance that may authorize data centers
as accessory and/or supportive uses to nuclear energy generating facilities following appropriate
planning, technical review, public participation, and legislative action; and


       WHEREAS, the Board intends that this moratorium be temporary in nature, limited in
duration, and used solely to allow sufficient time to evaluate existing regulations, obtain additional
technical information, engage with stakeholders, prepare technical studies, develop or update

planning documents as appropriate, and determine whether amendments to the Linn County
Unified Development Ordinance, Comprehensive Plan, or other County policies are appropriate;
and

       WHEREAS, Iowa Code Chapters 331 and 335 authorize counties to adopt and administer
zoning regulations, protect the public health, safety, and welfare, and amend zoning regulations
when appropriate;

   BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED by the Linn County Board of Supervisors that a moratorium is
hereby established on the acceptance for filing and processing of new applications to rezone
property to the EU-3 Large-Scale Data Center District.

   BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium shall become effective immediately upon
adoption by the Board.

    BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium shall remain in effect until 11:59 p.m. Central
Time on January 1, 2028, and thereafter may be extended by further action of the Board if the
Board determines that additional time is necessary to complete technical studies; collect and
evaluate relevant data; coordinate with municipalities, neighboring jurisdictions, utility providers,
regional planning organizations, emergency management agencies, state and federal agencies,
and other affected stakeholders; evaluate opportunities for regional planning and
intergovernmental collaboration; consider updates to the Linn County Comprehensive Plan and
related planning documents; obtain public input; and develop any amendments to the Unified
Development Ordinance the Board determines to be appropriate, including evaluating potential
amendments to the EU-2 zoning district regarding data center uses accessory to nuclear energy
generating facilities; and


    BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Board reserves the right to terminate the moratorium
prior to its expiration upon adoption of amendments to the Linn County Unified Development
Ordinance concerning Large-Scale Data Centers, or upon determination by the Board that such
amendments are no longer necessary.
