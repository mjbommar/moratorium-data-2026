      The Board of Supervisors met in adjourned session on June 30, 2026, at 9:00 a.m.
Members present Devlun Whiteing, Creston Schubert and Kyle Rohlk. Schubert called the
meeting to order. A motion to approve minutes from June 23, 2026, by Schubert, seconded
by Rohlk. Motion carried all voting Aye.

changes to the EMS Levy from Department of Management and concerns the funding will not
be provided as what had been voted on by our citizens.

Whiteing, seconded by Schubert. Motion carried all voting Aye.

carried all voting Aye.

Grell Farm.
