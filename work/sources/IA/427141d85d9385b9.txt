RESOLUTION 2026-07-04
Designation of Official Location of Public Meeting Notices

WHEREAS, Iowa Code Chapter 21 requires governmental bodies to provide notice of the time,
date, and place of each meeting and its tentative agenda; and

WHEREAS, House File 2490, effective July 1, 2026, requires governmental bodies to designate
an official location for posting notices and agendas that is visible and accessible to the public 24
hours per day; and

WHEREAS, the Cedar County Board of Supervisors finds it necessary to designate an official
location for the posting of public meeting notices and agendas in compliance with lowa law;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Cedar County Board of Supervisors as
follows:

1. The official physical location for posting notices and agendas of meetings of the Cedar
County Board of Supervisors shall be the display case located on the light pole at the
northeast entrance of the Cedar County Courthouse, 400 Cedar Street, Tipton, lowa
52772. This location is designated as the County's official public posting location and is
visible and accessible to the public twenty-four (24) hours per day.

2. Notices and agendas shall also be posted on the County's official website in aocordance
with Iowa Code Chapter 21.

3. Any amended agenda shall be clearly identified as "AMENDED." The amended agenda
shall identify the specific item or items that have been added, removed, or modified, in
accordance with lowa Code Chapter 21.

4. Ifthe designated physical posting location is temporarily inaccessible due to emergency
conditions, maintenance, or circumstances beyond the County's control, the County shall
restore posting at the designated location as soon as reasonably practicable while
continuing to comply with all applicable provisions of lowa Code Chapter 21.


5. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its adoption and shall
remain in full force and effect until amended or repealed by subsequent resolution of the
Board.
ADOPTED this 14" day of July, 2026.
