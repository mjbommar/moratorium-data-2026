                                                 RESOLUTION NO. 2-10-2026

           A RESOLUTION APPROVING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON BATTERY ENERGY
           STORAGE SYSTEMS

        WHEREAS, Iowa Code Chapter 335 granted counties the ability to adopt a Zoning Ordinance to regulate
the land use within its jurisdictional boundaries; and,

       WHEREAS, from time to time, it is necessary and prudent to review and study said ordinances for
necessary updates to ensure proper regulation; and,

         WHEREAS, the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors is aware of persons and/or companies who may
desire to construct Battery Energy Storage Systems in the unincorporated areas of Palo Alto County, Iowa; and,

        WHEREAS, the Palo Alto Board of Supervisors believes that before any such facilities are constructed in
Palo Alto County, ordinances should be put in place to ensure that the Board and Assessor are aware of these
projects in Palo Alto County and that the construction, operation, and deconstruction of these facilities would be
subject to reasonable regulations that would ensure that said facilities are constructed and operated in a safe
manner, the right of persons or property within reasonable distance of said facilities area appropriately protected,
and the conditions of County roads are protected; and,

        WHEREAS, Battery Energy Storage Systems store electricity to be sold to wholesale or retail markets. In
addition, Battery Energy Storage Systems facilities shall be to promote public health, safety, comfort and general
welfare, while facilitating economic opportunities for rural residents; and,

        WHEREAS, the County has an interest in protecting the County’s infrastructure, natural resources and
property rights through adequate setback provisions. The Planning and Zoning Commission will require time to
gather information and coordinate with multiple agencies for the purpose of reviewing, updating, or creating
ordinances, policies, procedures related to Battery Energy Storage System development; and,

        NOW THEREFORE, the Palo Alto County Board of Supervisors, following review, consideration, and
discussion, RESOLVES to establish and impose a temporary moratorium on the development and construction of
Battery Energy Storage Systems, and on the application for permits relating to such development and
construction of Battery Energy Storage Systems effective immediately and until to February 1, 2027; or upon
passing of an ordinance in relation to Battery Energy Storage Systems development, whichever comes first, for
the purpose of drafting and adopting a necessary and proper Battery Energy Storage Systems Ordinance.

         BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that no Battery Energy Storage Systems facilities or appurtenances thereto
shall be constructed or installed in the unincorporated areas of Palo Alto County. This shall include the renovation
of any existing structures to be used as said facility.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium approved by this Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon adoption of this Resolution and that upon February 1, 2027, if a Battery Energy Storage
Systems Ordinance has not been passed, the moratorium could be extended by resolution of the Palo Alto
County Board of Supervisors if deemed necessary.

If any section, provision, or part of this resolution shall be adjudged invalid or unconstitutional, such adjudication
shall not affect the validity of the resolution as a whole or any section, provision, or part thereof not adjudge
invalid or unconstitutional.
