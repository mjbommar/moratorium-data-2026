Overview

This webpage provides information on data center regulations and applications in unincorporated Johnson County, Iowa.

Johnson County's Unified Development Ordinance, which applies only to the unincorporated area, currently allows data centers only in industrial zoning districts but does not include any other specific data center regulations.

A moratorium on data centers in unincorporated Johnson County is in effect through Aug. 8, 2027. This means data center applications cannot be accepted by the County for review. The moratorium allows Johnson County to develop more detailed regulations and processes for any future data center development.

There are currently no applications to build a data center in unincorporated Johnson County.

How to Stay Informed

This webpage will be updated as information becomes available.

Upcoming public meetings of the Johnson County Board of Supervisors or Planning (BOS) and Zoning Commission (PZC) that include data center-related agenda discussion items will be listed under "Upcoming Activities." However, data centers may come up in general discussion on other regular agenda items such as public comment and general Supervisor reports; Iowa law prohibits both the BOS and PZC from taking formal action on a topic without the item first appearing in a published agenda. All public meeting agendas for Johnson County Boards and Commissions are posted at this Granicus site.

FAQs

Are there any existing data centers in Johnson County?

There are no records of existing permitted data centers in unincorporated Johnson County.

Can the County just ban data centers?

The legality of an outright ban is still being studied by the CAO. The CAO is tentatively scheduled to update the BOS on this topic in early September in a closed session, meaning not open to the public.

Can the County extend the moratorium on new data center applications forever?

No. The courts have indicated that moratoria must be limited in length and intended for a specific government interest or purpose (e.g. to research and update regulations).
