Moved by Sup. Kaufmann seconded by Sup. Barnhart to approve the updated structure of the
EMS Advisory Council to consist of five members appointed by the Board: one at-large member,
one Board of Supervisors member, one EMA/911 representative, one Public Health
representative, and one representative from local EMS services. Terms were set at four years,
with the at-large and Board of Supervisors positions serving an initial two-year term to allow for
staggered terms thereafter. The local EMS services will collectively provide a recommendation
for their representative. Sup. Barnhart was appointed to serve as the Board representative with
Sup. Bixler as alternate.

Ayes: All

Blank and effective upon signature.
