Franklin County Ordinance No 2026A

ORDINANCE 2026A – “A MORATORIUM ON NEW PERMIT APPLICATIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT OF DATA CENTER PROJECTS WITHIN THE UNINCORPORATED AREA OF FRANKLIN COUNTY, IOWA”


Be it ordained by the Board of Supervisors of the County of Franklin County, Iowa:

WHEREAS, the County currently does not have a specific ordinance regarding the development of data centers within the

unincorporated area of Franklin County; and

WHEREAS, the County has adopted a Comprehensive Plan for the future use of land within the unincorporated

areas of the County; and

WHEREAS, the County has an interest in regulating the use of land through its zoning regulations consistent with

the County’s Comprehensive Plan;

WHEREAS, the Board of Supervisors will require time to gather information regarding the impact of data center

construction in the unincorporated area of the County, and to coordinate with multiple agencies for the purpose of

reviewing, updating or creating ordinances, policies and procedures relative to data center development;

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Supervisors of Franklin County, Iowa, that Franklin County

now imposes a moratorium, effective immediately for eighteen (18) months; or upon the passing of an ordinance in

relation to data center development (whichever comes first), on all rezonings, conditional use permit applications and

building permits associated with construction of a data center project, for the purpose of drafting and adopting any

necessary and proper zoning ordinances regulating the construction of data centers.
