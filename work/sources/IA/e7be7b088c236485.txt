                                                                           Ida Grove, Iowa
                                                                             July 14, 2026
      The Board of Supervisors met in adjourned session on July 14, 2026, at 9:00 a.m.
Members present Devlun Whiteing, Creston Schubert and Kyle Rohlk. Whiteing called the
meeting to order. A motion to approve minutes from June 30, 2026, and July 2, 2026, by
Schubert, seconded by Rohlk. Motion carried all voting Aye.
      There were no public comments to the board.
      EMS Director Corey Trucke talked to the board about the Department of Management
and the response to the EMS property tax rate.
      County Engineer Colin Ryan met with the board to discuss road updates and possible
dust control ordinance. A motion to approve Underground Utility Permit for West Central
Iowa Rural Water by Rohlk, seconded by Schubert. Motion carried all voting Aye.
      Northwest REC representatives Lyle Korver and Dale Ullrich, met with the board to
discuss possible Data Centers for Ida County.
      A motion to approve quarterly reports for Auditor, Sheriff and Recorder by Rohlk,
seconded by Schubert. Motion carried all voting Aye.
      A motion to approve additional Nelson bids; $1,250 for rock in the SW corner and
$6,250 for Harley Rake/Hydroseed bare spots by Schubert, seconded by Rohlk. Motion
carried all voting Aye.
      A motion to approve Annual Treasurer’s Report by Schubert, seconded by Whiteing.
Motion carried all voting Aye.
      A motion to approve monthly reports for Treasurer and Clerk of Court by Whiteing,
seconded by Rohlk. Motion carried all voting Aye.
      A motion to approve new water meter by Whiteing, seconded by Schubert. Motion
carried all voting Aye.
      Acknowledgement of Manure Management Plans CBT Farms LLC, Heilman’s Hog Haven,
Marshall Production LLC and Kiron Finishing #64768.
      A motion to approve the following claims: Benefits Inc $68.24 & $295.68; Iowa
Department of Revenue $67.59 & $112.25; IPERS $20; US Treasury $295.68; 7-10-2026 Payroll
Expense Checks $42,793.09; 7-10-2026 Payroll $134,355.38; by Schubert, seconded by Rohlk.
Motion carried all voting Aye.
