TIPTON, IOWA June 4, 2026

The Cedar County Board of Supervisors met in special session at 8:00 a.m. June 4, 2026 with the
following members present: Bixler, Barnhart, Gaul, and Chairperson Bell. Kaufmann was
absent. Minutes reported by Deputy Auditor Hamdorf.

Moved by Sup. Barnhart seconded by Sup. Gaul to approve the agenda.
Ayes: All
Absent: Kaufmann

Chairperson Bell addressed the public for comments. There were none.

Chairperson Bell read a letter submitted by Auditor Welch asking the Board to approve an
administrative recount for the Cass/Center/Iowa/Rochester Precinct for the June 2, 2026 Primary
Election. The purpose of the administrative recount is to correct the discrepancy and ensure an
accurate accounting of all ballots cast in the precinct.

Moved by Sup. Barnhart seconded by Sup. Bixler to approve an administrative recount for the
Cass/Center/Iowa/Rochester Precinct for the June 2, 2026 Primary Election.

Ayes: All

Absent: Kaufmann

Moved by Sup. Barnhart seconded by Sup. Bixler to adjourn at 8:05 a.m., to June 9, 2026.

Ayes: All
Absent: Kaufmann

Nicki Welch, Auditor Jon Béll, Chairperson
