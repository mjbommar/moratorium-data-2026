Present: Supervisors: Nin Flagel, Mike Steines, Don Schwenker; Auditor Alisa Smith; IT Director Bjorn

Meeting brought to order by Chair Schwenker at 9:00 a.m.

Zoning Administrator Becca Pflughaupt updated the Board.

Motion Steines, second Flagel to approve Resolution #1205-08-04-2026 extending and updating the

temporary moratorium on high-density computing and data processing facilities in unincorporated

Jackson County, Iowa until August 4, 2027 as recorded in Resolution Book 5, pages 400 & 401 as

presented. Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

Motion Flagel, second Steines to Resolution #1206-08-04-2026 approving the final plat of Sieverding

Estates, a minor subdivision in section 12 of Bellevue Township as recorded in Resolution Book 5, page

402 as presented. Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

Environmental Health Director Elizabeth Townsend updated the Board.

Motion Steines, second Flagel to approve setting the date for a public hearing on August 25, 2026 at 9:00

a.m. for Ordinance #323 – Wastewater Treatment as presented. Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

Assistant to the Engineer Tom Hughes updated the Board.

Motion Flagel, second Steines to approve Chair signature on the FY27 Rural County Transportation Program

(RCTP) Agreement between Jackson County and the City of LaMotte for $15,412.00 as presented.

Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

Motion Steines, second Flagel to approve a right-of-f-way utility permit to Maquoketa Valley Electric

Cooperative (MVEC) in section 31 of Maquoketa Township on 211th Ave as presented.

Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

Auditor Alisa Smith presented county business.

Motion Flagel, second Steines to approve the minutes of the July 28, 2026 meeting as written by Auditor

Smith and authorize publication in the official newspapers as presented.

Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

with outdoor service to The Tabor Family Winery, LLC at 3570 67th St, Baldwin effective August 7,

2026 through August 6, 2027 as presented. Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel

Motion Steines, second Flagel to adjourn the meeting at 9:20 a.m. until the next board meeting on August 11,

2026 at 9:00 a.m. in the boardroom at the courthouse in Maquoketa. Aye: Schwenker, Steines, Flagel
