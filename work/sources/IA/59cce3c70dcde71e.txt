The Board received an email from Lisa Obrecht, DOT Condemnation Coordinator regarding
individuals who have requested to be removed from roster. The Board discussed citizens to ask
and is placing this on the agenda for next week.

David Furry met with the Board regarding County Wide EMS. Furry said he was representing
himself and wasn’t aware of an advisory council. Furry said the problem wasn’t vehicles or
buildings but more staffing was needed. Furry thought the EMS levy should have been spent on
staffing what we currently have with paramedics not buildings and other new things. Bell said
that they are doing what the citizens of Cedar County voted for. A study was done on what was
needed in Cedar County as well as 40 meetings were held to educate taxpayers. No decisions
were made.
