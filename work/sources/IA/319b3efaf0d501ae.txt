                      The Board led the Pledge of Allegiance.
                      Chairman Kretzinger called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. with Kretzinger, Bryant & Longhorn present.
                      Bryant moved Longhorn seconded motion to approve the agenda as posted. All ayes. Motion carried.
                      Chairman Kretzinger asked if there were any questions or items to be removed from the Consent Agenda;
            no request was made. Bryant moved, Longhorn seconded the motion to approve the following items on the
            CONSENT AGENDA: August 26, 2026 meeting minutes; accepting resignation from Luccas Hart, Seasonal
            Conservation effective August 22, 2026; accepting resignation from Julie Hinman, Auditor’s Office effective
            September 11, 2026; approval of Pay Adjustment for Tyler McCumber, Secondary Roads to $31.75 per hour
            effective September 9, 2026 for 2 Year Step; approval of Pay Adjustment for Kyle Keigley, Secondary Roads
            Promotion to Foreman at $36.16 per hour effective August 30, 2026; approval of Secondary Roads claims #30723
            to #30740 and other county claims #181815 to #181875 totaling $1,571,772.80 and issuance of warrants
            #104362 to #104429 in payment of the same; approval of drainage claims #1265 to #1266 totaling $11,411 and
            issuance of drainage warrants #1740 to #1758 in payment of the same. All ayes. Motion carried.
                      Discuss and consider action on public petition received. Bryant moved Kretzinger stepped down as
            Chairman to second motion to move forward with a resolution to establish a 1 year moratorium on data centers in
            order to create an ordinance concerning data centers. All ayes. Motion carried.
                      Boone & Story County Board of Supervisors Meeting as Trustees for Joint Drainage District Boone
            140/Story 7. Story Co moved Boone Co seconded motion to approve of Agenda. All ayes. Story Co moved Boone
            Co seconded motion to approve signing Application for Easement Through Joint Drainage District Boone 140/Story
            7 Right-of-Way to execute Resolution 26-29: To Grant Luther Investments LLC DBA Boxcar BBQ a Drainage District
            Crossing Easement. All ayes. Motion carried. Story Co moved Boone Co seconded motion to approve signing
            Agreement for additional clarification of future responsibility between Joint Drainage District Boone 140/ Story 7
            and Luther Investments, LLC. All ayes. Motion carried. Boone Co moved Story Co seconded motion to Adjourn. All
            ayes.
                      John Roosa, Landfill Administrator gave a Departmental Update. Longhorn moved Bryant seconded motion
            to approve of Change Order #3 of Leachate Forcemain Project for $12,020.09. All ayes. Motion carried.
                      Brenda Terrell & Darren Helton, CFR was present for the reading of Recovery Month Proclamation
                      Mike Salati, Planning & Development presented preliminary plat for Scenic Valley Acreage Subdivision,
            planning & zoning commission approved. Longhorn moved Bryant seconded motion to approve of Resolution 26-
            33: Accepting Preliminary Plat of Scenic Valley Acreage LLC at 1447 285th Ln, Madrid. All ayes. Motion carried. A
            full copy of the resolution can be obtained from the Auditor’s office.
                      Longhorn moved Bryant seconded motion to adjourn the meeting. All ayes. Motion carried. Chairman
            Kretzinger adjourned the meeting at 10:42 a.m.
                      Approved this 9th day of September, 2026.
