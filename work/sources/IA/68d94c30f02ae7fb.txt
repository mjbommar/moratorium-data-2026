RESOLUTION #1157-12-23-2025

RESOLUTION APPROVING AN EXTENSION OF THE MORATORIUM ON UTILITY-SCALE BATTERY
ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS

WHEREAS, Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are large-scale electrochemical devices
designed to store and discharge electricity from the electrical grid or power plants, utilizing technologies
including but not limited to lithium-ion, sodium sulfur, and lead-acid batteries; and

WHEREAS, concerns have been raised by public officials and residents regarding Jackson County’s level
of preparedness to regulate the development, siting, construction, operation, safety, and long-term
impacts of Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems, and the County does not currently have a fully
adopted ordinance governing such facilities; and

WHEREAS, Jackson County has a substantial interest in protecting public health, safety, and welfare,
including protection of infrastructure, natural resources, agricultural land, emergency response
capabilities, property values, and neighboring land uses through adequate siting and development
standards; and ,

WHEREAS, the Jackson County Planning and Zoning Commission requires additional time to research,

“evaluate, and coordinate with state agencies, emergency management, fire officials, planning

professionals, and legal counsel for the purpose of drafting, reviewing, and recommending
comprehensive regulations for Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems; and

WHEREAS, the Jackson County Board of Supervisors previously adopted a moratorium on the
development and construction of Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems and on the acceptance
and processing of applications related to such development, most recently extended through
December 31, 2025; and

WHEREAS, the Jackson County Board of Supervisors finds it necessary and in the public interest to
further extend the moratorium to allow sufficient time for completion, review, and potential adoption of
a Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage Systems ordinance.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Jackson County Board of Supervisors, Jackson County, lowa,
that the moratorium on the development and construction of Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage
Systems, and on the acceptance, processing, and approval of applications, permits, or approvals related
to such systems, is hereby extended through August 31, 2027; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Jackson County Board of Supervisors reserves the right to terminate
the moratorium prior to August 31, 2027, upon adoption of a Utility-Scale Battery Energy Storage
Systems ordinance or upon a determination that continuation of the moratorium is no longer necessary;
and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its adoption.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Jackson County Board of Supervisors on this 23rd day of December,
2025. ,
