RESOLUTION #8-2025 ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE ISSUANCE  OF REZONING REQUESTS AND/OR CONDITIONAL USE PERMITS FOR DIGITAL ASSET MINING/DATA CENTERS


WHEREAS, Digital asset mining/data centers have the potential to create an excessive burden on the

County’s infrastructure as well as nearby property owners; and

WHEREAS, Digital asset mining/data centers are subject to consideration and action by the Clayton County

Zoning Board of Adjustment; and

WHEREAS, the Clayton County Board of Supervisors recognizes that digital asset mining/data centers

may affect the character of the surrounding area and the Board desires that all applications for energy

storage solutions be considered consistently within the purpose and intent of the Clayton County Zoning

Ordinance so that the most appropriate uses of land are encouraged throughout unincorporated Clayton

County; and

WHEREAS, the Clayton County Board of Supervisors desires to explore the possibility of updating the

approval requirements for Digital asset mining/data centers to ensure the protection of the public’s health,

safety, and general welfare; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Clayton County Board of Supervisors hereby establishes

a temporary moratorium on the issuance of any rezoning requests, conditional use permits or other

approvals or authorizations for the construction or operation of digital asset mining/data centers, expiring

at 11:59 p.m. Central Time on Wednesday, December 31, 2025, unless extended by further action of the

Board of Supervisors, to allow the above referenced changes to the Clayton County Zoning Ordinance

concerning energy storage solutions to be duly considered by the Clayton County Planning and Zoning

Commission and the Clayton County Board of Supervisors; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Clayton County Board of Supervisors reserves the right to end the

moratorium prior to the approved term of the moratorium upon completion of the amendments to the Zoning

Ordinance and satisfactory resolution of the issues as determined by the Clayton County Board of

Supervisors; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the moratorium approved by this resolution shall become effective

immediately upon adoption of this resolution.
