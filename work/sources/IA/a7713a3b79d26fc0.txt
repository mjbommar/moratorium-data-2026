                                     RE:     Landus Cooperative requests Board of Supervisors approval to replace
                                             the existing 26,000 gallon anhydrous ammonia tank with a 90,000
                                             gallon anhydrous ammonia tank at the Landus Perry Agronomy Facility
                                             located at 2229 Clover Avenue, Perry, Iowa 50220. The current 26,000
                                             gallon tank sits on the East side of an existing 90,000 gallon anhydrous
                                             ammonia tank. The 26,000 gallon tank will be disconnected and
                                             removed, along with the piers that support it. In its place, new
                                             foundations will be poured to support the new 90,000 gallon tank.
                                             After the new tank is set, piping will be installed to connect this new
                                             tank to the existing one. The additional storage is being added to meet
                                             the demand of anhydrous ammonia in the Perry area.
