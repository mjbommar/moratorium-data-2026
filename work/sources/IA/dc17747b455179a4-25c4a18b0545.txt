Resolution 26- 151

Resolution Establishing a One-Year Moratorium on Small, Medium, Large-Scale, and Hyperscale

Data Center Development in Dubuque County, Iowa.

WHEREAS, Dubuque County has received increasing inquiries regarding the development of large

scale digital infrastructure facilities; and

WHEREAS, such facilities may require major utility, water, transportation, and emergency

response infrastructure; and

WHEREAS, the County's existing zoning ordinance does not address facilities of this type, scale or

intensity; and

WHEREAS, approving applications before comprehensive standards are adopted could create

incompatible land uses and lasting impacts on agriculture, infrastructure, water resources, and

taxpayers;

Purpose

The purpose of this Resolution is to protect public health, safety, welfare, agriculture, natural

resources, and County fiscal interests while the County develops comprehensive regulations for

Small, Medium, Large-Scale, and Hyperscale Data Center development. During the moratorium,

no application shall be accepted or processed if it could establish rights before enforceable

standards are adopted.

Data Center  means any building, campus, structure, or group of structures including, but not

limited to those primarily used to house servers, networking equipment, cloud computing, artificial

intelligence computing, cryptocurrency processing, colocation services, enterprise computing,

digital storage, or similar electronic data processing equipment, together with related utility,

cooling, water, backup power, storage, and accessory infrastructure.

Classification by IT Load, Facility Power, and Building Size:

Category IT Load Range Facility Power Range Building Size

Small Under 1 MW 1–3 MW Under 25,000 sq ft

Medium 1–10 MW 3–15 MW 25,000–100,000 sq ft

Large-Scale 10–50 MW 15–75 MW 100,000–500,000 sq ft

Hyperscale 50+ MW 75–500+ MW 500,000+ sq ft or multi-building

Scope

This moratorium applies to all new Small, Medium, Large-Scale, and Hyperscale Data Centers,

including, but not limited to supporting infrastructure, within the unincorporated areas of Dubuque

County. Covered activities include utility, water, wastewater, cooling, campus, phased, expansion,

speculative, annexation, development-agreement, and other improvements intended to support

the construction or operation of a Data Center. Exemptions are limited to routine information

technology infrastructure serving local businesses, government facilities, educational institutions,

healthcare providers, telecommunications facilities, and utility operations, provided such

infrastructure is not designed or expanded primarily to support a Data Center subject to this

moratorium.

Moratorium

This Resolution becomes effective immediately upon adoption and publication as required by Iowa

law.

The moratorium commences on May 26, 2026, and remains in effect through May 26, 2027, unless

earlier terminated or lawfully extended. During the moratorium, neither Dubuque County nor any

County department, board, commission, official, employee, or agent shall accept, process, review,

approve, issue, or otherwise authorize any discretionary approval for a covered Data Center project

or supporting infrastructure.

Required Deliverables Before Expiration or Termination

Before this moratorium may expire, be lifted, or be terminated early, the Dubuque County Board of

Supervisors shall ensure the minimum deliverables have been completed as set forth in the

Dubuque County Data Center Planning Process  also adopted by the Board on July 6, 2026.

Duration

Unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Resolution, the moratorium shall remain in full

force and effect through May 26, 2027.

Expiration of the one-year term shall not, by itself, authorize any application, permit, zoning

approval, development agreement, or other authorization for a Small, Medium, Large-Scale, or

Hyperscale Data Center or any supporting infrastructure.

Conditions for Termination

The moratorium may be terminated only upon the adoption of a Resolution by the Dubuque County

Board of Supervisors following a duly noticed public hearing and written findings that each of the

following conditions has been satisfied:

1. All studies, assessments, technical analyses, and planning activities required by this

Resolution have been completed.

2. A comprehensive Data Center Ordinance and all related regulations, development

standards, administrative and evaluation procedures, and enforcement provisions have

been formally adopted.

3. All public participation requirements have been completed, including required public

meetings, work sessions, public hearings, publication of draft ordinances, supporting

studies, and opportunities for public comment.

4. The Board finds, based upon substantial evidence in the record, that Dubuque County has

established clear, comprehensive, and enforceable standards that adequately protect:

o Prime agricultural land and agricultural operations;

o Groundwater and surface water resources;

o Public infrastructure and utility systems;

o Emergency response capabilities;

o Neighboring property owners;

o Public health, safety, and general welfare; and

o The long-term fiscal interests of Dubuque County taxpayers.

Dubuque County Board of Supervisors

Harley V. Pothoff, Chair
