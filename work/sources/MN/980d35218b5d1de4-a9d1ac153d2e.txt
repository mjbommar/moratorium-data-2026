Amending Title 21 of the Minneapolis Code of Ordinances by adding a new Chapter 597 to Interim

Ordinances: Providing for a moratorium on the establishment, re-establishment, or expansion of data

centers.

The City Council of  the City of Minneapolis  do ordain as  follows:

SeSection 1. That the Minneapolis Code of Ordinances be amended by adding thereto a new Chapter 597

to read as  follows:

CHAPTER 597. PROVIDING FOR A MORATORIUM ON THE ESTABLISHMENT,

RE-ESTABLISHMENT, OR EXPANSION OF DATA CENTERS

597.10. Authority. Pursuant to Minn. Statutes section 462.355, subd. 4, the city is authorized to

establish interim ordinances to regulate, restrict, or prohibit any use or development in all or a part of

the city while the city or its planning department is conducting studies, or has authorized a study to be

conducted, or has scheduled a hearing to consider adoption or amendment of  the comprehensive plan

or official zoning controls. In furtherance of  this statutory authority, the city has enacted Chapter 529,

Article VII, of  the zoning code, which governs the establishment of interim ordinances. The city declares

that  this interim ordinance is established pursuant to this statutory and local ordinance authority.

597.20. Findings and purpose. The city council is concerned about potentially unknown or adverse

impacts that the increased siting, development, re-development or expansion of data center facilities

might have on the city as a whole and the neighborhoods and zoning districts in which such uses are

located. The city's official controls do not currently define data centers as a separate use category

although the prevalence of such facilities is expanding on a national, regional, and local basis. The city,

through its planning department, has been studying potential amendments to its official controls in this

area, including its zoning code, and desires to enact this interim ordinance to allow for the continuation

and completion of this ststudy to inform regulation of future uses of this type. The city finds that this

interim ordinance should be adopted to protect the planning process and the health, safety and welfare

of  the citizens.

597.30. Study. (a) The department of community planning and economic development, in cooperation

with other applicable city departments, is hereby authorized to conduct a study to evaluate the impact

of  data centers on communities citywide and to propose such amendments to the city's official controls

and other regulatory devices that the department deems necessary and advisable in furtherance of the

city's policy objectives.

(b) The study area consists of  the entire city.

(c) The department of community planning and economic development shall return to the appropriate

committee of  the city council to present a progress report in no more than six (6) months' time.

597.40. Definition and restrictions. (a) For purposes of  this chapter, the term "data center" shall mean a

principal use, consisting of new or expanded space, where the primary function is to house computers

and/or related equipment  for  the purpose of  collection, processing, storage, or retrieval of  data.

(b) From the date of introduction of  this ordinance on May 21, 2026, through and including November

21, 2026, or upon completion of  the study and adoption or amendment of  the official controls affecting

the subject matter of  this interim ordinance, whichever occurs first, throughout the city of Minneapolis,

no data center facility shall be established, re-established, or expanded. No zoning approval, building

permit, construction permit, demolition permit, or administrative waiver for the establishment, re

establishment, or expansion of any data center facility shall be allowed or granted by any city

department.

(c) The restriction imposed by this section shall not apply to data centers consisting of less than three

hundred fifty thousand (350,000) square feet of new or expanded space located in the downtown area

bounded by Interstate 35W, Interstate 94, Plymouth Avenue, and the Mississippi River.

Consistent with section 525.830, these restrictions shall apply only to the issuance of permits for

development for which an application has not been filed prior to the effective date of this interim

ordinance.

597.50. Hardship. In cases of hardship, any person having a legal or equitable interest in land and

aggrieved by the requirements of  this interim ordinance may apply to the city council for a waiver of all

or a portion of  the applicable restrictions as provided in Chapter 525, Article VII, of  the zoning code. A

waiver may be granted where the city council finds substantial hardship caused by the restrictions and

finds that the waiver will not unduly affect the integrity of the planning process or the purposes for

which the interim ordinance is enacted.
