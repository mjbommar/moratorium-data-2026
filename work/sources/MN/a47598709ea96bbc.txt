                                    CITY OF WAITE PARK
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 2026-02

             AN INTERIM ORDINANCE PLACING A MORATORIUM
                        ON ISSUANCE OF PERMITS
    FOR POTENTIAL DATA CENTER USES WITHIN I-1 LIGHT INDUSTRIAL
DISTRICT, I-2 SUNDIAL CORRIDOR DISTRICT, AND I-3 28TH AVENUE CORRIDOR
                DISTRICT WITHIN THE CITY OF WAITE PARK

The City of Waite Park HEREBY ORDAINS:

       SECTION 1: Purpose and Intent

        A.      The City of Waite Park (the “City”) recognizes significant public interest in
potential data center uses as they rapidly increase throughout the State of Minnesota. The City of
Waite Park is aware of the potential for such uses to be of concern due to their potential for high
amounts of water and subsequent impacts on the public water system, as well as potential concerns
to neighboring properties due to noise and environmental impacts that may be associated with such
projects. The City desires to ensure that all applicable ordinances are in the best possible position
to address future interest in data center facilities, while acknowledging that none have thus far
been proposed or considered.

        B.     The purpose of the moratorium is to allow time to study the existing ordinances,
consider new ordinances, ordinance amendments, additions or removals specific to data center
projects and facilities.

        C.      Minnesota Statutes Section 462.355 allows the City to adopt a temporary interim
ordinance for a period of up to twelve (12) months from the date it is effective to allow for such
study and adoption of comprehensive plan, zoning and/or other official land use controls in order
to protect the public health, safety and general welfare of its citizens.

        D.      The City has concluded that a study is required related to data center projects, which
would currently be most likely to fit under the classifications of “telecommunications facilities”,
or “service structures, public or private, designed and used to serve the uses in the surrounding
area, such as electric power substation, telephone buildings, deep wells, elevated tanks, and similar
structures and uses” as referenced within the applicable I-1, Light Industrial District, I-2, Sundial
Corridor District, and I-3, 28th Avenue Corridor District standards of permitted and conditional
uses.

        E.     There is a need for an interim ordinance to be adopted for the purpose of protecting
the planning process and the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of the City until such a study
has been completed.



      F.     The City acknowledges that there is current potential interest in a small (less than
300 square feet) unoccupied, telecommunications shelter building consisting of fiber-optics

       SECTION 2: Prohibition

        A.      Pursuant to Minnesota Statutes 462.355, the City hereby adopts and approves this
interim ordinance establishing a moratorium temporarily prohibiting the consideration or issuance
of building or conditional use permits for establishment of any projects or developments
considered and determined by City staff to constitute “telecommunications facilities”, or “service
structures, public or private, designed and used to serve the uses in the surrounding area, such as
electric power substation, telephone buildings, deep wells, elevated tanks, and similar structures
and uses” as referenced within the applicable I-1, Light Industrial District, I-2, Sundial Corridor
District, and I-3, 28th Avenue Corridor District standards of permitted and conditional uses, save
for the current proposal as noted in Section 1-F above.

         B.     During the effective period of this interim ordinance, the City will not accept any
application for or issue any permit for any building, development, or conditional uses permits to
facilitate consideration and development of any “telecommunications facilities”, or “service
structures, public or private, designed and used to serve the uses in the surrounding area, such as
electric power substation, telephone buildings, deep wells, elevated tanks, and similar structures
and uses”, or any other proposal which would be determined by City staff to constitute a data
center, as referenced within the applicable I-1, Light Industrial District, I-2, Sundial Corridor
District, and I-3, 28th Avenue Corridor District standards of permitted and conditional uses, save
for the current proposal as noted in Section 1-F above


       SECTION 3: Effective Date and Duration

        A.      This interim ordinance shall exist for a period of twelve (12) months from and after
its adoption.

       B.       This interim ordinance is hereby adopted by the City Council of the City of Waite
Park on this 4th day of May 2026 and shall be effective upon publication.
