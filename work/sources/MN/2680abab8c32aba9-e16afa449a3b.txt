AN INTERIM ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A MORATORIUM ON THE PERMITTING OF DATA CENTERS WITHIN LE SUEUR COUNTY


.

Le Sueur r County hereby finds and declares as follows:

WHEREAS, the purpose of this Interim Ordinance is to protect the public health, safety, and general

welfare of residents of Le Sueur County by temporarily prohibiting the establishment, expansion, or

permitting of data centers while the County undertakes a study to develop appropriate performance

standards, zoning controls, and regulatory frameworks for such facilities; and

WHEREAS, the County Board finds that Data Centers are an emerging land use with significant

impacts related to energy demand, water consumption, noise, heat generation, stormwater

management, and land use compatibility; and

the ’

WHEREAS, Countys existing zoning and performance standards do not adequately address

these impacts; and

WHEREAS, Minn. Stat. § 394.34 authorizes counties to adopt interim ordinances to regulate,

restrict, or prohibit land uses while studies or plans are being conducted; and

WHEREAS, a temporary moratorium is necessary to allow the County sufficient time to study, draft,

and adopt appropriate regulations; and

WHEREAS, this Interim Ordinance is adopted pursuant to Minnesota Statutes §§ 394.21–394.37,

including the authority to adopt interim ordinances under § 394.34;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE LE SUEUR COUNTY BOARD OF

COMMISSIONERS:

SECTION 1. DEFINITION


of this Interim “”

For purposes Ordinance, Data Centermeans a facility used to house computer

systems, servers, networking equipment, and associated infrastructure, including but not limited to

cooling systems, backup power systems, and data storage equipment, whether for private, public, or

commercial use.

SECTION 2. MORATORIUM


A. No new Data Center may be established, constructed, or permitted within Le Sueur County for

the duration of this Interim Ordinance.

B. No land use or zoning permit applications  related to a Data Center shall be accepted, processed,

or approved during the moratorium.

SECTION 3. STUDY


During the moratorium period, County staff, in coordination with the Le Sueur County Board of

Commissioners, shall conduct a study addressing:

 Energy consumption and electrical infrastructure impacts

 Water use and cooling technologies

 Noise, vibration, and heat emissions

 Air quality and environmental impacts

 Stormwater and wastewater management

 Emergency services and hazard mitigation

 Land use compatibility and siting criteria

 Potential taxation, economic, and infrastructure cost impacts

 Best practices from other Minnesota counties and jurisdictions

The study shall result in recommended performance standards and zoning amendments for

consideration by the County Board.

SECTION 4. DURATION


This Interim Ordinance shall remain in effect for twelve (12) months from the date of adoption,

unless earlier repealed or extended as permitted under Minn. Stat. § 394.34.

SECTION 5. SEVERABILITY


If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Interim Ordinance is determined to be

invalid or unconstitutional by a court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the

validity of the remaining portions of the Ordinance, which shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVE DATE


This Ordinance shall become effective June 4, 2026.
