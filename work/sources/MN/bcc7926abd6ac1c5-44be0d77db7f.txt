Catch up on the July Otsego City Council meetings with these key highlights:

July 13 Council Meeting Highlights:

Swearing in of Firefighters - Mayor Jessica Stockamp officially swore in the City of Otsego's newest firefighters, Aaron Ende and Grant Carlson. Following the oath of office, Fire Chief Jim McCoy and members of the City Council welcomed the new firefighters. Commemorative photos were taken to mark the occasion and celebrate their commitment to serving the Otsego community.

2026 Trail Maintenance - The City of Otsego will continue its annual Trail Maintenance Program this year with crack filling and seal coating on selected trail segments. This preventative maintenance helps extend the life of the City's trail system and keeps trails safe for residents.

The City Council approved a contract with TAG Asphalt and Concrete, a contractor that has successfully completed previous street and trail maintenance projects for the City. The project will be completed within the approved budget of $72,000, with any remaining funds used for additional crack filling in high-priority trail areas.

July 27 Council Meeting Highlights:

Data Center Moratorium - Considerable attention is being given to the development of data centers in Minnesota and nationwide as the demand for these facilities has increased with the expansion of internet-based services resulting in both more facilities and larger facilities. Otsego has not received an application for development of a data center, and no known projects are pending. But it is advantageous for the City to anticipate an application and establish allowed uses and performance standards. State law allows authorizes the City to establish a moratorium to temporarily pause data center proposals to allow the City study data center issues and make necessary amendments to development regulations. With the recommendations of Planning Commission, the City Council adopted a moratorium for data centers pausing any application development for up to one year. The Planning Commission will lead the study of data centers and preparation of updated regulations.
Jacobs Plat - New user for Blu Dot building and road improvements to 65th Street - The City Council approved a final plat for Jacobs Plat 2nd Addition, located east of Queens Avenue and south of 65th Street. The current tenant will be moving from the existing facility with a new incoming tenant who will be expanding the use of the site for outdoor warehousing and distribution. This approval necessitated the improvement of 65th Street including conversion of the current gravel roadway to industrial street standards along with water, sanitary sewer, and storm water utilities. The construction of these improvements are scheduled for 2027!
Stormwater PPP - The City held its annual Stormwater Public Meeting as required by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency (MPCA) and the City's Stormwater Pollution Prevention Program (SWPPP). Staff provided an overview of the City's Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) permit and shared how Otsego protects local waterways through annual inspections of stormwater outfalls, storm ponds, and treatment devices. Staff also highlighted ongoing public education efforts, including providing stormwater guidance to developers and contractors and sharing information with residents through The Otsego View newsletter.
