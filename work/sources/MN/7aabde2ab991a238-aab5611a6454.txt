INTERIM ORDINANCE NO. 26-2

RESOLUTION ADOPTING AN INTERIM ZONING ORDINANCE INSTITUTING

AN EMERGENCY MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS

Whereas, new and evolving technologies related to artificial intelligence, cloud services,

and other digital infrastructure have created a need for physical data centers; and d

Whereas, the Office of Planning and Zoning has begun receiving inquiries about data

center regulations, which has resulted in comments, questions and concerns from townships,

cities, and members of the general public; and

Whereas, the Office of Planning and Zoning and the Wright County Planning

Commission have begun experiencing development pressure related to data center operations

and permits; and

Whereas, the Wright County Zoning Ordinance does not expressly regulate or impose

specific  standards for the issuance of interim or conditional use permits related to data centers;

and

Whereas, the Wright County Board of Commissioners has been reviewing the Wright

County Zoning Ordinance with respect to data centers  and recognizes that amendments  may be

necessary to protect public health, safety, and general welfare; and

Whereas, p pursuant to Minnesota Statute § 394.34, the Wright County Board of

Commissioners deems it necessary to adopt an Interim Ordinance placing a moratorium on the

location, establishment, and finalization of new and pending data center interim or conditional

use permits  for a period of one year from the effective date of this Interim Ordinance to permit

the planning process to occur r and to allow County staff, the Planning Commission, and the

County Board sufficient time to proceed in an orderly fashion to make any necessary changes,

modifications, and updates to the County’s Official Controls.

Whereas, an emergency  exists and a temporary emergency moratorium is necessary to

protect public health, safety, and general welfare until public hearings and further study may b be

conducted to consider amendments to official controls; and

Whereas, the Wright County Board of Commissioners finds:

1. A temporary emergency zoning ordinance instituting a moratorium prohibiting new

or pending interim or conditional  use p permits related to data centers is necessary in

order to hold discussions and hearings to determine whether a longer-term  zoning

ordinance shall be adopted and implemented, to protect the public health, safety, and

general welfare of the residents of Wright County.

2. An emergency exists because based on the recent development pressure as well

, b

comments received from the community, townships, and cities, the Wright County

1

Zoning Ordinance does not afford the protection necessary  to ensure that the residents

of Wright County are not adversely affected.

3. It is in the interests of public health, safety, and the general welfare to impose a

temporary moratorium until a public hearing can be noticed and a permanent

ordinance can be p published pursuant to Minn. Stat. § 375.51.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Wright County Board of Commissioners Hereby Ordains:

Section 1. Temporary Restrictions on D Data Center Zoning and Permitting. For a period of one

year from the effective date of this Interim Ordinance, or until such earlier time as

the Wright County Board of Commissioners determines by resolution that the

reasons for the interim ordinance no longer exist, no new or pending applications

for zoning, rezoningor land use permit (including, without limitation, interim or

,

conditional use permits) for a data center shall be accepted, processedor approved

,

by the County.

Section 2. Definition and Application. The term “data center” shall mean any building or

facility housing computers, servers, storage, or networking equipment used

principally to process, transfer, manage, or store digital information. The term shall

also encompass all accessory structures or facilities comprising offices, generators,

water cooling or storage systems, utilities, or other infrastructure related to the

digital data operation. The temporary restrictions set forth in Section 1 shall  apply

to any pending or future zoning applications.

Section 3. Enforcement. Wright County may enforce any provision of this Ordinance by any

and all means authorized by Wright County Ordinances and State Statutes.

Section 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect on the date of its passage and shall

be in effect for a period of one year from the date hereof, unless earlier terminated

by the Wright County Board of Commissioners.

Section 5. Severability. Every section, provision, or part of this Interim Ordinance is declared

severable from every other section, provision, or part thereof to the extent that if

any section, provision, or part of this ordinance shall be held invalid by a court of

competent j jurisdiction, it shall not invalidate any other section, provision, or part

thereof.

RULES AND PROCEDURES FOR PUBLIC

HEARINGS

•All persons wishing to address the Board will be given an opportunity.

•All statements made in the board room are being recorded.

•All persons wishing to address the Board must use the podium, speak into the microphone, and

must begin by stating their full n name and residency (Township or City).

•All comments must be addressed to the Board. Members of the public may not enter into  dialogue

with others during the hearing.

•In order to allow all persons an opportunity to address the Board, comments will be limited

to three (3) minutes per person

.

•Time will be accounted for with a countdown timer to assist speakers, the Board, and the

audience.

•All questions will be addressed by staff at the conclusion of the public comment period.

•The Board has received and reviewed all letters submitted by the public.

•Discussions are not permitted in the gallery. Proper decorum is expected. Persons who create a

disturbance or fail to follow these rules may be asked to leave.
