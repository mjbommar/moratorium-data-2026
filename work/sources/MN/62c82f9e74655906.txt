             PROPERTY RECORDS AND TAXPAYER SERVICES

             Approval of Joint Powers Agreements for Election Recount Services

             Matt Moore, Elections Division Manager, presented two Joint Powers Agreements for election
             recount services with the State of Minnesota, Office of the Secretary of State. The first was Joint
             Powers Agreement No. 294285 for the 2026 Primary Election, and the second was Joint Powers
             Agreement No. 294286 for the 2026 General Election.

             Mr. Moore reported that that State Statute administrative rules name the Office of the Secretary of
             State the recount official for any federal and state contests in those elections. Washington County
             will act as the designated recount official for ballots cast in the county for State and Federal
             contests which require recount. Washington County will be paid four cents for each ballot it
             handles with a minimum payment of $100.00.

             Commissioner Cox moved to approve Joint Powers Agreement No. 294285 for election recount
             services for the 2026 Primary Election with the State of Minnesota, Office of the Secretary of
             State. Commissioner Karwoski seconded the motion, and it was adopted 5-0.

             Commissioner Cox moved to approve Joint Powers Agreement No. 294286 for election recount
             services for the 2026 General Election with the State of Minnesota, Office of the Secretary of
             State. Commissioner Miron seconded the motion, and it was adopted 5-0.

             PUBLIC HEALTH AND ENVIRONMENT

             Resolution Recognizing Breastfeeding Awareness Month

             Amanda Schueler, Public Health Program Supervisor, presented a resolution recognizing August
             2026 as Breastfeeding Awareness Month in Minnesota, which is celebrated nationally and
             worldwide to highlight the essential role of human milk in community health.

             Ms. Schueler reported that the Department of Public Health and Environment (PHE) teams such
             as WIC, Family Health, and Healthy Communities collaborate to encourage breastfeeding and
             provide community and client-focused support. There are 10 certified lactation specialists, as well
             as one staff member who is an internationally board-certified lactation consultant within PHE.
             PHE collaborates with other internal departments to ensure that lactation rooms are meeting the
             needs of employees, and noted that there are lactation spaces at the Historic Courthouse, R.H.
             Stafford Library, County Courts area and the Law Enforcement Center.

             Commissioner Cox moved to adopt Resolution No. 2026-091 as follows:

             PUBLIC WORKS

             County State Aid Highway 14 (34th Street North) Trail Improvement Project

             Becky Haydon, Engineer III, presented two items for the County State Aid Highway 14 (34th Street
             North) Trail Improvement Project identified in the Capital Improvement Plan as #RB-2803. Ms.
             Haydon reported that a recent pavement condition assessment determined this off road, multi-use
             trail had a pavement condition index (PCI) of 38, placing it in the county’s red zone classification.
             A PCI at this level indicates widespread surface distress and deterioration consistent with the end
             of pavement life. The existing condition is negatively affecting usability for all trail users and will
             continue to degrade without reconstruction.

             Ms. Haydon reported that there were four bids received for construction services and Sunram
             Construction has been selected. Ms. Haydon noted that consultant costs on this project are a larger
             portion than other typical projects due to the environmental work that was required because of the
             presence of contaminated soils.

             Commissioner Karwoski moved to approve Contract No. 18400 in the amount of $182,314.00
             with Sunram Construction Inc. for the CSAH 14 (34th Street North) Trail Improvement Project.
             Commissioner Miron seconded the motion, and it was adopted 5-0.

             Commissioner Karwoski moved to approve Cooperative Agreement No. 18395 with the City of
             Oakdale for maintenance responsibilities for the CSAH 14 (34th Street North) Trail Improvement
             Project. Commissioner Cox seconded the motion, and it was adopted 5-0.
