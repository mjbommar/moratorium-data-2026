                               CITY OF WAITE PARK
                  AN INTERIM ORDINANCE PLACING A MORATORIUM
                             ON ISSUANCE OF PERMITS
  FOR POTENTIAL DATA CENTER USES WITHIN I-1 LIGHT INDUSTRIAL DISTRICT, I-2 SUNDIAL
                CORRIDOR DISTRICT, AND I-3 28TH AVENUE CORRIDOR
                      DISTRICT WITHIN THE CITY OF WAITE PARK
                              SUMMARY PUBLICATION

                                       ORDINANCE NO. 2026-______

        The City Council for the City of Waite Park, HEREBY ORDAINS:

        For the purpose of protecting the planning process and the health, safety and welfare of the

citizens of the City of Waite Park, the City Council has adopted Interim Ordinance No. 2026 - ______

adopting an interim ordinance enacting a moratorium, pursuant to Minnesota Statues Section 462.355,

temporarily prohibiting the issuance of permits for potential data centers within the I-1, Light Industrial

District, I-2, Sundial Corridor District, and I-3, 28th Avenue Corridor District within the City of Waite Park

until a study has been completed.

        The moratorium shall exist for a period of twelve (12) months from and after the date of adoption.

        This material is a summary of Interim Ordinance No. 2026- ____. The full text of the Ordinance is

available for inspection by any person during regular office hours at the office of the Waite Park City

Administrator.

        This Interim Ordinance was adopted by the City Council of the City of Waite Park on the 4th day of

May 2026, and shall be effective upon publication.
