Resolution Bill No. 2026-20: Consideration of a One-Year Moratorium on Applications for Data Centers


A RESOLUTION of the City of Neosho, Missouri, imposing a one-year moratorium on the

acceptance of applications relating to the establishment or expansion of data centers within the

limits of the City of Neosho.

City Attorney Snyder read Resolution Bill No. 2026-20 in title only.

Councilwoman Thomas made a motion to approve and discuss Resolution Bill No. 2026-20;

Councilman Cobb seconded.

Development Services Director Worster addressed the city council stating that the purpose of this

agenda item is to seek council approval for the adoption of a one-year moratorium on the

acceptance and processing of applications related to the establishment of data centers within the

City of Neosho while staff evaluates potential impacts and develops appropriate zoning and

development standards. Data centers are intensive users of land and utilities and can create

impacts related to water consumption, wastewater capacity, stormwater runoff, noise, lighting, and

compatibility with surrounding properties. The City of Neosho relies on underground aquifers for

its water supply, has limited wastewater capacity for large-volume users, and continues to address

Page 10

stormwater concerns in various areas of the community. Staff believes additional time is needed to

study the effects of data center development on public infrastructure, utility capacity, land use

compatibility, and community character. A temporary moratorium would allow staff to evaluate

these issues and bring forward recommendations for zoning code amendments and development

standards to ensure any future proposals are reviewed under clear and consistent regulations.

Roll call vote:

Yes: Carl Cobb, Julie Humphrey, Ashton Robinson, Angela Thomas, Tom Workman, Jake Evans

No: None

Abstain: None

Passed.

Resolution Bill No. 2026-21: ACH Policy and Notice


A RESOLUTION of the City of Neosho, Missouri, adopting an Electronic Fund Transfer Policy to

ensure Automated Clearing House (“ACH”) or Electronic Fund Transfers (“ETFs”) are processed

and completed securely to protect nonpublic personal information or protected information for

payments to the City of Neosho.

City Attorney Snyder read Resolution Bill No. 2026-21 in title only.

Councilwoman Robinson made a motion to approve and discuss Resolution Bill No. 2026-21;

Councilman Cobb seconded.

Finance Director Forest addressed the city council stating that the purpose of this item is to seek

approval to move our vendors to payment via ACH/EFT. Currently, staff issues the majority of

our vendors' paper checks for payment of invoices. With mail delivery, check washing and fraud,

staff would like to request that all vendors be paid via ACH. Staff has moved a few of the vendors

with larger checks to ACH or wires. We know some vendors will not want to participate in this.

However, we feel the majority will as they are paid sooner, and it eliminates the need to send

personnel to the bank to make a deposit.

Yes: Carl Cobb, Julie Humphrey, Ashton Robinson, Angela Thomas, Tom Workman, Jake Evans

Rocketdyne Park Inclusive Playground Project – – Committee Introduction & Sponsorship Presentation


Public Information Officer Johnson addressed the city council and she went over the sponsorship

concepts that have been created by the committee. Johnson introduced the Inclusive Playground

Committee and provided an update on the Inclusive Playground Project at Rocketdyne Park and

present a proposed sponsorship recognition concept to support

fundraising efforts. The City of Neosho has partnered with Unlimited Play and Little Tikes

Commercial to develop a fully inclusive playground at Rocketdyne Park. A committee of

community members and partner organizations has been assembled to assist with fundraising,

community outreach, grant opportunities, sponsorship development, and project promotion. As

the project moves into its fundraising phase, staff and the committee have developed a preliminary

sponsorship recognition concept inspired by Neosho's Rocketdyne legacy. The concept features

space-themed sponsorship levels and donor recognition elements that reflect the community's

aerospace history while providing long-term recognition for project supporters. Staff is seeking

Council's input and support before finalizing the sponsorship program and launching fundraising

efforts.

The council, by a consensus, was very happy with the progress of the all-inclusive playground

project.
