The Independence City Council approved a temporary 180-day moratorium on new applications, permits and licenses related to data centers and battery energy storage system facilities.

A moratorium is a temporary pause. In this case, it gives the City time to review current rules and decide whether updates are needed.

The moratorium applies to new data processing and hosting facilities, commonly known as data centers, and battery energy storage system facilities, also known as BESS facilities.
