Be it remembered that a session of the County Commission of Stone County was held in the

courthouse at Galena, on June 04, 2026. Those in attendance today were Presiding Commissioner

Gordon Weathers, Southern Commissioner Hank Smythe and Northern Commissioner Wayne Blades.

10:15 AM

Presiding Commissioner Gordon Weathers opened the meeting with a pledge of allegiance. Those

that were in attendance were: County Counsel William McCullah; Jail Captain Billy Frisbie, J Jonn Latta

and Sheriff Doug Rader with Stone County Sheriff’s Office; David Cash; Steve Seaton; Logan

Stewart; Denise Dickens, Stone County Clerk.

Approval of Commission Minutes

The matter to approve the minutes for Stone County Commission meeting dated May 26, 2026, was

brought to decision by a roll call vote: Weathers – yes; Smythe – yes; Blades – yes. Minutes ordered

approved.

Approval of Commission Minutes

The matter to approve the minutes for Stone County Commission meeting dated May 28, 2026, was

Approval of Commission Minutes

Schedule of Bills - 1

Presiding Commissioner Gordon Weathers presented a schedule of bills that have been submitted to

the County Commission from the County Clerk’s office in the amount of $85,459.23. This matter to

accept the bills for payment was brought to a decision by a roll call vote: Smythe – yes; Blades -yes;

Weathers – yes. Order to pay carried.

Schedule of Bills - 2

Presiding Commissioner Gordon Weathers presented a schedule of bills that have been submitted to

the County Commission from the County Clerk’s office in the amount of $45,669.03. This matter to

accept the bills for payment was brought to a decision by a roll call vote: Smythe – yes; Blades -yes;

Weathers – yes. Order to pay carried.

Open Bids – X-Ray Machine Judicial Building

Billy Frisbie, Jail Captain, came before the Commission to recommend STVS (Secure Technology

Value Solutions) for a new parcel scanner x-ray machine for the lobby of the Judicial Building in the

amount $32,000.00 plus a three (3) year extended maintenance agreement in the amount of

$10,368.00 for a total of $42,268.00 to be paid out of the general revenue reserve fund. The matter

to accept the recommendation for STVS was brought to a decision by a roll call vote: Blades – yes;

Smythe – yes; Weathers – yes.

Interview Library Board Applicants

The Commission received two (2) applications for library board members. Steve Seaton came

before the Commission to interview for reappointment of the Hurley Area for a four (4) year term

ending 2030. Logan Stewart came before the Commission to interview for Galena Area for a four

(4) year term ending 2030. The matter to appoint Steve Seaton and Logan Stewart to the Library

Board was brought to a decision by a roll call vote: Blades – yes; Smythe – – yes; Weathers – yes.

Appointment carried.
