Open Bids – X-Ray Machine Judicial Building
Billy Frisbie, Jail Captain, came before the Commission to recommend STVS (Secure Technology
Value Solutions) for a new parcel scanner x-ray machine for the lobby of the Judicial Building in the
amount $32,000.00 plus a three (3) year extended maintenance agreement in the amount of
$10,368.00 for a total of $42,268.00 to be paid out of the general revenue reserve fund. The matter
to accept the recommendation for STVS was brought to a decision by a roll call vote: Blades – yes;
Smythe – yes; Weathers – yes.


Interview Library Board Applicants
The Commission received two (2) applications for library board members. Steve Seaton came
before the Commission to interview for reappointment of the Hurley Area for a four (4) year term
ending 2030. Logan Stewart came before the Commission to interview for Galena Area for a four
(4) year term ending 2030. The matter to appoint Steve Seaton and Logan Stewart to the Library
Board was brought to a decision by a roll call vote: Blades – yes; Smythe – yes; Weathers – yes.
Appointment carried.


Moratorium on data centers, wind farms, solar farms and battery storage
On June 02, 2026, the Stone County Planning and Zoning Board signed a resolution of the Stone
County Planning Commission relating to a temporary moratorium on solar energy systems, wind
energy conversion systems, battery energy storage systems and data centers to allow amendments
to the Stone County Missouri Zoning regulations.
