Construc,on is underway on the West I-44 Lif Sta,on, which will extend wastewater service to the developing area

Improved Sewer Maintenance Efficiency

The Sewer Maintenance Division has made significant gains in produc,vity over the past two years, par,cularly

within its sewer construc,on crews. Improved staffing, new technology, and beher communica,on and

coordina,on have allowed crews to complete substan,ally more work with the resources available. Recent

Public Works

Work con,nues on the interior restora,on of Historic City Hall and installa,on of major building systems, including

electrical and access-control cabling, ceilings, plaster restora,on, pain,ng and carpentry repairs. Crews installed

Informa8on Systems

The Virtual Server Environment Replacement is substantially complete. Staff migrated the remaining virtual hosts

from VMware to HPE Morpheus on Oct. 17 and decommissioned the VMware environment Oct. 18. Staff

submitted a final RVTools export to VMware for confirmation. Integration of the final two VMware ESXi hosts into

HPE Morpheus remains outstanding.

The Firewall Security Improvements initiative experienced a delay while staff purchased FortiAuthenticator to

serve as an authentication proxy for Police and user VPN tunnels. Staff has received the required licenses and

continues design and configuration for the WAN edge implementation at SDS.

For GIS Modernization, staff provisioned, installed and licensed new GIS servers, including servers for performance

monitoring and aerial imagery. Staff is configuring the Portal server to host internal GIS web services and working

with Networking to resolve a domain URL error. Next steps include establishing governance and migrating data

layers. Phase 2 will begin after substantial completion of Phase 1.

The Incident Reporting system remains on track. The Airport launched the system Aug. 31, and Parks, the final

department, plans to launch Nov. 2. Staff expects to complete the initiative ahead of its Dec. 31 target.
