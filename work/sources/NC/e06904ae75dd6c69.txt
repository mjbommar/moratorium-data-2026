e RESOLUTION 2026-087-RESOLUTION SETTING PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING
MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS:

RESOLUTION NUMBER 2026-087

RESOLUTION SETTING PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING TEMPORARY MORATORIUM
ON DEVELOPMENT APPROVALS FOR DATA CENTERS AND RELATED HIGH-IMPACT
FACILITIES

WHEREAS, the City of Mount Airy Board of Commissioners desire to hold a public
hearing to receive public comment regarding the adoption of a proposed temporary
moratorium on the acceptance and processing of applications for new or expanded data
centers within the City of Mount Airy; and

WHEREAS, the purpose of the proposed moratorium is to allow the City to evaluate
the impacts of data center facilities on utility capacity, environmental resources, energy
consumption, and local zoning, and to prepare appropriate land-use regulations; and

WHEREAS, the proposed ordinance would prohibit for a period of sixty-days the
construction, development, or permitting of data centers, cryptocurrency mining operations,
and any use associated with data center processing facilities; and

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY OF MOUNT AIRY
BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS MEETING IN OPEN SESSION THAT:

Section 1. A public hearing be scheduled for Thursday, July 16, 2026 at 6:00 pm to hear
public comment on the above-mentioned item.


Section 2. Notice of the public hearing shall be published in the Mount Airy News, a
newspaper having general circulation in the City of Mount Airy, at least ten
(10) days prior to the date of the public hearing.

Section 3. This resolution shall become effective upon approval.

Approved and adopted this the 18 day of June, 2026.

ORDINANCE NUMBER 2026-066

ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE CITY OF MOUNT AIRY ZONING ORDINANCE
Article X-Landscaping, Screening, and Buffer Areas

WHEREAS, the Mount Airy Planning Board and Planning staff have reviewed the
proposed amendment to Article X of the City of Mount Airy Zoning Ordinance; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendment will reduce the landscaping requirements of
display and parking areas for vehicles for sale, lease, or rental; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Board has recommended these proposed amendments to
the Board of Commissioners for their consideration:

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED that the following Statement of
Consistency is hereby adopted:

Statement of Consistency:
“This proposed amendment would make the distinction of parking lot screening requirements
and eliminate requirements for internal parking lot vegetation; however, a parking lot
perimeter buffer (being a minimum of five (5) feet and planted with shrubs) would still be
required. This amendment is also reasonable and in the public’s best interest.

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments are as follows (in bold/red):

B. Landscaping Yards
Landscaping yards include:
1. Street Yards (Section 10.3)
2. Buffer Yards (Section 10.4)
3. Parking Lot Yards (Section 10.5)
4. Screening Yards (Section 10.6)
5. Building Yards (Section 10.7)
6. Residential Yards (Section 10.8)

C. Exceptions
1. The provisions of Sections 10.3, 10.4, 10.7 and 10.8 shall not apply to
properties in the Municipal Service District.
2. The provisions of Sections 10.5.A, B and C, shall not apply to
vehicle sales, rental and/or lease outdoor display areas.

10.2 General Provisions & Maintenance
A. All plants shall be installed in accordance with the latest edition of the
American Standards for Nursery Stock, published by the American
Nurserymen’s Association and the American National Standards
Institute (ANSI.


B. The Planting List can be found in Section 10.10. The Zoning
Administrator may approve alternative trees or shrubs that are native to
the area.

C. Screening fences or walls shall be maintained by the property owner, in
good condition, throughout the period of the use of the lot.

D. Required landscaping shall be maintained to mature growth habit and
trees shall not be topped. Any deceased vegetation that is part ofa
required landscaping area shall be replaced within sixty (60) days. All
landscaping materials shall be protected from damage by erosion, motor
vehicles or pedestrians.

E. Ground cover may include any plant materials that reach an average
height of not more than twelve (12”) inches. Alternative materials may be
used in lieu of grass provided they present a finished appearance and
provide reasonably complete coverage at the time of planting.

BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED that this ordinance shall become effective upon
approval.

Approved and adopted this the 18th day of June, 2026.
