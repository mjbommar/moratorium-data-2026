Published on June 08, 2026

On Oct. 26, City Council approved an extension of the temporary moratorium on the acceptance, processing and approval of applications for new telecommunications and data storage facilities in Charlotte. The extension is set to expire no later than Oct. 18, 2027.

Council originally established the moratorium on data centers on June 8.

Below are some frequently asked questions and a timeline regarding data centers and moratoriums in North Carolina and what this vote means for Charlotte.

Data Centers Impact Report

The World Resources Institute partnered with the city to outline the known impacts of data center development and actions other local governments across the nation are taking.

The report provides some assessment of environmental impacts specific to Charlotte but does not make direct recommendations for changes to Charlotte’s policies and regulations.

Read the report

Community Engagement

The city hosted six community engagement sessions, and the feedback was compiled for Council consideration.

During the extension, community engagement opportunities will continue. A timeline will be made available as a new plan is finalized.

Sign up to stay informed and engaged on this topic by visiting Data Center Public Engagement.

What's Next?

From Oct. 27, 2026, until the moratorium ends, city staff will engage with additional experts to develop the tools needed to ensure responsible data center development. The extension is set to end no later than Oct. 18, 2027.
The community task force will continue to engage with the work by using the extension to review research, meet with policy experts and consolidate their policy recommendations.
The city will also engage with a consultant for detailed mitigation requirements and policy language and coordinate with Entities with Regulatory Control outside of the city’s scope.

For more information about the work happening during the extension, please see the Oct. 12 council presentation.
