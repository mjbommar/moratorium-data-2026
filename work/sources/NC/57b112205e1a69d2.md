# Data Centers Moratorium

source: https://www.co.surry.nc.us/departments/development_services/data_centers_moratorium.php
fetched: 2026-09-23T11:53:28+00:00

On July 20, 2026, the Board of Commissioners adopted an ordinance extending the temporary moratorium (i.e. originally approved on June 1, 2026) on development approvals for data centers and related high-impact facilities in all areas subject to Surry County zoning authority and jurisdiction.

The moratorium will expire on July 20, 2028.

On this page you can find current information about the moratorium and the plan of action to be taken during the moratorium to address the statement of problems or conditions and the alternatives being considered.
