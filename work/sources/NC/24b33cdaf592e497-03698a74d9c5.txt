ORDINANCE OF THE ROWAN COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS
IMPOSING A ONE-YEAR MORATORIUM ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF DATA
CENTERS IN ROWAN COUNTY, NORTH CAROLINA

WHEREAS, pursuant to N.C. Gen. Stat. § 160D-107, local governments may adopt
an ordinance imposing a temporary moratorium on any development approval required
by law; and

WHEREAS, the Rowan County Board of Commissioners feel the Rowan County
zoning ordinance may benefit from additional standards on the development of data
centers; and

WHEREAS, the Rowan County Board of Commissioners, at its April 20, 2026
meeting held a properly noticed legislative hearing regarding this moratorium in
accordance with N.C. Gen. Stat. § 160D-107(b); and

WHEREAS, the Rowan County Board of Commissioners recognizes that certain
property and/or projects are exempt from this moratorium under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 160D-
107(c) and understands that Rowan County cannot enforce this moratorium as to exempt
property and/or projects.

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Rowan County Board of Commissioners,
does and enacts the following:

Section I - Term of the Moratorium

1. This moratorium shall be known as the Temporary Data Center
Moratorium (“Moratorium”).

2. This Moratorium shall commence on May 4, 2026 and expire no later than
May 4, 2027. The Rowan County Board of Commissioners (“Board”) shall
be permitted to terminate this Moratorium early upon the passage of a
resolution by a majority vote of the Board.

3. For the purposes of this Moratorium, data centers shall be defined as one
or more buildings housing a group of networked computer servers
typically used by organizations for the remote storage, processing, or
distribution of digital data; these facilities are within the scope of SIC 73 -
Business Services and are generally assumed to be SIC 7374 - Computer
Processing and Data Preparation and Processing Services and SIC 7379 -
Computer Related Services, Not Elsewhere Classified; but may also include
other computer related hardware and software applications within the
broader industry group.

Section II - Statement of Problems, Conditions, and Alternatives

1. The Board acknowledges that the Rowan County Zoning Ordinance
(“Ordinance”) does not clearly define a “data center” and therefore intends
to enact this moratorium to consider amending its Ordinance subject to
these considerations:

a.

The Board recognizes that data center development is increasing at
a rapid rate in North Carolina and across the United States. The
Board recognizes that data center uses are ever-evolving as are the
technology and infrastructure elements thereof.

The Board recognizes the economic benefits data center
development may provide, including creating an increased tax base
that has significant potential to subsidize the cost of local
government for citizens.

The Board also recognizes concern amongst citizens regarding the
ever-evolving nature of data center development and potential
impacts from data centers to the quality of life of those living near
them.

The Board recognizes that an ideal Ordinance would permit data
center development to increase the economic success of Rowan
County (“County”) while addressing and alleviating legitimate
concerns regarding data center development.

Given the rise in data center development regionally, the Board feels
it pertinent to review the Ordinance to ensure coexistence of these
goals of economic development and citizen welfare.

The Board recognizes that it could have undertaken said review
without issuing this Moratorium yet feels doing so, without a pause
in development, would have been inadequate in light of the rapid
rate of data center development in the region.

Section III - Scope of the Moratorium

1. This Moratorium shall apply to all data centers as defined in Section I herein
excluding those exempted by Section VI herein.

This Moratorium shall prohibit the issuance of any permit within the
County's zoning jurisdiction for data center development including but not
limited to zoning, building, and demolition permits as well as receiving
submittals for soil erosion and sedimentation control plans.

This Moratorium shall prohibit the rezoning of any property for use as a
data center.

This Moratorium shall be in effect for one (1) year beginning on May 4, 2026
and expiring on May 4, 2027.

A one-year moratorium will provide the Board and County staff adequate
time to engage with all stakeholders including Rowan County citizens and
data center developers to better understand the concerns of citizens and
benefits of data centers.

A one-year moratorium will further allow the Planning Board and County
planning staff adequate time to review and revise, if necessary, the
Ordinance related to data centers to ensure it addresses legitimate concerns
while permitting responsible data center development.

Section V - Statement and Schedule of Proposed Actions

1.

From May 4, 2026 to no later than September 14, 2026, the Board, Planning
Board, and County staff shall study the benefits and potential harms of data
centers. This process shall include at least one (1) properly noticed public
hearing at a 6:00 PM Board meeting to allow interested parties to voice
concerns regarding data centers and at least one (1) public presentation at
a 6:00 PM Board meeting from a data center developer or industry expert
on the potential benefits of data centers and the ways in which they may
mitigate presumed harms.

Simultaneous with the actions in Paragraph 1 of this Section, County
planning staff shall study development restrictions placed on data centers
by other counties and municipalities with a focus on emerging data center
technologies.

No later than September 14, 2026, planning staff shall begin preparing
proposed revisions to the Ordinance based on information gathered in the
first phase of this Moratorium timeline as well as any new information
learned in preparing said proposals. If planning staff makes a
determination that no changes to the Ordinance are needed, they shall
prepare a memorandum supporting that determination.

No later than February 8, 2027, the Planning Board shall conduct a courtesy
hearing on the proposed changes to the Ordinance developed pursuant to
Paragraph 3 of this Section. If planning staff determines that no changes to
the Ordinance are warranted, a courtesy hearing shall be held on that
determination as well. The Planning Board shall then make a
recommendation to the Board.

5. No later than May 3, 2027 the Board shall hold a public hearing on the
proposed changes to the Ordinance and vote on whether to adopt said
proposed changes. If no changes to the Ordinance are proposed, planning
staff shall provide a report of the same to the Board at a public meeting no
later than May 3, 2027.

6. This Moratorium shall expire on May 4, 2027.

Section VI - Exemptions

1. All property or projects which qualify for an exemption from this
Moratorium under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 160D-107(c) and North Carolina
common law are exempt.

2. The Board recognizes that it cannot legally enforce this Moratorium as to
ongoing projects on the following parcels pursuant to N.C. Gen. Stat. §
160D-107(c) and makes clear no intent to do so: Rowan County Tax Parcel
IDs: 603-112; 603-113; 603114; 603-116; 603118; 603-045; and 603-061.

Section VII - Severability

1. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is,
for any reason, held to be invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity
of the remaining portions of this ordinance. The Board hereby declares that
it would have passed this ordinance, and each section, subsection, sentence,
clause, or phrase thereof irrespective of the fact that any one or more
sections, subsections, sentences, clauses, or phrases be declared invalid.

Section VIII - Other Ordinances
1. All Rowan County ordinances which conflict with this Ordinance are

hereby repealed to the extent of such conflict.

Adopted by the Rowan County Board of Commissioners, this the 4t day of May 2026.
