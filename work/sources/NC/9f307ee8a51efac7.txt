Manager Ferguson requested Water Management Director Don Greeley to share a brief
overview of the stage 2 water restrictions in effect:

Director Greely provided an update regarding Durham’s water supply and the City’s
Stage 2 Water Shortage Response. He stated that Durham is experiencing an unusual
severe drought and that Lake Michie and Little River Reservoir were approximately 85%
full on April 1, instead of at full capacity as expected.

Director Greely stated that Stage 2 restrictions primarily focus on reducing outdoor
water use, including prohibiting spray irrigation, turning off irrigation systems, limiting
hand watering and drip irrigation, and restricting car washing to compliant commercial
car washes. He encouraged residents and businesses to fix leaks, shorten showers,
and eliminate unnecessary water use.

Director Greely stated that moving out of Stage 2 will depend on consistent rainfall in
Durham’s watersheds and improved lake levels. He explained that enforcement begins
with education and warnings and may escalate to notices of violation, fines, or service
disconnection only in repeated serious cases. He also highlighted Durham’s
conservation efforts and encouraged residents to visit www.durhamsaveswater.org for
additional information.
