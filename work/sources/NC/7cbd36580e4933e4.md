## Data Centers Impact Report

The World Resources Institute partnered with the city to outline the known impacts of data center development and actions other local governments across the nation are taking.

The report provides some assessment of environmental impacts specific to Charlotte but does not make direct recommendations for changes to Charlotte’s policies and regulations.

[Read the report](https://www.charlottenc.gov/files/sharedassets/city/v/5/story-image/documents/data-centers-executive-summary-and-impact-report-v4.pdf)

## Task Force Meetings

A task force has been convening to prepare policy recommendations through a series of seven meetings. The group will work to offer policy adjustments that align with residents’ daily lives and reduce the impact of data centers on the community. These representatives will bring land use and development review experience, infrastructure and environmental knowledge, perspectives from key growth corridors, and technical and research-based insight on air quality and energy systems to the task force's work.

Learn more about [task force members and where to watch the meetings](https://www.charlottenc.gov/City-News/Data-Centers-City-Establishes-Task-Force).
