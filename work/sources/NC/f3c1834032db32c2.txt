“UNOFFICIAL”
TOWN OF ELIZABETHTOWN
May 26, 2026 Rescheduled Meeting
Noon

The Elizabethtown Town Council met on Tuesday, May 26, 2026 at Noon in the Council
Chambers. Those present were Mayor Sylvia Campbell, Mayor Pro Tem Paula Greene, Council
Members Bruce Dickerson, Rich Glenn, Bobby Kinlaw, Ricky Leinwand and Rufus Lloyd. Also
present were Town Manager Eddie Madden and Town Attorney Goldston Womble. Department
Heads in attendance included Police Chief Mark McMichael, Fire Chief Hollis Freeman, Public
Works/Engineering Services Director Steve Duffy, Finance Director Regina Valenta and Town
Clerk Juanita Hester. Mr. Sonny Jones with the “Bladen Journal” was present as well as
numerous others from the community. For the swearing-in of Town Manager Eddie Madden at
the Noon meeting, Mr. Madden’s wife, Dawn, his daughter, Abigail, and son, Emory, were

present for this special occasion.

Item #1.1 Opening and Call to Order

Mayor Sylvia Campbell opened the meeting and called to order. Mayor Campbell then called
upon Council Member Rufus Lloyd to give the Invocation. Mayor Campbell asked that the
Council Members join her at the side of the room where Town Manager Eddie Madden was to be
sworn-in. Council Member Lloyd delivered the most beautiful and heart-felt prayer for our

Town.

Item #2.1 Presentation of Colors, Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation
The presentation of Colors will be made by the Paul R. Brown Leadership Academy Cadets at

the evening meeting. Cadets did not attend the 7 p.m. rescheduled meeting.

Item #3.1 Oaths of Office — Town Manager and Finance Director — Zo Be Administered at Noon
Mayor Sylvia Campbell gave a quick rundown of the many accomplishments of Town Manager
Eddie Madden during his prior tenure with the Town. She noted that Mr. Madden demonstrated
leadership, vision and integrity during his 13 years as Town Manager and that she is excited for
the Town’s future under Mr. Madden’s leadership. At this time, Mayor Campbell called upon
Town Attorney Goldston Womble to administer the Oath of Office to Town Manager Eddie
Madden and to new Finance Director Regina Valenta. Mr. Madden’s family members were by his

side.

Mayor Campbell acknowledged the presence of Mr. Oscar Taylor at the meeting. Mr. Taylor
served many years as the Fixed Base Operator at the Elizabethtown Airport.

TOWN OF ELIZABETHTOWN
Evening Meeting
May 26, 2026

The Elizabethtown Town Council met for its evening meeting on Tuesday, May 26, 2026, in the
Council Chambers at 7:00 pm. Those present were Mayor Sylvia Campbell, Mayor Pro Tem
Paula Greene, Council Members Rufus Lloyd, Bobby Kinlaw, Ricky Leinwand, Rich Glenn,
Bruce Dickerson and Town Attorney Goldston Womble. Department Heads in attendance
included Police Chief Mark McMichael, Town Clerk Juanita Hester, Fire Chief Hollis Freeman,
Public Works & Engineering Services Director Stephen Duffy and Finance Director Regina
Valenta. No members from the Press attended the meeting.

[tem #1.1: Opening and Call to Order

Mayor Sylvia Campbell opened the meeting and called to order. Mayor Campbell noted that
Town Manager Eddie Madden was sworn in at Noon. “We are glad to have him back, we know
his love for Elizabethtown, and he has the same values as we do.”

Item #2.1: Presentation of Colors, Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation
Paul R. Brown Leadership Academy Cadets were not available. Mayor Sylvia Campbell then
called upon Council Member Rufus Lloyd to give the invocation. Then all joined in reciting the

Pledge of Allegiance.

Madden and Finance Director Regina Valenta.

Item #4.1: Special Recognition Patrick DeVane
Agenda Item #4.1 taken off agenda.

Item#5.1: Request from Bladen County Board of Elections

Mr. James Nance III, Director of Bladen County Board of Elections is asking if the new
Community Center can be used as an early voting site. There are some problems at the Armory.
Council Member Bobby Kinlaw asked about fees for use of the building. Mr. Nance noted it
would be $500.00 for early voting and $100.00 clean-up fee. Council Member Bruce Dickerson,
seconded by Council Member Rich Glenn, made a motion to approve the use of the Community

Center for an early voting site (Unanimous).
