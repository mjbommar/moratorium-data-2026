Pasquotank County Board of Commissioners

2026 Meeting Schedule

Regular meetings begin at 6:00 PM. Finance Committee meetings are held on the same day as each regular meeting beginning at 4:00 PM. The Appointments Committee meets following the first Finance Committee meeting of the month, and work sessions, if needed, are held prior to the second regular meeting of the month at 3:30 PM. All meetings will be held in the Commissioners Boardroom on the first floor of the Pasquotank County Courthouse and are subject to change.
