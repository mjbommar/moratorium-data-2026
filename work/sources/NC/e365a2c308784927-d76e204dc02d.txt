Mr. Adam Tyson, Planning Director, presented for the Board’s consideration Public

Hearing on Temporary Development Moratorium: Data Centers, Data Processing

Facilities, Cryptocurrency Mining Operations, & Similar Uses. He advised that the Board,

at its previous regular meeting, adopted a resolution to hold a public hearing at today’s

meeting on a proposed temporary moratorium on development approvals for data

centers, data processing facilities, cryptocurrency mining operations, and similar uses

within the planning and zoning jurisdiction of Nash County. He also advised that the

purpose of the moratorium is to allow time for the County to sufficiently study the

challenges and impacts presented by these types of facilities, including:

· High electricity consumption;

· Significant noise generation;

· Substantial water usage;

· Potential environmental impacts; and

· Land use compatibility concerns

And to consider appropriate regulatory amendments to the County’s Unified Development

Ordinance (UDO) to adequately address those concerns and to protect the public interest

and welfare of the County’s residents. If adopted, the moratorium would commence

immediately and shall expire either on June 30, 2027 or upon the County’s approval of

new zoning regulations addressing these land use types, whichever occurs first.

On motion of Gwen Wilkins seconded by Robbie B. Davis and duly passed that

the Nash County Board of Commissioners  go into a public hearing.
