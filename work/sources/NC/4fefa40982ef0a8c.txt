Council Member Baker thanked residents for their patience during public comment sign-

Heritage Month, and reminded residents of upcoming City budget discussions and

celebrations and protests in Raleigh advocating for stronger public school funding. He
thanked NCAE and participants involved in the statewide demonstrations. He also
discussed the Garmin Marathon held in Durham and thanked the Durham Sports
Commission for organizing the event. He shared his attendance at the Hayti Heritage
Center screening of the documentary Moonchild by filmmaker Kenny Dalsheimer and
discussed the film’s focus on Durham jazz musician Brother Yusuf Salim, NCCU’s jazz
program, and Durham’s Muslim community history.

and thanked residents who participated in the Raleigh May Day demonstrations
supporting public schools. She reflected on ongoing public school funding concerns in
North Carolina and encouraged continued civic engagement. She also informed
attendees that the evening’s agenda would be reordered due to the Mayor’s delayed
arrival.

Unified Development Ordinance Zoning Map Change Consistency Statement Regarding
Z2500020A, 1735 Fletchers Chapel Road

Whereas the Durham City Council, upon acting upon a zoning map change to the
Unified Development Ordinance and pursuant to state statute N.C.G.S. Sec. 160D-605,
is required to approve a statement describing how the action is consistent with the
Durham Comprehensive Plan; and

Whereas the Durham City Council, upon acting upon a zoning map change to the
Unified Development Ordinance and pursuant to state statute N.C.G.S. Sec. 160D-605,
is required to provide a brief statement indicating how the action is reasonable and in
the public interest.

Now therefore, be it adopted by the Durham City Council as appropriate:

That final action regarding zoning map change Z2500020A, 1735 Fletchers Chapel
Road, is based upon review of consistency with the Durham Comprehensive Plan and
any other officially adopted plan that is applicable, as provided in the ‘Compliance with
UDO and Adopted Plans’ section of the staff report; regarding the subject ‘Z2500020A,

1735 Fletchers Chapel Road’ along with additional agenda information provided to the
City Council and information provided at the public hearing; and

That the proposed zoning map change designation is consistent with the Place Type
Designation as provided on the Place Type designation of the Comprehensive Plan in
Attachment C; and

It is the objective of the Durham City Council to have the Unified Development
Ordinance promote regulatory efficiency and consistency and the health, safety, and
general welfare of the community. This request promotes this by offering fair and
reasonable development regulations supported by the ‘Compliance with UDO and

reasonable and in the public interest.
