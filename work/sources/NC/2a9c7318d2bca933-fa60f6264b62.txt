For Immediate Release: 07/2/20/2026


“Yadkin County Board of Commissioners After-Action Report for July 20, 2026.”

During the July 20, 2026 Board meeting, the Yadkin County Board of Commissioners took the following action:

• Approved Tax Adjustments and Refunds for June 2026.

• Approved Tax Collector to collect taxes for Tax Year 2026 and the annual settlement for 2025-

2026.

• Approved Lease Agreement with North Carolina Department of Adult Correction to provide

office space for Adult Probation staff at 207 East Main Street.

• Approved amendment to Lease Agreement with Fall Creek Fire Department for EMS Medic

Outpost that is located on Fall Creek Fire Department property.

• Approved FY27 Contract with Piedmont Triad Regional Council to administer the Home and

Community Care Block Grant (HCCBG) and HCCBG for Older Adults County Funding Plan.

• Approved annual non-profit agreement to provide funding for Partners Behavioral Health

Management.

• Approved annual non-profit agreement to provide funding for Richmond Hill Law School

Commission.

• Approved annual non-profit agreement to provide funding for Northwestern Regional Library

System.

• Approved annual non-profit agreement to provide funding for YVEDDI.

• Approved annual non-profit agreement to provide funding for Yadkin Arts Council.

• Approved General Services Contract with Advanced Appliance Service to provide appliance

repair services as needed.

• Approved General Services Contract with Champion Systems, Inc. for providing building

control services as needed.

• Approved Contract Amendment with Municipal Engineering, Inc. to extend contract timeline

for them to continue providing LCID Landfill Design services until June 2027.

• Approved Contract with Mission Critical Partners for implementing Automated Secure Alarm

Protocol for Emergency Communications.

• Approved Contract with Southern Software for CAD Interface to support Emergency

Communications operations.

• Approved Contract with Southern Software for software and customer support for Sheriff’s

Office operations.

• Approved Contract with LeadsOnline, LLC to provide technological support to Sheriff’s Office

investigators.

• Approved Contract with Lexipol, LLC to provide training policies and resources to the Sheriff’s

Office.

• Approved Contract with South Western Communications for warranty extension for the

Integrated Security Systems at the Detention Center.

• Approved Contract with Avineon to support GIS data operations for the County.

• Approved Social Media Archiving Contract with CivicPlus, LLC.

• Approved Contract with Unifirst to provide linen services for Yadkin County Emergency

Services.

• Approve five Hilco invoices for the month of June.

• Approved setting a public hearing for August 17, 2026, at 7:00 pm to address an Ordinance

setting a twelve month moratorium for Data Centers.

• Approved setting a public hearing for August 17, 2026 at 7:00 pm to address amendments to the

Solid Waste Management Ordinance.

• Approved Case RZ26-006, which requested to zone 26.887 acres at 2624 US 601 HWY to

Residential Institutional (RI).

• Approved acceptance for consideration of the 2027 Schedule of Values for the 2027

Countywide reappraisal.

• Approved negotiating a contract with Hennings Construction Company to perform driveway

improvements at the Medic 2 Building.

• Approved bid and Notice of Intent to Award to Shelco to perform construction project to

support new location for Emergency Communications

.

• Approved proposal from Inspire Placemaking to update the Yadkin County Land Use Plan.

• Approved appropriation of fund balance to support projects that were not completed during

FY26.

• Tabled appropriation of $9,000.00 in fund balance for another project.

• Approved authorization of NCGS 160A-269 to sell two-county owned parcels through the upset

bid process.

For a complete account of Board Action, refer to https://www.yadkincountync.gov/agendacenter. Board

minutes are posted online once approved.
