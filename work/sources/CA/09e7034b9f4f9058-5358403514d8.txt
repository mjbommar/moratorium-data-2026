Councilmember Melendez asked if the ordinance could be changed so that it reads data centers and not Artificial

intelligence data centers, so it catches a broader coverage.

Director Palombi stated that there is no issue with amending the ordinance. He noted that while the ordinance does not

specifically define “data centers,” it is intended to prohibit all data centers.

City Attorney Lam confirmed that the ordinance may be amended using broad terminology to ensure it captures both AI

data centers and smaller data centers that may not involve artificial intelligence. He added that further analysis and

recommendations would be left to the Planning staff.

Councilmember Peralta motioned, seconded by Councilmember Alonzo, to approve the item; the motion was approved

unanimously.
