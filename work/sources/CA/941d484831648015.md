## Background

Battery Energy Storage Systems store energy for later use, improving grid reliability and supporting renewable energy like solar and wind. BESS benefits include:

* Better energy security
* Lower greenhouse gas emissions
* Reduced peak energy demand

However, challenges include fire risks, effects on farmland, and battery disposal impacts.

On January 23, 2024, Solano County enacted a two-year pause on approving large-scale BESS projects to create safety-focused land use standards.

## Public Workshop

On July 18, 2024, the Solano County Planning Division hosted a public workshop to gather input on the County’s efforts to develop a zoning ordinance for battery energy storage systems (BESS) in response to the Urgency Ordinance approved and extended by the Board of Supervisor on February 27, 2024.

At the workshop, speakers from the Solano County Planning Division and Clean Coalition explained how BESS technology works and discussed possible impacts on nearby communities. They showed examples of BESS projects, described applications already received in Solano County, and introduced an alternative permitting process under Assembly Bill 205 (the “Opt-In” process), which lets applicants bypass local permitting rules.

During public comment, many residents voiced concerns about BESS facilities being too close to homes and schools, especially near the Vaca-Dixon substation in Vacaville. Some suggested ways to protect taxpayers, such as collecting part of the revenue made by these facilities, funding independent safety checks, and training first responders. They also highlighted the need for cybersecurity and U.S.-based service and manufacturing requirements. Other concerns included effects on property values, insurance costs, and farmland protection. Many people wanted BESS facilities placed farther from residential areas, noting that rural communities also deserve the same level of protection.

Several attendees recommended using safer battery chemistries, like iron flow or graphene, instead of lithium-ion. Some suggested placing BESS projects in the Lambie Industrial Park, where existing natural gas peaker plants offer grid connections without the same concerns as near the Vaca Dixon substation.

The County remains committed to listening to stakeholders as it move towards developing a strong rules for BESS that prioritize safety, environmental stewardship, and community engagement.
