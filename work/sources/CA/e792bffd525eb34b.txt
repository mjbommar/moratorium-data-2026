INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND

The City of Calipatria received $7 million from the State Department of Parks and Recreation
for the development of a new park and community center in 2021. The location of the park and
community center was later changed to a proposed 3.0-acre site within the southern portion of
the Cliff Hatfield Memorial Airport. Prior to approving construction activities and
reimbursements, the State requires a Deed Restriction designating the project site as parkland.
This requires a subdivision to create two parcels: 3 acres for the community center and park
(Parcel 2) and the remaining 165.54 acres (Parcel 1).

The City contracted with GAFCON, Dempsey Construction and Dynamic Consulting Engineers
to project manage, design-build the community center and park, and to prepare the Tentative
Parcel Map (TPM) and Final Parcel Map.

The City Council approved a TPM for the proposed project during a public hearing held on July
14, 2026, A Final Parcel Map was subsequently prepared and submitted for review and
approval, conditional approval, or rejection by the City Council.

ISSUES FOR DISCUSSION

Project Location & Existing Conditions — The project site is an existing168.54-acre parcel at
440 West Main Street within the Airport Overlay District Zone. The existing parcel is bounded
on the north by Young Road along the City’s northern city limits; partially by Highway 111,
Delta Street, and International Avenue on the east; by Main Street on the south; and Lyerly
Road on the west. (Refer to Attachment A — Project Location Map)

Final Parcel Map — The purpose of a Final Parcel Map is to legally divide a single tract of land
creating smaller distinct parcels while a TPM illustrates the proposed layout and details of a
subdivision or development project allowing planning authorities to review a proposal before a
Final Parcel Map is approved. The Final Parcel Map will subdivide the existing 168.54-acre
airport property to separate a 3.0-acre parcel to accommodate the construction of the proposed
community center and park. “Parcel 1” will measure 165.54 and is to remain as the Cliff
Hatfield Memorial Airport. “Parcel 2” will measure 3.0-acres and will accommodate the
construction of the proposed community center and park. (Refer to Exhibit A — Final Parcel
Map)

RESOLUTION NO. 26-15

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF CALIPATRIA
APPROVING A FINAL PARCEL MAP AT 440 WEST MAIN STREET (APN 023-070-
007)

WHEREAS, in 2024 the City of Calipatria was awarded $7,000,000 in funds from the
State Department of Parks and Recreation Statewide Park Development and Community
Revitalization program for the development of a new park and community center at the southern
portion of the Cliff Hatfield Memorial Airport; and

WHEREAS, the City is proposing to subdivide the existing 168.54-acre parcel at 440 West
Main Street (APN 023-070-007) into two (2) parcels to accommodate the construction of the
Calipatria Community Center; and

WHEREAS, “Parcel 1” will measure 165.54-acres and remain as the Cliff Hatfield
Memorial Airport, and “Parcel 2” will measure 3.0-acres to accommodate the construction of the
Community Center Park; and

WHEREAS, a Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) was previously approved on
December 9, 2025, for the Community Center and the proposed Tentative Parcel Map is consistent
with the adopted MND in accordance with the requirements of the California Environmental
Quality Act (CEQA); and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission of the City of Calipatria scheduled and held a duly
noticed public hearing on July 7, 2026, and motioned to recommend approval of Tentative Parcel
Map 26-01 with Conditions of Approval to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Calipatria scheduled and held a duly noticed
public hearing on July 14, 2026, and motioned to approve Tentative Parcel Map 26-01 with
Conditions of Approval; and

WHEREAS, a Final Parcel Map was prepared in accordance with the conditions of
approval for Tentative Parcel Map 26-01 and submitted to the City of Calipatria for review; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Calipatria reviewed the Final Parcel Map
during a regularly scheduled meeting on July 28, 2026, and took action.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of Calipatria
as follows:

A) That the foregoing recitations are true and correct; and

B) The project has been reviewed in accordance with the requirements set forth by the
City of Calipatria for implementation of the California Environmental Quality Act
(CEQA); and
1. The proposed project is consistent with the adopted policies and land uses

of the City’s General Plan.

2: The proposed project is consistent with the adopted objectives of the
Calipatria Zoning Ordinance.

C) The City Council of the City of Calipatria hereby APPROVES a Final Parcel Map,
attached hereto as Exhibit A, for 440 West Main Street (APN 023-070-007) and
based on the following:

1. That the Final Parcel Map is consistent with the Calipatria General Plan and
Zoning Ordinance.
