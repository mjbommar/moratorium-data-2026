Data Center Moratorium

On September 15, 2026, the City Council adopted Urgency Ordinance No. 13-26 N.S., which placed a temporary 45-day moratorium on any new data center in the City of Richmond and declared the urgency of that action under Government Code Section 65858.

What This Means

The City has a temporary 45-day moratorium on the issuance or approval of permits or land use entitlements for data centers within the City of Richmond, at least until October 30, 2026. During this time, the City is preparing to study data centers and to engage the public in setting reasonable regulations for them, if the City allows them, to protect the public health, safety, and welfare.

Upcoming Meetings:

October 20, 2026 - Public Hearing to consider a 22-month and 15-day extension of Urgency Ordinance No. 13-26 N.S., which established a temporary moratorium on the approval or issuance of new data center land use and permit applications within the City of Richmond.

10-Day Report on the 45-Day Data Center Moratorium - Under Government Code section 65858(d), at least 10 days before the 45-day ordinance expires, the City must issue a written report describing the measures it has taken to alleviate the conditions that led to the moratorium. This report satisfies that requirement. The 45-day moratorium expires on October 30, 2026.
