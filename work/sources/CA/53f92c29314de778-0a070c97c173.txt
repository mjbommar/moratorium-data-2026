LEGAL NOTICE


NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING


NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT THE CITY OF PATTERSON CITY COUNCIL will hold

a Public Hearing  at a Regular Meeting  on AUGUST 18, 2026  AT 7:00 P.M.,  or soon thereafter

as a matter may be heard in the City Council Chamber, 1 Plaza, Patterson, CA to review and

consider the  following:

An Urgency Ordinance of the City Council of the City of Patterson Extending Urgency

Ordinance No. 899, Which Established a Temporary Moratorium on Data Center Development,

for an Additional 10 Months and 15 Days Pursuant to California Government Code Section

65858

On July 16, 2026, the City Council adopted Urgency Ordinance No. 899, imposing a temporary

moratorium on the processing and approval of new land use applications for data center

development within the City while the City evaluates potential public health impacts and

considers additional regulations. The moratorium will expire on August 30, 2026, unless

extended by the City Council. At the public hearing, the City Council will consider extending the

moratorium for an additional 10 months and 15 days. The City Council will also consider

issuance of a written report describing the measures taken to alleviate the conditions that led to

adoption of the moratorium, as required by Government Code section 65858.

ALL INTERESTED PARTIES  are invited to attend the Tuesday, August 18th, 2026, public

hearing to express opinions or submit evidence for or against the adoption. Members of the

public may also participate via teleconference by calling 1-669-900-9128, Meeting ID: 693 538

4239, Password:  20995363 or join from a PC, MAC, iPad, iPhone, or android device by using:

https://us02web.zoom.us/j/6935384239?pwd=ZmZwMFdYajRsTjlKTnNTamZCWXFXdz09

At the above noted time and place, testimony from interested persons will be heard and

considered by the City of Patterson City Council before taking action or making any

recommendation on the proposed item. On request, the agenda and the documents in the agenda

packet can be made available to persons with a disability. In compliance with the Americans

with Disabilities Act, the City of Patterson encourages those with disabilities to participate fully

in the public hearing process. Any person requiring special assistance to participate in the

meeting should notify the City Clerk’s Office by calling (209) 895-8012 or in person at 1 Plaza,

Patterson, CA at least forty-eight (48) hours before the meeting.

Written comments are also accepted, before the hearing, by the City Clerk’s office, at 1 Plaza,

Patterson, CA 95363.

If you challenge the Urgency Ordinance in court, you may be limited to raising only those issues

you or someone else raised at the public hearing described in this notice, or in written

correspondence delivered to the Patterson City Council at, or prior to, the public hearing.
