ORDINANCE NO. O2M26-4868

AN ORDINANCE OF THE ALHAMBRA CITY COUNCIL PROVIDING FOR A GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION IN SAID CITY TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 3, 2026, PROVIDING FOR THE CONSOLIDATION OF THE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION WITH THE STATEWIDE GENERAL ELECTION; SAID ELECTION BEING HELD FOR A BALLOT MEASURE TO BAN DATA CENTERS WITHIN THE CITY OF ALHAMBRA 

WHEREAS, the City Council is authorized to submit a measure for voter consideration to enact an ordinance at a regular election pursuant to California Elections Code Section 9222; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has previously banned data centers in the City via City Council Ordinance No. O2M26-4863; and

WHEREAS, other cities in California, such as Monterey Park, have banned data centers through ballot initiatives based upon the negative impacts of such uses, as presented at meetings before the Monterey Park City Council, and as commented upon by residents at Alhambra City Council meetings; and 

WHEREAS, the City Council wishes to submit a ballot measure to the voters of the City of Alhambra to let them decide whether they wish to affirm the data ban via voter initiative to further restrict future changes in legislation governing data centers; and 

WHEREAS, this Ordinance is not subject to further review under the California Environmental Quality Act (“CEQA”) because a CEQA analysis and determination was already made in connection with the ban on data centers adopted via Ordinance No. O2M26-4863 and this ballot measure is therefore not a new project under California Public Resources Code section 20165 requiring further review. Accordingly, it can be seen with certainty that there will be no additional impacts not previously studied under CEQA Guidelines Section 15061(b)(3). 

NOW, THEREFORE, THE ALHAMBRA CITY COUNCIL DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION ONE. The City Council hereby finds that the facts and determinations set forth in the recitals to this ordinance are true and correct and support the City’s Council’s adoption of this ordinance.

SECTION 2. The City Council hereby calls a General Municipal Election on November 3, 2026, for the purpose of submitting the Ballot Measure for consideration of the qualified electors of the City, pursuant to California Elections Code Sections 9222. 

SECTION 3. The City Council hereby orders the submission of the Ballot Measure to the voters of the City of Alhambra at the general municipal election on November 3, 2026; and, subject to approval by a majority vote of the electorate, the City Council hereby approves the ordinance, and the form thereof as set forth on Exhibit “A” (the “Ballot Measure). Upon approval by a majority of the electorate, the ordinance will amend section 23.43.050 of the Alhambra Municipal Code to amend the definition of “Data Centers”.

SECTION 4. The City Council hereby approves, orders and determines: 

A.  That the following question shall be submitted to the voters of the City of Alhambra at the November 3, 2026 general municipal election:

Data Centers Prohibited. Shall an ordinance be adopted affirming the Council adopted ban on “Data Centers” by amending the Alhambra Municipal Code to again define “Data Centers” and prohibit them in all zones of the City? | YES | 
 | NO | 

B.  That the text of the Ballot Measure Ordinance to be submitted to the voters, including the changes to Alhambra Municipal Code Section 23.43.050, is as found in Exhibit “A” hereto.

C.  That the vote requirement for the measure to pass is a simple majority (50% + 1) of the votes cast.  

D.  That the ballots to be used at the election shall be in the form and content required by law.

E.  That the City Council directs the City Clerk to submit to the City Attorney a copy of the ordinance, and the City Attorney is hereby authorized and directed to prepare an impartial analysis of the ballot measure showing the effect of the measure on the existing law and operation of the measure. The impartial analysis shall be submitted by the City Attorney to the City Clerk as required by Elections Code Section 9280 and shall not exceed 500 words in length, and otherwise shall comply in all respects with the applicable provisions of the Elections Code of the State of California.

F.  That the last day for filing direct arguments for or against the measure shall be as set by the County, based upon the consolidation of the election as contemplated in Elections Code Section 10400 and all such arguments shall be filed with the Alhambra City Clerk, 111 South First Street, Alhambra, California, and shall not exceed 300 words in length. The City Clerk, upon receipt of arguments and after the filing deadline, shall immediately transmit copies to any known opposing parties who may then submit rebuttals within the time period established by the County.  If more than one argument is submitted in favor or against the measure, the Clerk shall select the argument based upon the priorities set forth in Elections Code 9287, and if more than one argument shares the same highest priority, the City Clerk shall use a random selection methodology. Arguments received prior to the deadline shall be confidential until the deadline.

G. That the last day for filing rebuttal arguments for or against the ballot measure shall be as set forth in Elections Code 9285 and all such arguments shall be filed with the Alhambra City Clerk, 111 South First Street, Alhambra, California, and shall not exceed 250 words in length.  Arguments received prior to the deadline are confidential until the deadline.

	H.  The ten (10) day public review period for all ballot materials shall open and close pursuant to Elections Code section 9295.

I.  That in all particulars not recited in the Ordinance, the election shall be held and conducted as provided by law for holding municipal elections.

J.  That the City Clerk is authorized to transmit a certified copy of this ordinance to the Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors and the Los Angeles County Registrar of Voters.

	K.  That the City Council, by separate resolution, shall request that the Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors consolidate such Alhambra General Municipal Election with the general statewide election to be held on such date.

SECTION 5: The General Municipal Election hereby called is hereby ordered to be consolidated with the Statewide General Election to be held on November 3, 2026, by the County of Los Angeles; and, upon the same ballot as that provided for said Statewide General Election, there shall appear the Ballot Measure. The election shall be held in all respects as if there were only one election, and the election precincts, polling places and voting booths shall in every case be the same.  There shall be only one set of election officers in each of said precincts, and the Board of Supervisors shall canvass the returns of said election.

SECTION 6: The polls for said election shall be opened at seven o’clock on the morning of the day of the election and shall be kept open until eight o’clock of the evening of the same day when the polls shall be closed, pursuant to Election Code § 10242, except and provided, however, that if at the hour of said closing there are any voters qualified to vote and have not been able to do so since appearing, the polls shall be kept open for a sufficient time to enable them to vote, but no one who shall have arrived at the polling place after eight o’clock in the evening shall be entitled to vote although the polls may be open when he or she arrives.

SECTION 7:   Notice of the time and place of holding said General Municipal Election is hereby given, and the City Clerk is hereby authorized, instructed and directed to give such further and additional notice of said election in time, form and manner as required by laws.

SECTION 8: Inasmuch as this ordinance provides for the calling and the holding of a municipal election, it shall take effect immediately upon the date of its adoption, and the City Clerk shall certify to the adoption of this ordinance and shall cause the same to be published once a day for at least seven (7) days in a newspaper published at least six (6) days a week in the City, or once a week for two weeks in a newspaper published less than six days a week in the City, or posted in three public places in the City for a period of ten days as required by Minute Order No. M2M8-76.

Signed and approved this 27th day of July, 2026.

Exhibit “A”

(Ballot Measure Ordinance)

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF ALHAMBRA AMENDING ALHAMBRA MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 23.43.050 TO DEFINE AND PROHIBIT DATA CENTERS IN ALL ZONES OF THE CITY 

THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF ALHAMBRA DO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. AMENDMENT. Alhambra Municipal Code Section 23.43.050, is hereby amended to repeal the definition of “Data Center” and to adopt a new definition to replace it with the text set forth below. To illustrate the change in the definition that is authorized by this ordinance, Exhibit A (Strikethrough Amendments) shows additions by underline and shows deletions by strikethrough. 

“Data Centers. Facilities used primarily for analyzing, processing, storing, converting, transmitting, distributing, and managing electronic data for on-site or off-site use. These facilities typically house computer, network, and telecommunication equipment, systems, servers, appliances, and other associated components related to electronic data operations. This classification excludes data facilities that are incidental to another primary use and that are strictly for the purposes of supporting the on-site operations of the primary use.  Data Centers are prohibited in all zones of the City.”

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF AMENDMENTS. Unless specifically amended by this ordinance, all other provisions of Alhambra Municipal Code Section 23.43.050 remain unchanged.

SECTION 3. CEQA. This Ordinance is not subject to environmental review under the California Environmental Quality Act ("CEQA") because the City Council already conducted CEQA analysis and made a determination thereon for the adoption of this definition of Data Centers and the prohibition of same in connection with the adoption of Ordinance No. O2M26-4863. This Ordinance is therefore not a new project under Public Resources Code Section 20165 and would not have any additional environmental impacts not previously studied under CEQA Guidelines Section 15061(b)(3). 

SECTION 4. EXECUTION. The Mayor shall sign and the City Clerk shall attest to the passage of this Ordinance upon certification by the City Council of the results of the election approving this Ordinance. 

PASSED AND ADOPTED by the people of the City of Alhambra on November 3, 2026.
