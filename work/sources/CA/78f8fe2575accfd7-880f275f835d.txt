Document Description

Adoption of an ordinance to enact a temporary moratorium on the development of data centers in the City of San Diego. Data centers present a threat to public safety, health and welfare, and a moratorium provides the City with time to study, consider, and potentially adopt regulations to address whether, where, and under what conditions data centers should be allowed within the City.

Notice of Exemption

Exempt Status

Categorical Exemption

Type, Section or Code

CEQA Guidelines Section 15308 (Actions by Regulatory Agencies for the Protection of the Environment)

Reasons for Exemption

The City of San Diego conducted an environmental review that determined the project is exempt from CEQA pursuant to CEQA Guidelines Section 15061(b)(3), as CEQA only applies to projects which have the potential for causing a significant effect on the environment As the proposed temporary moratorium would not result in any direct or reasonably foreseeable indirect physical changes in the environment, it would not be subject to CEQA. Furthermore, the project would not have the potential for causing a significant effect on the environment pursuant to CEQA Guidelines Section 15308 (Actions by Regulatory Agencies for the Protection of the Environment), which applies to actions taken by regulatory agencies to maintain, restore, enhance, or protect the environment; and where the exceptions listed in CEQA Guidelines Section 15300.2 would not apply in that no cumulative impacts were identified; no significant effects on the environment were identified; the project is not adjacent to a scenic highway; no historical resources would be affected by the action; and the project was not identified on a list of hazardous waste sites pursuant to Section 65962.5 of the Government Code.
