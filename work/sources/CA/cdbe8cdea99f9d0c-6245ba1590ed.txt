CITY OF INDIO

NOTICE OF CITY COUNCIL PUBLIC HEARING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the City Council of the City of Indio will hold a public hearing on Wednesday, October 7, 2026, beginning at 5:00 p.m. The public hearing will be held in person at 100 Civic Center Drive, Indio, CA 92201, to consider the following:

Public Hearing Item: Council consideration of a 90-day extension to urgency Ordinance 1838 regarding a moratorium on the establishment, expansion, or intensification of Data Centers and other similar high-intensity computing uses to allow additional time to finalize amendments to the Unified Development Code banning data centers. The City of Indio, as Lead Agency under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), determined the project is categorically exempt based on CEQA guidelines section 15061(b)(3) (general rule exemption).

City Council meetings may be viewed via Livestream, Facebook Live, or YouTube.

Please note: In the case of technical difficulties with the livestream or broadcast, the meeting will proceed as scheduled, provided the physical meeting location remains open and accessible to the public.

Interested persons wishing to express their views on the hearing item(s) referenced above may participate by providing oral or written comments as follows:

Written Comments: Written comments may be emailed to the City Clerk at cityclerk@indio.org. Comments will be distributed to the City Council and posted on the agenda portal. Written comments will not be read during public comment period.

Oral Comments: Oral testimony can be provided in person.

Accessibility Accommodation: If you are an individual with a disability and need a reasonable modification or accommodation pursuant to the Americans with Disabilities Act, please contact the City Clerk at cityclerk@indio.org 24 hours in advance of the hearing for assistance.

Publication Date: September 25, 2026
