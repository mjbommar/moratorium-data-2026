November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election Information - Ballot Measures

MEASURE HT: Proposed Modernization/No Tax Rate Increase Ballot Measure

On July 27, 2026 the Alhmabra City Council placed Measure HT, also known as the City of Alhambra “Modernization/No Tax Rate Increase Measure” on the November ballot for voter consideration. If approved, Measure HT would apply the existing 12% Transient Occupancy Tax that guests in Hotels and Motels currently pay to also include Short term rental properties.

Measure HT will not impact the taxes of Alhambra residents. Measure HT will only apply to visitors who stay in local hotels and short-term lodging to address their impact on our community while here.

MEASURE DB: Proposed Data Centers Prohibited Ballot Measure

On July 27, 2026 the Alhmabra City Council placed Measure DB, also known as the Data Center Ban Measure on the November Ballot. If approved, Measure DB would define and prohibit Data Centers in all zones of the City.
