The Imperial Valley Resource Management Agency (IVRMA) is currently preparing an application
for the California Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery (CalRecycle) Beverage Container

Valley Public Water Refill Station and Blue Bin Reimbursement Program, a regional initiative that
would provide reimbursement funding to eligible public agencies for the installation of public water
refill stations and beverage container recycling ("Blue Bin") infrastructure throughout Imperial
County.

The purpose of this program is to increase public access to drinking water, reduce the use of single-

use plastic beverage containers, expand convenient beverage container recycling opportunities, and

reduce litter in parks, public buildings, community centers, recreational facilities, and other publicly
accessible locations.

Under the proposed program, IVRMA would administer the grant and establish a competitive
reimbursement process. Participating jurisdictions would have the opportunity to submit project
proposals for eligible public facilities. Approved projects would receive reimbursement for eligible
costs in accordance with program guidelines and CalRecycle requirements.

Dear Ms. Galarza:

On behalf of the City of Calipatria, | am pleased to provide this letter in support of the Imperial
Valley Resource Management Agency's (IVRMA) Imperial Valley Public Water Refill
Station and Blue Bin Reimbursement Program and its efforts to secure funding through the
California Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery (CalRecycle) Beverage Container
Recycling Grant Program.

Our agency recognizes the importance of increasing convenient public access to drinking water
while expanding beverage container recycling opportunities throughout Imperial County. The
proposed reimbursement program represents a practical and collaborative regional approach that
will assist local jurisdictions in developing projects that encourage the use of reusable water
bottles, reduce reliance on single-use beverage containers, increase California Redemption Value
(CRV) beverage container recycling, and reduce litter in our public spaces.

The reimbursement model proposed by IVRMA provides local agencies with the flexibility to
identify projects that best meet their community's needs while ensuring that all projects are
administered in compliance with CalRecycle grant requirements. By establishing standardized
application procedures, project evaluation criteria, reimbursement guidelines, and grant oversight,
IVRMA will provide an efficient and accountable framework for implementing these important
environmental improvements throughout the region.

The City of Calipatria supports IVRMA's efforts to implement this regional reimbursement
program and recognizes the long-term environmental and community benefits that public water
refill stations and Blue Bin recycling infrastructure will provide. These improvements will enhance
public amenities, encourage sustainable practices, increase recycling participation, and contribute
to cleaner parks, community facilities, recreational areas, and other publicly accessible locations
throughout Imperial County.
