An Act To Bring a Green Data Center to Maine

CONCEPT DRAFT

SUMMARY

This bill is a concept draft pursuant to Joint Rule 208.

This bill proposes to create a program to encourage the development of a data center in this State that is powered by indigenous renewable energy sources, uses the new so-called "Three Ring Binder" fiber-optic infrastructure and creates jobs in the State.
