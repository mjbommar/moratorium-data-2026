## Public Hearing

September 17, 2026

The Town of Bethel has scheduled a Public Hearing to take questions and comments concerning the 180-day moratorium on Data Centers on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 6:00pm in the Bethel Town Office meeting room at 19 Main Street. Public comments will be heard at the meeting or prior to the hearing date by sending written comments to the Bethel Town Office at:

Bethel Town Office PO Box 1660 Bethel, ME 04217 or [info@bethelmaine.org](mailto:info@bethelmaine.org)
