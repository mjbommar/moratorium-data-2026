MORATORIUM ORDINANCE ON GRID-LEVEL BATTERY ENERGY
STORAGE SYSTEMS, GRID-LEVEL SUBSTATIONS AND GRID-SCALE
WIND ENERGY PROJECTS
TOWN OF ALBION

SECTION ONE
Authority, Definition and Effective Date

Authority. This Moratorium Ordinance is enacted pursuant to 30-A M.R.S.A. §3001, et

seq., 30-A M.R.S.A. §4356 and Article VIII, Part Second, of the Maine Constitution.

Definitions.

1. Grid-level Battery Storage Energy Systems: electrochemical devices that store energy from
the power grid or electricity generation source and release it later to provide electricity or

other grid services.

2. Grid-level Substation: a facility that receives high-voltage power from generation stations

and transforms it to lower voltages for distribution to consumers.

3. Grid-scale Wind Energy Project: a wind energy project that creates one acre or more of

impervious area, such as roads and turbine pads.

Effective Date. This Moratorium Ordinance shall take effect immediately, and

notwithstanding the provisions of 1 M.R.S.A. §302, to the maximum extent permitted by
law, apply to all proceedings, applications, and petitions not pending within the meaning

of 1 M.R.S.A. §302 as of the date of adoption of this Ordinance.

SECTION TWO
Term

This Ordinance shall remain in effect for a period of 180 days from the date of its adoption

and expire on the 180" day unless the select board votes, after notice and hearing, to extend the
moratorium for additional 180-day periods.

SECTION THREE
Need_and Purpose

The Town currently has no ordinances addressing the regulation of Grid-level Battery
Storage Energy Systems, Grid-level Substations or Grid-scale Wind Energy Projects. The purpose

of this moratorium ordinance is to allow the Town a reasonable period of time to evaluate any

concerns raised regarding the installation of such Systems or Projects and to develop necessary
ordinances, or amendments to ordinances, to prevent serious harm. Serious harm includes, but is

not limited to, visual impact, noise, environmental degradation and public health.



SECTION FOUR
Moratorium

During the term of this moratorium, no official, officer, board, body, agency, agent, or
employee of the Town of Albion shall accept; process, or act upon any application for any

" approval, including, but not limited to, a building permit, certificate of occupancy, site plan review,

conditional use, or any other approval related to the establishment of Grid-level Battery Storage
Energy Systems, Grid-level Substations or Wind Energy Projects. No person shall test for the
suitability or develop such Systems or Projects within the Town of Albion during the time this
moratorium is in effect. Thereafter, no person shall test for suitability, or develop such Systems or
Projects within the Town of Albion without complying with applicable Town ordinances.

SECTION FIVE
Enforcement

For any violation of this Ordinance, the select board is hereby authorized to institute all
actions, either legal or equitable, deemed necessary or appropriate to enforce the provisions of this
moratorium ordinance. Any violation of this Moratorium Ordinance is subject to an enforcement
action under 30-A M.R.S.A. §4452. Each day of violation shall constitute a separate offense. All
civil penalties shall inure to the benefit of the Town of Albion. Any violation of this Moratorium
Ordinance constitutes a nuisance.

SECTION SIX
Severability

Should any provision of this Moratorium Ordinance be declared by any court to be invalid,
such decision shall not invalidate any other section.
