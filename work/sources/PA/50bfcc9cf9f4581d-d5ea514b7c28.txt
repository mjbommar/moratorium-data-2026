Sign Unveiling: Mayor Joseph DiGirolamo Senior Center

Friday, October 16, 2026

Mayor Joseph DiGirolamo was surrounded by the Bensalem community including senior center members, family, friends, coworkers, and neighbors as we gathered for a truly meaningful occasion: the official renaming of the center to the Mayor Joseph DiGirolamo Senior Center! This special celebration honored Mayor DiGirolamo’s years of service, his deep connection to Bensalem, and the lasting impact he has made on generations of residents. The center represents more than a building—it is a place where friendships grow, memories are made, and our community comes together. Renaming it in his honor is a heartfelt tribute to the dedication, leadership, and compassion he has shared with Bensalem. Thank you to everyone who joined us to celebrate this important milestone and helped make the day so memorable. Together, we honored not only Mayor DiGirolamo, but also the strong community spirit that makes Bensalem so special. Congratulations, Mayor DiGirolamo, on this well-deserved honor! ❤️
