Commissioner Sortman’s statement:


I think there may be some confusion on my comments last week to the resident who inquired
about Data Centers, so I would like to just clarify.
The county does not receive applications for development except with the 19 municipalities that
are part of our partnership that facilitates their planning and zoning. This is a service that the
following municipalities pay for the service from the county. Bastress, Brown, Cogan House,
Gamble, Jackson, Jordan, Limestone, McHenry, McIntyre, McNett, Mifflin, Moreland, Penn,
Pine, Porter, Cascade, Lewis, Plunketts Creek Townships and Saladasburg Borough. The
commissioners do not see these applications. The County planning department either approves
or denies based upon the County ordinance. Commissioners do not vote on what is allowed or
not allowed in any of the local municipalities. To be clear the commissioners did vote to
approve the ordinances followed by the Planning Department.
Absolutely there can be companies that approach the chamber of Commerce and most definitely
Jason Fink would update all County and State legislators of these. To date none have been
brought to our attention with the exception of the current Muncy Twp one, that we too are only
privy to what the news outlets have shared with us. Muncy Twp is awaiting some guidance,
based upon what new ordinance is added at the county for Data Centers, but since they are not
part of our partnership, they would have to adopt their own ordinance based upon the county’s or
some version of it. All other municipalities in the county will need to do the same. They all will
have the county ordinance available to them for review but must adopt their own and as I said
last week, I urge them to do that quickly. Again, I must repeat, we cannot deny any type of
business, by Pennsylvania law all municipalities must allow any type of business somewhere
within their ordinance but can add conditions.
We are following the Allenwood Data Center very closely, but have no part in any decisions or
input.
Today we approved a “stop gap” on data centers to give us time to create a quality ordinance and
tonight our Planning Commission will meet to discuss such ordinance and get it done. The
meeting will be held in the public meeting room on the third floor of the Third St Plaza at
6:00pm. I encourage you to attend and not just be a keyboard warrior with speculations.
