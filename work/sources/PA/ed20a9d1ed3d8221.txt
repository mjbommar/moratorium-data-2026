DECLARING THE CITY OF WILLIAMSPORT’S ZONING ORDINANCE
SUBSTANTIVELY INVALID INSOFAR AS IT EXCLUDES DATA CENTERS AND
ANCILLARY BUILDINGS OR USES, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO POWER
GENERATING FACILITIES, AND PROPOSING THE PREPARATION OFA
CURATIVE AMENDMENT TO OVERCOME SUCH INVALIDITY

WHEREAS, the City of Williamsport is a Pennsylvania Third Class Optional Charter
Law City which enacted its present Zoning Ordinance (the “Zoning Ordinance”) on April 16,
1998, and has amended it several times since; and

WHEREAS, the City of Williamsport administers the Zoning Ordinance; and

WHEREAS, a data center is a physical facility that houses information technology (IT)
infrastructure for building, running, and delivering applications and services, and stores and
manages the data associated with those applications and services; and

WHEREAS, the residents of the City of Williamsport have come to rely on data centers
for the activities of daily life in the present economy, including to support their online banking,
storing and accessing data from the cloud, using the internet, and queuing ride share services;
and

WHEREAS, in the past few years, major advances in artificial intelligence have required
ever more sophisticated data centers of varying sizes and types to support the immense
computing power needed for the next generation of revolutionary technology; and

WHEREAS, these next-generation data centers require increasing amounts of land,
ancillary structures, a massive amount of energy to operate their equipment which may be
provided by on-site power generating facilities, other ancillary uses, and immense quantities of
water to cool the servers associated with them; and

WHEREAS, data centers can bring revenue and jobs to the area in which they are
located, but can also constitute a burden on a community’s natural resources as well as a massive
footprint in a community; and

WHEREAS, the Council of the City of Williamsport has determined that it is essential to
the well-being and best interests of the residents of the City that data centers be regulated in a

way that recognizes their potential benefit to the community while minimizing potential

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, AND IT IS DECLARED, AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Zoning Ordinance of the City of Williamsport, insofar as it fails to provide
for data centers and ancillary buildings or uses, including but not limited to power generating
facilities, is exclusionary.

2. Insofar as the Zoning Ordinance is exclusionary with respect to data centers and
ancillary buildings or uses, including but not limited to power generating facilities, it is
substantively invalid.

3. Pursuant to the powers vested in it by 53 P.S. §10609.2, the Council of the City of
Williamsport proposes to prepare a curative amendment to validate, or reaffirm the validity of, its
zoning ordinance, and to enact same within one hundred eighty (180) days of the date of
adoption of this Resolution.

4. Within thirty (30) days of the date of adoption of this Resolution, the Council of
the City of Williamsport shall, by resolution, make specific findings setting forth the declared
invalidity of the zoning ordinance which may include (A) references to specific uses which are
either not permitted or not permitted in sufficient quantity; (B) reference to a class of use or uses
which require revision; or (C) reference to the entire ordinance which requires revisions.
