### NOTICE TO THE PUBLIC

Notice is hereby given that all 2026 meetings of the County of Lycoming, including the Board of County Commissioners, the Election Board, the Salary Board, the Retirement Board, and the Board of Assessment Appeals shall be open to the public at the following times, days, and places unless otherwise advertised:

Regular meetings of the Board of Commissioners for the County of Lycoming will be held on Thursdays commencing at 10:00 a.m., prevailing time, except on legal holidays. Meetings will be held in the Lycoming County Commissioners Board Room, third floor of the 3rd Street Plaza, at 33 West 3rd Street, Williamsport, PA 17701.

The Lycoming County Salary Board, the Lycoming County Retirement Board, the and the Lycoming County Board of Assessment Appeals, will meet Thursdays, as necessary during or immediately following the Commissioner’s Meeting.

The Lycoming County Prison Board will meet the second Friday of each month commencing at 8:30 a.m., prevailing time, except on legal holidays, in which event meetings will be held the last workday prior to the holiday. Meetings will be held in Lycoming County Commissioners Board Room, third floor of the 3rd Street Plaza, at 33 West 3rd Street, Williamsport, PA 17701, the Lycoming County Prison, 277 W. Third Street, Williamsport, PA 17701, Lycoming County Pre-Release Center, Montoursville, PA 17754, as the Board deems necessary.
