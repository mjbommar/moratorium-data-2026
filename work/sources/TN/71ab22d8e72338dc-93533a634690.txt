The Hartsville/Trousdale County Regional Planning Commission is charged with establishing the zoning regulations, making recommendations to the County Commission concerning rezoning of property, adopting a regional development plan, reviewing plat designs submitted for the development of property and other duties set out under Tennessee Code Annotated Title 13, Chapter 3, Part 1 (T.C.A. § 13-3-101 et seq.). The Commissions regularly on the second Monday of each month at 7pm in the County Courthouse, unless otherwise stated in publications.

The TN Center for Local Planning presented an overview of the Public Chapter 1044. PC 1044 will go into effect on January 1, 2027 and introduces mandatory timelines and standards for local planning commissions and municipal governments regarding development application reviews, site inspections, and project surety releases.
