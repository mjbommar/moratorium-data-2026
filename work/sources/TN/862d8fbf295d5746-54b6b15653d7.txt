May 21, 2026 Approved by Board of Commissioners

SUBJECT: Consider approval of a Resolution extending the twelve(12)-month moratorium on

accepting all new industrial land use applications and the issuance of building permits

for new construction in the I-2 (Heavy Industrial) zoning district for an additional six

months (Legal Department)

MEETING: City Commission - May 21 2026

DEPARTMENT: Economic Development: Planning Division STAFF CONTACT: Whitney Hodges, Planning Manager


SUMMARY:


The Board of Commissioners approved a twelve month moratorium on accepting all new industrial land use

applications and the issuance of building permits for new construction in the I-2 zoning district on June 5,

2025. This moratorium was to allow staff time to engage an acoustical engineer to conduct a noise study and

consult in the drafting of legislative changes to City regulations. City staff have diligently pursued this process

and have hired an acoustical engineer who has begun a noise study and is still in the process of preparing his

final report. The report will assist in the final changes to the affected City ordinances. The extension of the

moratorium by an additional six months will allow the City an opportunity to finalize its study and

implementation of regulations regarding the impacts and natures any future industrial land uses will impact

development and demographic trends, infrastructure, resources, traffic congestions, neighborhood

characteristics, as well as the environmental and health impacts. The extension of the moratorium is in the

best interest of the health, welfare, and safety of the City and its residents.

A RESOLUTION EXTENDING THE MORATORIUM

A RESOLUTION EXTENDING THE MORATORIUM ON ACCEPTING ALL NEW INDUSTRIAL LAND USE APPLICATIONS AND THE ISSUANCE OF BUILDING PERMITS FOR NEW CONSTRUCTION IN THE I-2 DISTRICT FOR SIX MONTHS


WHEREAS, the City of Johnson City, Tennessee (“City”), a Tennessee municipal

corporation adopted a resolution instituting a moratorium on accepting all new industrial land use

applications and the issuance of building permits for new construction in the I-2 district on June 5,

2025, has been vested with substantial power to regulate the use and zoning of real property for

the purposes of maintaining the health, morals, safety, security, peace, and general public welfare

of the City and its residents, which includes the governmental purpose of implementing moratoria

for the reasons stated herein; and

WHEREAS, the purpose of instituting the moratorium was to allow the City an

opportunity to study, research, analyze and/or assess the likely impacts and nature of any future

Industrial Land uses and so the City could develop and implement new ordinances in accordance

with said study; and

WHEREAS, since the institution of the moratorium City staff has worked diligently to

effectuate the intent of the Board of Commissioners. City staff engaged the services of an

acoustical engineer and began drafting new ordinances based on his ongoing study. At this time,

the acoustical engineer’s scope of work is not complete and drafts of the proposed ordinances will

not be ready to present to the Board of Commissioners until after the current moratorium has

expired; and

WHEREAS, the extension of the moratorium will allow the City to complete the study

and present ordinances that provide a comprehensive plan for any future Industrial Land use

development; and

WHEREAS, said extension is the best interests of the health, welfare, and safety of the

City and its residents, and also wholly consistent with the police and other powers vested in the

City; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City of Johnson City Board of

Commissioners meeting in Regular Session May 21, 2026, that this Board formally institutes an

extension of the moratorium for an additional six (6) months which is hereby imposed, effective

upon the adoption of this resolution, to temporarily halt the acceptance of applications and the

issuance of construction permits for new Industrial Land uses in the I-2 district (including locations

within mixed use projects or planned developments). This is not intended to include applications

for construction permits by businesses currently operating in the I-2 district that are seeking to

renovate and/or expand their existing structures consistent with their established use.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that, notwithstanding the foregoing, this moratorium

extension shall not apply to any Industrial Land use applications or construction permits for the

I-2 district that have already been approved at any stage of the development process by the City,

including any of its boards or departments, nor shall it apply to any application or construction
