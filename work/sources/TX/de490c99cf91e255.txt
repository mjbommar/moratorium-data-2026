ORDINANCE 2025-004 - Authorizing and approving the City of Pasadena, Texas, to offer
an additional benefit of severance pay and health insurance benefit to the new City
Controller, Lindsay Koskiniemi. The additional benefit will include ninety (90) days of
compensation and health insurance benefits after the last date of employment in the event
she is separated by City Council without cause.

Councilmember Ybarra moved, seconded by Councilmember Guerrero that Ordinance
2025-004 be passed on first reading only.

Councilmember Ybarra – Congratulated Lindsay Koskiniemi on being the new City
Controller. Thanked Councilmember Villarreal for the good job he did on the search and
interview process for the new City Controller.
