Liquor License(s) rescind or suspend: Consideration, discussion and possible approval for a request to

rescind or continue suspension of liquor license 206 and 207 issued to Bhupinder Singh and to comply

with HCC 5.32 regarding the revocation process. A copy of  the Sheriff's Notification regarding the license

suspension was included on the online agenda for review (see attached). Sheriff Cardenas reviewed the

matter for the Board and recommended that the license remain suspended until the applicant has a

hearing and we see if  the license holder is convicted; he reviewed the charges against the license holder.

Chief Deputy District Attorney Gordon offered comment on what charges are being filed with the Justice

Court against Bhupinder Singh. Discussion ensued regarding what the Board's options are and what else

will need to occur with this matter. Kyle B. Swanson, legal counsel on behalf of Bhupinder Singh, offered

public comment on behalf  of  his client stating that Mr.  Singh had just learned of  the information regarding

the allegations and categorically denies the allegations, that  these are just allegations and explained what

had occurred noting the lack of  corroborating evidence; he stated that this license is part of Mr. Singh's

livelihood and so he would encourage this Board not to revoke the license based on nothing but

allegations; he stated that  this body either needs to set the matter  for  a hearing  to allow Mr. Singh to have

due process or this Board should wait until the criminal proceedings have occurred. Sheriff Cardenas

stated his recommendation that the license remain suspended until the Court makes a finding. Chief

Deputy District  Attorney Gordon explained that  to maintain  the suspension,  the Board would need to have

a hearing within the next thirty to forty days; he continued explaining how the criminal matter would

proceed through the Courts.

Commissioner Brooks noted her concerns as this is an accusation and her concerns with withholding a

license without  the facts as she thought it  was innocent until proven guilty. Commissioner Evatz noted his

respect for the Sheriff and his recommendation but stated his concurrence with Commissioner Brooks'

concerns with suspending the license until a more robust conversation can occur and his concerns with

what any type of hearing would allow for as the matter would be proceeding through the court process

which might restrict what could be discussed. Discussion ensued regarding how the hearing could be

handled. Commissioner Hill asked the Sheriff he believes that by rescinding the license at this time offers

protections  to the citizens of  the County with Sheriff  Cardenas responding stating  that he does and noting

that there are several allegations which have to be looked at. The following motion was made by

Commissioner Hill:

To follow the Sheriff's 's recommendation and continue with the suspension of  the license.

Page 5 of 9  9

Commissioner Evatz asked the Sheriff how prior service to minors issues have been handled with Sheriff

Cardenas responding noting the difference in this matter  with the additional charges. Discussion ensued

regarding the charges, the time period for the charges and if this individual had been cited before.

Commissioner Cerri offered a  second to Commissioner Hill's motion noting that he does put  weight in the

Sheriff's recommendation . Commissioner Hill stated that a hearing to allow the Board to hear additional

evidence needs to be expedited and that he stands by the Sheriff's recommendation . Mr. Swanson offered

comment as to the Commissioners' respect of  the opinion of  the Sheriff but explained that this Board is

only hearing one side of  the equation and to revoke this license without hearing both sides is a  violation

of  due process so he believes the proper  course of  action is not  to continue with the suspension until  there

is full hearing, either civilly before the Board or criminally to determine if has actually done what he is

alleged to have done. Sheriff Cardenas called Commissioner Hill's motion to a vote and it failed with

Commissioners Hill and Cerri voting aye and Commissioners Tipton, Evatz and Brooks voting nay. Sheriff

Cardenas stated that he will rescind the suspension.

Public Comment: Sheriff Cardenas asked for public comment. Commissioner Evatz asked that whatever

the District Attorney can do to expedite this matter  through the appropriate process would be better as

the sooner we can have a resolution and make a determination if  this needs to be brought back before

this Board, the better. Mr. Swanson noted that the Sheriff was wondering if his client had any public

comment, but he would advise Mr. Singh that he does have a Fifth Amendment right to remain silent with

pending criminal charges and so his advice would be not to say anything at this point. No further public

comment offered.

ordinance. Chairman Cerri called the public hearing to order at 10:15 a.m. and read the title of  the item
