AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM
ACCEPTANCE OF, REVIEW OF, AND/OR ACTION UPON
APPLICATIONS FOR ZONING APPROVALS, CONSTRUCTION |
PERMITS, SITE PLAN APPROVALS, AND ANY OTHER OFFICIAR

DEVELOPMENT AND CONSTRUCTION OF DATA CENTERS, DATA
PROCESSING FACILITIES, CRYPTOCURRENCY MINING
OPERATIONS OR ANY USE ASSOCIATED WITH DATA PROCESSING

CHESTERFIELD COUNTY PENDING THE CONSIDERATION OF
AMENDMENTS TO THE CHESTERFIELD COUNTY CODE OF
ORDINANCES; AND ADDRESSING OTHER MATTERS RELATED
THERETO,

WHEREAS, Chesterfield County Council has observed an increase in the number of data centers
seeking to locate to the State of South Carolina; and ;

WHEREAS, the Chesterfield County Council recognizes that the construction and operation of
such facilities, if not properly regulated, may adversely impact the residents of Chesterfield
County, including but not limited to placing significant demands on the local electrical grid and
water supply, contributing to air pollution, and generating electronic waste and noise pollution;
and

WHEREAS, the Chesterfield County Code of Ordinances does not define ‘data center,’ ‘data
processing facility,’ or ‘cryptocurrency mining operation’; and

WHEREAS, given the risks associated with such facilities, Chesterfield County Council is
informed and believes that the Chesterfield County Code of Ordinances does not provide an
adequate mechanism to regulate and control the location, design, size, density, land use, and
infrastructure needs for data centers, data processing facilities, and cryptocurrency mining
operations; and

WHEREAS, Chesterfield County Council believes that time is needed so that the Council and
staff can study the impacts of data centers, data processing facilities, cryptocurrency mining, and
any other uses associated with data processing facilities on communities, and develop reasonable
regulations that would promote the safety and wellbeing of its citizens; and

WHEREAS, Chesterfield County Council believes that this moratorium will protect the public
interest and welfare of the citizens of Chesterfield County until such regulations regarding the
aforementioned uses are adopted.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY CHESTERFIELD COUNTY COUNCIL:

Section 1. Moratorium. Chesterfield County Council hereby imposes a temporary moratorium
on the acceptance of, review of, and/or action upon applications for zoning approvals, construction
permits, site plan approvals, and any other official county action that would authorize or permit
the development and construction of data centers, data processing facilities, cryptocurrency mining
operations or any other uses associated with data processing facilities within the unincorporated
areas of Chesterfield County. This moratorium shall take effect immediately upon adoption and
shall remain in effect until January 1, 2028, or until such time as the County adopts regulations
governing these uses, whichever occurs first.

Section 2. Definitions. For the purposes of this moratorium, a “data center” or “data processing
facility” is a building, a dedicated space within a building, or group of buildings that houses
computer systems and associated components, such as telecommunication and data processing
systems, to be used for remote storage, processing, or distribution of large amounts of data.

Such uses include but are not limited to, computationally intensive applications such as:
cryptocurrency mining, artificial intelligence (A.I.) computing, weather modeling, genome
sequencing, application hosting, cloud storage, and video and technical streaming services.

These facilities may also include supporting infrastructure such as air handling systems, backup
power generation, water cooling and storage systems, utility substations, and other related
equipment necessary for operations.

Section 3. Development of Reasonable Regulations.

A. County staff, including but not limited to the County Attorney, the Building Codes
Department, and the Economic Development Director, shall develop zoning, land use, and
performance standards, as well as mitigation measures, for data centers, data processing
facilities, cryptocurrency mining operations, or related uses that may adversely affect the
natural environment and the quality of life of County residents.

B. Within the first six months, the Chesterfield County Building Codes Department, in
coordination with the County Attorney and Economic Development Director, shall:

1. Study the impacts of data centers, data processing facilities, cryptocurrency mining,
and any other uses associated with data processing facilities on infrastructure, the
environment, and surrounding communities; and

2. Review regulatory approaches adopted by other jurisdictions within South Carolina
and throughout the United States.

C. Following completion of the initial study period, County staff shall:
1. Develop the land use regulations required to mitigate the negative impacts
associated with land uses described as data centers, data processing facilities,
cryptocurrency mining operations or any other uses associated with data processing

facilities;

2. Prepare a series of text amendments to the Chesterfield County Code of
Ordinances; and

3. Initiate the legislative process for adoption of such amendments. Public input shall
be sought during each part of the process.

Section 4. Severability. Severability is intended throughout and within the provisions of this
Ordinance, If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase or portion of this Ordinance is held
to be invalid or unconstitutional by a court of competent jurisdiction, then that decision shall not
affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance.

Section 5. Repeal. All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict with this Ordinance, or
inconsistent with its provisions, are hereby repealed or superseded to the extent necessary to give
this Ordinance full force and effect.

Section 6. Effective Date. This Ordinance is effective immediately upon adoption.
