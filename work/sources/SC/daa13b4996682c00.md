**June 23, 2026, County Council Meeting**

Sumter County Council approved an emergency 60-day moratorium on battery energy storage systems (BESS), data centers and solar farms. Emergency Ordinance 26-1049 went into effect immediately after it was approved, noted Sumter County Attorney Johnathan Bryan. Members of Council and County Government will study the issue more in-depth.
