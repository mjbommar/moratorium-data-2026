**CHESTER COUNTY COUNCIL APPROVES SIX-MONTH PAUSE ON DATA CENTER DEVELOPMENT**

CHESTER, S.C – During the June 15th, 2026, Chester County Council Meeting, Council voted to approve the first reading of a six-month pause on the acceptance of data center applications. The temporary pause will allow the County the opportunity to continue a proactive approach to data center regulations and to collect additional information regarding data center impacts and opportunities.

Chester County will be strategically using this time for further research, public workshops and expert testimony to evaluate how data centers fit into the County’s long term economic development and land use goals. This advanced learning period follows the recent passage of the County’s data center zoning regulations, becoming one of the first counties in South Carolina to regulate the most pressing aspects of data center development such as water usage, noise pollution and strict setbacks through updated zoning codes.

During the six-month period, Chester County will host a series of educational workshops focused on key topics related to data center development, including:

* Impacts on the regional and local power grid
* Local utility capacity
* Economic impacts for both government and citizens
* Environmental impacts
* Community impacts

County officials emphasize that this pause is not a permanent decision against data centers. Rather, it is an opportunity to gather more information, engage the public, and ensure that any future considerations are made with a full understanding of community impact.

“Chester County is committed to making informed decisions that balance economic opportunity with responsible growth,” said Council Chair Pete Wilson “This pause gives us the opportunity to learn from experts, hear from residents, and continue to develop policies that serve the best interests of our community. “

Information about workshop dates, locations, and public participation opportunities will be announced in the coming weeks.
