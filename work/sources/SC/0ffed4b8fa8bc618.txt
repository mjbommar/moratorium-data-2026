                           STATE OF SOUTH CAROLINA
                      COUNTY COUNCIL FOR FAIRFIELD COUNTY
                               ORDINANCE NO. 856

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING FAIRFIELD COUNTY COUNCIL TO DECLARE A
    TWELVE MONTH TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS IN
     FAIRFIELD COUNTY, SOUTH CAROLINA, TO INVOKE THE PENDING
    ORDINANCE DOCTRINE, AND OTHER MATTERS RELATED THERETO.

        WHEREAS, Fairfield County, like many counties in the State of South Carolina, has had
inquiries regarding the development of Data Centers in Fairfield County; and

       WHEREAS, the exponential growth of these facilities, particularly large-scale campuses,
could potentially create an unprecedented strain on Fairfield County’s essential public resources,
including without limitation, the electric power grid, finite water supply, and local infrastructure;
and

        WHEREAS, Fairfield County Council also recognizes that Fairfield County currently
does not have a regulatory framework or zoning ordinance structure that is sufficient to properly
manage the scale and impact of this industry, necessitating a comprehensive, independent study to
protect the public health, safety and welfare of Fairfield County’s citizens; and

       WHEREAS, Fairfield County wishes to place a temporary moratorium on the permitting,
construction, or development of Data Centers in Fairfield County for no longer than twelve (12)
months following the adoption of this Ordinance and the completion of an independent study on
concerns as stated herein; and

        WHEREAS, a moratorium authorized and implemented in accordance with this Ordinance
is a reasonable, time-limited planning measure.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by Fairfield County Council, in a duly
assembled and noticed meeting this ____ day of ____________, 2026 that:

        Section 1. Whereas. Each “Whereas,” above is confirmed and ratified as a finding of
Fairfield County Council.

        Section 2. Definition. For purposes of this Ordinance, “Data Center” shall mean a facility,
campus of facilities, or array of interconnected facilities which house working computer servers
and that are developed to cool, secure, and connect data associated with computer applications and
servers.

        Section 3. Moratorium. Fairfield County Council does hereby declare a temporary
moratorium on Data Centers no more than twelve (12) months from the Effective Date of this
ordinance, and shall not accept any application or permit for a Data Center, nor take action on any
permit or application for a Data Center which has not received final approval until Fairfield County
establishes, if any, a regulatory framework or zoning ordinance structure through comprehensive

oversight and approval process that is satisfactory to Fairfield County Council.

      Section 4. Prohibition. This prohibition may apply to an application, petition, or
submission seeking approval, review ,or relief for a Data Center including but not limited to:
              a) A zoning map or text amendment specific to a site or user;
              b) Conditional uses, special exceptions, or variances;
              c) Land development plans or subdivision applications;
              d) A building permit or certificate tied to an approval for a Data Center when the
              permit or certificate requires prior land use or zoning approval.

       Section 5. Comprehensive Study.              Fairfield County Council shall conduct a
comprehensive study of the cumulative impacts of Data Center development and shall bring
forward a written report of its findings and policy recommendations for discussion by Council.
Such report shall include:
                a) Findings relating to the effect of Data Centers on energy consumption and grid
       capacity, environment and water supply, health and community well-being, residential
       market and housing prices;
                b) Recommendations, if any, for zoning ordinances that specifies appropriate
       criteria for Data Center locations, which may include guidance regarding residential zones,
       proximity to essential infrastructure, and minimum requirements for environmental
       mitigation and resource efficiency.

      Section 7. Pending Ordinance Doctrine. First reading of this Ordinance shall invoke the
Pending Ordinance Doctrine.

        Section 8. Severability. If any part of this Ordinance is unenforceable for any reason,
then the remainder of this Ordinance remains in full force and effect.

       Section 9. Effective Date. This Ordinance is effective immediately after its enactment by
approval by Fairfield County Council after third reading and public hearing.


ADOPTED BY FAIRFIELD COUNTY COUNCIL THIS ______ DAY OF _____________,
2026.
