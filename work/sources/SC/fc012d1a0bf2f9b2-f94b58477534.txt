ORDINANCE NO. O04

AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A MORATORIUM ON THE
CONSIDERATION AND ISSUANCE OF SPECIAL EXCEPTIONS,
CONDITIONAL USE APPROVALS AND ALL OTHER LAND USE OR
DEVELOPMENT APPROVALS FOR DATA CENTERS.

NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained by the County Council of Colleton County (the
“Council’), the governing body of the Colleton County, South Carolina (the “County”), as
follows:

Section 1 Findings. The Council hereby makes the following findings of fact in
connection with the enactment of this ordinance (this “Ordinance’’):

(a) The Council is empowered pursuant to Section 4-9-25 of the Code of Laws of South
Carolina 1976, as amended, to enact regulations, resolutions, and ordinances not inconsistent with
the Constitution and general law of the State of South Carolina (the “State”), including the exercise
of these powers in relation to health and order in the County or respecting any subject as appears
to the Council necessary and proper for the security, general welfare, and convenience of the
County or for preserving health, peace, order, and good government therein. The Council is
additionally empowered pursuant to Title 6, Chapter 29, Article 5 of the Code of Laws of South
Carolina 1976, as amended (the “Acf”), to regulate land use within the County. Pursuant to the
Act, the Council has enacted the Land Development Regulations of Colleton County, South
Carolina (the “Land Development Regulations’) and the Zoning Ordinance of Colleton County,
South Carolina (the “Zoning Ordinance” and, together with the Land Development Regulations,
the “Land Use Code”) which collectively regulate development and land use within the County.
Capitalized terms used in this Ordinance and not otherwise defined shall have the meaning or
usage given to such term in the Land Use Code.

(b) Pursuant Section 140.08-2 of the Land Use Code, Data Centers are allowed as a
Special Exception in the RD-1, RD-2, and LID Zoning Districts and as a Conditional Use in the
ID and HID Zoning Districts. The Council has determined that it is appropriate to review the areas
within the County where Data Centers are allowed, either as a Special Exception or a Conditional
Use, and the conditions under which Data Centers may be developed, and to further consider
amendments to the provisions of the Land Use Code concerning Data Centers if necessary and
prudent for the security, general welfare, and convenience of the County or for preserving health,
peace, order, and good government therein.
(c) To give the Council, the Colleton County Planning Commission (the “Planning
Commission’), and the County’s staff adequate time to conduct such a review and consider
appropriate amendments to the Land Use Code, the Council has determined that it is appropriate
to implement a temporary moratorium on approvals related to the location of Data Centers within
the County, as set forth in greater detail in Section 2 of this Ordinance.

Section 2 Moratorium on Data Center Approvals. By and through the enactment
of this Ordinance, the Council hereby imposes a moratorium (as described hereby, the
“Moratorium”) on the consideration applications for Special Exceptions and Conditional Uses for
Data Centers, the issuance of approvals for Special Exceptions or Conditional Uses for Data
Centers, and the issuance or approval of other land use applications or approvals, as applicable,
for the development of Data Centers within the County for a period of six months from the date of
the enactment of this Ordinance.

Section 3 Recommendations Regarding Data Center Regulations. During the
pendency of the Moratorium, County staff and the Planning Commission are hereby directed to
study the areas of the County where Data Centers may be an appropriate land use and the
conditions under which Data Centers may appropriately be developed within such areas. The
Planning Commission and County staff are further directed to prepare and recommend appropriate
amendments to the Colleton County Comprehensive Plan 2030 and the Land Use Code concerning
Data Centers.

Section 4 Vested Rights. The Council is informed that there are no proposed
developments or projects involving Data Centers that have progressed beyond the vesting point
for the establishment of vested rights pursuant to Section 14.08-1.060 of the Land Use Code. The
County staff, the Planning Commission, and the County’s Board of Zoning Appeals are hereby
directed to hold in abeyance any applications for Special Exceptions and Conditional Uses
involving Data Centers that have been received and not yet considered by the appropriate
approving-body during the pendency of the Moratorium.

Section 5 Moratorium Extension. The Council hereby finds and determines that the
Moratorium imposed hereby may be extended by resolution of the Council.

Section 6 Severability. If any one or more of the provisions of this Ordinance should
be contrary to law, then such provision shall be deemed severable from the remaining provisions
and shall in no way affect the validity of the other provisions of this Ordinance.

Section 7 Repealer. All ordinances, resolutions or parts of any ordinances or
resolutions inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed to
the extent of the conflict or inconsistency. Nothing in this Ordinance shall be construed to affect
any suit or proceeding impending in any court, or any rights acquired or liability incurred, or any
cause of action acquired or existing, under any act or ordinance hereby repealed; nor shall any just
or legal right or remedy of any character be lost, impaired or affected by this Ordinance, except as
expressly set forth herein and as may be permitted under State law.

Section 8 Effect. This Ordinance shall be effective upon third and final reading by the
Council.
DONE AND ENACTED IN COUNCIL DULY ASSEMBLED, this 6th day of July
2026.
