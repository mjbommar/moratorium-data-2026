                                RESOLUTION # 202 6-012
              TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS
A RESOLUTION TO ADOPT A TEMPORA RY MORATORIUM ON DATA
CENTERS IN CAROLINE COUNTY FOR A PERIOD OF ONE (1) YEAR
PENDING SUBMISSION OF A DATA CENTER REPORT AND
CONSIDERATION AND ADOPTION OF SUCH LEGISLATION AS THE
COUNTY COMMISSIONERS MAY CONSIDER ADVISABLE TO
PROMOTE PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WELFARE.


WHEREAS, the County Commissioners of Caroline County, Maryland (the
“County Commissioners”) are authorized under Article XI-F of the Maryland
Constitution and §9-308 of the Local Government Article to adopt public
local laws in general; and

WHEREAS, the County Commissioners are authorized under the Land Use
Article, Title 4, of the Annotated Code of Maryland to enact and administer
zoning and land use Bills and a Comprehensive Plan; and

WHEREAS, the County Commissioners, pursuant to Local Government
Article Sections 10-324 and 10-328 of the Annotated Code of Maryland,
have the power to enact local laws relating to zoning and planning to
protect and promote public safety and health, and to provide for the
prevention, abatement, and removal of nuisances; and
WHEREAS, a data center is defined as a building or group of buildings
used to house computer systems, computer storage equipment, and
associated infrastructure that businesses or other organizations use to
organize, process, store, and disseminate large amounts of data; and
WHEREAS, the County Commissioners recognize that the data center
industry provides clear economic and tax benefits in Northern Virginia and
other areas of the United States, but the construction and operation
of data centers raise significant concerns for the communities where they
are located, including sustainability, energy and water usage, noise,
vibration, and impacts on residents’ quality of life; and
WHEREAS, given the Federal and State encouragement for expansion of
the industry, and the fact that the Department of Planning and Codes has

received many calls from potential developers of data centers, the County
Commissioners recognize the need for Caroline County to balance the
community’s concerns with the reality of data center industry growth; and
WHEREAS, in the last year, as the public infrastructure and environmental
impacts of data centers have come into focus, it has become clear that
regulation is needed, specifically regarding mitigation of the large amounts
of public utility usage of data centers, such as water and electricity, in order
to maintain the health, safety, and economic wellbeing of County residents;
and
WHEREAS, the County Commissioners: (1) acknowledge the above facts;
(2) understand that the County does not control development within the
County’s municipalities; and (3) find and declare that it is in the County’s
best interest to determine where data centers will be allowed to locate, how
the County will regulate and tax the industry, and what benefits can be
provided to County residents; and
WHEREAS, to leverage the benefits data centers can provide to our local
economy while protecting our environment and quality of life, the County
Commissioners desire to establish a comprehensive approach to the data
center industry, including a definition for data centers, designation of the
zones where data centers will be permitted, and establish bulk and area
regulations, landscaping requirements, and noise limitations for such
centers in the County; and
WHEREAS, in order to maintain a consistent regulatory environment for
data centers, a temporary moratorium on data centers is needed while the
County Commissioners formulate and enact clear regulations; and


WHEREAS, the County Commissioners desire to review the new
Comprehensive Plan, which is expected to be submitted later this year, and
the Public Local Laws of Caroline County, Maryland to consider data
centers throughout the County for public safety concerns associated with
these facilities;
WHEREAS, the County Commissioners desire to impose a one (1) year
moratorium on that which may be contrary or hostile to the above-cited
goals; and

RESOLUTION #2026-012: MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS                               2
WHEREAS, completion of this process is anticipated to occur within the
one (1) year period to allow the results of the studies and the
recommendations of the Department of Planning and Codes to be
considered by the County Commissioners in connection with enactment of
legislation on this subject.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the County Commissioners of
Caroline County, Maryland, that:
       1. Moratorium established. A temporary moratorium on the issuance
          of permits for approval and construction of data center facilities in
          the unincorporated areas of Caroline County shall be and is
          hereby established for a period of one (1) year from and after the
          adoption of this Resolution.

       2. Purpose. The purpose of this Moratorium is to protect the public
          health, safety and welfare of the residents of Caroline County and
          visitors to the County.

       3. Study, recommendations, and legislation. This temporary
          moratorium is established to allow sufficient time within which to
          complete the pending study, finalize recommendations, and enact
          appropriate legislation:

               a) to promote public safety by ensuring that there is adequate
                  water and energy for such facilities before such facilities are
                  installed that may be contrary or hostile to such goals;

               b) to develop suggestions to minimize any adverse effects or
                  impacts to public health and property arising from the
                  installation of data centers in the unincorporated areas of
                  Caroline County;

               c) to consider restrictions on the siting of data centers in the
                  County;

               d) to obtain such expert advice and assistance as necessary;



RESOLUTION #2026-012: MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS                                    3
               e) to consider such other matters as the Planning Commission
                  or County staff deem appropriate.

       4. New and pending applications. The County hereby declares a one
          (1) year moratorium on the application for new data centers to be
          located in the unincorporated areas in Caroline County. For the
          period of one (1) year immediately following the effective date of
          this Resolution: (1) the authority to issue or grant a permit for a
          data center to be located in the unincorporated areas of Caroline
          County is hereby suspended; and (2) a County agency or
          department, including the Department of Planning and Codes, the
          Planning Commission, the Board of Zoning Appeals, and the
          Zoning Administrator shall not accept any application, petition, or
          request for a hearing regarding a data center to be located in the
          unincorporated areas of Caroline County, including an application
          for any permit regarding such a data center, and shall not process,
          interpret, deliberate upon, decide or approve any application for
          the installation of data centers subject to this Resolution.

       5. This moratorium shall apply to all currently pending and future
          applications for data centers to be located in the unincorporated
          areas in Caroline County, Maryland. This moratorium does not
          apply to any currently pending or future applications for data
          centers to be located within an incorporated municipality, and
          County staff may respond to requests for information and input,
          and participate in public hearings on such applications as if this
          moratorium were not in effect.

       6. Effective date. This temporary moratorium shall be effective upon
          adoption of this Resolution.

       7. Authority. This Resolution was adopted in accordance with the
          authority authorized under the Local Government Article and the
          Land Use Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that staff, Boards and Commissions
are directed and requested to act in a manner consistent with the provisions
of this moratorium regarding the activity covered herein;


RESOLUTION #2026-012: MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS                               4
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Caroline County Department
of Planning and Codes is directed to review the current County laws and
regulations which may apply to data centers and submit a report and
recommendations to the County Commissioners, no later than April 1,
2027, which:
 (a) provides a general summary of the permitting of data centers in
Maryland and a comparison of the relevant County regulations to other
Maryland jurisdictions;
 (b) provides a general assessment of the average electricity and public
water usage of data centers, broken into relevant categories, such as the
size and location of the data center;
 (c) compares the average electricity and public water usage of a data
center to other uses of a similar size currently permitted in the same zones
and locations as data centers;
 (d) community benefits that can be derived from the data center industry;
and
 (e) makes recommendations on:
  (1) appropriate regulations or policies to mitigate the increased demand
for electricity and public water of a data center compared with other
permitted uses of a similar size, including a ban on data centers, if
appropriate;
  (2) the appropriate siting of data centers, including, but not limited to,
considerations for underserved and overburdened communities,
agricultural land, and undeveloped land;
  (3) changes to the Caroline County Zoning Regulations regarding data
centers, including bulk, area, and size limitations;
  (4) changes to data center permitting requirements;
  (5) appropriate community notice and outreach requirements for data
centers; and
  (6) in conjunction with the Department of Finance, taxation of data
centers and whether data centers should be eligible for County grants,
loans, and tax credits, and, if so, appropriate considerations.

RESOLUTION #2026-012: MORATORIUM ON DATA CENTERS                               5
AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that nothing in this Resolution 2026-
012 shall be interpreted as waiving, or in contradiction of, any authority or
power existing in or granted to the County Commissioners of Caroline
County, Maryland to institute such a moratorium at any time, or extend such
moratorium, to protect the health, safety, and welfare of the County and its
residents, and that to the extent of such power, this moratorium shall be
effective immediately.
