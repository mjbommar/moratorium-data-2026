                     RESOLUTION #2025-008
A RESOLUTION TO EXTEND THE MORATORIUM ENACTED
BY RESOLUTION #2023-011 TO ADOPT A TEMPORARY
MORATORIUM ON BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS IN
CAROLINE COUNTY THROUGH MARCH 31, 2026 PENDING
CONSIDERATION AND ADOPTION OF SUCH LEGISLATION
AS THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS MAY CONSIDER
ADVISABLE TO PROMOTE PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, AND
WELFARE.

WHEREAS, on June 13, 2023, the County adopted a (1) one-year
moratorium on Battery Energy Storage Systems (“BESS”) in Caroline
County, pursuant to Resolution #2023-011; and,

WHEREAS, the County extended the moratorium through March 31,
2025, pursuant to Resolution #2024-012; and,

WHEREAS, the extended moratorium will expire before the effective
date of any new legislation that may be adopted although the reasons
which prompted the moratorium still exist; and,

WHEREAS, the very purpose of the moratorium may be undermined
were the moratorium to expire before the effective date of new
legislation being considered; and,

WHEREAS, the legislative changes proposed deserve careful study and
consideration by the Caroline County Commissioners and the public;
and,

WHEREAS, the County has become aware of legislative bills proposed
or pending before the General Assembly which may affect the County’s
ability to regulate the siting, construction and operation of BESS
facilities.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the County
Commissioners of Caroline County, Maryland, that:
      1. The moratorium previously adopted by Resolution #2023-011
         and extended by Resolution #2024-012, shall be and is hereby
         extended through March 31, 2026.

      2. All the original terms, provisions, and conditions of Resolution
         #2023-011 shall be and are hereby extended accordingly.

      3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon the
         date of its adoption.



ADOPTED/EFFECTIVE: February 11, 2025
