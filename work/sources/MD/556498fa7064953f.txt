EXECUTIVE ORDER NO.: 05-2026
EFFECTIVE DATE: SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

TEMPORARY HOLD ON ACCEPTING, CONSIDERING, AND PROCESSING
APPLICATIONS FOR CRITICAL DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE FACILITIES AND
CRITICAL DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE ELECTRIC SUBSTATIONS

WHEREAS, communities throughout the nation are addressing the necessity of regulating
the data center industry across all levels of government to safeguard access to water, clean air,
affordable and reliable energy, and overall quality of life.

WHEREAS, Frederick County Government has enacted laws regulating light, vibration,
and sound from data centers, increasing setbacks for data centers near residential neighborhoods,
and making data centers the most regulated industry in the county.

WHEREAS, the authority to regulate air quality, energy generation, and electricity use lies
with the State of Maryland, pursuant to the Maryland State Environment Article and Public
Utilities Article.

WHEREAS, in December 2025, Chapter 10 of the Acts of the Maryland General Assembly
2025 Special Session, Data Center Impact Analysis and Report, went into effect, requiring the
Department of Legislative Services to conduct an analysis of the likely environmental, energy, and
economic impacts of data center development in the State and issue a final report on or before
September 1, 2026; however, that report has not yet been completed.

WHEREAS, on July 1, 2026, I issued Executive Order 04-2026 placing a temporary hold
on accepting, considering, or processing applications for critical digital infrastructure facilities and
critical digital infrastructure electric substations, and now find it necessary to replace that Order
and extend the temporary hold.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, JESSICA FITZWATER, COUNTY EXECUTIVE OF
FREDERICK COUNTY, MARYLAND, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME
BY THE CHARTER OF FREDERICK COUNTY, MARYLAND, AND THE LAWS OF THE
STATE OF MARYLAND, HEREBY ISSUE THIS EXECUTIVE ORDER, EFFECTIVE
IMMEDIATELY.

TEMPORARY HOLD ON ACCEPTING, CONSIDERING, AND PROCESSING
APPLICATIONS FOR CRITICAL DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE FACILITIES AND
CRITICAL DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE ELECTRIC SUBSTATIONS

The Frederick County Planning and Permitting Division staff is directed to temporarily
discontinue accepting, considering, processing, or reviewing any applications for Critical Digital
Infrastructure Facilities and Critical Digital Infrastructure Electric Substations, as determined by
County Staff.

This Executive Order does not apply to applications for Critical Digital Infrastructure
Facilities and Critical Digital Infrastructure Electric Substations on a property that has obtained
any approved Adequate Public Facilities Ordinance Letter of Understanding prior to June 17, 2025.

This Executive Order supersedes Executive Order 04-2026.

This Executive Order is issued on this 14° day of September, 2026, is effective
immediately, and shall expire on July 1, 2027, unless superseded or terminated earlier.
