                                         ORDINANCE

        IMPOSING A TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON SITE PLAN APPROVALS
                     FOR DATA CENTER DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS


WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Calvert County, Maryland (hereinafter, the
"Board of County Commissioners" or “Board”), has authority under Title 4 of the Land Use
Article of the Maryland Annotated Code, to adopt zoning and land use regulations to promote
the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Calvert County;
WHEREAS, the County’s Department of Planning and Zoning received an application for a
large-scale data center development within Calvert County and the Board anticipates the
County receiving additional applications and proposals for large scale data center development
within the County;
WHEREAS, the development of large–scale data centers is a relatively new and evolving land
use in many jurisdictions, including Calvert County, and local governments are working to
understand their long-term impacts on land use, infrastructure, and surrounding communities;
WHEREAS, data centers may involve substantial land disturbance, electrical and mechanical
equipment, backup generators, cooling systems, stormwater and wastewater considerations,
noise, heat, water demand, and other environmental and infrastructure impacts that warrant
careful review;
WHEREAS, the Board finds that additional time is needed to review regulations concerning
data center development and to prepare and consider related zoning ordinance text
amendments;
WHEREAS, the Board finds that a temporary moratorium on site plan approvals for data
centers is necessary to preserve the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Calvert County
while the County studies potential impacts and considers appropriate regulatory standards;
WHEREAS, the Board intends that the moratorium be temporary and limited in duration to six
(6) months from the effective date of this Ordinance;
WHEREAS, a public hearing regarding this Ordinance was duly advertised in at least one
newspaper of general circulation for two consecutive weeks before the public hearing and
held on August 18, 2026, at which time the County Commissioners of Calvert County,
Maryland received public comment; and

WHEREAS, upon due consideration of the comments of the public and staff and in the
furtherance of the public health, safety and welfare, the County Commissioners of Calvert
County, Maryland find it is in the best interest of the citizens of Calvert County to impose
a temporary moratorium on site plan approvals for data center development projects.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Board of County Commissioners of Calvert
County, Maryland, that the Calvert County Code is hereby amended by adding a new
temporary Chapter 145 to read as follows:

Chapter 145: Temporary Moratorium on Data Center Site Plan Approvals.
A. Findings; Purpose.
  (1) The findings and recitals set forth in Ordinance No. 26-____ are incorporated herein as
  legislative findings.
  (2) The purpose of this Ordinance is to temporarily suspend approval of site plans for data
  centers in order to provide the County with an opportunity to review and adopt regulations
  and zoning ordinance text amendments related to data centers use conditions, and perform
  any needed studies or analysis related to the same.
B. Definitions.
For purposes of this Ordinance, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings
stated:
  (1) Data Center. An establishment, facility, building(s), or series of buildings which are part
  of a unified development and arranged in a campus setting engaging in the storage,
  management, processing, or transmission of digital data, and housing computer or network
  equipment, systems, servers, appliances, and other associated components related to digital
  data operations.
  (2) Site Plan. Any concept, preliminary, final, amended, or revised site plan, or equivalent
  development plan, submitted for County review or approval under the Calvert County Zoning
  Ordinance or applicable implementing regulations.
  (3) Site Plan Approval. Any approval, endorsement, signature, certification, or action that
  constitutes approval of a Site Plan by the Calvert County Planning Commission.
  (4) Moratorium Period. The period beginning on the effective date of Ordinance No. 26-____
  and ending on the earlier of: (a) six (6) months after the effective date; or (b) the date the
  Board terminates the moratorium by resolution upon finding that all necessary conditions of
  this moratorium have been satisfied.
C. Moratorium Imposed.
  (1) During the Moratorium Period, no approval shall be granted for any site plan proposing a
  data center.
  (2) This Ordinance applies to any pending or new site plan approval requested during the
  Moratorium Period.
D. Permits Dependent on Site Plan Approval.
During the Moratorium Period, the County shall not issue any grading permit, building permit,
or other County permit for a new Data Center.
E. Enforcement.

Any site plan approval issued in violation of this Ordinance is voidable to the fullest extent
permitted by law. The County may enforce this Ordinance through any lawful means, including
withholding approvals or permits that are dependent upon site plan approval.
F. Sunset; Automatic Repeal.
This Ordinance is temporary and shall automatically be repealed upon expiration of the
Moratorium Period as defined in subsection B (4).
G. Amendments.
Any amendment, extension, reduction or other substantive change to the Ordinance shall be
subject to the same public notice, public hearing, and adoption process required for adoption
of this Ordinance.
BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED by the Board of County Commissioners of Calvert County,
Maryland that, in the event any portion of this Ordinance is found to be unconstitutional, illegal,
null or void, by a court of competent jurisdiction, it is the intent of the Board of County
Commissioners to sever only the invalid portion or provision, and that the remainder of the
Ordinance shall be enforceable and valid, unless deletion of the invalid portion would defeat
the clear purpose of the Ordinance, or unless deletion of the valid portion would produce a
result inconsistent with the purpose and intent of the Board of County Commissioners in
enacting this Ordinance.
BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED by the Board of County Commissioners of Calvert County,
Maryland that the foregoing recitals are hereby incorporated and adopted as if fully set forth
herein.
BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED by the Board of County Commissioners of Calvert County,
Maryland that this Ordinance shall be effective following recordation and publication of a fair
summary, but not sooner than ten (10) days following adoption.
