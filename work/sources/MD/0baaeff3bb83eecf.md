On September 14, County Executive Fitzwater [rejected](https://frederickcountymd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/375040/DRRA-Letter-091426) the application for a Development Rights and Responsibilities Agreement sought by Catellus for the Frederick Digital Campus in Adamstown. She also signed an [Executive Order](https://www.frederickcountymd.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/17236) extending the pause on data center applications until July 1, 2027. \_\_\_

Frederick County is not accepting new applications for new data center developments.

On July 1, 2026, County Executive Jessica Fitzwater ordered a pause on new data center development until December 31, 2026, after the State of Maryland is scheduled to complete its Data Center Impact Analysis and Report. Learn more by reading the [executive order](https://frederickcountymd.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=17112) and the [state law](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2025S1/chapters_noln/Ch_10_sb0116E.pdf) requiring the report. The pause does not apply to properties that received any development approvals prior to the County Council’s consideration of CB 25-09 on June 17, 2025, and the pause does not stop work on projects currently under construction. View [this map](https://frederickcountymd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/374476/CDI-approved-site-plans-July-2026) to learn more.

County Executive Fitzwater also ordered greater transparency around data center power consumption by requiring proof of registration with the Maryland Public Service Commission’s large load customer registry as a condition of the development review process. Learn more by reading the [executive order](https://frederickcountymd.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=17111). Frederick County has continually prioritized the health and safety of its residents with a comprehensive approach to managing data center development. As a result, data centers are the most heavily regulated industry in the County.
