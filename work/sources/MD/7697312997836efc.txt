(1) the Department of Public Works:

(2) the Depariment of Housing and Community Development;

(3) the Commission on Sustainability,

(4) the Department of Finance;
(5) the Office of Equity and Civil Rights

(6) the Baltimore City Health Department; and

(7) the Baltimore Development Corporation.

(111) a comparison of data center fiscal returns relative to altemative

uses of target industrial and commercial sites; and

(iv) a review of community benefit agreement structures used in
comparable jurisdictions;

(3) an analysis of the environmental health impact a data center would have on
