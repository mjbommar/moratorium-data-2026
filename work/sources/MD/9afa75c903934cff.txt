                            By the County Council, August 3, 2026


                                              A BILL
                                             ENTITLED

AN ACT concerning

      Data Centers – Extension of Temporary Controls Enacted Under Bill 3-26



FOR   the purpose of extending the temporary controls enacted under Bill 3-26 that suspended

      the permitting of data centers until the earlier of January 1, 2027 or 90 days after a report

      is submitted to the County Council by the Planning Board to December 31, 2027; and

      generally relating to data centers.



BY    repealing and re-enacting, with amendments

      Section 2 of Bill 3-26
