## Safety and replay details

The initial Ohio pilot accidentally repeated six searches after a script error.
Its report records the cap breach and overwritten first responses. Subsequent
workers must use `scripts/research_client.py`, which reserves attempts in a locked,
persistent ledger before network access, writes uniquely named artifacts, retains
failed reservations and records response exchanges with credential redaction.

Active SQLite files are on the verified local NVMe root filesystem; durable bodies
and new bulk job outputs are on `/data0`. The store blob symlink and device/free-space
preflight are recorded in `work/audit/storage-layout-2026-11-05.json`.
The 113 initial pilot files have hash-verified copies under
`/data0/moratorium-refresh-2026/pilot-originals/`. Use
`work/audit/pilot-originals-restore-2026-11-05.json` to restore ignored source bytes
before source-backed normalization replay tests in another checkout. Full private
article bodies and provider responses must stay out of Git.

The release environment `.venv` supplies Markdown, pymdown-extensions and jsonschema.
Research scripts use the existing bc-modules interpreter. Keep these environments
distinct; installing into the shared research environment was unnecessary.
