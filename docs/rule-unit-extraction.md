# Direct rule-unit extraction (experimental)

This opt-in Python interface sends the **entire supplied source text** once per externally reviewed local rule-unit slot through the `bc_llm` backend's typed `generate` interface. It does not call the bc-evidence claim selector, apply its 12-claim ceiling, join units, promote records, or determine legal status. The one-slot response is either one `NativeConditionClause` and supporting quotations or an unresolved explanation. An unknown expression is retained but counted as unresolved. A packet includes every planned slot, including slots not reached after a failure or cap. Candidates are always `accepted: false` and `review_required: true`.

## Plan and preflight

Supply a document dictionary with `text`, its UTF-8 `text_sha256`, and the original payload `body_sha256`. The caller is responsible for checking that the text faithfully represents that payload, including OCR and page coverage. The plan is a JSON-compatible object with exactly `source_text_sha256` and `units`; each unit has exactly `unit_id`, `anchor_quote`, `label`, and `instruction`. IDs are local to this plan. Anchor quotes must occur exactly once, and IDs and anchors must be distinct. All strings must be nonempty. The plan must be independently reviewed to contain neutral labels and instructions: no canonical IDs, golden thresholds, expected answers, or answer leakage. Structural checks cannot establish neutrality. No reviewer identity or legal conclusion is manufactured by the program.

Before any live evaluation, a separate reviewer must inventory the whole source, including dispersed definitions, exceptions and cross references, and compare that inventory with the plan. A reviewed plan is not evidence that the inventory is complete. Freeze development sources and plans separately from unseen holdout sources; do not tune on holdout outcomes. A source byte ceiling rejects oversized sources before requests; it is neither truncation nor evidence of completeness. Context-window and output limits must be chosen conservatively: provider errors, output truncation or semantically incomplete output do not establish coverage.

## Invocation and controls

Import `scripts/extract_rule_units.py` with `scripts` on `PYTHONPATH` and call `await run(directory, doc, plan, backend=backend, model_identity=identity, today=date, max_calls=N, max_usd=cap, reservation_usd=per_call, max_source_bytes=ceiling, settings=settings, storage_policy=policy)`. Use an explicitly admitted private directory; the optional `PrivateRunStorage` policy adds a retained-byte budget. `identity` must include the resolved `model` and enough provider/configuration identity for the caller to reproduce the backend; `settings` are forwarded to `generate`. The caller must ensure identity and settings actually describe the configured backend. The returned model is checked, but this adapter cannot independently inspect every backend setting or provider revision. The explicit date is context only, not a legal cutoff inferred by the model.

The file lock serializes each workspace. Immutable context binds the document, plan, model identity, date, role, settings, schema, prompt, implementation and condition transport hashes, source ceiling, caps, and storage policy. Changed context refuses reuse without modifying retained state or packet; use a separately authorized workspace and budget for a new run. Successes have request and output hashes and are revalidated before replay. Every call reserves a request and dollars durably before dispatch. Failures, malformed responses and cancellations retain their full reservation. Successes charge their reported cost; an unpriced, invalid, over-reservation or model-mismatched result stops the workspace for reconciliation. Request caps count calls even when a success costs less than its reservation. This local ledger is not an invoice or authentication mechanism and cannot guarantee costs within a reservation; an overrun is detected only after the response.

Provider HTTP retries are a separate budget surface. For live use, configure and verify `ControlledModelTransport`, backend model-request limits, SDK retries, and backend dollar controls as described in [controlled-model-transport.md](controlled-model-transport.md). This function does not automatically install or verify those controls. Do not use an unobserved backend for a live run. Use the same frozen context and persistent caps for the HTTP ledger; backend and network retries cannot be inferred from this adapter's call count.

The packet records source, body, plan, context and request hashes; exact quote spans; per-slot outcome; validation flags; and planned, candidate, unresolved and attempt counts. Quote locations must be unique and exact. The strict condition validator runs independently of native transport, but schema validity and a located quote do not establish entailment, completeness, correct scope, source authenticity, or legal effect. Independent source reviewers must inspect the full source and each clause, supporting quotation, definition, condition and exception. Do not interpret a successful model response or complete slot count as a complete source inventory. No canonical write or acceptance is performed.

## Sealed ledger and private workspace

The workspace directory must be owned by the caller and inaccessible to group and other users. Existing public directories or relevant files, symlinks in the path or workspace artifacts, and hard-linked artifacts are rejected. New workspace directories and files use private modes (0700 and 0600). The optional `PrivateRunStorage` must have the workspace strictly below its root; its description is included in immutable context and its byte accounting applies to writes. Supply a private policy root as well. The default writer uses same-directory atomic replacement, file and directory fsync, and 0600 temporary files. These checks assume the private directory and its ancestors are not being concurrently manipulated by a hostile local principal; they are not an adversarial filesystem sandbox.

The state and separately stored SHA256 seal are verified together, then every attempt, request, charge, accounting record, stop, success and output is structurally checked before dispatch. Writes replace state and seal separately; a crash between them produces a mismatch requiring explicit reconciliation. A reserved attempt left by interruption retains its full reservation and blocks further dispatch until reconciliation. If a state or seal write fails after dispatch, the prior reservation or a mismatched pair blocks another call. If only the later packet write fails, the sealed response remains authoritative and the packet can be rebuilt. Missing state or seal fails closed, including deletion of both while the workspace lock remains. A local unkeyed seal detects independent corruption, not coordinated editing of state and seal or rollback/deletion of all workspace evidence. Maintain an independently protected external audit trail if those threats matter. This is not a transactional proof or invoice.

The packet is a disposable derivative of the validated state, regenerated after validation and before a budget stop. Its prior bytes are not authenticated; preserve a copy elsewhere if a historical packet must be retained. Context mismatch and preflight rejection do not regenerate it or alter retained state. A crash may leave the packet absent or stale until a successful validated restart. Each unit has `not_attempted`, `reserved`, `failed`, or `succeeded` status; reserved means the budget was reserved and does not prove dispatch. Optional document identifiers, title and URL supplied by the caller are copied into provenance. The body digest and its relationship to extracted text remain caller assertions, not independently verified. The plan binds the exact text; document and body identity bind the workspace context.

The full source is a stable prompt prefix followed by the per-unit locator; this makes no claim about cache pricing. The prompt asks for typed structure where supported and text predicates where qualification or negation cannot be represented. Neither that instruction nor source location establishes semantic correctness. The generic backend interface does not verify configured tools, retries or settings: settings and model identity are caller metadata, may be ignored by the backend, and must contain no secrets.
