# Rule-unit trial scoring

`scripts/score_rule_unit_trial.py` implements the extraction scoring rules in the [frozen Virginia protocol](/data0/moratorium-refresh-2026/full-scope-closeout-2026-11-07/next/prospective-va-holdout/protocol-preparation/PROTOCOL.md). It consumes retained native audit, packet, source, reference and independent judgments; it makes no model calls. The original VA run is exposed, failed and spent. This tool does not authorize another trial or establish that any population is an unexposed holdout.

For the `holdout` population, the extraction threshold decision requires all of: at least 95% of generated clauses supported, at least 95% of change units completely covered, at least 95% of change slots covered, and zero high-impact errors. Integer comparisons are used; empty gated denominators fail. Controls enter clause support and error accounting. Pooled and control unit/slot coverage and calibration results are diagnostics, not additional threshold gates. The optional `calibration` split is development data, not an original VA requirement. A later experiment must explicitly authorize its own design and account for actual development and exposure. Family labels and hashes do not prove independence or non-exposure.

Every retained top-level clause is counted before review joins, including invalid or unresolved responses and extra packet units. Missing or unparseable output makes clause enumeration incomplete; the count is then a known lower bound and `denominator_complete` is false. Missing, invalid or uncertain review makes the affected split's metric `pass` and `thresholds_pass` null, and makes `high_impact_complete` false. Known counts remain visible. No `no_clause` judgment may describe a generated clause. Frozen units and slots remain in coverage denominators for missing reviews or outputs. A covered slot must independently express the full proposition and qualifications with valid source and clause evidence; an unrelated unsupported assertion does not erase it. A clause remains unsupported if any assertion or qualification is unsupported or its quotations are not uniquely literal.

`threshold_result` is an extraction result, not P4 acceptance. `acceptance` remains `not_scored` after a threshold pass, and `p4_acceptance` is always `not_scored`: the broader required discovery, source recovery, election, polarity, temporal, behavioral and other research-plan gates have not been established. A mechanical extraction failure is reported as `fail`. The scorer cannot independently prove semantic entailment, completeness of source inventories, chronology, independence, current legal force, or the authenticity of its externally supplied evidence.

## Inputs and execution

All files are UTF-8 JSON with unique object members. Independently retain the SHA-256 anchors computed by `digest` in the script: canonical Python JSON with sorted keys, `ensure_ascii=False`, `allow_nan=False`. Pins detect changes, not backdating or independence. Keep source and review data private.

```sh
python scripts/score_rule_unit_trial.py --protocol protocol.json --protocol-anchor SHA256 \
  --cases cases.json --judgments judgments.json --judgment-anchor SHA256
```

The CLI exits 1 for fail or not-scored and 2 for invalid input. The stdout JSON contains split metrics, per-unit and per-family pooled/change/control counts and rates, evidence, gaps and final P4 status. Review incomplete flags before interpreting counts. Quotes in output make this a private report.

`protocol.json` uses `schema_version: rule-unit-scoring-protocol-1`, `freeze_time`, `first_dispatch_time`, `reference_provenance`, `exposure_provenance`, and `cases`; optional `calibration_complete_time` is descriptive. Times include time zones; freeze must precede dispatch. Each case has `case_id`, `split` (`holdout` or `calibration`), `family`, `source_text_sha256`, `source_body_sha256`, `context_sha256`, `plan_sha256`, `reference_sha256`, `role`, `exposure` (a disclosure, not a certification) and `units`. Each unit has `unit_id`, `kind` (`change` or `control`) and a nonempty `slots` array of objects with `slot_id`. Freeze the complete inventory, including dispersed qualifications. Group related documents conservatively and independently document any exposure; explicitly disclose development informed by earlier results.

`cases.json` is an array of `{case_id, audit, packet, document, reference, context}`. The audit is native prospective audit stdout; the packet is the retained packet it reconciled. Context includes the full document, plan, model, settings, schema, system and role; document includes `text`, `body_sha256` and `reviewed_source_role`. Reference has `source_text_sha256`, `source_body_sha256`, `role`, `provenance`, and `units`. Each reference unit has `unit_id`, `slots`, and `nonextractable_issues` (an explicit empty array when none). Each slot has `slot_id`, `evidence`, a `proposition`, a `qualifications` array (empty when none), and `source_locators`. Each nonextractable issue has `field`, `issue`, and `source_locators`. Locators are objects `{quote, start, end}` with a nonempty exact Unicode quote, zero-based character offsets, exclusive end, and a unique occurrence in the supplied source. Independently establish that locators actually support the stated proposition; the reader can only check coordinates.

`judgments.json` has `schema_version: rule-unit-scoring-judgments-1` and `cases`; each case has `case_id`, `audit_sha256`, `packet_sha256`, and `units`. Each reviewed unit has `unit_id`, `output_sha256`, `reference_sha256`, `support` (`supported`, `unsupported`, `uncertain`, `no_clause`), `high_impact` (`yes`, `no`, `uncertain`), `provenance` (nonempty `reviewer`, `independence_basis`, `source_evidence`, `adjudication`), `slots`, `errors`, `disagreements`, and `nonextractable_reviews`. Empty inventories are explicit arrays.

Each slot decision has `slot_id`, `decision` (`covered`, `uncovered`, `uncertain`), `evidence`, `rationale`, and `source_locators`. A covered slot also requires `clause_evidence`, an exact nonempty substring in the serialized generated clause. Source locators and reviewer rationale must establish the full slot independently of whole-clause support. Each nonextractable review has matching `field`, `assessment` and `source_locators`; reviewers must identify unsupported definitive guesses as errors when consequential. Each high-impact error has `category`, `description` and `source_locators`; supported categories are `polarity_modal_reversal`, `scope_actor_misbinding`, `missing_exception_condition`, `incorrect_threshold_date`, `unsupported_legal_status`, and `invented_consequential_fact`. `high_impact: yes` requires one or more specific errors and `no` requires none. Each disagreement records `issue`, `status: resolved`, `resolution`, a distinct second `adjudicator`, their `independence_basis`, and `source_locators`. An unresolved dispute must remain unscorable. These fields record claims requiring substantive independent review; nonempty text is not proof.

The output lists every occurrence and zero-based offsets for each clause `source_text` and supporting quote, including absent or ambiguous quotes, alongside per-unit slot judgments, nonextractable reviews, errors and disagreements. Quotes alone do not establish date, actor, polarity, role, or semantic support. In particular, proposed text must not be treated as enacted, and original dates do not establish current legal force. Audit pins and role checks are mechanical checks and do not replace source and reviewer access records.

Supporting quotes must be a nonempty list of nonempty strings for a generated clause; malformed containers make the result unscorable and cannot substantiate support. The retained generated-clause denominator remains visible. Unknown output cardinality is reported as `not_scored`, a conservative block rather than the original protocol’s explicit gate-failure label.
